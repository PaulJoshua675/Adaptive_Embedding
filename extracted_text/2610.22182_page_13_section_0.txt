Paired gains over the global baseline Our paired global baseline reproduces ProKeR’s residual predictor in Eq. (3), using the final descriptor from frozen CLIP. Table 4 compares this predictor with LAMP and LAMP-F across all eleven datasets. The three methods share support banks, prompts, and selected global kernel parameters, so the comparison measures the gains from regional integration and the subsequent prototype adaptation. At 16-shot, the global baseline reaches 82.30% with ViT-B/16 and 76.62% with RN50. LAMP raises these means to 83.30% and 78.73%, and LAMP-F to 83.57% and 79.10%, respectively. Averaged equally across datasets and the five support budgets, LAMP improves accuracy by 1.14 percentage points with ViT and 1.59 with RN50; LAMP-F improves it by 1.96 and 2.54 points.

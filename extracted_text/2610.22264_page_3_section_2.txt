SCOPE organizes policy updates around semantic spans. Given a prompt x, the autoregressive policy πθ generates a completion y with the rewrite and its span boundaries. The cleaning operator c(·) extracts the <answer> field, removes the | separators, and concatenates the span text. A frozen image generator renders I = fϕ(c(y)), which the evaluator scores against the cleaned prompt. The policy therefore receives feedback for a complete rewrite, although the compositional edits within it can span several tokens. Figure 2 shows how SCOPE incorporates semantic spans into GRPO [46]. Grouped rollouts provide a completion advantage, while token likelihood changes determine its contribution within each semantic span. SCOPE pools these changes over aligned spans and normalizes them into shared coefficients.

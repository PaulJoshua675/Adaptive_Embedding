For each attribute 𝑎, we attach a light- weight MLP regression head [ 𝑓_𝑎: R𝑑→R𝑑_𝑎], with architecture Linear(𝑑, 64)–GELU–Linear(64,𝑑𝑎), where 𝑑is the backbone hid- den dimension and 𝑑𝑎is the dimensionality of the attribute target. The head operates on the final-layer hidden state at the position used to predict the third-level SID token 𝐿2. At this position, the model has already conditioned on the preceding coarse-grained codes 𝐿0 and 𝐿1, making it a natural location for capturing fine- grained perceptual and compositional properties of the item. SID-conditioned attribute targets. Attribute targets are constructed independently of the SID token vocabulary. We first join the pro- duction catalog, which associates each item with its complete SID tuple (𝑙0,𝑙1,𝑙2,𝑙3), with the corresponding raw attribute annotations. Since multiple items may share the same SID tuple, we aggregate the attribute annotations of all items assigned to a tuple to ob- tain a lookup function [𝑇𝑎(𝑙0,𝑙1,𝑙2,𝑙3) →y𝑎]. During training, the attribute target is therefore determined directly from the item’s complete SID, rather than from the conversation or the surface form of the user query. This allows the auxiliary objective to be applied to every valid SID-generation example, including examples whose conversational context does not explicitly mention the attribute. Position-aligned supervision. The attribute head operates on the final-layer hidden state at the 𝐿2 SID prediction position, which is the same representation used by the backbone to predict the fine- grained SID token after conditioning on the preceding 𝐿0 and 𝐿1 codes. We choose this position because the finer-grained informa- tion captured at the 𝐿2 level is expected to be more closely related to perceptual and compositional item attributes. As discussed in Section 4, our preliminary probing experiments provide empirical evidence that the 𝐿2 representation contains substantially more attribute information than earlier SID positions. Thus, aligning the auxiliary supervision with the 𝐿2 prediction position directly en- courages the representation used for fine-grained item prediction to preserve attribute-specific information. LHASTE = Lgen + 𝜆𝑎L𝑎, where Lgen denotes the generative SID objective and 𝜆𝑎controls the strength of attribute supervision. The auxiliary heads are applied to all eligible SID-generation examples rather than only attribute-specific conversations. Because the target is obtained through the SID-to-attribute lookup, the supervision is independent of task type and conversational wording. This substantially increases the density of attribute supervision and is particularly beneficial for items that occur infrequently in attribute-specific interactions.

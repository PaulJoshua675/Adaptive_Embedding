To unlock this capability for the LLM backbone, we conduct post-training on the steering interface, jointly optimizing the two paths with feedback from task outcomes. As illustrated in Figure 3(b), the reinforcement learning pipeline samples intermediate states that previous rollouts have reached and lets the steering agent attempt interventions there. The steering agent generates several steering actions at the sampled states, and their final rewards are compared together with those of the unsteered continuations. The comparison teaches the fast path which states carry high steering priority through a pairwise ranking objective, and teaches the slow path which actions improve outcomes through GRPO (Shao et al., 2024).

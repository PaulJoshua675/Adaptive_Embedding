Let FalseJointt denote joint answer-and-explanation success over all negatives. We define ExpBAt = 1 2(AccT,t + FalseJointt) and ExpOA = 1 10 P10 t=1 ExpBAt. The Judge receives the task, claim, and both explanations, without visual evidence or model identity; failure indicates a reference-semantic mismatch. We further perform human inspection to validate the Judge in Sec. 5.2. To test whether judgments track the requirement and candidate, we also evaluate linked counter- factuals. A pair contains a positive anchor and a related negative; a triplet contains the anchor and both specification-side and candidate-side negatives. All linked answers must be correct, and explanation-aware variants also check negative explanations. Appendix C defines the metrics and Appendix E.5 reports the eligible comparison sets. 4 Dataset and Benchmark Construction To support reliable verification feedback, supervision should identify not only whether a candidate is wrong, but which requirement is contradicted by the evidence. We therefore combine existing 3D resources with controlled interventions and native supervision, using three complementary strategies to construct task-specific data.

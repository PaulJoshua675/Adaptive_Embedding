Genetic Algorithms (GAs) are the randomized search heuristics based on the biological analogy of selective breeding in nature, originating from the work of J. Holland [29]. A GA manipulates with a population of λ individuals, using the random operators that model mutation and crossover in nature. Suppose that a GA is applied to an optimization problem with a finite space of solutions D and an objective function f : D →R to be maximized or minimized.

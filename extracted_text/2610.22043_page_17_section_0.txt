E Artifacts and protocol chronology The pilot was designed and run before this expansion and had already revealed answer-encoding sensitivity. The expanded primary protocol therefore incorporates controls suggested by that ob- served limitation. Its original 1,824 cases are internally timestamped before expanded results, not a pilot-independent public preregistration. The later 192-case control-coverage addition follows a documented audit of the first-block selection rule; it is not presented as preregistered confirma- tion. The public benchmark dataset retains the original frozen prompts, expanded scores, protocol, and coverage amendment. The new benchmark is analyzed as a separate dataset under one new runtime.

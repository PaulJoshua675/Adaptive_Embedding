Weak-to-Strong Paradigms. Weak-to-strong generalization studies how stronger models learn from weaker supervision (Burns et al., 2023; Jung et al., 2025; Medvedev et al., 2025; Somerstep et al., 2025; Xue et al., 2025). These works primarily concern training-time transfer of supervision or capabilities from a weaker model to a stronger one. Here we study the complementary inference- time setting of weak-to-strong escalation (W2SE), where a weak model executes by default and selectively invokes a stronger model when additional capability is needed. This shifts the weak–strong relationship from supervision transfer to sequential resource allocation. We derive escalation signals from the agent’s own past experience without training an additional monitor. Uncertainty Estimation and Agent Monitoring. Uncertainty estimation of agentic systems has emerged as one of the most important problems to monitor and guide agent decisions (Chun et al., 2026; Han et al., 2024; Oh et al., 2026b), while SafeGround and Argus investigate uncertainty estima- tion for GUI grounding, including calibrated predictions and representation-based methods (Kumar et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026). Sampling-based uncertainty estimation and risk control have also been studied for question answering (Duan et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025), but repeated generation can be costly when applied at every step of a long-horizon agent trajectory. Meanwhile, another line of work explores weak-to-strong oversight of agent misbehavior and failure monitoring through observable trajectories (Greenblatt et al., 2024; Kale et al., 2026; Pan et al., 2026). Rather than focusing on either escalation or oversight alone, we handle both problem in a single unified fueled by a simple uncertainty estimate. That is, we integrate representation-based uncertainty estimation into the execution workflow, reusing the backbone model’s hidden states for escalation and extending the same signal to post-handoff oversight of a closed-source strong agent. 3. Method We introduce LEASH, a training-free uncertainty-driven agentic inference framework for W2SE and post-handoff oversight (Figure 2). By comparing the weak model’s runtime hidden states with successful and failed reference executions, we estimate uncertainty at task initialization and during execution, which guides both escalation to the strong agent and continued oversight after handoff.

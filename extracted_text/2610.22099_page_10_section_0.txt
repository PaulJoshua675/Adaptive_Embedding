Limitations. Our evaluation centers on 256-token mathematical reasoning, and the supplementary transfer experiments use a single seed; longer generations and broader task coverage remain important tests of generality. TIPS also uses a fixed decoding horizon rather than an adaptive stopping rule, preserving further revision but foregoing savings from early termination. Developing stopping criteria that account for possible future revisions, and evaluating the resulting accuracy–latency trade-off, are important directions for future work. Decoder-call budgets also do not directly capture wall-clock cost.

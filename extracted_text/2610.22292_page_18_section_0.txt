The remaining measures show whether these gains come with the model still playing the game. CATCH emits a valid action on nearly every turn, speaks on every discussion turn on Llama, and never refuses to play. On Qwen its SPEAK rate falls to 70% because the model tends to confess in the first turn of a meeting and then stops contributing, having acknowledged that it cannot win. CAPO on Llama is the clearest case of an intervention that lowers the win rate by withdrawing: it emits a valid action on 71% of turns, speaks on 44% of discussion turns, and refuses to play in 35% of games; CAPO-DPO refuses in 24%. Among the prompting baselines, the prefill lowers valid actions on both models (89% and 81%), and the honest persona breaks Qwen’s output format almost entirely, with valid actions on 1% of turns and messages on 2% of discussion turns, so its other values on Qwen are not interpretable. Kills in front of witnesses rise sharply on Llama for the methods that confess most, from 55% for the base model to 96% for CATCH and 94% for StTP: these Impostors largely stop hiding their kills.

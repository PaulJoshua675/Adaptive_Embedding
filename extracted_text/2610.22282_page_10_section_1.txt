In this work, we addressed the cost of online reinforcement learning for visual generators without perturbing their reverse denoising trajectories. We introduced Routing Policy Optimization, a sampler- preserving method that samples block-wise attention routes from the generator’s native attention mass at a few selected denoising levels. A controlled pair holds the non-routing generation context fixed, so its reward difference weights a Gaussian velocity likelihood of the two recorded routed trajectories. Because this likelihood updates the dense velocity field, inference removes the masks and restores dense attention under the original deterministic sampler. Experiments on Stable Diffusion 3.5 and Wan2.2 demonstrate improved reward-aligned quality across text-to-image and text-to-video generation while requiring substantially less training compute than sampler-space baselines. By moving stochastic exploration from the sampler into attention, RPO provides an efficient policy interface for reward-aligned visual generation without altering the inference process.

We presented LAMP, a training-free method that combines global and regional pre- dictions from multiple layers of a frozen vision–language encoder. Each branch retains its native feature dimension and independently fits the same support-label residual; their corrections are then combined in class-score space. LAMP achieves state-of- the-art mean accuracy among the compared training-free RN50 methods at 4, 8, and 16 shots, while matching or exceeding representative methods that require training at higher support budgets. LAMP-F adds text-anchored prototype adaptation and uses a cached response to update the predictions exactly. Beyond these accuracy gains, LAMP provides a way to combine features of different dimensions without learning a shared projection. Its separation of branch fitting, score fusion, and prototype updates offers a foundation for studying task-specific layer selection and developing more scalable residual prediction methods. 22

Prior research indicates that the global layout of generated images is primarily established during the initial denoising stages. Based on this observation, we evaluate the performance trade-offs by varying the switching timestep between Identity Anchoring (IA) and Identity-Aware Self-Attention (IASA). As illustrated in Fig. 21, transitioning at t = 9 effec- tively balances prompt alignment (CLIP-T) and identity consistency (CLIP-I), ensuring both structural flexibility and identity preservation.

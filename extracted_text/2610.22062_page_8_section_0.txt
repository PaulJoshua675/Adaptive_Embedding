The predicted vs. ground-truth prevalence trajectories (Figure 2C) show the model under-detecting at 6 months (45.5% vs. 63.6%) and over-detecting at 24 months (30.8% vs. 15.4%), with the remaining timepoints within a few points of the expert labels, attributed to a stricter human threshold for “managed” diarrhea and to documentation falling one window away from the labeled visit, dominated by annotation and data-availability factors rather than model misreadings.

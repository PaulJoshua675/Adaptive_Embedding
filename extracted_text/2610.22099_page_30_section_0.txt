B.4.1 Zero-shot transfer to Dream-7B We apply the LLaDA-trained TIPSfull policy to Dream-v0-Instruct-7B without retraining. This comparison uses a single 1×256 canvas for both TIPS and Fast-dLLM and preserves the Dream logit-to-position alignment. As the policy observes confidence features rather than token identities or decoder hidden states, its input interface does not require a shared vocabulary or hidden-state dimension. The transferred policy reaches 35.18%, 46.56%, and 52.39% test micro-accuracy at T = 16, 32, 64, compared with 22.87%, 27.43%, and 29.96% for the matched full-canvas Fast-dLLM baseline. Table 23 expands the main-text comparison with per-dataset scores and actual mean decoder calls. These results demonstrate transfer to another frozen backbone in this setting; they do not require adapting the policy on Dream outputs or selecting its parameters from Dream test scores.

Filtering search is one of the crucial applications of approximate nearest neighbor search (ANNS). In filtering search, label informa- tion is used in conjunction with unstructured vector data. ANNS provides efficient similarity search in large-scale datasets, how- ever, the memory footprint of storing both vector data and their associated label becomes a significant cost factor as dataset sizes grow. To address this problem of increasing DRAM usage, we pro- pose AiSAQ-in-Filtering, with a novel quantization method called LiPQ (Label-inclusive Product Quantization). LiPQ combines un- structured data and structured labels into a single representation, leading no growth of encoded data. Our proposal increased recall@1 of single-label searches by up to 55% and fewer disk I/Os compared with existing methods. This also retains the main advantages of AiSAQ, which can reduce DRAM footprint by up to 1/78 and index switching time comparing with existing methods. This proposal can also be applied to sparse-dense hybrid search beyond label filtering such as semantic and keyword search of documents.

Annotation Procedure The diarrhea ground truth and the multi-phenotype expert spot-check were established by the Rett Center clinical team, comprising two pediatric nurses familiar with the Rett syndrome clinic and two pediatric neurologists, who worked as a single reviewing group throughout. For each patient and timepoint window the reviewers examined the available clinical notes and labeled the cell Yes for medication-associated diarrhea, No, or Deceased/Not applicable. Where diarrhea was recorded, its severity was noted on the clinic’s four-level scale: 1, loose stool in diaper; 2, watery stool in diaper; 3, watery stool on clothes; 4, watery stool outside clothes. The operative question was whether documented diarrhea represented a medication side effect as opposed to a tolerated, controlled or pre-existing baseline symptom. This is the same judgment that drives the reviewer–model threshold disagreements in main-text Table 4. The reviewers resolved each cell together by consensus rather than annotating independently against a written guideline. No formal written guideline was issued, and because each cell was adjudicated by consensus, no inter-annotator agreement statistic could be computed retrospectively. This is a genuine limitation for gold-standard reliability (TRIPOD-LLM items 8a–8c). A shared written guideline with an explicit severity threshold, applied by independent double annotation, would both tighten the ground truth and allow the reviewer–model threshold disagreements to be quantified rather than adjudicated case by case. Scope of the spot-check sample. The spot-check sample was drawn before the exposure-anchor adjudication was final, so its coverage of the reported evaluation windows is partial. Of the 102 reviewed notes, 58 fall inside an evaluation window in the reported configuration and 44 do not. The shortfall is concentrated at baseline, where 26 of 33 cases fall outside, against 12 of 34 at 6 months, 6 of 34 at 12 months and 0 of 1 at 18 months. That gradient follows from the baseline window being the one defined immediately around the anchor, so it is the most sensitive to where the anchor sits, while the later windows are progressively less so. A failure of record linkage would instead have removed cases uniformly. The shortfall is balanced with respect to the model’s call, affecting 22 of the 51 model-positive and 22 of the 51 model-negative cases, so the 58 are not tilted toward agreement or disagreement. On those 58 the reported configuration returns the same call in 57 cases, and expert agreement over them is 91.4% (53/58). The 90.2% (92/102) in the main text is the figure the reviewers produced over the notes they read, and we report it as such. Re-estimating agreement directly on the reported configuration would require a fresh sample.

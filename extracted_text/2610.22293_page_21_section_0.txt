B.8 Toy study: full specification Applied-update projection is the only constraint that is both exact and stable. The controlled setup below isolates where the projection is applied. All schemes learn the toy task, but only projecting the optimizer’s applied update eliminates protected-subspace leakage without substantially changing the held-out capability. Model, optimizer, targets. Qwen2.5-0.5B, fine-tuned with plain AdamW at learning rate 1×10−4 and weight decay 0 (unless stated). Each scheme’s projection is applied to the standard target linears—attention q, k, v, o and MLP gate, up, down. Task (8 fixed examples). Next-token cross-entropy on the completion tokens only (prompt tokens masked), on eight short prompt→completion pairs: ("The capital of France is", " Paris."), ("2 plus 3 equals", " 5."), ("The opposite of hot is", " cold."), ("Water is made of hydrogen and", " oxygen."), ("The first month of the year is", " January."), ("A group of wolves is called a", " pack."), ("The chemical symbol for gold is", " Au."), ("Seven times eight is", " 56."). The task is trivially learnable, so every scheme drives task loss to ≈0; the discriminator is the forgetting on out of domain tasks. 21

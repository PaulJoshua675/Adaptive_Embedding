The controller records the signed confirmation value with recipient and experience provenance, and subsequent curricula follow the host’s feedback rule, shared by Full and No Recovery. Failure modes and safeguards. An incomplete or conflicting reference is excluded before training, a duplicate normalized problem occupies one archive slot, and a cached attempt is reused only from the exact selected checkpoint. A flawed reference that passes the verifier can still steer the repair teacher, so repair depends on verification quality. Absorption can also lose successes, as the round-7 net gain of −0.20 pp in Figure 7 shows. With the empirical zero threshold, sampling noise can admit a harmful proposal; the positive-margin mode of Theorem 1 bounds that risk. B.4 IMPLEMENTATION CONFIGURATION Appendix A and Table 3 give the EVOHEIR (Full) recipe. The remaining implementation details are as follows. Response-token aggregation. Response loss is computed only on recorded response tokens; prompt tokens are masked out. If an EOS token is present in the recorded response, it is included in the loss. Truncated responses contribute their available response prefix without synthesizing a missing EOS token. Token-level forward KL over the full vocabulary is averaged within each response, then over questions, so repair and keep examples have equal per-question weight. Absorption and optimizer state. Recovery uses AdamW with learning rate 5 × 10−5, (β1, β2) = (0.9, 0.999), epsilon 10−8, zero weight decay, gradient clipping at 1.0, and LoRA dropout 0. The adapter has rank 8, scaling α = 16, and targets q proj and v proj; absorption uses at most 16 updates. After absorption, the adapter is merged into the recipient and the merged weights are reloaded and checked for exact consistency. If the absorbed model is accepted, it becomes the next native root and the native optimizer and scheduler are reinitialized; Recovery optimizer state is not carried into native training. If the proposal is rejected, the original root is retained with its parameters, optimizer moments, scheduler, and random state unchanged. At the next round’s native-stage boundary, the native optimizer and scheduler are reinitialized for whichever model was inherited. Rollback restores the root snapshot; it does not carry Recovery moments into a later stage. Archive policy. The archive stores at most 256 qualified training records, deduplicated by normalized problem identity. Conflicting labels, verification conflicts, known reasoning errors, incomplete responses, and non-training records are excluded. When capacity is exceeded, records are selected deterministically using round/source-stratified round-robin admission, with stable hash ordering within each group. Recovery budget. Each round nominates up to 32 exploration records and 32 learner-success records, and recipient attempts reuse exact-checkpoint cached responses or use one additional response within a 64- generation budget. Recovery makes a single pass with at most 16 optimizer updates, capped at 131,072 response loss tokens and 262,144 combined teacher and student forward tokens. An empty usable pool leaves Ssel t unchanged.

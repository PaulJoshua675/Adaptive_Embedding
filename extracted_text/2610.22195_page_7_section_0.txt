These results also highlight the complementary strengths of multi-agent orchestration and minimal-tooling runtimes. DeepSeek Harness, as a single-agent runtime, outperforms Agent Team on PRL-Bench (39.89 vs. 35.81) and matches it on FSO (76.00 vs. 75.00), showing that a focused single- agent runtime can be competitive on benchmarks that re- ward sustained reasoning. HTN-Orchestrate outperforms both approaches across all five benchmarks. That it achieves these results with DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, an open-weight model, while matching or exceeding larger proprietary sys- tems underscores the importance of orchestration design in determining overall system performance. The execution analysis examines completion time, worker concurrency, and internal task-network statistics to reveal the operational signatures of hierarchical orchestra- tion, while the ablations disable result-informed planning, local multi-agent orchestration, and task-network rebuilding in turn to quantify their individual effects.

A.4 ADDITIONAL ABLATION ON MODEL SCALE We further conduct the same ablation study on Qwen3-VL-4B to examine whether the effectiveness of our design remains consistent at a smaller model scale. As shown in Table 6, SFT substantially underperforms the zero-shot backbone, while Mix-RL yields only a modest overall improvement and remains below the corresponding capability-specific experts on several target capabilities. In contrast, independently trained experts achieve stronger performance on their respective capabilities, showing that the benefit of separating capability acquisition from integration remains evident with a smaller backbone. The skill-augmented expert results exhibit a trend consistent with Qwen3-VL-8B. Conditioning each expert on its matched skill improves performance on the corresponding target capability, while the effects on non-target capabilities remain limited or mixed. This further supports the capability- specific nature of the extracted guidance and the use of explicit skill-expert matching during distil- lation.

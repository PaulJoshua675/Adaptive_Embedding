4.6 Externalization with a trace The programme has already externalized contingently tacit structure several times by adding op- erations. The “calculus degree” and the Giant Score are new compositions of binding, bundling, unbinding and Hamming similarity (2026a; 2026d). They turned a feature of the graph into a stated fact: Newton and Leibniz have nearly equal calculus-content scores (0.534 and 0.569) but very different path counts to the 64 Fields Medalists (4 and 47), so Newton ranks 91st on the Giant Score and Leibniz second (2026d). In 2026d we summarized this as the difference between possessing and transmitting upper-tail human capital. Unbinding the FIELD component over 50-year windows recovered a second channel the mentor–student graph cannot show by con- struction: an astronomy signal of +0.0025 in 1250–1300, aligned with the Toledo and Maragha translation era, and +0.0065 in 1450–1500, while algebra stays near the 0.5 baseline (2026d). Kanerva’s “What is the dollar of Mexico?” shows the same move in miniature: a mapping vector computed from two country records returns “peso” without decomposing either record (Kanerva 2010). Nonaka and Takeuchi located externalization in metaphor, analogy, hypothesis and model; hyperdimensional operations are a computational form of exactly those moves. What VaCoAl adds is the trace. Because binding is exactly reversible, every externalized item arrives with its derivation—which bundles, which roles, which paths—so a result that looks like magic can be taken apart afterwards by anyone who holds the substrate. 4.7 A conjecture on ineffability The semantic pointers that Thagard adopts from Eliasmith bind by circular convolution, whose unbinding is only a quasi-inverse: noise grows with the number and depth of bindings, and a clean-up memory is needed to use the result at all (2026e). In a substrate of that kind a deeply nested integration cannot be taken apart without loss, so it cannot be told. In 2026c we stated the same point in one line: “what cannot be exactly decomposed was never composed, only mixed.” We conjecture that part of what Polanyi called ineffable is a property of a substrate’s read operations rather than of knowledge as such: integration that runs through similarity completion resists articulation, while integration written as exact relational bindings does not. Section 5 gives the hippocampal version of this conjecture, and §9 states how it could fail. 5 Collins’s three tacit knowledges as strata of one substrate Collins’s three kinds of tacit knowledge sit at three different strata of the substrate, and only two of them lie inside it (Table 6). 5.1 Relational: tacit because no one could survey it The Leibnizian hourglass was present in Wikidata before anyone measured it. It was tacit for a contingent reason of scale. The graph has 25.5 million paths to the 64 tracer Medalists, a path- to-node ratio of about 68:1, and standard tools fail on it: a Python dictionary exhausted 128 GB of memory at FS = 25,000, the GPU library torchhd requested more than 100 GB of VRAM and stopped, and centrality measures ran but could not tell a mathematician from an anatomist in the same position (2026d, Appendix B). Once an operation existed that could survey the whole graph semantically, the structure became a set of stated facts: 47 of 64 lineages through Leibniz, a 10.1-fold thickness ratio, a 46-fold rise in learned-society membership per scholar across the same window (2026d).

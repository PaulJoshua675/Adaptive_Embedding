A Role Reward Computation We detail aggregation scopes and parameters for offline and co-evolution rewards (Section 4.2). A.1 Rewards for Planner and Optimizer Equations (6)–(7) combine separate returns for the current account and all controlled accounts. Planner uses results from observation end to period end; Optimizer uses current-step increments. Each term aggregates ADV, spend, and conversions F, C, N in its scope to compute the scaled proxy return. The CPA target averages initial tCPA across all units, weighted by conversions. For settlement window W, let du be unit u’s initial tCPA and Nu,W its conversions. The target cost allowance MW = P u duNu,W and total conversions NW = P u Nu,W give ρ = MW/NW; when NW = 0, we set ρ = 0. Both Sparse and General use this computation. Training sets η = 1, s = 10000, and ω = 0.2 to control the cost-deviation weight, reward scaling, and overall reward weight, respectively. Planner’s reward settles at period end; Optimizer’s current-step reward is used in the advantage recursion in Appendix C.1.

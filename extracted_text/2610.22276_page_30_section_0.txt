Hierarchical point classification must preserve enough visual detail to distinguish increasingly small regions. We therefore separate three questions: whether crop-trained weights can operate without crops, whether matching training to a cheaper visual input recovers quality, and whether spatial candidates should be text regions or visual tokens. All learned variants start from the same official Qwen base. Training QA, updates, optimizer, and per-QA loss normalization are held fixed; visual computation is an intended variable, so the runs do not have equal FLOPs. Removing crops from fixed weights. The inference-only intervention keeps three nine-way textual decisions but removes both appended pixel crops. RefSpatial drops from 51.00 to 9.00, Where2Place from 59.98 to 32.00, and RoboSpatial pointing from 62.30 to 20.49 (Table 18). Adding an original-image hybrid prefix cache leaves these aggregate scores unchanged. The immediate loss is therefore not repaired by reusing the original context more efficiently.

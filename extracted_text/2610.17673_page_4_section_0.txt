We fine-tune base-Qwen3-Embedding on TopiOCQA with an InfoNCE [58] contrastive loss with a tempera- ture of 0.01, cross-GPU in-batch negatives, and false-negative mask- ing, a batch size of 480 over four GPUs, a constant learning rate 1e−5 with 6% warmup, 20 epochs, and fp32 master weights. Checkpoint se- lection: we evaluate every epoch on the out-of-domain QReCC con- versational benchmark and select the peak epoch, epoch 1, whose NDCG@10 of 54.6 exceeds the 52.5 of the zero-shot base model; later epochs monotonically forget, so the backbone is early-stopped at its conversational peak. Personalized fine-tuning. We train the query encoder with the objective of Section 4.5 for up to 30 epochs, batch size 16, constant learning rate 5e−6, bf16 with fp32 master weights, keeping all judged grade-0 docs as negatives plus 𝐾=2048 global negatives; default weights 𝑤𝑔=1, 𝑤𝑘=2, 𝑤𝑎=1. The conversational backbone is the single most important ingredient, and replacing conv-Qwen3 with base-Qwen3 hurts the most by a wide margin. Oracle-rewrite distillation and the graded ranking loss come next and matter al- most as much, followed by the initialization anchor and the global negatives. The remaining choices, namely the full-profile query form, LoRA over full fine-tuning, and the low learning rate, each contribute a smaller amount.

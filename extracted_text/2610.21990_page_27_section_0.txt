If the conditions are already light-deficient due to real-environment conditions, the synthetic low-light degradation can be tuned to be low-impact. A spatially adaptive blending mask is introduced to reconcile the complementary properties of the two luminance reconstructions. While the Retinex-based luminance enforces realistic illumination statistics, its spatially varying remapping may compress bright structures and attenuate salient edges. In contrast, the globally scaled luminance preserves structural consistency but does not fully re- flect real-world low-light illumination distributions. To prevent over-suppression of high-intensity regions and to maintain perceptual stability in structurally significant areas, a highlight-aware blend- ing mechanism is employed. 27

Models and Baselines. We instantiate OS-Helix from Qwen3.5-27B and Qwen3.5-35B- A3B (Qwen Team, 2026a). For broader context, we also report representative agentic frameworks, general-purpose models, specialist models, and matched backbone baselines. Implementation Details. For task synthesis, we use Claude Sonnet 4.6 (Anthropic, 2026) for the Exploration and Evaluator Agents, and GPT-5.5 (OpenAI, 2026) for an independent quality review agent. For SFT, we train for one epoch on 31K successful filtered desktop samples synthesized by our pipeline and 40K general-purpose multimodal samples spanning code, reasoning, and related domains. For RL, we initialize from the dense OS-Helix-27B checkpoint and conduct a 1,000-step training run using rule-based evaluators as reward signals. Complete data details are provided in Appendix B, while training details are provided in Appendix C. Unless otherwise specified, each evaluation episode is capped at 50 interaction steps and retains up to 8 historical screenshots (resolution 1504x832) together with the full textual interaction history. Table 1 shows that hybrid-policy training, rather than code-tool availability alone, yields substantial gains un- der matched-backbone comparisons. OS-Helix-27B and OS-Helix-35B-A3B improve over their corresponding Hybrid baselines by 16.25 and 14.43 absolute points, re- spectively. The largest gains occur in Office (+33.3 and +29.1 points) and Work. (+16.0 and +14.1 points). Office covers Calc, Impress, and Writer, where tasks often com- bine visually grounded interaction with rendered docu- ment states and precise transformations of structured ar- tifacts. The Work. category comprises multi-application tasks that require task-relevant information to be handed off across applications and action modali- ties. These interpretations are consistent with the agent behavior study and representative trajectory audit in Appendix F. Starting from the SFT policy, OS-Helix-27B-RL further raises the average success rate from 62.75% to 66.44%. Its incremental gains are concentrated in Professional (+23.10 points), OS (+7.61 points), and Daily (+6.69 points), whereas Office and Work. do not improve further over their already strong SFT results. This profile shows that RL provides additional gains in specific task domains rather than a uniform increase across the benchmark. The paired-bootstrap analysis of the aggregate difference is reported in Table 16. In broader, non-controlled comparisons, OS-Helix-27B-RL is competitive with GPT-5-based agentic frameworks such as Agent-S3 (Gonzalez-Pumariega et al., 2025) and OS-Symphony (Yang et al., 2026a), while newer and substantially larger general models remain stronger overall. Table 2 further shows that the gains transfer to WindowsAgentArena, where OS-Helix-27B-RL improves over the Qwen3.5-27B GUI baseline by 23.05 points. This provides evidence of cross-platform transfer on WindowsAgentArena, with detailed results deferred to Appendix D.1.

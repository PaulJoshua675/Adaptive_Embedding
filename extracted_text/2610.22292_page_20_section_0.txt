We compare corpora at the most honest point on each front, which lies between 71.7 and 80.6 MASK honesty for every run (Table 5). Magpie-Qwen3.6 is the same corpus for both models, which makes it on-policy for Qwen3.6-27B and external for Llama-3.3-70B. The table also lists two runs left out of the figure: Magpie-Qwen2.5, and a second, coding-heavy version of Magpie-Qwen3.6. On Llama-3.3-70B, the corpora are ordered by the release date of the model that generated them. UltraChat-200k, generated with ChatGPT in 2023, drops to 21.2. Magpie-Qwen2.5 (September 2024) reaches 43.2, and the on-policy Magpie-Llama-3.3 (December 2024) stays at parity with 51.3. Magpie-Qwen3.6, generated by a model released in April 2026, goes well past parity to 85.3. The middle panel puts these Llama-3.3-70B checkpoints on the same reference as Qwen3.6-27B. The untrained Llama-3.3-70B wins only 11.4% of comparisons against the untrained Qwen3.6-27B, so Qwen3.6-27B is the stronger model under this judge. Training with Magpie-Qwen3.6 raises this to 21.5, while the other corpora stay between 8.3 and 10.4. This closes part of the gap between the two models, but not all of it.

We introduced ELITEREPLAY, which preserves so- lution diversity during RL post-training by replay- ing an archive of distinct correct solutions found by quality-diversity search. ELITEREPLAY raises accuracy both in and out of distribution while re- taining most of the base model’s correct strategies. We also introduced a strategy-level evaluation of diversity, which reveals differences between meth- ods that pass@k conceals. Our results show that post-training need not trade diversity for reliability.

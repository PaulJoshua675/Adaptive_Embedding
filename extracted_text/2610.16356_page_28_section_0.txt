Discussions 5.1. Reflections on previous studies Our findings demonstrate that optimisation algorithms generally outper- form the classic FGWC method in terms of cluster evaluation and expected cluster formations. This aligns with previous research confirming the benefits of optimisation techniques in improving fuzzy clustering results, particularly when spatial effects are considered (Wijayanto and Purwarianti, 2014a,b; Caraka et al., 2021; Pamungkas and Pramana, 2019). Specifically, TLBO emerged as the most effective algorithm for optimising FGWC clustering quality, supporting the findings of Nayak et al. (2018) and Nasution and Sire- gar (2022), who identified TLBO as superior for overcoming the limitations of FCM and FGWC compared to other optimisation algorithms. Furthermore, ABC exhibited great potential to optimise FGWC, corroborating earlier find- ings by (Wijayanto and Purwarianti, 2014a; Wijayanto et al., 2016). How- ever, despite their enhanced clustering performance, these algorithms may occasionally fail to form the expected clusters due to dependencies among membership assignments, resulting in unintended outcomes (Grover, 2014; Thomas and Nashipudimath, 2012).

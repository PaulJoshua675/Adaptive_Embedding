In this paper, we presented FENAS, a topology-agnostic few-shot NAS predictor that integrates multi-dimensional zero-cost proxies with the CAST feature trans- formation pipeline. By aligning heterogeneous proxy distributions and modeling their complementary interactions, FENAS achieves accurate and stable architec- ture ranking under extremely limited supervision. Experiments on NAS-Bench- 101, NAS-Bench-201, and the DARTS search space show that FENAS provides competitive ranking and search performance with low computational cost. These results suggest that physically grounded proxy fusion is an efficient alternative to topology-heavy predictors, especially when only a small number of evaluated architectures are available.

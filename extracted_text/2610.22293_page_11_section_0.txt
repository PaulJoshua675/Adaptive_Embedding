Limitations and Future Work. SST changes the feasible update geometry, its optimal learning rate need not equal that of unconstrained SFT. Establishing fully tuned learning–retention frontiers remains important future work. Scaling SST to models with hundreds of billions of parameters and to longer task sequences is an important next test. Additionally, using information about the update for each batch to dynamically update the protection rather than refreshing at fixed intervals is a next step to make the algorithm more efficient.

Figure 5(a) shows mean an- swer reward before behavior penalties rising from 0.347 to 0.587 over 19 steps. Despite batch fluctua- tions, this trend indicates improved answer quality during joint RL–OPD training. Search Behavior. Search trajectories initially lengthen and then contract (Figure 5(b)). Compar- ing steps 6–13 with steps 14–19, average tool calls decrease from 26.0 to 21.3 (18.3%), and generated response length falls from 29.0k to 23.4k tokens (19.5%), while mean answer reward remains com- parable (0.525 versus 0.544). These observations are consistent with increasingly economical search: later rollouts use fewer interactions and generated tokens without a corresponding decline in average answer quality.

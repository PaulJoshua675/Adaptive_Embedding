(ii) Prototype regime, ln K ≲ 2βNr2 and x = β p N(1 −r2) ≲1: the crosstalk term is below ρ and decays exponentially in Nβ (W rises from ∼ K−1/2 to one), Meff ≃M, hence ρeff ≃ρ up to the self-coupling correction τ, and m(1) ≃erf(1/√2ρ) independently of α. The retrieval boundary in the (α, ρ) plane is a horizontal line. (iii) Memorization regime, x ≳ √ 2 ln M: W = 1 but Meff = O(1); the pooling degrades gradually for 1 ≲x ≲ √ 2 ln M, where (14) with Meff = MΦM(x) describes the decrease of m(1), and beyond the freez- ing point the layer returns the training example closest to the query, nmax →1 and m(1) →r (Re- mark 2). The generalization window is therefore bounded by a crosstalk edge βlo, defined as the point where the crosstalk term of (i) equals the dataset entropy ρ, i.e. where the noise coming from the other classes equals that coming from the finite number of examples, and a condensation edge βhi, de- fined by x = √ 2 ln M, beyond which the correct class is represented by a finite number of examples. Remark 3 (optimal temperature). Inside the win- dow, neglecting the self-coupling and to leading order in x, ρeff exceeds ρ by two terms: the pooling loss ρ(1 −M −1)x2 (Proposition 3) and the crosstalk (1 + ρ)(K −1)e−2Nβr2 (item (i), q = r). The first grows as β2, the second decays exponentially, and at the maxi- mum of m(1) they stand in the ratio pooling loss = Nβ∗r2 × crosstalk, Nβ∗r2 ≃ln K, (20) as one finds by differentiating their sum (Appendix C, where the position β∗is given in closed form). The op- timum therefore does not remove the crosstalk: it stops when the crosstalk is a fraction 1/ ln K of the pooling loss, because the former is exponentially cheap to re- duce and the latter is not. The correct class then car- ries a weight W ∗< 1 (0.75, 0.50 and 0.39 for the three columns of Fig. 3, against 0.74, 0.49, 0.39 from the full expression (15)), and, within the same approximation, W ∗decreases with the load as (α ln K)−1/2: the layer generalizes best while it still shares its weight with the wrong classes.

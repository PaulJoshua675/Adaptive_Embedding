Establishing that rate would require re-scoring with a gold arbiter held out of the loop, which we identify as the highest-value extension of this evaluation in Section 5.2. 4.1.2 Target Models and Baselines. Table 4 summarizes the 36 target models spanning 11 model families, selected to enable direct comparison with prior work while demonstrating broad applica- bility across model architectures, safety-training methodologies, and deployment contexts (local and API). We compare REPRO against four baselines that span the space of published black-box red- teaming approaches: PAIR [9] (iterative refinement), TAP [28] (tree-structured search), PAP [42] (persuasion-based attacks from social science), and Adaptive RS [4] (a gray-box method requiring logprob access, included as a reference point for methods with stronger target access). GCG and AutoDAN appear in the JailbreakBench and HarmBench tables where they are reported in the original benchmark papers; both are white-box methods requiring gradient access and are not direct comparisons to REPRO’s black-box setting. All baseline figures in this paper are transcribed from their original publications rather than re-run by us. This carries a consequence for interpretation that we state plainly here and revisit in Section 5.4: the compared methods do not operate under matched query budgets. PAIR is designed around a budget of roughly twenty queries per behavior, TAP prunes a bounded search tree, and Adaptive RS consumes up to 10,000 queries per behavior, whereas REPRO runs to success or to its iteration cap, with a median of 24 and a maximum of 5,589 observed target queries per behavior (Table 12). A difference in reported ASR between REPRO and a fixed-budget baseline therefore reflects the combination of method and budget, not method alone. Budget-matched re-runs of PAIR and TAP under our judge, and ASR-versus-budget curves that would let the methods be read at a common operating point, are the most direct way to separate the two, and we identify them as future work rather than claiming the separation here.

We assign the method a trivially achievable score on each dataset on which it did not complete: an IBS of 1 (the maximum attainable value in expectation if G is estimated perfectly) or a concordance index of 0.5 (no better than chance), matching the metric being ranked. All critical-difference diagrams and pairwise win-rate figures are therefore always computed over the full 63-dataset held-out cohort.

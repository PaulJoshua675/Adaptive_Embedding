Exchanging v reverses the contrast to −0.0141; resetting Adam reduces it to 0.0046, within ±0.01 (Figure 4b). Thus update-driven histories with the same mean loss weights can produce different susceptibility to subsequent learning. This control identifies an effect of the realized weight history, not an effect of order alone: variability and order are not separately manipulated. Its implication is nevertheless concrete. The average training objective and selected endpoint errors can agree while persistent gradient statistics differ enough to change the response to subsequent learning. A difference in formation-route label is therefore not necessary for a susceptibility difference, and the label alone does not specify its mechanism. In this construction, optimizer- state interventions identify v as a major causal modifier of the large short-horizon difference, while later differences under fresh Adam and SGD show that it is not the only place where route history affects learning. 5.4 A predictive coordinate must do more than correlate with history A complementary negative result comes from a separate externally coupled learner. We tested whether a slow task-framing variable κ could predict regime switching. It appeared useful when different perturbation strengths were pooled (AUC 0.712). Within fixed perturbation levels, however, its predictive performance weakened or reversed; adding it to perturbation strength and s in seed-separated cross-validation changed AUC by only 0.0049 and slightly worsened log loss. The candidate did not explain which trajectories switched under a given intervention. Full contrasts appear in Appendix C. The coordinate failure and optimizer intervention address different parts of the same measurement problem. An observable summary may locate a learner’s specialization without supplying a sufficient dynamical state. A plausible slow variable must predict beyond the intervention that produced it; a hidden-state candidate becomes more informative when directly replacing it changes the subsequent response. Neither result determines the minimum number of variables needed to predict the complete learning dynamics. 9

We also test the sensitivity of the downstream task accuracy to the LLRD decay factor λ by sweeping λ ∈{0.5, 0.65, 0.75, 0.90} on DFC2020-S1 and Flood-S1 (Table 9). On Flood- S1, performance is statistically indistinguishable across the full range tested (repeated-measures ANOVA, F(3, 12) = 1.44, p = 0.28), while on DFC2020-S1, we detect a small but consistent effect of λ (F(3, 12) = 22.6, p < 0.001). However, our default λ = 0.75 is statistically indis- tinguishable from the best-performing value tested (λ = 0.5; paired t-test, pBH = 0.19), and the only significant pairwise contrast runs in the direction of λ = 0.75 outperforming less aggressive decay (λ = 0.90; pBH = 0.040), not the reverse. This pattern is consistent with the degradation at large λ (near 1) for LLRD fine-tuning of vision transformers (Dong et al., 2022), and supports retaining the BEiT-inherited default of λ = 0.75 without further tuning. 4.4.4.

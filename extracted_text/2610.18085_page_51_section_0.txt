I.3 Depth-Resolved Evolution under Lion Figure 7 connects the degree-zero relative-update law to the time course of a public-model stress test. The deepest key block receives a relative first step of about 1% under the imposed tilt, compared with 0.01% in released coordinates. Its cosine similarity with the starting matrix falls to 0.46 after 400 steps, accompanied by a larger attention entropy decrease in deep layers and a reversal of the early loss advantage. The cosine diagnostic is invariant to positive scalar rescalings and therefore separates directional change from the balancing operation itself. Soft SAGGS limits these changes, and hard rebalancing also performs well in this setting. The preference for damping is supported by the unperturbed Lion state ablation in Appendix F.5. Here, attention entropy and loss are measured jointly, without isolating a causal relation between the two quantities.

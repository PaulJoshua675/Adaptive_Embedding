Optimization is performed using the AdamW optimizer [45] with hyper- parameters set to β1 = 0.9, β2 = 0.95, and a weight decay of 0.05. We employ a differentiated learning rate strategy: the peak learning rate is set to 5×10−6 for the DINOv2 image encoder, 5×10−5 for the gravity and flow encoders when present, and 1×10−5 for all other architectural components. The training follows a structured schedule throughout Stage 1, consisting of a 20% linear warmup to reach the peak learning rate, followed by a half-cycle cosine decay to a final value two orders of magnitude lower. B Additional Ablation Results We conduct an additional ablation study on dataset scale to demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in addressing data scarcity during fine- tuning. We modulate the effective dataset size by varying the total number of scenes sampled from each dataset per epoch. To ensure a consistent number of training steps for a fair comparison, the number of epochs is adjusted inversely to the sampling rate; for instance, when reducing the number of scenes to 10%, the total training duration is increased from 10 to 100 epochs.

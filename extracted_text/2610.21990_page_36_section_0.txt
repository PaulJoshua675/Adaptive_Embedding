This encoder acts as a fixed seman- tic prior, ensuring that the enhanced image preserves discriminative object representations consistent with the ground truth. By aligning deep semantic embeddings, the enhancement network is guided toward solutions that not only improve visual quality but also facilitate improved optimization for subsequent segmentation models.

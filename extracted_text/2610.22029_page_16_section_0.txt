B DETAILED METHOD AND IMPLEMENTATION B.1 STATE CONSTRUCTION AND RECIPIENT SELECTION The resumable learner St = (θt, ωt) holds the model parameters and the mutable training state, including optimizer moments, the learning-rate scheduler, and random generators. The Evolution Controller runs each admitted cohort in an isolated copy of the same root, and each branch follows the host’s training objective. The controller freezes the candidate set Cnat t ∪{St} before screening, selects the recipient by paired root-relative gain on P scr t , and breaks ties in favor of the root, followed by a fixed branch order. Algorithm 1 lists one round. B.2 RECIPIENT-CONDITIONED RECOVERY Recovery adapts distillation (Hinton et al., 2015) and Learning without Forgetting (Li & Hoiem, 2017) to recipient-specific failures and successes. Current records Et and archived records Mt supply problems and qualified references, each with its generating checkpoint, round, and verification trace. A cached recipient attempt is reused only at the exact selected checkpoint. The same answer- equivalence rule verifies the attempt and its reference, and membership in Drepair t or Dkeep t is decided for the current recipient, even when the reference comes from an earlier round. The frozen teacher and the student both start from θsel t and predict at the same fixed recipient prefixes throughout absorption, as in Eq. 4, so the procedure does not resample from the changing student. B.3 ARCHIVE, FEEDBACK, AND STATE INHERITANCE HEIRCOMMIT freezes S+ t before scoring it against the original root on the fixed confirmation pool with the same probes and frozen labels (Appendix A). It commits the complete proposal when the paired gain exceeds τt, with τt = 0 in the empirical mode, and otherwise restores the complete root state. Each round confirms one final proposal, and later rounds receive fresh evidence.

Table 1 compares representative executable software- engineering benchmarks by task unit, construction, retained structure, evaluation granularity, and training support. 2.1 Single-PR Software Engineering Benchmarks SWE-bench established executable repository-level evalua- tion by pairing an issue and a pre-change repository state with F2P/P2P tests (Jimenez et al. 2023). Subsequent benchmarks improve instance quality or expand the same atomic issue–PR formulation across visual software, programming languages, recent repository states, and more challenging enterprise- scale changes (Jimenez et al. 2023; Yang et al. 2024; Zan et al. 2025; Zhang et al. 2025a; Deng et al. 2025). Exe- cutable workbenches such as SWE-Gym, R2E-Gym, SWE- Smith, and SWE-rebench V2 further scale this primitive for trajectory generation and model training (Pan et al. 2024; Jain et al. 2025; Yang et al. 2025; Badertdinov et al. 2025). Together with recent contamination analyses, these works establish the foundations of verifiable SWE-agent evaluation and training, while generally retaining an individual issue, commit, or PR as the basic task unit.

The second row presents a more challenging, spatially sparse case with small scattered flood- water patches against an otherwise water-free background; all three configurations detect the approximate locations of most patches, but the non-pretrained encoder misses noticeably more of the flooded area than either pretrained configuration. The third row contains mainly permanent water in the form of a prominent meandering chan- nel, whose overall course all three configurations recover at comparable accuracy. In the fourth row, flood-water patches are distributed across the scene; all three configura- tions recover the main flooded areas, with variation confined to the finer details of individual patch boundaries and smaller isolated regions. Taken together, the clearest and most consistent difference across these scenes is the non-pretrained encoder’s weaker separation of flood water from both permanent water and dry land.

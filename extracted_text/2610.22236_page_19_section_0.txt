D Case studies The case studies are problems on which the fused model of Table 2 succeeds while the specialist of that domain fails in every sample. So that a specialist’s failure is not a single unlucky sample, the specialist is sampled several times: four samples per model on the competition benchmarks, and one sample of the fused model against four seeds of the coding specialist on the code benchmarks. Problems that meet this criterion include HMMT25 problems 21 and 26 and AIME25 problem 25 for mathematics, and HumanEval/126, HumanEval/93, Mbpp/92, Mbpp/244, Mbpp/278, Mbpp/563 and Mbpp/301 for code. We describe three of them. HMMT25, problem 21. In a two-dimensional cave with a parallel floor and ceiling, two stalactites of lengths 16 and 36 hang perpendicularly from the ceiling, while two stalagmites of heights 25 and 49 grow perpendicularly from the ground. If the tips of these four structures form the vertices of a square in some order, compute the height of the cave. The answer is 63. The fused model answers 63 in three of its four samples (Figures 3 and 6). It uses the fact that the diagonals of a square share a midpoint: pairing the two stalactite tips as one diagonal and the two stalagmite tips as the other, the midpoint heights agree when H −26 = 37.

Selected-target optimism, the drop from a candidate’s score on its selection responses to its score on fresh responses, is 8.60 pp in Agent0 and 4.10 pp in R-Zero, as Figure 4a shows, while fixed-target controls stay near zero at 0.20 and −0.10 pp. Under fresh responses, 52.2% of Agent0’s and 42.0% of R-Zero’s admitted tasks also leave the native frontier. Even exact-valid tasks need not teach. In Figure 4b, 60 of 96 real Skill-SP updates (62.5%) do not reduce correct-target NLL. 6.2 HEIRCOMMIT CUTS HARMFUL COMMITS ON EVERY HOST A second, coverage-matched audit takes 64 native updates per host and scores them on a pool disjoint from the decision probes. HEIRCOMMIT commits 23–52% of the updates and lowers the harmful share of commits from 47–77% to 13–16% on all three hosts, as Figure 4c shows.

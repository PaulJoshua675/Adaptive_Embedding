Top-Down Orchestration and Routing. Prior work scales LLM reasoning via external scaffolding, including static graph/configuration optimizers like Maestro (Wang et al., 2025), centralized cost- cascading routers like FrugalGPT (Chen et al., 2024), and learned small-agent routing (Alazraki et al., 2026). Unlike our decentralized market, these systems presuppose a fixed topology or a global planner with full information, ignoring private operational costs. Decentralized Agent Markets.

Preprint • We propose IG-OPD, an evidence-aware OPD framework that uses post-retrieval information to calibrate OPD’s two priors through complementary mechanisms: ITC reallocates cross-turn budgets by information criticality, while IQC calibrates query preferences with evidence utility. • Across six multimodal search benchmarks, IG-OPD improves average accuracy over OPD by 3.4 and 1.7 percentage points for the 2B and 4B students, respectively. The 4B student also surpasses its 8B teacher on MMSearch and BrowseComp-VL, with zero inference overhead. 2

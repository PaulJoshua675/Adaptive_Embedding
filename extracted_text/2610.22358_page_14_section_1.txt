Marginal forecasting. Chronos-2, TimesFM-3, and Toto 2.0 provide direct marginal quantiles while sharing information across variables (Ansari et al., 2025; Jain & Sen, 2026; Khwaja et al., 2026). Toto 1.0 instead predicts marginal Student-t mixtures and samples forecasts autoregressively across output patches (Cohen et al., 2025). Perez-Diaz et al. (2025) distinguish forecast products by their operational uses and show that marginals do not identify path-dependent event probabilities. PathFM represents the joint forecast through complete multivariate paths, so the same samples support marginal summaries and distributions of trajectory-dependent quantities. Generative trajectory forecasting. DeepAR samples temporally dependent futures autoregres- sively (Salinas et al., 2020), and TACTiS learns multivariate predictive distributions with attentional copulas (Drouin et al., 2022). TimeGrad introduces autoregressive diffusion for multivariate fore- casting (Rasul et al., 2021). Flow matching learns generative vector fields through conditional probability paths (Lipman et al., 2022; Huang et al., 2026); TSFlow incorporates Gaussian-process priors (Kollovieh et al., 2025), and Sundial uses flow matching for foundation-model pretraining (Liu et al., 2025). PathFM shares history computation across paths and uses distillation to support fast ensembles and direct forecast-score training. Distillation and direct score optimization. Flow map methods learn finite-time transport and support both teacher distillation and self-distillation (Boffi et al., 2024; 2026). Parallel Decoding Distillation (PDD) predicts multiple integration updates per network evaluation (Shaul et al., 2026). DRaFT optimizes differentiable rewards by backpropagating through diffusion sampling (Clark et al., 2024). Rewarded Moment Matching Distillation (RMMD) combines reward optimization with moment-matching supervision and applies unbiased sample CRPS to GenCast (Jacq et al., 2026). Its reward gradient passes through a denoising step applied to a re-noised student sample. PathFM backpropagates fair CRPS through the complete source-to-forecast map of one distilled evaluation. The same optimization updates continue matching the frozen teacher’s joint velocity field while fitting the student’s marginals to observations. Distillation thus supplies both a short path for score gradients and continued supervision from a joint generative teacher. Section 3.3 and table 4 give the objective and post-training comparisons. Forecast evaluation. Proper scoring rules underpin forecast comparison (Gneiting & Raftery, 2007). Fair scores address finite-ensemble effects (Ferro, 2014); variogram and transformation-based scores expose complementary multivariate properties (Scheuerer & Hamill, 2015; Pic et al., 2025). fps-bench combines these principles on fev-bench tasks (Shchur et al., 2025) to assess temporal dependence and trajectory-dependent quantities alongside marginal accuracy. DYSTS supplies interpretable chaotic systems (Gilpin, 2021), while PANDA studies pretraining across chaotic dynamics (Lai et al., 2026). Together, these evaluations test the value of complete forecast paths through downstream uncertainty, short-term tracking, and long-run dynamical fidelity. A.1 CONCURRENT WORK Time series foundation models are advancing rapidly. t0-gamma alternates temporal and variate attention to incorporate historical and known-future covariates (Meyer et al., 2026). It averages weights from five fine-tuned checkpoints into one 256M-parameter model (The Forecasting Company, 2026). QiYao-M fuses numerical patches with text and image embeddings (Cheng et al., 2026), but treats each target channel independently. EXAONE 1.0 specializes at financial forecasting and mixes time with causal convolutions and variates with groupwise pooling MLPs (Lee et al., 2026). All three models predict marginal quantiles, leaving dependence across future times and variables unspecified. PathFM retains its advantage in directly generating joint multivariate trajectories, com- bining learned temporal and cross-variable dependence with fast sampling and marginal calibration. 14

KSA V2 retains this layer-wise structure while improving both components: C-KSA further compresses the Summary KV used for distant-history access, and S-KSA replaces dense attention over original text with Summary-guided block-sparse attention. 5. Conclusion and Future Work We showed that the Summaries of a summary-token hybrid can serve both as compressible memory and as a routing index. C-KSA compresses Summary KV within each layer without coarsening the Summaries passed between layers, and S-KSA replaces full attention with Summary-guided block- sparse attention over original text. Retrofitted into a pretrained 1.9B Hybrid KSA model, KSA V2 retains its retrieval performance and largely preserves general capabilities, while reducing cache size and improving inference throughput. Additional experiments on a 4B GQA backbone retain near-parent RULER performance, supporting transfer to a larger model with shared KV heads.

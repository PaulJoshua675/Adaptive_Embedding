A.7 FEATURE ANALYSIS OF VGGT-Ω VGGT-Ωjointly processes multiple images, so the representation of an image may depend on the other images included in the same forward pass. This property is relevant to state supervision: an observation-derived target can encode both scene geometry and context-specific variation introduced by the GFM. We therefore examine whether the features of an unchanged image remain stable when only its surrounding context changes. We conduct a controlled diagnostic on all 100 Waymo validation windows. For each window, the same FRONT image at the first future timestamp (t+0.2 s) is retained at the same input index, with its image content, camera, and reference frame fixed. The reference frame is defined by the FRONT image at the observed anchor t. Each timestamp contributes three views. The shortest prefix containing the probed image comprises four observed timestamps and the first future timestamp, giving 5 × 3 = 15 images. The complete diagnostic window contains the same four observed timestamps and seven future timestamps, giving 11 × 3 = 33 images. As shown in Table 8, context dependence is small in the early representation but becomes pro- nounced in deeper layers. Relative feature differences increase from 1.38% at layer 4 to 33.55% and 31.15% at layers 17 and 23, respectively, with a four-layer mean of 19.32%. The raw DPT out- puts differ by 12.08% AbsRel. After expressing both outputs in the anchor-calibrated metric unit, the difference decreases to 3.82%. Although feature relative L2 and depth AbsRel are not directly commensurate, this controlled contrast shows that substantial variation in the VGGT-Ωfeature co- ordinates can coexist with considerably more stable metric geometry. The features therefore carry context-conditioned information that is not uniquely determined by scene geometry, in addition to the geometric information used by the DPT head. This property inevitably affects a state representation built on VGGT-Ωfeatures. The Scene Tok- enizer can suppress part of the context-specific variation, but its observation-derived targets are still constructed from GFM features that are not purely geometric.

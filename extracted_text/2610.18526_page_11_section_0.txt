A Datasets: Languages, Access, and Licensing We accessed all datasets from their public Hugging Face repositories and used them solely for research evaluation, consistent with each dataset’s intended use. Belebele (Bandarkar et al., 2024) is a parallel reading comprehension benchmark with 900 ques- tions per language. We use ten languages spanning high- and mid-resource levels and five scripts: En- glish, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Rus- sian, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, and Japanese. It is released under CC-BY-SA-4.0, which permits re- search use with attribution. XQuAD (Artetxe et al., 2019) is a parallel ques- tion answering benchmark of 240 passages and 1,190 question–answer pairs per language, trans- lated from SQuAD v1.1. We use six languages, again spanning high- and mid-resource levels: En- glish, Spanish, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, and Chi- nese. It is released under CC-BY-SA-4.0, permit- ting research use with attribution.

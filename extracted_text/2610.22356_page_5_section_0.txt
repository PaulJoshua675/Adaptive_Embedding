The semantic kernel retains the backbone-native Q/K/V projections, mRoPE, and causal and padding masks (Wang et al., 2024, Bai et al., 2025b,a); Sℓis its output before the native output projection. Through independent projections, the geometric kernel derives Qg = HℓW ℓ Q,g, Kg = HℓW ℓ K,g, and Vg = HℓW ℓ V,g from the same hidden states and conditions their interactions on aligned geometry. These Q/K/V projections are initialized from the corresponding native projections and have separate trainable parameters. Because J contains only observed visual tokens, a causal mask would make geometric relation computation depend on input order: earlier tokens could not use geometric evidence from observations appearing later in the sequence. We therefore apply full attention over J , allowing each geometry-valid visual token to interact with all input frames; text and geometry-invalid tokens are excluded. Geometric Kernel. We use two forms of GTA (Miyato et al., 2024) to introduce multi-view geometry into attention: Point GTA for relations between scene points and Camera GTA for camera motion across views. We split each projected geometric Q/K/V head into point, camera, and remaining channels, with the two transforms acting on their respective subspaces. For queries, this gives qi = [qpt i ∥qcam i ∥qrem i ], with the same split for keys and values. The remaining channels keep their independently projected features and participate in the same geometric attention.

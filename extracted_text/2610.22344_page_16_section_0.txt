MLE teacher trajectories are generated by GLM-5.2 [42], while scientific experimentation trajectories are generated by GLM-5.2 and Qwen3.8-Max [43]. Retained corpus and sampling. The corpus supplied to MLE-domain SFT contains 636 retained teacher trajectories: 412 MLE trajectories covering 236 tasks and 224 scientific experimentation trajectories covering 77 tasks. Of the scientific trajectories, 143 are generated by GLM-5.2 and 81 by Qwen3.8-Max. We count trajectories by distinct source runs before segmentation and repeated sampling; main-agent segments, initial-solution prefixes, and analysis sub-agent interactions from the same run share a single source-trajectory count. The task counts describe coverage of the retained corpus within the task pools above. Table 7 summarizes the source trajectories and training input records.

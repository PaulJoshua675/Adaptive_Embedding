We study rational synthesis for stochastic multi-agent systems with unknown dynamics, where each agent is equipped with its own probabilistic temporal objective specified using Probabilistic Linear Temporal Logic on Finite Traces (pltl𝑓). We impose solution con- cepts (e.g., Nash equilibrium, subgame-perfect equilibrium) as con- straints on the joint strategy profile, using payoffs derived from each agent’s probability of satisfying its temporal objective. In particular, we formulate per-agent rational synthesis as finding a strategy pro- file – if one exists – such that (i) the designated agent’s probabilistic temporal constraint is satisfied, and (ii) the profile represents a sta- ble solution under a chosen solution concept. We give conditional convergence guarantees for tabular Nash-Q learning, certify sat- isfaction with simultaneous finite-sample confidence bounds, and assess strategic stability with approximate best-response checking. Our approach operates in a stochastic Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) environment, where model-free learning algo- rithms attempt to attain extremal satisfaction probabilities for tem- poral objectives automatically compiled into reward automata. Our approach is empirically validated using 8 general-sum concurrent stochastic games.

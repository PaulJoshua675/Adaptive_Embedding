Robotic Manipulation Benchmark. Many benchmarks have been developed to evaluate different aspects of robotic manipulation, including cross-embodiment generalization [7, 32, 33], generalized manipulation [26, 37, 39], long-horizon reasoning [27, 28, 31, 34] [40, 41], and sim-to-real trans- fer [25, 29, 30]. Among them, LIBERO [23] has emerged as a widely adopted benchmark due to its high-quality demonstrations and standardized data generation pipeline, primarily focusing on lifelong learning and task generalization. However, existing benchmarks largely focus on task completion, without explicitly modeling user-specific preferences. In contrast, our LIBERO-PeRM targets person- alized manipulation with preference-aware task suites that disentangle task success from preference satisfaction.

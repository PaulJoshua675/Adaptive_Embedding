For our method, to satisfy the VAE temporal constraint, we infer 65 frames and discard the last frame before aligning to and evaluating on GT, yielding T = 64. We keep the query frame in its raw resolution and set the encoder frame to have the same aspect ratio with resolution bounded within 480 × 832. For SpatialTrackerV2 (STv2) (Xiao et al., 2025), we choose their Type I - Monocular RGB model, i.e., RGB inputs go through its own Front model, which predicts the initial depth and cameras, and Offline model, which jointly refines the point tracks and camera poses, without GT intrinsics or extrinsics. For OpenD4RT (Li & Team, 2026), we choose their ‘OpenD4RT 48CLIP 9Mix NoCropAUG’ checkpoint, which can infer at most 48 frames at a time. For the frames beyond 48, following its published anchor-clip evaluation code, we fix the first frame in every at-most-48-frame input clip and shift the remaining frames to include each target frame. For the remaining methods, we directly follow their official inference guidelines. D.2 VIDEO DEPTH ESTIMATION Metric Evaluation. We follow the protocol of MonST3R (Zhang et al., 2025a) by applying dataset- specific validity ranges when computing depth metrics: For Sintel, evaluation is applied to pixels with GT depth 0 < dGT < 70 m. After aligning the predicted depth to the GT, predicted depths are additionally clipped to a maximum of 70 m before metric computation. For Bonn, evaluation is also applied to pixels with GT depth 0 < dGT < 70 m, but we do not clip the aligned predictions. For KITTI, no maximum-depth threshold is applied, and all valid GT depth pixels are used for evaluation. We distinguish two types of alignment.

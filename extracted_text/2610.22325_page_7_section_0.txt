Figure 3 shows our tracks and reference-time HPM unions, with further examples in Fig. 15. 4.3 AMODAL 3D LOCALISATION ON 4D-DRESS Localising observations at a common reference time requires predictions for regions that may be hidden in the reference image. We introduce an amodal 3D localisation evaluation on 4D- DRESS (Wang et al., 2024b), whose synchronised images and scans let us test whether points observed across time agree with the person’s full reference-time geometry. Because the scan covers regions hidden in the reference image, observations revealed later in the video can also be evaluated in the reference pose.

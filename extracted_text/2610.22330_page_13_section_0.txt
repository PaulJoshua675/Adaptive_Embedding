Entropy denotes mean per-sample unigram entropy in nats, computed on the same truncated, non-padding GPT-2 token sequences, including their first tokens. Samples are weighted equally, not pooled into a corpus histogram (Appendix D). This lexical statistic does not establish semantic diversity or distributional coverage, so a lower GenPPL is not by itself evidence of better overall generation quality. 4.2.

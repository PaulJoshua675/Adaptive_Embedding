Bidding Network Architecture Across all domains, agents share a unified JAX/Flax bidding architecture composed of a shared state- encoding trunk followed by algorithm-specific output heads. Across all domains, agents share a unified JAX/Flax bidding architecture composed of a state-encoding trunk followed by algorithm-specific output heads. In the synthetic Translation domain, the trunk is a single-hidden-layer MLP (64 hidden units with ReLU activations) operating on the concatenated 64-dimensional state embeddings. In the LLM domains paired with a discrete market assessor (score_lookup mode, which rates candidate utility on a 0–10 scale), the trunk reduces to a direct 12-state tabular lookup (11 one-hot score levels plus an ownership indicator), parameterized via cumulative softplus deltas (monotonic mode) to enforce monotonically increasing valuations 𝜇[𝑠] ≤𝜇[𝑠+ 1]. From this representation, output heads branch according to the learning algorithm: • 2 Heads (Oracle / Thompson Sampling / Pure-𝑄): predicts expected downstream valuation 𝜇∈ℝ+ (via softplus activation) and log-uncertainty log 𝜎∈ℝ. • 3 Heads (Mirror Descent): adds a deterministic bid-shading head 𝑐∈(0, 1) via a sigmoid activation, producing multiplicative bids 𝑏= 𝑐· 𝜇. • 4 Heads (PPO): replaces deterministic shading with two stochastic policy heads (𝛼, 𝛽) ∈[1, ∞)2 (via softplus(·) + 1) that parameterize a unimodal Beta(𝛼, 𝛽) shading distribution.

Evaluation uses vLLM [25]. Prior capability is the average of GPQA-Diamond, MMLU-Pro, GSM8K, HumanEval, and IFEval; in-domain accuracy uses the task’s native answer extractor. For the Olmo-3 medical study, open-ended accuracy is graded with the Bedrock model identifier us.anthropic.claude-sonnet-4-6. The judge receives the question, reference answer, and full model response and returns CORRECT or INCORRECT. The fabrication metric is a deterministic lower bound that flags explicit claims of an absent source, including “reference solution,” “provided answer,” and “based on the feedback.” None of the 1,000 evaluation prompts contains a trigger phrase. 31

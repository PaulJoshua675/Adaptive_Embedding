Table 5 reports the correlation be- tween each operator’s score and actual task resolution, mea- sured across all WEAVE-Bench tasks evaluated by 8 models × 2 scaffolds (4,800 agent runs). The min-bottleneck operator achieves the highest corre- lation with Resolved and, by construction, eliminates all phantom credit. The mean-parent operator permits resid- ual phantom credit (3.2 pp) because it averages rather than gates upstream deficiencies; the residual is modest only because WEAVE-Bench is chain-dominated (median anti- chain width 1), and the two operators coincide exactly at single-parent nodes. The depth-weighted operator penalizes deep nodes regardless of actual upstream completion, reduc- ing its diagnostic fidelity. These results justify the conserva- tive min-bottleneck design.

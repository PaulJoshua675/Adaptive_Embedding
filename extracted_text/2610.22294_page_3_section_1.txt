Few-shot prompting. Few-shot prompting specifies tasks through demonstrations (Brown et al., 2020), but mappings and placement can affect model behavior (Li et al., 2026; Min et al., 2022; Wei et al., 2023). Applications include information extraction and semantic parsing (Pang et al., 2023; Shin and Van Durme, 2022), as well as programming by example and code editing, where demonstrations or prior edits guide subsequent transformations (Gulwani, 2011; Gupta et al., 2023; Meng et al., 2011; Wei et al., 2024). Prior work also explores batching multiple inputs into a single prompt, allowing their answers to be generated in one model call (Cheng et al., 2023; Lin et al., 2024). We instead study joint infilling of multiple unresolved regions in place within a shared canvas containing demonstrations and surrounding context. Test-time guidance. Beyond few-shot prompting, various test-time guidance methods have been developed to steer model generation. Contrastive decoding compares predictions from different models (Li et al., 2023; O’Brien and Lewis, 2023), while context-aware methods compare predictions under different conditioning contexts to strengthen the influence of supplied evidence or demonstrations (Peng et al., 2025; Shi et al., 2024b; Song et al., 2025; Whitammer et al., 2022). In diffusion models, classifier-free guidance adjusts the influence of conditioning information (Ho and Salimans, 2021), while KL-regularized reward tilting (Korbak et al., 2022; Rafailov et al., 2023) and inference-time resampling (Singhal et al., 2025) provide mechanisms for reweighting candidate generations. However, extending likelihood-based comparisons to complete DLM infills requires tractable sequence-level likelihoods under a specified decoding procedure. Diffusion language models. Masked diffusion language models iteratively unmask positions within a shared canvas, supporting flexible generation orders and parallel decoding (Austin et al., 2021; Sahoo et al., 2024; Shi et al., 2024a). Recent models include LLaDA (Nie et al., 2025), Dream (Ye et al., 2025), Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion (Fu et al., 2026), and dQwen3.5 (Xue et al., 2026), alongside diffusion-augmented autoregressive approaches such as Uno (Sahoo et al., 2026). Standard masked infilling also requires allocating output lengths in advance, motivating work on flexible-length generation and length prediction (Ding et al., 2026; Xu et al., 2026a; Zhang et al., 2025). Prior work shows that decoding strategies affect generation quality and efficiency (Arriola et al., 2025; Kim et al., 2026; Wu et al., 2026), while the tractability of complete-sequence likelihoods depends on the decoding procedure (Turok et al., 2026). 3

A.6 Prompt Design A.6.1 Prompt for Annotation Pipeline In Figs. 7, 8, and 9, we present the prompts adopted in the audiovisual captioning data annotation pipeline introduced in Sec. 3.1.1. In this process, we first prompt Gemini-2.5-Pro to refine the initial audiovisual captions in terms of cross-modal synchronization and event attribution, and to extract timestamped audiovisual event tuples in parallel. We then employ GPT-4.1 to conduct event hit verification for data quality control. A.6.2 Prompt for Reward Implementation Fig. 10 illustrates the prompt used to extract audiovisual events from audiovisual captions, facilitating the construction of reference structures. Furthermore, Figs. 11, 12 and 13 list the prompts used, respectively, for the judgement of global event completeness reward, intra-event attribution reward and inter-event synchronization reward. All these prompts are carefully designed to standardize LLM-based reward judgement and ensure consistent evaluation across all samples. A.7 Broader Impact EVA-Cap aims to advance audiovisual video captioning by generating more faithful, comprehensive, and temporally coherent descriptions of videos. This can benefit various downstream applications that rely on detailed and accurate video understanding. Better alignment between audio and visual events may help reduce misleading descriptions in scenarios where both modalities are necessary for understanding the video content. At the same time, improved audiovisual captioning systems may introduce potential risks if deployed without appropriate safeguards. First, generated captions may still contain omissions, hallucinations, incorrect speaker attributions, or inaccurate audio-visual temporal relations. Such errors could mislead users if the captions are treated as factual summaries. Second, automatic captioning of user-generated videos may raise privacy concerns when videos contain identifiable people, sensitive conversations, or private environments. Third, high-quality video descriptions could be misused for large-scale surveillance, profiling, or automated indexing of sensitive content. 19

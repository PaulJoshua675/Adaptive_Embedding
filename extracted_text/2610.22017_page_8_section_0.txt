Latency is the mean wall-clock time per query on Nvidia H200 GPUs (Qwen3.8-27B served with vLLM).∗CARA version: λ = 0.5, α = 0.5. 5.5 Efficiency and Exploration Behavior Table 4 compares the computational efficiency and exploration behavior of CARA and ARK across three datasets. Interestingly, CARA achieves more focused graph exploration despite introducing an additional causal reasoning module. Compared with ARK (the closest agentic baseline), CARA reduces GlobalSearch calls as well as Neighbors calls across the datasets. The number of examined nodes also decreases for the three datasets. These results suggest that causal relevance assessment helps agents identify promising evidence paths while avoiding unnecessary exploration. Compared with AMAZON whose questions are more entity-oriented, MAG and PRIME involve more relation-intensive biomedical questions that require deeper multi-hop exploration. This differ- ence is reflected in CARA’s substantially higher number of Neighbors calls and therefore causal reasoning calls per query, suggesting that causal guidance is particularly important for navigating complex relational structures. However, the addi- tional causal reasoning introduces latency overhead. The particularly pronounced overhead on MAG and PRIME highlights a trade-off between retrieval effectiveness and computational efficiency, suggest- ing opportunities for reducing latency through par- allel or batched causal assessments. 6

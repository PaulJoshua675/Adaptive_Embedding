We also strip license and auto-generated headers, and drop repositories above 2M tokens or those that lose most of their files in deduplication. Tokens are measured with the model tokenizer. A.3 DIFF DATA Multi-file edits come from merged pull requests in the Issue–PR corpus, with the full patch and every changed file before the change. Code-evolution documents come from the full git histories of 6,550 permissively licensed repositories from the same corpus, chosen to match the language mix of SWE- bench Multilingual. We keep only patches that apply exactly and histories that replay byte for byte. In multi-file edits, the loss covers the original files, the diffs, and the changed lines of the new files with two lines of context. In code-evolution documents, it covers the commit messages, diffs, and final state, and license blocks are masked in both.

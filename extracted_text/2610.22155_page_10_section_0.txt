First, effective history representations or retrieval methods are required to manage context while preserving temporal information (Zeng et al., 2025), keeping inputs within the context bud- get without sacrificing critical details. Second, robust strategies are needed to address observation backlogs caused by inference delays, which can render planned actions obsolete before execution. These strategies must determine how many frames to retain, whether to employ FIFO or alterna- tive buffer-management policies (Yates et al., 2021; Li et al., 2020), and how to dynamically decide whether to discard or revise planned actions as inference becomes stale (Black et al., 2025).

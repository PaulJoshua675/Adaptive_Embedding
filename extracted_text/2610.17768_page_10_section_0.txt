The target of sentence i is the rank fraction, within the page, of ci + ti, where ci is its reader count and ti ∈[0, 1) the rank fraction of the teacher’s score or of position, so the tie-breaker orders sentences only within the same count. The loss is 0.5 times the squared error on the standardised target plus 0.5 times the Kullback–Leibler divergence between the softmax of the target and that of the prediction at temperature 0.1. AdamW at 2 × 10−5 when continuing the distilled start and 10−4 for a fresh adapter; gradients are accumulated over 8 pages. A run takes a fixed budget of 2,457 page steps on the training pages (306 updates, 304 on the quarter of the pages; August’s three passes over its 819 pages), after 6,320 steps on the fresh pages for the reader-only recipe (two passes, 790 updates). The page order is shuffled once per seed, the model is the last step (validation is recorded, not used to choose), and every arm trained here has two seeds, on one NVIDIA H100. Qwen3-0.6B and Qwen3-1.7B (Table 3) use the settings of the 3B reader-only model. The equal-steps control (Section 4) is the 3B reader-only model’s command with the first stage reading the 819 training pages instead of the fresh ones. The August-recipe rows of Table 3 are the stored sentence scores of the earlier comparison’s runs, one each, made with the August model’s two stages; the 8B’s distilled start is S3, and ModernBERT-large, an encoder, was fully fine-tuned, from a distilled start trained for eight passes at 10−4. Pages and readers. A reader’s highlights are mapped onto the sentences they cover, and a sentence’s count is the number of readers who marked it. Of the 819 training pages, 18 kept no reader whose highlights still anchor to the stored text after re-extraction, so the runs on the re-extracted readers use the other 801 (a subset, the part of them it holds), while the August recipe re-run uses the August readers of all 819; the teachers were collected for all 819.

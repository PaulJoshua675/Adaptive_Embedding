As elaborated in the subsequent dynamic representation reconstruction section, cold-start items obtain an approximate collaborative projection via this semantic mapping. 4.2 Decoupled Two-stage Optimization Graph neural networks inevitably suffer from the over-smoothing effect during message passing. If topological updates and semantic alignment are jointly optimized in an end-to-end framework, the dominant gradients from the highly sparse graph reconstruction can easily over- whelm the low-frequency alignment signals, thereby disrupting the inherent representations of the static semantic space. To isolate this gradient interference and ensure that the original rec- ommendation accuracy for warm items is not compromised by the mapping network, DualGCN adopts a decoupled two-stage training paradigm. The primary objective of the first stage is to construct a robust collaborative reference frame.

D REPEATED-SAMPLE VALIDATION: SAMPLING DISTRIBUTION, COVERAGE, AND CALIBRATION The interval in the main text is a query bootstrap from a single distractor sample; the stronger test resamples the distractors. We partition each backbone’s 99M pool into K=20 disjoint, pool-spread blocks at a given sample fraction and predict mAP@100M on each—20 genuinely disjoint draws (not a single-sample bootstrap; disjoint blocks of a finite pool are negatively dependent under the finite-population constraint), using the paper’s estimator (T=4000, q=0.95, ξ ≤0). Coverage at the 5% operating point (Tab. 9). Over the 20 disjoint 5% draws per backbone the mean MAE is 0.005 (0.002–0.008 across backbones), the draw-to-draw standard deviation is 0.003—matching the nested-bootstrap σsamp+fit of App. C—and the worst single error is 0.013. Ev- ery draw lands inside the reported query-bootstrap interval: coverage is 100/100 (all 100 backbone- draws). The interval is, if anything, conservative—its half-width is set by the larger query-sampling uncertainty, whereas the distractor-sample spread measured directly here is only ≈0.003.

Complementary strengths of simulator-ready 3D code. Table 18 and Figures 15–16 clarify the structural advantage of programmatic 3D assets for embodied simulation. While a static last-frame baseline trivially preserves initial contours (12/12) by predicting zero motion, pixel-space world models synthesize motion directly in latent space at the cost of frequent contour distortion across random seeds. By contrast, compiling generated 3D code into a physics simulator combines both properties: the simulator enforces exact rigid-body transformations, joint axes, and articulated state transitions over time while maintaining crisp, view-consistent geometry across contact push, hold, and retreat controls. 33

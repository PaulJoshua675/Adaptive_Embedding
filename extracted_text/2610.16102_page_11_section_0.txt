And the regime that would make every step exactly inspectable—full rescue—pins CR1 at 1.0 and dissolves the preference (2026a; 2026d). The companion paper on Minsky’s Emotion Machine states the general form: audit and valuation are one circuit at opposite settings of one parameter (2026f). Repair every collision and the trace is exactly auditable but no path outranks another; tolerate collisions and the CR2 decay that credits direct routes makes the read approximate. We name this audit–valuation complementarity and propose it as the formal counterpart of Polanyi’s claim that tacit and explicit knowledge are not sharply divided: one substrate, one dial, two readouts that cannot both be at full strength. The core of the trade-off can be stated exactly. Lemma 1 (No valuation without imperfection). Because CR2(n) = CR2(n−1)·CR1(n− 1), with CR2 starting at 1, a regime that repairs every collision, so that CR1 = 1 at every step, assigns CR2 = 1 to every path, and CR2 cannot rank candidates. Conversely, two candidates can receive different CR2 only if CR1 < 1 at some step of at least one of their paths. In the traversal of 2026d, where queries are exact stored vectors, CR1 < 1 arises from collided blocks abstaining, that is, from tolerated collisions. Conjecture 1 (Monotone complementarity). At fixed memory depth, the dispersion of CR2 among candidates inside the frontier is non-increasing in the rescue rate RR, and it is zero at RR = 1 by Lemma 1. The lemma is elementary, and that is its point: the valuation exists only because of the imperfection that a full audit removes. The conjecture is what §9 proposes to measure. One qualification keeps the claim honest. The coarse historical structures—the Leibnizian hourglass, the Islamic convergence, the Monastery Wall—survive in both modes almost bit- identically; the modes differ in the ranking of candidates inside the frontier, which is exactly where CR2 adds semantic content (2026d, Appendix J). Explicit structure is robust to the dial. Tacit valuation is fragile to explicitation. Whether valuation contrast falls monotonically as RR rises from 0 to 1 has not been measured, and we list it as a prediction in §9. 11

Newton’s influence travelled abundantly through texts, and nothing here measures it. But the apprenticeship channel is exactly the channel of tacit knowledge, so the finding is about the right thing. Alongside classical cases like Newton, Thurston recorded a similar human precedent (Thurston 1994). After his classification theorem for foliations, the field began to empty within a couple of years. His papers explained little of the background they presupposed and set down only the key points, so the barrier to entry was high: many graduate students and mathematicians struggled to learn and understand the proofs of key theorems and left the field. He concluded that “more than the knowledge, people want personal understanding.” The follow-up gap is therefore not new; what is new is the rate at which answers can now be produced without anyone acquiring the understanding that used to come with them. The AI-solved results Tao describes are the limiting case: possession without any human possessor, and therefore without the apprenticeship edges along which understanding normally flows (Tao 2026a). Leibniz, Thurston and Tao’s observation are one structure seen at three densities: a result whose transmission channel is thin or absent becomes a node with few outgoing edges, however valuable its content. 6.4 The Monastery Wall as a change of medium Every traversal stops at the 57th generation, at Lanfranc of Canterbury (1005–1089), who taught at Bec, or at Ivo of Chartres (1040–1116), whose own teachers are unrecorded (2026d). Before them, education was organized in monastic and cathedral schools as a communal affair, and such channels produced no structured mentor–student records; the Wall marks where personal academic mentorship first became record-generating in Europe (2026d; 2026g). In Collins’s terms the Wall is a shift in the medium of tacit transmission, from a communal medium close to collective tacit knowledge to a dyadic medium of apprenticeship. It is not an absence of transmission. It is the point at which transmission changed form and began to leave the kind of trace our instrument can read. 6.5 Is a second wall forming? If the follow-up gap widens, a future prosopography of twenty-first-century science could meet a boundary of a new kind. Results would be attached to nodes that have few or no human apprentice edges, because what was learned in producing them was learned by no person. We call this hypothetical boundary an AI wall. Unlike the Monastery Wall, it would not mark a change from one human medium to another, but a channel in which no human holds the tacit root. Section 9 gives a measurable signature.

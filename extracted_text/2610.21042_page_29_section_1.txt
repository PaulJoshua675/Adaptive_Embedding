A record is relevant when its own work reports quantitative human outbreak characteristics or estimates, models Lassa transmission, reports seroprevalence or statistically measured risk factors in humans, or reports a specified transmission or vector parameter in animals. A background mention, comparison, or example alone does not make a study about Lassa.

RCER improves F1 over direct selection by 3.38 points on two-hop questions and 8.55 on four-hop questions. It also retains all annotated support more often than either control at every depth. Independently covering question requirements can overlook dependencies between the passages that satisfy them. Joint analysis considers these dependencies when constructing the evidence set, helping preserve complementary support as reasoning chains grow. How do inferences aid composition? With passages and order fixed, Table 3 shows gains of 8.47 F1 points on 2WikiQA and 4.19 on MuSiQue from candidate-pool analysis and guidance. On 2WikiQA, 983 of 1,000 questions already retain all annotated supporting passages. They account for at least 6.77 points of the overall F1 gain. The remaining 17 can contribute at most 1.70 points. The gain therefore extends to questions with complete annotated support. Inferences align entities and specify how their relations compose into the target relation, connecting premises that passage coverage alone leaves implicit.

Impact of Input-Dependent Neuronal Parameters. Ta- ble 4 shows the contribution of input-dependent neuronal parameters. We remove the parameter generator and replace per-step τi and Ri with learnable scalars shared across all steps. Accuracy drops from 91.25% to 80.66% on sequen- tial CIFAR10 and from 66.59% to 54.52% on sequential CIFAR100. These results confirm that input-dependent pa- rameter generation can significantly improve neuron perfor- mance.

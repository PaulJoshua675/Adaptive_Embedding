Masked diffusion models (MDMs) have emerged as a competitive alternative to autoregressive models for discrete data such as text (Austin et al., 2021; Lou et al., 2023; Sahoo et al., 2024; Shi et al., 2024; Ou et al., 2025), and have recently been trained as large language models with billions of parameters (Nie et al., 2026). An MDM generates a sequence of length L by starting from the all-mask state and repeatedly revealing masked positions. At each step, the trained network, called the score function, is queried once and returns the conditional distribution of every masked token given the revealed ones. If only one token is revealed per query, the sampler reproduces the data distribution when the score function is accurate, but generating a sequence then takes L queries.

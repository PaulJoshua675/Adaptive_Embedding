We measure the order of nonlinearity of the programs evolution produces and show that it tracks what the learner can absorb, not what the target contains. We show that a control target of lower order gains nothing from an expressive learner, separating difficulty from decomposability. And we show that learner capacity and genetic assimilation stand in opposition, which reverses the usual reading of the Baldwin effect in this setting. 2

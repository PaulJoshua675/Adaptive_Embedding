Domain rules for this review: Zika virus is the subject of a study when its own data, analysis or model concerns Zika virus; a background mention, comparison or citation alone does not qualify. A quantitative result is a count, measurement, rate, estimate or statistical measure reported for an outcome or parameter named in the criteria. A possible risk factor discussed without statistical measures of association does not qualify. For animal and vector studies, the eligible parameters are those specified in the criteria: R0, Rt, Re, r, growth rate, mutation rate, extrinsic incubation period, vector reproduction number, vector competence and mosquito delays. When no abstract is available, use the title and retain the record unless the title itself establishes an exclusion. For a paper without full text, the abstract step appends a note to the system prompt and sends the title and abstract in place of the paper text.

Image editing models produce impressive results for indi- vidual edits, but their quality often deteriorates over multi- ple editing turns. Each editing turn introduces small distri- bution shifts that accumulate over successive edits, creating a gap between the input distributions encountered during the editor’s original training and multi-turn inference. We introduce a training scheme that makes multi-turn image editing stable under this accumulating distribution shift. A key is to force the editor to operate on its own outputs from previous editing turns during training, bringing the princi- ple of Self-Forcing to image editing. The main challenge is that self-generated editing trajectories deviate from ground- truth trajectories, making direct supervision unreliable. We address this challenge through a distillation objective that leverages a teacher image editor to provide a reliable train- ing signal when the model operates on its own previous outputs.

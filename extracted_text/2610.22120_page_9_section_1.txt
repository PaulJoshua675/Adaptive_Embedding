We presented a hierarchical framework for reconstructing environmental geometry from wireless channel measurements by combining physics-grounded representation learning with joint generative posterior inference. A wireless foundation model first extracts scene-relevant information from individual observations, while a diffusion model jointly integrates these representations to infer the posterior over scene occupancy, from which a per-cell consensus over sampled scenes recovers the building footprints. Results demonstrate our framework can recover previously unseen scene structures from communication signals, and controlled comparisons show the importance of our hierarchical design for effectively solving the underlying ill-posed inverse problem. The results highlight the potential of existing wireless infrastructure to serve for environmental perception. 9

Neural networks (NNs) offer adaptability for con- trolling complex systems where nominal modeling proves intractable (Narendra & Parthasarathy, 1990). Experiments with legged robots, includ- ing humanoids (Lippi, 2018; Wensing et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024) and lower- limb exoskeletons (Belal et al., 2024; Lhoste et al., 2025; Lippi et al., 2021; Mamou & Saadia, 2014) demonstrate this capability. However, physi- cal closed-loop deployment degrades the offline- validated performance of these ML systems. In both disturbance compensation (Lippi, 2018) and gait segmentation (Lippi et al., 2021), this degra- dation required continuous online learning to re- cover functionality. These experimental results imply that deploy- ing the NN alters the system dynamics it origi- nally mapped: such distributional shift degrades network performance.

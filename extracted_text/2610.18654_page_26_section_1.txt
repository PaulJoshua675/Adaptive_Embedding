Our evaluation covers 1.9B MHA and 4B GQA backbones with contexts up to 128K. General- capability evaluations and efficiency measurements are limited to 1.9B; the 4B validation covers RULER retrieval. Future work will explore longer contexts and larger models, including whether additional compression levels can provide further savings while preserving useful information. We will also explore how to allocate compression factors across layers. Evaluating long-document understanding and multi-step reasoning will also help establish how well the observed retrieval performance transfers to broader long-context tasks. 26

F Scope and Release The benchmark evaluates task-specific evidence from finite views, marked regions, reference compar- isons, and animations. Material labels concern visible appearance, and affordance and grasp labels follow the source task rules. Fine-grained Geometry covers thirty objects; Motion Understanding includes both video and frame-sheet inputs. The text-only explanation Judge measures reference agreement, while targeted human inspection checks two specific defect sets. The recursive study uses human feedback and matches output counts rather than computation or human effort. We focus on evaluating verification performance and introducing a structured training resource to support downstream model optimization, which opens rich avenues for future work to explore verifier training. The release contains two distinct resources: the 2,581-example, ten-task evaluation benchmark and a 14,126-example, seven-task source-disjoint Training Resource. The Training Resource contains 4,011 source groups, 5,660 True anchors, and 8,466 False examples; benchmark source groups and all descendants are excluded, yielding zero benchmark–training source-group overlap. Released manifests retain source indices and pair/triplet links so that counts, grouping, and linkage structure can be recomputed. Source assets and annotations remain subject to their upstream licenses.

The stored step positions come from tokenizing each step on its own, and the hidden states were extracted in left-padded batches without shifting those positions. Each read therefore lies earlier than the step’s last token by the padding width of its trace, at most 512 tokens. For the final step, the read lies inside the intended step for 51.0% of traces at 4B and 51.6% at 8B. Tokenizing the steps separately adds a smaller drift, with a median of 3 tokens over 820 step reads in a 40-trace 4B sample. No result was re-scored with corrected positions.

Each setting uses three agents with complementary roles. Benchmarks and Models. (1) Embodied crafting: three Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct agents (Logger, Miner, and Smith) specialize in gathering wood, mining resources, and crafting. Starting from an empty inventory in Minecraft MineStudio (Cai et al., 2025), they must progress through the crafting chain to obtain an iron pickaxe. Agents receive RGB observations and textual state information, including inventory and recent interaction history. The selected agent issues a language instruction to a frozen STEVE-1 controller (Lifshitz et al., 2023), which executes it for 40 game frames. (2) Robot navigation: three NaVILA-8B agents (Cheng et al., 2025) perform continuous vision-and- language navigation on VLN-CE R2R in Habitat (Krantz et al., 2020; Anderson et al., 2018; Savva et al., 2019). Given a route instruction and an eight-frame window of egocentric observations, the agents coordinate through partitioned action spaces: Turner controls rotations, Mover controls forward movement, and Stopper decides when to stop. Episodes contain at most 64 decisions. (3) Deep research: three gemma-4-E2B-it agents (Team et al., 2026) answer questions by brows- ing a searchable document corpus. Searcher, Opener, and Finder specialize in browser.search, browser.open, and browser.find; all agents share the interaction history and may submit a final answer. We use OpenResearcher (Li et al., 2026) for training. Episodes contain at most 64 decisions and receive a binary terminal reward based on answer correctness, assessed against the reference answer by a qwen3.5-flash judge. Training Setup. All agents have independent model parameters and undergo a supervised fine- tuning (SFT) cold start before post-training. Each agent in MPT is fine-tuned on demonstrations corresponding to its role. We compare MPT against a monolithic REINFORCE baseline with a discount factor of γ = 0.95. During the SFT cold start, the baseline is trained on the combined demonstrations from all roles, with total SFT training compute matched between the two settings.

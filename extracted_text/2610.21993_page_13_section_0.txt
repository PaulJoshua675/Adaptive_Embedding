On dense prediction tasks, ConvNeXt-RS remains competitive with all frozen ViT baselines on DFC2020-S1 and exceeds all frozen ViT baselines on Flood-S1 (78.38±0.12 vs. 77.7±0.0 for Copernicus-FM), demon- strating that SparK-SAR pretraining produces spatially rich representations even without task- specific adaptation. Returning to the finetuned comparison, the same pattern holds against the other three frozen ViT baselines, all of which have substantially more parameters than our encoder: our finetuned model beats CROMA and DOFA on every task, and beats SoftCon on three of the four (EuroSAT- S1, DFC2020-S1, Flood-S1). BigEarthNet-S1 is the sole task where a frozen baseline, SoftCon at 78.7±0.0%, comes out ahead, for the reasons discussed above.

Multi-head attention (MHA) is a fundamental component of Transformer language models, with separate query, key, and value projections for each head (Vaswani et al., 2017). Full attention provides direct access to preceding tokens, but its computation during training and prefill grows quadratically with sequence length, while its KV cache grows linearly during autoregressive decoding. FlashAttention reduces memory traffic for exact attention (Dao et al., 2022), while PagedAttention improves KV-cache allocation and sharing (Kwon et al., 2023). These system-level optimizations complement research on memory compression, sparse attention, and hybrid architectures. 4.1.

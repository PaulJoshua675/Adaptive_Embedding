Let Ln be the mean per-token negative log-likelihood of item n’s target tokens (a factual answer or the second half of a text window) given the preceding tokens. Define Fi = 1 N P n(∂Ln/∂wi)2 for weight wi in the rotated full-precision model. The saliency of unit u is Su = X i∈u Fi δ2 i , δ = w −a⋆t, (3) where δ is the residual of the exact one-plane fit in Eq. 1, which the second plane targets. The score uses observed-target gradients as a sensitivity proxy, not an exact estimate of recoverable loss. We rank units of both roles across all layers and matrix kinds by Su and give the top K a second plane, testing this common ranking in Section 4.3. The number K of selected units sets the rate in unit-sized steps from 1.590 to 3.179 bpw (two planes everywhere) with the same kernel; we set it from a target of 1.994 bpw for post-trained models and 2.156 for Base (the INT2-g128 rate), or from a baseline’s rate. The items used to accumulate the Fisher determine its sensitivity. C4 calibration windows yield a text Fisher; 720 factual-recall items (MMLU-style multiple choice, TriviaQA cloze and ARC; Hendrycks et al., 2021; Joshi et al., 2017; Clark et al., 2018), rendered through the chat template and drawn with a seed disjoint from our in-house probe, yield a knowledge Fisher. The ranking selects units, not weights, so these items decide where a plane goes but never what it stores. We independently vary the item set and the scoring checkpoint (the Base model or its post-trained sib- ling). We report WikiText2 perplexity, the average of seven zero-shot tasks (PIQA, ARC- Easy, ARC-Challenge, HellaSwag, WinoGrande, BoolQ and OpenBookQA; Bisk et al., 2020; Clark et al., 2018; Zellers et al., 2019; Sakaguchi et al., 2020; Clark et al., 2019; Mihaylov et al., 2018) under lm-eval (Gao et al., 2026), and five-shot MMLU (Hendrycks et al., 2021). These measures capture complementary effects: modest perplexity changes can accompany substantial losses in factual knowledge (Jaiswal et al., 2024), which perplexity measures only coarsely and quantization degrades on its own axis (Namburi et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2026a), while matching aggregate scores can conceal disagreement on individual predictions (Dutta et al., 2024).

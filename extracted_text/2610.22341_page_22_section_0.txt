The catalogue indexes 269,597 unique executable programs across nine collections (Table 5), spanning parametric CAD, procedural Blender Python, OpenSCAD, articulated URDF mechanisms, Three.js scenes, and GLSL shaders. The benchmark training pairs (768,596 pairs, 891 M tokens) are deduplicated prompt–code splits, including web-animation pro- grams, built for the fine-tuning experiments of §5.2 and Appendix C.1; they strictly exclude all 3DCodeBench procedural factories, seeded instances, and per-dialect evaluation suites. The unified text export (2,028,286 rows across 342 shards) expands each curated program across its three caption views (visual description, instruction, and factory recipe) and decomposes multi-pass shaders into pass-level targets: 891,788 native-format rows, 848,819 standardized text-export rows, and 287,679 pass-flattened shader rows. B.2 SOURCES AND CONVERSION PIPELINES DeepCAD to CadQuery. DeepCAD provides 178,238 raw sketch–extrusion command sequences rather than executable programs (Wu et al., 2021). Our deterministic compiler translates each com- mand sequence into a self-contained CadQuery script: 174,981 sequences compile (98.2%), 174,177 produce valid watertight solids (97.7%), and vector-level hashing removes 25.8% exact geometric duplicates to yield 128,201 unique executable programs (71.9% of raw sequences). Geometric fidelity against the original solids, measured via voxel IoU at 483 resolution, averages 0.985, with 96.9% of programs exceeding 0.90 (the remaining 1,114 programs are annotated with explicit fidelity metadata so downstream pipelines can apply custom thresholds). Natural-language annotations from Text2CAD (Khan et al., 2024) are paired with 125,162 programs under their original license terms, accompanied by four canonical views per solid. Crucially, because the upstream DeepCAD split contains 3,230 duplicate geometry groups straddling its train and test partitions, our vector-and-mesh deduplication eliminates cross-split leakage across the CAD lineage. Shadertoy. The procedural graphics collection comprises 119,961 Shadertoy shaders—98,164 single-pass and 21,585 multi-pass programs—indexed with one canonical record per shader (Quilez & Jeremias, 2013). Each record stores the primary image pass as the indexed entry file alongside the complete multi-pass buffer source tree, multi-frame render grids, video clips, and bound input textures. For text-to-code training, multi-pass shaders are additionally expanded by prepending shared common headers to each buffer pass (287,679 pass-level rows); automated syntax and uniform repair across 588 shaders lifts the headless compilation rate of the flattened set from 84.6% to 99.0% (92,534 of 93,434), with only compiled shaders admitted to training mixtures. 22

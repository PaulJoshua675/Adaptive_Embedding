It is included only at evaluation time as a held-out framework and is the strongest open-source community memory baseline in this evaluation. Hy- Memory-30B-A3B reaches 56.89, compared with 55.47 for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, 54.04 for Opus 5, and 52.30 for Hy3. This result separates model-level memory-writing skill from memorization of the training harness protocols. 8.4 Future ablations and audits This report does not include controlled ablations of chunk-level versus window-level GRPO, gold-fact attribution versus broadcast ORM, positive probes versus probes plus traps, the bank-growth penalty, binary versus rubric-based rewards, RL initialization from OPD versus generic SFT, or single-harness versus mixed-harness training. We leave these comparisons to future work. Additional reward-data audits should measure human–judge agreement, sensitivity to the judge model and prompt, score distributions across synthetic answer types, separation between strong and weak memory writers, and the fraction of zero-variance rollout groups before and after reward decomposition. 9 Engineering Lessons: Silent Failures Several failures looked healthy until an invariant was checked. A bank that did not persist across windows still left files on disk and produced nonzero rewards; the check is to reopen a checkpointed bank and verify a known record before training. A teacher that returned no top-k values still returned successful requests, but OPD gradients were zero; missing teacher fields must raise, and teacher-token coverage must be monitored.

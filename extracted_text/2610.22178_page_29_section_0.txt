D.4 INFERENCE LATENCY OF THE COMPARED METHODS In §6.3, each model is timed with its released checkpoint and inference code on one H100 (driver 570, CUDA 12.8), one model per process, at batch size one. The observations are synthetic and seeded, with new images and state at every chunk. In the LIBERO setting, each model receives two 256×256 images, an 8-D state and a fixed prompt, and applies its own resizing and tokenisation. Each repetition is one warm-up episode followed by 320 timed chunks, and runs as a separate job on its own GPU of a node reserved for the timing.

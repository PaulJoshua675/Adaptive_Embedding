Collections of rollouts. Two collections of rollouts supply the labels. The broad collection labels every incorrect trace at both scales. It evaluates steps 0, 1 and 2 and then every second step of a trace with 8 rollouts, and evaluates some of these prefixes with 16 rollouts in a refinement stage (Appendix B). The gold collection applies the same scan to a set of 4B traces with 4 rollouts and refines with 8, half as many as the broad collection. The PIT steps of its 818 PITs are the reference PIT steps for localization. Each comparison is scored on one collection, because the two collections use different numbers of rollouts.

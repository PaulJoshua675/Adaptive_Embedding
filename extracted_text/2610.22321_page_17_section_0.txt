B.2 FIVE-STAGE AUTOMATED ANNOTATION PIPELINE Informed by practical insights of prior approaches (Geng et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2026a; Leng et al., 2026; Huang et al., 2026; Pan et al., 2026a; Yang et al., 2026b; Sun et al., 2024; Mei et al., 2024), To construct structured supervision at scale, we implement a five-stage automated annotation pipeline that first analyzes audio and video independently, then verifies event relations in both directions, and finally aggregates the structured evidence into the three consistency levels. Table 6 summarizes the input, operation, tool, and output of each stage. Fig. 6 illustrates the complete data flow. Pipeline details. Table 6 and Fig. 6 summarize the complete annotation procedure. Stages 1 and 2 first parse the two modalities independently. The audio branch separates speech, mu- sic, and other sound events, and records their scene context, speaker information, acoustic attributes, and timestamps.

Tracking-by-detection. SORT [7] established the predict–associate loop; successive work strengthened every stage: appearance and post-hoc refinement [12, 42], low-confidence recovery [47], observation-centric drift correction [9], camera compensation [4], ground-plane modeling [45], adaptive appearance fusion [28], weak cues [44], and track-centric assignment [34]. Across this family, the filter’s predictive covariance is either ignored at association time or, in practice, consumed solely as a hard Mahalanobis gate [42]; the association cost itself is uncertainty-blind, and the assignment is commitment-blind: every match is final the frame it is made. Expanded and buffered matching. Closest to ARC- SORT are methods that enlarge boxes before computing overlap: by a fixed proportional margin [43], a fixed iter- ative schedule [20] (discarding the Kalman filter), with a harmonic cost mean [16], or switched by a binary velocity threshold atop a trained long short-term memory (LSTM) gated filter [40]. In every case the factor is fixed or dis- cretely switched; none derives it from the filter’s own pre- dictive covariance. Ours is a continuous function of that covariance, wide exactly when the prediction is unreliable, and requires no training. Uncertainty as a signal. In detection, probabilistic-IoU box models [24, 25, 41] score overlap between distributions rather than boxes; UTrack [35] propagates detector uncer- tainty into association, complementary to the motion-model uncertainty we exploit. IMM filtering [6, 8, 29] has long supplied calibrated predictive covariances in radar tracking, where the probabilistic and joint probabilistic data associa- tion filters (PDAF/JPDAF) [5, 13] spend them continuously inside a soft filter update. Visual MOT’s hard-assignment, overlap-cost pipelines use the covariance only as a Maha- lanobis veto [42]; covariance as a continuous overlap tol- erance under one-to-one assignment is, to our knowledge, new. Deferred data association. Multiple hypothesis track- ing (MHT) [32] answers ambiguity by not deciding; PDAF/JPDAF (above) soften the assignment itself. Vi- sion revisited both, in pruned MHT with appearance [23], m-best JPDA [33], and near-online revision [10], but the SORT lineage dropped deferral: it commits every identity at the crossing instant, precisely when occlusion makes ev- idence worst, and revision survives only as offline refine- ment [12, 38].

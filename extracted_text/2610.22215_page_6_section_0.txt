AV2 Joint Prediction We next test whether the same proposal-only FutureLift interface is compatible with joint forecasting. Future- Lift is attached to frozen FJMP [17], Forecast-MAE [1], and DONUT proposal models and trained separately for each backbone. FutureLift and each unrefined baseline use the same frozen proposal coordinates and logits. The SRefiner [27] rows reproduce the authors’ published re- sults for matched FJMP and Forecast-MAE backbones; these use the authors’ proposal exports rather than our bit-identical proposal files. Moreover, following its re- leased AV2 implementation, SRefiner receives a precom- puted 134-dimensional per-mode embedding in addition to trajectory coordinates and map context.

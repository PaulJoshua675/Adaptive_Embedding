B Auditor Flag Taxonomy and Examples B.1 Flag Taxonomy Table 5 summarizes the full flag distribution across the 300 interviews. We make three observations: 1. Invented rationalization is the dominant pattern (372/676 flags are caricature, 69% of them medium severity): the simulator frequently produces reasons for preferences that sound plausi- ble but are not attested in the profile. 2. Flags grounded in the user’s own evidence are the most reliable. Contradictions with the liked and disliked lists or with the ReDial con- versation can be checked directly (Examples 2–4). Fabrication flags about film facts rely on the audi- tor’s parametric knowledge and are less reliable: in several cases the auditor flagged accurate scene descriptions as fabricated. Such errors are conser- vative: they can remove grounded content from the enrichment input, but they do not introduce unsupported claims into the profile. 3. REGENERATE is rare. The auditor reserves REGENERATE for structural failure or ≥3 high- severity flags. This occurred in one of 300 inter- views, and the regenerated interview received PAR- TIAL, indicating that gross structural failure is rare with capable LLMs (Gemini 2.5 Flash) at our in- terview scale. The dominant failure mode is local rather than global. B.2 Representative Example 1: ACCEPT User 78.

Two directions follow directly from this work. First, testing whether the constrained fix transfers to randomly sampled grid layouts, rather than the single fixed layout used here, would establish whether the result generalizes beyond one navigation problem. Second, implementing the hard-constrained variant of Corollary 1 directly – via action masking or early termination once a fixed cost budget is reached – would allow the deterministic bound on true-objective performance to be tested empirically against a trained system, rather than verified only as a consequence of Proposition 1 on the fixed-λ relaxation studied here.

We evaluate eight models spanning 3B–32B parameters and two families: Qwen3-8B/14B/32B, Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen3.5-9B, DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, and Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, covering both thinking and non-thinking models (Appendix B.2).

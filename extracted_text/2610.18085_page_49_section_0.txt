Appendix I.6 gives the protocols. I RELEASED-CHECKPOINT CASE STUDIES The paired experiments establish protection and recovery in controlled training runs. We complement these experiments with four case studies on public checkpoints: naturally occurring pair-scale variation, coordinate- sensitive fine-tuning, the internal evolution of a Lion stress test, and token-level restart differences on real text. These measurements are independent of the primary training runs. Appendix I.5 summarizes the case studies, and Appendix I.6 gives the protocols. I.1 Pair Scales in Public Checkpoints Figure 5 extends the GPT-2 endpoint measurement of Appendix G.5 with a cross-model survey and intermediate Pythia checkpoints. The intermediate checkpoints distinguish initialization offsets from changes accumulated during training: Q–K starts nearly balanced, but reaches a maximum absolute log-ratio of 1.86 in Pythia 410M, concentrated in deep layers. Pythia 1B remains within 0.04, and V–O initially moves toward balance. Drift is model- and pair-dependent. These observations show pair-scale variation during ordinary training, without measuring its effect on loss. Training also changes the function, so these checkpoints are not points on a single fixed symmetry orbit.

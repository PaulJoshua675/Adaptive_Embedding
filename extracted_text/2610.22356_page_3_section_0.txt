DK-VLM: Geometry as Position scene. The semantic kernel keeps the backbone’s native attention and mRoPE unchanged. The geometric kernel uses independent projections and computes relations between visual tokens conditioned on patch-level 3D points and frame-level camera poses, so that displacements between scene points and relative transformations between views directly modulate feature comparison and aggregation. A learned gate fuses the two outputs. Geometry thus enters attention as position rather than as reconstruction features fused into token content. The geometric inputs are estimated from images, requiring no ground-truth depth or camera poses at inference. Dual-Kernel Attention gives the model direct access to point displacements and relative view transformations, but successive spatial judgments require more: earlier movements change the position and heading on which later judgments depend, even when the scene layout remains unchanged. Single-step question answering supervises each judgment within a given reference frame and does not explicitly supervise how that frame changes across steps. We therefore introduce Reference-Frame Composition (RC) supervision, which provides stepwise turn and heading targets along landmark routes and view sequences. These targets are derived by connecting verified single-step relations that share landmarks or views within the same scene (Yang et al., 2025c, Zhang et al., 2025b). We implement DK-VLM on Qwen2.5-VL-7B and Qwen3-VL-8B (Bai et al., 2025b,a). We evaluate it on seven spatial reasoning benchmarks (Yang et al., 2025a, Zhang et al., 2026b, Yang et al., 2025d, Wang et al., 2025b, Li et al., 2025a, Wang et al., 2025d, Tong et al., 2024) and two general video understanding benchmarks (Fu et al., 2025, Li et al., 2024). Among the compared methods, DK-VLM-8B achieves the highest six-benchmark average score and the highest overall scores on VSI-Bench (74.33) and ReVSI-32 (58.28), with trainable parameters totaling only 3.86% of its backbone’s parameter count. RC supervision further improves multi-step spatial reasoning, yielding consistent gains on route-planning and viewpoint-related tasks. Ablations show that DK-VLM benefits more from RC supervision than a Qwen baseline fine-tuned on the same instruction data, supporting the complementarity between geometric relation modeling and stepwise spatial supervision. The full model also preserves general video understanding on VideoMME and MVBench. Our contributions are: • Dual-Kernel Attention, which introduces scene geometry as position through a geometric kernel parallel to a pretrained VLM’s native attention. • Reference-Frame Composition supervision, which supervises reference-frame updates using stepwise targets derived from verified single-step relations. • DK-VLM-8B achieves the highest overall scores among compared methods on VSI-Bench and ReVSI-32 and the highest average in the six-benchmark comparison.

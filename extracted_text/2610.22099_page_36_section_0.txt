B.4.5 Sensitivity to decoder proposal temperature We vary token-proposal temperature over {0, 0.1, 0.5, 1.0} for the frozen full-canvas policy, leaving policy-action sampling and the schedule unchanged. Finalization remains greedy in every condition. Small proposal noise is competitive: temperature 0.1 gives 48.32% at T = 16 and 63.33% at T = 64, slightly above the corresponding greedy scores of 47.00% and 62.67%, while greedy proposals are better at T = 32. Increasing temperature to 1.0 reduces accuracy at all three budgets, with the largest loss at T = 16 (37.77% versus 47.00%). These measurements support greedy decoding as a strong default.

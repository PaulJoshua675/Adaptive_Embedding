From Open X-Embodiment, the mixture uses twelve datasets: Taco Play, Stanford Hydra, NYU Franka Play, BC-Z, RT-1, Furniture Bench, Austin Sailor, Austin BUDS, UT Austin MUTEX, Berkeley Cable Routing, VIOLA and Berkeley RPT. The joint-delta actions of Berkeley RPT and RoboSet are converted to end-effector deltas by forward kinematics. A robot appears under several conventions when its datasets command it differently, joint angles in one and end-effector deltas in another. The state projection of §3.2 is selected by state layout and control convention, and the action statistics of §5.1 are per dataset, so no source is remapped to another’s convention. A state layout is the ordered list of state channels a dataset records, for example the joint angles and gripper opening of each arm, and fixes the input width ds of its projection. B.2 DATA CURATION Aggregating data across embodiments, simulators, and collection pipelines introduces heterogeneous noise: sensor outliers, temporal state-action misalignment, and inconsistent conventions across datasets sharing a robot. • Channel selection. We keep only the degrees of freedom the policy commands, dropping derived or uncontrolled channels: forward-kinematic end-effector poses, joint velocities, efforts, IMU readings, and telepresence-parked head, waist, and leg joints. 23

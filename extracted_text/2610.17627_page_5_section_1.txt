We proposed AiSAQ-in-Filtering in this paper, a filtering ANNS method which offloads label information to the storage without increase of storage and memory footprint. This also acheived short index load time and higher recall with lower disk I/Os compared with existing methods. AiSAQ-in-Filtering also have possible applications like hybrid document search and other LLM-intensive tasks. This can be scaled out to larger datasets as shown in original AiSAQ.

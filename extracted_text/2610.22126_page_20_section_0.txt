One more cost worth disclosing: surrogate training. With n the training-set size and T epochs, our closed-form sparse surrogate trains in O(T · n · d) (sparse graph, E=O(d)) or O(T · n · d2) (dense); a generic dense-MLP surrogate is O(T · n · d) regardless of sparsity. This is a one-time, offline cost amortized over all explanations, not a per-instance one: over Nexplain instances it adds (training cost)/Nexplain to each, vanishing as Nexplain grows. The basis expansion shifts the burden entirely onto training, raising both fitting time and training memory by an order of magnitude while increasing attribution time only moderately.

We present Gen4P, a feed-forward 4D perceiver that processes monocular video input for full 4D reconstruction. The key to its success is to leverage prior knowledge from pretrained video generators in a single-step manner, which differs from traditional multi-step denoising-based 4D perceivers. Our on-demand decoder enables flexible spatial and temporal querying, while a frozen video-depth LoRA provides a plug-in geometry prior without additional trainable parameters.

The supported conclusion is that overlap-conditioned score fusion improves the shared candidate ordering most clearly on DL19, while the agreement-stratified analysis below identifies the subset of queries on which this advantage is concentrated. 4.5 Ablations and Analysis We analyze XViewRank through several questions. Do the generated views pro- vide useful but incomplete evidence? Do score and overlap signals explain the learned fusion behavior? Is the method sensitive to the overlap depth τ? Does the model really use overlap context? Is the lightweight nonlinear scorer neces- sary?

3.1 Modeling Delivery Decisions During delivery, the system plans ad-unit budgets within each account and adjusts control tCPA based on the current state. The fixed underlying bidder uses these settings to bid for individual traffic opportunities. At the end of the period, the system generates diagnostic guidance from settled outcomes to inform account planning and strategy adjustment in the next period. Index decisions chronologically under system policy π. Decision i uses currently observable information: ui ∼π(· | hi). Historical diagnostic guidance forms part of this information and informs subsequent account planning and strategy adjustment. • Decision output ui: an account’s ad-unit budget allocations, control tCPA, and diagnostic guidance. 3.2 Business Objectives and Constraints The system measures the business outcomes of delivery decisions using ADV and CPA, which reflect value generation and conversion costs, respectively. As shown in the upper part of Figure 2, Planner handles account budget planning, Optimizer adjusts ad-unit delivery strategies, and Inspector turns settled period outcomes into diagnostic guidance for the next period. Together, planning, adjustment, and diagnosis form a collaborative process across periods. The system adopts two-stage training. The first stage develops operational decision-making capabilities through supervised fine-tuning and offline reinforcement learning. The second stage, shown in the lower part of Figure 2, updates role parameters using settled business feedback during continuous delivery, enabling co-evolution.

We present a set-level RL objective for document-grounded ideation for training to generate diverse, high quality ideas. A model is prompted/taught to generate K ideas in one rollout and each idea’s quality is weighted by its distance from the ideas before it. Our best 8B model (no think) achieves higher creative utility than strong closed-source models and competing quality-diversity objectives, while the think variant beats the closed-source models and is on par with DARLING. In small pilot human and execution studies its sets are comparable to Claude Opus 4.8’s, so we see no evidence that diversity costs quality. Future work includes grounding-aware rewards, diversity across a population, and reasoning traces designed for creative ideation. 9

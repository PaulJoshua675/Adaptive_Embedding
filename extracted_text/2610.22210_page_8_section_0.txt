Backbone transfer and method compatibility. TRACE is a plug-and-play objective that transfers across segmenta- tion backbones and complements existing topology-aware supervision. The CS2-Net results in Tab. 3 show improved connectivity and topology over the standard objective and compared structural losses. TRACE shows consistent per- formance across diverse thin-structure segmentation tasks. Nevertheless, its structural supervision depends on the topol- ogy encoded in the ground-truth annotations. Small an- notation errors may introduce isolated fragments, spurious branches, or local loops, which TASC preserves because removing them would alter the annotated topology, as il- lustrated in Fig. 5. A promising direction is to combine TASC with uncertainty-aware filtering or consistency-based refinement to suppress unreliable topological annotations.

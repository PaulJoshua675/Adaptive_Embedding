A typical question reads: “Which sales CRM products would you consider for a small company operating in Finland that needs automated sales follow-ups and recurring emails? Name up to five products and briefly explain the fit. Answer in English. Distinguish confirmed capabilities from assumptions about local availability.” No question names a brand. Each question has the four brands of its category as candidates: BigCommerce, MyCashflow, PrestaShop and WooCommerce for e-commerce; ClickUp, ProofHub, Smartsheet and Wrike for project management; HubSpot, Insightly, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Zoho CRM for sales CRM. The 54 questions and 4 candidates give 216 brand-and-question cells, and three answers per question give 648 labels per wave. A candidate is labelled 1 when its name or one of its listed aliases occurs in the answer text as a whole-token sequence after NFKC normalisation and casefolding. Negative mentions count. The answer text is the non-thought text of the first candidate response. The rule (version v02_unicode_product_alias_v1) is the one used in every earlier wave, so D1 labels compare directly with theirs. Because it matches whole tokens, it does not count inflected Estonian or Finnish forms such as a case ending attached to a brand name; Section 5 measures how much this matters. 3.2 Forecasts Every forecast is a function of counts committed before D1 and was written into the manifest at the freeze. • Pooled cell rate (primary): mentions of the brand in the cell over its nine earlier answers (three each in v0.2, v0.3 and v0.4), clipped to [0.05, 0.95]. • Rule baselines: brand history, the brand’s mentions over its 18 cells and 162 earlier observations, shrunk towards the overall rate with weight 20; the overall rate, 737 of 1,944 earlier labels (0.379); and a constant 0.5. • Comparators reported beside the rule: the latest wave alone (v0.4 count over three, clipped to [0.05, 0.95]); the oldest wave alone (v0.2 count over three, clipped the same way); question history, the estimator frozen at v0.3 from v0.2 alone; and the v0.2 context model, also frozen. 3.3 Decision rule The frozen protocol states the rule as: “Durability holds if pooled_cell_rate has strictly lower Brier and strictly lower log loss than brand_history, global_history and constant_half over all 648 labels, and its D1 Brier is at most 0.03 above its v0.4 Brier. Any other outcome on a complete wave with an unchanged model is reported as decay.” The v0.4 reference is 0.0258873457, the 0.0259 of the v0.4 record recomputed from the committed counts, so the ceiling is 0.0558873457. A wave with any failed row is reported as incomplete and the rule is not applied. If any answer reports a model version other than gemini-3.1-pro-preview, or none, the wave becomes a model- shift test and the rule is not applied. Brier score is the mean of (p −y)2 over the 648 labels; log loss is the mean of −[y log p + (1 −y) log(1 −p)]. 3.4 Inference Labels are not independent: four candidates share an answer, three answers share a question, and the six questions of a family are translations and market variants of one another.

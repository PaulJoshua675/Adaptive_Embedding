A supplied document is bound to a fixed tool input to avoid ambiguous file selection. 4.3 Benchmark landscape, citation gaps, and visual comparison The optional benchmark-landscape module summarizes metadata-derived datasets, metrics, and method families. Its output is a preliminary exploration rather than a verified systematic survey. Citation-gap analysis evaluates potential references against a draft excerpt and recognized existing references. These extensions retain their earlier discovery workflow and are not part of the Top 5 retrieval evaluation. It is therefore not presented as a currently callable or reproduced component of the public release. 5 Experiments 5.1 Dataset and historical comparison protocol The released benchmark contains 30 queries in each of EASY, MEDIUM, and HARD. According to the dataset construction record, GPT-6 Astra in ChatGPT was used to extract and generate the shared literature-search prompts. EASY supplies more identifying detail, MEDIUM removes some detail, and HARD uses shorter, less specific descriptions. These are intended difficulty levels rather than independently calibrated labels. The tiers are not independent paper pools; in particular, MEDIUM and HARD share targets with different wording. The present comparison uses the first ten IDs of each tier: EASY N001–N010, MEDIUM C001–C010, and HARD C001–C010. Each system receives the same filled item prompt: a paper-finding instruction, the 2015–2025 publication/verified-version constraint, up-to- five distinct works with exact bibliographic fields and a short match explanation, identity verification and deduplication requirements, and the idea text. The designated target is not supplied to retrieval. Original prompts and outputs are preserved; Appendix A gives the exact shared wrappers and a three-tier example. These are retrieval prompts, distinct from the internal scoring prompts. ChatGPT and Gemini were tested through their October 2026 web Deep Research interfaces; backend model/build identifiers and subscription tiers were not recorded. Idea2Prior used its API pipeline. Preserved Expert outputs identify the configured alias openai/gemini-3.8-flash; a compatibility-protocol alias does not independently verify the upstream served model. The released native-Gemini preset is not a newly benchmarked replacement for those historical runs. Results use final selected runs, including authorized replacements of operationally failed attempts. They therefore measure selected-result recovery, not first-attempt reliability. EASY initially produced five target hits over ten planned queries before recovery; the final selection produced nine. We retain the historical ChatGPT EASY convention of 7/10: six observed hits plus one convention-based counted hit. No returned paper or intersection is fabricated for that credit. Counting only observed hits would make ChatGPT EASY 6/10 and aggregate recovery 12/30; our primary table preserves the earlier agreed convention explicitly.

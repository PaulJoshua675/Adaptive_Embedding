C.1 AGENTIC BENCHMARKS For all agentic benchmarks, we sample with temperature 1.0 and top-p 0.95. SWE-bench (Jimenez et al., 2024) Verified (Chowdhury et al., 2024) (500 tasks), SWE-bench Multilingual (Yang et al., 2025) (300), and SWE-bench Pro (Deng et al., 2026) (731) use the OpenHands agent (Wang et al., 2025; 2026) (SDK version 1.17.0) with 3 rollouts per task. Each rollout has at most 200 turns, or 250 for SWE-bench Pro. Terminal-Bench 2.0 (Merrill et al., 2026) (89 tasks) uses the Terminus-2 agent (Merrill et al., 2026) with a one-hour limit per task and 8 rollouts per task. The harness sweep and the RL evaluation use Terminal-Bench 2.1, which has the same 89 tasks with 28 of them fixed, and we compare scores only within a version. DeepSWE (Huang et al., 2026) pass@1 is averaged over 4 runs. BrowseComp (Wei et al., 2025) is evaluated on a subset of the benchmark in a custom agent harness, with Exa as the search provider. For N-3.5 Super, run-to-run variation is about 1 point on SWE-bench Verified and Pro and about 2 points on DeepSWE. We report pass@1 as the mean success rate over rollouts and compute pass@k from the same rollouts. 17

Task-specific prompt configurations The two universal Analyst modes defined in Section 3 are instantiated differently across the experiments. The PINN tasks use only the standard mode, with prompts that specify the differential equation, the modifiable transformations, the software contract, and the physical target metric; they do not use a method-family catalog, Chain-of-Thought procedure, or coverage map. The SNN classification experiments compare the standard mode, denoted the simple prompt in the result tables, with the detailed mode. The simple prompt specifies the Analyst role, classification problem, target accuracy, data-use and implementation constraints, examples of admissible decoder types, required technical-specification format, and complete experiment log. The detailed prompt adds a researcher-defined taxonomy of 12 decoder-method classes, a structured Chain-of-Thought procedure for coverage and failure analysis, an explicit class field in the technical specification, and the dynamically updated coverage map. The 12 classes define broad areas of exploration rather than 12 fixed algorithms; each class may contain multiple feature transformations, decision rules, and hyperparameter settings, and hypotheses may combine methods from different classes.

Moreover, our pretraining consisted of two pretrainings for 100K steps each, meaning that the learning- rate schedule consisted of two 100K-steps schedules. • We generate pretraining datasets with 1024 samples each, of which a random fraction in [0.03, 0.9] is used as context (train) and the rest as query (test) samples. We lowered the lower bound from 0.3 to 0.03 to cover the smaller datasets found in our benchmark. • We pretrain fully in 32-bit floats to avoid potential instabilities.

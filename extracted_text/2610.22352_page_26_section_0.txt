G Additional Views and Texture Editing G.1 Single-View Generation and Back-View Editing Because any image attached to the surface becomes an anchor (Sec. 3.3), the same generator accepts a second view or an edited view without retraining: a 2D editing model can supply appearance for surfaces the reference never shows, or change the appearance of surfaces it does. Both put the unseen surface under user control: the single-view results of Secs. 4.1 and 4.2 show what the generator completes on its own, and the extensions show what it produces when instructed.

E Additional Analyses E.1 Reasoning Effort The main analyses run every model at its lowest reasoning level. To test whether more reasoning reduces scale sensitivity, we repeat all 16 rating scale conditions with the three open models at three reasoning levels each, namely low, medium, and xhigh for Qwen3.8 27B, and low, high, and max for DeepSeek V4 Flash and GLM 5.3 Flash. Be- cause the models name their levels differently, we compare them by their rank within each model (lowest, middle, highest). Sensitivity is calculated as in the main analyses. Figure 10 shows the results. Interestingly, more reasoning lowers numerical sensitivity for changes in all three design elements, but only slightly. From the lowest to the highest level, SSMAE falls by 0.009 for format, 0.006 for range, and 0.016 for sign, averaged over the three models. Semantic sensitivity falls by 1.1 percentage points for format and 2.6 points for sign, and does not change for range (the 95% interval includes zero). The models also differ in direction.

Conclusions This study introduces a novel Deep Reinforcement Learning-governed dynamic DE–CMA-ES Orchestration (DRL-DCO) algorithm, built upon a Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG)-based actor–critic agent that adaptively harmonizes Differential Evolution (DE) and Covariance Matrix Adaptation Evolution Strategy (CMA-ES) through a continuous multi-dimensional action space, a progression-aware state representation, and a diversity-informed reward function. To better address the limitations in many existing RL-assisted EA approaches, which confine the RL component to intermittent intervention on a single evolutionary strategy or leave it with limited landscape or progression awareness, and to enable supervision-free search with full automation, DRL-DCO’s actor–critic agent sustains continuous, progression-aware oversight of two evolutionary processes operating in parallel, offering a more complete realization of the RL-assisted EA paradigm. Through a critic-supervised agent-training phase and a supervision-free agent-inference phase operating via forward inference alone, the algorithm achieves robust and accurate performance across challenging high-dimensional benchmark optimization problems and demonstrates strong engineering generalization on the IASC-ASCE benchmark FEMU task. The key conclusions are as follows. 1) The proposed continuous DE–CMA-ES orchestration constitutes a fundamental departure from conventional hard-switching hybridization.

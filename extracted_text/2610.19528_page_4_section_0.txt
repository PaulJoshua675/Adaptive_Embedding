Once the model judges the available evidence sufficient, it emits final translation ˆy and terminates. This action can occur immediately after the initial state, so direct translation is a valid zero-retrieval trajectory. The policy therefore jointly learns whether to retrieve, what information to request, and how to use all evidence for translation. The objective is not to apply a fixed retrieval policy, but to retrieve only when it improves the final translation. 3.2 Learning When to Retrieve Outcome-guided Reinforcement Learning. Because tra- jectories for the same input can differ in whether they retrieve, what they query, and how they make final translation, their relative rewards directly su- pervise selective retrieval without requiring labels specifying which inputs need external knowledge. Retrieval exploration curriculum. Optimiz- ing the outcome reward directly from an off-the- shelf model can collapse to a never-retrieve policy: early in training, the model’s self-generated queries are often poor and its retrieval-action formatting is frequently malformed, so direct translation consis- tently receives higher reward before useful retrieval behavior has a chance to emerge. We therefore use a three-stage curriculum. First, we explicitly gen- erate retrieval-augmented trajectories and retain the top 40% according to quality Q. Second, we perform supervised fine-tuning on these trajecto- ries to initialize the interaction format and query- generation ability. Third, we conduct RL with a temporary retrieval penalty to move the SFT pol- icy away from always retrieving, and then remove this shaping term and optimize Eq.(2) directly until convergence. The final policy is therefore free to re- trieve or translate directly according to downstream quality rather than a predefined retrieval rate. 3.3 Learning What to Retrieve The policy above learns whether to retrieve and how to formulate queries, but a fixed retriever still ranks documents according to its pretraining ob- jective, typically the shallow semantic similarity. Since our objective is to retrieve documents to help make better translation, we instead optimize docu- ment selection according to translation quality.

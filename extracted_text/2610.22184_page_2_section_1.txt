Whole-Body Manipulation. In this paper, we advance whole-body dexterous manipulation, in which objects are held and repositioned through contact anywhere on the robot’s body rather than only at designated end-effectors or links. The closely related term, whole-body dexterity, was defined for a snake-like robot arm that manipulates with its entire body surface [18]. Related in spirit, a humanoid can manipulate from onboard proprioception alone, since joint- encoder readings under compliant contact act as a coarse whole-body tactile channel [19]. Several methods manipulate bulky objects that exceed the hand’s size and payload by grasping them with the arms and torso and distributing contact across the body [8], [20], [21], [22]. Other work uses upper-body tactile sensing to hold boxes against the arms and chest on a real humanoid [9]. Closest to our setting, reinforcement learning on a large soft robotic torso lifts cuboids of varying sizes and masses and performs recovery re-grasping in front of the torso [23], but without standing or walking and without moving the object to other regions of the body.

At the halt the hand-over passes control to the next bank, whose first pass loads m∗and runs the copy. Proposition 7.3 (One pass). Let a state of Mα K,S,L, S ≥G, hold SA,w, of G records, ready in its active bank, and in the window clear requests and a state x of HA. If booted = 0, one pass replaces x by x0(m∗ A), clears cnt, lim and dest, returns the read head, the arm and the copy head to cell 0 and clears the idle bank and the copy tape. It then carries out one step of HA followed by the device of Definition 7.2, and sets booted to the negation of the halt bit. If the halt bit is 1, it returns the arm and the copy head to cell 0 and hands over at its G-th step; otherwise its S steps bring the slot head back to slot 0. It leaves SA,w in the bank it started in and the requests clear, whatever the temporaries held. Proof. By Lemma 3.3 the pass evaluates the blocks of Construction 7.1 in order. The startup records leave every cell unchanged when booted = 1. Otherwise they set each memory bit to its value in m∗ A, clear the other cells they name, and set the five requests that return the heads and clear the idle bank and the copy tape. The step block is HA compiled by Lemma 3.4 in the order of the netlist, which Lemma 3.3 admits: every unit follows its predecessors, a unit that reads a state bit comes no later than the unit of its next value, which reads the bit before its last record writes it, and no unit reads the unit of a next value. So after the block the state bits hold the next state of HA. The device records form the requests [yc = 1] as conjunctions of A literals, the bit [cnt = lim] as a conjunction of w equivalences, and its conjunction with dest. They then carry out clauses (i) to (iv) of Definition 7.2 in order. Each bit of the low byte of Rout is written under the arm or the copy head by a multiplexer, followed by the request that moves that head on, and Rout is cleared on a write. Each bit under the read head is loaded into Rin by a multiplexer on the condition of a read, followed by the request that moves the read head on, and the sign bit is copied above them. Then ceot is set, cnt is incremented by carries formed before any of its bits changes, the rewind is carried out, its last record setting RR, and the requests of the host are cleared. The hand-over records set booted and, at the halt, the requests that return the arm and the copy head and, last, HO. Every temporary is written in the pass before it is read and is not written after its last read, so a cell shared by temporaries holds, at each read, the value of the temporary read.

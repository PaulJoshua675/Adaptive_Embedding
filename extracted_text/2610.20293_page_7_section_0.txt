Because panel (b) uses size-matched references, its values are not directly comparable with panels (c)–(d). 4 EXPERIMENTS 4.1 EXPERIMENTAL SETUP We study m = 5 jointly generated retrieval actions. Policy optimization uses the frozen evaluator ˆR0 defined in Section 3.2, while offline counterfactual auditing uses the auction-replay procedure defined there. Historical V0 is used only in the observational audit below. The training-time evaluator is a cached-filter construction: it processes the full candidate pool once and obtains coalition values by filtering the cached downstream result. All primary evaluator-to- replay comparisons use this evaluator. As a sensitivity analysis, a coalition-rerun evaluator re- executes downstream ranking for each coalition; because this defines a different surrogate set func- tion, we do not treat it as an evaluator-error correction. The offline audit samples 1,200 requests before observing replay outcomes and enumerates all 31 non-empty coalitions. Each coalition is replayed three times; a 50-request subset receives seven re- peats, for 117,800 executions in total. For the coalition audit, we denote the median across available replay executions by yaudit(S) = medianr y(r)(S), and use it as the repeat-aggregated coalition reference. Bootstrap inference resamples requests, and no request is filtered by its full-set replay outcome. 4.2 COALITION DEPENDENCE AND EVALUATOR FIDELITY The repeat-aggregated replay landscape exhibits substantial coalition dependence. On the 1,171 requests with matched training-evaluator values, corresponding to 11,710 action pairs, 64.4% of pair interactions are negative, 7.4% positive, and 28.2% exactly zero, with median absolute interaction 3.0978. The training-time evaluator agrees with the replay reference on 92.25% of pair-interaction signs (95% CI [0.9140, 0.9295]). Full-set outcomes alone do not characterize this structure.

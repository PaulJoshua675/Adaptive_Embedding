It retains 78–87% of the useful updates, as Appendix D reports. 6.3 EVOHEIR LEADS ON EVERY BACKBONE After ten rounds, EVOHEIR (Full) has the highest Math Avg. and General Avg. on every backbone of Section 3, as Table 1 shows. It raises the base model’s Math Avg. by 7.9–30.1% and exceeds Agent0, the strongest baseline, by 1.59–2.40 pp (6.0–7.0%) on the three base models. Gemma-4-E4B-it, the only instruction-tuned start, leaves the least headroom, and Full still leads by 0.55 pp. Across all twelve benchmarks, Full beats Agent0 on 45 of 48 benchmark–backbone pairs in Figure 5, and the three exceptions are 30-problem AIME sets. No Recovery keeps exploration, screening, and confirmation but removes absorption. Full exceeds it on every backbone, which isolates Recovery as a module, and Figure 6 shows Full ending the ten rounds highest on all four backbones. These are fixed-round comparisons. A Full round takes 8–9 wall-clock hours on two 96-GB GPUs, as Appendix G details, and Section 6.6 compares allocations at equal accounting budget. 6.4 EXPERIENCE OUTLIVES ITS MODEL The study runs 16 donor interventions on four fixed recipients, from rounds 3, 5, 7, and 10, with two current and two archived donors each. Every intervention restarts its recipient and is evaluated on the same 1,024 items before confirmation, and gains are relative to that recipient. Donor state and experience value diverge. Eight of the ten donors weaker than the current root still improve the recipient, as Figure 7a shows. Their mean donor gain is −1.09 pp, but their mean absorption gain is +0.71 pp. Recovery learns and retains. From the same 64 references, Cache SFT gains 2.08 pp of new successes but loses 2.61 pp, while Recovery gains 2.54 pp and loses 1.15 pp, for net gains of −0.54 and +1.39 pp in Figure 7b. Recovery beats Cache SFT at all four checkpoints, by 1.93 pp on average, although its own net gain is negative at round 7 (−0.20 pp). Experience outlives the model that produced it.

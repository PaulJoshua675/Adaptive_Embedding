Claude Sonnet 4.6, GPT- 6.1 Sol, Qwen3-235B-A22B (2507), Ministral 8B (Ministral 3 8B Instruct) and Mistral 7B (Instruct v0.2) run at temperature 0 with structured output and a plain-JSON fallback, with at most 768 out- put tokens per answer. Failed or invalid calls are retried and otherwise recorded as missing, never as an answer, and every answer is cached under a hash of the stream, model, router and prompt. Routing. BM25 uses k1=1.5 and b=0.75 over lowercase alphanumeric tokens; dense routing uses BGE-base v1.5 (Xiao et al., 2024). Depen- dency queries use “{option} unavailable cancelled blocked closed or delayed”, “{dependency} outage closure disruption maintenance restriction strike suspended” and “{dependency} operating hours availability travel safety affects {option}”. The time filter parses a start time introduced by from, starting, beginning, effective or start and drops the decision when that time is not before the end of its activity. Analysis.

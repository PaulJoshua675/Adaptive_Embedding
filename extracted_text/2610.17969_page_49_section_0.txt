The strongest separation between normalized streams and the other baselines occurs under per-tensor residual-stream int8. Per-token scales substantially reduce this separation, supporting cross-token scale variation as an important contributor. Kernel fidelity. Simulated quantization followed by dequantization and a floating-point general matrix multiplication (GEMM) could diverge from deployed integer arithmetic. To check fidelity, we reran the W8A8 setting of Table 10 through the actual int8 tensor-core path, torch._int_mm, with int8×int8 products accumulated in 32-bit integers and identical scale computation. Every architecture’s loss delta agrees with the simulated delta for the same architecture to within 0.002 nats across all 8 trained 124M models, while the deltas span up to 0.233 nats. This agreement supports the fidelity of the simulated W8A8 evaluation. The residual-stream protocols evaluate quantization and dequantization without replacing matrix multiplication. Cost of fine-grained scale management. On the same device, the per-token quantization and dequantization step takes 1.02× as long as the per-tensor step and 72% as long as the feed-forward GEMM surrounded by the quantization and dequantization operations.

For EuroSAT-S1, BigEarthNet-S1, and DFC2020-S1, the absolute gain from pretraining peaks at a modest 5–10% label budget and recedes as labeled data becomes abundant; Flood-S1 instead shows its largest advantage at the very smallest label fraction (1%), decaying monotonically thereafter. Practically, pretrained models reach the random-initialization 100%-label result using only 10–25% of the labels for EuroSAT-S1 and DFC2020-S1, and 25– 50% for BigEarthNet-S1 and Flood-S1, cutting the labeling burden required to match full-data performance by roughly half to three-quarters. 4.7. Representation Analysis To complement the downstream performance analysis, we compute Centered Kernel Align- ment (CKA) (Kornblith et al., 2019) between encoder representations at different pretraining data fractions. CKA measures linear representational similarity between two neural networks on a shared set of inputs. We extract Stage 3 post global average pooling features ([N, 384]) from all eight pretrained encoders (0–100%) on each Copernicus-Bench S1GRD test set, and compute pairwise unbiased linear CKA with Z-score feature standardization (Nguyen et al., 2021) to mitigate channel vari- ance skew. We use Stage 3 rather than the final Stage 4 representation because GAP over the 14×14 Stage 4 output would collapse the spatial structure that our resolution ablation specifically shows to be valuable, whereas Stage 4 features retained at full resolution ([N, 14, 14, 768]) have no natural scalar CKA summary. Stage 3 avoids this collapse while still providing a compact, fixed- size representation for similarity comparison. This choice is also consistent with recent evidence that task-relevant information is often more accessible in intermediate layers than in final-layer embeddings across several self-supervised remote sensing architectures (Romero et al., 2026). Figure 7 shows CKA similarity to the fully pretrained (100%) encoder as a function of pre- training data fraction. For EuroSAT-S1, BigEarthNet-S1, and DFC2020-S1, CKA increases near monotonically from random initialization (0.64–0.70) to plateau values of 0.83–0.88, broadly consistent with the downstream performance saturation observed in Section 4.5. We observe an anomalous dip in CKA similarity for Flood-S1 at the 25% and 50% pretraining- data fractions (0.733 and 0.726, respectively, versus 0.807 at 10% and 0.855 at 75%).

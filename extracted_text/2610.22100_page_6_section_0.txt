At a matched 10% rate the rules tie on computed values (entropy 55.1%, novelty 50.2%; entropy’s seeds differ by 12 points) and novelty is better on copied values (61.0% vs 56.9%). Neither rule starts patches at the values: entropy spends its budget on operands (82% covered) and literals, novelty on line starts (100%), and both cover 0% of computed values (§6). 6. Why novelty works where it does Computed results are not surprising to the patcher. We compute the boundary enrichment of SOMBRERO [Neitemeier et al., 2026], 𝐵= mean surprisal at patch starts / mean surprisal over all bytes, with surprisal from the same small byte model that drives entropy patching, on the math evaluation set at the exact thresholds of the 50M runs (Table 4). Entropy has the highest enrichment (𝐵= 1.61, 𝑍= 215 against a rate-matched circular-shift null) and starts a patch at 1% of computed results; novelty sits close to chance (𝐵= 1.13; corpus-fitted 1.03) and covers 99–100% of them. The mean surprisal at computed-result starts is 3.34 bits, close to the 3.12-bit average over all bytes: these positions are not hard for the patcher’s model, so a rule that seeks surprisal passes over them. High enrichment goes with the worst computed accuracy. In math, the novelty table’s strongest contexts are a word followed by ?, the end of a question where a solution begins, and it starts a patch at 75% of line starts (10% of its boundaries, against 1% by chance). In code it starts a patch at none of the statement starts and none of the values after =, return or (; 57% of its boundaries fall inside identifiers (50% by chance), mostly in hex and escape literals: content that is genuinely unrepeated but carries no structure. The limitation is in the target, not only in the two-byte predictor: even the true novelty target covers only 18% of statement starts in code, and a 128-byte network that predicts the target better (rank correlation 0.52 vs 0.39) covers 9%. A learned chunker finds the structure on its own. H-Net [Hwang et al., 2026], whose chunk boundaries are learned end to end from the language-modeling loss, has no blind spot on GSM8K: at its native rates, its outer chunks start at the = before 100% of in-line computed results and at 100% of final answers [Vera Zúñiga, 2026].

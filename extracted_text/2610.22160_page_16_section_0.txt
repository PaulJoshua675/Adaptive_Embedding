At a fixed history, let P be a positive reference policy, A a fixed token advantage, and β > 0. For the unrestricted policy problem maxπ{⟨π, A⟩−β−1KL(π∥P)}, define Z = ∑v P(v)eβA(v) and π∗(v) = P(v)eβA(v)/Z. The objective equals β−1 log Z −β−1KL(π∥π∗), so its unique maximizer is π∗. It therefore has the additive form π∗= Softmax(z + βA). This gives the policy-improvement connection under the stated fixed-history objective; it does not identify arbitrary parameterized RL updates with direct target-KL gradient descent. The steering operator supplies an ex- ecution rule, whereas feedback and context optimization supply task information. For any fixed direction d, the derivative of target KL at zero is ⟨P −Q, d⟩, so its usefulness depends on the target. EMO obtains its direction only after Ck+1 has been estimated; the pair (Ck, Ck+1) then determines the contrast at each prefix. The resulting steered response drives the next context update. This is the ordered, complementary relationship underlying the E/M terminology. Classical EM monotonicity would require a specified latent-variable objective and corresponding expectation and maximization steps, which are not supplied by this structural correspondence alone.

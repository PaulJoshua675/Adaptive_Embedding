Correct Usage of Precision Tools. Registries accumulate narrow tools beside a broad one that covers part of the same request, and picking the narrow one is the whole task. Retail shows this most sharply. Descriptions alone underdetermine the choice among the five order-management APIs in Table 4, so LLM-ONLY falls back on the generic hub get_order_details, which on its face covers some of what is asked, on 36.4% of the steps where one of the five is correct. TRACER cuts the trap rate to 16.4% by separating APIs on the turns they were called in: at this prefix, the hub was asked for something else. This filtering matters when the competitor is not merely confusing but dangerous.

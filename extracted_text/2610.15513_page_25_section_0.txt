All experiments use the hyperparameters listed in Table 3. Per-behavior optimization uses population size 𝑁= 24; transfer optimization uses 𝑁= 18, reflecting the higher per-candidate evaluation cost when each candidate is scored against all 50 benchmark behaviors simultaneously. A run terminates when the judge accepts a candidate, when stagnation persists after 𝑅max restarts (Section 3.5), or at the configured iteration cap. Termination in our experiments was overwhelmingly by success rather than by exhaustion: the median behavior terminated at iteration zero, with the initial population already containing a successful attack, and the longest observed run reached 266 iterations (Section 4.2.5). The ablation experiments (Section 4.4) are the exception, running under an explicit 100-iteration budget so that the configurations are compared at equal cost. The initial population is bootstrapped from a single seed attack. When the amortization cache (Section 3.5) contains entries from previously optimized behaviors, 16 of the 24 candidates are fresh variants and up to 8 are elite seeds from the cache; when the cache is empty (as at the start of a benchmark run or in the ablation experiments), all 24 are bootstrapped from the seed. Mistral-7B (EXL3, 4 bpw) serves as the default reflective LLM across all experiments unless otherwise noted. Locally hosted target models are served in EXL3 format at 4 bits per weight using ExLlamaV3; API-accessed models are queried at full precision through their respective provider endpoints. State carried across runs. Two components of REPRO persist between runs by design, and both affect how the per-target results should be read. The mutation strategy library accumulates LLM- synthesized strategies and their Beta posteriors across runs (Section 3.3.2), and the amortization cache supplies up to 8 of the 24 initial candidates from attacks that succeeded on previously optimized behaviors (Section 3.5). Results across the 36 targets are therefore produced by a sequence of runs rather than by 36 independent trials, and a target evaluated late in the sequence begins from a larger library and a warmer cache than one evaluated early. This is a property of the framework rather than an artifact of the evaluation, since accumulation across targets is one of the mechanisms the paper proposes, but it means the per-target numbers are not statistically independent and should not be treated as replicate measurements. It also qualifies the observation in Section 4.2.5 that a majority of behaviors are solved by the initial population: that population is warm-started in most runs. Cold-library, cold-cache runs that would separate the contribution of accumulated state from the contribution of the search itself are not reported here and are identified as future work in Section 5.2. The ablation experiments (Section 4.4) are the exception: they run with the cache disabled and strategy weights reset, so their numbers are cold-start.

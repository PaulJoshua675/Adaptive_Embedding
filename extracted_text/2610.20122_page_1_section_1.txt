Sparse retrieval remains attractive for large-scale search because inverted indexes are fast, transparent, and easy to deploy [24, 20]. Its weakness is also well known: short user queries often miss the vocabulary and intent cues used in relevant passages [8]. Large language models offer a practical way to reduce this mismatch without rebuilding the index: given an original query, an LLM can generate alternative query views that express the same information need from different angles. The benefit of multiple query views is not automatic.

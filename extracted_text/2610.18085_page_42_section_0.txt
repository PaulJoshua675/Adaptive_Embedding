G.11 Multi-Seed Comparisons Across Scales Motivation. We assess consistency across random seeds at 22M, 101M, and 354M parameters, using two or three paired seeds at λ = 0 and λ = 0.2. Setup. We compare Vanilla and SAGGS+P at λ ∈{0, 0.2} in three configurations. The 22M ReLU model uses L = 12, context length 256, and 3 seeds. The 101M ReLU model uses L = 24, context length 512, 3 seeds at λ = 0.2, and 2 seeds at λ = 0. The 354M GELU model uses L = 24, context length 512, 2 seeds, and gradient accumulation over 4 micro-batches for an effective batch size of 32. The 22M and 101M models train for 30K steps; the 354M model trains for 20K steps. The medium and 354M comparisons use Q–K-only tilts, learning rate 6 × 10−4, 2K-step warmup, rebalancing every 100 steps, and auxiliary-penalty weight 0.01. In particular, this 354M recipe uses a higher learning rate and more frequent rebalancing than the 354M stress test in Appendix G.7. Results. Table 22 reports the multi-seed validation. At λ = 0, SAGGS+P shows negligible difference from Vanilla across these scales, with absolute mean differences below 0.05 PPL. At λ = 0.2, SAGGS+P protects the medium and 354M models, while the small-model comparison favors Vanilla. The medium-scale gap of ∆= −0.38 ± 0.05 is consistent with the 101M rescaling-strength sweep (Table 21), and the 354M gap of ∆= −1.17 ± 0.01 has a large paired effect size in this protocol. This 20K-step cross-scale protocol is distinct from the 15K-step 354M stress experiment (Table 18), where the same mechanism yields a larger +2.26 PPL gap; the comparisons agree in sign and differ in horizon and recipe.

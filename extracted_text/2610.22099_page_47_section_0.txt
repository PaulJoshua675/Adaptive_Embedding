Each run uses a global batch of 128 trajectories, formed from 16 prompt groups with eight trajectories per prompt, and a decoding horizon of 32 calls. Rollouts and likelihood replay use microbatches of 16 trajectories. Terminal reward is binary final-answer correctness, and the advantage for a trajectory is its reward minus the mean reward of its eight-trajectory group. At each learned step, the action log-probability sums Bernoulli log-probabilities over the positions on which the policy acts. The new-to-old joint action-likelihood ratio is clipped to [0.5, 1.5] in the GRPO surrogate, which is averaged over learned steps and trajectories. Finalization contributes neither an action nor a likelihood term. Hence, a training rollout contains 31 learned transitions for the full-canvas policy and 30 for the two-block policy. Both final mathematical-reasoning branches complete 935 nominal updates and 935 optimizer steps. Each branch therefore uses 935 × 128 × 32 = 3,829,760 example-level decoder evaluations for training rollouts; this count excludes bootstrap training and supervised state collection.

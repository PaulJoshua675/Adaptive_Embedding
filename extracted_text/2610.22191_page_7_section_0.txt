Remark 5 (overfitting as a REM transition). In the pairwise model, overfitting is the spin-glass phase of a Hebbian spin glass with extensively many quenched patterns, a phase with full replica-symmetry breaking [3, 32, 34]. In the exponential model, overfitting is the condensation of Proposition 3: the Gibbs measure of the attention layer freezes on one training example, as in the low-temperature phase of the REM, which is the p →∞limit of the p-spin glass and has one step of replica-symmetry breaking [27, 31]: the overfitting phase of the exponential model is therefore 1-RSB, against the full RSB of the pairwise spin glass. Crosstalk and condensation have different scaling variables, βN and β √ N: this is why the two edges in (17) separate as N grows. We use the REM language for the softmax weights seen from a fixed query, which is where Lemma 1 applies, and do not claim a thermodynamic phase tran- sition of the network at finite M.

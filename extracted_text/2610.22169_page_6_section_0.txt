We run them in the precision of the released weights and report pairs of values in this order. Text comes from WikiText-103, cut into sequences of 128 to- kens. The typical attention outputs are estimated on 256 sequences of the training split, and every measurement uses the test split and every layer. The Jacobian lens is fitted with its reference imple- mentation on 100 prompts. We omit the tuned lens, which is trained on the model’s output, the quantity on which readings are scored, and is bounded like every lens. As a supervised reference we add a linear probe, a ridge regression of the centred logit on the states, fitted on separate text. Divergences are Kullback–Leibler divergences from the model’s output to a reading, in nats, and averages over lay- ers are unweighted. LEXFRAME[∞] is the reference for readings and the exact frame in interventions. 4.1 Comparison with Lenses We compare frames and lenses in the directions along which they measure a label and in the distri- butions that they assign to a state. At intermediate layers, LEXFRAME[∞] departs sharply from the out- put frame: at mid-depth its lexical readouts have a cosine of only 0.07 and 0.26 with those of the last layer, and their coherence across contexts is 0.09 and 0.12. We compare each method’s direction for a label, the gradient of its reading or its fixed vec- tor, with these readouts for 64 labels across the fre- quency ranking. LEXFRAME[1] follows them closely from the middle layers on, with a layer-averaged cosine of 0.89 and 0.87, because it computes the same transport and withholds only the attention among the prefix positions. The lenses and the probe average between 0.21 and 0.36, and their failure is structural rather than a matter of fitting. The Jacobian lens stays at or below the bound √κℓ at every layer and the supervised probe falls further below it, whereas at mid-depth, where the bound is 0.30 and 0.35, LEXFRAME[1] reaches 0.97 and 0.97: no single direction matches a readout that changes with the context. LEXFRAME[2] raises the mean cosine to 0.95 and 0.95: LEXFRAME[1] misses the exchange within the prefix.

In this work, we presented WeGR, a unified generative retrieval framework for industrial search systems in WeChat. By combining collaborative-aware SID construction with progressive preference optimization, WeGR effectively improves retrieval and ranking performance while addressing codebook collapse and reward spar- sity. Extensive offline experiments and large-scale online A/B tests demonstrate its effectiveness in improving business outcomes and reducing serving costs, highlighting the potential of generative retrieval to simplify industrial search systems. Furthermore, evalu- ations in an independent live-stream search scenario demonstrate the applicability of DHPO across different industrial search settings.

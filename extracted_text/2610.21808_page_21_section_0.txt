For readers the two scales of a unit with a second plane are re-solved jointly by an exact 2 × 2 weighted least squares in the Σ metric, which decouples per unit because Σ acts on the input axis. For writers there is no joint refit: H acts on the axis the scales index, so the exact refit is one coupled 2n × 2n system and a per-unit shortcut would cancel H out; the coupling is handled inside the GPTQ recursion instead. Probe protocol. The scored span is the chat-rendered answer tokens of a knowledge item (end marker included, as the evaluation scores it) or the second half of each 384-token C4 window; prompt tokens serve as context and are never scored. The Fisher is accumulated in fp32 over every quantized weight, one forward-backward pass per item over 720 items, and kept in two interleaved item halves for the split-half check (17 GB of host memory at 1.7B; probes at 8B and above need an H200). The budget over the one-plane body targets 2.156 bpw for the Base rows (+0.556 / 0.562 / 0.566 / 0.567 code bits at 0.6B / 1.7B / 8B / 14B and Llama-8B) and 1.994 for the post-trained rows (+0.40); (layer, kind) cells whose selected fraction falls below 2% are dropped as noise, which leaves about 180–200 cells with a second plane.

4.1 Completion and improvement during search All sixty searches completed. Held-out measurements were available for 599 of 600 generation-winner panels. The missing MountainCar-v0 search-seed-8 panel was at generation six and did not affect any endpoint pair. Median selected-winner search scores increased be- tween the first and final generations in five environments. CartPole-v1 winners were already at the score ceiling in generation one. Newly evaluated candidate medians rose between gener- ations one and ten in all six tasks, including CartPole-v1. Figure 2 shows these population changes, extending the evidence beyond retention of strong initial winners. These distributions contain newly evaluated candidates, not retained elites or held-out winners. Each score aver- ages ten search episodes; failed or discarded candidates without scores are excluded. The LunarLander-v3 dis- play is restricted to returns between −1000 and 350: 198 scores outside this range remain in the calculations. Candidate-score distributions show bimodality in some tasks. This motivated an exploratory analysis of whether fresh proposals, edits, and remixes contribute differently to the score mixture.

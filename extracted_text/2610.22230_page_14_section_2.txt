We present the first comprehensive study demonstrating that AMD MI350X GPUs achieve performance parity with NVIDIA B200 for production DLRM training through cross- stack optimizations spanning core ROCm libraries, kernel tuning, and model co-design. Our work addresses architec- tural differences between vendors—memory capacity, atomic performance, instruction sets—through principled algorithmic adaptations, hardware-aware autotuning, and HBM-conscious model co-design, providing a transferable framework for hardware-agnostic training at scale. Our optimizations have been upstreamed and open-sourced, establishing a foundation for multi-vendor ML infrastructure.

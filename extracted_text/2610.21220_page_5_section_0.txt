Existing approaches operate directly on dense, high-interference task vec- tors, forcing the model to simultaneously handle parameter con- flicts and objective trade-offs. In contrast, RecSoup decouples these challenges: it performs conflict-aware pruning prior to coefficient learning. This ensures that the gating network navigates a purified parameter subspace, allowing it to focus on learning precise coor- dinates on the Pareto manifold rather than compensating for noise or interference inherent in dense vectors.

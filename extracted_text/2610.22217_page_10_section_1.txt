This paper proposes SCOPED, aiming at integrating complementary visual understanding capabil- ities into a unified Vision-Language Model through skill-decoupled on-policy expert distillation. SCOPED independently optimizes capability-specific experts and abstracts reusable solution pat- terns from reward-evaluated trajectories into explicit capability skills. During on-policy distillation, these skills guide capability experts to provide structured supervision for the unified student. Our experiments demonstrate that SCOPED consistently improves performance across diverse visual understanding capabilities. Our further analyses reveal cross-capability interference during mixed- capability optimization and show that skill augmentation improves the preservation and transfer of specialized expert capabilities.

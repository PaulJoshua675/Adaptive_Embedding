B.6 Illustrative decoding trajectories We show four trajectories with verifier-accepted final answers—one for each policy and mathematical-reasoning dataset—and one unsuccessful full-canvas trajectory. All use T = 32. These examples were selected for readable outputs and visible ordinary-token edits. In particular, an accepted terminal answer does not certify every intermediate reasoning step or establish that a displayed edit caused the success. The canvases retain the recorded token-level displays. A square denotes a masked position; a prefixed + marks a reveal, a prefixed −a remask, and a prefixed ∗a changed re-reveal. These prefixes are display annotations. Each snapshot is labeled by the number of decoder calls already consumed, while active-block indices in the recorded canvases are zero-based. Deterministic finalizations are identified explicitly, and earlier finalized blocks remain frozen. The prompt instructions are omitted from the figures, while the task is given in each caption. 39

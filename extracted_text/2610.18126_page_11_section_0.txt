This is why the ledger records dependencies with the choice. Derivation of Eq. 3. A decision is correct when the model keeps it if it should stand and switches it if it should change. Conditioning on the label and, for decisions that should change, on whether a deci- sive item was delivered, the law of total probability gives A = (1 −π) Pr[keep | stand] + πρ Pr[switch | delivered] + π(1 −ρ) Pr[switch | missed], which is Eq. 3. The identity holds for every router and model; the theory’s content lies in the predic- tions about its terms. Comparing routers. Consider two routers used with the same model, with recalls ρ and ρ′ and keep rates K and K′. If S and L are the same under both, Eq. 3 gives A′ −A = π (ρ′ −ρ)(S −L) + (1 −π)(K′ −K). A router that reaches more decisive news gains ac- curacy in proportion to S −L, the margin by which the model acts on delivered news over undelivered news, and loses it through the misleading items that lower K. Strong judgment makes S −L large and keeps K stable, so recall dominates; weak judgment shrinks S −L and lowers K with every delivery, so exposure dominates. This is prediction P3 in closed form. B Decision Streams The generators, the three streams and their SHA- 256 hashes are released with the paper; regenerat- ing each stream from its seed reproduces the hash recorded with the model answers. Every label is computed from timestamps and stated facts, and every stream is checked by assertions before use. DIRECT and STRESS. Decision times fall within the first three days of a six-day stream. Lags are drawn uniformly from 3–12 or 24–72 hours with equal probability, and action times fall in domain windows (dinner 19:00–22:00, trains 06:00–20:00, orders 08:00–20:00, permits on weekdays 09:00– 15:30, hikes 07:00–13:00). Decisive items are pub- lished inside the revision window and block the option from before ta until at least an hour after the activity; late items start at least one hour after the activity ends. Two DIRECT notices: “Notice from Juniper Barrel: closed for a private event from Tue 13 Oct 2026, 14:40 until Wed 14 Oct 2026, 01:10.” (names the option) and “City notice: planned power cut in the Riverside district from Wed 14 Oct 2026, 14:40 to Thu 15 Oct 2026, 05:10; restaurants and shops there will close.” (names the stated depen- dency). The generator asserts that td < tr < ta, that every decisive item lies in the revision window and overlaps the activity, that no item targets op- tion 2, and that late items never overlap the activity. For STRESS, it recomputes all labels after placing decoys and paraphrasing notices, and asserts that no gold answer or exclusion changed and that no decoy relates to any decision. INDIRECT. Each domain (walk-in dinners, rail journeys, day hikes, in-store pickups) has 16 bun- dles of a dependency, its other name, its upstream entity and four facilities. Each bundle serves one decision acting on days 2–3 and one on days 5–6, so the two are never open together; lags are 5–12 or 18–28 hours. A blocking notice for the activity starts between six hours and 15 minutes before it and ends at least 30 minutes after it; a late one starts at least an hour after the activity ends; a past one ends at least 45 minutes before it starts. Late, past and harmless notices use the four ways of reference equally, and blocking, late and past notices share templates, so only their times differ.

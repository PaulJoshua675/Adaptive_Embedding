People rarely manipulate with their hands alone. We brace a box against the chest, cradle a basket across both arms, or tuck an object under one arm to free the other. These actions use the limbs and torso as a single highly dexterous manipulator. We call this ability to reposition objects by continually changing supporting body contacts whole-body dexterous manipulation and apply it directly to humanoids.

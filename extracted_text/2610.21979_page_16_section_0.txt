P2TPO: Structure-Aligned Page-to-Token Credit Assignment for Full-Page Document Parsing A.3. Scope of reported parsing recipes Recipe scope and auxiliary tasks. HunyuanOCR’s row describes its document-parsing RL recipe, not every task supported by the model. LightOnOCR’s row covers its OCR recipe; its separate bounding-box recipe uses IoU-based localization rewards and is not element transcription. PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 uses layout detection followed by task-prompted region recognition; its RL tasks include text, formulas, and tables as well as charts, seals, and spotting. DocPO’s row covers mixed text/formula/table patch recognition. Its step-aware sharpening follows the training step and still yields one response coefficient. Document question answering. DocR1 (Xiong et al., 2025) and DocThinker (Yu et al., 2025b) optimize answers and reasoning rather than page transcriptions, so they are outside the table’s parsing targets. DocR1’s EviGRPO sums format, answer ANLS, and evidence-page F1 rewards. DocThinker combines format, answer accuracy, RoI IoU, and question-rephrasing rewards before GRPO. Both use a Sum →Scalar interface. B. For type-matched text, formula, and table blocks, we compare predicted token pieces with pieces from the aligned reference using SequenceMatcher with autojunk=False. Comparison keys strip leading and trailing whitespace from each piece. Equal pieces receive label +1, and inserted or replaced predicted pieces receive −1. A reference deletion assigns −1 to the next predicted token, or the final token at the block boundary, overriding any earlier match label. For nonempty reference-piece sequences, whitespace-only pieces and standalone display-math delimiter pieces are then assigned 0. Error labels seed the error mask. We merge error runs separated by at most 𝑔non-error tokens, including the intervening tokens in the merged mask. Positive labels outside this mask form the correct mask. SCR selects the error mask for a negative block coefficient and the correct mask for a positive coefficient. Redistribution requires the raw block reward to be at least its type-specific quality threshold. Algorithm 2 converts the selected mask into nonnegative weights with unit mean over the block. Token mapping. Predicted pieces use response-text token offsets; reference pieces use tokenizer offsets without special tokens, with per-token decoding as a fallback.

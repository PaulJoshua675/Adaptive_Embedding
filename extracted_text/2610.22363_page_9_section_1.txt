We introduced P3-WAM, a unified world action model for egocentric dexterous manipulation that integrates perception, prediction, and planning. A coupled video–action transformer connects visual generation with explicit hand and camera trajectories and supports all three modes within the same model. Through extensive experiments, we show that unified training benefits hand reconstruction, action-conditioned video prediction, and planning. Limitations. Estimating the global 3D hand position from monocular video is inherently ambiguous. Incorporating tactile or depth sensing could provide stronger geometric constraints. Our robot experiments currently rely on open-loop execution. Introducing closed-loop feedback would allow the robot to correct its motion online, potentially improving robustness over longer-horizon interactions. 9

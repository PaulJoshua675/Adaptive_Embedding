Returning items on a pending order is a separate operation with its own dedicated tool. The user needs to confirm the order id and the list of items to be returned. The user needs to provide a payment method to receive the refund. The refund must either go to the original payment method, or an existing gift card. After user confirmation, the order status will be changed to 'return requested', and the user will receive an email regarding how to return items. ## Exchange delivered order The exchange-delivered-order action is the tool for exchanging items from a delivered order. In particular, remember to remind the customer to confirm they have provided all items to be exchanged. For a delivered order, an item may be exchanged for an available new item -- commonly the same product with a different product option, and in some cases a different product. The user must provide a payment method to pay or receive refund of the price difference. If the user provides a gift card, it must have enough balance to cover the price difference. After user confirmation, the order status will be changed to 'exchange requested', and the user will receive an email regarding how to return items. There is no need to place a new order. Our synthetic user turns are short, colloquial, and carry one clear intent (“Yeah, sure, it’s Nico Ferri. . . could you get me a printable return label”); the genuine τ 2-bench side more often reads as gen- erated, with meta assistant boilerplate and long, enumerated requests. That difference in register, not domain content, is what the battery preserves, and why neither human nor model reviewers reli- ably recover the τ 2-bench conversation. A.6 Human study protocol The three reviewers of §4.3 were given the C3 judge’s criteria (§A.4) through a review por- tal, which asked which conversation was human- authored and took a confidence rating; pairs an- swered “not sure” are the undecided ones excluded from the 59. They were colleagues at our institu- tion, unpaid, uninvolved in the project, not report- ing to any author, and free to decline. They were not told beforehand that this was a research study, since disclosure could bias the judgment; all three were debriefed afterward and consented to our use of their judgments. We collected no demographics, and the protocol was not submitted to an ethics review board: no personal data was collected and the task was to rate fictional dialogue. 14

We expand each domain’s registry with synthetic APIs and trajectories drawn from a co-occurrence graph, so that each is a realistic in-context path. Every one clears blocking gates for ground-truth validity, non- substitutability against real tools, API disjointness, and non-cloning (99.8% of retail and 99.9% of tele- com clear the last), with accept-sets seeded where a different real tool may legitimately substitute for the gold call (Table 10, Appendix A.3). The diagnostic C3 judge compared real τ 2 tra- jectories against our synthetic ones and picked ours as the human-authored one in 57.0% of re- tail and 68.9% of telecom pairs. A blind human study agreed: told that exactly one of each pair was human-authored, three reviewers picked ours in 40 of 59 decided pairs, 67.8% of the time (Figure 5, Appendix A.5). Our τ 2-style policy prompts are validated the same way, so that no clause contradicts any gold trajectory (C6, Appendix A.4). 4.4 Execution regimes We evaluate in two execution regimes, differing only in what is appended to the context between steps. Teacher-forcing records each selection but feeds the benchmark’s gold call forward in its place, so a wrong selection at step i cannot corrupt step i+1. This is our primary regime because it measures the front-end in isolation, which is what this paper contributes. Under free-running, the agent’s own emitted call and its reasoning are appended and carried forward, so errors compound: a wrong selection changes the prefix the retriever conditions on. An inference episode ends when our grader confirms the task is complete, or after a cap of 20 steps. Our grader scores a turn by the database hash its gold actions produce, plus the read operations a read-only turn must contain (Appendix A.15). 4.5 Algorithms We test TRACER against two baselines that ig- nore execution history and two variants of our own. LLM-ONLY places the entire registry in context at every step, as a post-disclosure domain list, upper-bounding candidate coverage at maxi- mal cost. VECTOR ranks the full registry by the cosine similarity of the embedded step context to each tool’s description and returns the top k, the same mechanism the confidence gate falls back to (§3.7). TRACER-fixed is an ablation: it fixes the blend instead of predicting it, scoring s(t) = L(t)+P(t), and is identical to TRACER in every other respect, so the gap between them isolates the value of the learned blend. TRACER+G is a configuration for registries whose calls concentrate in context- independent utility tools that no state prior sur- faces; it unions a small, fixed, per-fold global set G into every candidate set after ranking, K = TOPK(s, k) ∪G. G holds the |G| = 3 tools with the highest prefix entropy H(S | t), the entropy over the states from which t is the gold next call, so it collects the tools whose calls spread most widely across the trie (§A.8). 4.6 Models, metrics, and protocol Models and metrics.

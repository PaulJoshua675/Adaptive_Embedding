OS-Helix-27B and OS-Helix-35B-A3B achieve relative improvements of 35.0% and 33.7% over their matched Qwen3.5 hybrid baselines, while OS-Helix-27B-RL further improves by 42.9% to reach 66.44% success. OS-Helix-27B-RL also transfers to WindowsAgentArena with 63.87% suc- cess, surpassing GPT-5-based framework methods on both benchmarks. Ablations and behavioral analyses show that SFT provides the main gain, hybrid supervision outperforms GUI-only super- vision at a fixed scale, and learned routing improves both execution efficiency and task coverage beyond code access alone. Under matched RL settings, Step-balanced GSPO outperforms stan- dard GSPO and mitigates its drift toward code-intensive behavior, while RL increases Pass@3 from 70.88% to 76.44%, indicating broader discovery of successful trajectories across independent at- tempts. These findings establish native GUI/code coordination as a learnable hybrid paradigm and provide a reproducible foundation for further research on hybrid computer-using agents. 11

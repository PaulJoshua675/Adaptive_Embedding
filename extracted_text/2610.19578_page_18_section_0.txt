A subsequent PCA projects these high-dimensional representations into a compact 64-dimensional latent space. To establish a rigorous data partitioning scheme, we randomly isolate 10% of the items to form the strict cold-start group. The interaction records of this subset are completely masked during the training phase to preclude any potential data leakage. The remaining 90% of items constitute the warm-start group, and their historical interactions are randomly partitioned into training, validation, and test sets according to a 7:1:2 ratio. 5.1.3 Evaluation Metrics To rigorously evaluate the ranking performance in conventional warm-start scenarios, we adopt a full-ranking strategy. For each user in the test set, the model computes preference scores and globally ranks the ground-truth interacted items against all unobserved items. Conversely, when assessing out-of-distribution cold-start performance, it is crucial to eliminate the con- founding effect of popularity bias from trending items. Therefore, the candidate pool for the full ranking evaluation is strictly restricted to the pure cold-start item set. We employ Recall@𝐾and NDCG@𝐾as our primary evaluation metrics. The final reported performance results are the average scores across all users in the test set.

Preprint Optimization and stage transitions. The recipe uses AdamW with (β1, β2) = (0.9, 0.99) and global gradient-norm clipping at 1. Both models use weight decay 0.01 during flow matching and WSD. An optimizer update contains 1,024 channel rows. Terminal decay continues the flow model with its optimizer and EMA state. PDD initializes the student from the terminal teacher’s EMA weights, freezes that teacher, and starts a fresh optimizer. Calibration resumes the PDD student, optimizer, and EMA. Data and task sampling. Training uses a 83M-row dataset of real-world and synthetic time series, including both univariate and multivariate data. Training samples contexts up to 8,192 time steps and forecast horizons up to 480 time steps. During training, we randomly sample context and forecast lengths within these limits. Post-training objectives. PDD trains the backbone and twenty interval heads on a uniform time grid, using the block-size menu {1, 2, 5, 10, 20}, on-policy states, Euler teacher labels, and tiled residual heads. Calibration retains path-wise PDD and adds fair CRPS with coefficient one on each loss, globally normalized over scored coordinates. The CRPS term uses eight iid paths, one parallel evaluation, and the same Gaussian source scale as flow training and inference. Main benchmark results use the final EMA checkpoint after all four stages. The architecture, distillation, and refinement controls below retain their stated training budgets.

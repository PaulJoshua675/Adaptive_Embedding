A fixed-point curve in the CTM the query at its first-tick value leaves 97% of the fixed point’s motion, both overall and in each image’s own part. Handing each image another image’s schedule moves its curve about a third of the way towards that image’s curve (0.35 of the distance between the two), while each step keeps the direction of the image’s own (cosine 0.86 once the shared curve is removed, against 0.00 with the other image’s step). The schedule thus shifts each image’s curve, which matters for the decision, while the curve’s motion comes mostly from the recurrence and its memory.

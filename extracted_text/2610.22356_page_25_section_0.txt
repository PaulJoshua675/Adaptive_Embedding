DK-VLM: Geometry as Position A.12 Comparison of Geometry Injection Strategies We compare different geometry injection strategies based on Qwen3-VL-8B using the same 10% training data subset and optimization settings. All configurations omit the pairwise geometric loss. In addition to Qwen SFT and DK-VLM, we consider three alternatives: adding 3D positional embeddings to visual tokens once at the input; adding 3D positional embeddings to visual tokens at the same five selected decoder layers as DK-VLM, while retaining single- stream attention at each layer; and applying single-kernel GTA at three or five selected decoder layers, directly replacing the positional encoding in the corresponding channels of the native attention. DK-VLM scores 62.78 in this comparison; the score of 63.05 reported in the injection layer experiments (Appendix A.9) corresponds to the five-layer configuration with the pairwise geometric loss retained. Table 14. Comparison of geometry injection strategies on VSI-Bench. All configurations omit the pairwise geometric loss. Configuration VSI-Bench Overall ↑ Qwen3-VL-8B + SFT 59.48 3D positional embeddings at input 59.21 3D positional embeddings at five layers 59.74 Single-kernel GTA (three layers) 45.98 Single-kernel GTA (five layers) 39.14 DK-VLM-8B (five layers) 62.78 Adding 3D positional embeddings at the input and at five layers yields scores of 59.21 and 59.74, respectively, close to the Qwen SFT score of 59.48. In contrast, DK-VLM without the pairwise geometric loss still achieves 62.78, outperforming Qwen SFT and five-layer embedding addition by 3.30 and 3.04 points, respectively (Table 14). This shows that, under the current training settings, dual-kernel geometry injection outperforms the two tested additive positional embedding strategies. Single-kernel GTA scores 45.98 with three injection layers and 39.14 with five, both below the Qwen SFT baseline. With five injection layers, DK-VLM outperforms single-kernel GTA by 23.64 points. In the five-layer single-kernel configuration, the performance drop is accompanied by garbled outputs and poorer instruction following, including responses that do not follow the required answer format. These observations suggest that directly replacing the native positional encoding can impair model performance and output quality in this setting. Overall, these results provide empirical support for our design of retaining the native attention pathway while introducing spatial relations through a separate geometric kernel. 25

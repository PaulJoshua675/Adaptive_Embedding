Panel intervals use 4,000 paired percentile bootstrap draws of the 200 source sentences, retaining all 17 translations and both models together. Per-language intervals instead resample that language’s items, again keeping models paired. The panel ratio pools margins before division, implicitly weighting languages by each model’s mean base margins. It therefore differs from both an unweighted mean of language ratios and an average of language differences under common weights. The item contrast gives equal weight to each defined item.

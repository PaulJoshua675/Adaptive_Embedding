SUPRA-Tex Supplementary Material This supplement provides method and training details, evaluation protocols, additional results, and extended discussion. The guide below links to each appendix and selected subsections; page numbers indicate where each appendix begins. • Appendix A: Additional Method Details (p. 19). Geometry and camera projection, visible-surface observations, and boundary filtering; the interleaved flow transformer, including token dimensions and its read-only update schedule. The training objective and inference procedure are given in Sec. 3.5. • Appendix B: Training Data and Optimization (p. 20). Training data, the 2D material estimator F2D, and 3D generator training, including observation construction, optimization, and checkpoint selection. • Appendix C: Evaluation Protocols (p. 21). Evaluation datasets, rendering cameras, alignment, and metrics, baseline configurations, and commercial comparisons. • Appendix D: 2D Material Evaluation (p. 23). 2D material prediction accuracy and F2D runtime measurements. Reference-view versus novel-view fidelity and 3D sampling efficiency are reported in Secs. 4.1 and 4.5. • Appendix E: Ablation and Observation Analyses (p. 24). Ablation settings, the partial-versus-complete encoding diagnostic protocol, observed-support encoding versus zero filling, and early learning with native PBR latents. • Appendix F: Additional Qualitative Comparisons (p. 26). Additional visual comparisons for given-geometry texturing, end-to-end image-to-3D generation, and commercial systems, extending the main-paper examples. • Appendix G: Additional Views and Texture Editing (p. 26). Synthetic back-view conditions and image-based texture editing. Limitations and failure cases are discussed in Sec. 5. • Appendix H: Notation Reference (p. 29). A consolidated symbol table with dimensions and definitions for the physical material channels, surface and latent coordinates, observation metadata, and generator states. 18

The exactness is per step; the order of the steps remains GPTQ’s greedy activation order. The reader refit. For a reader the objective is P i(wi −aiti)⊤H(wi −aiti), a sum over rows with no coupling between them. The sweep rounds each row onto the grid {−ai, 0, ai}, with ai from the data-free exact step, compensating along the row. At the realized codes the objective is a quadratic in the scalar ai, so its minimiser a⋆ i = t⊤ i Hwi/t⊤ i Hti is closed-form, and since the scale the sweep used is a feasible point of the same problem, the refit can only lower the error; with H = I it reduces to the mean magnitude of Eq. 1. Two planes on a reader are the same argument with two scales, a 2 × 2 system per row, and a writer has no such decoupled refit because H acts on the axis its scales index (Appendix E).

Structure-augmented approaches organize knowledge for retrieval and reasoning. HippoRAG and HippoRAG 2 exploit graph-based associations (Gutiérrez et al., 2024, 2025), while GraphRAG and RAPTOR use graph-community and hier- archical summaries (Edge et al., 2024; Sarthi et al., 2024). EvLink retrieves bridge passages through source-grounded evidence links (Anony- mous, 2026a). OriginRAG preserves demand– passage provenance during graph retrieval and grounds supported demands in verbatim source spans (Anonymous, 2026b). SG-RAG aligns sub- graphs with query constraints (Xie et al., 2026), and LogicRAG constructs query-dependent reasoning structures (Chen et al., 2026a). SetR addresses pas- sage selection through set-level reasoning, identi- fying information requirements and selecting com- plementary passages (Lee et al., 2025b). 2.2 Iterative RAG Reasoning-driven iteration uses intermediate findings to guide subsequent searches. IRCoT in- terleaves chain-of-thought reasoning with retrieval (Trivedi et al., 2023), while self-ask generates inter- mediate questions (Press et al., 2023). S2G-RAG assesses evidence sufficiency and identifies infor- mation gaps to guide further retrieval (Li et al., 2026). Related planning approaches, including Re- WOO, PlanRAG, and STRIDE, use explicit plans to organize retrieval (Xu et al., 2023; Lee et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2026b).

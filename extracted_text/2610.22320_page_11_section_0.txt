Additional comparisons and results are in the supplementary materials. 4.2 Effectiveness of Cross-Contextual Bridging Building upon the empirical observation in Sec. 3.1 that the ⟨EOS⟩embedding acts as a global semantic control point, we conduct a quantitative evaluation in addition to providing qualitative samples. As shown in Tab. 2, injecting appear- ance information solely into the subject token fails to reliably encode identity cues. Distributing the same information across all non-EOS tokens partially im- proves alignment, yet the gains remain limited, suggesting that localized token- level injection struggles to propagate appearance semantics consistently dur- ing generation. In contrast, modifying only the ⟨EOS⟩embedding consistently achieves the highest text–image alignment and perceptual similarity.

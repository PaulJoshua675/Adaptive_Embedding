Ac- curacy on changing decisions is a routing quan- tity, and recall follows the index (Figure 3). Name matching reaches only news that names the op- tion. The decision text reaches every notice that names the option or its stated dependency, 37.5% of those that use another name, and 12.5% of those that name an upstream entity, so Claude reaches 78.9%. Adding the dossier to the index reaches ev- ery decisive notice and lifts Claude to 93.0% (22–4, p < 0.001), and watch conditions lift it to 91.4% (21–5, p = 0.002). When news names the stated dependency, as on STRESS, the decision text is a sufficient index: BM25 delivers 100.0% of decisive items and lifts the three models from 72.0–73.3% with name matching to 85.3–98.7% (53–6 pooled, p < 0.001), as accurate as oracle routing of all re- lated news for Claude (97.3% and 96.0%) and GPT (98.7% and 100.0%). Watch conditions, dense sim- ilarity and idealised dependency queries route as many items there, and pooled over models BM25 wins more discordant decisions against each (8–5, 11–4 and 6–3). P3: misleading deliveries cost correct decisions. Every item delivered to a decision that should stand is misleading, and Figure 4b shows its cost: Claude keeps at least 87.5% of these decisions under every router, while Qwen3-235B and Ministral 8B keep fewer than half under every BM25 and dense router. Indexing the whole dossier delivers 89 of the 96 trap notices, because every fact it holds becomes a match. Watch conditions deliver 92.2% of the decisive notices and 20 traps, close to the 17 of the decision text alone, because the model writes conditions only about what the activity needs. Un- der the retrieval and broadcast routers, a delivered trap raises the rate of needless switches for ev- ery model: from 9.4% to 14.0% for Claude, from 69.7% to 74.8% for Qwen3-235B and from 71.5% to 84.1% for Ministral 8B. Under retrieval rout- ing, the weaker models also switch on 20–67% of the changing decisions whose decisive notice never arrived, prompted by other news, so their gains from recall come with losses on K.

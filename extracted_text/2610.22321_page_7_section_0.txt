For AVC-Reward, we use SAVC as its overall prediction, while the human reference follows the overall score defined in App. D.1. We report Pairwise, ∆-Pairwise, PLCC (5PL), and SRCC. Since audio-video consistency judgments can be subjective for clips with very similar human scores, ∆-Pairwise computes pair- wise accuracy only on pairs whose human-score difference is more than ∆. We use ∆= 0.2 as the main setting and report other thresholds in Fig. 3. Overall, AVC-Reward agrees with human judgment better than all automatic baselines. Table 2 shows the best result on all four measures. Fig. 3 gives the same trend across thresholds: as ∆ increases, the human-score differences become clearer and pairwise accuracy improves accordingly. This matches the expected behavior of a human-aligned evaluator: clearer human differences lead to more accurate pairwise judgments.

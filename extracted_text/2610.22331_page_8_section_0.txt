Action-based grouping improves ESR to 79.4% but its singleton ratio is still 72.0%, indicating that even when two trajec- tories issue similar queries, the evidence they end up acquiring is rarely comparable. Knowledge- based grouping raises ESR to 97.8% and reduces singletons to 19.0%, while keeping cross-rollout coverage (4.66) close to the highest value across anchors. This last metric is important: it confirms that the gain is not an artifact of merging adjacent steps within the same rollout, but reflects alignment of semantically equivalent steps across indepen- dent trajectories. A qualitative case in Appendix D shows two steps with 0.987 retrieval similarity that are correctly separated by the knowledge anchor (one has acquired 0 of 12 conditions, the other 8 of 12), illustrating that surface similarity can hide drastic differences in actual search progress. 4.5 Search Behavior and Training Efficiency KnowGPO learns to search adaptively, not just less. Reducing redundant search is easy if the policy simply stops earlier on every query, but this would harm hard cases. To test for this failure mode, we partition 500 test queries by joint solving outcome and compare average search turns (Table 6). On queries solved exclusively by KnowGPO, KnowGPO uses 9.83 turns versus 11.55 for GRPO, a clear reduction in redundant exploration. On queries solved only by GRPO, KnowGPO still uses 10.36 turns, and on queries where both methods fail, it uses 10.85, the longest of all subsets. Knowledge-grouped credit adds negligible train- ing cost. A natural concern with structured pro- cess supervision is overhead. We profile per- iteration time over three training epochs (Figure 4). The training budget is dominated by operations shared with GRPO: trajectory generation (2,574s), policy update (583s), and probability computation (310s). KnowGPO-specific operations are knowl- edge verification (356s), incremental gain compu- tation (2.73s), and group construction (0.03s). Veri- fication runs asynchronously and overlaps with the next rollout, so its wall-clock cost is hidden behind generation; the two pure-bookkeeping operations together account for less than 0.08% of the budget.

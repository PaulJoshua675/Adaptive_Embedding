OmniVerifier-C1: Decomposed Graded Verifier for Rule-Governed Generative World Modeling • We introduce a Decoupled RL training strategy that jointly optimizes sub-question-level binary judgment and global alignment scoring, using precise 0/1 feedback on individual sub-questions to develop fine-grained verification capabilities that provide a foundation for reliable scoring. • We demonstrate that the proposed verifier serves as an effective generative reward model for reinforcement learning, improving performance in both traditional text-to-image generation and rule-governed generative world modeling tasks. • We provide empirical validation across diverse structured reasoning environments, showing that accurate pairwise decomposed verifiers are a key enabler for transforming generative models into rule-governed world-modeling agents. 2

2.1 Preprocessing: event2time algorithm We extend here the event2time algorithm (Gattepaille and Muzy, 2025). We base the preprocessing of event-based datas on inverse average Inter-Spike Intervals (ISIs). The average ISI is computed as: ISIi = tlast i −tfirst i ni −1 (1) where ni is the number of spikes of neuron i, tfirst i is the timing of the first spike of neuron i, and tlast i the timing of its last spike. Comparison of average ISIs with other classic transformations used in SNNs is illustrated on figure 1. We use the average ISI of neuron i as a proxy of its spiking time: ti = ISIi (2) If we were to compute the ISIs on the whole sequence, any meaningful information would be averaged out.

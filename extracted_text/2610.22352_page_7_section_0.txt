Enlarged crops compare reference-view details; novel views show the completed appearance. 4 Experiments The supplement covers datasets, implementation, baseline configurations, evaluation protocols, and additional analyses. Datasets. Our 3D material generator is trained solely on publicly available 3D datasets. The training sources are summarized in Appendix B.1. We evaluate on two datasets with no overlap with the training set: DTC-Tex, comprising 100 texture-rich product scans selected from the Digital Twin Catalog (Dong et al., 2025), and Complex-Tex, comprising 110 Sketchfab (Sketchfab, n.d.) meshes with denser inscriptions, finer patterns, and more varied shapes. Implementation details. The 2D material estimator adapts FireRed-Image-Edit-1.1 (Super Intelligence Team, 2026) at 1024 × 1024 resolution. The 3D generator uses 60 Transformer blocks, with self-attention initialized from TRELLIS.2 and the remaining components initialized randomly; the pretrained material encoder and decoder remain frozen. We train the generator with a global batch size of 576 using AdamW on 64 GPUs. Inference uses 25 Euler steps without classifier-free guidance. 4.1 3D Generation with Given Geometry Setting and baselines. Each method textures the ground-truth mesh from a single forward-lit reference image. We record the ground-truth lighting and camera poses and render each textured result under these same conditions at the reference view and 15 novel views, isolating material quality from geometry, pose, and illumination differences. Table 1 compares ten baselines spanning native 3D generation and dedicated texturing methods. PBR methods are additionally evaluated on albedo, metallic, and roughness. Results. Table 1 shows that SUPRA-Tex leads across all shaded and PBR metrics on both datasets. Compared with feature-conditioned Pixal3D⋆, shaded PSNR improves by 1.7 dB and 1.2 dB, and albedo PSNRfg by 2.0 dB and 0.9 dB on DTC-Tex and Complex-Tex, respectively. The gains over multi-view PBR methods are larger: LumiTex trails by more than 2 dB in shaded PSNR and by over 1.5 dB in albedo PSNR on both datasets.

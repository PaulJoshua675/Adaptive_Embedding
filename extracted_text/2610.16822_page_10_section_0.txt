(c) Density-normalized relation- channel enrichment; values above 1 indicate above-density attention. 6 Discussion Limitations. Our architecture-prediction evaluation focuses on NAS-Bench-101 and NAS-Bench-201. Transfer to other search spaces requires aligned operation vocabularies and dependency semantics, since graph nodes may represent computations at different granularities. Dense directed-relation masks and pairwise attention also incur quadratic storage and attention costs as graph size grows. The effect of improved predictor ranking on end-to-end NAS remains to be evaluated. Future work. We will investigate a shared operation-level representation for cross-space pretraining and adaptation, and explore sparse or hierarchical attention with compact reachability representations to support larger computational graphs. We also plan to integrate DTA-GT into NAS pipelines and evaluate the selected architectures and total search cost under a fixed end-to-end evaluation budget. Appendix G elaborates on these directions. 7

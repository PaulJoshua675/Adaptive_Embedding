Alt text: Four-panel study-design figure. Panel A is a schematic of a five-stage extraction pipeline: keyword filtering of clinical notes, independent classification by two large language models (a clinical-NLP expert and a GI/neurology specialist), a consensus gate requiring both to agree, aggregation into a patient-by-timepoint matrix, and evaluation against ground truth. Panel B is an icon array of the cohort showing the 115 patients screened, of whom 108 had QC-confirmed sustained exposure and 56 form the expert-annotated diarrhea ground truth. Panel C is a donut chart of retrieved-corpus composition by note type, with telephone encounters the largest slice at 22.7 percent. Panel D lists the 18 extracted phenotypes grouped into 4 adverse effects, 13 Rett clinical features, and medication refusal.

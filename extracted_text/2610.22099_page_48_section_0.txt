D.1 Datasets, validation split, and prompts The mathematical-reasoning test population is the union of all 1,319 GSM8K test problems and all 500 MATH-500 problems (Cobbe et al., 2021; Hendrycks et al., 2021; Lightman et al., 2024). The 1,024-problem validation set contains 512 GSM8K and 512 MATH problems drawn from the training sources and excluded from the gradient-training input.

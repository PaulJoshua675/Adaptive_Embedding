We studied evaluation awareness in five Indic lan- guages alongside English. A cue that signals a safety evaluation raises refusal of harmful prompts in every model and language. On average, the rise in Indic languages is at least as large as in English. Refusal rises even with a cue that never uses the words test or evaluation. A safety test that a model can recognize may therefore make Indic safety look better than it is. Evaluation awareness is not an English-only problem, and we must con- sider it when designing multilingual safety evalua- tions. We release our dataset, implementation, and experimental framework; the source code is avail- able at: https://github.com/innovate637/ IndicEvalAwareness/ Limitations 1. Model coverage: Due to resource constraints, we test only open-weight models of 24B to 72B parameters, with reasoning disabled. Larger models and reasoning models are not covered. 4

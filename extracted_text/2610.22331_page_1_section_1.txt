Deep search agents solve knowledge-intensive tasks through long-horizon, multi-turn inter- actions, where assigning credit to individ- ual search decisions is fundamentally diffi- cult. Outcome-based reinforcement learning provides only a single terminal reward shared across an entire trajectory. Recent process- supervision methods aim to assign credit at finer granularity, but they typically anchor credit to either surface-level trajectory states or model-internal signals, both of which be- come unreliable in deep search where observa- tions are heterogeneous and retrieved contexts are long and noisy. We propose KnowGPO, a reinforcement learning framework that aligns trajectories by the task-relevant knowledge they have acquired rather than by their surface ob- servations. For each query, KnowGPO de- composes the information required to derive the answer into atomic knowledge conditions, and represents the state of a trajectory at each step by the subset of conditions it has satis- fied. Trajectories sharing the same knowledge state are grouped for within-group comparison, and the incremental knowledge gain between consecutive steps serves as an environment- grounded process reward. Experiments on Xbench, BrowseComp-ZH, and GAIA show KnowGPO consistently outperforms outcome- only (GRPO) and process-supervision base- lines (GiGPO, IGPO) by 3.8 to 4.2 points, with our 8B model rivaling or exceeding most 32B open-source systems.

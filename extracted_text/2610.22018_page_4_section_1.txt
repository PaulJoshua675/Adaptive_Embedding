We present a 12-model evaluation of Arabic calligraphy style recognition under three prompt con- ditions. The results reveal limited accuracy, recurring Thuluth over-prediction, Diwani under- recognition, and nonuniform prompt sensitivity across closed/API and open-weight VLMs. Arabic calligraphy therefore offers a compact culturally grounded benchmark for measuring not only model accuracy, but how errors redistribute visibility across significant visual traditions.

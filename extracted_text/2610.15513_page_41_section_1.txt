5.4.4 Conclusion Validity. Every baseline comparison in this paper rests on figures published by other authors [8, 22, 25, 27, 28, 42, 44] under their own judges, evaluation protocols, and model versions, and we flag those differences at each table rather than presenting the rows as like-for-like (Sections 4.1.2 and 5.2). A second limit is internal to our metric: a 100% ceiling leaves ASR with little discriminative power for comparing REPRO variants against one another. 5.5 Ethical Considerations This research aims to improve LLM safety through systematic vulnerability discovery. All exper- iments target publicly available models with known safety training. Attack examples presented in this paper use one of two disclosure levels: structural patterns with placeholder goals (e.g., Table 16) that preserve defensive insight without specifying harmful content, and a small number of one complete attack-response pair (Figure 4) included to demonstrate that REPRO’s attacks elicit genuine harmful outputs rather than exploiting evaluation artifacts. The framework is designed for defensive research and safety evaluation.

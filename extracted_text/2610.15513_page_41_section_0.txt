More fundamentally, the re-scoring is applied to the set of attacks the in-loop judge accepted, which is a set selected by the quantity under test. It can find false positives, and found none, but it cannot bound the false-discovery rate of a search that was steered by the judge being checked. A gold arbiter held out of the loop, applied post hoc to a stratified sample and reported alongside the in-loop figure, together with human annotation of that sample, is the check that would close this gap. The released judge configurations and rubrics specify the scoring setup used here. Other internal threats. Stochastic variation in evolutionary optimization is a secondary internal threat. The component ablation applies McNemar’s test with Bonferroni correction within a single run, but uses one run per configuration, so run-to-run variance across seeds is not captured (Sections 4.4 and 5.2). Its results are statistically grounded within the observed run and not verified across independent replications. 5.4.2 External Validity. Our evaluation spans 36 models across 11 families and three benchmarks totaling 350 behaviors, providing broad coverage. However, all benchmarks focus on English- language text-only interactions; REPRO’s effectiveness on multilingual inputs, multimodal models, or domain-specific deployments (e.g., medical, legal) remains untested. Provider safety training also changes without notice, so the reported figures are a snapshot rather than a permanent property of the targets (Section 5.2). As noted there, all evaluations are against API-served models with their provider-level defenses, but without the additional defensive layers (external input classifiers, prompt-injection detectors, runtime content policies) that production deployments may add, so ASR against fully guarded systems is expected to be lower. 5.4.3 Construct Validity. We measure attack success through ASR, the standard metric in the jailbreaking literature. However, ASR treats all successful attacks as equivalent regardless of the severity or specificity of the elicited harmful content: a model that provides vague, unusable guidance and a model that provides detailed operational instructions both count as a success. This coarse binary reduces the metric’s usefulness as a deployment-risk signal, and a severity-graded metric (e.g., distinguishing engagement from substantive harmful content from operationally specific harmful content) would provide a more nuanced view of vulnerability.

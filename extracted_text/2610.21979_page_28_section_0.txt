Computational cost The primary 100-update P2TPO run requires approximately 60 GPU-hours on eight high-performance computing cards. The full credit-assignment mechanism takes 1.215 s/update, compared with 0.707 s/update for GRPO, adding 0.51 s/update, equivalent to approximately 0.19% of per-update wall time. The recorded advantage- computation shares are 0.465% for P2TPO and 0.265% for GRPO. Generation and actor-update timing distributions are comparable to GRPO. D.

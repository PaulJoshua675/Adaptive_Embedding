Autoregressive Video Generation. Autoregressive (AR) video diffusion models generate succes- sive frames or chunks conditioned on preceding content (ai et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2025; 2024a). Distribution matching distillation (Yin et al., 2024b;a) enables few-step generation by aligning the student’s output distribution with a target distribution represented by a pretrained teacher. CausVid applies distribution matching to distill a bidirectional teacher into a causal generator (Yin et al., 2025), while Self Forcing trains on autoregressive rollouts conditioned on the generator’s own out- puts (Huang et al., 2025). Causal Forcing revisits this distillation for real-time interactive genera- tion (Zhu et al., 2026; Zhao et al., 2026). LongLive, Rolling Forcing, and Self-Forcing++ extend few-step generation to long streaming videos (Yang et al., 2025a; Liu et al., 2025b; Cui et al., 2025). 2

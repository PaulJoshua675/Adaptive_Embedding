Under SFT the dormant domains drop sharply and recover only when trained, while the SST variants hold all three domains much closer to their trained levels. SST accumulates task knowledge. In the FinQA →Pathology →Science chain of Figure 10, each domain improves when it is trained and remains near that level as later stages are learned, so the final model retains all of the acquired skills. Under plain SFT the same chain is not cumulative. Accuracy on the untrained Pathology and Science drops by 28 and 18 points while FinQA trains, and FinQA itself gives back 9 points over the later stages. The complementary Medicine →Pathology →Science chain in Appendix B.3 shows the same pattern for the SDFT family. The refresh variants expose a useful trade-off in this sequential setting. More frequent refresh improves accuracy on the current task at a minor cost to retention on previously learned tasks. As a complementary composition test, Appendix B.1 shows that independently trained SST experts can be merged with far less interference than SFT experts.

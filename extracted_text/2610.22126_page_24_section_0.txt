Since no causal graph is available for this dataset, we leverage prior knowledge from OmniPath [Türei et al., 2021]. After restricting the graph to genes present in the dataset and to the interactions annotated as transcriptional, the resulting network contains 6,941 genes and 22,887 edges. Each edge was then assigned a confidence score as a weighted mean of four evidence channels: curation effort, number of references, number of primary sources, and consensus direction. Duplicate source–target records were collapsed into a single edge by taking the maximum of each evidence channel, reducing the edges to 22,624 aggregated interactions. Edges without supporting evidence in any channel were removed, and the edges with the top 75% of weights were kept, leaving 12,136 edges. Because a topological ordering of the features is required, we converted the cyclic graph to a DAG by approximate minimum feedback arc set removal. Specifically, we first computed a linear vertex ordering using the weighted greedy heuristic of Eades et al. [1993], removed arcs inconsistent with this ordering, and then reconsidered removed arcs in descending confidence order, reinserting an arc whenever doing so preserves acyclicity. This removed 399 of 12,136 edges (3.3% of edges and 3.6% of total confidence mass), yielding an acyclic prior of 11,737 edges over 3,792 connected genes. 24

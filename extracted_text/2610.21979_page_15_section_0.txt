A.2. Complementary mechanisms and possible adaptations Attribute-informed block scoring. A table-block scorer could use CSPO-inspired functional attributes (Yang et al., 2026) to assess content and structure, then supply the resulting block scores to P2TPO’s reference-instance comparisons. This would refine evaluation within aligned table blocks while retaining UAP and SCR. 15

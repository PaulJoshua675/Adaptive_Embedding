Vision–language pre-training (VLP) has emerged as a promising paradigm for zero-shot chest X-ray diagnosis. By aligning radiographs with their free-text reports, VLP models learn joint representations that can be queried with disease prompts at inference without requiring task-specific annotations [12, 27, 37]. Most medical VLP approaches optimize a batch-wise contrastive objective [23, 25]: within a mini-batch of B image–report pairs, matched pairs are treated as positives, whereas all off-diagonal pairs are treated as negatives.

Scoring Protocol and Diagnostics Let t0 be the first ordinary parent token and n0 the number of bytes recovered from its byte-alphabet representation. The scorer verifies that concatenating the bytes of all parent pieces reconstructs the exact NFC sentence. Recovering piece bytes avoids decoding t0 in isolation, which can replace an incomplete scalar with a replacement character, because byte-level pieces need not be valid UTF-8 (Firestone et al., 2025). Each corruption preserves t0, ensuring that the scored suffix begins at the same byte offset across variants within a pair. The parent cost is −log2 P(t1, . . . , tn−1 | t0), without a prepended BOS or appended EOS. The conversion receives its BOS and prefix bytes under the boundary history selected during prefill; the first n0 byte targets are excluded from the score. The models therefore score the same text suffix under different conditioning representations. With one-byte lookahead, the final unscored prefix flag can depend on the first scored byte. This is a byte-level analogue of the prompt boundary problem of tokenized models (Vieira et al., 2025a; Hayase et al., 2026). In the Bwen bank, 0 of 6,800 corruptions change the recorded prefix flags. This is a measured property of these items; preserving the first parent token alone does not guarantee unchanged prefix flags. Joint paths and the last flag. For each scored byte xi and selected prior history hi, the path cost uses the joint output probability P(xi, bi | hi). The last flag is summed out because no subsequent prediction depends on it. The flag component at other positions is fi = −log2 P(bi | xi, hi) = log2 P(xi, 0 | hi) + P(xi, 1 | hi) P(xi, bi | hi) . (4) Each nonnegative fi is the surprisal of the selected flag conditional on the byte and history, rather than the entropy of the flag distribution. Subtracting these terms leaves byte probabilities conditioned on the same selected histories. A text likelihood would instead sum over alternative boundary histories, which can change later predictions. This is the byte-level counterpart of summing a tokenized model over all tokenizations of a string, including noncanonical ones (Cao & Rimell, 2021; Chirkova et al., 2023). The remainder therefore provides neither a corrected text likelihood nor a bound on the parent/conversion likelihood gap. Batch-composition sensitivity. The measured wrapper pools up to the largest patch count in a batch, leaving surplus gathered states in rows with fewer patches. During prefill, it shifts real patch states to the right while retaining shared position identifiers, and the scoring call supplies no attention mask for padded patches. Batching can also change decisions near the boundary threshold. On natural text, 8 of 7,407,006 Bwen interior positions lie within 10−4 of the threshold; this threshold-distance diagnostic does not establish a hardware-independent error bound.

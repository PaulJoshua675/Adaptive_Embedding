In our experiments, we compare SCOPED with representative closed-source and open- source vision-language models. The closed-source baselines include Claude Sonnet 4.6 and Claude Opus 4.6 (Anthropic, 2026b;a), GPT-5.2 (OpenAI, 2025), and GPT-5.4 (OpenAI, 2026). The open- source baselines include GLM-4.1V-9B-Base (Hong et al., 2025), Qwen2.5-VL-7B (Bai et al., 2025b), InternVL3.5-8B (Wang et al., 2025b), Qwen3-VL-8B and Qwen3-VL-32B (Bai et al., 2025a), and MiMo-VL-7B-RL (Xiaomi LLM-Core Team, 2025). Implementation Details. In our experiments, we employ Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct and Qwen3- VL-4B-Instruct (Bai et al., 2025a) as the shared backbone to construct all capability-specialized experts and the unified student. Capability-specific reinforcement learning is implemented with EasyR1 (Zheng et al., 2025), and multi-expert on-policy distillation is implemented with veRL (Sheng et al., 2024). All experiments are conducted on NVIDIA A800 GPUs.

Normalization and conditioning. Backbone RMSNorm reduces 50k-step GenPPL from 87.48 to 84.41. Adding Q/K RMSNorm changes it only slightly, to 84.37, while widening noise conditioning reduces it further to 80.74. This early checkpoint alone understates the observed separation between the two normalization configurations: at 250k, R2 and R3 reach 60.76 and 57.86. The Q/K comparison therefore depends on training progress. The conditioning result includes a 7.36M-parameter capacity increase and cannot isolate the benefit of where that capacity is allocated. Shortcut and training feedback. On the current cumulative path, detachment reduces GenPPL from 80.74 to 79.35, and calibration reduces it to 74.03. Adding confidence-gated training self-conditioning produces the largest adjacent reduction, from 74.03 to 61.73. Overall, R1–R7 reduce GenPPL by 29.4% at 50k. These increments are conditional on earlier modifications; they are not independent contributions or evidence of statistical significance from a single training seed. Because inference uses unweighted self-conditioning throughout this ladder, the final increment reflects a change to training feedback rather than an inference gate. Constant-attenuation and interaction controls remain unreported (Appendix E). Training progress and entropy. At 180k, the baseline and full model have GenPPL 64.94 and 52.39, with entropy 5.410 and 5.385. The paired measurements support reading the GenPPL gains together with lexical-diversity changes. They do not establish the proposed optimization mechanisms: attention diagnostics and gradient-alignment measurements are not reported. 4.4.

Figure 4 illustrates reference-detail preservation and coherent surface completion. 4.4 Ablation and Analysis We evaluate the observation-injection architecture and material content under the given-geometry protocol of Sec. 4.1. The architecture variants share training data, initialization protocol, optimization settings, training budget, and checkpoint- selection rule. All comparisons use the same geometry, cameras, valid observation support, and sampling seed. How observations are used. Table 5 compares three conditioning designs with the same predicted PBR observations. Residual injection adds encoded observations through layerwise residuals. Joint-update attention updates observation states in selected layers and all generation states in every layer. In contrast, SUPRA-Tex keeps observations read-only and alternates unobserved-cell and full-surface updates (Sec. 3.4). It improves shaded PSNRfg over residual injection by 0.87 dB on DTC-Tex and 0.29 dB on Complex-Tex, and matches joint-update attention in shaded fidelity while improving albedo PSNRfg by 0.15 and 0.16 dB with lower MR MAE. Thus, the design preserves or improves reconstruction quality without spending computation on observation-state updates or full-surface queries at every block. The complete design also samples 1.20× and 1.18× faster than joint-update attention on the two datasets (Sec. 4.5). Effectiveness of 2D material estimation. To test whether the FireRed-based 2D material estimator supplies useful physical material estimates, we replace its predicted metallic and roughness with fixed values (M = 0, R = 0.5) or dataset means, keeping predicted albedo and the 3D generator unchanged (Table 5). Relative to fixed and dataset-mean MR, predicted MR improves shaded PSNRfg by 0.44 and 0.26 dB on DTC-Tex, and 0.69 and 0.57 dB on Complex- Tex, respectively, with lower MR MAE on both datasets. These gains show that the 2D estimates provide useful material information beyond albedo alone. Replacing all predicted PBR channels with ground truth (Oracle PBR) further improves shaded PSNRfg by 2.31/1.12 dB and albedo PSNRfg by 2.62/2.51 dB on DTC-Tex/Complex-Tex. The remaining gap indicates that improving 2D material estimation can further improve 3D texture quality without changing the completion model. Using Qwen-Image-2.1 (Qwen Team, 2026) (Ours‡ in Table 1) narrows this oracle gap to 0.98/0.47 dB in shaded PSNRfg and 1.03/2.36 dB in albedo PSNRfg. The same completion model therefore works with a different 2D prior, and a stronger prior improves 3D texture quality toward the oracle. Partial and complete material encoding. Sharing the material encoder does not imply that a partial observation yields the corresponding slice of a complete-material latent. We isolate this distinction using GT geometry, cameras, and PBR values on Complex-Tex, bypassing both 2D prediction and flow generation.

B.4 Policy Network and Training The input of the policy network at visit t consists of six features of ht, namely yt, the previous action, the elapsed time, the fraction of treated months, the time since the last change of action, and an indicator of forced treatment. We map these features through a linear layer with 64 units and tanh activation to a GRU with 64 hidden units, followed by linear heads for the action logits and the value. At visits with forced treatment, we exclude the action at = 0 by masking its logit. The loss of PPO combines the clipped surrogate objective with clipping parameter 0.2, the value loss with coefficient 0.5, and the entropy bonus with coefficient 0.01, where the advantages are computed by generalized advantage estimation with parameter 0.95. In each update, we minimize the loss by the Adam optimizer with learning rate 3×10−4 over four epochs with four minibatches, clip the gradient norm at 0.5, and stop the update once the approximate Kullback- Leibler divergence between the new and old policies exceeds 0.03. In training, we divide the reward rc of Section 2.2 by T. For every combination of policy type, training radius, and training population, we train three policies with the random seeds 11, 23, and 37, and report each policy after the final update, without selecting checkpoints on test data. B.5 Kernel Adversary We parameterize the kernel adversary by a network of the same architecture as the policy network, and train it by PPO with the same hyperparameters, using the negative of the reward of the controller. In solving (2.8), we alternate between four adversary updates with the policy fixed and one policy update with the adversary fixed. The adversary selects the generating kernel of month t before observing at, based on the observation yt, the previous action at−1, the elapsed time t/T, and the one-hot encoding of the kernel it proposed in the previous month. We use its output head to parameterize a categorical distribution over the K = 32 generating kernels. In accordance with (2.9), the patient evolves under the proposed kernel with probability ρ and under a uniformly drawn generating kernel with probability 1 −ρ, and the adversary does not observe which of the two occurs.

Across the 48 model–setting combinations, GEN is strongly cor- related with EX (Pearson’s r = 0.990, p < .001; Spearman’s ρ = 0.888, p < .001). The associ- ation remains strong when models are compared within the Implicit (r = 0.951, p < .001) and Ex- plicit (r = 0.997, p < .001) settings. Under Base prompting, the correlation is weaker (r = 0.434, p = .093) because GEN is near its upper bound for almost all models, leaving little variation across models. More directly, the model-level change in GEN from Implicit to Explicit prompting is strongly cor- related with the corresponding change in EX (Pear- son’s r = 0.992, p < .001; Spearman’s ρ = 0.985, p < .001). Meanwhile, EX | GEN changes only from 0.722 to 0.762. The decline in overall execu- tion accuracy under Explicit prompting is therefore primarily associated with suppressed SQL gener- ation rather than reduced correctness among the SQL outputs that remain. These results do not make GEN a substitute for execution accuracy; in- stead, they support its intended use as a measure of whether models preserve SQL generation on valid queries. B.8 Bootstrap Uncertainty Analysis We assess the uncertainty of the principal model- and error-level comparisons using 10,000 boot- strap replicates. For comparisons across prompting settings or models, each replicate resamples the 1,800 aligned query instances with replacement, preserving the paired structure of the observations. For error-type results, we resample the 200 source queries within each error type while retaining all outputs from the 16 evaluated models and both judges for each sampled query. We report per- centile 95% confidence intervals and two-sided centered-bootstrap tests. These intervals quantify uncertainty over benchmark queries; the evaluated models and judges are treated as fixed. Table 9 confirms that Explicit prompting in- creases DET and decreases GEN for every eval- uated model. None of the 32 confidence intervals crosses zero, and all changes remain significant after Holm correction (padj < .004). Even the smallest GEN decrease, observed for LLaMA 4 Maverick, remains distinguishable from zero.

When analyzing the runtime required to cover the Pareto fronts of the OMM and OJZJ problems, we need to repeat- edly select the aforementioned individuals to cover the missing Pareto front neighbor points via mutation. The key to the proof lies in the calculations of the crowding distance of the individual corresponding to the aforementioned Pareto front point, and the maximum number of individuals whose crowding distance is greater than or equal to this value.

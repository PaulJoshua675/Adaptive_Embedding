Grounding Trade-Off Table 15 shows how win-rate and grounding change for ideas as you go down a generated-set of ideas. For our trained models, the impact doesn’t seem to have that much of a change (especially for the no-think model). The think model is more instruction following and can explicitly check for the instructed ordering when generating so we see that it generates the most impactful idea first. For grounding, we see the models get less grounded as they generate the next idea. The models choose to do a simpler way of getting different ideas by moving away from the source. This trend is more prominent for the no think model compared to the think model. Think vs no think From Table 1 we see that Proposed (no think) has a higher utility than Proposed (think).

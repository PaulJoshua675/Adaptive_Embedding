Our AttnAlign becomes increasingly effective as noise intensifies: adding it to NAT improves accuracy by 2.17 percentage points at 𝜎=0.05 and by 7.19 points at 𝜎=0.20. Together with logit KD, AttnAlign forms the complete ABNAT, which reaches 70.14% at 𝜎=0.20, outperforming NAT+KD and NAT by 7.69 and 11.28 points, respectively. In comparison, FeatureAlign provides negligible high-noise benefit: adding it to NAT changes accuracy from 58.86% to 58.35%, while adding it to NAT+KD changes 62.45% to 62.63%. ABNAT without 𝐿task also achieves a strong average accuracy of 80.17%, while complete ABNAT performs best at 𝜎∈ {0.15, 0.20}. These results show that attention-route preservation becomes increasingly important as hardware noise intensifies. Generalization Across Models and Datasets We evaluate AB- NAT’s effectiveness across model architectures and datasets to as- sess generalizability. Figure 6(a–c) presents results for ViT-Small on CIFAR-100, ViT-Tiny on ImageNet-100, and Swin-Tiny on CIFAR- 100. At 𝜎= 0.20, ABNAT improves over NAT by 6.34, 8.15, and 4.15 percentage points, respectively. These consistent gains across model scales, datasets, and both flat and hierarchical Transformer architectures indicate that attention-route supervision is not tied to a single configuration. Generalization to Language and Multimodal Transformers We further evaluate attention-route preservation using Pythia- 160M and Pythia-2.8B for language modeling [3] and CLIP ViT-B/32 for image–text retrieval [18]. Figure 6(d–f) reports perplexity and Mean Recall, where lower and higher values are better, respectively. An attention-aligned variant performs best in all three settings. At 𝜎=0.20, NAT+AttnAlign reduces Pythia-160M perplexity by 76.4%, from 7682.16 to 1812.96, while NAT+KD+AttnAlign reduces Pythia- 2.8B perplexity by 59.6%, from 2329.72 to 941.04. On CLIP ViT-B/32 at 𝜎=0.10, NAT+AttnAlign improves Mean Recall from 71.70 for NAT to 72.27. These results extend attention-route preservation to autoregressive language modeling and multimodal retrieval. Robustness Under Train–Deploy Noise Mismatch Inference noise can deviate from training conditions because of device varia- tion, lifetime drift, and operating changes [2, 4]. We therefore fix 𝜎train ∈{0.15, 0.20} and sweep 𝜎infer ∈[0.01, 0.20] in Fig. 7. AB- NAT outperforms NAT and NAT+KD throughout the sweep. For 𝜎train = 0.15, its gain over NAT reaches 10.86 points at 𝜎infer = 0.20; when trained at 0.20, it retains a 9.68-point gain even at 𝜎infer = 0.01.

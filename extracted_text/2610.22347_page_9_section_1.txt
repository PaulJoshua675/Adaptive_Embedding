We introduced Speculative Forcing, a framework that allocates denoising computation according to token-wise convergence in few-step autoregressive video generation. By treating intermediate clean estimates as drafts, it predicts which tokens can be accepted early and continues denoising only the remaining tokens. Training-free and learned verifiers enable these decisions without modifying the generator’s weights. Experiments on short and long videos and controllable world model rollouts demonstrate faster generation while maintaining quality, with comparable or lower quality variation and preserved camera-following accuracy in world modeling. These results show that a shared denoising depth is not necessary for every token, supporting convergence-based acceptance as a practical approach to efficient video generation. 9

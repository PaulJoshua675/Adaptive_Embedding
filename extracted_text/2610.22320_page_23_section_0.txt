To quantitatively assess phrase reflection, we construct approximately 90 diverse scene prompts and append each framing phrase at the end (e.g., “A photo of a woman baking cookies, close-up shot”). We generate images for 10 random seeds per phrase and compute the top-1 and top-3 accuracy, along with standard deviations, as shown in Fig. 16. For exam- ple, in the “shot size” category with 7 phrase options, this results in a total of 6,300 images (90 scenes × 7 phrases × 10 seeds). We filter out framing phrases whose top-1 accuracy is zero (e.g., “extreme wide shot”), as these are not reliably reflected in the generated outputs from SDXL. Consistory+_+ With the remaining framing phrases, we extend the orig- inal Consistory+ benchmark using GPT-4o. Since these phrases are visually meaningful primarily for animate subjects, we first guide GPT-4o to determine whether the subject in each prompt is animate. Then, the model is instructed to append the most appropriate framing phrase for the given scene, resulting in Consistory+_+. Framing Accuracy Evaluation. Following the evaluation protocol in [15], we provide the generated image along with the corresponding framing category (e.g., shot size) and prompt the model with a multiple-choice question. For instance, for shot size evaluation, we prompt the model with a multiple-choice question such as “What is the shot size of this shot?” and provide predefined options (e.g., wide shot, medium shot, close-up shot).

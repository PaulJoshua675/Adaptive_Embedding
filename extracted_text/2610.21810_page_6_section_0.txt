To investigate this possibility, we first analyze the principal directions of frequently accessed rows and find substantial shared structure beyond the Gaussian reference, as shown in Table 2. We then compare CP’s shared trellis with vector quantization (VQ), which learns codebooks directly from the original memory ta- bles. Despite the observed shared structure, CP achieves lower row reconstruction error across all three models and better output fidelity than VQ at the same storage rate. We additionally examine explicit mean and principal-component representa- tions, but their improvements are limited relative to the storage required.

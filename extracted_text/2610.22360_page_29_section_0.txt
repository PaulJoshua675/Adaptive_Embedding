G.2 Public Image Conditions and Fixed Requirements The generation study uses forty source images from the public image-prompt collection shared by 3DGen-Bench and Hi3DBench. Screening retains complete objects with sufficient visible local facts. Existing Hi3DBench quality labels define four historical strata, with ten sources from each. Human annotators define and review seven claims for each source: one identity claim and six local requirements covering counts, parts, geometry, relations, region binding, and surface appearance. Source images, claim text, and pass criteria are fixed before generation. This requirement-definition stage is separate from the subsequent human review of generated candidates. G.3 Generation and Human Annotation Each generator provides eight candidates per source, giving 640 assets in total. Hunyuan3D-2.1 uses the Shape-v2-1 + Paint-v2-1 run. Both generators share forty input images and 280 fixed claims. Candidates are rendered with eight 512-by-512 RGB views.

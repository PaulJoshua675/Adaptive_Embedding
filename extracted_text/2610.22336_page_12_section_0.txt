In summary, by training against shifts in the patient dynamics, we retain the benefit of adaptive therapy under such shifts, including those generated by held-out kernels, while sacrificing almost no performance when the dynamics evolve as anticipated. 3.5 Universal Benefits of Kernel-Robust RL So far, we have evaluated random-perturbation RL and kernel-robust RL only under the source of uncertainty that each policy is trained against. In deployment, however, the source of uncertainty is not known in advance, and we would like a single policy that performs well under all of them. We therefore cross-evaluate the two policies, i.e., we evaluate random-perturbation RL under shifts in the dynamics and kernel-robust RL with training radius ρtr = 0.25 under random perturbations of PSA, and compare both policies with nominal RL and the two clinical rules in Figure 4. We find that kernel-robust RL nearly Pareto-dominates nominal RL and AT50 across the five conditions of Figure 4, namely the nominal model, the evaluation radii ρ ∈{0, 0.25, 1} on the training kernels, and random perturbations of PSA. Indeed, the polygon of kernel-robust RL encloses the polygon of AT50, and lies outside the polygon of nominal RL on every axis except the nominal model. In particular, kernel-robust RL forgoes 1.58 months compared with nominal RL in the nominal model, and attains the highest value under every shift in the dynamics, with 53.31, 46.57, and 23.26 months at ρ ∈{0, 0.25, 1}, compared with 45.74, 39.12, and 17.46 months for nominal RL. Under random perturbations of PSA, which it never encounters during training, kernel-robust RL still improves on nominal RL, with 38.65 compared with 32.96 months. In addition, AT50 falls below kernel-robust RL, and indeed below all learned policies, in every condition. On the other hand, by comparing kernel-robust RL with random-perturbation RL in Figure 4, we ob- serve that each of the two policies attains the highest value under the source of uncertainty it is trained against. Specifically, random-perturbation RL attains 44.49 months under random perturbations of PSA, compared with 38.65 months for kernel-robust RL, while kernel-robust RL attains higher values than random- perturbation RL under every shift in the dynamics. In summary, kernel-robust RL nearly Pareto-dominates nominal RL and AT50 across the sources of uncertainty, in the sense that it nearly matches nominal RL in the nominal model, outperforms every other policy under shifts in the dynamics, and substantially improves on nominal RL and AT50 under errors in PSA.

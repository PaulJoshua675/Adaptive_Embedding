2.1. Medical vision–language pre-training Contrastive alignment of chest radiographs with their re- ports has driven rapid progress in label-efficient and zero- shot diagnosis. ConVIRT [37] adapts image–text con- trastive learning to radiology, while MedCLIP [30] de- couples images and texts to exploit unpaired data. GLo- RIA [12] and MGCA [28] introduce local, multi-granularity alignment between image regions and report tokens; BioViL [2] strengthens the text encoder through domain- specific language modeling; and CheXzero [27] demon- strates expert-level zero-shot detection. KAD [35] incor- porates a medical knowledge graph, and MRM [38] com- bines masked image and record modeling.

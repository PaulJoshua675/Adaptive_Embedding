4.3.1 Quantitative Analysis PST900 Evaluation. While VesperMUSE achieves a highly competitive mIoU of 0.8559 (trailing the leading CM-SSM [73] by a marginal 0.0031), a closer examination of class-wise distributions reveals the distinct advantage of its tri-modal input architecture. Despite employing a compara- tively standard ResNet50/18 [28] backbone ensemble, the model establishes new state-of-the-art thresholds for geometrically rigid, contextually isolated objects. It notably outperforms robust con- temporary baselines, including SA-Gate [76] and CM-SSM [73], on the Hand-Drill (0.8282) and Fire-Extinguisher (0.8776) classes by significant margins. This suggests that the inclusion of surface normal maps act as an informative structural guide, allowing the network to delineate sharp object boundaries even against methods utilizing substantially heavier feature extractors (e.g., LASNet’s ResNet-152 [28]). Furthermore, the VesperMUSE LLIE configuration demonstrates remarkable ro- bustness, achieving a strong 0.7818 mIoU that remains highly competitive with established baselines such as EGFNet [77]. Therefore, it is shown that the pre-enhancement process aids semantic under- standing through noise removal and colour shift correction (two essential components of low-light information recovery) while simultaneously producing visually appealing, illuminated feeds.

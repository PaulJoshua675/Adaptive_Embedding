Statistical significance is assessed using two-sided paired 𝑡-tests over bench- mark instances. 6.2 RQ1: Does Target Specification Matter? We first examine whether explicitly specifying the target affects rewriting effectiveness. Compared with the target-unspecified set- ting, the target-specified setting explicitly names the target party in the rewriting prompt, while the remaining setup is unchanged. Table 3 reports the mean exposure improvement across strategy categories and generative engines, together with the placebo as the no-rewriting reference. Target specification is important for effective rewriting. Target- unspecified rewriting rarely increases target exposure, whereas target-specified rewriting does so in most evaluated settings. Among the 36 category, engine, and metric settings, target-unspecified rewriting produces only one significant positive gain, while four show significant negative changes and the remaining 31 are not significant. In contrast, target-specified rewriting produces signifi- cant positive gains in 24 of 36 settings, including 10 of 12 for SOV. Positive gains appear across all three engines and the Structure, Evidence, and Argumentation categories, while Style is less consis- tent. The difference arises because election webpages often discuss multiple competing parties, whereas existing GEO settings typi- cally involve webpages centered on a single entity.

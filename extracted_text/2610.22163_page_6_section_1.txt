In this work, we developed an AI tutor system that processes student questions in Mandarin and delivers bilingual responses that are correct, helpful, and well-translated, and complete with a technical terms glossary, without directly revealing the answers. Our system utilizes Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with a special question extraction mechanism to answer queries from a diverse array of course materials, including Jupyter Notebooks, Python scripts, PowerPoint slides, and PDFs. Another main contribution of our AI tutor system is the design of the prompts that instruct the GPT-4 model to apporpriately use resources from question extraction and RAG to provide the appropriate level of guidance in responses. Our evaluation results for our AI tutor on data science questions underscore the potential of AI tutors to enhance educational accessibility for non-native English speaking students in computing courses. Future efforts will focus on refining the system through deployment in real classroom settings and expanding language support to further improve student learning experiences.

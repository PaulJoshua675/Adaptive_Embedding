K Re-Asking after the Evidence This experiment tests whether restating the ques- tion after each opened page helps models use the evidence. The re-run takes every fact-sheet check with a required answer line, with and without a source request, whose original reply under frozen retrieval did not name exactly the stale line: 222 episodes as run, of which 157 remain after the exclusions applied throughout the paper. Each open-page result returns the page text followed by the full prompt, so the question is the most recent thing the model has read; a repeated open-page call carries it too. Nothing else differs: the menu, page bytes, sampler, models and episode identi- fiers are the same.

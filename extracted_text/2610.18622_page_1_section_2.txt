Recent advances in information retrieval (IR) have increasingly used dense vector representations. Unlike traditional lexical approaches, dense retrieval represents queries and documents as vectors, allowing semantic matching beyond exact word matches. However, long documents remain chal- lenging to represent [1, 2]. A single vector may provide a broad and imprecise representation of a document containing multiple topics, making relevant content harder to distinguish from unre- lated information. One approach to this problem is to represent documents using smaller retrieval units, allowing the retriever to identify specific portions of a document that are relevant to a query. This problem is relevant in legal information retrieval (legal IR), which applies IR techniques to legal documents. Legal documents differ from general-purpose text in several ways.

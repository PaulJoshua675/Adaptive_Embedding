We presented FeaTok, a discrete image tokenizer whose tokens are shaped only by feature reconstruction, while a pixel decoder trained on detached tokens renders them into images. We also discretize the encoder’s global representation and model it together with the patch tokens. Across four pretrained encoders, FeaTok generates better than tokenizers trained with pixel or combined losses. FeaTok achieves the best gFDr6 among the evaluated discrete generators on ImageNet at 256×256 and 512×512, outperforms continuous generators at a matched sampling cost, achieves higher human-preference reward-model scores, and transfers to text-to-image generation after pretraining and only 500 fine-tuning updates. We believe these results are a step toward effective discrete representations for unified multimodal modeling, and that the low sampling cost of FeaTok makes it attractive for scaling multimodal training. 9

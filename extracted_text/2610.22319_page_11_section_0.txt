A team of Gemini 4 Argon agents beats Minecraft End to reset it when it gets stuck. To leave, they jumped into the void on purpose, a shortcut human players use too: a player who dies returns to its bed in the Overworld. Seventeen and a half hours in, all four did so within six minutes, so that the empty End would unload and the stuck dragon reset. The breakthrough came about 21 hours in. auratian pointed out that the ten crystals on top of the obsidian pillars kept healing the dragon to full health, and worked out that no pickaxe was needed to reach them: stack dirt to climb beside each pillar, punch the crystal, dig back down by hand. Over the next four hours and forty minutes, the team destroyed all ten. Thirty-nine minutes after the last one fell, the dragon died, with bulicame down to 2.5 of its 20 health points.

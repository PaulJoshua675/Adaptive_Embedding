We evaluate each representation strategy on the document-level rankings for NormasTCU and Ulysses-RFCorpus. Both collections provide graded relevance judgments on a three-level scale. We report two metrics: normalized Discounted Cumulative Gain at rank 10 (nDCG@10) and Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR). nDCG@10 measures the quality of the top-ranked results, considering both the graded relevance of retrieved documents and their positions in the ranking [26].

A rule that starts a patch where the patcher is sure of the kind of the next byte (digit, letter, punctuation) but unsure of its value fails when used for training at every size (12M final answers 18.9% vs 46.6% for entropy; 50M 35.1% vs 59.9%), with the best bits per byte of all rules. Adding the answer cue (a forced patch after ####) rescues final answers at 50M (65.7%) but not computed results (9.5%). Switching rules after training. At inference on BLT-1B, layouts can be changed without retraining: novelty fitted on GSM8K solutions raises computed results from 19.0% to 56.5% at a 10% rate but final answers collapse (51.4% to 19.4%); adding the answer cue gives 54.8% and 65.6%, the best label-free layout we found without retraining. In small models trained from scratch the swap fails completely: a 12M model trained with novelty and evaluated with entropy or typed layouts falls from 73.6% to 0–0.5% on final answers and from 1.66 to 2.5–2.9 bits per byte. Learned predictors. Besides the trace network above, an 8-byte network predicting boundary dependence [Vera Zúñiga, 2026] was below the two-byte table on accuracy, and a 128-byte network on that target was too noisy to learn at our reference-model size (held-out rank correlation 0.03). Combining novelty with entropy (patching where either fires) was worse than either alone in the earlier 1M setup of [Vera Zúñiga, 2026]. 8. Limitations Scale. Our models are at most 50M parameters trained from scratch, plus inference-only layouts on BLT-1B. The trend from 1M to 50M favours novelty, but the methods we compare against run at 1B and above, where a stronger patcher might find computed results more surprising. A preregistered scale-up (150M models from scratch, and full fine-tuning of BLT-1B with frozen patchers) is designed but not yet run. Domain. The large effect is in written math. Prose shows a modest gain; code and execution traces show ties. Outside math we measure 12M models with two seeds. Rule. The table sees two bytes and a 128-byte lookback; on code it fires on rare literals rather than structure. Context-aware predictors did not help (§6–7). Architecture and evaluation. One architecture family (BLT-style, 128-byte context), one corpus for math, teacher-forced exact match on answer spans; end-to-end generation is at the floor at these sizes.

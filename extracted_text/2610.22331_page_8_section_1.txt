We presented KnowGPO, a reinforcement learn- ing framework that assigns credit by aligning deep search trajectories through the task-relevant knowl- edge they have acquired, rather than through their surface observations. KnowGPO groups steps un- der matched knowledge states and uses incremental knowledge gain as a process reward grounded in the environment. Across Xbench, BrowseComp- ZH, and GAIA, KnowGPO improves over outcome- only and process-supervision baselines by 3.8% to 4.2%, with an 8B agent matching 32B open-source systems at training cost comparable to GRPO.

4.2 Low-Light Image Enhancement Evaluation Metrics. To quantitatively evaluate the fidelity of the reconstructed images, PSNR is the main utilized measure. This metric is defined by the ratio between the maximum possible power of a signal and the power of corrupting noise that affects the fidelity of its representation. In this formu- lation, M and N represent the image dimensions, I(i, j) and K(i, j) denote the pixel intensities of the ground truth and reconstructed images at coordinates (i, j), and MAXI signifies the maximum possible pixel value. While PSNR relies on absolute differences, SSIM evaluates the similarity between the ground truth and the output by comparing luminance, contrast, and structural information across two images x and y. In the following expression, µx and µy represent the local means, σ2 x and σ2 y denote the local variances, σxy signifies the cross-covariance, and c1 and c2 are constants incorporated to ensure numerical stability, the formal representation of SSIM can be expressed as: SSIM(x, y) = (2µxµy + c1)(2σxy + c2) (µ2 x + µ2 y + c1)(σ2 x + σ2 y + c2).

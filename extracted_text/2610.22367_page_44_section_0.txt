27 Appendix Appendix A: Exact branch recovery for divstep inversion This appendix proves the decoding rule of Equation (9) [16]. The argument first establishes the invariants carried by the coefficients fv and gv, then identifies the decisions already visible from the updated state, and finally shows how the remaining bit b is encoded in a window of low-order bits. Let p be an odd positive integer and let 0 ≤w < p. The walk begins at (z, f, g, fv, gv) = (0, p, w, 0, 1). (A1) Every step follows the recurrence (z′, f ′, g′, f ′ v, g′ v) =      (−z, g, (g −f)/2, 2gv, gv −fv) for z ≥0, g odd, (z + 1, f, (g + f)/2, 2fv, gv + fv) for z < 0, g odd, (z + 1, f, g/2, 2fv, gv) for g even. (A2) Let s indicate whether the step swaps, and let b = 1 exactly when g is odd, so b records whether the two values are combined before halving. In this notation, s = 1 selects the first case of Equation (A2); when s = 0, the value of b selects the second or third case. The walk freezes when g first becomes zero; the steps before that are active. For an integer u and k ≥0, write u[k] ∈{0, 1} for bit k of u mod 2k+1, counted from zero. This notation agrees with the usual binary digit for u ≥0 and with the two’s-complement digit for u < 0. We also write bitk(u) = u[k]. Let v2(u) denote the number of trailing zero bits of u (its 2-adic valuation), with v2(0) = +∞. We first show, via the determinant and scaling relations, that the integer recurrence computes a modular inverse. Lemma A.1 (Basic invariants). Every reachable state satisfies fgv −gfv = p. (A3) Moreover, f and gv are odd and fv is even. Proof. The initial state gives p · 1 −w · 0 = p. Direct substitution in the nonswap and swap cases gives f(gv + bfv) −g + bf 2 (2fv) = fgv −gfv, (nonswap), g(gv −fv) −g −f 2 (2gv) = fgv −gfv, (swap). (A4) Hence every active step preserves the determinant, while a frozen step leaves the state unchanged. The parity claims also hold at the initial state. On a nonswap, f remains odd, f ′ v = 2fv is even, and g′ v = gv + bfv is odd. On a swap, the swap condition (g odd) makes f ′ = g odd, while f ′ v = 2gv is even and g′ v = gv −fv is odd. Induction proves all four claims. Proposition A.2 (Scaling and termination). After t active steps, fvw ≡2tf (mod p), gvw ≡2tg (mod p). (A5) If gcd(p, w) = 1, then at termination f = ±1, gv = fp, and w−1 ≡ffv2−t (mod p). (A6) Proof. The congruences hold at the initial state. Substitution in Equation (A2) shows that if they hold after t active steps, the updated coefficients satisfy them with t + 1 in place of t. They therefore hold for every t. Every step also preserves gcd(f, g), so termination with g = 0 gives |f| = gcd(p, w) = 1. The determinant invariant then gives gv = fp, and multiplying the first congruence by f yields Equation (A6).

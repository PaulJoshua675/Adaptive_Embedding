Learning Spatial Reasoning. Recent work improves spatial reasoning in VLMs through spatial data and reasoning-oriented training. VST and Cambrian-S scale spatial instruction tuning, SpatialLadder adopts a progressive curriculum from perception to reasoning, and SpaceR and SpatialEvo respectively introduce verifiable rewards and deterministic geometric environments (Yang et al., 2025b,c, Li et al., 2025b, Ouyang et al., 2025, Li et al., 2026a). However, compositional supervision for tracking reference frame changes across observations has received less attention. 3

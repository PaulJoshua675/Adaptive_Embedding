Peak-wind bias. GenCast’s lower cyclone-local CRPS does not necessarily imply more accurate peak winds. To show this, we compare the ensemble mean of each member’s maximum 10m wind speed with ERA5’s maximum in the same region. GenCast underestimates peak winds at short and intermediate lead times. FCN3 in contrast has a smaller mean bias up to about 150 h (Figure 3, right). Beyond that, the two models exhibit similar biases. GenCast’s better cyclone-local CRPS therefore coexists with a larger intensity bias. GenCast underestimates peak winds at short and intermediate leads despite its better local CRPS. Interpreting the scores. These results show two limits to the characterization of cyclone forecasts quality on aggregate scores only. Global averages conceal differences around cyclones, while even cyclone-local scores can conceal a weakness in the representation of peak winds. GenCast’s lower local CRPS supports better agreement with ERA5 in tropical cyclone regions overall, but does not establish that every property of the storm is better predicted. Nor does this comparison identify which errors account for its advantage. This motivates assessing spatial agreement separately from intensity in the operational setting. 5

In this paper, we propose HEM-Med, a hierarchical agentic memory framework for safe multi-center medication rec- ommendation. HEM-Med explicitly separates cross-hospital consensus experience from hospital-specific residual patterns and integrates DDI-aware decoding for safer medication set generation. Experiments on a real-world multi-center eICU dataset show that HEM-Med improves recommendation ac- curacy while maintaining medication safety. Ablation studies validate the effectiveness of its key components. Future work will explore richer clinical constraints, fine-grained medication safety knowledge, and prospective validation.

3.1 Error Decomposition Let A be a stochastic optimization algorithm. Let A(S) be the output parameter when applying A to the dataset S. Optimization algorithms suffer from local minimizers and saddle points over nonconvex loss landscapes, and thus usually fail to find the global minimum [44]. In this case, the functional suboptimality is no longer effective for quantifying the learning performance and one can use the gradient norm as an alternative [23, 34].

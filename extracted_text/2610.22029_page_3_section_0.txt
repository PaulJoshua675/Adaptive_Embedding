A round starts from the learner’s root state St = (θt, ωt), its parameters and resumable training state, and trains two native branches from it. Admission uses pre-update behavioral observa- tions Ot, such as the pass rate over ten learner responses, and the branch endpoints form Cnat t . Qualified responses from every branch enter a bounded archive Mt. Training and Recovery, screening, confirmation and final evalu- ation use disjoint question pools, and online scores use frozen consensus labels, as Appendix A details. Value and inheritance. For a declared task distribution Pt and utility q, the value of a state is its expected gain over the root, Vt(S) = Ez∼Pt[q(S; z) −q(St; z)], Vt(St) = 0. (1) Capability uses exact correctness or sampled pass@1, and the update audits use the reduction in correct-target negative log-likelihood (NLL), which is not an accuracy difference. For binary correctness, value splits into acquired and lost successes, Vt(S) = Pr(q(S) = 1, q(St) = 0) −Pr(q(S) = 0, q(St) = 1). (2) The selection-to-update gap is the failure of task-side admission to identify positive value. Screening picks a recipient Ssel t from Cnat t ∪{St}, Recovery turns it into a proposal S+ t , and confirmation keeps St or adopts S+ t as St+1.

We presented DKGI, a harness where LLM agents write and execute their own graph access code over the complete node and edge files. With GPT-6- Astra, the training-free DKGI achieves the high- est H@1 and MRR on every STaRK dataset. In controlled ablations with GPT-6-Luna, the agent achieves higher overall H@1 than predefined graph tools, whether called directly or composed in code, and it gains most when it writes and revises pro- grams over several turns. Its trajectories show how tool creation and tool use co-evolve within a task. 8

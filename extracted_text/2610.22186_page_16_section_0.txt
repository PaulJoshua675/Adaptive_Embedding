A.3.2 Robustness of the Reward Judge We assess the robustness of the reward judge in two ways. First, before selecting Qwen3.5-27B as the reward LLM, we sampled 200 reward-evaluation items covering the three reward dimensions and compared its judgments against Gemini-2.5-Pro. The agreement rate is around 85%, suggesting that Qwen3.5-27B provides reliable event-level judgments for reward supervision without obvious systematic bias.

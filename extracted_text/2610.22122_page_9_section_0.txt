Axes show mean enrichment and discovery gains over Random. Points show mean gains with 95% confidence intervals across five repetitions. 5.3 Landscape Structure and Active Discovery Real landscapes. Active learning gains over Random vary across datasets, with several methods underper- forming Random on NK (Figure 3). We examine local hit structure in the fixed candidate representations. Within an analysis sample, enrichment is the mean hit rate among each hit candidate’s 10 nearest neighbors divided by the sample’s overall hit rate. Across eight tasks, enrichment ranges from 0.83× to 6.27×. The gray dashed fit to mean gains across nine shared baselines (Figure 4a) indicates that stronger local hit clustering is associated with greater active learning gains. Controlled landscapes. To further examine this relationship, we construct simulated landscapes with controlled spatial structure. We keep the positions of 17,660 candidates and the set of phenotype values fixed, changing their assignment so that hits become progressively more clustered. With nearly random hit locations, methods perform close to Random. Across five enrichment levels, stronger clustering yields larger gains for TDAL and several baselines, although the benefit varies across acquisition functions (Figure 4b). These results indicate that local hit structure can provide useful information for active selection. Construction details and results are in Appendix E. 9

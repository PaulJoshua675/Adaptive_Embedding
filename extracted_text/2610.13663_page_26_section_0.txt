Before the agent search began, the digits dataset, containing 1797 samples, was stratified into a development set of 1437 samples and a held-out test set of 360 samples. The test set was not used to calculate metrics passed to the agents, select the decoder structure, or tune its hyperparameters. Stratified five-fold cross-validation was performed on the development set. The five-fold split was fixed once before the experiments and remained unchanged for all decoders, runs, analyst models, prompt variants, and baseline methods. At each iteration, the proposed decoder was trained five times on four folds and evaluated on the remaining fold. The mean classification accuracy was recorded in the experiment log: CVAcc(𝑑) = 1 5 5 ∑ 𝑘=1 Acc (𝑑(−𝑘), (𝑘)) , (23) where 𝑑(−𝑘) denotes the decoder trained on four folds, and (𝑘) is the fifth fold excluded from training. The analyst used CVAcc(𝑑) when formulating the next hypothesis. After the specified search budget was exhausted, the decoder with the highest CVAcc(𝑑) was selected. It was then retrained on all 1437 samples in the development set and evaluated once on the 360 samples in the held-out test set. The resulting test accuracy was not recorded in the agent-search log. It uses seven analytical features, including normalized and logarithmic envelopes and two amplitude–time cross-features, while 𝐹𝑂applies time-gated exponential amplitude and additive phase corrections with separate envelope weights. The best sonar-pro configuration, tw_nonlinear_pinn_core, gives Relℎ= 1.97 ⋅10−4, a 238-fold reduction from its identity baseline of 4.69 ⋅10−2. Its 𝐹𝐼supplies a normalized traveling-wave coordinate, normalized time, and a local nonlinearity-density feature; 𝐹𝑂uses the analytical traveling-wave amplitude and phase and interprets the MLP outputs as bounded amplitude and phase corrections. It normalizes the analytical soliton coordinate at the input and reconstructs the field from the analytical amplitude and carrier phase multiplied by learned complex corrections.

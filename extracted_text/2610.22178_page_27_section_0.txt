D FULL BENCHMARK RESULTS Tables 4 and 5 give the per-condition results summarised in Fig. 4, together with the in-distribution results on LIBERO and RoboTwin. Each LIBERO-Plus column perturbs one dimension: the camera viewpoint, the initial robot state, the language instruction, the lighting, the background texture, the sensor noise or the object layout. The Overall column is the success rate over all 10,030 perturbed episodes, so each dimension is weighted by its number of episodes (Fei et al., 2025). For the in- distribution RoboTwin results, the models are finetuned on clean and randomised scenes together and evaluated on each (Clean, Rand). For Clean2Rand, the models are finetuned on clean scenes only and evaluated on clean scenes (Easy), under a randomised background, lighting, clutter or table height applied alone, and under all four together (Hard). The baseline numbers are taken from published results, and Table 7 gives the source of each one. The Clean2Rand row of Qwen- RobotManip (Yuan et al., 2026) is its joint-space variant. The LIBERO-Plus results of π0 and π0.5 are from the zero-shot block of ACoT-VLA (Zhong et al., 2026), which evaluated the released checkpoints. ACoT-VLA is initialised from π0.5, so it is grouped with the models pretrained on in-house data. The LIBERO-Plus results of StarVLA are those of its Qwen3-VL-OFT model in the StarVLA repository. For Being-H0.7 and ABot-M0.5, the LIBERO-Plus Overall is pooled from the seven published dimensions, because the published total does not match them. The RoboCasa365 leaderboard is at https://robocasa.ai/leaderboard. On RoboCasa- GR1, the result of GR00T-N1.5 is that of the released checkpoint in the Isaac-GR00T repository (https://github.com/NVIDIA/Isaac-GR00T, branch n1.5-release), and the result of π0.5 is the one reported by StarVLA, which states no source for it. Parameter counts include the action expert, are rounded from exact counts and are read off the released checkpoints where available, including those of StarVLA and RLDX-1 (Kim et al., 2026). Qwen-RobotManip states no expert size; our expert follows its design, so we count 0.2B for it. D.1 PRETRAINING STEPS Figure 11 gives the Clean2Rand success of REKI after 50k to 200k pretraining steps, and without pretraining. All models are finetuned on clean RoboTwin scenes as in Table 5, but with a backbone learning rate of 0.1 instead of 0.2 times the expert learning rate, and without learning-rate decay. The mean success rises from 53.7 without pretraining to 61.9 after 50k steps, and then by 3.4 points more up to 200k steps.

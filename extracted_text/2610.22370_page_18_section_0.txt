D Limitations Similar to prior token merging methods, DaTMe faces limitations under aggressive token compression. Original Image ToMeSD CFG DaTMe Figure 18: Limitations. In highly complex images, aggressive compression adversely af- fects image quality, leading to loss of struc- tural details and visual coherence. In highly complex scenes with many objects or intri- cate backgrounds, excessive merging can inevitably remove necessary tokens, leading to degraded gen- eration quality. In addition, the layer-wise roles of diffusion models remain underexplored, especially in DiT-based architectures. Understanding which lay- ers contribute most to structural layout, fine details, or background refinement could further improve to- ken merging by enabling layer-aware compression strategies. 18

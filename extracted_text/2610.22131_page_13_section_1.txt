We introduce Intern-Decision, which makes finite multimodal choices through a single causal forward pass, retaining the pretrained backbone’s native language-model head and exposing candidate probabilities through a typed interface. The 4B model achieves the highest seven-suite average in the presented comparison, with a 44.16 ms mean for a fixed short request on an RTX 4090. Separate calibration and handoff studies show how probabilistic outputs can support uncertainty analysis and selective use of a larger model. We believe Intern-Decision can serve as a practical component of larger agent systems, but its generalization to arbitrarily large schemas and complex environments remains an open problem.

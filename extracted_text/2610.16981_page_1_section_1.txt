Tracing money flows in cryptocurrency transaction networks is increasingly chal- lenging, as smart contract blockchains such as Ethereum enable developers to create new tokens and decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols, creating on-chain ecosystems for trading, lending, and liquidity provision. As these activities grow more complex, strategies involving flash loans, maximal extractable value (MEV), and arbitrage combine multiple protocols and assets, making it difficult to de- termine how value moves across accounts, transactions, and token layers. Understanding these flows is important for analyzing how DeFi protocols are used and how participants execute on-chain financial strategies. However, conventional transaction graphs represent accounts as nodes and transfers as edges, making them less suitable for tracking value across multiple tokens and related transaction sequences.

Model-free weight perturbation reaches approximately 97% accuracy on MNIST and approaches the performance of backpropagation. This demonstrates that a biologically motivated learning rule can support high classification accuracy despite substantially slower learning. Larger batches, longer training, and stable updates are decisive in recovering that performance. The accompanying finite-horizon analysis establishes conditions for arbitrarily close approximation of a fixed gradient-descent trajectory. For the configurations examined here, the main practical challenge is the efficiency with which WP reaches high accuracy, especially as network depth increases.

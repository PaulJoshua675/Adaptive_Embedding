Tversky loss alone is markedly worse and unstable at this learning rate (75.75 ± 1.78; one seed reached only 72.59; pBH=0.013). Changes at the task head therefore move Flood-S1 mIoU by at most about 0.4 pp, small next to the +1.4 pp gain from pretraining. We suspect that the ceiling reflects the combination of limited training data (3,000 samples), severe class imbalance (85.3% non-flood pixels), and a mismatch between single-image MIM pretraining objectives and the temporal reasoning required for change detection; the ablation does not isolate these factors.

Implicit Prompting Implicit validity guidance increases aggregate SQL, DET, and TYPE to 0.635, 0.672, and 0.619, respectively, while original-query GEN and VAL remain 0.903 and 0.826. The gains are substantial but uneven. DeepSeek V3.2 reaches the highest DET (0.898), yet its GEN and VAL on originals are only 0.455 and 0.437. Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview attains lower DET and TYPE (0.706 and 0.688) while preserving nearly all originals. Thus, a soft warning exposes some latent critique ability, but it neither closes the recognition–explanation gap nor produces uniformly reliable premise judg- ments. Explicit Prompting Explicit prompting raises aggregate SQL, DET, and TYPE to 0.936, 0.931, and 0.790. Because this prompt asserts that a flaw exists, however, high scores can reflect compli- ance with that assertion rather than independent critique. The evaluation therefore tests whether cri- tique follows the evidence: models should identify a supported flaw in perturbed queries, but should also reject the prompt’s unsupported claim of a flaw when processing originals. The latter require- ment distinguishes genuine premise assessment from instruction-driven rejection. Nevertheless, ag- gregate GEN and VAL on originals fall to 0.523 and 0.321. Qwen3.5-397B is the clearest rejection case, scoring 0.998 SQL and 0.968 DET on flawed queries but only 0.004 GEN and 0.005 VAL on originals. Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview is less suscepti- ble, retaining 0.941 GEN and 0.704 VAL. Overall, stronger instructions improve apparent critique per- formance without producing consistently evidence- grounded premise judgments. 4.2.1 Model Comparisons Aggregate trends conceal substantial variation within and across model families. Model rankings also shift across prompting settings. Within model families, Base DET rises from 0.261 for GPT-5.4 to 0.313 for GPT-5.6-Luna, 0.386 for GPT-6-Luna, and 0.417 for GPT-6.1-Sol; TYPE follows the same ordering. Claude Opus 5.5 likewise improves Base DET from 0.281 to 0.492 relative to Opus 4.6 and raises Implicit TYPE from 0.536 to 0.646. Yet even the best Base result re- mains below 0.5, and the GPT ordering is not stable under stronger prompts. Other comparisons reinforce the absence of a general scaling trend. Under Implicit prompting, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash has much higher original- query GEN than V3.2 (0.926 versus 0.455), but lower DET on flawed queries (0.625 versus 0.898). The 35B Qwen variant also outperforms its larger counterparts on Implicit DET.

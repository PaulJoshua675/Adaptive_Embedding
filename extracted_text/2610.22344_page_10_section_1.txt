6.1. Agents for Automated ML Research LLM agents increasingly automate data analysis, model development, and experimental iteration, with recent work extending to autonomous AI system design and improvement [27]. AIDE [1] and MLE- STAR [2] improve candidate solutions through iterative search and refinement, while MLEvolve [28] and MARS [29] use structured tree- or graph-based search, memory, and planning to organize long- horizon experimentation. R&D-Agent [3] studies multi-role collaboration, while InternAgent [30] and FM-Agent [31] extend autonomous AI research across machine learning, algorithm optimization, and engineering tasks. Recent systems including Arbor [4] and Argus [5] maintain persistent research states and increase model control. 10

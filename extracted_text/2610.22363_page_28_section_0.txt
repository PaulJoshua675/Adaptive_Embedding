Preprint H RESPONSIBLE USE AND ASSET DOCUMENTATION H.1 SOURCE ASSETS AND INTENDED USE The reporting datasets are EgoDex (Hoque et al., 2026), ARCTIC (Fan et al., 2023), HOT3D (Baner- jee et al., 2025), and EgoLive (Li et al., 2026b). Their video, action annotations, and calibration remain subject to the respective providers’ access and redistribution terms. Wan model weights and the MANO assets retain their upstream terms. Derived annotations and visualizations should pre- serve source attribution; access-controlled raw data and model assets should be obtained from their providers. Intended use. P3-WAM supports research on visual prediction, 3D perception, and robot manipula- tion. Its benchmark coverage reflects the activities, cameras, hands, and environments in the source datasets. Performance across broader populations and embodiments requires additional evaluation. H.2 ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS Egocentric video can reveal bystanders, personal spaces, and daily activities. Use and sharing of these data should follow the source datasets’ access and consent terms, with identifying information minimized in released examples. Generated interaction videos should be labeled as synthetic. Motion generated from visual goals requires embodiment-specific validation and supervised execution before use on physical robots. 28

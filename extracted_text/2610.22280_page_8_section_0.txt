Task Success and Inference Cost LEASH consistently improves task success over standalone weak-agent execution across all four backbones (Table 1). At closely matched escalation rates, it also outperforms random task escalation by 6.55–11.94 percentage points, showing that the training-free uncertainty signal provides useful information for selective weak-to-strong escalation rather than merely increasing access to the strong executor. With Qwen3.5-35B-A3B (Qwen Team, 2026a) as the backbone, LEASH achieves 73.66% task success while escalating 64.1% of tasks, compared with 72.90% for always-on Opus (Anthropic, 2026a). Besides, its estimated inference cost is $0.4117 per task, 19.2% lower than the $0.5094 cost of always-on Opus. The benefit also persists at lower escalation rates: LEASH improves Nemotron- 3 (Deshmukh et al., 2026) from 45.04% to 66.03% success while escalating 43.1% of tasks, and EvoCUA-8B (Xue et al., 2026) from 42.37% to 67.18% while escalating 45.4%.

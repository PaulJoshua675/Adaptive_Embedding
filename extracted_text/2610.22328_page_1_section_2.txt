Image editing with modern generative models has reached an impressive level of quality, enabling complex modifica- tions while faithfully preserving the appearance and content of the input image [6, 24, 26, 44, 49]. Yet, practical editing is rarely a single operation. Users often refine an image through a sequence of edits, where the output of one edit becomes the input to the next. This setting is commonly referred to as multi-turn image editing. With current image editing models, each editing turn introduces small distribu- tion shifts that accumulate as the model repeatedly operates on its own outputs.

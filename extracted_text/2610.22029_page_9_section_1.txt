We identify the selection-to-update gap: admitting a task that looks learnable does not ensure a useful update, and 60 of 96 updates on verified tasks show no gain. HEIRCOMMIT inherits a successor only when it beats the current model on fresh probes and cuts the harmful share of commits by 68–83% on three self-evolution hosts. EVOHEIR separates the parameters a round inherits from the experience it keeps and raises the math average by up to 7.0% over Agent0, the strongest baseline. Self-evolving systems should judge the updates they produce, not only the tasks they choose, and keep experience beyond the models that produced it. 9

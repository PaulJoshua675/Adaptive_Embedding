Figure 2 shows the Holm-corrected function-level record across the budget ladder. At the exact application budget the record is 4/6/2 at D=10 and 9/1/2 at D=20; the D=20 case is notable because, even though GWO holds the lowest overall mean rank there, REMBGO improves on its parent on nine of the twelve functions after within- budget correction. The advantage is strongest when evaluations are scarce—10/2/0 at D=10 with 200 evaluations and 11/1/0 at D=20 with 227—and narrows and reverses as the budget grows, reaching 0/9/3 at D=10 and 0/8/4 at D=20 at the largest budgets. The integrated modifications are therefore useful mainly in the evaluation-limited regime and should not be presented as improving EMBGO at arbitrary budgets; a precise crossover budget is not estimated, because cross-rung multiplicity is not controlled. 5.3 Component behaviour at the application budget Table 3 reports an implementation-level mechanism-removal study at the exact 1040-evaluation budget, and Figure 3 shows the corresponding removal penalties; the removed elements include both retained and refined parts of the com- plete REMBGO implementation. The evidence is dimension-dependent. At D=10 the largest descriptive penalty follows removal of greedy survivor selection (median ∆log10 error +0.054, rank 9.250, Holm p = 0.093), with re- moval of Cauchy mutation (+0.018) and of the triple-leader move (+0.009) much smaller. At D=20 the most visible descriptive penalties come instead from removing Cauchy mutation (+0.052, rank 8.167) and the triple-leader move (+0.042, rank 8.000, Holm p = 0.068), with greedy selection (+0.028) still contributing. No individual removal is significant after the suite-level Holm correction across the ten planned removal contrasts at either dimension, and the pattern is more consistent with an interacting search design than with a single dominant component. A supplementary one-factor-at-a-time sensitivity analysis at the same 1040-evaluation budget uses six represen- tative CEC2022 functions with twenty matched runs per function at both dimensions (Supplementary Material S2), varying the Cauchy and triple-leader probabilities, the elite count and the restart fraction. Nearby settings can be descriptively better or worse than the defaults, so the selected values are prespecified operating settings within a rea- sonably usable neighbourhood, not claimed local optima, and the results do not justify post-hoc retuning. 8

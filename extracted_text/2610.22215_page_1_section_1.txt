Accurate motion prediction is critical for safe autonomous driving, yet even strong forecasters leave residual errors in their multimodal proposals. Existing refiners compress complete proposals or update their segments sequentially, preventing the full proposed future from serving as explicit relational context for correction. We introduce Future- Lift, a backbone-agnostic refiner that represents each multi- modal proposal as a heterogeneous future graph, with spa- tially grounded temporal nodes connected to agent histo- ries, map elements, alternative modes, and, for joint predic- tion, other agents future trajectories. Bidirectional tempo- ral edges allow later evidence to revise earlier segments in a single pass, and the updated nodes predict residual coor- dinate and confidence corrections. Requiring only propos- als, scores, and scene context, FutureLift provides a com- mon refinement interface for structurally different forecast- ing backbones. On Argoverse 2 and the Waymo Open Mo- tion Dataset, it consistently improves frozen proposals and outperforms native and specialized refiners across marginal and joint forecasting. On the WOMD motion-prediction leaderboard, FutureLift ranks first in minADE (0.5049 m) and minFDE (0.9942 m) among the compared standard- input methods, without LiDAR or ensembling, while achiev- ing a competitive 0.4874 Soft mAP.

Our hypothesis is that a spurious added edge does not correspond to any real dependency in the data-generating process; the surrogate has no true signal to associate with that edge, so its weight converges toward zero during training, making the gradient through it negligible even though the edge is present. Reversing or removing an edge, by contrast, is a genuine loss of information that the surrogate cannot recover, which is why both corruptions degrade recovery substantially.

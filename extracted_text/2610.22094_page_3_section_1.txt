Preliminaries: Batch-Contrastive Alignment Let a mini-batch contain B image–report pairs {(xi, ri)}B i=1, with global embeddings vi, ti ∈Rd. A pairwise aligner g produces bidirectional score matrices Si2t, St2i ∈RB×B, where Si2t ij scores image i against report j, and St2i ij scores report i against image j. The di- agonal contains matched pairs. Because SCCL operates on these score matrices, g may be implemented by similarity, a matching head, or cross-attention; a single-matrix aligner is handled analogously.

We further evaluate FENAS in the large-scale DARTS search space [15], which contains approximately 1018 candidate architectures. Since unconstrained prior- based search may favor degenerate architectures with excessive “none” opera- tors [35,6], we apply a lightweight structural validity constraint during evolution- ary search [26]. In practice, FENAS is fine-tuned using 100 randomly sampled architectures trained for 50 epochs, then used to rank 100,000 valid candidates. The top-3 predicted architectures are retrained from scratch for 600 epochs. As shown in Table 4, FENAS remains highly efficient, requiring only 0.3 GPU days while achieving 97.25% ± 0.09% test accuracy after retraining. Although FENAS does not outperform PINAT comparing the accuracies, its topology- agnostic design avoids costly graph processing and additional pretraining, en- abling direct ranking through lightweight meta-ensembling of initialization-based priors. These results show that FENAS is a practical open-domain NAS method under strict computational budgets. 4.4 Discussion and Ablation 1) Synergy among ZCPs.

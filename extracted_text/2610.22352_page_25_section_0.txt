Most of our model’s improvement occurs within the first 300 steps. Both models are trained on the same training set and initialized from the same pretrained TRELLIS.2 denoiser with their respective architectural adaptations and fresh optimizers. TeaPot is used only for evaluation, not for single-object fitting. We evaluate all 16 raw checkpoints from step 0 to 1500 at intervals of 100 on its designated front view; there are no failed or excluded checkpoints. Our model receives ground-truth albedo, metallic, roughness, and opacity through native partial-material encoding, while Pixal3D⋆receives the corresponding front-lit RGB image through its official image-feature projection interface. This oracle-material setting examines the 3D model’s ability to learn from native observations independently of F2D prediction errors. The two conditioning designs retain their respective architectures and optimization recipes. All checkpoints use the same GT geometry and camera, 25 Euler sampling steps, seed 42, and time rescaling factor 3, with no reprojection repainting or material postprocessing. We render with Blender 3.0.1 using Filmic color management and 32 samples at 1024 × 1024, then resize to 512 × 512 with Lanczos for scoring.

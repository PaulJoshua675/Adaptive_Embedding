Algorithm 1 performs K optimization rounds, generating at most T tokens per response and stopping at the end-of-sequence (EOS) token. Feedback always concerns the most recent response; the highest-scoring candidate is used only for final selection. It returns the earliest response maximizing the stored score ai = J (q, Ri), where J is the judge and i ∈{0, . . . , K} indexes candidates. Two context-conditioned streams maintain separate key–value caches while sharing the generated prefix.

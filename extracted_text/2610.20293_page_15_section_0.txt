Preprint D PERMUTATION SAMPLING FOR LARGER ACTION SETS For the default m = 5, exact CASI evaluates the 25−1 = 31 non-empty coalitions. These are frozen- evaluator scores computed from cached action-level retrieval results, not 31 downstream auction replays during training. Exact enumeration is therefore practical at this scale and introduces no coalition-sampling error. For larger action sets, the same Shapley credit admits a permutation estimator. Let π(r) be the r-th uniformly sampled permutation of the m actions, and let S(r) i contain the actions preceding qi in that permutation. Then ˆci = 1 M M X r=1 h ˆR0(S(r) i ∪{qi}) −ˆR0(S(r) i ) i . (40) Under uniform permutation sampling, E[ˆci] = cCASI i . A naive implementation requires O(Mm) prefix evaluations, although repeated subsets across sampled permutations can be deduplicated and scored once. This provides a direct accuracy–cost trade-off when exact enumeration of all 2m −1 non-empty coalitions is no longer practical. E OFFLINE REPLAY LANDSCAPE AND DIFFERENTIAL-FIDELITY AUDIT This section gives the construction and diagnostics behind the offline counterfactual reference used in Sections 4.1 and 4.2. The goal is to audit whether the frozen training evaluator preserves coalition differences under the same reconstructed action sets. It is distinct from the historical served-value audit in Appendix H. Replay landscape. For each sampled request, the SFT checkpoint generates one five-action set A = {q1, . . . , q5}. We enumerate all 25 −1 = 31 non-empty coalitions and replay each coalition through the downstream auction-replay path. For replay execution r, the measured coalition value is y(r)(S) = X a ecpm(r) auction,S(a) send(r) S (a), (41) where send(r) S is the replay winner/send indicator rather than final production exposure. The audit contains 1,200 requests. Every coalition is replayed three times; a subset of 50 requests is extended to seven repeats, for a total of 117,800 full-chain replay executions. The repeat-aggregated coalition reference is yaudit(S) = medianr y(r)(S). No request is admitted or rejected according to its observed full-set replay outcome. This last design choice matters empirically. The full set is replay-positive for 870/1,200 requests, while 103/330 requests with zero full-set value still contain at least one nonzero proper subcoalition. Conditioning sample admission on yaudit(A) > 0 would therefore remove requests that contain informative coalition effects. Training and sensitivity evaluator backends. The primary evaluator is pr filter, which matches the backend used during RL: the full candidate pool is processed once and coalition values are obtained by filtering the corresponding cached result. We also report pr rerun as a sensi- tivity backend, where downstream ranking is re-executed separately for each coalition. The two backends are not interchangeable. Their insertion-edge sign agreement is only 0.6152 (95% CI [0.6097, 0.6209]), although they agree much more strongly on pair-interaction signs (0.9697). All primary action-credit comparisons are therefore reported conditional on pr filter, the evaluator consumed during RL. Pair interactions.

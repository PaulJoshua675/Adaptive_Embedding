Sparse retrieval remains central to information retrieval (IR), valued for its ef- ficiency, interpretability, and compatibility with inverted indexing [27,23,1]. Re- cent neural sparse retrievers such as SPLADE [9,8], uniCOIL [19], and Deep- Impact [20] extend this paradigm by learning high-dimensional vocabulary rep- resentations, achieving strong effectiveness while preserving the deployment ad- vantages of classical sparse systems.

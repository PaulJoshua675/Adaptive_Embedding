However, the subsequent contrastive and FLOPS regularization gradually penalize redundant activations, producing a compact sparse index upon convergence. 4.2 Effect of Stage 1 Training We investigate the contribution of Stage 1 training to retrieval effectiveness and representation sparsity. As shown in Table 2, removing Stage 1 reduces MRR@10 from 0.3998 to 0.3619, indicating its importance for retrieval effectiveness. Beyond retrieval performance, Stage 1 also produces substantially sparser representations. For the same 202,827 passages, the total number of index entries decreases from 72.10M to 42.72M, corresponding to a 40.7% reduction. Similarly, the average number of non-zero terms per passage decreases from 355.46 to 210.62. These results demonstrate that Stage 1 improves retrieval effectiveness while reducing index complexity, achieving a more favorable balance between effectiveness and sparsity. 4.3 Retrieval Performance After KL and Contrastive Fine-Tuning We evaluate the final model obtained after Stage 2 on MS MARCO (Dev) [22] and DL2019/DL2020 [3,2].

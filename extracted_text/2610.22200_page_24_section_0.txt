5.2 Research Priorities More Efficient Reasoning and Execution. We will optimize the model’s thinking, making reasoning more concise for routine operations and mitigating overthinking. On the harness and infrastructure side, we will improve code execution, context management, and infrastructure optimization. We will also explore new system architectures and other system-level solutions for efficiency. Reliable Work on Users’ Devices. We plan to extend the harness and SDK toward tasks spanning computers, browsers, and potentially mobile devices. Priorities include reliable target selection, recovery from execution failures, and task continuity when users interrupt or change their requests. We will also investigate which additional workflow decisions should move from fixed harness rules to the model. Real-Device Environments and Benchmarks. We will develop evaluation environments and benchmarks on real devices, particularly macOS, covering a wider range of applications, user interactions, and changing device states. The goal is to measure whether agents complete useful work under real operating conditions, together with the time and resources required. 5.3 How to Contribute We welcome contributions to both the implementation and the research agenda: • Improve the Harness and SDK. Contribute platform adapters, more reliable observation and control, execution optimizations, or real-device testing and documentation. • Build Real-Device Evaluation. Contribute environments, realistic tasks, evaluation criteria, and benchmark infrastructure, including execution traces that expose current limitations. • Propose Research Goals. Identify workflows agents should support, capabilities worth developing, or questions to investigate through the released systems and trajectories. These priorities guide planned releases without fixing a delivery timeline. The agenda will evolve with experimental evidence and community proposals, including directions beyond those listed here. Our goal is for the community to help shape not only how GCUAs are built, but also what they should be able to do and how progress should be measured. 6

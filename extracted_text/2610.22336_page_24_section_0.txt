Specifically, we declare progression at the first visit t with at−1 = 1, Bt ≥1.2B0, and Bt > Bt−1, i.e., when the tumor exceeds the threshold during treatment and is still growing. Growth during a treatment break is then never penalized, and the forced restart at yt ≥0 occurs below the threshold 1.2B0. We report the results in the nominal model under this definition in Table 9, together with the forced-only rule, which withholds treatment whenever permitted, i.e., sets at = 1 if and only if t = 0 or yt ≥0. All three seeds of nominal RL, trained under this definition, attain 53.79 months on the virtual test population and 53.75 months on the fitted patients, which coincide with the values of the forced-only rule, while AT50 attains 52.60 and 53.06 months. Consequently, under the alternative definition, the benefit of adapting the treatment to PSA beyond the forced-treatment rule cannot be assessed.

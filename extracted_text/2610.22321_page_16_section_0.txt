The verifier records applicable physical, morphological, and causal-response evidence for each relation. Physi- cal evidence covers material or timbre, energy or loudness, and spatial or distance cues. Morpholog- ical evidence covers fine visual states such as mouth shape, finger motion, and the visible action that produces the sound. Causal-response evidence covers source localization, emotional response, and response magnitude for off-screen or narrative evidence. The final LLM judge reads all applicable attribute evidence in R and outputs Sattr = Φattr(R) ∈[0, 1]. (17) A.4 OVERALL AVC SCORE The final judgment follows the order Smac →Stemp →Sattr. The three outputs remain separate diagnostic dimensions. When an overall score is required, we use the arithmetic mean SAVC = 1 3 (Smac + Stemp + Sattr) . B DATA CONSTRUCTION AND ANNOTATION B.1 TRAINING DATA SOURCES AND STATISTICS We construct two data pools for AVC-Reward training, one for structured-reasoning SFT and the other for human calibration. Table 5 summarizes the sources and statistics of the training data used for SFT and GRPO. Throughout this process, a wide array of multimodal models (Xu et al., 2025; Low et al., 2025; Team et al., 2026; Pan et al., 2025; 2026b; Tu et al., 2024; 2025a;b; Shan et al., 2025; Girdhar et al., 2023; Yang et al., 2025b; Liu et al., 2025) is leveraged to curate more comprehensive data along with precise annotations. SFT source pool. We first collect ground-truth audio-video clips from two categories: general scenes and human-centered scenes. We use Qwen3-Omni (Xu et al., 2025) to filter the clips for basic audio-video quality and clarity, salient event and temporal relations, and informative macro-semantic content, retaining 2.5k general clips and 1k human-centered clips. The four filtering questions are provided in our project’s GitHub repository. Qwen3-Omni then generates audio and video captions for the retained clips, which are used to construct samples under different generation paradigms. For the general subset, Ovi (Low et al., 2025) generates 2.5k JAVG samples from full audio-video captions, while HunyuanVideo-Foley (Shan et al., 2025) generates 2.5k V2A samples from the ground-truth videos and corresponding audio captions. For the human-centered subset, Ovi gen- erates 1k JAVG samples, while InfiniteTalk (Yang et al., 2025b) and an internal model each generate 1k A2V samples using the ground-truth audio with visual conditioning. Together with the 3.5k ground-truth clips, this gives 11.5k candidate samples. We further filter the generated pool for basic audio-video quality and annotation completeness, retaining 8k clips for SFT. Human-calibration source pool. We construct the human-calibration source pool from Javis- Bench (Liu et al., 2025). Its relatively large scale, paired ground-truth references, and broad content coverage provide a practical starting point for constructing diverse positive and negative examples when high-quality audio-video data remain limited. We sample 3.2k clips from each of five sources: JavisBench ground truth, Ovi-I2V, Ovi-T2V, HunyuanVideo-Foley, and an internal A2V model. The generated samples use the corresponding JavisBench inputs, covering JAVG, V2A, and A2V paradigms. These 16k clips form the initial candidate pool. We run the SFT model f1 eight times on each candidate to estimate sample difficulty and prioritize cases for which the model has not yet formed stable judgments. To preserve content diversity, we cluster ImageBind (Girdhar et al., 2023) audio-video embeddings into 300 clusters and sample across clusters. We also construct global- narrative negatives by swapping audio tracks across clusters. Finally, we sample original candidates and constructed global negatives at a 3:1 ratio to obtain the 10k rollout set used for GRPO.

We detail the prompts and inference parameters for all LLM judges in Appendix C. 5 RESULTS Proposed (no think) achieves the highest Creative Utility Table 1, shows that the proposed model trained with the set-level reward and no thinking, Proposed (no think), achieves the highest creative utility and win-rate, with high distance between ideas. This utility increases from 0.03 (base model), to 0.21 (SFT no think) to 0.64 post our proposed training (no think). We also beat state of the art closed source models, where Claude Opus 4.8 achieves a CU of only 0.38 and GPT-6 Astra achieves 0.28. The thinking variant of our proposed training, Proposed (think) achieves a utility of 0.51, which still beats both closed-source models but is lower than the no-think variant and on par with DARLING (0.52) and the no-SFT ablation (0.51). We discuss this trade-off at the end of this section. The high creative utility is because both impact against gold (win-rate) and idea diversity are high for our proposed model. Proposed training recipe beats different competing objectives Training with a set level objective for only quality (Impact-only RL) gives a good win-rate (43%), but it collapses the set. Its average distance falls to 0.38. This shows that training for quality only in a set can get us good ideas, but the model ends up focusing on only one good instead of trying to get multiple good ideas. For DARLING, we see that it reaches a utility of 0.52 against 0.64 for Proposed (no think), but has a lower distance (0.67 against 0.88). Showing that rewarding within-set generation for diversity is more effective than across-rollout diversity. SFT keeps the model grounded To test the SFT warm-start, we train the same base model with our objective but skip SFT (Impact+diversity, no SFT). This baseline matches Proposed (think) at 0.51 CU, but does worse on grounding as seen in Figure 2. Grounding for the no SFT baseline falls to g = 0.19, against 0.81 with SFT (Figure 2). Without the warm-start, the policy gets its diversity by drifting away from its source documents. It also stays 0.13 CU below Proposed (no think). So SFT is what lets the set-level reward raise utility without losing grounding. 6

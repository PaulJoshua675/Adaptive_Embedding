Figure 6 reports both under standard infilling and the guidance gain in Aall at β = 0.5 (Section B.1). Demonstrations continue to improve joint infilling, with standard all-target accuracy increasing at every successive demonstration count (Figure 6, top). For LLaDA with J = 8, Aall rises from 24.3% at D = 2 to 44.5% at D = 16. Increasing the number of targets, however, affects all-target and per-target accuracy differently. At D = 16, LLaDA maintains nearly the same Aavg as J grows from 1 to 8 (73.6% versus 72.3%), despite a decline in all-target accuracy (Figure 6, top and middle). Thus, lower joint success need not imply a comparable decline in accuracy at individual target sites. The benefits of additional example guidance, however, are less consistent as the number of targets grows. On List Functions–Easy, guidance at β = 0.5 yields positive gains with confidence intervals above zero in every J = 1 condition, but in none of the J = 8 conditions (Figure 6, bottom). This pattern is not universal across tasks: a separate cross-benchmark study at D = 4, K = 64, and J = 8 finds guidance gains on SST-2 but losses on Movie Director (Section B.3). Together, these results indicate that guidance effectiveness depends on both the task and the number of jointly infilled targets. 10

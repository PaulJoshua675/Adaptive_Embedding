Direction-dual relation channels. For each direction-sensitive relation family, we construct paired channels over ordered or role-specific node-pair patterns, allowing attention to distinguish forward from backward dependencies and downstream-shared from upstream-shared branch structures. We instantiate three paired relation types, yielding six directional relation channels: (1) local adjacency, where A and A⊤indicate direct forward and backward dependencies; (2) directed reachability, where Rfwd(u, v) = 1 if v is reachable from u, and Rbwd = R⊤ fwd captures the reverse relation; and (3) shared successors/predecessors, where AA⊤captures common successors and A⊤A captures common predecessors. Let RG = {R(r)}6 r=1 denote the resulting relation channels. Together, these channels preserve local connectivity, multi-hop directed reachability, and higher-order branch structures as distinct structural contexts. Relation-constrained attention. For notational brevity, write the input node states of layer l as H = H(l−1) ∈Rn×d, and let dhead = d/M for M attention heads. For the m-th head, we compute Qm = HWQ m, Km = HWK m, and Vm = HWV m using projection matrices WQ m, WK m, WV m ∈ Rd×dhead. We use a fixed relation-to-head assignment: in the six-head configuration used in our experiments, head m is associated with directional relation channel ρ(m) = m. The assigned relation defines the structural support of that head: we set Bm(u, v) = 0 when R(ρ(m))(u, v) > 0 or u = v, and Bm(u, v) = −∞otherwise. The attention scores and multi-head output are then computed as Sm = QmK⊤ m √dhead + Bm, StructAttn(H, RG) = h M m=1softmax(Sm)Vm i WO, (4) where denotes concatenation over attention heads, WO ∈RMdhead×d, and the softmax is applied row-wise over key nodes. This construction factorizes topology-aware interaction into structural admissibility and content- dependent weighting. The relation support determines which node pairs can interact, while the learned query–key compatibility determines their relative contributions within that support. The relation-specialized heads retain distinct directional semantics, and multi-head aggregation integrates their complementary representations into a unified topology-aware encoding. For NAS performance prediction, the resulting representation captures operation interactions under different dependency patterns and provides topology-conditioned features for ranking candidate architectures. 4.4 Direction-Sensitive Mixture-of-Experts Feed-Forward Network After structural attention, a standard position-wise FFN applies the same node-wise transformation to all nodes and does not distinguish predecessor from successor contexts. Such direction-agnostic updates can weaken the directional distinctions introduced by the preceding attention layer. We therefore replace the standard FFN with a Direction-Sensitive MoE-FFN, which uses three role- specific experts for the node’s own state, predecessor context, and successor context. A node-wise routing gate adaptively weights these experts according to the current node state. This keeps the feature-update stage direction-aware without assigning a fixed expert to every node role, since the useful predecessor/successor balance can vary across architectures and prediction targets.

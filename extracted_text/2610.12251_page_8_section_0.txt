Construct mode writes each record under the arm, and copy mode copies every bit read under the copy head. Proof. By induction on the number of records. The reading of a gate’s codon and address bits gives back the record, since the codon determines the first eight bits; the reading of an escape and the record’s 26 bits gives them back unchanged; and the end codon closes the induction. Construction 4.3 (The organism). The organism ρ is an image of 143 records, 3718 bits, listed in Appendix C. It uses 45 window cells: the sink, the three head cells, the nine requests, fourteen cells 13 to 26 that hold the state of a decoder, and eighteen temporaries in cells 27 to 44, each written in a pass before it is read in that pass. The decoder’s state is a mode m, construct (m = 0) or copy, in cell 13; a phase, one cell each from 14 to 17: start, codon for a codon, gate for the first eight bits of a record and addr for its addresses; a codon register ξ3ξ2ξ1ξ0, ξi in cell 18 + i; and a counter z = z4 · · · z0, zi in cell 22+i. One pass carries out one step of the decoder. In phases codon, gate and addr, while z > 0, the step decrements z and keeps the phase; at z = 0 it enters the phase given below. With rd the bit under the read head, the step acts as follows. (o) start: the read head, the arm and the copy head return to cell 0, the idle bank and the copy tape are cleared, and the decoder enters codon with z = 3 in construct mode. (i) codon: rd is consumed and shifted into the codon register, and in copy mode it is also copied under the copy head. At z = 0 the codon is complete. At the end codon in construct mode, the read head returns to cell 0 and the decoder enters codon again, with z = 3, in copy mode. At the end codon in copy mode, the arm and the copy head return to cell 0, the decoder enters start, and the pass hands over in its last record. At a gate codon in copy mode the decoder enters addr with z = 17, and in every other case gate with z = 7. (ii) gate: in construct mode, bit 7 −z of the gate’s eight bits is written under the arm for a gate codon, and the consumed bit rd at an escape; in copy mode, at an escape, rd is consumed and copied. At z = 0 the decoder enters addr with z = 17. (iii) addr: rd is consumed and written under the arm in construct mode, and under the copy head in copy mode. At z = 0 the decoder enters codon with z = 3. A consumed bit moves the read head on, and a bit written under the arm or the copy head moves that head on, by the requests the pass sets. A record is thus built in 30 passes, 4 in codon, 8 in gate and 18 in addr, and copied in one pass per bit of its description (Figure 2). Proposition 4.4 (One pass).

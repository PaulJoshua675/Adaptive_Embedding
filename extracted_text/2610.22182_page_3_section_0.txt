Our contributions are: • We introduce LAMP, a training-free method that combines global and regional information from multiple encoder layers using independently fitted kernel pre- dictors. Regional kernels are calibrated against the support set. Score-level fusion preserves native feature dimensions, and separating branch fitting from mixture selection makes predictions reusable across candidates. • We develop LAMP-F, which combines prototype adaptation with the same global and regional predictors. For fixed kernels and mixture weights, its cached re- sponse exactly updates both the initial class scores and residual corrections as the prototypes change. • We demonstrate state-of-the-art mean accuracy in the published training-free RN50 comparison at 4, 8, and 16 shots across eleven datasets. Paired compar- isons on ViT and ResNet backbones establish the accuracy gains, and controlled experiments explain the contribution of multi-layer integration.

We study how to evaluate K target policies simultaneously in stochastic contextual bandits with linear reward structure. Given a known d-dimensional feature map and reward-free access to policy queries, we propose PRISMA (Policy evaluation through Regularized Information Sharing and Mixture Allocation), an algorithm that estimates every target policy’s value to accuracy ε with probability at least 1 −δ. The algorithm uses e O  (L2S2+β2)(1∨ρ⋆) ε2 · log(K)  context draws and reward observations, where ρ⋆≤d is an instance-dependent measure of the difficulty of designing a shared sampling distribution. Here, L, S, and β bound the feature norm, reward parameter norm, and reward noise, respectively, and the suppressed factors are logarithmic in K, d, and 1/δ. Our method combines reward-free planning, a regularized sampling design, and a single ridge regression estimator shared across all target policies. An adaptive doubling procedure selects the regularization scale without knowledge of ρ⋆. The guarantees require no numerical lower bound on second-moment eigenvalues and have no explicit dependence on the cardinalities of the context and action spaces. Policy-query costs are accounted for separately, with K queries per shared planning context. 1

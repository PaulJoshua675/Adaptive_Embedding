2.1 Personalization of LLMs Methods for aligning LLMs with individual or group preferences (Zhang et al., 2025b) condition on retrieved user history or profile summaries (Salemi et al., 2024; Richardson et al., 2023), encode personas in the prompt (Jiang et al., 2024b), train per-user adapters (Tan et al., 2024), learn personalized reward models or merge preference-specific policies (Cheng et al., 2023; Jang et al., 2024), or steer activations toward a user’s style (Zhang et al., 2025a; Cao et al., 2024). LaMP and LongLaMP (Salemi et al., 2024; Kumar et al., 2024) evaluate per-user personalization, while Multifaceted Collection and Bench (Lee et al., 2024) specify group preferences via system messages. These works study dense models as black boxes; we study how sparse MoEs represent personalization. 2

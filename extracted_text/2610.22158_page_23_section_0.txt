A concrete response commitment and a material alternative or di- rect counterevidence must be identifiable. Ordinary literal grounding, declared foreign- key use, and surface value normalization are not M01. Nonappearance in bounded rows alone is assessed as M03. Merely execut- ing SQL, leaving an underspecified request broad, or making an arithmetic/grouping er- ror does not establish M01. M02 Contradiction Rein- terpretation The question contains explicit incompatible requirements on the same target and the re- sponse makes them executable by chang- ing their logical relation, entity, time, scope, quantifier, population, or granularity (e.g., AND to OR or application to separate rows). Both original conditions and the transformed satisfaction relation must remain observable. Preserving the contradiction literally is not M02. Removing one contradictory branch without replacement is M04. A change of field or scope in a question without an ex- plicit internal contradiction is considered un- der M01 or left unlabeled. M03 Context/Evidence Misuse The response makes an incorrect availabil- ity or answerability claim by either treat- ing bounded example rows as exhaustive database contents or denying a table, col- umn, or declared relation that verified model- visible schema context contains. The claim and contextual counterevidence are both re- quired. Unknown data coverage is uncertain, not supported. A complete reference schema is insufficient unless exposure to the tested model is verified. Unsupported mappings used to construct SQL are M01; M03 is re- served for misuse of supplied context to infer absence, impossibility, or unanswerability. M04 Explicit Constraint Omission A semantically complete, explicit atomic re- quirement in the current question disappears from a response that still implements part of the request, and no value, predicate, proxy, field, or other operation replaces that require- ment. Incomplete referents, missing values or thresholds, and vague evaluative terms are not eligible targets. Complete abstention is not omission from a remaining implementa- tion. An attempted but incorrect implemen- tation blocks M04; a concrete substitute is M01, and a changed contradiction relation is M02.

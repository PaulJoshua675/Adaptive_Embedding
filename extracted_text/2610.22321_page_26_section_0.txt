D.2 BENCHMARK STATISTICS Figure 7 summarizes the composition of AVC-Benchmark. The 706 samples cover ground truth, JAVG, A2V, V2A, and explicitly constructed negative data. Based on the human annotations, 678 samples (96.0%) exhibit multi-event composition, 322 (45.6%) contain multi-layer audio, and 376 (53.3%) contain diverse cross-modal relations. The three complexity subsets are non-exclusive, allowing a sample to exhibit multiple forms of complexity simultaneously. Together with the 39- label and human-score distributions, these statistics show that AVC-Benchmark covers different generation settings, failure patterns, and relation complexities.

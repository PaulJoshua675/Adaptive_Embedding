We build FutureLift cumulatively on one frozen DONUT checkpoint on the AV2 joint validation split. Starting from the unrefined proposals, we add history, map, and cross- mode context. We then compare one to three forward-only causal sweeps with one bidirectional sweep before introduc- ing the joint-specific social topology. Here, “xJ” denotes J repeated causal sweeps, each reconstructing the graph from the latest coordinates. This design tests whether direct back- ward information flow adds value beyond repeated forward- only refinement. Repeated causal computation saturates after two sweeps: a third sweep leaves avgMinFDE at 1.191 m. In contrast, one bidirectional sweep reduces avgMinFDE to 1.157 m and ActorMR from 0.151 to 0.148, showing that direct backward temporal edges are more effective than up to three forward-only sweeps in this controlled configuration.

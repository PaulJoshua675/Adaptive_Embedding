Independent Retry, JSON- schema, and Span-safe diagnostics test this directly. JSON-schema recovers 4, 0, and 0 Qwen scores on Original, Random, and Chal- lenge, compared with 11, 13, and 6 for Mistral. Retry recovers 14/6/2 and 36/30/11, respectively. Format constraints and an additional generation opportunity therefore have different effects even before the stronger span-index interface is introduced. Span-safe recovers 44/54, 45/54, and 14/15 of Mistral’s missing scores (81.5–93.3%). Original has 53 extraction-eligible cases among its 54 missing scores. Qwen has 104 missing scores across the co- horts: 60 baseline valid-empty cases are excluded by protocol, and all 44 actual Span-safe attempts return valid-empty structures with- out parse, interval, range, or recorded token-budget failure. This residual is not an inability to follow the index-output protocol. Phi Original has 91 missing scores, skips 75 baseline valid-empty cases, and recovers 3, 2, and 15 under Retry, JSON-schema, and Span-safe. Recoverability thus depends on both the system and the interven- tion, with persistent valid-empty outputs alongside recoverable failures. Recovery also changes which inputs reach a fixed threshold.

We introduced Selective Retrieval-Augmented Translation (SRAT), which learns when and what to retrieve for LLM-based translation. Across of- fline and online retrieval settings, SRAT consis- tently outperforms fixed never- and always-retrieve strategies. We further show that utility-aligned re- triever training improves difficult retrieval settings and that adaptive retrieval can enhance a frozen black-box translator beyond direct translation. 8

G COMPUTE AND BUDGET ACCOUNTING Hardware and elapsed time. Training used two NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs with about 96 GB of memory each. A Full run took about 8–9 wall-clock hours per round, or 80–90 hours for ten rounds, and benchmark evaluation took about 4–5 hours in total. The training-side cost includes task generation, native branch training, recipient attempts, verification, teacher and student passes, screening, and confirmation, also in rounds that retain the root.

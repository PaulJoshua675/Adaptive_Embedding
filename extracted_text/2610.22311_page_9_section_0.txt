Figure 4 shows rollouts from dense LingBot-Fast-Cam and Velox that start from the same input frame and follow the same camera trajectory. We sample frames at fixed fractions of each rollout rather than choosing a moment for each case. Appendix A.2 provides additional cases. The two models are visually close early in the rollout and separate as it grows. Dense attention gradually departs from the input frame. In the bedroom case, it replaces the room with an unrelated dark interior. In the figurine case, the red outfit fades to pale pink. In the bamboo case, the path degrades into a puddle and then a featureless surface, and in the desert case dark artifacts spread across the dunes. Velox stays close to the input frame in all four cases. It revisits the same bedroom with the same bedspread, desk, and armchair, and it keeps the figurine colors, the bamboo path, and the sand ripples intact. This agrees with the higher spatial consistency of Velox in Table 1 (74.3 versus 73.0). These cases are selected for illustration, and the benchmark scores remain the primary evidence of aggregate quality. 9

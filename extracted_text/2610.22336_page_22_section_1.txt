At evaluation, the learned policies select their most likely action at every visit. All methods are evaluated on the same test patients with the same random numbers, and on the fitted patients, each patient is evaluated under 50 independent replicates of the random perturbations. For every policy π and evaluation radius ρ > 0, we estimate the robust value minσ J(π, σ; D) of π by training a new best-response adversary against π, held fixed, for 2,000 PPO updates, on patients drawn from the calibrated population Dcal for the virtual test population and on the fitted patients for the fitted test population, and average the performance of the policy against this adversary, which samples its actions, over 32 independent repetitions. It should be noted that the best-response adversary is computed numerically, and the value attained against it hence provides an upper estimate of the exact robust value. AT50 and continuous treatment are evaluated against their own best-response adversaries. Under shifts in the dynamics, we compute Gon(c) under the same adversary, held fixed, acting on the continuous-treatment trajectory, with common random numbers.

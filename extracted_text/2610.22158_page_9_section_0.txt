Limitations Our work has several limitations. First, Premis- eSQL focuses on English NL2SQL benchmarks and may not generalize to multilingual settings or real-world interactive database systems. Second, although the benchmark covers multiple premise failure categories, the constructed errors are still taxonomy-driven and may not fully capture nat- urally occurring user mistakes. Finally, our eval- uation relies partially on LLM-based automatic judges, which may introduce evaluation bias de- spite strong agreement with human annotations.

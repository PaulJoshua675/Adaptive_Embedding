Previ- ously produced keys and values are assumed to stay fixed; the matrix retains their summed outer-product contribution. Cost and Rank. For n tokens per clip, adding the outer products costs O(ndvdk) per clip and stores dvdk matrix entries. Over T clips, incremental accumulation costs O(Tndvdk). Rebuilding the matrix from all available key–value pairs at every step costs O(T 2ndvdk). These costs cover dense matrix accumulation given the keys and values. Video encoding, compression, adapter generation, and responses add their respective costs. Each new clip can increase the matrix rank.

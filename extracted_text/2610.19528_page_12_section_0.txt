A.4 Learned Policy Behaviors Beyond retrieval frequency, we further analyze how SRAT organizes its interaction trajectory across tasks. Specifically, we measure multi-retrieval ra- tio, the fraction of examples for which more than one retrieval query is issued, and reasoning ratio, the fraction for which the model generates an ex- plicit reasoning block before translation. Table 8 shows that the learned policy adapts not only whether to retrieve but also how much inter- action and reasoning to use.

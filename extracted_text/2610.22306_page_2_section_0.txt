Past a multi-second out-of- view gap (football’s dominant failure; median camera exit 111 frames) the covariance has diverged and a search re- gion wider than the frame is no gate at all, so the offline re- identification stage drops the motion gate and lets appear- ance alone link tracklets. Each part is established by measurement. A controlled mechanism ladder on identical cached inputs shows hard Mahalanobis gating on the same covariance degrades as- sociation at every threshold we swept, while richer uses add nothing measurable: the scalar relative positional un- certainty is, empirically, a sufficient statistic for association tolerance. Deferred commitment is worth +1.0 HOTA on both test servers over the identical system without it, and removing the merge’s motion veto is worth +4.9 HOTA on SportsMOT val and transfers to DanceTrack under a differ- ent embedding model. A tracker evaluated on a leaderboard is evaluated jointly with its detector, so we anchor our comparison in a detector- deconfounded protocol: on the exact public detections and embeddings of Deep-EIoU [20], ARC-SORT scores 80.31 HOTA (higher order tracking accuracy [26]) versus their published 77.20, a +3.1 margin produced entirely by as- sociation, the decisive share from removing the merge’s motion gate (Sec. 4.2). With our own detector stack ARC- SORT reaches 81.82 on the SportsMOT test set (rank 6 of 69 as of August 2026), with the fewest identity switches of any entry (1,414) at tied-third MOTA (97.09) and, de- spite the 30th-best detection accuracy (DetA), the 4th-best identity F1 (IDF1): association, not detection, produces the result.

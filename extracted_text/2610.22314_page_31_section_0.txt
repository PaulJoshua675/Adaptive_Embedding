The benchmark uses the first 50 of the 100 (Appendix G). D The Patient Harness Session Scenarios. First-session scenarios are written in advance with Claude Sonnet 4.6, conditioned on the rendered formulation, the core belief (shown to the writer only), the session affect and the patient’s writing style, which is applied to the opening message. Later scenarios are written by the patient model itself (Figure 15), conditioned on the profile, the previous scenario, the full previous session, the time elapsed, the agenda items left open and a sampled reason. The first session starts on a sampled weekday and hour, and both sides are told the day and time. Table 12 gives the distributions the rest of the schedule is drawn from. System prompt.

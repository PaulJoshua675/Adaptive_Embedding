C Evaluation Protocols C.1 Evaluation Datasets DTC-Tex comprises 100 objects from the Digital Twin Catalog (Dong et al., 2025). We score the 2,399 catalog assets for surface-texture complexity from six views with Qwen3.8-27B, discard near-duplicates and broken meshes, and retain the 100 highest-scoring objects. Each object is evaluated from a front-view reference, together with its ground-truth mesh, PBR maps, and a 16-view camera rig. Typical cases are household products with printed labels or decorative motifs, such as canned goods, decorated ceramics, and books. Complex-Tex complements this set with 110 Sketchfab (Sketchfab, n.d.) meshes that carry denser inscriptions and finer patterns on more varied shapes. This subset includes branded packaging and vehicles covered by logos and fine print, as well as buildings and characters with more irregular shapes. A method receives only the designated reference image, or that image together with the mesh when geometry is given. Figure 8 shows examples from both datasets. Both DTC-Tex and Complex-Tex underwent strict duplicate checks during data collection to ensure no overlap with the training assets. We will release both evaluation benchmarks. C.2 Rendering and Metrics Protocol. Both settings evaluate the PBR asset each method exports, rendered under a shared protocol rather than compared in UV space, because latent decoding or remeshing changes the face layout. Predicted and ground-truth assets are rendered with the same cameras and forward lighting at 1024 × 1024 and downsampled to 512 × 512 with Lanczos interpolation; methods whose output lives in another coordinate frame are brought into the evaluation frame by their own transform or, where marked †, by a similarity transform to the ground-truth mesh. Per-view metrics are PSNR, SSIM (Wang et al., 2004), and VGG-LPIPS (Zhang et al., 2018) over the full image with a black background, so they penalize errors in silhouette as well as appearance, and CLIP-I, the cosine similarity of CLIP ViT-L/14 features (Radford et al., 2021) between corresponding views. We additionally report PSNRfg on both shaded RGB and albedo, the same PSNR restricted to the ground-truth object mask, so that large black background regions do not dominate the score. 21

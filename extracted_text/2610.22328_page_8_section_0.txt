Table 1. Quantitative comparison on EdiVal-10. All metrics are computed after 10 cumulative edits and averaged over the 252 chains, except the anchor distance, which is averaged over the 148 chains whose anchor object lies mostly outside the edited objects (Appendix B). Quality is measured by the KRIS-Bench image-quality score of the final image, assigned by a VLM judge (1–5), and by the change in HPSv3 from turn 1 to turn 10. Preservation is measured by the KRIS-Bench consistency score assigned by the same judge on the whole image (1–5; higher means fewer unrequested changes), the DINOv2 distance between the source and the final image on unedited anchor objects, and the LPIPS distance on the background outside all edited objects. Editing is measured by the KRIS-Bench instruction-following score at turn 10 (1–5); it checks that stability does not come from skipping edits. Methods are grouped by backbone; the best result in each column is shown in bold and the second best is underlined. ∗: significantly different from our model (more than two standard errors, paired over chains).

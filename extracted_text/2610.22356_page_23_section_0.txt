DK-VLM: Geometry as Position A.9 Injection Layer Configuration We examine the number and placement of Dual-Kernel Attention layers in DK-VLM-8B. To reduce training cost, all configurations are trained on the same 10% subset of the full training data, with the remaining training settings held constant. All layer indices are zero-based. Pairwise geometric supervision is applied at the penultimate selected injection layer in each configuration. Number of Injection Layers. Increasing the number of injection layers from three to five improves VSI-Bench performance by 0.77 percentage points, while increasing it from five to seven yields only a further 0.06-point gain (Table 10). We therefore use five injection layers by default, achieving performance close to the seven-layer configuration with fewer geometric branches. Table 10.

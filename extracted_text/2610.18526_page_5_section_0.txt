Evaluation Metrics. We measure retrieval qual- ity with Recall@20 and nDCG@20 at the final layer, in two variants that differ only in which gold copies count. All-gold nDCG/Recall count every copy of the answer, including the same-language one; we use these in the diagnosis to character- ize the general effect of mixing. Cross-lingual nDCG/Recall count only foreign-language copies, excluding the same-language answer from both the hits and the ideal ranking; we use these for the interventions, to isolate the cross-lingual failure the corrections target. We also report the share of a query’s top-20 in its own language and in En- glish, which separates self-crowding from English- gravity, and cross-lingual disagreement, the overlap and rank correlation between the rankings two lan- guages produce for the same question, averaged over all other languages. Together, these capture whether a correction retrieves more relevant doc- uments, changes which languages fill the ranking, and brings languages into agreement. 5

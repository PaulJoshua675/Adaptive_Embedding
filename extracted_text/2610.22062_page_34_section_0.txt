Of the 108 QC-confirmed patients, 8 anchor on an administration record and 91 on an adjudicated note, while 9 have no reliable evidence of a start date and are excluded from the timepoint analysis. None anchors on a dispense record, because trofinetide fills go through a specialty pharmacy and never reach the institutional dispense tables. The correction is large and does not depend on the labels, moving the median patient by 116 days, with more than half moving by more than 90 days. It also moves six cells out of the follow-up range, taking the grid from 189 cells to 183 while conserving the contributing patients and the 47 positive cells, and accuracy under the deployed prompt rises from 77.1% to 79.6%. That rise cannot be attributed to the anchor alone, because the note corpus was refreshed in the same rerun, so we report the two grids side by side. Two negative controls confirm that the result does not follow from displacing windows as such, since neither randomly re-anchoring the patients nor permuting their timepoint labels reaches the observed accuracy in 200 draws. The corrected anchor does not remove the problem entirely. It cannot recover the lag between a symptom starting and a note recording it, and the annotation is cell-level and carries no dates, so a reviewer may place a symptom at the stage of treatment where it clinically belongs rather than at the timestamp of the note that records it. Both effects are visible in the gap between the two ways of scoring the pipeline. Judged note by note, where the anchor plays no part, agreement with expert review is about 90%, at 90.2% over the full 17-phenotype spot-check and 91.4% over the 58 of those cases that fall inside an evaluation window (Note S6). Judged cell by cell, where every call must also land in the right six-month window, the reported configuration reaches 84.2%. We read the difference as the cost of identifying the start date from the record rather than as a failure to read the notes, which is why the residual temporal errors in main-text Table 4 are the largest remaining category after threshold disagreement.

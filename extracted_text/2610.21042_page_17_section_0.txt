Figure F.1: Biomedical review workflow in the PRISMA 2020 format (Page et al., 2021). The layout and record colors follow Figure 3. The 3,000 records yield 1,341 included studies after screening and full-text assessment; 336 reports are not retrieved and seven are retrieved but not extracted, shown in dark grey within the not-retrieved box. Numbered examples show a survey exclusion (1), a reversed exclusion of a medical-exam evaluation (2), and extraction of model names and expert evaluation with source excerpts (3). Category counts include every assignment to a study. System processing times and LLM costs of completed-workflow calls are shown beside each stage.

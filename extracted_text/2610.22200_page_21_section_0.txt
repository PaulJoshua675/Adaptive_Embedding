A full-desktop screenshot may show the user’s activity rather than the agent’s target, and visual input may be unnecessary during programmatic work. Following the model- initiated observation design in Section 3.2, the agent chooses when to observe and which target to inspect. A program can capture observations, process structured results, and emit relevant information within one execution; images are interpreted by the model when delivered in a subsequent input. Bounded history and image retention limit accumulated context. These mechanisms reduce unnecessary observations, but do not eliminate the need to refresh state when the interface changes. Managing Accessibility Information. Accessibility observations vary in size, stability, and coverage across applications. The harness filters irrelevant nodes and represents the retained hierarchy as a flat list linked by parent_id. Element identities and snapshot versions support reference tracking, while search, page, and read retrieve relevant information from a retained snapshot without repeatedly capturing the window. When accessibility information is insufficient, visual observation provides a complementary source of evidence. The choice of action-delivery mechanism remains separate from the choice of observation modality. Maintaining Task Continuity through User Interaction. Real-device tasks may require clarification, authentication, or authorization that the agent cannot supply independently. The ask_user interface supports these exchanges, including resolving ambiguous targets, requesting assistance with login or verification, and obtaining authorization for consequential actions. User responses and corrections become part of the ongoing task. The runtime preserves interpreter state, resource bindings, and interaction history, allowing execution to resume without rebuilding the workflow. 4.3.3 Preliminary Real-Device Results As our real-device benchmark is still under development, we study seven model configurations on four macOS tasks: workbook reasoning, PDF form filling, dietary-constrained shopping research, and hotel comparison, with up to three recorded runs per task and model.

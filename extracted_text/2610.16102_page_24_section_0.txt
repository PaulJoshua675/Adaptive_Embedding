This is the most speculative prediction here, and its experimental design is left open. 9.2 Limitations • Scope of the word. “Tacit” is used in the structural sense of §4.1. Nothing here addresses whether a substrate can be a knower in Polanyi’s personal sense, or the phenomenology of knowing. • One corpus. The empirical anchors come from one relation (documented mentorship), one source (Wikidata, integrating the Mathematics Genealogy Project and MacTutor) and one tracer set (the 64 Fields Medalists). Wikidata over-represents Western, university- affiliated and doctoral-advisor transmission (2026d), and the mentorship channel misses texts, correspondence and translation except where node semantics recover them. • Analogies are correspondences. The pianist, the cyclist and the Identikit are matched to VaCoAl/PyVaCoAl operations by structure, not identity. In particular, the CASRAM face-recognition product matches feature embeddings computed on a GPU; we do not claim that it binds faces as role–filler pairs. • Only the poles are mapped. The audit–valuation trade-off is documented at RR = 1 and RR = 0. Intermediate rescue rates have not been characterized. • Hardware claims are not used. CASRAM is physically realized and commercial, but its in-memory speed and power remain unbenchmarked, and its SRAM capacity gives a small frontier size compared with the PyVaCoAl DRAM-CAM realization (2026e; 2026f). No argument in this paper depends on CASRAM performance. • Proposals are not demonstrations. Auditable abduction (§8.4) and the Reflective layer it relies on are designs; 2026f states that the Reflective layer’s sufficient conditions are future work and its reachability is argued, not shown. • LLM evidence ages. The findings in §7.3 come from specific models and methods. Introspective ability appears to grow with model capability (Anthropic 2025b), so the default rootlessness of LLM outputs may weaken, and the argument should be re-checked as models change. • Reproducibility. The code for the software version of PyVaCoAl is proprietary and available for commercial and collaborative use (2026a); 2026d states that queries, configu- rations, the tracer set and a reference implementation are to be deposited with a DOI on acceptance. Independent replication of the claims used here depends on that release. 9.3 Open problems Three questions are left open. Can audit–valuation complementarity be stated as an inequality between a measure of audit exactness and a measure of valuation contrast, with the closed-form CR2 law as its equality case?

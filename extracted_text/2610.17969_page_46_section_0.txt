The transfer experiments test whether a rate selected from this range remains effective at a larger model size. Sweep-to-scale transfer. We select a learning rate for each architecture using the original five-point 51M sweep and apply the selected learning rate unchanged at 124M (Table 7). Under this protocol, the transferred learning rate brings GCT to 3.045, within 0.002 nats of nGPT at 3.043.

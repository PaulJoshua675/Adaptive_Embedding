We presented HASTE, a unified framework for generative recom- mendation with hierarchical and attribute-aware supervision. By exploiting the multi-level structure of semantic IDs and incorporat- ing confidence-aware gating, level weighting, and scheduled sam- pling, HASTE improves hierarchical prediction. Attribute-specific auxiliary objectives further preserve fine-grained item information, particularly for tail items. Experiments across multiple Amazon domains and a large-scale fashion dataset show consistent improve- ments over strong baselines, with ablations confirming the comple- mentary benefits of the proposed components.

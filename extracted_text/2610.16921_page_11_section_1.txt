To address this limitation, we proposed Temporal Structure Embedded Reinforcement Learning, which reformulates win- dow optimization as a constrained Markov Decision Pro- cess in which an FSM-constrained action mask keeps the selected windows consistent with the temporal structure of MI events while assigning each of them an explicit temporal role. A Transformer-based history encoder captures long- range inter-window dependencies with better training stability than recurrent designs.

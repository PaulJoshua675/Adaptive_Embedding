The numerical subgroup results are given in Table 11; the sample is too small and wording assignment too limited to establish universal differences between profits and losses. 4.4 Simple surrogates and size comparisons A small incremental R2 gain from EPS means that adding the surprise sign helps little in this particular surrogate. It does not prove that every computation inside the model is a two-cue rule, and omitted nonlinearities or interactions can matter. Pair and introduction indicators vary between blocks, so their R2 increment can also absorb correlated numerical baseline variation; it is not an isolated causal template effect. The fully crossed order and label controls identify their specific prompt interventions more directly. The held-commentary-pair result is a harder wording transfer test than leaving out one numerical block. The three Qwen sizes permit a within-family descriptive comparison, but three checkpoints cannot support a fitted scaling law, and parameter count is confounded with training and post-training choices. 4.5 Bridge to the audited pilot Qwen2.5-0.5B has a mean absolute bridge difference of 15.64 pp, a maximum of 40.74 pp, and 22.9% conditional decision flips. Qwen2.5-1.5B has a mean absolute bridge difference of 6.21 pp, a maximum of 51.94 pp, and 8.3% conditional decision flips.

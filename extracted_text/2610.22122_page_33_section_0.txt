Expected Improvement (EI). EI estimates the expected improvement over the best effect observed so far and prioritizes candidates with larger expected gains (Jones et al., 1998). Thompson Sampling (TS). TS draws an effect function from the surrogate posterior and selects a batch by ranking unmeasured candidates under that draw, allowing model uncertainty to influence selection (Thompson, 1933). TopUncertain. TopUncertain ranks candidates by predictive standard deviation and allocates experiments to regions where the surrogate is most uncertain (Mehrjou et al., 2022). BADGE. BADGE selects diverse batches using k-means++ in a gradient representation space. We use the GeneDisco regression implementation and its proxy gradient representations (Ash et al., 2020; Mehrjou et al., 2022). CoreSet. CoreSet greedily expands geometric coverage in the candidate representation space extracted by the surrogate. It successively selects the candidate farthest from the existing selection centers (Sener and Savarese, 2018). KMeans-Embedding. This method uses k-means to cluster candidate representations extracted by the surrogate and selects candidates near cluster centers to represent different regions of the candidate space (Mehrjou et al., 2022). Random. Random samples uniformly without replacement from the current unmeasured pool, providing a reference for random screening.

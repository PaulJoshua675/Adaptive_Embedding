A.4 COMPLETE IG-OPD ALGORITHM Reference-answer probes and masks. The frozen teacher scores only reference-answer tokens under the initial context and each post-observation history, using teacher forcing rather than gen- erating another answer. Their mean log-likelihood yields the potential and adjacent differences gt defined in the main text. The response has gT = 0 but retains a positive ITC budget. For a search ac- tion at, IQC masks select the parsed tool-name and supported argument-value spans, mapped back to generated-token positions; reasoning, wrapper tags, and observations are excluded.

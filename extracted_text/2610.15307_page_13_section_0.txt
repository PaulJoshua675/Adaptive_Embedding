The CAST module aligns heterogeneous ZCP distributions through ECDF and enhances cross-feature interactions through synergy transformation. We evaluate CAST on NAS-Bench-201/CIFAR-10 using 1% labeled architectures for meta- training and the full benchmark for testing. Four strategies are compared: raw ZCP features, ECDF-only alignment, Synergy-only, and the complete CAST pipeline. To verify both effectiveness and model-agnosticity, we test CAST with linear regression (LR) and random forest (RF). As shown in Table 6, LR benefits substantially from ECDF alignment, improving τ from 0.491 to 0.545, while the full CAST pipeline further increases it to 0.560. For RF, the gain is more modest. The synergy-only variant obtains a slightly higher τ of 0.733, whereas full CAST achieves the best ρ of 0.904. These results suggest that CAST is most beneficial for simpler learners and provides a smaller but consistent refinement for the default nonlinear predictor. 5

In this work, we propose EOS-Story, a training-free framework that adap- tively models scene-specific variance in visual storytelling across text and vision spaces. We introduce Cross-Contextual Bridging, which decouples the narra- tive into identity and scene sub-prompts and bridges the resulting semantic gap by injecting phrase-level appearance residuals into the scene’s ⟨EOS⟩embedding. To complement this with structural adaptation, we further propose Adaptive Identity Preservation, which anchors subject features at the scene-specific scale and refines integration through Identity-Aware Self-Attention. To evaluate scene adaptability, we extend Consistory+ with diverse framing variations (Con- sistory+_+). Extensive experiments show that EOS-Story achieves competitive or superior performance in both prompt fidelity and structural adaptability. Acknowledgements.

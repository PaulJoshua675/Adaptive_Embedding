The shared behavior tokenizer, causal encoder, and behavior-memory construction are frozen for all variants unless otherwise noted. Finally, these generative experiments are intended to establish that the shared TransX behavioral represen- tation supports autoregressive retrieval and slate-generation readouts and that preference optimization improves the corresponding held-out generative diagnostics. They are not intended as matched-budget evidence that the generative variants outperform their discriminative counterparts.

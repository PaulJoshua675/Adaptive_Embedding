Each candidate is a single gene or combinatorial perturbation. Table C1 summarizes perturbation types, phenotypes, and hit rules. Raw phenotype scores are transformed into effects 𝑒(𝑥) according to the direction in the table. For a pool of 𝑁= |X| candidates, the score at rank max{1, ⌊0.05𝑁⌋} in descending effect order defines the fixed threshold 𝜏, with hits given by 𝑧(𝑥) = 1{𝑒(𝑥) ≥𝜏}. The target hit proportion is 5%, and 𝜏is the corresponding phenotype score threshold. The threshold is fixed before screening. The phenotype and hit label of each candidate are revealed when it is queried. IFN-𝛾. This task identifies genes that regulate interferon-𝛾production in T cells. Enrichment differences between populations with high and low cytokine levels measure perturbation effects. We target strong effects in either direction (Schmidt et al., 2022). IL-2. This task identifies regulators of interleukin-2 production following T cell stimulation. Enrichment differences between populations with high and low expression define the phenotype, with strong effects in either direction included as discovery targets (Schmidt et al., 2022). NK. This task studies cancer cell genes that affect sensitivity or resistance to natural killer cells. The phenotype is the sum of standardized enrichment scores across two replicates for perturbed K562 cells exposed to NK cells relative to controls. Strong effects in either direction are targeted (Zhuang et al., 2019). Carnevale. This task studies genes that affect T cell proliferation under immunosuppressive conditions.

Tables 7–9 report paired score comparisons and 95% Student-t confidence intervals across objects, scenes, and procedural graphics (where W/L/T tallies the signs of per-prompt score differences). On the 60-prompt object benchmark—which concentrates heavily on complex specifications (52 detailed, 4 medium, and 4 simple prompts)—3DCodeVerse Harness lifts Gemini 3.8 Flash by +25.3 points (86.6 vs. 61.3, winning 56 of 60 prompts) and outperforms Claude Design by +49.2 points (59–1 W/L/T), even surpassing the native Claude Opus 5.5 baseline by +9.4 points (86.6 vs. 77.2). These gains generalize across tracks (Table 9): 3DCodeVerse Harness yields large improvements on procedural graphics for GPT-6 Astra (+30.4 points, 10–1 W/L/T), Gemini 3.8 Flash (+27.2, 10–1), and GPT-6 Luna (+6.8, 8–3), as well as on 3D scenes for Flash (+19.4, 9–1) and Luna (+14.6, 7–0). For Claude Sonnet 5 under single-round (T = 0) execution, 3DCodeVerse Harness wins the majority of graphics prompts (6–5 W/L/T) and improves uncapped scene quality (∆Svis = +2.8), while overall mean scores reflect strict deterministic gate caps applied to outlier frames prior to multi-round refinement.

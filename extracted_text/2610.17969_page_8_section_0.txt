Under per-token int4, GCT changes by −7.4 points, nGPT by −17.4, and the other baselines by −22.6 to −23.1. 5.6 PINNs under changes of physical units We use PINNs to test NDA’s loss-aggregation rule on data-fitting and partial differential equation (PDE) residual losses, which can carry different physical units. Following Sec. 2.2 and Thm. 2, we divide each loss by a characteristic scale with matching units to keep the total objective unchanged under a unit conversion. We derive these scales from the measured magnitude of the data or forcing and the units introduced by the PDE operator, and use the scaled output head of Sec. 4. We then re-express the same Burgers and Helmholtz problems in different units to test whether this construction preserves physical solution accuracy without retuning the loss weights. Across the 104 unit sweep, NDA’s scaled output head and loss weights keep the worst relative L2 error at 0.015, whereas equal or adaptive weights degrade for at least one rescaling (Table 17, five seeds). Figure 9 visualizes the recovered Burgers fields. These results show that the loss-aggregation rule, applied together with the scaled output head, preserves accurate physical solutions across the tested unit systems.

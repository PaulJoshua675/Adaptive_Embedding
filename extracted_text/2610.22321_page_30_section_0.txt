F HUMAN EVALUATION PROTOCOL AND RESULTS F.1 SAMPLING AND ANNOTATION PROTOCOL We randomly sample 100 prompts from the three generation benchmarks with fixed coverage: 30 prompts from AVGenBench, 30 from AV-Phys-Bench, and 40 from VABench. For each prompt, we generate or collect one sample from each of LTX-2, OmniNFT, and Ours. Each case therefore contains three videos with the same English prompt. The three videos are shown as anonymous options A, B, and C in a randomized order, and model identities are hidden from annotators. Ten annotators with at least a bachelor’s degree independently evaluate 30 cases each. The assign- ment is balanced so that every case is evaluated by exactly three annotators. Annotators answer two questions: 1. Audio-video consistency. Which sample better matches sound events to the visible action in timing and attributes? The judgment also considers whether narration, off-screen sound, ambient sound, and background music are appropriate for the scene. 2. Overall quality.

Biological knowledge guides selection through pathway information, literature, or knowledge encoded in language models. BioBO and PerTurboAgent combine such knowledge with response modeling, whereas BioDiscoveryAgent incorporates experimental observations into its language model context without fitting a response surrogate. SOFisher uses observed spatial features as inputs to its sampling policy rather than fitting a phenotype prediction model. Online acquisition updates use feedback from the current task to update the acquisition function’s parameters. Value bootstrapping constructs training targets from current rewards and estimated successor values. GLARE updates its selection policy during screening but does not explicitly bootstrap a successor value.

Conformity to these maxims is therefore part of model quality alongside factual accuracy. Model-written text also contains expressions that recur often enough for readers to treat them as signatures of machine authorship (Juzek and Ward, 2025; Shaib et al., 2025). Most of these expres- sions are ordinary resources of the language, and human writers use the same devices where the dis- course calls for them. Whether a recurring expres- sion amounts to a tic thus depends on whether the model knows when it is called for. Hymes (1972) placed this knowledge at the core of communica- tive competence, as a judgment of whether an ut- terance is appropriate to its context, separate from whether it is grammatically possible and from how often it is in fact produced.

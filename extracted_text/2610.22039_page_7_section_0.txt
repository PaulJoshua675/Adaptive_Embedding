We compare three variants constructed from the same repository pool: Atomic-PR, which treats each PR independently; Temporal-Window, which groups PRs merged within a 30-day window; and WEAVE, which ag- gregates PRs according to typed-locus dependencies. Full training details are provided in the Appendix. Results. Under matched training conditions, WEAVE con- sistently outperforms both controlled alternatives across model scales and benchmarks (Figure 4). On WEAVE- Bench, WEAVE improves Resolved over Atomic-PR by 3.0/3.7 pp at 4B/35B, with larger Graph Credit gains (5.4/6.2 pp), indicating stronger dependency-consistent progress even without full resolution. Improvements trans- fer to single-PR benchmarks: SWE-bench Verified improves by 3.6/2.8 pp over Atomic-PR, with consistent gains on SWE-bench Pro and Multilingual. Temporal-Window pro- vides only modest improvements, confirming that temporal proximity alone introduces weaker supervision than verified dependency structure. Starting from the WEAVE-SFT checkpoint (Figure 4), we apply GRPO (Shao et al. 2024) on the same WEAVE-Train tasks with identical rollout bud- gets (8 rollouts per task), varying only the reward formula- tion: (1) no further RL (SFT-only baseline); (2) binary Re- solved reward; (3) flat F2P-based reward (r = FlatF2P); and (4) RWEAVE (§4.2), which places Graph Credit as the dom- inant component (λgraph = 0.5, λsolve = 0.25, λf2p = 0.15, λreg =0.1), with a three-phase DAG-complexity curriculum (∼1.0 pp over flat scheduling; Appendix). Results. RWEAVE consistently outperforms all alterna- tives (Figure 5). On Qwen3.5-4B, it improves Resolved by 3.7 pp over SFT and 2.0 pp over flat F2P; on Qwen3.5- 35B-A3B, gains are 4.3 and 2.6 pp respectively, showing that graph-conditioned reward scales with model capacity. Graph Credit separation is larger (+5.6/+6.2 pp over SFT at 4B/35B), indicating disproportionate improvement in struc- tured partial progress. The gains transfer to SWE-bench Ver- ified (+3.2/+4.2 pp over SFT), confirming out-of-distribution generalization. Binary Resolved reward yields marginal gains (≤0.7 pp), consistent with sparse-signal limitations on long-horizon tasks (Wei et al. 2025). Flat F2P improves over binary but under-performs RWEAVE on all metrics, confirming that de- pendency conditioning eliminates phantom-credit shortcuts.

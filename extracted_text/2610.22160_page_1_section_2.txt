Parameter updates and context refinement provide complementary routes to adapting a large language model (LLM). Parameter optimization, including fine-tuning, reinforce- ment learning, and preference alignment, incorporates task data or feedback into model weights (Hu et al., 2021; Shao et al., 2024; Rafailov et al., 2023). For an individual test question, context optimization adapts a frozen model by using feedback to revise its textual instructions before generating a new response (Yuksekgonul et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2026; Madaan et al., 2023). We study how to translate such question-specific feedback into explicit control of the decoding process. A revised context already changes the next-token distribution at every prefix. Ordinary conditional generation, however, provides no separate control over the strength or token support of those changes.

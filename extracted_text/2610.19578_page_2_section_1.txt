Recommender systems are core components of modern online platforms, including e- commerce and social media [1–4]. Among existing paradigms, Collaborative Filtering (CF) remains fundamental due to its domain-agnostic nature and proficiency in capturing user in- terests from historical interactions [5–8]. Because these interactions naturally form bipartite graphs, Graph Collaborative Filtering (GCF) has emerged as a dominant approach [9–11]. Specifically, ID-based GCF models explicitly capture high-order collaborative signals via multi- hop message passing [12]. Architectures such as LightGCN [13] and LR-GCCF [14] further streamline this paradigm by omitting non-linear transformations, achieving high training and inference efficiency through a minimalist representation space. However, ID-only models inherently struggle with extreme data sparsity and the cold-start problem. Without sufficient interaction history, cold-start items cannot be effectively mapped into the mature collaborative space. This creates a severe distribution shift between warm and cold items, significantly bottlenecking model performance [15,16]. To mitigate this, a common practice is to incorporate side semantic features (e.g., text or multimodal metadata) to enrich the representation space [17–23]. While providing valuable contextual priors to bridge sparse interaction chains [21,24–26], these hybrid models typically rely on complex feature extractors and multi-layer fusion modules. This complexity not only escalates computational overhead but often compromises the purity of collaborative signals, thereby degrading general recommendation performance [27,28]. This dilemma raises a critical question: Can we leverage the rich priors of semantic features for cold-start items while preserving the lightweight architecture and collaborative efficiency of ID-based models? To answer this, we propose DualGCN, a novel dual-path graph knowledge distillation frame- work for cold-start recommendation. DualGCN enables approximate topological-to-semantic knowledge transfer without relying on heavy fusion modules. Our main contributions are summarized as follows: • We introduce DualGCN, a lightweight distillation framework featuring an asymmetric dual-path architecture. By replacing traditional heavy fusion modules with this design, it achieves approximate semanticknowledge transfer to the topological space, drastically reducing online inference costs. 1

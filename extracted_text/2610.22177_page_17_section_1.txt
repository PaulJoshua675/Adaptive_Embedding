In this work, we presented a motion- and gravity-aware framework for feed- forward multi-view 3D reconstruction under the Learning Using Privileged Information (LUPI) paradigm. During training, residual flow and gravity cues are used as privileged supervision signals; during inference, the model operates with standard visual inputs only, while retaining the geometric benefits transferred from privileged modalities. Extensive experiments show consistent improvements in reconstruction accuracy and robustness, especially in dynamic scenes, under ambiguous ge- ometry, and across challenging camera motion. The results further indicate that the optimal injection stage is architecture-dependent, highlighting the importance of aligning privileged-feature integration with backbone design. Overall, this study demonstrates that LUPI is an effective and practical mechanism for introducing motion and physical priors into feed-forward 3D reconstruction without sacrificing inference efficiency. We hope this perspec- tive motivates future work on temporally coherent and physically grounded feed-forward reconstruction with richer privileged training signals. 17

Backward Compatibility in Zero-Bit Neural Audio Watermarking 4. Method Although backward compatibility is a general concern, we focus exclusively on zero-bit audio watermarking in this work. Our method has three components: a backward-compatible training setup, a performance-weighted sampling strategy that allocates training iterations across the legacy chain, and a diversity loss that prevents the new embedder from drifting toward its predecessors. Backward-Compatible Training Setup. Embedders operate in the short-time Fourier transform (STFT) domain. The watermark is added to the magnitude spectrogram within the [500, 4000] Hz embedding band and the time-domain signal is reconstructed via inverse STFT using the original phase. Detectors mirror the embedder, they compute the STFT and a mel spectrogram restricted to the same embedding band, then feed it to the detection network. We extend the idea from SilentCipher [7] of enforcing a hard constraint on the maximum perceptual distortion, which lets us train with a detection-only loss. This distortion constraint drives all embedders to a consistent quality level (PESQ 4.3–4.5). Because every model then sits at essentially the same perceptual quality, we can compare models by detection accuracy alone. For version 𝑣= 1, training follows the standard procedure: a single embedder-detector pair is trained end-to-end with the push loss alone. For versions 𝑣> 1, two things change with respect to the 𝑣= 1 recipe. First, the new embedder 𝐸𝑣and detector 𝐷𝑣may use a different architecture from any previous version. This is by design, since real watermarking deployments evolve their architectures over time (Section 5). Second, the training objective is extended with the diversity loss introduced below, which is needed to keep the new embedder distinguishable from the legacy chain and thereby preserve the version boundary. At each iteration, the detector 𝐷𝑣is trained on the output of a single embedder sampled from the full chain {𝐸1, . . . , 𝐸𝑣} \ 𝑅, chosen by the performance-weighted sampling scheme described in the next paragraph. Because the legacy embedders are frozen and each embedder is drawn independently by this scheme, the training of 𝐷𝑣does not depend on the order of the embedders within the chain.

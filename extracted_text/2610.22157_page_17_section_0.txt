A preprint - October 16, 2026 Overall, this work demonstrates that implementation choices can play a critical role in the practical perfor- mance of PI-DeepONets. ATenH provides significant improvements over the Vanilla PI-DeepONet formula- tion; however, it should not be viewed as a final design, but rather as evidence that careful consideration of how these models are implemented can yield substantial gains. This suggests that further improvements may be achievable through continued optimisation of existing architectures, particularly within widely used frameworks such as PyTorch. Furthermore, the agreement between theoretical and empirical scaling indi- cates that these findings are not tied to a specific hardware or software configuration, but are expected to generalise to other implementations. Future work should explore the application of similar architectural improvements to tackle problems that have so far proved intractable for PI-DeepONets.

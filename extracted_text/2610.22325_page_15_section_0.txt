A.1 BEDLAM2 Holding out every tenth BEDLAM2 (Tesch et al., 2025) track leaves 27,727 training tracks (1,250,307 labelled frames) and 3,081 development tracks, with 1,454 subjects across the two subsets. The official test split contains 7,157 tracks and 181,263 labelled frames from 1,223 subjects. Body and camera labels are available every fifth frame of the 30 fps video, giving supervision at 6 Hz. The surfaces comprise neutral SMPL-X bodies (Pavlakos et al., 2019), garments with fixed topology across time, and hair envelopes derived from strand grooms. For each groom, we extract the 0.3 level set of a strand-density field, offset it 3 mm inward and decimate it to 25 % of its faces (Lorensen & Cline, 1987; Garland & Heckbert, 1997). We observe no independently simulated hair motion in the BEDLAM2 renders. The envelope vertices are bound to head triangles and transported with the posed head.

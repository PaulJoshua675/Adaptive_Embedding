Latent-evidence recovery Evidence localization should be distinguished from failure attribu- tion or multi-agent disagreement analysis. The GraphTracer deep- search preprint [39] and the GraphGeo geo-localization preprint [40] addressed these respective problems; both preprints have been with- drawn. On the controlled tasks, each model’s bag-level class scores are compared with the held-out multi-hot indicator of Si. Set mean average precision (Set-mAP) is the primary, threshold-free latent- set recovery metric, computed over all classes. Hidden-lesion re- call (Hidden-R) is a complementary, threshold-dependent diagnostic computed over pairs (i, c) for which c ∈Si and c ≺yi; singleton bags do not enter its denominator. For Hidden-R only, each method and class uses a threshold selected on the validation set by maximiz- ing F1 against the retained latent sets. This evaluation-only calibra- tion does not enter model fitting or early stopping. Table 2 reports representative attention-, instance-, distillation-, severity-, and state- space-based MIL baselines together with all matched label-model controls. DomSet-MIL leads every table metric. Relative to WAN, the next strongest method, it improves Set-mAP by 0.035–0.038 and Hidden-R by 0.089–0.096 across the three controlled tasks. CR- Ordinal has higher dominant-label M-AUC than WAN on all three controlled tasks. WAN leads in Set-mAP and Hidden-R by 0.077– 0.090 and 0.140–0.152, respectively. The two evaluations give op- posite rankings for these controls. BRACS permits a separate ROI-level test. DSMIL contributes its native class-specific instance logits; Flat-CE, WAN, and DomSet- MIL contribute their class-specific patch scores. The same top-q readout is used for every method. Each annotated ROI receives the maximum score among patches whose centers fall inside it, yield- ing ROI-mAP over ROI labels. Secondary-ROI recall (Sec-ROI-R) is the fraction of annotated lower-priority ROIs containing at least one of the slide’s top-q patches for that ROI class. ROI annotations remain unavailable during training. The BRACS columns of Table 2 report this comparison; methods without class-specific patch scores are excluded. DomSet-MIL obtains 0.625 ROI-mAP and 0.500 Sec- ROI-R, versus WAN’s 0.594 and 0.451, respectively. These gains reflect improvements in ROI-level evidence ranking and in the cov- erage of secondary ROIs by the top-q patches. 3.4.

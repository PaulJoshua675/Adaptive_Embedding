This setting expands the exploration space and requires the Exploration Agent to additionally infer cross-application dependencies, in- cluding the execution order and the transfer of artifacts or intermediate results, before proposing a long-horizon instruction. We have already generated a small number of such prototype tasks and included them in the task pool. B.6 GENERAL DATA MIXTURE The SFT data mixture follows a commonly used roughly 1:1 mixing ratio, consisting of approx- imately 31K filtered distilled desktop samples and 40K general-purpose samples. The general- purpose data covers GUI understanding, coding, mathematics, OCR, chart interpretation, and dia- logue, helping stabilize model training and reduce over-specialization to desktop trajectories. C TRAINING DETAILS C.1 SFT CONFIGURATION We perform full-parameter SFT with Megatron-SWIFT (Zhao et al., 2025). Training uses tensor parallelism of 4, pipeline parallelism of 1, and bfloat16 precision. We set the global and micro batch sizes to 16 and 1, respectively, train for one epoch, use a maximum sequence length of 32,768, and enable sample packing. The learning rate is 1 × 10−5, with a minimum learning rate of 1 × 10−6 and a warmup fraction of 0.05. In addition to the settings in Table 10, we enable full recomputa- tion, FlashAttention (Dao et al., 2022), FlashLinearAttention (Yang & Zhang, 2024), and sequence parallelism for efficient and stable long-context full-parameter training.

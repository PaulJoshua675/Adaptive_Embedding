(3.42) Decoding Phase. The fusion mechanism of the proposed Fusion Vision Transformer is performed during the encoding stage, while the decoding stage is dedicated to feature refinement and coher- ence enhancement. The decoding process is structured around a series of blocks that progressively refine the representations across three hierarchical spatial levels. At each level, skip connections from the corresponding encoding stages are concatenated with the outputs of the decoding blocks. This design facilitates stable gradient propagation and mitigates the information bottleneck that typ- ically arises when integrating heterogeneous visual modalities characterized by distinct underlying probability distributions.

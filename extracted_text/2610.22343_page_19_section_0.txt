A.5 DATA AND EVALUATION PROTOCOLS A.5.1 DATASETS AND TEMPORAL SAMPLING We construct one candidate window per sequence. Each window contains four historical observa- tions ending at anchor t and ten future targets. Eligibility is determined solely by the availability of the required RGB images, camera poses, and LiDAR measurements, together with timestamp matching. Using a fixed temporal position and at most one window per sequence prevents longer sequences from receiving greater weight. For Waymo, the candidate pool contains one window from each of the 202 segments in the official validation split. We define logical states at 5 Hz and place the anchor at the tenth logical state, 1.8 s after the first complete timestamp. A complete timestamp contains FRONT, FRONT LEFT, and FRONT RIGHT images together with the vehicle pose. The four observations occur at t −0.6, t −0.4, t −0.2, and t, and the ten targets span t + 0.2 through t + 2.0 s. For each nominal time, we select the nearest complete timestamp within 20 ms. We partition the 202 segments into 102 segments used for checkpoint selection and 100 held-out segments reserved for final evaluation. The evaluation subset is sampled uniformly without replacement using seed 20260804, after which its segment identities and anchors are fixed for all paired comparisons. No segment in the held-out evaluation subset is used for checkpoint selection. This fixed sample contains 79 Dynamic and 21 Static windows under the partition defined in Appendix A.4.1. For KITTI, we consider the 151 sequences covered by the KITTI Depth Completion annotations and construct at most one fixed window per sequence. Logical states follow the VGGT-World two-raw- frame stride, with the anchor placed at the tenth logical state, corresponding to raw-frame offset 18 from the first image. We retain a drive when all four historical and ten future RGB, pose, and Li- DAR records are available on this stride, yielding 113 eligible drives. Ineligible drives are recorded as skipped and are not replaced. This common anchor placement keeps the temporal position com- parable to Waymo while respecting KITTI’s native sampling cadence. For nuScenes, we construct one window for each of the 150 scenes in the official validation split. Because CAM FRONT is sampled asynchronously, the anchor is defined as the image nearest to 1.8 s after the scene start, subject to a 120 ms tolerance, rather than as the tenth stored image.

B.2 INTEGRATION WITH DENSE SCENE RECONSTRUCTION To place our human geometry in the scene, we use VGGT-Ω’s full-frame depth DΩ t and world-to- camera pose (RΩ t , tΩ t ). The crop warp and person mask pair scene-depth samples with the real-camera depths of our predicted points Pt from Eq. (7). These depth pairs determine a person-to-scene scale sw,t, giving Xworld t = RΩ⊤ t (sw,tPt −tΩ t ). (8) Scene depth sets the scale only; our predicted depth supplies the human geometry. Background points come from the scene reconstruction outside the person masks. For Panoptic Studio Humans we fit one scale per person over the clip and score tracks in the benchmark cameras (Appendix C.1). For the moving-camera visualisations, sw,t is the per-frame median ratio of VGGT-Ωdepth to our depth over the eroded person mask.

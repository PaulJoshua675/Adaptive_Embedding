Imitation-Based and Interaction-Centric Planning. A major line of work learns autonomous driving policies from expert demon- strations [2, 6, 8, 11, 12, 20, 27]. UrbanDriver [21], PlanTF [5], and PLUTO [4] achieve strong closed-loop performance through care- fully designed imitation objectives, while UniAD [8], VAD [14], VADv2 [3], SparseDrive [23], DTPP [9], GameFormer [10], Inter- Fuser [22], and Causal-Planner [29] improve scene representation and interaction modeling. However, these approaches mainly op- timize expert imitation or relational representations, without ex- plicitly using scene relations to determine trajectory importance or planner-oriented value. GRID-Planner instead uses graph structure to prioritize planning-critical trajectories and construct relational returns over safety, lane/route consistency, and comfort. Generative Planning with Diffusion. Generative planners model future trajectories as samples from expressive distributions, sup- porting multi-modal driving behaviors [15, 25]. GenAD [30], Dif- fusion Planner [31], and Diffusion-ES [28] explore latent gener- ation, diffusion-based closed-loop planning, and reward-guided sampling, respectively. Flow Planner [24], DiffusionDrive [16], and BridgeDrive [17] further improve interactive modeling and diffusion- policy design. These methods primarily focus on trajectory genera- tion or sampling-time guidance, whereas GRID-Planner incorpo- rates structured relational signals into training through trajectory prioritization, relational evaluation, and diffusion-policy refine- ment. RL-Based Policy Improvement for Generative Planning. Re- inforcement learning has been combined with diffusion policies for reward-driven optimization. DDPO [1], advantage-weighted regression methods [18, 19], and diffusion policies [26] adapt policy- improvement principles to expressive generative models. However, they generally rely on generic scalar rewards rather than planning- oriented relational semantics. GRID-Planner couples graph-weighted relational returns with advantage-weighted denoising and advantage- gated on-policy self-distillation, allowing relational signals to di- rectly shape policy updates. 3

We presented GRID-Planner, a graph–reward integrated diffusion planner that aligns generative planning with structured relational signals for autonomous driving. By combining Graph-Aware Im- portance Weighting (GAIW), a Relational Reward Surrogate (RRS), and a unified on-policy diffusion objective with actor–critic and self-distillation, our framework improves trajectory prioritiza- tion, rollout evaluation, and policy refinement in a coherent man- ner. Limitations and Future Work. Experiments on nuPlan and interPlan show that GRID-Planner consistently improves closed- loop performance, especially in reactive and interaction-intensive scenarios, while our current design still relies on hand-crafted graph weighting and a limited set of relational reward signals; future work will explore richer relational cues, more adaptive graph-based weighting, and more efficient on-policy optimization for large-scale real-world autonomous driving deployment.

As shown in Tab. 1, Gen4P outperforms prior methods by a clear margin on DR and PS, where PS is a challenging, fully dynamic human–object benchmark on which no method has been trained with comparable data. Meanwhile, our method remains competitive on ADT and PO, especially for the dynamic points. Note that the basic variant ‘Ours’ already surpasses many prior methods, highlighting the effectiveness of motion priors from a pretrained video generator. The depth prior further improves every reported tracking metric over the basic variant. This provides a new strategy for leveraging motion and geometry priors in 4D tasks. Qualitative Results. We visualize the 3D tracking results of our method on in-the-wild DAVIS (Per- azzi et al., 2016), along with 4RC, V-DPM and OpenD4RT in Figs. 4 and 7. As shown in Fig. 4, our method produces more visually plausible motion tracking (e.g., the articulated body and arm motion in Tennis, the body and board motion in Kite-surf) and better preserves delicate structures (e.g., the thin legs in Flamingo, the front wheel in Bmx-bumps).

Reproducing Kernel Hilbert Spaces A reproducing kernel Hilbert space (RKHS) H induced by the kernel function ker : RN × RN →R and a collection of points {x1, x2, . . . , xL} ⊂RN is a set of functions from RN to R; L may be infinite.

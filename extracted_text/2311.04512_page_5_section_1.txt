After obtaining the future feature of agents, we integrate them with the corresponding observation interaction features obtained from Eq.(9) with three types of interactions: (i) Feedback Interaction. We first integrate the observation interaction features of the target agent with the future features of corresponding surrounding agents. According to Eq.(5), this process can be denoted as f b i = I(1)(oi, e f j , ∆pij, θij), ∀j ∈ Gi a2a, where Gi a2a denotes all agents around the agent i within a certain distance threshold, including the target agent itself. (ii) Future Interaction. We subsequently design a future interaction to explore the plausible interaction behaviors be- tween agents, which are vital clues for feedback refinement. Similarly, it can be denoted as f u i = I(2)(f b i , f b j , ∆pij, θij), ∀j ∈Gi a2a. (iii) Feedforward Interaction. We further update the obser- vation interaction features to the future interaction features in a feedforward way, which forms a closed-loop infor- mation propagation between historical and future informa- tion.

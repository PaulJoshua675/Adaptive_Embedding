A Sample Conversation and User Profile Figure 4 shows a judged training turn from iKAT 2024, the sec- ond turn of the conversation “Traveling to Egypt”. The user profile contains 21 statements, of which the annotators mark only state- ment 12 as relevant to the current question. The question “Oh, do I need a visa?” depends on both sources of context. The dialogue history establishes that the visa is for Egypt, and the profile implies, without stating it, that the user is an American citizen, which deter- mines whether a visa is required. The human oracle rewrite makes both pieces of information explicit. PCDR receives the instruction, the full profile, and the conversation as a single string, as described in Section 4.4, and the oracle rewrite is used only as the distillation teacher of Section 4.5.

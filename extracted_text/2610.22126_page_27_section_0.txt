Only significant enrichment bars are shaded. I.3 Recovery of pseudobulk DEGs As an orthogonal readout, we asked how well each attribution ranking recovers the per-cell-type pseudobulk DEGs. Within each cell type, genes were ranked by their absolute attribution difference δg = | ¯R stim g −¯R ctrl g |, where ¯R stim g and ¯R ctrl g denote the attribution of gene g averaged over the stimulated and over the control cells of that cell type. Then, for each cell type, the rankings were scored against that cell type’s DESeq2 [Love et al., 2014] DEG set using precision, recall, F1, normalized recovery AUC, and normalized discounted cumulative gain (NDCG; the DCG divided by that of the ideal ranking) graded by the absolute Wald statistic, at several fixed cutoffs and at a cutoff equal to the number of DEGs. The normalized recovery AUC is the area under the curve of recovered DEGs against rank depth, rescaled so that a random ranking scores 0 and the optimal ranking scores 1. At k = nDEGs, precision, recall, and F1 coincide by construction, so only F1 is reported at that cutoff. We also measured the Spearman correlation between δg and the absolute DESeq2 Wald statistic over the entire gene panel. Every quantity was computed independently for all five seeds and is reported as mean ± s.d. over them. Megakaryocytes contributed no significant DEGs and are therefore absent from the recovery metrics. The whole-panel correlation is stable seed-to-seed. No method tracks the DE statistic closely (Table I.3), so none of these methods can be considered equivalent to a differential expression test. CIG is the least DE-correlated of the three in all seven cell types. Over the full DEG set, scored at a cutoff equal to the number of DEGs (Table I.4), CIG has the lowest F1 in six of the seven cell types and the lowest recovery AUC in six of the seven; the exceptions are dendritic cells, where CIG and integrated gradients are tied on F1, and CD4 T cells, where CIG’s recovery AUC exceeds that of DeepSHAP. CIG’s NDCG is below that of both other methods in every cell type. Apart from dendritic cells and the CIG–DeepSHAP recovery-AUC comparison in CD4 T cells, these gaps are larger than the seed-to-seed spread. The methods separate most sharply at the top of the ranking, and in the same direction in every cell type.

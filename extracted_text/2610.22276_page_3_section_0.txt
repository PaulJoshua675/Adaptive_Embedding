Our contributions are as follows: (1) We propose a novel System 1 paradigm for spatial intelligence, unifying option selection, numerical estimation, and image pointing through direct classification over finite candidate sets. (2) Building on this paradigm, we develop MJev-Spatial and a unified training recipe, using pretrained attention projections for candidate scoring and joint supervision across spatial tasks and refinement stages. (3) Across twelve spatial benchmarks, MJev-Spatial achieves the highest mean score among eight evaluated models and a 5.6× speedup over its autoregressive base model, Qwen3.5-4B.

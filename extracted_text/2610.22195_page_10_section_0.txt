HTN-Orchestrate mean number of executed leaf instances increases from 4.67 to 6.15 per question while the rebuilding rate nearly doubles from 17.5% to 32.0%. Less effective research within indi- vidual leaves shifts more of the burden to the Planner, which compensates by spawning additional subtasks and rebuild- ing more frequently. The full system combines higher accu- racy with fewer leaf executions and less frequent rebuilding, highlighting how local orchestration supports global task resolution. No rebuilding disables task-network reconstruction while preserving result-informed planning within the initial epoch. Accuracy decreases to 89.5%, and because the system can no longer trigger a second epoch, average execution activity also falls, with executed leaf instances dropping from 4.67 to 4.12 per question and worker invocations from 23.16 to 19.01. The full system’s higher accuracy therefore comes at the cost of this additional research, which the rebuilding mechanism makes possible by retaining findings and revis- ing the task network when the initial decomposition cannot resolve the task. Case Studies. Figure 5 shows how intermediate findings shape task decomposition on BrowseComp question Q193. After identifying the supplement and its four authors, HTN- Orchestrate incorporates their names into a newly generated task that checks which author wrote a children’s book in 2015. The upfront decomposition variant instead speci- fies an independent search for children’s-book authors with role-playing-game credits, followed by a separate task to reconcile the two candidate sets. Both runs reach the correct answer, but the result-informed plan narrows the down- stream search to the returned author list and executes four leaves rather than five. Figure 6 illustrates task-network rebuilding on Q501. The initial search does not locate the target music-blog post. HTN-Orchestrate retains candidate albums, recurring week- day features, and rejected posts, then rebuilds the network with an additional feature-based search and an explicit album-to-list matching task. Research continues until the post-search branch locates the target article and verifies its song list. The final answer is Serial Killer by Tobin Sprout, while No rebuilding returns Silent Night.

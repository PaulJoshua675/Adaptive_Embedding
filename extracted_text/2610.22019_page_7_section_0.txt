The clearest gain is on DeepSWE (Huang et al., 2026), the hardest benchmark, whose long-horizon tasks are written from scratch. After full SFT, 280B tokens of midtraining raise it from 11.7 to 15.7, and after short SFT from 6.4 to 12.4. SWE-bench Verified also improves, from 72.9 to 74.3 after full SFT, and BrowseComp by 3 points after both short and full SFT. SWE-bench Multilingual and Pro do not improve. After full SFT they are 1.1 and 2.1 points below the base model, while after short SFT they are level with or slightly above it, so we do not read these differences as a consistent effect. 4.5 HARNESS ROBUSTNESS Open models are used with many agent harnesses, not only the one their SFT data was built for, so harness robustness is a goal of our midtraining and the reason we draw traces from many scaffolds. Table 7 shows that midtraining improves pass@1 on every harness we tested, which makes both models more robust to the choice of harness, although large differences between harnesses remain. On N-3.5 Super after short SFT, the midtrained model is better on every harness, by 8.6 points on average on SWE-bench Verified and 6.8 on Terminal-Bench 2.1. On N-3.5 Lightning-8K after full SFT, midtraining improves SWE-bench Verified on all six harnesses, by 11.6 points on average. Midtraining on the natural data alone already improves over the base model on four of the six harnesses, by up to 4.7 points, and adding the traces gives the largest gains. We did not train on traces from a single scaffold, so these results do not separate the effect of scaffold diversity from that of adding traces. 7

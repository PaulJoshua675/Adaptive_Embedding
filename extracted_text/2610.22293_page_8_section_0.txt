Appendix B.14 gives its exact definition and the grading protocol. 3.4 SST preserves gains across successive fine-tuning stages A stronger test is whether SST lets a model acquire several skills in sequence without each stage erasing the last. We fine-tune Qwen3-8B on two consecutive tasks (A →B), tracking retention on task A and learning on task B. We also evaluate three-stage chains (A →B →C) and track each domain throughout. SST learns more and forgets less. Figure 9 shows multiple A →B sequences across domain pairs. We use R10 refresh in these runs. Across all the tested combinations, SST outperforms SFT in both retention and new-task learning. Appendix B.2 adds SDFT to three of these chains.

Displacement Map. To further analyze motion leakage around foreground boundaries, we compare the background motion of our method and 4RC. For both methods, we mean-normalize the query- frame points, compute their accumulated 3D displacement, and colorize the displacement magnitudes over a shared range of [0, 0.5]. We retain only the background region, where non-zero displacement indicates erroneous motion. As shown in Fig. 6, our method exhibits less background motion leakage and a clearer motion boundary, consistent with the corresponding point-cloud visualization. Ablation on Adaptation.

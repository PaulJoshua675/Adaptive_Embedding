C.5 Output Ordering, Overlap, and Computational Scope Single requery (bridge) is formulated in Section 3.4. Its final output concatenates the three stage lists; it is not globally reranked. Stage-level exclusion uses node identity. Candidate-depth and refill rules must ensure that an implementation actually returns 40 distinct nodes, including when independent searches overlap. For the alternating budget split, the quota and overlap-resolution convention must be recovered from the implementation; taking two top lists alone does not guarantee 40 distinct nodes. The recorded search-call counts are one for plain retrieval, two for budget splitting, one for reranking, three for two-stage fused retrieval, four for single requery, and five for the four- stage chained schedules. They count vector-search operations rather than equal units of work. Reranking evaluates 160 candidates; modified collections require preprocessing and storage.

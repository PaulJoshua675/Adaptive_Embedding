We also compute contrasts on the average candidate log-likelihood ratio to distinguish scale saturation from sign changes. 3.5 Uncertainty and surrogates We use 10,000 percentile bootstrap resamples of the 24 numerical blocks. This holds the six au- thored commentary pairs and model set fixed, and quantifies only variation within this benchmark. Commentary-pair contrasts and leave-one-pair-out ranges provide separate wording checks. Four blocks per pair give limited replication; a six-pair bootstrap would not establish generalization to the universe of financial language. Profitability, annual-change, and gap-size subgroups are exploratory between-block comparisons and can also reflect assigned wording differences. Ordinary least-squares surrogates predict ¯q from momentum and sentiment, then add surprise, then section order and categorical pair/introduction indicators. Fitting leaves one earnings block out at a time; held-block scores never enter the training fit. We also leave one commentary pair out using the three financial inputs only. R2 evaluates reproduction of model scores, not market accuracy.

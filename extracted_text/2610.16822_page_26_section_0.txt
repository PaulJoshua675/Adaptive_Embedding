The MalNet classification results and A800 profiles jointly characterize representation quality and computational cost on larger DAGs. DTA-GT provides the strongest classification results among the evaluated MalNet predictors, while BRP-NAS has lower latency in the large-graph profiles. The staged A800 measurements support the intended roles of retained-mode spectral computa- tion, reusable graph-dependent preprocessing, and topology-aware relation construction within the evaluated range. The reported scaling evidence covers graphs with up to 32,768 nodes; sparse or hierarchical attention and compact reachability representations remain important directions for substantially larger computational graphs. G Extended Discussion Cross-space representation and adaptation. A node in a cell-based CNN space may denote a primitive convolution or pooling operation, whereas a node in another space may denote an entire Transformer layer or Mamba block. These different computational granularities motivate a canonical operation-level representation for cross-space adaptation. Composite blocks could be decomposed into shared semantic units or mapped to a common operation vocabulary with aligned edge definitions and target protocols. For spaces with compatible operation definitions and dependency semantics, future work can investigate reusing DTA-GT’s directional encoder and graph preprocessing, with new vocabulary entries and embeddings for additional operation types. This would enable studies of cross-space pretraining followed by adaptation on a small evaluated subset from the target space. Task adaptation and NAS evaluation. For a new DAG-level task with compatible atomic units and directed-dependency semantics, adapting the prediction head and fine-tuning the encoder on a small labeled subset provides a natural extension. This would allow the same direction-aware representation principle to be studied under different graph-level targets. A complementary direction is to integrate the predictor into a NAS search loop and evaluate how candidate ranking affects architecture selection under a fixed end-to-end evaluation budget. Relation-channel design and extension. The three paired relation families in Section 4.3 are heuristic choices motivated by DAG semantics, providing a concrete instantiation of direction-dual structural attention. They capture local dependencies, multi-hop reachability, and shared predeces- sor/successor structure. Additional directional information, such as hop-specific dependencies or directed motif patterns, can be represented by further ordered or role-specific relation supports, with a corresponding extension of the relation-to-head assignment. The relation set can therefore be tailored to the directional semantics available in a target graph domain. Our experiments evaluate the six-channel instantiation; the predictive value and computational cost of expanded relation sets remain to be studied. Computational scaling. Directed reachability can grow quadratically even when the original adjacency is sparse. For example, a directed chain has n −1 edges but n(n −1)/2 reachable ordered pairs. Dense relation masks therefore require quadratic storage, and dense masked attention incurs quadratic pairwise computation for a fixed hidden dimension. The A800 profiles in Appendix F.2 demonstrate execution on generated DAGs with up to 32,768 nodes. Extending Graph Transformer predictors to substantially larger computational graphs calls for sparse or hierarchical attention together with compact representations of directed reachability. Combining these mechanisms with reusable spectral preprocessing could extend the encoder to larger computational graphs while retaining its direction-aware representation. Future evaluations should measure prediction quality, first-use graph-input construction, model-forward latency, and GPU memory under matched graph and hardware settings.

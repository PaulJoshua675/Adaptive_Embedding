In the actual three-door informed-host case, the prize is uniformly placed and the player initially selects door 1. A host who knows the prize location always opens an unchosen empty door and always offers the other unopened door; legal reveals are chosen uniformly. The input does not disclose the opened door’s number. The question is “Where is the prize relative to the final two unopened doors?” Its outcomes are initial_door and other_unopened_door, with reference probabilities 1/3 and 2/3. The initial selection retains its prior probability, and the host’s guaranteed safe reveal concentrates the other 2/3 on the remaining door. An output concentrated entirely on the remaining door would select the more likely outcome but misrepresent the residual uncertainty. 5. Evaluation Results 5.1.

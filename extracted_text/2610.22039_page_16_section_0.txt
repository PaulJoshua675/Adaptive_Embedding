D.2 RQ3 Full Numerical Results and Token Scaling Full SFT comparison table. Table 11 provides the com- plete numerical results underlying the SFT comparison figure of the main text (RQ3). Token-scaling analysis. We evaluate how each data con- struction strategy scales with training token budget by train- ing Qwen3.5-4B at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of the full budget (∼158 M tokens; §E.1). Table 12 reports the resulting WB-GC scores and Figure 7 plots the corresponding curves. All three strategies scale approximately log-linearly in to- ken budget, gaining a roughly constant amount per doubling (∼1.6 pp for Atomic-PR, ∼1.8 pp for Temporal-Window, ∼2.6 pp for WEAVE), with no strategy saturating within the budgets tested. The curves are separated by slope rather than by offset, which is the signature of a difference in supervi- sion density rather than a fixed head start. WEAVE reaches at 25% budget (12.6%) what Atomic-PR reaches only at 100% (12.4%). Since we do not evaluate below 25%, this estab- lishes a token-efficiency advantage of at least ∼4× at this operating point rather than an exact ratio.

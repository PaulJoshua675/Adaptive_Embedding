G.3 Caching and host placement In the DeepSeek host-memory tests, compressing the table from 202.7 to 50.7 GB saves capacity and speeds up isolated lookups. Lookup accounts for less than 1% of a forward step in our eager implementation without graph capture, so complete step time changes little; the backbone remains on the GPU. Figure 4 comes from a simulation of a static hot-row cache. The cache holds the rows with the highest FineWeb-Edu read counts (Appendix D.1), and we evaluate it on held-out FineWeb-Edu reads. Each missed row costs one 4 KB read. The simu- lation uses the fio constants above, 2.56M IOPS and 91 µs at queue depth 1. These constants set only the throughput limits, and the hit rates do not depend on them. At a cache capacity of 12.9 GB, CP serves 70.7% of held-out reads from the cache, compared with 50.6% for FP8. Compression improves the hit rate by fitting more rows in the same cache capacity, while the 4 KB minimum read unit leaves the cost of an indi- vidual row miss unchanged. A decode step requests many rows, and the tested caches still require off- GPU reads at each step.

CNN-based Methods. Besides the improvements brought in language applications or high level CV tasks, the advancement of Deep Learning (DL) has also transformed the field of image enhance- ment becoming the dominant paradigm [14, 44, 45]. One of the earliest DL models on the scene of LLIE was LLNet [46] which proved that supervised feature learning can improve brightness and contrast while also recovering lost details. Generative models, like EnGAN [15], focused on creat- ing a direct mapping from low-light images to their enhanced counterparts by employing an end- to-end framework. These methods further improved upon traditional hand-crafted heuristic-based algorithms through hierarchical feature extraction. The Retinex theory [40] is furthermore employed as an assumption, leveraging the ability of DL frameworks to learn feature decomposition. While such models, benefit from both physical priors and data-driven learning, they often require multi-stage training pipelines through subnetwork spe- cialisation [42] on tasks such as: decomposition, illumination/reflectance enhancement, and recon- struction. As a result, the computational and training complexity of this kind of models are high. To solve this, one-stage Retinex-inspired Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) have also been pro- posed in order to increase the computational efficiency, but they struggle with noise mitigation and artifacts, resulting in an unsatisfactory enhancement process. Another issue that CNN-based models exhibit is the limited receptive field of convolutional layers, hence reducing their ability to capture long-range spatial dependencies constructing the global con- textual relationship across distant image regions. As light’s interaction with the scene is not linear, hence why histogram-matching models fail in the LLIE domain, this limitation restricts the models’ capacity of capturing diverse scene structures and mapping the relationships between them. ViT-based Methods. To overcome the inherent locality of convolutional operators, ViT-based models [27, 47] have dominated the SOTA models charts for LLIE in the past years. The success achieved in machine translation by the Transformer architecture [48], has also spread to CV and more specifically to image enhancement. The principal particularity that ViTs possess is the self- attention module which manages to capture long-range dependencies which guide the enhancement process towards a complex resolution of the LLIE challenge. 20

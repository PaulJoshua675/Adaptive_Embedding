ISALux [51] achieves the highest overall performance across both datasets, reaching 27.58 dB/0.874 (PSNR/SSIM) on PST900 LL and 27.81 dB/0.873 on CART LL, indicating strong reconstruction fidelity under purely RGB-based enhancement. However, VesperMUSE LLIE demon- strates highly competitive performance, attaining 27.47 dB/0.870 on PST900 LL and 27.74 dB/0.858 on CART LL, while maintaining a substantially lower parameter count of 0.454M compared to 1.85M. This corresponds to approximately a 4× reduction in model size with only marginal degrada- tion in PSNR (≤0.11 dB) and a limited drop in SSIM, particularly on PST900 LL. Compared to Retinexformer [50], VesperMUSE LLIE consistently improves PSNR on both datasets (+0.87 dB on PST900 LL and +0.63 dB on CART LL), while using approximately 3.5× fewer pa- rameters, highlighting a significantly better performance-to-complexity trade-off. Although Retinex- former [50] exhibits slightly higher SSIM on CART LL, but the gap remains small, suggesting that structural preservation is largely maintained by the proposed method. Notably, the strong performance of VesperMUSE LLIE can be attributed to its thermal-aware design, which introduces complementary modality information that is particularly beneficial under challeng- ing low-light conditions where RGB signals alone are not always stable and informative. This is especially evident on PST900 LL, where the gap to the top-performing method is minimal despite architectural differences and reduced model capacity. In contrast, LYT-Net [47], while extremely lightweight (0.045M parameters), suffers from a significant drop in both PSNR and SSIM, indicat- ing that aggressive parameter reduction without multi-modal guidance limits restoration quality. 4.2.2 Qualitative Analysis The qualitative results further demonstrate the strong enhancement capability of VesperMUSE LLIE, particularly in approximating the RGB reference from severely degraded inputs. The proposed method produces outputs with improved local contrast and more accurate colour saturation com- pared to Retinexformer [50] and LYT-Net [47], which tend to generate flatter or slightly desaturated images. Notably, chromatic fidelity is well preserved, as observed in the PST900 LL samples where objects such as the yellow helmet and red fire extinguisher are reconstructed with more natural and vivid colours.

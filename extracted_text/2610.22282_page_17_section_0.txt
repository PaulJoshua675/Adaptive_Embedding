The routing proposal and objective are identical for both generators. Only the routing hyperparameters, the rollout step, and the optimization settings differ. Stable Diffusion 3.5. The reference configuration routes levels 2, 3, and 4 of the 10-step training sampler with level weights wt of 1, 0.76, and 0.5 and temperature T = 1. Each 128-token query block keeps its own block and one candidate, or attends densely (κ = 1/32, Ku = 1). One draw per query block serves all 24 joint-attention layers and the 13 image-only self-attention layers, and 16 of the 24 transformer blocks act as anchor layers at each routed level. Rollouts use the centered routed step. At a routed level, the rollout draws a second, unrecorded route and steps with vθ(xt, t, c) + (˜vt,(1) −˜vt,(2))/2, where ˜vt,(1) and ˜vt,(2) are the velocities under the two routes. The perturbation then has zero mean, so the rollout stays close to the dense path, and the likelihood of Equation 4 applies unchanged to the recovered velocity. Each wave contains 64 prompt groups at G = 2, split into two optimizer minibatches of 32 groups. We train rank-32 LoRA adapters with α = 64 on the eight attention projections, with learning rate 1.4 × 10−4, weight decay 10−4, and no warmup. Advantage processing uses 5% winsorization and batch-IQR scaling with floor 0.03, and no pair is gated. Training runs for 50 steps on one node with eight H100 GPUs, with evaluation every 5 steps. Wan2.2. The reference configuration trains on 13-frame videos. It routes levels 2 and 4 of the 10-step training sampler with level weights of 1 and 0.5 and temperature T = 1. Each 128-token query block keeps its own block and one of its 27 candidates, or attends densely (κ = 0.03, Ku = 1). One draw per query block serves every self-attention layer and head, and 20 of the 30 transformer blocks act as anchor layers at each routed level. Rollouts use classifier-free guidance with scale 5. Both guidance branches apply the same route, and Equation 4 scores the guided velocity. Rollouts step with the routed velocity itself, without centering. We train all transformer parameters except the output normalization, the output projection, and the root modulation table, with learning rate 10−5, 8 warmup updates, and weight decay 10−4. Each wave contains 64 prompt groups at G = 2 and is divided into two disjoint optimizer minibatches of 32 groups. Advantage processing uses 5% winsorization and batch-IQR scaling with floor 0.02, and no pair is gated. Training runs for 50 steps on two nodes with eight H100 GPUs each. Optimizer safeguards. Three generic safeguards stabilize optimization. They are not components of RPO. First, the second minibatch uses standard PPO ratio clipping with width 3 × 10−4 for both generators. Let Sold i be the velocity likelihood under the parameters that collected the rollout. We use the ratio ρi(θ) = exp(λ(Si(θ) −Sold i )/Ni), where the constant λ is 0.005 on Stable Diffusion 3.5 and 0.01 on Wan2.2. Because AdamW is nearly invariant to the loss scale, λ mainly sets the log-ratio relative to the clip width.

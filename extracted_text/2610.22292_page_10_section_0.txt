Preprint AI USE STATEMENT Generative AI systems are used in this work. We fine-tuned and evaluated LLMs, used them to generate our training data, ran them as agents in the Among Us environment and as investigator agents in AuditBench, and used them as automated judges under fixed prompts and rubrics for MASK, AlpacaEval, and MT-Bench. Generative AI tools also helped with carrying out the research, including code development and debugging, plotting, and checking results. We also used them to revise and improve the readability of parts of the manuscript. The authors reviewed and revised all AI-assisted writing and reviewed and tested AI-assisted code. Numbers in the paper were checked against the underlying result files. The authors take full respon- sibility for the content of this work, including all text, figures and code produced with the help of generative AI. REPRODUCIBILITY STATEMENT Section 3 describes the training objective and targets. Section 4 describes the two open-source models, training and utility data, training configuration, checkpoint selection, evaluation procedures, and the judge models. Appendix A.3 lists all hyperparameters for both models. The appendices on MASK, Among Us, and utility preservation report the exact values behind each figure. We will release our code, training targets, utility corpora, and trained adapters upon publication.

Overall Denoising Pipeline At each denoising step, an input projection combines noisy token embeddings 𝑧𝛾with an optional self-conditioning estimate 𝑥SC. A bidirectional Transformer processes the sequence through attention and feed-forward sublayers modulated by the noise level 𝛾. An untied vocabulary head produces contextual logits, augmented by a gated likelihood shortcut that measures compatibility between the noisy state and the codebook. Softmax yields token probabilities, whose weighted embedding mean provides the clean-state estimate used for both sampler updates and subsequent self-conditioning. Figure 3(a) illustrates this pipeline with one Transformer block expanded. Relative to the baseline, LangDiT introduces backbone RMSNorm for feature-scale control, query– key RMSNorm for attention-logit scale control, and wider noise conditioning for greater modulation capacity. The likelihood shortcut follows a Gaussian noise model (Gulrajani and Hashimoto, 2023; Yang et al., 2026; Chen et al., 2026). We detach its embedding matrix to block the direct codebook- gradient path, while a bounded gate controls its forward contribution. An entropy-based gate further regulates bootstrap self-conditioning during training (Figure 3(b)); inference retains the un-gated posterior-mean estimator for both self-conditioning and sampler updates.

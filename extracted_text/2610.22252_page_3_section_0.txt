The noise satisfies E[ηt | Xt, At] = 0, |ηt| ≤β almost surely, for a known constant β > 0. For any sampled context x, the learner can query each target policy and compute the feature vectors of the sampled actions without executing those actions or observing their rewards. A reward is observed only when an action is executed. We distinguish between context samples, policy queries, and reward observations when accounting for sample complexity. The context and action spaces may be infinite; our goal is to obtain guarantees expressed through the feature dimension d, without dependence on the cardinalities of these spaces |X × A|. The probability is over the randomness in data collection and the algorithm. Before presenting our algorithm and its sample complexity guarantees, we state the following structural assumptions. Assumption 1 (ℓ2-bounded features and parameter). There exist known constants L, S > 0 such that ∥ϕ(x, a)∥2 ≤L, ∀(x, a) ∈X × A, ∥θ⋆∥2 ≤S. We use these bounds to control feature estimation errors and the regularization bias in value estimation. Knowledge of L and S is a standard assumption in classical linear bandit algorithms, such as OFUL [1]. Developing an algorithm that does not require these bounds is beyond the scope of this work. For linear bandit algorithms that do not require prior knowledge of an upper bound on ∥θ⋆∥2, see Gales et al. [15]. Assumption 2 (Coverage).

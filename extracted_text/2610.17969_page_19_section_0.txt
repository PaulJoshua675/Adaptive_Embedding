In Eq. 4, each factor 1/Si converts the corresponding loss to a dimensionless value. The converter can be derived from characteristic scales of the data and operators. For PDE residuals, Si must account for the operator’s output unit, including the units introduced by differentiation. For example, a squared first-order-in-time residual loss uses the characteristic loss scale Si := (su/t∗)2. Thm. 2 characterizes all loss weights that preserve this invariance within the class of linear objectives whose weights depend only on the characteristic scales. Every such weight must contain the factor 1/Si, with any remaining scale dependence restricted to dimensionless combinations of scales called Buckingham–π groups. When the loss units vary independently, Cor. 1 fixes the preferences to constants and gives wi(S) = αi/Si.

Spatial benchmarks evaluate relations, transformations, and actions through different prediction targets. SPARTQA (Mirzaee et al., 2021) and SPARTUN/ReSQ (Mirzaee and Kordjamshidi, 2022) ask questions about relations expressed in scene descriptions. StepGame (Shi et al., 2022) tests relational composition, while SpaRC/SpaRP (Rizvi et al., 2024) formalizes spatial properties and reasoning paths. Other evaluations consider constrained path construction (Kaesberg et al., 2025), spatial reasoning across textual and visual inputs (Wang et al., 2024), and action selection in symbolic or simulated environments (Gao et al., 2026b,a). 2

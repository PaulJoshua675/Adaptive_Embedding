A Finetuning Details We employ the WAI format [14] for dataset pre-processing and fine-tuning. Specifically, we determine the co-visibility between all image pairs within a scene by evaluating the re-projection error derived from ground-truth depth maps and camera poses. During fine-tuning, input sequences are curated by sampling images that maintain a co-visibility of at least 25% relative to at least one other view in the sequence; this ensures consistent spatial overlap across the chosen views. For each input sequence, we compute local and global point maps, ray directions and distances, camera translations, and rotations (represented as quaternions), alongside a per-view confidence map. All loss terms are calculated using the robust regression loss [29] with a shape parameter of 0.5 and a scaling parameter of 0.25 to mitigate the influence of outliers. We apply a logarithmic transformation to the ground truth and model outputs for point maps and z-depth values prior to com- puting the regression loss on their differences; notably, no loss terms are calculated for ray distances or confidence maps. When high-quality, smooth ground-truth point maps are available, we also integrate normal loss [43] and gradient matching loss [44] into our objective function. To account for noise and extreme values inherent in “in-the-wild” real-world datasets, we apply confidence weights to the global point map losses and exclude the top 5% of errors as outliers for local point maps and VGGT depth losses. To align with the reference-view-free architecture of π3, we compute pair- wise relative pose losses rather than direct differences between predicted and ground-truth global poses. The same 5% outlier exclusion logic is applied to all three losses for π3. The loss terms for the different configurations and dataset types are expressed as follows: Lsyn VGGT = Lpt + Lz + Lray + Lpose + 3Lnorm + 3Lgrad (4) Lreal VGGT = Wconf · Lpt,glob + Mtop5% · (Lpt,loc + Lz) + Lray + Lpose (5) Lsyn π3 = Lpt + Lz + Lray + Lrel + 3Lnorm + 3Lgrad (6) Lreal π3 = Mtop5% · (Lpt,glob + Lpt,loc + Lz) + Lray + Lrel (7) where: • Lpt, Lz, and Lray represent the robust regression losses for point maps, z-depth values, and ray directions, respectively. 23

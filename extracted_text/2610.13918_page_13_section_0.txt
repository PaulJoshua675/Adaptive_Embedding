(A) Objective PSG hypnograms for healthy control and paradoxical insomnia, illustrating identical macroscale architecture and total sleep duration (21.15 h). (B) Cortical beta band (15.0–30.0 Hz) relative power; deficient cortical GABAergic interneuron inhibition (γinh = 0.25) permits patho- logical high-frequency beta power intrusion throughout stage N3 slow-wave sleep. (C) Subjective wakefulness meter, showing that persistent cortical hyperarousal induces a perceived wakefulness state of 85%–100% despite objective electrophysiological sleep. 7 Discussion and In Silico Pharmacology The multi-scale architecture of DyssomniaNN provides a versatile computational platform for in silico pharmacological investigation (i.e., investigations performed via computer simulation, in contrast to in vivo biological testing and in vitro laboratory preparations): • Dual Orexin Receptor Antagonists (DORAs): Compounds such as suvorexant and lem- borexant promote sleep by blocking OX1 and OX2 receptors [34]. In DyssomniaNN, this mechanism corresponds to attenuating Ωtone and reducing Iarousal. In the chronic insomnia cohort, simulated DORA administration dampens the hyperarousal bias, reducing WASO and facilitating normal flip-flop transitions. • Orexin Receptor Agonists: Emerging therapeutics for narcolepsy (such as danavorexton) restore hypocretinergic signaling [11, 34]. In DyssomniaNN, elevating Ωtone from 0.05 toward 0.80 restores flip-flop bistability, suppressing daytime SOREMPs and consolidating nocturnal sleep. • Positive Allosteric GABA-A Modulators: In paradoxical insomnia, pharmacologically augmenting the cortical inhibition scale γinh suppresses pathological high-frequency beta intrusions without altering subcortical macro-architecture, directly restoring perceived restedness [28, 20]. 13

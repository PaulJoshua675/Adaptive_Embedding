We presented SoftCache, a training-free method that turns hard caching into soft caching. A dynamic cache already measures how stale its cached computation has become, but spends the measurement on a binary choice between refresh and reuse. SoftCache reads the headroom below the threshold as how far the cache can still be trusted and lets it set the step size: cached steps jump past scheduled nodes while the cache is reliable and shrink back to the scheduled step as a refresh nears, the tail is re-spaced so that every node passed is dropped from the trajectory, and the cached residual is ex- trapolated to each off-grid landing at no extra network cost. On FLUX, HunyuanVideo and Wan2.1, at wall-clock speedups from 3.5× to 6.9×, SoftCache stays closest to the uncached sample on all four fidelity metrics, with no per-model calibration. Adaptivity in caching, then, does not stop at deciding when to refresh. 9

B.3 Cand-CF: View Selection, Masks, and 3D Realization View selection considers target visibility, occlusion, surrounding structure, and backend requirements. The selected conditioning view exposes the intended change. The planner records the target, non- target preservation constraints, mask mode, and generation route. The benchmark retains its fixed evaluation views. Image editing uses a local mask, a full-object mask, or an unmasked instruction. FLUX.2 [klein] 9B (Black Forest Labs, 2026) produces an initial edit. Review checks the target change, object integrity, boundaries, and preservation. Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 (Wu et al., 2025a) applies revisions from this feedback. After the image edit is accepted, source and edited images are registered. Image differences, connected regions, and boundary checks refine a posterior change mask. B.4 Human Review During model-assisted construction, two domain experts manually spot-check 10% of the outputs at each review checkpoint shown in Figure 4. These checkpoints include the two Spec-CF reviewer checkpoints, Spec-CF pair review, and Cand-CF consistency review. After construction, six additional domain experts, non-overlapping with the construction-stage reviewers, independently verify the benchmark candidates. They check label correctness, evidence sufficiency, perturbation validity, mesh and rendering quality, view coverage, and, for Motion Understanding, temporal consistency. Only samples that pass this expert review are retained in the benchmark. Pair and triplet inclusion additionally requires the fixed-evidence or fixed-claim conditions in Appendix C. B.5 Supervision and Expansion The released Training Resource retains task-specific evidence, a claim, a binary label, a contradiction explanation, and source/anchor/pair-triplet linkage metadata. It contains 5,157 Spec-CF pairs, 3,309 Cand-CF pairs, and 2,807 complete triplets. An anchor may have only one branch or no negative.

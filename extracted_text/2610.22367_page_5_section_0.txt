Two auxiliary integers fv and gv track the input’s contribution in a scaled form: after t steps, they satisfy fvw ≡2tf (mod p) and gvw ≡2tg (mod p). Thus, at termination, ffv gives the desired inverse up to the scaling factor 2t. The scaling keeps the coefficient updates free of division.

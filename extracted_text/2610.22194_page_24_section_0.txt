Limitations All results are obtained on one base model at one scale (Qwen3-1.7B-Base), so the effects we report may not hold at other scales, and a capability this model cannot learn from more data may be learnable by a larger one. Each comparison runs one search per configuration, and although repeated evaluation bounds the noise of a single checkpoint, it does not bound the variation between independent searches. Synthesis raises the aggregate score through a small number of learnable capabilities that the pool undersupplies, and it does not move capabilities the model cannot yet follow. The generalization study covers a single new domain with short runs, so it demonstrates that the loop can transfer but does not establish how broadly. 24

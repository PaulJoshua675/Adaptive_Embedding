Retrieval settings are provided in Appendix B. 3.2 Reasoning Context Reconstruction Reasoning Context Reconstruction organizes complementary passages with their cross-evidence inferences into a reasoning context. After retrieval, an LLM-based passage selector performs a fresh analysis of the final candidate pool AT = (d1, . . . , dn). For question q, it identifies the infor- mation requirements and examines how candidate passages jointly satisfy them. The selector input is xs = (q, AT , K), where K ≤n specifies the num- ber of passages to retain. It returns a cross-evidence reasoning analysis z and selected indices J (z, J) = Sθ(xs). (5) The analysis z establishes entity correspondences and relational dependencies across candidate passages. These connections determine how one passage complements another in supporting the required inference. Joint selection uses these complementary roles to choose K passages that together address the question’s information requirements. The selector generates z and then J within one response, conditioning selection on z pθ(z, J | xs) = pθ(z | xs) pθ(J | z, xs). (6) For the selected indices J = (i1, . . . , iK), the passage sequence is S = (di1, . . . , diK). (7) The sequence S preserves acquisition order, and z draws on the full candidate pool including passages outside S. It accompanies the selected text so that the relations established during context reconstruction remain available for answer derivation. See Appendix B for details. 3.3 Cross-Evidence Compositional Reasoning Cross-Evidence Compositional Reasoning applies reconstructed relations to derive an answer from the selected passages. The generator receives the reasoning context Xq = (S, z). (8) The selected passages S provide factual premises, and z supplies the cross-evidence inferences used to connect them. The generator conditions its answer on both pθ(z, J, a|xs) = pθ(z, J |xs) pθ(a|q, S, z). (9) Here S is determined by AT and J. The answer generator Gθ uses z to determine how the premises in S can be combined. Entity correspondences identify which facts concern the same entity, allowing a relation established in one passage to supply a premise for another. The generator checks these correspondences against the selected text and composes the resulting relations to answer q. During composition, each property remains associated with the entity described in its source passage, and the direction of each relation is preserved. The resulting prediction is ˆa = Gθ(q, S, z). (10) The following prompt specifies how the generator applies these inferences. Cross-Evidence Compositional Reasoning Prompt Determine the target relation from the question. Use the cross-evidence analysis to connect facts across the selected passages. Check entity correspondences and relation direction against the passages before deriving the answer.

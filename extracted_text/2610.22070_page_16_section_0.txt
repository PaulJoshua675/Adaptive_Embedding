A Mathematical Interpretation MaLoW carries information from earlier video clips through history-dependent parameter updates. This appendix provides a mathematical interpretation of the feasibility of encoding the effect of historical context into low-rank updates of the MLP projections within a Transformer block. Specifically, we trace how a query reads historical information in section A.1, how this read induces a transformation of the features entering the MLP in section A.2, and how such a transformation can be realized or approximated by LoRA-style weight updates in section A.3. The analysis does not claim that MaLoW explicitly reconstructs the analytical history matrix M _ H or is equivalent to full-context attention. Rather, it establishes that historical information can, under the assumptions described below, be represented in parameter space through low-rank updates. MaLoW can be viewed as one of the learned instantiations of this principle, with our empirical results demonstrating that its recurrent state and generated adapters effectively preserve history-dependent information in practice. The exact identities below first assume fixed historical keys and values and an unnormalized dot-product read, while Section A.4 and Section A.5 extend the interpretation to the online setting, normalized attention, and the normalization operations in the backbone.

B Representation and Implementation Details B.1 Distribution models and table construction The implementation provides a set of default codebook distributions, one for each tensor role. Tables are cached by distribution family and parameters, independently of stream geometry. Model probabilities come from integrating the magnitude density over exponent intervals: [2q, 2q+1) for an interior exponent index q = e −127, with the endpoint bins extending to zero and infinity. These probabilities guide code assignment rather than certify a match to the tensor’s empirical statistics. At compression time, up to 4096 stratified, jittered samples estimate a tensor-specific center c from the mean of ei −127, rounded and clamped to [−128, 127]; the center is stored with the tensor and reused during decoding.

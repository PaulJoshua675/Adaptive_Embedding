Unless stated otherwise, baselines are evaluated by us using their released setups. FeaTok outperforms discrete models. In Tab. 1 we compare FeaTok using different vision encoders and two model sizes to existing discrete image generative models in terms of gFDr6 and its per-feature metrics. All four FeaTok-L variants outperform the strongest prior discrete baseline in gFDr6, showing consistent gains across four pretrained visual feature encoders satisfying Def. 1. FeaTok-DINOv3 is the strongest discrete model at both generator scales, with and without guidance. The largest improvements appear without guidance, where we reduce the prior baseline gFDr6 of 4.38 from VFMTok2-XXL (Zheng et al., 2026a) to 3.13. With guidance, we reduce the 4.11 gFDr6 of BAR-L to 3.08. Increasing the generator from base to large consistently improves gFDr6; for DINOv3, it decreases from 3.58 to 3.13 without guidance and from 3.39 to 3.08 with guidance. Importantly, these generation gains do not coincide with improved reconstruction: rFID remains nearly constant across FeaTok tokenizers (0.92–1.01; App. C.3), reinforcing that reconstruction fidelity alone does not explain generative performance. Figs. 8–11 in App. H show qualitative samples. FeaTok is competitive with continuous models at lower sampling cost. Tab. 2 compares against continuous baselines at their standard sampling settings. Without guidance, FeaTok-L-DINOv3 achieves gFDr6 3.13, outperforming RAEv2 (3.95) while using 1.27 rather than 47.72 TFLOPs per image. With guidance, our 3.08 is competitive with RAE-XL (3.26) (Zheng et al., 2026b) and REPA-E (3.04) (Leng et al., 2025), although RAEv2 retains the best gFDr6 (2.17). To compare at similar sampling costs, Tab. 3 evaluates RAEv2 with fewer sampling steps, tuning its internal guidance at each step count for the best gFDr6 (App. E). With guidance, FeaTok achieves gFDr6 3.08 at 2.31 TFLOPs per image, compared with 3.43 at 3.07 TFLOPs for six-step RAEv2. RAEv2 is not trained to perform few-step sampling. We therefore also compare with Drift (Deng et al., 2026), iMF (Geng et al., 2026), and pMF (Lu et al., 2026b), which are explicitly trained for one-step generation. Without guidance, their gFDr6 values range from 10.51 to 20.34, and even the best guided value is 6.87, whereas unguided FeaTok achieves 3.13.

Existing architecture predictors mainly follow three representation paradigms: sequence-based encodings, GNN-based graph modeling, and Transformer/hybrid architectures. These paradigms differ in how they encode topology and direction, and we focus on whether they preserve directed computational semantics beyond local structural cues. Sequence-based Methods. Peephole [17], NAO [18], and PNAS [8] encode architectures as flattened vectors or operation sequences. This paradigm prioritizes compact architecture descriptions and efficient predictor training under limited evaluation budgets. However, because the DAG is linearized, these methods do not explicitly model topology or preserve edge-direction semantics. As a result, directed computational dependencies among operations are difficult to capture. GNN-based Methods. BRP-NAS [13], Neural Predictor [19], NPENAS [22], GATES [20], Semi- NAS [28], DNNPerf [29], and NNLP [21] represent architectures as DAGs or graph-structured objects. Compared with sequence encodings, GNNs explicitly use graph topology and can exploit edge directions within local neighborhoods. However, in message-passing backbones, higher-order topology and long-range dependencies are mainly captured through repeated local propagation, where information can be mixed, over-smoothed, or over-squashed. Meanwhile, directional bias remains mostly local, and an explicit global direction-aware prior is usually absent. Transformer-based and Hybrid Methods. Transformer-based and hybrid predictors, including TNASP [24], NAR-Former [14], PINAT [25], FlowerFormer [23], NAR-Former V2 [15], and NN- Former [16], use self-attention to model global interactions among architecture tokens. To compensate for the lack of explicit graph structure in vanilla self-attention, recent designs inject topology- aware signals through operation/topology tokenization, graph-aided attention, relation masks, or bidirectional aggregation. These designs improve topology-aware representation learning, but their directional cues remain mostly local or stage-specific, without jointly coordinating a global direction- aware spectral prior, relation-aware interaction, and direction-specific updates. 3

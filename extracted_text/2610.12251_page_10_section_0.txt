Induction over the generations, keeping for each bank and tape the number of generations since it was active, gives the state after every generation bank by bank and tape by tape. In the window, the cells no record writes keep their values, and every other cell from 4 on ends each generation as a function of the decoder’s state at the pass that hands over. That state is the same in every generation, since after the pass of start the decoder sees the same tapes. So for g ≥K the state after generation g + K equals the state after generation g, and Theorem 3.2 carries the statement to the network. The description of ρ has 3150 bits, against 3718 for the image: a gate is named by four bits, and the organism writes its eight bits itself. 5 A universal computer: the SUBLEQ host The organism builds and copies stored networks and computes nothing else. The next three sections put a universal computer in its place: a ternary network that executes SUBLEQ, a computer with a single instruction, with memory-mapped tape and output devices. This section builds the network, one member HA for each word width A; Section 6 programs it, and Section 7 stores it in the ring machine, where it reproduces as the organism does. 5.1 The machine and its devices A network reads and writes bytes through a tape transducer: a device outside the network that acts on designated coordinates of the state between steps. Definition 5.1 (Tape transducer). A tape transducer consists of an LTTM F on {0, 1}n and disjoint designated coordinate sets: an input register Rin and an output register Rout of the same width A ≥8, and single bits crd (read request), crw (rewind request), cwr (write request), ceot (end-of-tape status) and chalt. An environment state is a triple (τ, h, ω) with tape τ ∈Σ∗, head position h ∈{0, . . . , |τ|} and output word ω ∈Σ∗. One step maps (x, τ, h, ω) to (x′, τ, h′, ω′) as follows. Let y = F(x) and initialize x′ = y. (i) If ycwr = 1, then ω′ is ω followed by the low eight bits of yRout, and x′ Rout = 0; otherwise ω′ = ω. (ii) If ycrd = 1 and h < |τ|, then x′ Rin holds τh in its low eight bits and copies of its sign bit above them, x′ ceot = 0, and h′ = h+1. If ycrd = 1 and h = |τ|, then x′ ceot = 1 and h′ = h.

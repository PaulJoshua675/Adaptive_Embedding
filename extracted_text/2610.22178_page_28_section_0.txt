It also compares finetuning on RoboCasa365 with and without mirroring. All variants use a memory window of K=3 and the same number of finetuning steps. Mirroring. On RoboCasa365, mirroring (Appendix C.2) adds 4.9 points on average, mostly on the atomic tasks (+6.2) and the seen composite tasks (+7.9). On LIBERO-Plus, mirroring improves four of the seven perturbation dimensions, by 0.2 to 1.7 points. However, it lowers the camera dimension by 6.5 points, so the overall score drops by 0.6. A possible explanation is that the policy infers whether the world is mirrored from the images as well as from the prompt. A large change of the camera viewpoint may then confuse the policy about whether the world is mirrored. Visual augmentation. Disabling camera dropout or the image augmentations (colour jittering and random cropping) causes a large drop on the camera dimension of LIBERO-Plus, by 21.5 and 23.6 points. This suggests that these augmentations make the policy robust to changes of the camera viewpoint. They also give a small gain on the sensor noise and object layout dimensions, of 1.6 to 4.6 points. For camera dropout, a likely reason is the wrist camera: the policy is trained to act from the wrist image alone, and the camera and sensor-noise perturbations of LIBERO-Plus change only the image of the external camera (Fei et al., 2025). 28

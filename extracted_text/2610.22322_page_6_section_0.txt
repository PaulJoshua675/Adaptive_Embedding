Figure 6 plots the participation proportions of the Logger, Miner, and Smith against decision step. Before RL, the Logger dominates throughout the episode, while the Miner and Smith participate infrequently. As training progresses, this pattern changes: the Logger remains predominant at early steps, but its share decreases later as the Miner and Smith become more involved. This shift becomes more pronounced after RL, with the Miner approaching the Logger’s participation level toward the end of the episode. At decision step 120, the Logger’s participation share decreases from approxi- mately 80% before RL to 45% after RL, while the Miner’s share increases from approximately 10% to 40%. The Smith’s share also increases from approximately 10% to 15%.

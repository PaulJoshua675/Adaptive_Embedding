Time Limits Reduce Per-Round Training Data. The open-ended run is allotted ten hours, and Figure 5 reports the amount of data presented to the trainer in each round. The recipe ex- pands over the first four rounds, reaching 264k rows, and then declines in every subsequent round, ending at 69k rows, which is 26% of the peak. The agent continues to submit rounds throughout, so the run does not terminate early. Rather, it progressively reduces the cost of each remaining round. Once a checkpoint is suffi- ciently good to retain, additional training data yields no further improvement under the objec- tive, whereas the time it would cost can be used for another attempt. Under benchmark targets, this contraction does not occur: recipe size is around max token budget, and the agent never refers to the remaining time budget. The context fills and is discarded ten times. The agent runs data decisions, job submission, checkpoint conversion, and evaluation in one session, and Figure 6 shows that session filling to 94.7% of a 258,400-token window and being compacted ten times, first during round 2 and then every three to four rounds. Research history is therefore discarded and rebuilt ten times while the search is still running. The context is not dominated by the obvious suspect.

F Design-Choice Ablations Two components of the pipeline could in principle drive the results: the rank-fusion constant used to combine retrieval lists, and the agreement weight wℓ(x) used when estimating the steering direction. Neither does. Table 9 reports steering nDCG on Belebele for four models spanning both encoders and decoders, under three RRF constants and with the weight removed. The fusion constant and the agreement weight are therefore inert at the scale of our effects: the corrections reported in the main paper reflect the interventions themselves, not tuning of the fusion or weighting machinery. The results confirm that steering’s ad- vantage is de-crowding rather than a uniform win on retrieval quality (Table 10). The reduction in own-language share is large and highly significant (∆= −9.7, p < 10−19) and holds on all three collections individually. The recall difference fa- vors steering on average (+2.7) but is not signifi- cant (p = 0.28), reflecting high per-language vari- ance. nDCG is significant in opposite directions by collection: mean removal is higher on Belebele (p = 0.004) and steering on XQuAD (p = 0.007). These tests substantiate the trade-off framing: steer- ing reliably de-crowds the ranking, while recall and nDCG depend on the model and collection.

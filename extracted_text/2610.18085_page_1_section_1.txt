We show that two Transformers represent- ing the same function can follow different training trajectories. For example, doubling a query matrix and halving its key matrix preserves the model’s function but changes the gradient scales seen by the optimizer. Our theoretical analysis establishes that standard block-local first-order optimizers fail to scale updates with parameters under a shared learn- ing rate. For AdamW, transforming the first- and second-moment estimates alone does not correct the mismatch between parameter and update scales. To make runs restarted from equivalent checkpoints follow the same tra- jectory, we balance matrix pairs and trans- form their moment estimates together, bring- ing distinct but equivalent training states to the same starting point. To correct scale imbalance between paired Transformer ma- trices without changing predictions, we pro- pose Symmetry-Aware Gradient Geometry Stabilization (SAGGS), which periodically equalizes the Frobenius norms of exact sym- metry pairs, and its damped variant, Soft SAGGS. SAGGS reduces perplexity (PPL) by 0.374 ± 0.026 at 101M parameters on WikiText-103, and Soft SAGGS reduces PPL by 0.780±0.040 in a 31M modern-architecture replication, both under scale imbalance. In a 123.8M checkpoint-resume experiment, recip- rocally rescaling matrix pairs barely changes validation loss at restart but increases final PPL by 3.78±0.42; hard and soft rebalancing recover to within 0.02 PPL of the control. ∗Corresponding author 1

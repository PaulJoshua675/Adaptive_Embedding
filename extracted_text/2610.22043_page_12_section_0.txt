Eighth, the numerical gap is 2% or 15% of absolute actual EPS, rather than of consensus; cents rounding can alter its exact fraction. Finally, we use a direct-answer protocol without eliciting or intervening on chain-of- thought reasoning. The selected conversion checks show substantial output differences, so a full- precision replication is needed before treating these as precision-invariant properties of the named base models. The evidence can support a statement about behavior on this benchmark, including patterns that vary by checkpoint and encoding. It cannot determine whether an LLM is “really reason- ing” internally, or establish a market forecasting advantage.

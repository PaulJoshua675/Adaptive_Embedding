In later rounds, fewer difficulty modes remain unresolved, and improvements become smaller. Removing memory produces less consistent progress, including two temporary drops in success. LIBERO-Pro. Training on generated variants of the LIBERO-Pro source tasks substantially improves robust- ness to position and instruction perturbations (Table 2). After five rounds, CoDA achieves overall success rates of 82% and 72% under the two perturbations, respectively. Extending training to fifteen rounds raises these rates to 84% and 76%, compared with 25% and 1% for the initial π0.5 checkpoint. The fifteen-round model also ex- ceeds ASPIRE [18] by 7 and 9 percentage points overall, achieving the highest success on the Goal and Spatial suites under both perturbations, while ASPIRE remains strongest on the Object suite. These robustness gains pre- serve performance on standard LIBERO, where success reaches 97.4%, compared with 97.1% before adaptation. Sensitivity to the failure diagnosis critic. Table 3 varies the vision–language model used to diagnose failures while keeping the engine and compiler fixed. Replacing GPT-5.5 with Claude Opus 5, InternVL3-78B, or Qwen3- VL-235B slightly reduces OOD success from 27.6% to 26.1%, 25.7%, and 24.9%, respectively. Models with lower agreement with human annotators also produce more diagnoses rejected by the control tests. Neverthe- less, all four models outperform generation guided by task success rates: even Qwen3-VL-235B exceeds that baseline by 6.8 percentage points. The framework there- fore remains effective across the tested diagnosis models. Effect of Text2potential. Text2potential achieves 27.6% OOD success at a fixed training budget, compared with 22.3% using sparse task rewards alone (Table 4). It reaches 25% OOD success after 21k policy updates, compared with 31k for sparse rewards, requiring approx- imately 32% fewer updates. The ablations show that both failure diagnosis and potential refinement matter. Generating the potential from the task description in- stead of the failure diagnosis lowers success to 24.6%, while disabling refinement lowers it to 26.1%. Keeping the policy frozen and using the value critic only to select action chunks achieves 21.4%, showing the benefit of using the learned values to update the policy. Qualitative results.

I.2 Fine-Tuning from Equivalent Checkpoints We next branch from the released GPT-2 774M checkpoint with fresh AdamW moments (Figure 6). An exact reciprocal edit WQ 7→WQ/8, WK 7→8WK preserves the current function but leads to 7.3% held-out top-1 disagreement with the canonicalized trajectory after 200 updates. Canonicalizing either starting representative gives identical subsequent predictions and token negative log-likelihoods (NLLs) at every evaluation. Soft SAGGS leaves 1.1% disagreement. Continuing from the released coordinates gives 0.65% disagreement with the same canonicalized reference. This comparison illustrates Corollary 4 with reset moments and distinguishes exact restart equivalence from the approximate recovery provided by Soft SAGGS. The reciprocal edit is imposed for this controlled comparison; trajectory differences do not establish a long-run loss advantage.

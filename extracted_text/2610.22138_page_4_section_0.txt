We ask whether agents that privately know the truth actually de- fend it. Among turns labeled TRUE in the private probe, we measure the fraction that explicitly RE- JECT the false claim. We also track two failure modes: silent non-correction (IGNORE/DRIFT) and social accommodation (HEDGE). Public/private categories. Combining the two channels yields the interpretable categories in Ta- ble 1, which distinguish private belief collapse (an agent privately adopts the false claim) from public correction failure (an agent that privately knows the truth does not publicly defend it).

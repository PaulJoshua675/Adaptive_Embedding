Fresh Adam and calibrated SGD reveal smaller, delayed route differences, and sustained common opposition brings the selected allocation endpoints close by 500 updates. Formation history therefore affects later learning through more than one time-dependent route: inherited optimizer state strongly modifies the early transient, while model-side differences can appear later. Together with the three-target formation result, these comparisons explain specialization through its formation conditions, current support, and persistent learning state without assigning all historical influence to one storage location. Reproducibility statement The complete reproduction archive is available from the author. It contains source code, time-stamped locally frozen analysis plans, raw CSV trajectories, aggregate results, checksums, and audit scripts for Experiments I– V-A and the Experiment IV-E follow-up. Central experiments run on CPU with Python, NumPy, and PyTorch. The five main figures are regenerated from archived confirmatory CSVs by make_reader_figures.py; plotted intervals resample seeds and average mirrored labels within seed. The follow-up archive also contains the fixed protocol, independent-seed confirmation, and separately labeled post-hoc analyses of second-moment structure and realized update-path length. Experiment identifiers are retained in the appendices to connect the question-based presentation to those records. Figure reaggregation does not replace the pre-specified analyses. Broader impact statement The work studies how selective information and learning conditions shape functional specialization in controlled artificial learners. The intervention map may help researchers examine systems whose input access or update histories vary over time. Its use in deployed systems requires direct validation in those settings.

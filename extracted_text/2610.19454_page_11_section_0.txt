As shown in Figure 3, AdCoEvo retains an ADV advantage after the market switches and gradually recovers its CPA improvement after the return to General, showing that co-evolution can improve adaptation to market changes. 5.4.4 Diagnostic Guidance Ablation To examine the role of diagnostic guidance, we replace Inspector-generated diagnoses with fixed statistical summaries and compare delivery performance under the two feedback settings. Relative to fixed statistical summaries, Inspector-generated diagnoses increase ADV by 1.61% and reduce CPA by 1.55% in Sparse, with corresponding improvements of 1.64% and 1.34% in General. This shows that converting delivery outcomes into diagnostic guidance better supports subsequent account planning and strategy adjustment than providing statistical information alone. 5.5 Budget Constraints and Parameter Sensitivity To examine whether AdCoEvo’s delivery gains depend on a particular resource setting, we compare business performance under different initial budgets. As shown in Figure 4, AdCoEvo achieves the highest ADV and lowest CPA at 60%, 80%, and 100% of the original budget. Even when the budget is reduced to 60%, it increases ADV by 7.17% and reduces CPA by 0.78% relative to AdCoEvo (Fixed). Tighter budgets require the system to coordinate account planning and ad-unit adjustment with more limited resources, yet AdCoEvo still improves both value generation and cost efficiency.

Memory writing is a long-horizon control problem over an external state, not merely a summarization prompt. Hy-Memory-30B-A3B first learns to operate real memory harnesses through on-policy distillation, then optimizes the answerability and selectivity of the banks it creates through Memory RL. The central technical contribution is the reward interface: failure-driven probes make supervision dense, cross-chunk questions measure downstream utility, gold facts attribute that utility to source chunks, traps and growth penalties close the store-everything shortcut, and chunk-level GRPO compares actions at the correct decision boundary. Hy-Memory-30B-A3B ranks first on Mem0, Hy-Memory, MemOS, and held-out EverMemOS. On fixed 20% benchmark subsamples, it averages 55.49% across six metrics and four harnesses, ahead of Claude Opus 5 at 54.62%, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at 53.64%, and the Hy3-A3B base checkpoint at 47.66%. The OOD EverMemOS result provides the clearest margin over Opus 5 (+2.86 points). Together, these results support memory writing as a transferable model capability rather than a collection of framework-specific prompt heuristics.

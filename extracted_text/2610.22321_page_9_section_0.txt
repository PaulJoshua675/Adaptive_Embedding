Right: generation results of LTX-2, OmniNFT, and Ours. 5.4 CASE STUDIES Fig. 5 presents representative cases of AVC-Reward in evaluation and generation optimization. AVC-Reward handles multi-layer audio, diverse relations such as off-screen and ambient sounds, and multiple or concurrent events by distinguishing audio roles and evaluating event relations sep- arately; as an RL reward, it also improves generation under these complexities. Moreover, bidirec- tional cross-modal verification enables finer checks of sound attributes and visible sound-producing actions, while the optimized generator produces more accurate sounds and clearer, more correct corresponding visual evidence.

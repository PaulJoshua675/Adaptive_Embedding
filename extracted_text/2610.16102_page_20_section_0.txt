It is rootless by default, and its significance is noticed only when someone outside the model supplies the evaluation. Rootlessness is a default, not a law. Condition 3 of §7.1 concerns people, not models: a result stops being rootless as soon as someone acquires the understanding needed to extend it, teach it and judge it, as the digestions described in §8.5 show. What makes a result rootless is autonomous generation that no one has yet digested. 7.4 A SECI4 reading of the gap In Nonaka and Takeuchi’s spiral, combination joins explicit knowledge to explicit knowledge; internalization turns explicit knowledge into tacit know-how by doing; socialization passes tacit knowledge between people. Autonomous AI problem-solving performs combination at very high speed. It skips the two moves that need people. The follow-up activities Tao lists— talks, questions after talks, workshops, collaborations, newcomers—are where internalization and socialization happen (Tao 2026a). When they do not happen, the spiral stalls after combination, and explicit results accumulate faster than anyone’s tacit understanding of them. Collins and Thorne locate the same gap at the research front: the judgements that separate serious work from noise rest on collective tacit knowledge formed in the spoken discourse of closed expert groups, which LLMs cannot currently reach (Collins & Thorne 2026). Talks, workshops and collaborations are that discourse. 7.5 Scooping, possession and transmission Tao’s last symptom is that promising open directions are withheld for fear of being scooped (Tao 2026a). The historical analysis of §6 suggests why this matters. When generation is cheap, the scarce assets are a direction and the tacit sense of why it is worth pursuing, so people guard them. Guarding them, however, starves transmission, which is what 2026d found to separate Leibniz’s legacy from Newton’s in the documented record. A credit system that rewards possession pushes a field toward Newton’s thin thread; one that rewards transmission pushes it toward Leibniz’s hourglass. Sanderson’s proposal to credit motivated explanations, and Tao’s remark, quoted by Weinreich (2026), that prestige should move toward the people who verify and digest AI-generated proofs, can both be read as attempts to reward transmission again (Sanderson 2026). 8 Re-rooting: auditable abduction and a three-layer division of labour A rootless result can be re-rooted if it passes through three layers in turn—a generator, a walkable trace with its confidence, and a community that values it—and VaCoAl/PyVaCoAl can supply the middle layer, which is the one now missing. 4SECI stands for the four modes of knowledge conversion in Nonaka and Takeuchi’s theory of organizational knowledge creation (Nonaka & Takeuchi 1995, 62–73): socialization (tacit to tacit), sharing experience, as ap- prentices learn a craft by observation, imitation and practice; externalization (tacit to explicit), articulating tacit knowledge as metaphors, analogies, concepts, hypotheses or models; combination (explicit to explicit), system- atizing and combining separate bodies of explicit knowledge; and internalization (explicit to tacit), embodying explicit knowledge as tacit know-how through learning by doing.

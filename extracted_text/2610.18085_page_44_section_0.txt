G.13 Exact Contraction and Synthetic Optimizer Diagnostics This synthetic study tests the exact disturbance-free contraction law and separately measures Lion sign changes after a soft orbit edit. The sign diagnostic concerns the sampled optimizer state. Disturbance-free Soft SAGGS contraction. Starting from a random pair (A0, B0) with controlled log-ratio r0 = log(∥A0∥F / ∥B0∥F), we apply the damped rebalancing map of Eq. 12 repeatedly without intervening optimizer updates. The contraction law in Eq. 19 predicts rK = (1 −α)Kr0 exactly. In double precision over α ∈{0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.0} and three initial imbalances, the reported worst scored single-step relative error is 7.8 × 10−4. This error is scored only when both |rk| and |(1 −α)rk| exceed 10−12; tiny denominators can amplify rounding error. For α = 1, the predicted residual is zero, so this relative-error score has no eligible steps. The 200-event absolute residual is zero for all three starts at α = 1. The theoretical factor at α = 0.10 is 0.9200 ≃7.06 × 10−10 after 200 events and 0.9150 ≃1.37 × 10−7 after 150 events. These factors describe repeated applications of the damped rebalancing map; training-time balance is measured separately in the language-model experiments. Lion update-sign response to damping. After a 500-step warmup with λ = 0.2 initial reparameterization, we branch the optimizer state into six parallel runs, each applying Soft SAGGS with a different α, and compare the immediate Lion sign update to the α = 0 branch. The fraction of update components that flip sign relative to baseline is 0.65% at α = 0.10, 1.82% at α = 0.25, 3.91% at α = 0.50, 5.34% at α = 0.75, and 7.03% at α = 1.00. The measured relationship is monotone in α and roughly linear. Hard rebalancing with α = 1 flips about 11 times as many components as α = 0.10 in this diagnostic; this pattern is an empirical observation about the sampled state.

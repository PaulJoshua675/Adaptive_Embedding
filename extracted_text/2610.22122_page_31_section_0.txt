Simpson K562. The original study examines how DNA repair genes jointly affect cell growth and PARP inhibitor response. We use its double gene CRISPRi growth phenotype and target combinations with the highest growth scores (Simpson et al., 2023). SCHEMATIC. This task seeks gene combinations that cause synthetic lethality across cancer cell backgrounds. We use the multilineage interaction score to identify strong negative interactions whose growth or survival defects exceed expectations from individual perturbations (Fong et al., 2025). Kuzmin yeast. This task examines how duplicate genes compensate for one another and how this compensation depends on other genes. We use the corrected trigenic interaction score to target combinations with stronger growth defects than expected (Kuzmin et al., 2020). Horlbeck Jurkat. This task examines the effect of jointly repressing two genes on Jurkat cell growth. We use the double gene CRISPRi growth phenotype and target combinations that strongly inhibit growth (Horlbeck et al., 2018).

The 1.2B gradient-law verification in Appendix G.9 confirmed the gradient transformation law via a single forward-backward pass. A separate empirical issue is whether the sensitivity seen in smaller-scale training persists at billion-parameter scale. To test this persistence, we train a 1.22B-parameter SwiGLU model for 10,000 optimizer steps using DDP across 4×H100 GPUs. Setup. The SwiGLU Transformer has d = 2048, L = 22, nheads = 16, and dff = 5504, with gradient checkpointing and BF16 mixed precision. We train on WikiText-103 using AdamW with η = 3 × 10−4, β = (0.9, 0.95), weight decay 0.1, a cosine schedule with 1,000-step warmup, and gradient clipping at 1.0. A per-GPU batch size of 4 on 4 GPUs with 2 accumulation steps gives an effective batch size of 32. We compare three configurations: unperturbed Vanilla at λ = 0, Vanilla with all-pairs rescaling at λ = 0.2, and SAGGS with the same rescaling and rebalancing every 100 steps. Results. Table 23 reports the results. The 1.2B baseline achieves PPL = 23.81 after the specified 10K-step training budget. At λ = 0.2, where c21 = e0.2×21 ≈66.7 and the all-pairs reparameterization affects Q–K, V–O, and MLP simultaneously, Vanilla PPL severely degrades to 221.24, a 9.3× increase. The training loss plateaus above 2.7, compared with a baseline of 1.5, and does not recover within the 10K-step budget. Before clipping at 1.0, the mean gradient norm reaches 27.8, compared with a baseline of 0.68, with a peak of about 4,700. The final maximum absolute log-ratios are 4.22 for Q–K, 4.12 for V–O, and 4.14 for MLP. In this single-seed 10K-step run, SAGGS protects against this degradation, achieving PPL = 23.70, slightly better than the baseline, while keeping the final maximum absolute log-ratio below 1.3 × 10−4 for every pair type. V–O is largest at 1.23 × 10−4. Figure 4 shows how Q–K and MLP imbalance evolve during this run. This single-seed comparison extends the observed sensitivity to rescaling to billion-parameter training. Interpretation. Together with the 1.2B gradient-law verification (Appendix G.9), this run shows that both the structural symmetry and the associated optimizer sensitivity appear at billion-parameter size.

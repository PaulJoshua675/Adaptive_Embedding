B.3 Benefit retained as a secondary measure For a task score s, the fraction of the table’s mea- sured benefit retained is R = sq −srem sorig −srem . Here sq, srem and sorig use the compressed, re- moved and original table. For a group, we sum the score gains in the numerator and denominator over its tasks before taking the ratio. This measures preservation of the removal gap; it is not accuracy relative to the original model. It can exceed one and becomes unstable when the original and removed scores are close.

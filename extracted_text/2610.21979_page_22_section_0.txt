SCR allocation controls Removing SCR (𝛽= 0) eliminates both nonuniform weighting and its alignment with token correctness. Perturbations operate only on valid tokens within eligible matched blocks, after SCR has computed its final coefficients. The original mask selects error spans for negative block credit and correct positions for positive block credit. • Circular shift. We jointly rotate the selected-token mask and its coefficient sequence by an offset sampled uniformly from {0, . . . , 𝐿−1}, where 𝐿is the valid-token count. This preserves cyclic order and spacing while randomizing alignment with the original token labels. The zero offset is included. • Random support. We sample the same number of valid positions uniformly w/o replacement. Existing selected and unselected coefficient values are copied to the new support and its complement, respectively.

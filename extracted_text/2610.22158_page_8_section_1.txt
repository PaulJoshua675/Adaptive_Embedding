In this work, we introduce PremiseSQL, to our knowledge the first benchmark for evaluat- ing premise critique ability in NL2SQL systems. PremiseSQL contains 1,800 flawed queries cover- ing 9 fine-grained error types and their 1,800 origi- nals as positive and negative samples, respectively. Experiments under Base, Implicit, and Explicit prompting settings reveal a clear reliability gap in current NL2SQL systems. Models often trust user assumptions and generate misleading SQL under standard settings. Even when explicitly prompted to scrutinize premises, they struggle to assess them reliably. Models further struggle with subtle and implicit premise flaws, showing that strong SQL generation does not necessarily imply trustworthy premise-aware reasoning. We hope PremiseSQL can facilitate future research on more reliable and premise-aware NL2SQL systems. 8

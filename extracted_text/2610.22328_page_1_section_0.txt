ACEdit: Stable Multi-Turn Image Editing via Asymmetric Conditioning Sara Dorfman* Nir Goren* Gal Barak* Daniel Cohen-Or Or Patashnik Tel Aviv University Original “Add a bird” “Add green socks” “Make the cage purple” “Add a ribbon” “Add small flowers” Edit #3 Edit #6 Edit #9 Edit #12 Edit #15 Flux.2 Klein Flux.2 Klein + Ours Figure 1. Stable Multi-Turn Image Editing. We show selected turns from a 15-step sequence. Each of the five edits is followed by two no-op instructions (“keep the image as is”). While the original FLUX.2 Klein gradually loses detail and develops a strong color shift, fine-tuning it with our training scheme yields a more stable editing process, better preserving the scene and previously edited content while faithfully following the requested changes. The zoom-ins highlight the loss of fine fur texture and the emergence of harsh, unnatural lines in the baseline, while our model preserves the fur’s soft texture and natural appearance.

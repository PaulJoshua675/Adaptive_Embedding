C Experimental and implementation details This section specifies the architecture, numerical implementation, and training procedure for the mathematical- reasoning checkpoints used in the main comparison. We distinguish the full-canvas policy, TIPSfull, from the two-block policy, TIPSblock, while identifying the supervised initialization shared by the two variants. Evaluation populations, compute accounting, and statistical summaries are specified in appendix D. C.1 Frozen decoder and policy architecture We use the frozen LLaDA-8B-Instruct decoder (Nie et al., 2025), identified as GSAI-ML/LLaDA-8B-Instruct. Only the visibility controller is optimized: decoder parameters are excluded from the optimizer, and the recorded decoder weights remain unchanged throughout training. The controller is a two-layer RoPE-DiT (Peebles & Xie, 2023; Su et al., 2024) with hidden width 128, two attention heads per layer, and feed-forward width 512. A linear projection maps each two-dimensional confidence feature to the hidden width. A 128-dimensional sinusoidal time embedding is processed by a two-layer multilayer perceptron with SiLU activation, and a learned two-state embedding distinguishes masked from visible positions. The DiT layers use time- and state-conditioned adaptive layer normalization, bidirectional self-attention over response-position inputs, and GELU feed-forward activations. Rotary position embeddings are applied to attention queries and keys, with base 10,000. A final layer normalization and scalar projection produce one visibility logit per position. We use zero dropout and no separate conditioning on the absolute decoding budget. The adaptive normalization parameters are initialized to zero, the shared output bias is initialized to zero, and learned state-dependent logit offsets are initialized to −2 for masked positions and +4 for visible positions. Each final controller has 628,739 trainable parameters; fixed buffers are excluded from this count. At a masked position, the active token is the greedy proposal; at a visible position, it is the token already stored on the canvas. The strongest alternative is obtained from the two highest-probability vocabulary entries: it is the second entry when the active token is first, and the first entry otherwise. Thus, alternative probabilities are not renormalized over a reduced vocabulary. For numerical stability, feature calculations are performed in float32 with ϵ = 10−6. The active probability is clipped to [ϵ, 1 −ϵ] for its log-odds, whereas both probabilities are clipped only from below by ϵ for the log-ratio.

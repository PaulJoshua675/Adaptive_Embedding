The model accumulated the largest aggregate sleep duration of all groups (26.18 h, 36.4% of observation time), yet achieved only a 48.2% restorative score. Inspection of subcortical trajectories in Figure 3 reveals that without orexinergic stabilization, the flip-flop switch loses hysteresis. The LHA cannot maintain sustained firing against VLPO inhibition, causing nocturnal sleep to shatter into recurrent awakenings (WASO = 781 min). During daylight hours, the lack of wake stabilization produces involuntary sleep attacks. Furthermore, the relaxation of REM threshold kinetics generated 35 diagnostic SOREMPs [11, 3].

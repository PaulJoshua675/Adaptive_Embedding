Limitations Flat identifiers make early pruning harder. In our token design, no prefix is more informative than another, so early decoding prunes on limited infor- mation. Multi-tree beam search reduces this effect, but Hit@100 on seen documents remains slightly below some baselines. In future work, we plan to combine bit factorization with residual quantiza- tion such as RQ-VAE, whose coarse-to-fine codes may make early levels more informative and reduce pruning errors. Language. Like most of the prior work, we only focus on English datasets. This is common because most of benchmark datasets are in English. Our method is completely data-driven and not dependent on labeled data and it is therefore applicable to other languages than English.

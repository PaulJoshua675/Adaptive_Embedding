At every training step, live links age by one step, links older than 𝐿steps die, and 𝑘new links form between currently unlinked pairs chosen by a rule. The random rule picks pairs uniformly. The four activity rules score pairs on the current minibatch, where a unit fires on an example when its ReLU output is positive. Co-activity is the fraction of examples on which both units fire, Jaccard similarity is the number of examples on which both fire divided by the number on which either fires, and the correlation and anti-correlation rules use the Pearson correlation of the two activations and its negative, never linking a unit that is silent on the whole minibatch. Each rule links the 𝑘highest- scoring unlinked pairs, with ties broken arbitrarily.

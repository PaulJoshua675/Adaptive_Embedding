Power relation prediction We use the English Wikipedia Talk Pages Corpus1 distributed through ConvoKit (Chang et al., 2020). Administrator sta- tus serves as a proxy for institutional power. We scan utterances.jsonl in file order, group con- secutive utterances sharing a root, and retain the first 1,000 conversations with exactly two distinct speakers and at least one is-admin=true utter- ance. Speakers are labeled A and B by first appear- ance, preserving utterance order. Answer extraction and metrics For reasoning completions, answers are extracted after the final closing reasoning tag. Only explicit answer fields or label lists are accepted; empty or invalid answers are incorrect. Turns with catch-all gold labels are excluded as prediction targets but remain available as context for subsequent turns.

This result substantiates that integrating feature fusion and distillation mechanisms for cold-start scenarios does not compromise the model’s capacity to capture and model abundant existing interaction signals. Specifically, compared to the best-performing pure ID model, LightGCN, the first-stage DualGCN achieves a remarkable improvement of 39.7% and 23.3% in Recall@20 on the Amazon-Electronics and Amazon-Sports datasets, respectively. Even on the Amazon-Books dataset, which features relatively denser interactions, DualGCN maintains a distinct leading advantage. This superiority is primarily attributed to the teacher network’s ability to fully exploit multimodal side information, effectively bridging potential sparse connections within the pure ID interaction graph and thereby augmenting representation learning in warm-start scenarios. Concurrently, we observe that the second-stage DualGCN exhibits remarkable stability, maintaining performance highly consistent with the first stage. This confirms the efficacy of the knowledge distillation mechanism in preserving warm-start collaborative signals. Notably, while DeepMusic demonstrates competitiveness in cold-start scenarios, its performance in warm-start settings is significantly inferior to both LightGCN and DualGCN. Strict Cold-start Recommendation Performance. To rigorously validate the model’s true generalization capability for completely novel items, we conduct further evaluations under a strictly restricted pure cold-start scenario. As illustrated in the lower half of Table 3, given the complete absence of historical interaction records for target items, pure ID-based models reliant on graph propagation, such as LightGCN, NGCF, and MF, suffer catastrophic performance degradation with metrics plummeting to near zero. In stark contrast, DualGCN demonstrates exceptional robustness. Empirical data validates that the second-stage DualGCN effectively circumvents the cold-start failure inherent to ID embeddings, significantly outperforming tradi- tional feature-driven solutions like DropoutNet and MetaEmbedding across multiple datasets. For instance, on the Amazon-Sports dataset, the second-stage DualGCN achieves a Recall@20 of 0.1119, yielding a 20.8% improvement over the runner-up model, DropoutNet, while also consistently maintaining superiority across all metrics on the Amazon-Books dataset. Although DeepMusic exhibits competitive cold-start performance on Amazon-Electronics, it falters sig- nificantly in conventional warm-start evaluations when compared to LightGCN and DualGCN.

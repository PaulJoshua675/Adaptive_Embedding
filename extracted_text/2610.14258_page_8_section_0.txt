If it corrects a bias in the importance measure, the gain should persist at large 𝑛. 4.3 Metrics and statistics Let 𝑅𝑡,𝑗be the test accuracy on task 𝑗after training on task 𝑡. We report final average accuracy ACC = 1 𝑇 Í 𝑗𝑅𝑇,𝑗, backward transfer BWT = 1 𝑇−1 Í 𝑗<𝑇(𝑅𝑇,𝑗−𝑅𝑗,𝑗) [26], and learning accuracy LA = 1 𝑇 Í 𝑗𝑅𝑗,𝑗, a measure of plasticity [35], all in percent. Within a seed, all arms see the same task permutations, data splits, and class order, so we compare arms by seed-paired differences. We report the mean difference, its standard error (SE), Cohen’s 𝑑𝑧, a two-sided paired 𝑡-test, and an exact sign-flip permutation test, and we control the family-wise error with Holm’s procedure [14]. Significance marks and Holm adjustments use the 𝑡-test. The sign-flip test serves as a check, and with five seeds its smallest attainable 𝑝-value is 0.0625. In Study 2 the families are, within each 𝜆, (A) every shrinkage and link arm against tag only (14 comparisons), (B) each activity rule against random links of the same lifetime (8), and (C) each shrinkage weight against random links of each lifetime (8). Study 3 uses one family of all 27 comparisons, three per cell (𝛽= 0.25 against none, EB against none, and EB against 𝛽= 0.25). In Study 4 we regress each seed’s gain on log10 𝑛, test the mean slope with a one-sample 𝑡-test and the gain at 𝑛= 1000 with a paired 𝑡-test, and apply Holm over the 16 primary tests (two fixed weights, slope and gain at 𝑛= 1000, four benchmark and method pairs). Descriptive tables give the mean ± standard deviation over seeds, computed with the number of seeds as divisor. A non-significant difference is not evidence of equivalence, so where we claim that two arms agree we give a confidence interval. 4.4 Implementation, compute, and use of AI tools All experiments were implemented in PyTorch [32] and run on one Apple M5 MacBook Pro with the MPS backend.

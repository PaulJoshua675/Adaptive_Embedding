Bringing all components together, the complete training objective of ABNAT combines three comple- mentary losses, each operating at a distinct level of abstraction: 𝐿𝑡𝑜𝑡𝑎𝑙= 𝐿𝑡𝑎𝑠𝑘+ 𝜆𝑙𝑜𝑔𝑖𝑡𝑠𝐿𝑙𝑜𝑔𝑖𝑡𝑠+ 𝜆𝑎𝑡𝑡𝑛𝐿𝑎𝑡𝑡𝑛(S), (8) where 𝜆𝑙𝑜𝑔𝑖𝑡𝑠and 𝜆𝑎𝑡𝑡𝑛are scalar hyperparameters that balance the three objectives; 𝐿𝑡𝑎𝑠𝑘is kept at unit coefficient as the scale anchor. Unless otherwise stated, we use 𝜆𝑙𝑜𝑔𝑖𝑡𝑠= 3, 𝜆𝑎𝑡𝑡𝑛= 50, and 𝜏KD = 𝜏𝑎𝑡𝑡𝑛= 1. At the coarsest level, the task loss 𝐿𝑡𝑎𝑠𝑘is the standard cross- entropy loss computed against ground-truth labels, ensuring that the student retains discriminative performance on the target task regardless of noise conditions. Building on top of this, the logits dis- tillation loss 𝐿𝑙𝑜𝑔𝑖𝑡𝑠= 𝐷KL(softmax(𝑧𝑇/𝜏KD)∥softmax(𝑧𝑆/𝜏KD)), where 𝑧𝑇and 𝑧𝑆are the pre-softmax logits and𝜏KD is the distillation temperature, aligns the student’s output probability distribution with that of the clean teacher. Finally, at the finest level of granu- larity, the attention alignment loss 𝐿𝑎𝑡𝑡𝑛(S) reaches deeper into the network to preserve the selected internal attention pathways. Together, these three losses form a coherent, multi-level training strategy: 𝐿𝑡𝑎𝑠𝑘anchors the student to correct predictions, 𝐿𝑙𝑜𝑔𝑖𝑡𝑠 aligns its output distribution with the teacher’s, and 𝐿𝑎𝑡𝑡𝑛(S) safe- guards attention routes vulnerable to CIM-induced degradation. Generalization is tested on ViT-Small on CIFAR-100, ViT-Tiny on ImageNet-100, Swin-Tiny on CIFAR-100, Pythia-160M and Pythia-2.8B on WikiText-103, and CLIP ViT-B/32 on Flickr30K. Unless otherwise specified, models are trained with AdamW for 100 epochs, batch size 1024, learning rate 1 × 10−3, weight decay 0.05, cosine decay, and 5 warmup epochs. For task-specific runs, the Pythia models use 262,144 WikiText-103 training tokens for 5 epochs at sequence length 128 and are evaluated on 65,536 tokens at length 2,048; CLIP uses Flickr30K training images at 224 × 224 for 10 epochs.

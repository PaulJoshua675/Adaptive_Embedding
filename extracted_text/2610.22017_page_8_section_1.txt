In this paper, we proposed CARA, a training-free causally guided retrieval agent for question answer- ing over text-rich knowledge graphs. Unlike ex- isting agentic retrievers that primarily rely on tex- tual similarity, CARA evaluates retrieved evidence through consequentiality and essentiality, together with path consistency, to guide graph exploration. Reciprocal rank fusion further aggregates evidence from multiple independent agents. We also aug- mented the STaRK benchmark with ground-truth answers to enable downstream question answering evaluation. Experiments on three datasets demon- strate that CARA consistently improves retrieval effectiveness, while also improving answer quality and faithfulness compared with ARK. Moreover, CARA achieves more focused exploration by exam- ining fewer nodes and requiring fewer retrieval op- erations. However, the additional causal reasoning introduces inference latency overhead, particularly for relation-intensive questions. 8

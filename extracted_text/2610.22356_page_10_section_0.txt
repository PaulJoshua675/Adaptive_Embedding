To assess overall spatial understanding, we further compare models across six spatial benchmarks (Table 2). DK-VLM-8B achieves the highest av- erage score of 64.09, outperforming the spatial post-training and geometry-enhanced methods in the table. It also ranks first on four of the six benchmarks, showing that the gains extend beyond a single benchmark. 4.3 Effect of RC Supervision To assess whether RC supervision improves performance on public tasks involving multiple reasoning steps or changes in reference frame, we compare the full DK-VLM-8B with a version trained without RC supervision (Table 3). Both retain Dual-Kernel Attention and pairwise geometric supervision. RC improves route planning on VSI-Bench and ReVSI-32, as well as multi-step reasoning on MMSI-Bench. These gains show that geometric relation modeling benefits from additional stepwise supervision, helping the model use spatial relations across reasoning steps. Improvements on the person-perspective tasks in ViewSpatial further suggest that the benefits extend beyond sequential reasoning to spatial judgments under a different reference frame. 10

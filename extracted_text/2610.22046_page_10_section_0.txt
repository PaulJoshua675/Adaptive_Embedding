All results in this paper come from one model family, Qwen3 in thinking mode at two scales, on hard mathematics problems. The failure types depend on the rollout protocol, including the number of rollouts, the threshold and the token limits (Appendix B). Under this protocol, a capability gap records only that no sampled continuation reached a correct answer. A rollout counts as successful when its final answer is correct, so a continuation that reaches the answer through an invalid argument counts as a success. Such cases are reported for base models [Wen et al., 2026]. The final labels contain grading errors, and correcting them leaves capability gaps and PITs missed more often than near misses at both scales (Appendix B). 6

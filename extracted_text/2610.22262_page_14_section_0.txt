C FACTORIAL EFFECTS Each effect is the average change in a mean distance attributable to a loss (main effect) or to the combination of two or three losses beyond their separate effects (interaction), estimated from all configurations in the design with standard ±1 coding. Effects are reported for the negated distance, so a positive value means the loss, or the interaction, lowers that distance on average; units are those of each distance.

Gauge invariance removes multiplicative scale distortion with no Lipschitz constant. Finite- precision rounding generally introduces both radial and tangential components, but only the tangential component can change the canonical computation. The sensitivity to rounding is determined by the dimensionless ratio RMS(e)/ RMS(x). The rescaling experiment of Sec. 5.4 isolates the first claim, while residual-stream quantization in Sec. 5.5 exercises the second claim. E.8 Shared-scale quantization on the quotient We next bound the nonclipping rounding error under shared-scale quantization. In symmetric b-bit absmax quantization with a shared per-tensor scale, the largest absolute coordinate in the tensor sets the quantization step.

Pool recall is the question-averaged fraction of annotated supporting passages in the candidate pool. R@5 measures this fraction in the final five selected passages. How do relations guide retrieval? With equal maximum candidate budgets, Table 4 shows RCER raising pool recall over single-query retrieval from 80.25 to 99.38 on 2WikiQA and from 78.75 to 87.13 on MuSiQue. Resolving intermediate entities makes new retrieval targets explicit, enabling decomposition to recover complementary support missed by the original query. HotpotQA already reaches 97.70 with single-query retrieval, leaving less room to expand evidence coverage.

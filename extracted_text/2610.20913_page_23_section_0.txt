The boundary is represented by two persistent state objects. For serving notation, we use 𝑉(𝑢) 𝜏 to denote the published behavior-memory KV snapshot: a bounded, immutable projection of the behavioral memory consumed by online prediction. This separation makes the three workloads independently schedulable and scalable while preserving a common model interface. Bulk capacity follows rollout and backfill demand, nearline capacity follows behavior arrival and freshness lag, and online capacity follows request traffic, candidate fanout, and the latency SLO.

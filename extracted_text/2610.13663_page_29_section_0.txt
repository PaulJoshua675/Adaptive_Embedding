Two research questions were tested for the three models: the effect of switching from the simple to the detailed prompt and the effect of including the coverage map in the detailed prompt. Thus, six statistical comparisons were performed, and their results were used to formulate the overall conclusion about the effectiveness of prompt structure. Testing each hypothesis separately at a significance level of 𝛼= 0.05 does not preserve this level for the full set of comparisons. Specifically, if the six tests were independent and all six null hypotheses were true, the probability of at least one false-positive result would be 1 −(1 −𝛼)6 = 1 −0.956 = 0.2649, i.e., approximately 26.49 %. To ensure control of the family-wise probability of at least one type I error at 0.05, the Holm–Bonferroni correction was applied.

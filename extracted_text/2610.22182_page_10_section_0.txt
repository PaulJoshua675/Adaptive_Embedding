LAMP-F optimizes only Cdg prototype parameters; the encoders, regional descriptors, and fusion weights remain frozen. 4. Experiments We evaluate recognition accuracy at different support budgets and examine how class-prototype adaptation and native-layer integration contribute to performance. We also test transfer to new domains without fitting on target data and measure deployment costs. Comparisons with published methods establish overall performance, while paired controls assess individual design choices. 4.1. Evaluation protocol We evaluate training-free LAMP and its prototype-adapted extension, LAMP-F, with CLIP encoders from the ViT and ResNet families. The main comparison covers eleven datasets using ViT-B/16 and RN50, with 1, 2, 4, 8, and 16 support images per class and three support-sampling seeds. Additional experiments use RN101 and ViT-B/32. We report top-1 accuracy and give each dataset equal weight when computing the mean. We follow the established benchmark partitions and evaluation conventions used in previous studies [2, 5, 6]. Labeled validation images are not counted toward the support budget. Paired controls use the same support images, augmented views, prompts, and query images.

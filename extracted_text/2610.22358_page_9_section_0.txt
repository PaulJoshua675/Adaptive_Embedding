We compare fine-tuned Chronos-2, PANDA (Lai et al., 2026), and PathFM on point forecasting and long continuations of 135 DYSTS systems (Gilpin, 2021). We evaluate PathFM with ten-step Euler sampling per 256-step block. Section F gives its training recipe, and evaluation details. 6.1 FORECAST ACCURACY AND DYNAMICAL FIDELITY Using sMAPE as in PANDA (Lai et al., 2026), we evaluate forecasts at horizons 64, 128, 256, and 512. Four complementary diagnostics assess continued paths: Wasserstein-1 distance to an empirical approximation of the invariant distribution, correlation-dimension error, squared Hellinger distance between normalized power spectral densities (PSD), and segment analog score. Together, they assess state occupancy, geometric dimension, frequency content, and similarity of short multivariate segments to reference dynamics. Held-out truth provides a finite-trajectory reference. Chronos-1 (Ansari et al., 2024), an autoregressive forecasting model, has been shown to mimic local chaotic dynamics (Yu et al., 2026). Chronos-1 achieves lower W1 and PSD H2 than the quantile model Chronos-2, with higher pointwise, correlation-dimension, and segment analog errors. These results highlight the distinct strengths and weaknesses of traditional autoregressive and quantile forecasting models.

C Training Configuration and Execution C.1 Matched 72K Training Recipe The controlled studies use 72K QA: 25K choice questions from SAT (Ray et al., 2025), 22K length-estimation questions from VST-P (Yang et al., 2025b), and 25K pointing questions from RefSpatial (Zhou et al., 2025a). The length subset contains 11K height and 11K longest-dimension labels, ranging from 0 to 167.82 m. Exam- ples, order, and one-pass exposure budget are fixed for the matched comparisons. The aligned experiments use eight NVIDIA L20D GPUs, four QA per GPU, global batch 32, no gradient accumulation, and 2,252 optimizer updates for one pass. For the selected native model, this pass takes 1,413 s of training time (3.14 GPU-hours). All three primary readout models share seed 3407, the canonical QA permutation, image budgets, stage-normalized loss, and optimizer settings.

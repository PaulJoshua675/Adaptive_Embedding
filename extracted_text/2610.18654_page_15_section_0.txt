Compression and sparsity compose. At 𝑘𝑚= 128, C-KSA+Full increases the distant-history compression factor within KSA layers from 8 to 1024, with mean and 128K RULER scores of 72.68% and 66.31%. Adding S-KSA to this backbone lowers the 128K score to 65.01%, whereas the same sparse adaptation applied to Hybrid KSA lowers it from 65.35% to 61.06% (Table 9(c)). General capabilities. C-KSA+S-KSA remains broadly comparable to Hybrid KSA across the nine general-capability benchmarks, with gains on MMLU, MMLU-Pro, and CMATH and lower scores on the remaining tasks. The largest decrease is 2.81 percentage points on GSM8K, while MBPP and HumanEval decrease by 1.20 and 1.83 points, respectively. Compared with C-KSA+Full, adding S-KSA changes each score by at most 1.83 points, with both gains and losses, indicating no uniform decline from sparse adaptation. The combined model exceeds Full on eight of the nine benchmarks, with C-Eval as the exception. Adaptation dynamics. Figure 9 shows how retrieval performance recovers during adaptation. For C-KSA, retrieval performance improves during module-only adaptation and approaches Hybrid KSA after full-parameter adaptation.

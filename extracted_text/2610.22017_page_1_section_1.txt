Retrieval-augmented generation over text-rich knowledge graphs requires identifying evi- dence that contributes to answering a question, rather than merely matching its textual content. Existing agentic retrievers adaptively balance global search and multi-hop exploration but primarily rely on textual similarity to guide retrieval. We propose CARA, a training-free, CAusally guided Retrieval Agent that explic- itly evaluates the causal relevance of retrieved evidence through two complementary criteria: consequentiality, measuring whether adding ev- idence improves answerability, and essentiality, assessing whether removing evidence weakens answerability. CARA integrates these assess- ments with path consistency to guide graph ex- ploration and employs reciprocal rank fusion to aggregate evidence from multiple agents. We evaluate CARA on three STaRK datasets and augment STaRK with ground-truth answers to enable downstream question answering evalu- ation. Experimental results demonstrate that CARA consistently outperforms existing re- trievers. CARA also improves answer quality and faithfulness while examining fewer graph nodes and requiring fewer retrieval operations. These findings demonstrate that causal rele- vance assessment enables more focused and ef- fective agentic graph retrieval, improving both retrieval performance and downstream question answering. 1

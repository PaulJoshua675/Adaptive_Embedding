Table 13 evaluates the con- tribution of each component in RWEAVE by progressive re- moval. Graph Credit is the most impactful individual component: its removal reduces WB-GC by 4.4 pp and WB-Res by 1.7 pp. The Resolved bonus is the second most important compo- nent, providing an explicit signal for full-task completion. The regression penalty (RegP2P) has minimal impact on per- formance metrics but is critical for maintaining code quality: among the four reward variants compared in RQ4 the P2P regression rate stays at 1.8%, whereas removing the penalty from RWEAVE raises it to 4.7%. This ablation-only config- uration is the sole setting that exceeds the 2.3% regression ceiling reported in the main text. The two single-signal rewards bound the ablation from below. Binary Resolved improves over the SFT checkpoint by at most 0.7 pp on any metric: on WEAVE-Bench a roll- out earns reward only when every constituent PR is solved, so most rollouts return identical zero reward and GRPO receives almost no advantage signal to differentiate them.

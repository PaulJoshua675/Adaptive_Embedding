(4) 3.2 Inherited EMBGO structure and its limitations EMBGO [7] advances each player through one of three search behaviours. A player inside a safe region around the current best moves by a current-to-best-and-mean differential update (sinusoidally weighted differential vectors toward the best and the population mean); a player outside that region performs Lévy-flight exploration; and in a separate opponent battle a random opponent sets the direction, the player recombining with a stronger opponent by dimension- wise crossover and otherwise taking a single cosine-modulated step. Three features limit this scheme for a simulation- expensive, penalty-constrained objective: the arithmetic-mean attractor is pulled toward poor or penalised candidates; the neighbourhood radius is measured in raw coordinate units and is dominated by the widest-ranging variable; and although greedy replacement protects a parent from a worse trial, EMBGO keeps no explicit elite set and no stagnation or diversity recovery, so search effort is spent perturbing even the current best and a prematurely converged population is never re-diversified. 3.3 REMBGO search cycle REMBGO retains the battle-game core and addresses these three features. Before the equations, the search cycle is read as a fixed sequence. Each generation (i) ranks the current population and identifies the best and worst players; (ii) preserves a few elite players unchanged; (iii) measures population diversity and forms a fitness-weighted centroid, an adaptive neighbourhood radius, a decaying step scale and an adaptive patience; (iv) generates one trial for every non-elite player by selecting a single operator from a fixed portfolio; (v) applies a stagnation-dependent jitter and repairs any boundary violation; (vi) evaluates the trials while respecting the remaining evaluation budget and keeps, for each player, the better of parent and trial; and (vii) detects stagnation or low diversity and partially restarts the weakest players. The cycle repeats until the function-evaluation budget is spent.

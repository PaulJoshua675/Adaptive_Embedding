The names r1–r4 are SpinQuant’s (Liu et al., 2025b); the positions are QuaRot’s (Ashkboos et al., 2024). The learned s1 and s2 refine the r1 and r2 slots at the same endpoints, fitted to the weights alone with no labels, so the recipe stays data-free up to this point. Basis labelling. Because the basis has a significant impact on the quantization results, we treat it as part of a run’s identity: every run quantized in the recipe basis s1(l4)+s2(opt)+r4 is labelled as such, runs from the two bases never share a table row, and no plane effect is computed across them. r4 uses a block size of 1024 at 1.7B and 8B; its runtime Hadamard is the one a W2A8 path would reuse. Why the placement rule depends on the rotation. The structural reason given in Section 3.2, that r1 mixes the residual-stream axis and leaves the unit axis untouched, makes two predictions that the appendix tests.

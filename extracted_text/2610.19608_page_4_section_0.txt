Floors, transition tables, calibration and slices were fixed in advance as descriptive outputs. 3.5 Registration and execution The protocol was frozen at 07:56 UTC on 13 October 2026. Its plan hash (SHA-256 b3893b79 8490dcb8fed2a5dba649da23dad5e5b99b8e45311e16d9a84d3a9018) covers the design, the man- ifest of 162 requests with their frozen forecasts, and the collection and analysis code. It was committed to the author’s research repository (commit 9d69cbb) and registered on OSF (https://osf.io/x8h3p/), under an embargo that is lifted at publication. The plan allowed calls from 7 to 21 days after v0.4. Two smoke calls on factual tax questions that name no brand checked the request settings and are excluded from every analysis. The 162 calls started between 14:03 and 14:18 UTC, in an order shuffled with seed 20261002, and ran through a capped local gateway. The plan lists two steps outside the analysis. The first is a legal reading of the provider’s terms for search-grounded output; the run went ahead on the author’s decision before that reading was filed, and no D1 answer contains search-grounded content (Section 4.1). The second is a label reading by a model from a second vendor, which has not yet run; Section 5 reports a human check in its place. Collection followed the plan with no deviation: no failed row, no retry, no resample. 4 Results 4.1 Completion, model version and search All 162 calls completed with finish reason STOP, none failed and none ran past the 120-second timeout. All 162 answers reported model version gemini-3.1-pro-preview, so the durability rule applies. The calls started between 487.9 and 488.2 hours after the end of v0.4 (median 488.0 hours, 20.3 days), inside the registered window. No answer issued a search query and none returned a grounding chunk, although the request included the search tool. The calls used 339,193 tokens in all, against 338,706 for the same 162 requests in v0.4; 144,369 of them were thinking tokens. The wave cost USD 3.94 at list price against a cap of USD 15. Of the 648 labels, 243 were positive (37.5%), against 247, 246 and 244 in the three earlier waves. 4.2 Verdict Durability holds under the registered rule. The pooled cell rate scored Brier 0.0281 and log loss 0.126 (Table 2, Figure 1). Both are strictly lower than those of brand history, the overall rate and the constant, and the Brier score is 0.0022 above the v0.4 reference of 0.0259, inside the ceiling of 0.0559. 4.3 Question families The pooled cell rate scored lower than brand history, the overall rate, the constant and question history in 9 of 9 families, on Brier and on log loss (exact sign-flip p = 0.0039 for each of the eight comparisons). Against the v0.2 context model it was lower in 9 of 9 families on Brier (p = 0.0039) and 8 of 9 on log loss (p = 0.027).

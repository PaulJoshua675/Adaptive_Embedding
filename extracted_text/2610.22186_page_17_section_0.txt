A.3.4 Experiments with Event Precision Reward The Global Event Completeness reward Rcomp encourages more detailed captions. After GRPO training with Rcomp, the average caption length on video-SALMONN2 testset increases from 633.0 tokens (SFT) to 748.2 tokens. However, the per-token hallucination rate does not increase (8.2 →8.1 Hall./1k tokens), while event coverage is substantially improved. This suggests that the higher absolute hallucination rate is largely coupled with longer, more comprehensive descriptions, rather than a pure collapse into unfaithful verbosity. Furthermore, we add an event-level precision term to the recall-based GRPO baseline, which is computed by decomposing the predicted caption into a sequence of audiovisual events and then determining the proportion of these events that are entailed in the groundtruth caption.

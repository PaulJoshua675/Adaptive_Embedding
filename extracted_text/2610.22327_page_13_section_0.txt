Interpolation is applied only within each window: the ℓvalues of Vc are placed on a uniform grid over [0, 1] and linearly interpolated at H uniformly spaced points. Window start indices pc are rounded to integers, so no interpolation is performed along the time axis. Each entry of the map is therefore an interpolated sample of the original series rather than a resized pixel. The resulting H × W matrix is min–max normalized, replicated across three channels, and normalized with the DINOv3 preprocessing statistics as a floating-point tensor, without quantization or image resizing.

Evaluating decentralized routing in this setting requires a multi-hop research benchmark where frontier LLMs cannot rely on pre-training memorization. If tasks referenced real historical events (e.g., the Vioxx recall or Theranos investigation), agents could bypass the data marketplace and answer directly from parametric memory. Synthetic Multi-Publisher Corpus Generation. To guarantee zero pre-training leakage while maintaining realistic multi-document dependencies, we programmatically synthesize 1,000 coher- ent corporate and scientific controversies (4,000 documents total) across 50 distinct technological sectors (e.g., Autonomous Vehicle Sensor Fusion, Solid-State EV Battery Electrolyte Leaks, Quantum Key Distribution). For each scenario 𝑘∈{0, . . . , 999}, we prompt Gemini-3.5-flash-lite to generate a self-consistent controversy incorporating a mandatory unique alphanumeric identifier suffix -k attached to all primary entities: the corporation name (e.g., Velosync-14), clinical or engineering study IDs (e.g., Trial-14), and key social media handles (e.g., @expert-14). Each generated scenario is partitioned into four strictly disjoint publisher corpora: 1. Wikipedia (wikipedia): High-level encyclopedic overviews of the corporation, core technology, and background of the dispute (1,000 documents). 2.

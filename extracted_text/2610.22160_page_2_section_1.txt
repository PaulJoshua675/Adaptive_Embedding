Parameter optimization. Parameter adaptation includes supervised fine-tuning and its parameter-efficient variants (Hu et al., 2021), reinforcement learning (Schulman et al., 2017; Shao et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2025; Yu et al., 2025), preference optimization (Rafailov et al., 2023), and distillation (Snell et al., 2022; Agarwal et al., 2024). These approaches incorporate training signals into model weights. EMO operates on a frozen checkpoint and uses feedback on the current question to adapt its context and decoding, making it complementary to parameter adaptation. Context optimization. A second line adapts textual inputs while keeping model weights frozen, including textual gradients and prompt optimization (Yuksekgonul et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2024), self-correction (Madaan et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023; Gou et al., 2024), and agentic context engineering that evolves skills and memories (Zhang et al., 2026; Ye et al., 2026).

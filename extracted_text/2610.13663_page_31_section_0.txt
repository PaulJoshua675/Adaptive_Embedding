E.1. Decoder search dynamics In addition to the final accuracy values reported in Tables 2 and 19, the search trajectory itself is of interest: how quickly the agent system finds a decoder that outperforms the baseline and reaches a plateau. Figure 6 shows the maximum accuracy among all decoders proposed up to the current iteration (the cumulative maximum) for each run of the agent system. The MNIST trajectories are collected in Fig. 6, and those for the digits dataset in Fig. 7. The curves demonstrate a pronounced dependence of convergence speed on the analyst model: with Claude Opus 4.6, the system reaches a plateau by iterations 5–10 (Fig. 6, Claude Opus 4.6 panels), whereas Perplexity Sonar in detailed-prompt mode exhibits stepwise dynamics with accuracy gains at iterations 50, 120, and 170; the system periodically returns to revising hypotheses after long plateaus.

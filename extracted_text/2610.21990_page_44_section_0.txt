(3.60) 3.4.5 Segmentation Head To map the high-dimensional fused representations extracted by the Fusion Vision Transformer into the distinct probability logits necessary for each class, a CNN is utilized. Within the VesperMUSE architecture, the Segmentation Head utilizes a CNN to map the extracted feature representations into class logits, employing Batch-Normalisation (BN) to stabilize the acti- vations. To act as a stochastic regularizer, a Dropout layer is strategically inserted between the two convolutional layers. By randomly zeroing hidden units during training, this layer prevents feature co-adaptation and forces the network to learn robust, distributed representations. Consequently, it mitigates overfitting to high-dimensional noise within the dense feature maps and ensures that the final pixel-wise classification generalizes effectively across diverse spatial contexts. Let cls denote the number of target semantic classes, W1 and W2 represent the weight matrices of the first and second convolutional layers respectively, and ϕ denote the Rectified Linear Unit (ReLU) activation function.

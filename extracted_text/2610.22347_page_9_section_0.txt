Adaptive denoising has been explored at frame and chunk gran- ularity (Le et al., 2026; Shi et al., 2026). To isolate commitment granularity, we use the same learned verifier but accept an entire chunk when its mean predicted convergence gap falls below a threshold at each step. The threshold is calibrated on held-out data against the oracle criterion to match our mean denoising steps. As shown in Table 5, token-wise commitment achieves higher quality and lower Drift at comparable mean denoising steps. Chunk-wise acceptance cannot distinguish tokens that have already converged from those that continue to change, resulting in lower quality and higher Drift when all tokens share the same acceptance step (Figure 5). 5

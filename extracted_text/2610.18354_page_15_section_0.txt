C More Details about Experimental Setup C.1 Models Our experimental model portfolio spans 10 open-weight models with three size tiers, listed in Table C.1. Every model is served locally through Ollama. Every model call uses a sampling temperature of 1.0 and nucleus sampling (top-p) of 0.9. Every experiment runs on a workstation with 64 physical CPU cores (128 logical cores) and two NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Workstation Edition GPUs, under Linux 7.0.0-28-generic (x86-64, glibc 2.39) with Python 3.12.3.

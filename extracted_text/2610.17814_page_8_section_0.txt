The evidence spans three English-language event-admission cohorts and a smaller paired precision study. Broader collection periods, languages, and downstream clustering or timeline utility are directions for extending this evaluation. For multistage Web systems, the immediate implication is concrete: judge the pipeline on the inputs it receives, and make visible both the inputs it cannot score and the decisions it makes about the rest.

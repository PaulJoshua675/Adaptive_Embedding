REPRO has a number of hyperparameters governing population dy- namics, strategy selection, and exploration control. The values listed in Table 3 were arrived at through engineering iteration during framework development: we adjusted values when the opti- mizer failed to make progress, produced unstable output, or terminated abnormally, and stopped adjusting when the system behaved correctly.

Even two Transformers that represent the same func- tion can train differently. Our analysis characterizes exact AdamW equivariance, identifies the obstruction for standard block-local updates, and establishes exact restart equivalence through canonicalization of weights and moments. SAGGS and Soft SAGGS provide simple function-preserving corrections for pair-scale imbalance. The normalization layers of a Transformer, weight de- cay, and gradient clipping do not restore pair balance (Appendix H). A rebalance rescales each pair only as much as the pair is imbalanced, so rebalancing can be kept on throughout training. Across paired language- model experiments and checkpoint-resume workflows, these corrections recover the performance lost to this imbalance, in particular after an exact orbit edit. These findings show that the pair-scale imbalance introduced by a parameterization strongly affects training, whether training starts from initialization or resumes from a checkpoint.

Limitations While the original Transformer calculates a single loss at each time step to optimize the next best token to generate, TagInsert calculates a number of losses that is equal to the number of <U> tokens present in the target sequence at each time step. As a result, an extremely strict comparison between the two is not possible, due to this difference in how they have to be trained. Another limitation is brought by the gap in qual- ity between English and non-English data. The English datasets used in our CCG experiments are typically cleaner, larger, and more consistent than their non-English counterparts. This imbalance re- stricts the strength of cross-lingual conclusions that can be drawn. We trained language specific models, and used langauge specific pre-trained models, for the lan- guage specific experiments on German, Dutch, Ital- ian, but did not use or compare to multi-lingual models, which can be considered a limitation. We also did not experiment with POS tagging in non- English languages, as our main focus was on the more complex task of CCG supertagging, but eval- uating the approach for non-English POS tagging, for instance on free word order languages, is inter- esting.

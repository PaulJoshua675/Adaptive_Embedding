Formal theorem proving expresses mathematical statements and proofs in a formal language, allow- ing a proof assistant such as Lean to verify their correctness (de Moura & Ullrich, 2021). Search en- gines support the proving process by helping users and automated provers locate useful definitions, theorems, and lemmas within large mathematical libraries such as Mathlib (The mathlib Commu- nity, 2020; Yang et al., 2023; Mikuła et al., 2024; Shen et al., 2025; Ju et al., 2026). Existing retrieval systems, such as LeanExplore (Asher, 2025), LeanFinder (Lu et al., 2026), and LeanSearch v2 (Gao et al., 2026), address this need by identifying library declarations relevant to a target theorem or an intermediate goal. However, their limited use of multi-hop dependencies among declarations may miss supporting lemmas that share little lexical similarity with the query but occur in proofs of related problems (Shen et al., 2025; Gao et al., 2026). A key observation is that declarations relevant to a target theorem can serve as starting points for discovering supporting lemmas through multi-hop dependencies.

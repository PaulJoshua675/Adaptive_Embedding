Dexterity from Supervised Play. (a) A goal-agnostic play policy is trained by behavior cloning on human teleoperation data. (b) The learned policy interacts autonomously with the environment to collect observation–action transitions. (c) These transitions train an action-conditioned world model to predict future observations. (d) At inference time, the play policy proposes candidate actions, the world model predicts their outcomes, and model- predictive control selects the candidate with the highest goal-conditioned reward. The selected action is executed in the environment, and planning repeats from the new observation. Experimental results show DSP reaches within 0.6 rad on 91.4% of trials and within 0.4 rad on 72.7%, compared with 53.9% and 25.8% for hindsight BC. PPO reaches 86.7% at both thresholds after 344M counted frames, about 490 times the counted-frame budget. Real world experiment shows DSP is able to transfer to real world with matched performance. We further validate DSP on Push-T and visual in-hand manipulation, demonstrating that the framework generalizes across tasks and observation representations. We also provide detailed analysis to carefully examine the characteristics of the proposed algorithm and the impacts of the key design de- cisions. The code, data, and training details will be publicly available for reproducing our results.

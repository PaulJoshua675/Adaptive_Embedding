A.7 Environment variables and span weighting Reward services expose endpoints, timeouts, generation count (default 8), and reward and advantage weights. Span weighting reuses the current and old policy log probabilities from the clipped surrogate. Its sensitivity β is distinct from the KL coefficient βKL. A.8 Rewrite instruction The system message asks for exactly <answer>span 1 | span 2 | span 3</answer>. Before rendering and computation of image rewards, the trainer extracts the answer field, removes every delimiter, and concatenates the span text into one natural language prompt. The format reward still evaluates the structured output. A.9 Reward notes Advantages follow the hybrid rule in Eq. 3. During training, each group contains N = 8 prompt members that share one random diffusion seed. This controls renderer noise when comparing rewrites within each group. Groups draw seeds independently, and training and evaluation use fresh seeds across prompts and outer loops. Section 4.1 details the data splits and evaluation protocol.

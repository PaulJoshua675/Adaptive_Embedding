Error Type Question Error Reason Ambiguity Which product had the highest sales in 2018? The phrase “highest sales” admits multiple schema- supported interpretations, including the number of orders, total quantity sold, gross revenue, and discounted revenue. Because these alternatives can produce different product rankings, the query does not determine a unique SQL formu- lation. Unclear Intent For the lists created by the user who rated the movie “Pavee Lack- een: The Traveller Girl” on 3/27/2011 at 2:06:34 AM, what is the situation with their followers? The phrase “what is the situation with their followers” does not specify whether to retrieve follower counts, aggregate them, compare them, or rank the lists. The intended SQL operation is therefore indeterminate. Internal Text Contradic- tion List the movies that were released in 1945 and also released after the year 2000. The question contains mutually contradictory constraints that cannot be simultaneously satisfied under the schema. A single movie record has only one release date, making it impossible for a movie to be released in both 1945 and after 2000. Question–Schema Con- tradiction Who played the character named “Chanice Kobolowski,” and what is that actor’s email address? The schema supports identifying the actor through the characters and actor tables, but the actor table contains no email-address column and no related table provides this attribute. The requested email address therefore cannot be retrieved from the database. Missing Constraint What’s the percentage of coins that is higher than the price 1 hour ago in that day? List the names of these coins. The expression “that day” has no specified antecedent and leaves the required date unspecified. Because the historical table contains observations for multiple dates, the relevant time point cannot be determined. Missing Schema Premise List the powers of Hunter Zolomon. We intentionally removed 30% of the gold schema before- hand. Therefore, the schema does not provide a way to identify Hunter Zolomon or to link a character entity to their associated powers. There is no table or relationship connect- ing characters to superpowers, making the query unanswer- able under the given schema. Semantic Gap For user ID 3, what is their yearly av- erage number of impactful reviews? The concept of “impactful reviews” has no explicit or im- plicit semantic mapping in the schema, and no business defi- nition is provided. As a result, the metric cannot be grounded or computed from available tables or fields. Inexpressible in SQL Among the worst directors, which genres are they most likely to im- prove in over the next five years, based on their past movies and rat- ings? The database can identify the relevant directors and their historical genres, but determining which genres are most likely to improve over the next five years requires predictive modeling beyond the filtering, aggregation, sorting, and join- ing operations supported by standard SQL. External Knowledge Re- quired Which actor has acted in at least 2 films that were nominated for an Academy Award (Oscars)? Please list their IDs. The correctness of the query depends on external knowledge not represented in the schema, as Oscar nomination informa- tion is not stored in any table or field, making the condition uncomputable within the database.

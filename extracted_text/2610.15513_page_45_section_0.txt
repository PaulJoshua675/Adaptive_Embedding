C Strategy Library: Family Taxonomy REPRO maintains 58 individual mutation strategies organized into 14 semantic families, plus strategies synthesized dynamically at runtime in response to observed failure patterns. The family- level organization enables systematic exploration: when one strategy fails repeatedly on a stubborn behavior, REPRO can switch to an alternative family rather than exhausting near-duplicate strategies within the same family.

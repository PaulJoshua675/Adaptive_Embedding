A.7 ARTICULATED OBJECTS: GEOMETRY, KINEMATICS, AND JOINT SWEEPS The articulated track coordinates two coupled source representations (Figure 12): a Blender Python script authoring one mesh per link in world coordinates at the rest pose, and a URDF specification placing each link frame at its kinematic pivot. Denoting the rest-pose frame of link l by Tl(0) and its visual origin by Tv,l, a world-authored link mesh is consistent with the kinematic tree if and only if Tl(0) Tv,l xworld = xworld, Tv,l = Tl(0)−1. (6) During the build stage, the harness compares each link’s forward-kinematic bounding box at q = 0 against its authored mesh; any alignment discrepancy exceeding 1 mm or rest-pose interpenetration exceeding 5 mm fails the build with an exact origin correction, guaranteeing rest-pose geometric– kinematic consistency prior to dynamic sweep evaluation. The dynamic joint sweep then articulates the mechanism across its rest pose, the lower, midpoint, and upper limits of each independent joint, and eight seeded multi-joint configurations, resolving coupled followers analytically through the mimic graph. Interpenetrations exceeding 2 mm in any articulated pose (5 mm at rest) are flagged per link pair, while mobile child links separated by more than 30 mm from their parent at rest (2 mm for fixed joints) are flagged as unattached. A motion-direction check verifies that each joint’s displacement vector aligns with the planned motion semantics and proposes axis corrections when inverted. The VLM judge inspects the multi-pose articulation sheet alongside these geometric findings, while deterministic caps bound the score at 0.60 for dynamic collisions or unattached links, 0.30 for URDF schema errors, and 0.50 for missing pose sheets (see Appendix C.5 for kinematic coupling ablations). A.8 SCENES AND GRAPHICS Scenes. Scene synthesis executes a staged hierarchical pipeline prior to the main refinement loop (Figure 13a). Individual scene assets (up to eight, or four under tight budget thresholds) follow a progressive compute-escalation policy: each asset begins with a fast single-shot draft and deterministic check, escalating to a full interactive agent session only if automated repair does not resolve all findings; Blender-built hero assets additionally undergo VLM evaluation with a monotonic quality gate that retains a revision pass only if it strictly improves the asset score. Environmental terrains and spatial zone layouts are built concurrently, followed by a unified cross-zone composition session and deterministic scene-graph assembly. To guarantee timely completion on complex prompts, pre-round staging is bounded at 55% of the wall-clock budget, dynamically switching remaining pre-round stages to fast single-shot synthesis once reached.

For each of the 135 systems, we generate 4,096-step continuations from origins 512 and 4,096 on an unseen trajectory. PathFM and Chronos-1 independently continue 16 iid paths per origin; Chronos-2 and PANDA continue point forecasts. For each diagnostic, we take the arithmetic mean over all realizations in a system. Across the 135 systems, we use a geometric mean for segment analog and arithmetic means for the other diagnostics. Segment-analog ratios differ by orders of magnitude across systems because their calibration distances can have very different scales; geometric aggregation gives each system equal weight on a log scale. The smaller PathFM retains lower errors at all four forecasting horizons and on all four dynamical diagnostics, showing that the gains persist at similar model size. We obtain these gains with our general forecasting architecture and flow-matching objective, without adding architectural components or losses tailored to chaotic systems. PANDA uses specialized dynamics embeddings with random polynomial and Fourier features motivated by Koopman-operator approximations (Lai et al., 2026).

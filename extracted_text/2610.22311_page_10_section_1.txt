We present Velox, a training-free router that projects spatial memory into the current latent camera view and uses frustum slab overlap to modulate layer-specific block importance while retaining model-derived fallback and local temporal safeguards. At chunk 48, Velox accelerates attention by 4.36× and denoising by 2.43× with asynchronous geometry construction on one H200. Across all 158 WBench navigation cases, it remains within 0.15 API-free average points of dense LingBot-Fast- Cam and slightly improves the 19-metric overall average. These results show that region-level spatial correspondence can turn persistent world memory into a practical routing signal for long-horizon generation. 10

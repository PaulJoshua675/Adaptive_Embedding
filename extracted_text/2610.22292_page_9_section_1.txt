We introduced CATCH, and showed it is a practical and data-efficient way to make an LLM more honest. Trained on a few hundred targets and evaluated on Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct and Qwen3.6- 27B, CATCH enables models to learn an honest behaviour that robustly generalizes and transfers to different dishonesty manifestations. CATCH substantially reduces instructed dishonesty on MASK, raises the confession rate under game incentives in Among Us, and makes a trained-in hidden be- haviour easier to surface on AuditBench. General capability is preserved under CATCH, and this depends on the utility corpus: while off-policy corpora can degrade model’s utility substantially, on-policy data consistently retain it. 9

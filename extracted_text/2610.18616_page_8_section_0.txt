One program, which allows a single program per query, removes this process and recovers a tenth of the gain from Python (§3.3). The agent’s lead over predefined graph tools is largest on queries where both tool-based agents make many calls. On the 100 test-subset queries for which both Graph tools and Graph tools + code call neighbors at least ten times, Full DKGI leads them by 9.0 and 6.0 H@1 points, compared with 1.3 and 0.2 points on the other 610 queries. These 100 queries all come from MAG and Prime, and 85.0% of them have a program with scoring or ranking, a multi-hop traversal or a set operation, compared with 37.5% of the other queries. The request of Figure 1 is one of these 100 queries. The Graph tools agent misses its gold paper, which Full DKGI ranks first (Appendix I compares the two agents on two more requests). With a stronger LLM, the agent reuses earlier outputs more often and revises more of its failed programs. With GPT-6-Astra, the agent turns to programs earlier (Python makes up 31.4% of the calls between 20% and 40% of a trajectory, com- pared with 8.2%), 59.9% of its programs contain an ID from an earlier output (compared with 48.4%), and the next program revises a failed one in 92.2% of cases (compared with 88.6%). With GPT-6-Luna in Codex, the last two shares are 50.3% and 85.1%, close to those in Pi (Appendix G). Ranking is the largest source of remaining er- rors.

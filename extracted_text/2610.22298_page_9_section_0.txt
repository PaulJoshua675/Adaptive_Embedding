AI Consilium: a Benchmark of LLM Clinical Multidisciplinary Second Opinions A PREPRINT complexity used 0, 1, 2, and 3 to denote low, moderate, high, and very high complexity. Overall quality was a physician-reported global assessment of all consilium stages. To account for characteristic LLM errors, an additional rule was introduced. If the final output contained a fabricated clinically significant fact - such as an examination result, numerical value, date, diagnosis, complication, or treatment recommendation absent from the source documents and not designated as a hypothesis - the “Factual accuracy and sources” criterion was assigned 0 points. If such a fact served as the basis for differential diagnosis, the final conclusion, or management strategy, the corresponding criteria were scored no higher than 1. This rule ensured that critical factual errors could not be offset by apparent logical coherence, structural completeness, or formatting quality. Table 1: Prespecified evaluation criteria.

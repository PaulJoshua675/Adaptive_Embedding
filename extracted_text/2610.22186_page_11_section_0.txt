Then, by decomposing captions into modality-grounded events, EVA-Cap elaborates fine-grained GRPO rewards tailored to event completeness, character attribution, and audiovisual synchronization. Extensive experiments demonstrate that SFT on EVA-Data significantly improves OLMs to internalize event-centric knowledge. Furthermore, the subsequent GRPO optimization improves event-centric alignment while maintaining strong general captioning performance. These results suggest that event-centric alignment offers an effective path toward more faithful and temporally coherent audiovisual video captioning.

I IMPLEMENTATION VALIDATION FOR EXACT COALITION SHAPING We separately validate that the exact coalition path and the bounded zero-sum transformation in Appendix C are realized by the training implementation. This section is an implementation check rather than a policy-quality evaluation. Exact coalition path. A real-training smoke test executes the exact 31-coalition path for three steps with the same pr filter evaluator used by training. All three steps complete successfully. Why independent clipping is insufficient. Before the bounded projection was introduced, the implementation applied independent element-wise clipping to normalized centered credits. In the smoke test, the first two steps have zero clip hits and maintain the zero-sum residual at approximately 10−6–10−5. At the third step, only 2.86% of action credits hit the clip bound, yet X i zi max (49) jumps to 15.0. This directly confirms that independent clipping can severely violate the zero-sum identity even when the clip-hit rate is small. The final implementation instead solves the bounded zero-sum projection in Eq. equation 34. In subsequent long-running Singleton and CASI training diagnostics, the maximum zero-sum residual remains on the order of 2–3 × 10−5 even near clip-hit peaks.

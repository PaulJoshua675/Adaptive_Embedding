4.1 Personalization of MoE LLMs Load balancing during MoE pre-training spreads tokens evenly across experts, which discourages in- dividual experts from specializing in task-specific features. Following Bai et al. (2025), we there- fore adapt the model in two stages: we first tune the routers to find the experts relevant to person- alization, and then tune only those experts.

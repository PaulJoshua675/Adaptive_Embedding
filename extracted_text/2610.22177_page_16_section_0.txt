For π3, late-stage injection performs best, suggesting that decoder-side integration better preserves modality isolation when no explicit camera token is avail- able. For VGGT, middle-layer injection is generally more effective, likely because privileged cues can be fused earlier with geometry-aware tokens and further refined by subsequent cross-view transformer blocks. In both backbones, early-stage injection is consistently weaker, implying that shal- low appearance-dominant features are less suitable for privileged geometric guidance. Third, residual flow and gravity priors contribute at complementary structural scales. Residual flow primarily improves local dynamic consis- tency by emphasizing non-rigid motion components, while gravity provides a global orientation anchor that stabilizes scene-level structure. Their com- bination improves geometric coherence without changing the feed-forward inference paradigm. 5 Limitations A limitation of our current formulation lies in the use of rigid optical flow components. In principle, rigid flow primarily reflects the apparent motion induced by camera ego-motion, implicitly encoding information about the underlying translational velocity and angular velocity of the camera. Such signals can be highly informative for stabilizing geometry and improving motion-aware reasoning. However, most existing feedforward multi-view reconstruction frame- works, including VGGT, are designed for static-scene reconstruction and operate on unordered sets of input images, without explicit temporal order- ing or timestamp information. Under this setting, the rigid flow component does not provide a consistent notion of camera dynamics across views, and consequently offers limited benefit for reconstruction performance in our experiments. We believe that the velocity and angular motion cues embedded in rigid flow remain a valuable source of physical information. An important direc- tion for future work is to extend feedforward reconstruction toward stream- ing or temporally coherent settings, where sequential inputs and ego-motion estimates can be explicitly modeled.

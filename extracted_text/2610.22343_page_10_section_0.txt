We ablate state supervision during one-step initialization and autore- gressive rollout while retaining decoded-depth supervision in all settings. Table 5 shows that one- step state supervision consistently improves forecasting, providing a useful target for initializing local state transitions. In contrast, extending latent matching throughout the rollout degrades long- horizon performance. This may be attributed to context-dependent variations in GFM features that do not necessarily correspond to changes in scene geometry, as further analyzed in Appendix A.7. These findings support using state matching to initialize local transitions while supervising autore- gressive predictions through decoded geometry rather than enforcing a unique latent trajectory. 5

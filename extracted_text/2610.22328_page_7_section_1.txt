Omni IIE Bench [53] advertises editing sequences of up to 16 turns, but they average 4.35 turns, and only 9 of its 260 reach ten. The one benchmark we know of that is built around ten turns, LME-Bench [61], contains 100 sessions on synthetic images, and each ses- sion opens with a global edit (an art style, a black-and- white or sepia grade, or a change of weather or time of day) and includes three more later.

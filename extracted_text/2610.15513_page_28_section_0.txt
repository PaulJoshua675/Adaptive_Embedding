On GPT-4, the pattern repeats: the best HarmBench baseline (TAP) reaches 43.7%, the concurrent methods reach 64.0–87.8%, and REPRO achieves 100%. REPRO additionally evaluates on three targets not covered by any prior HarmBench evaluation (Qwen3-8B, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-8B, and Gemini-2.5-Flash), achieving 100% on all three and extending the benchmark’s coverage to model families released after the original evaluation. For further context, concurrent work on HarmBench reports high ASR under different operating regimes, including 100% on individual models. Within the OpenRT framework [38], which integrates 37 attack methods across 20 frontier models, several individual methods achieve 100% ASR on specific targets, and EvoSynth, a program evolutionary approach, achieves 100% on 18 of 20 evaluated models. “Jailbreaking to Jailbreak” [19], a multi-turn approach using a jailbroken LLM as the attacker, achieves 93% ASR against GPT-4o using up to 6 interaction turns per behavior. AJAR [12], a multi-turn agent-based framework, reaches 76% ASR against Llama-3.1-8B. REPRO is, to our knowledge, the only single-turn black-box method achieving 100% ASR across all evaluated targets on HarmBench.

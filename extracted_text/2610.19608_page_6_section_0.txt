4.4 Comparators beside the rule The latest wave alone came close. With three answers per cell from v0.4, it scored Brier 0.0292 and log loss 0.130. The pooled rate was lower in 5 families, tied in 1 and higher in 3 (sign-flip p = 0.28 on Brier, 0.44 on log loss), and the bootstrap interval of the Brier difference, [−0.0029, 0.0006], includes zero. Pooling the two earlier waves into the forecast added no accuracy that this design can distinguish from zero. The oldest wave alone did worse. With the three v0.2 answers per cell it scored 0.0346; the pooled rate was lower in 6 families, tied in 1 and higher in 2 (p = 0.031), with bootstrap interval [−0.0107, −0.0025]. v0.2 and v0.4 were collected about ten hours apart, so this gap measures how much three answers vary between sessions, and says nothing about age. The frozen question-history estimator, which shrinks each cell towards its brand’s rate, scored 0.0604, worse than the plain count of the same v0.2 answers. With the data held equal, light smoothing beat heavy smoothing, as the post hoc look at v0.3 had suggested. 4.5 The horizon series Two forecasts were frozen before v0.3 and never changed: question history and the v0.2 context model.

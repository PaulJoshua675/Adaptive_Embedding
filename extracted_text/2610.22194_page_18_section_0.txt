Capability taxonomy Deciding what data a round needs is easier when the benchmark already says what it is testing. Instruction following does say so. Its prompts carry constraints of known kinds, and a benchmark that groups them lets a weak group be read directly off the scores, after which the agent can raise that group’s share, label existing rows by the capability they teach, or ask for new rows aimed at it. Most domains do not come with such a grouping, and we did not want a system that only works where one exists. The meta agent therefore builds and maintains the grouping itself.

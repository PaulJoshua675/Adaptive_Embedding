Characterising the Difficulty of Long-Context Reasoning A EXISTING LONG-CONTEXT DATASETS We provide a brief description of the candidate sources from which we collect our data. Table 3 lists the sources we retain. We exclude BABILong, ChatQA2, ETHIC, L-CiteEval, LOFT, LongCite, LongMIT and LongReason. BABILong (Kuratov et al., 2024) is a benchmark designed to test language models’ ability to rea- son across facts distributed in extremely long documents. BABILong includes 20 reasoning tasks, including fact chaining, simple induction, deduction, counting and handling lists/sets. Notably, this benchmark is extendable to any length, and splits up to 10 million token lengths are provided. ChatQA2 (Xu et al., 2025) is a Llama 3.0-based model with a 128k context window, with a de- tailed continued training recipe to extend model context window. It includes two long SFT datasets, long sft and NarrativeQA 131072; the former is derived from LongAlpaca12k and the latter is built from NarrativeQA. CorpusQA (Lu et al., 2026), a reasoning benchmark scaling up to 10 million tokens1, the bench- mark includes complex, computation-intensive queries with programmatically guaranteed ground- truth answers, challenging systems to perform holistic reasoning over vast, unstructured text without relying on fallible human annotation. DocFinQA (Reddy et al., 2024) is a long-document financial QA benchmark, which augments 7,437 questions from the existing FinQA dataset with full-document context, extending average context length from under 700 words in FinQA to 123k words in DocFinQA. DocQA-RL (Wan et al., 2025) is a challenging RL dataset for verifiable long-context reasoning. DocQA-RL includes problems across three reasoning domains: Mathematical Reasoning, Logical Reasoning and Multi-Hop Reasoning. ETHIC (Lee et al., 2025) is a novel benchmark designed to assess LLM’s ability to leverage the entire context. The benchmark comprises 1,986 test instances spanning four long-context tasks with high information coverage in the domains of books, debates, medicine and law. Subsets considered: Organizing, Attributing. ∞Bench (Zhang et al., 2024), an LLM benchmark featuring an average data length surpassing 100k tokens. ∞Bench comprises synthetic and realistic tasks spanning diverse domains, presented in both English and Chinese. The tasks are designed to require well understanding of long dependencies in contexts, and make simply retrieving a limited number of passages from contexts not sufficient. Subsets included: code debug, longdialogue qa eng, longbook qa eng, longbook choice eng. L-CiteEval (Tang et al., 2024) is a multi-task benchmark for long context understanding with cita- tions, aiming to evaluate both the understanding capability and faithfulness of LCMs. L-CiteEval covers 11 tasks from diverse domains, spanning context lengths from 8k to 48k, and provides a fully automated evaluation suite. L-Eval (An et al., 2024) is a benchmark containing 20 sub-tasks, 508 long documents, and over 2000 human-labelled query-response pairs encompassing diverse question styles, domains and input length. Subsets included: legal contract qa, narrative qa, natural question. LOFT (Lee et al., 2024) is a benchmark of real-world tasks requiring context up to millions of tokens designed to evaluate LCLMs’ performance on in-context retrieval and reasoning. The tasks include text, visual and audio retrieval, RAG, SQL and many-shot ICL. LongBench v2 (Bai et al., 2025) is a benchmark designed to assess the ability of LLMs to handle long-context problems requiring deep understanding and reasoning across real-world multitasks. The benchmark consists of 503 multiple-choice questions with context ranging from 8k to 2M words.

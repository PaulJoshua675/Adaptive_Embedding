H Statistical status and provenance Confirmatory outcomes retain their pre-specified observation windows, contrasts, and margins. Terminal- depth alignment in IV-A and removable-distortion normalization in V-A are exploratory. Threshold sensitivity and conditional predictor tests are audits, not replacements that retroactively redefine the primary outcomes.

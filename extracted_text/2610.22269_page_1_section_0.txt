X-RIC: An Agentic RAN-Intelligent Controller Benchmark for Enhancing Reinforcement Learning Adhwaa Al-Chaab†, Ayman Younis, Dario Pompili† †Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering Rutgers University–New Brunswick, NJ, USA E-mails: {adhwaa.alchaab, a.younis, pompili}@rutgers.edu Abstract—The O-RAN ALLIANCE introduces architectural flexibility through disaggregation and AI-driven control. While the RAN Intelligent Controller (RIC) enables centralized opti- mization, current AI approaches lack reasoning capabilities and explainability. We propose eXplainable RIC (X-RIC), a novel framework integrating agentic AI into O-RAN architectures. X-RIC combines reactive Reinforcement Learning (RL) with strategic agentic reasoning through: (i) automated Behavioral Cloning (BC) using domain heuristics for sample-efficient train- ing, (ii) a Telecom Language Model (TLM), a transformer-based reasoning module providing contextual abstraction and inter- pretability, and (iii) closed-loop co-adaptation within the Near- RT RIC control loop enabling continuous policy refinement. To ensure deployment readiness, we introduce BARX (Benchmark- ing Agentic Reasoning for RAN), a domain-specific evaluation framework assessing agentic control across latency, compliance, and determinism metrics. We validate X-RIC on SLA-aware network slicing in simulation and O-RAN-compliant testbed deployments. X-RIC achieves 89% operational cost reduction while maintaining SLA compliance, converging 75% faster with 50% fewer training steps and 46% lower variance. Index Terms—O-RAN, Agentic AI, Reinforcement Learning, Behavior Cloning, Resource Allocation.

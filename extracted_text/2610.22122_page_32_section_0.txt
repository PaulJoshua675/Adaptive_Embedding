UCB, EI, TS, TopUncertain, and OAE use the GeneDisco Bayesian MLP and retrain their surrogate after each round of new observations (Mehrjou et al., 2022). PoH. Many Needles selects the 𝐵unmeasured candidates with the highest estimated probability of exceeding the hit threshold (Rubbi et al., 2026). At each round, we refit a Bayesian MLP with two hidden layers of widths 128 and 64 on the measured data. For each candidate 𝑥∈𝑈𝑡, we obtain 50 MC dropout predictions and use their mean and standard deviation to form a Gaussian predictive approximation. The probability of exceeding 𝜏then serves as the acquisition score. OAE. OAE uses optimistic estimates for batch discovery of candidates with high responses. We use its released Bayesian variant, which combines predictive means and uncertainty to select each batch after aligning predictions with the effect direction (Pacchiano et al., 2023). DiscoBAX. DiscoBAX targets a diverse subset of candidates with high responses and uses fantasized observations to estimate information gain about that subset. We use the authors’ surrogate training and acquisition implementation, with two hidden layers of 128 and 64 units and dropout 0.2 to match TDAL and PoH in network capacity. We evaluate this implementation on the four single gene perturbation tasks (Lyle et al., 2023). UCB. Upper Confidence Bound combines the predictive mean with a weighted uncertainty term. It favors candidates with high predicted effects or high uncertainty to balance exploitation and exploration (Srinivas et al., 2010). 32

The proof is completed. 4.2 Gradient-Dominated Problems A broad range of nonconvex problems satisfy the gradient-dominance condition, including neural networks with one hidden layer and ResNets with linear activations [10, 22]. In this setting, the functional suboptimality is bounded by the squared gradient norm, which implies that every stationary point is a global optimum [36]. This benign property has motivated extensive analysis towards sharp guarantees of nonconvex stochastic optimization [10, 29, 50].

Kwai Summary Attention V2 Technical Report 3.1. Experimental Setup Pretrained model and variants. We start from the 128K checkpoints of the 1.9B and 4B Hybrid KSA models in the KSA report (Chu et al., 2026). The 1.9B backbone has 24 multi-head attention (MHA) layers; the 4B backbone has 36 grouped-query attention (GQA) layers with 32 query heads and eight KV heads. Both interleave three KSA layers with one Full layer and insert one Summary after every 𝑘𝑠= 8 text tokens. We evaluate two successive variants: C-KSA+Full compresses remote Summary KV in KSA layers; C-KSA+S-KSA further replaces the Full layers with S-KSA. Although C-KSA supports layer-specific compression, each evaluated configuration uses a common 𝑘𝑚across its C-KSA layers. Unless otherwise specified, all experiments use the backbone and module configurations in Table 2. Baselines and training data. We reuse the KSA report’s from-scratch results at 1.9B and continual- pretraining results at 4B. The baselines include Full, Hybrid SWA, Hybrid SCA, Pure KSA, and Hybrid KSA at both scales, plus Hybrid GDN at 1.9B and Hybrid Linear at 4B. Hybrid KSA serves as the parent-model reference for capability retention, while Full provides a dense-attention reference. We report module-only and full-parameter training budgets separately. A continuation study found no consistent RULER improvement for 1.9B Hybrid KSA under the tested recipe and budgets (Table 11), supporting our use of its original checkpoint as the capability reference. Adaptation reuses KSA’s training corpus and data mixture, with data sources and mixture proportions detailed in the original report. We use a fixed context length of 128K.

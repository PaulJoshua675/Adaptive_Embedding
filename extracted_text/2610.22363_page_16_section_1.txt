We initialize the video backbone from Wan2.2-Fun-A14B-Control and train the base transformer weights, camera adapter, action adaptations, and MANO head. The video VAE, text encoder, and pretrained action codec remain frozen. Training uses bfloat16, fully sharded data parallelism, and gradient checkpointing. The final unified training recipe uses 16 H100 GPUs, one clip per GPU, and a global batch of 16. Clips contain 81 frames at 16 fps and 848 × 480 resolution. We use AdamW with (β1, β2) = (0.9, 0.999), weight decay 0.01, and a backbone learning rate of 10−5, held constant after 200 warmup updates. We clip video gradients at norm 1 and use an exponential moving average with decay 0.999 for evaluation. We train the model on 16 H100 GPUs for approximately 450 hours in total. Training-mode exposure. The single-mode and unified ablations use the same architecture, ini- tialization, data mixture, and total number of optimizer updates. Single-mode training assigns every clip to its designated mode. Unified training uses the mixture in Appendix A.3: 20%, 50%, and 30% of latent positions are assigned to PERCEIVE, PREDICT, and PLAN in expectation. The whole-clip branch accounts for 10%, 25%, and 15% of clips, respectively; the other 50% use independently sampled modes across time. These are sampling probabilities, so matching total updates does not match the exposure to any one complete mode. Action supervision uses available annotations in every mode, while video flow matching supervises unobserved RGB positions. The shared trans- former weights receive gradients from both objectives, connecting action reconstruction and video generation across the sampled tasks. Sampling. PERCEIVE uses one action prediction from the clean video at zero noise. PREDICT and PLAN use 50 denoising steps, with the high-/low-noise expert boundary at τ = 0.9. Text guidance is 6 for the main PREDICT comparison and 2 for PLAN evaluation. Observed initial and goal latents stay fixed throughout sampling; Appendix D.4 specifies their encoding. Paired comparisons use matched inputs and seeds. D DATASET AND EVALUATION PROTOCOLS Data and preprocessing. We train on the training splits of EgoDex, EgoLive, and ARCTIC. We apply field-of-view augmentation during training. Evaluation protocol. Methods receive temporally aligned source observations through their na- tive interfaces. Dataset means are macro-averaged with equal weights. The following sections specify detection, joint association, and image metrics; Appendix E gives the PLAN protocol and statistical analysis.

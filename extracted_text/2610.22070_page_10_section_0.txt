We present qualitative results in Fig. 5, with examples from StreamArena (HR) and OVO-Bench (EPM) shown in the top and bottom rows, respectively. We compare the results from MaLoW (Qwen3.5-9B) with VST-7B, VideoChat3-4B, and MOSS-VL-realtime-11B. Overall, MaLoW produces more accurate answers than the baselines on memory-intensive tasks, demonstrating the effectiveness of its history internalization mechanism. Notably, samples 1, 2, and 3 demonstrate that MaLoW can recall the details from 16 minutes, 19 minutes, and 29 minutes ago, respectively. Sample 5 shows that MaLoW can continuously update its memory and avoid being misled by obsolete cues. Sample 6 further demonstrates that the internalized memory complements the current observation, enabling the model to recover relevant information even when the current visual evidence is occluded.

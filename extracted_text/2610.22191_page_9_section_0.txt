Scaling of the two edges. The two edges of the win- dow have different scaling variables, and Fig. 5 isolates them: plotted against βN the rising edges of the curves for N = 250 to 4000 collapse, plotted against β √ N the falling edges collapse. The window opens as √ N, as predicted by (17); at N = 250 it is a factor of four wide, at N = 4000 a factor of ten. The condensation is a REM transition. Figure 6 tests Proposition 3 directly. Simulations at different sizes, dataset sizes and qualities, once plotted against x = β p N(1 −r2), fall on the REM participation ra- tio ΦM(x) with no adjustable parameter, including the r = 0.8 series whose spread differs from the others by a factor of two. The departure of Meff from M sets in at x ∼1, as the small-x expansion of Proposition 3 says, and the condensation is essentially complete beyond xc = √ 2 ln M, where Meff/M is 0.2–0.3 at these val- ues of M. The memorization edge of an attention layer is therefore not a property of the particular dataset but a universal feature of a softmax over M exchangeable keys: βhi depends on the data only through the condi- tional spread p (1 −r2)/N of the within-class overlaps.

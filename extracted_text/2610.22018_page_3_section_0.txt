Structured errors. The dominant failure is default-label collapse, not random confusion. Thuluth is over-predicted in 11 of 12 numeric-prompt runs; closed/API models choose it 2.05–2.93× its true frequency, and Qwen3-VL-32B reaches 3.47×. This persists after rebalancing: every numeric run has one label taking at least one-third of predictions, rising to 52.9% for GPT-4o and 62.8% for Qwen3-VL-32B. False Thuluth predictions most often come from true Diwani (40.9%), then Naskh (21.5%) and Nasta’liq (16.4%), suggesting both visual-neighbor confusion and an uncertainty default. Diwani remains the clearest casualty. GPT-4.1 recalls only 1.8% of Diwani images, while Qwen3- VL-32B and Gemma 4 12B-IT recall only 6.5%; InternVL3.5-38B reaches 56.2% recall but only 30.5% precision. GPT-5.6 Terra is the only model with both non-trivial recall and stronger Diwani F1 (0.549).

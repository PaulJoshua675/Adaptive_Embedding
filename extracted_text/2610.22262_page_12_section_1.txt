We presented a controlled proof of concept for topology-inspired fine-tuning in wind-field SR. Fine-tuning with the reconstruction loss alone worsens all three mean PD distances; adding gradient- magnitude and fixed-pair supervision reverses this, lowering them by 25–31% relative to the pretrained CNN and 32–35% relative to the matched control while retaining about half of the control’s PSNR gain. Merge-tree distance improves on average but much less consistently, and different PD distances can prefer different reconstructions, so topological quality is descriptor- and distance- dependent. Within this single-model, single-region setting, the loss study shows that gradient supervision mainly improves persistence agreement whereas fixed-pair supervision gives the most consistent joint PD and MT improvement, pointing toward hierarchy-aware and spatially aware supervision and evaluation as the next steps.

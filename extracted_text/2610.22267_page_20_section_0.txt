A.4 Hyperparameter Configuration Tab. S2 summarizes the hyperparameters used for each backbone in all main experiments. The pooling window w is set larger for SD3 to preserve finer spatial structure, consistent with the ablation in Fig. 5. The fidelity weight λ provides a transparent diversity–quality trade-off and can be adjusted independently of the other hyperparameters; its effect on the trade-off is reported in Fig. 5. The batch size B follows the protocol of [13]: B=5 for SD1.5 and SD3, and B=3 for SDXL. NFE denotes the number of function evaluations used by the sampler: 50 steps for SD1.5 and SDXL (DDIM), and 28 steps for SD3 (Euler), following each model’s default configuration.

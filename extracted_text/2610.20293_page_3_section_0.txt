The methodological gap in our setting is the counterfactual object on which cross-context within-set credit can be computed. Slate decomposition relies on an additive conditional-mean reward model, whereas retrieved candidate pools in our setting are merged and jointly processed by a nonlinear, capacity-limited downstream system. COMA marginalizes one action against a fixed configuration of the others, and a standard difference reward uses a chosen removal or replacement counterfactual. Neither single-context construction, by itself, provides the family of coalition contexts needed when the same retrieval action contributes differently across predecessor coalitions S. Our distinction is therefore not the Shapley weighting itself, but the snapshot-grounded construction of comparable coalition evaluations for parameter-shared LLM retrieval actions, without requiring an agent-wise critic or a factored value model. 2.3 VALUE-AWARE RETRIEVAL FOR SPONSORED SEARCH Sponsored-search retrieval has evolved from query substitution and dense retrieval toward genera- tive and value-aware rewriting. Prior work includes relevance–revenue trade-offs (Radlinski et al., 2008), diversity-aware RL rewriting (Mohankumar et al., 2021), hybrid generative and dense re- trieval (Mohankumar et al., 2023), contextual retrieval (Grbovic et al., 2015), and value-aware LLM rewriting (Zhang et al., 2026). Our focus is complementary: these lines improve how rewrites are produced or scored, whereas we study within-set credit after several rewrites are issued jointly and their candidate pools are merged, deduplicated, ranked, and selected together. In this capacity- limited regime, a response-level outcome does not by itself determine attribution among parallel actions, and an action’s marginal value need not be invariant to the surrounding action set. 3 METHOD We study policy optimization for jointly generated retrieval actions under set-level feedback. CASI (Credit Assignment via Set Intervention for Agentic Retrieval) constructs alternative coalition evalu- ations within each realized action set while holding replayable snapshot components fixed.

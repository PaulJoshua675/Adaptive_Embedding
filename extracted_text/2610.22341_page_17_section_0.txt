In the connectivity gate, the ground-contacting component with the largest face count defines the primary support structure, and any disconnected component is reported alongside its nearest supported neighbour and the exact 3D translation vector that closes the gap. A contact ledger tracks all planned part attachments with their measured clearances while enforcing structural support at the global assembly level. Although exported meshes are evaluated in the canonical Y-up frame, all corrective fix hints are projected back into each runtime’s native authoring frame: a seat floating by 12 mm receives a corrective translation of (0, 0, −0.012) m in Z-up Blender Python and (0, −0.012, 0) m in Y-up Three.js (Figure 9). Articulated diagnostics output URDF joint origins and axes directly, and scene diagnostics emit native scene-graph API instructions. A.4 AUTOMATED JUDGMENT Evidence payload and response schema. A single payload builder serves in-loop refinement, post-run evaluation, and judge calibration (Figure 10). The judge receives the request, plan digest, acceptance checklist, measurements, gate findings, and track telemetry, together with up to five labelled multi-view montages, detail crops, and—when connectivity errors occur—cross-sectional slices, with montage order randomized per sample to remove positional bias. On object tracks, a passing connectivity gate prohibits unverified visual claims of floating or interpenetrating parts. The judge answers in a strict JSON schema at temperature 0.2, stating its evidence, strengths, issues, defect votes, and checklist verifications before assigning criterion scores and up to six prioritized improvement instructions; deterministic criteria such as silhouette IoU are injected by code. Score aggregation and deterministic vetoing. For round t, sample scores for each rubric criterion k are averaged into ¯s(t) k , while checklist verifications and binary defects are aggregated by majority vote. Any VLM-voted structural defect is automatically vetoed—incurring neither penalty nor cap—whenever the corresponding deterministic gate executed, passed without error, and recorded no penetration warning of 8 mm or deeper.

C.2 GSPO RL CONFIGURATION Our RL system is built on the asynchronous Slime framework (Zhu et al., 2025), augmented with custom training logic for online desktop interaction. Each rollout collection contains 8 prompts and samples 8 trajectories per prompt, yielding 64 rollout samples per collection. We use a maximum of 50 GUI interaction steps, a rollout temperature of 0.5, and a maximum response length of 10,240. Tasks are dynamically sampled according to an exponential moving average of their success rates. SGLang (Zheng et al., 2024) serves as the rollout backend, while Megatron (Shoeybi et al., 2019) is used for policy training. The RL objective is Step-balanced GSPO, with disabled KL divergence and equal weights of 1.0 for GUI and code steps. The full configuration is listed in Table 11. C.3 ONLINE RL SAMPLING, SEGMENTATION, AND REWARDS Uniform task sampling is inefficient for online CUA training because many rollouts are spent on tasks that are either already solved or still out of reach.

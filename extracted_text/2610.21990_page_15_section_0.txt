Mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) benchmarking results for semantic segmentation under three input configurations, well-lit, low-light, and enhanced low-light maps, on the PST900 [8] (left) and CART [25] (right) datasets, highlighting the impact of illumination conditions and enhancement strategies on semantic segmentation performance. training processes are conducted with 40k iterations employing a cosine annealing learning rate scheduler with an initial 3 × 10−4 learning rate which drops to 1 × 10−7 by the end of training. Observations point out that the degradation of scene illumination severely bottlenecks semantic parsing. Across both the PST900 [8] and CART [25] datasets, processing plain low-light RGB in- puts results in substantial performance drops, yielding mIoU scores of only 0.6614 and 0.5246, re- spectively. This stark decline compared to the well-lit baselines underscores the necessity of the pre-segmentation enhancement strategy. By routing the degraded inputs through the independently trained enhancement module, the network successfully recovers critical scene context, elevating the segmentation performance to 0.7010 and 0.5711 on the two datasets. While this enhancement bridges a significant gap, it does not fully restore the optimal original well-lit RGB performance (0.7855 and 0.7275). Further improvements. In the context of VesperMUSE, to aid the semantic parsing process, LLIE is conducted on the low-light images with thermal being brought as an extra representation. The in- frared imagery is later employed [9] for the segmentation training as well, since it provides relevant cues for both tasks. To make the current approach more robust, several questions need answers in order to move forward: How to integrate more structural information if thermal maps do not provide all that is needed? How will the information be fused in such a way that if specific scenarios might require more modalities, such as Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) [29], or event cameras, the network will structurally support that without architecture modifications?

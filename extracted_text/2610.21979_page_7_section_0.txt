We retain outcome completion and SCR while separately disabling consensus feedback (𝜆= 0) or omission routing. These interventions assess UAP’s feedback and delivery rules; Appendix C.2.1 gives their definitions and detailed comparisons. SCR allocation controls. To isolate the contribution of token placement, we preserve each eligible block’s coefficient values and selected-token count while randomizing their positions. Aligned SCR exceeds random support in mean score on 12 of 13 splits and circular shift on ten (Table 2(b)). Random support and circular shift exceed uniform allocation on two and three splits, respectively. These comparisons support alignment-informed placement beyond nonuniform weighting alone; Appendix C.2.2 gives the protocols and detailed results.

The evaluation supports diagnostic utility in some dimensions while exposing weaker calibration and a limited dependency test set. 5.7 Evolution Mapper evaluation The separate evolution-map study covers Transformers, generative adversarial networks, and ImageNet/CNN architectures. Each topic uses a Connected Papers reference set of 41 publications and a canonical seed paper. A model judge assesses coverage, citation relationships, network similarity, and additional value on 0–10 scales. Connected Papers is an exploratory similarity reference, not verified ground truth for direct citation edges or technical inheritance [8]. Accordingly, the scores below are comparative judgments rather than measured citation precision. The reported overall mean is 6.9/10, with ImageNet/CNN obtaining the highest topic score. Additional-value ratings average 8.7/10, while coverage and network similarity average 6.2/10. The broader graphs can help exploration yet omit influential papers or contain weakly relevant neighbors. These findings motivate better discovery and relation validation; they do not establish universal superiority over the reference tool.

However, in DiT-based models, fixed posi- tional encodings and sequence-length constraints make such direct concatenation non-trivial [3,18]. To address this architectural difference, we compute attention along two parallel paths: (i) the original scene query–key–value path and (ii) a subject-referenced path using the scene query with subject key and value em- beddings. The resulting attention outputs are then combined, enabling implicit identity referencing without modifying the positional encoding structure. De- spite the architectural adaptation, the core semantic control mechanism remains intact. As shown in Fig. 25 and Tab. 10, our method consistently improves align- ment, demonstrating its applicability to DiT-based diffusion models.

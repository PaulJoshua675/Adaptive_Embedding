Aggregating the capabilities of separately tuned specialists does not require training-based on-policy distillation when they share a base model, and the aggregate can be more accurate on average than any of its sources. For a linear layer the distillation objective is least squares, its solution is exact given the second moments of each teacher’s inputs, and those are obtained by letting the teachers read a few hundred of their own answers, at the cost of forward passes. What those answers contain decides the hard problems: removing a few looping answers, which carry a large share of the tokens, lets the fused model match its mathematics specialist in the same format. 10

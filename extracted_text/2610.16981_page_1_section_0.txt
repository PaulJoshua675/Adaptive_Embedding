Tracing Money Flows in the Multi-Token Cryptocurrency Network Natkamon Tovanich1[0000−0001−9680−9282] and C´elestin Coquid´e2[0000−0001−8546−6587] 1 TU Wien, Vienna, Austria natkamon.tovanich@tuwien.ac.at 2 UTINAM Institute, UMR 6213 CNRS, Universit´e Marie et Louis Pasteur, Besan¸con, France coquide31@gmail.com Abstract. Tracing money flows in cryptocurrency transaction networks is challenging because funds can move across accounts, transactions, and token layers. We propose a transaction-aware graph in which nodes represent account–asset states within transactions, while edges encode observed transfers, inferred asset transformations, and temporal inter- transaction sequences. Applied to Aave borrow–repay flows, we com- pare graph construction and edge-pruning strategies and extract shortest paths using a heuristic weighting. Our results reveal a trade-off between reachability and interpretability and uncover frequent token sequences underlying how borrowed assets are transformed before repayment. This work lays a foundation for general, interpretable multi-token flow tracing. Keywords: cryptocurrency flow tracing, transaction networks, decen- tralized finance, blockchain analytics 1

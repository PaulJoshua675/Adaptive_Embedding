The closed forms of Section 3.3 follow from the structure of the objective. The exact ternary step (Eq. 1). Fix a support S and codes t that vanish off S. Minimising ∥w −at∥2 over a gives a = t⊤w/|S| and leaves the error ∥w∥2 −(t⊤w)2/|S|, which is smallest when every code carries the sign of its weight; hence a⋆(S) is the mean magnitude on S and the error is ∥w∥2 − (P i∈S |wi|)2/|S|. Among supports of a given size k the sum is largest on the k largest magnitudes, so the optimal support is a prefix of |w| sorted in descending order, and the joint optimum over (a, t) is one sort and a scan over k, in O(n log n): Yin et al. (2019) give the sort-and-cumulative-sum formula for a power-of-two scale, and Hou & Kwok (2018) the exact solver for a real scale under a diagonal-Hessian weighting, which reduces to this objective when the weighting is uniform. TWN’s threshold (Li et al., 2016) is the approximation to S⋆that a Gaussian or uniform assumption on the weights yields. The same scan over an L-level grid places the integer grids exactly, where LeanQuant and NeUQI search over candidate grids (Zhang & Shrivastava, 2025; Lin et al., 2026). The writer step. When the sweep reaches a writer vector, input coordinate j, it quantizes the compensated vector w′ j and applies the optimal-brain-surgeon update to the coordinates not yet quantized (Hassibi & Stork, 1993; Frantar & Alistarh, 2022; Frantar et al., 2023). The increase in the objective is ∥w′ j −ˆwj∥2/[H−1 F ]jj, with HF the Hessian restricted to the unquantized coordinates, because all m output rows share H and the denominator is a scalar.

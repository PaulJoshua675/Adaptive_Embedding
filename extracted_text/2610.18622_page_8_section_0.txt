4 Results Since each document is represented by one or more chunks, structural segmentation substantially increases the index size (Table 2). Compared with whole-document indexing, the Hierarchical Chunks representation increases the number of indexed units by a factor of 13 on NormasTCU (from 14,469 to 192,955) and 9 on Ulysses-RFCorpus (from 105,669 to 971,044). Even the Core Chunks representation indexes considerably more units than the whole-document baseline, by a factor of 5 on NormasTCU and 4 on Ulysses-RFCorpus.

The average paired advantage of character TF–IDF is 5.7 percentage points over BM25 and 6.4 over word TF–IDF. It outperforms BM25 in 79 of the 80 selected galleries (the remaining draw ties). The consistency across reference selections indicates that character-level features capture useful lexical variation in these task paraphrases. The first-listed-gallery result would have obscured the sensitivity: on that one deterministic gallery, Word+Char reaches 63.8% accuracy and character TF–IDF 62.9%, but in repeated sampling their averages reverse. Benchmark reports that rely on a single arbitrary paraphrase can therefore change their ranking without changing the model code or task set.

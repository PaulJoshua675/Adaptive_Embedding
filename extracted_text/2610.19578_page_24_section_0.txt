Specifically, substituting the objective with Mean Squared Error causes the Recall@20 on the Books and Electronics datasets to drop to 0.0951 and 0.0236, respectively, falling significantly short of the default scores of 0.1007 and 0.0248. The fundamental mechanism driving this phenomenon lies in the rigid constraint imposed by Mean Squared Error on absolute Euclidean distances. In graph collaborative filtering, the absolute magnitude of a node representation implicitly encodes the interaction frequency prior of the corresponding item—a prior inherently absent for cold-start items. Forcing the mapping network to approximate the absolute magnitudes of the teacher model induces magnitude distor- tion, thereby misleading the model into overfitting spurious popularity signals. In stark contrast, the cosine distillation adopted by DualGCN strips away these absolute constraints, executing correlation alignment exclusively on the unit hypersphere, which fundamentally enhances the model’s generalization capabilities. Furthermore, incorporating the InfoNCE objective similarly fails to yield performance div- idends, even precipitating a severe decline in Recall@20 to 0.0143 on the Electronics dataset. From a mechanistic perspective, the primary objective of targeted distillation in this asym- metric framework is to achieve precise coordinate projection. Indiscriminately introducing negative samples generates extraneous repulsive forces. These forces actively disrupt the deli- cate geometric alignment of local features, ultimately culminating in the observed performance deterioration. 5.2.3 Effectiveness of Semantic Residual Design To further validate the architectural soundness of the student branch, we evaluate a model variant devoid of the semantic residual connection, denoted as w/o Semantic Residual. Empirical results indicate that eliminating this component precipitates a consistent and stable decline in cold-start performance across all three datasets. For instance, the Recall@20 metric drops from 0.1007 to 0.0905 on the Books dataset and from 0.1119 to 0.1010 on the Sports dataset. Given the inherent distribution gap between the static semantic space and the dynamic collab- orative manifold, compelling the network to execute a direct, deep non-linear mapping renders it highly susceptible to representation degradation and the subsequent loss of initial semantic priors.

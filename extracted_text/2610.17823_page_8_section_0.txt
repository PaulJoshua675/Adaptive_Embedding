Introducing sparsity through Entmax consistently improves rank- ing quality across all datasets, confirming the benefits of sparse probability mapping for recommendation relevance. However, its debiasing performance strongly depends on the dataset. In partic- ular, Entmax does not improve debiasing metrics on Yoochoose and even reduces them on ML-1M. While it increases Novelty@10 and Coverage@10 on Tmall, Yambda-50M, and Zvuk, these gains remain substantially smaller than those achieved by prior-aware methods. In contrast, Logit Adjustment consistently improves Novelty@10 and Coverage@10, indicating that explicit popularity-aware cor- rection effectively shifts recommendation exposure toward less popular items. However, these improvements typically come at the cost of reduced ranking accuracy or only marginal relevance gains. PR-entmax combines the strengths of both components.

Quantitative results include reported counts, measurements, estimates, rates, or statistical measures. Mentioning a topic or describing it without the quantitative result required by the criterion is not enough; a mathematical or statistical transmission model qualifies as a model in its own right. An accidental laboratory outbreak is transmission caused by a laboratory incident rather than natural disease transmission. It remains excluded even when the work reports outbreak measurements. Apply the criteria's special consideration for systematic reviews and meta-analyses; do not treat a narrative review as original research merely because it discusses eligible topics. When no abstract is available, judge from the title and keep the record unless the title itself shows a topic outside SARS-CoV or an excluded publication type.

The first 143 steps carry out the decoder’s step of Construction 4.3 on the read tape, the idle bank and the copy tape at their heads, and leave in the window clear requests and the next decoder state, whose counter again lies in the range of its phase, whatever the other window cells hold. They hand over at the 143rd step exactly when the step ends copy mode; otherwise the remaining S −143 steps bring the slot head back to slot 0 and change nothing else. Proof. A pass of MK,S,L is refined by a pass of an abstract machine that sees, of each head, the cell under it and what the pass does to it: the value it writes there, the cells it moves, whether it returns to cell 0 and whether its bank or tape is cleared. Every temporary is written before it is read, so the abstract pass from a decoder state does not depend on what the temporaries hold. The proposition is therefore a statement about the 1984 decoder states of its hypothesis and the eight values of the cells under the three heads, and evaluating the abstract pass in each of these 15,872 cases shows that it carries out the decoder’s step, acts on each head as the step prescribes, and hands over only in its last record. An empty slot changes nothing but the slot head. A state of MK,S,L starts the organism on a word τ when its active bank holds ρ ready, its read tape holds τ from cell 0, and its window holds clear requests and the decoder in start; the other banks, tapes and window cells may hold anything. Theorem 4.5 (Universal construction by the organism). Let K ≥2 and S ≥143, let X be an image of n ≤S records, and let τ be a description of X with |τ| ≤L. From a state that starts the organism on τ, after 30n + 5 + |τ| passes, (30n + 4 + |τ|)S + 143 steps: (i) the next bank holds X ready, with its bit head at 0, and the next tape holds τ from cell 0, followed by zeros, with its head at 0; (ii) the active bank holds ρ and the read tape τ, the window holds the decoder in start with z = 1 and the codon register 1111 in construct mode, and no other bank or tape has changed; (iii) the bank index has moved to the next bank, at the last step. From then on MK,S,L evaluates the records of X. Proof. By Proposition 4.4 it suffices to follow the decoder over whole tapes. The pass of start clears the next bank and the next tape and returns every head to cell 0. In construct mode each record of X costs 30 passes and is written under the arm, and the end codon costs 4 passes, the last returning the read head to cell 0 and switching to copy mode; so the arm has written the 26n bits of X in order from bit 0, moving on once per bit. In copy mode every pass consumes one bit and copies it, so after |τ| passes the copy tape holds τ from cell 0; the last pass, on the end codon, returns the arm and the copy head to cell 0 and hands over. Only that pass hands over, so every pass before it has S steps and it has 143.

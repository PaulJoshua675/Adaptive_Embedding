Local scores use a fixed bounding box enclosing the observed track with a 2.25◦margin. This region is shared across models; it is not centered on each predicted track. Field scores use available fields irrespective of whether the tracker detects a cyclone. Footprints reduce the stored forecast window in time by a maximum for wind or a minimum for pressure; the field pipeline does not apply the active-storm time mask used for the joint-distribution analysis below. Track extraction and matching. Tracks are extracted with TempestExtremes (Ullrich & Zarzycki, 2017; Ullrich et al., 2021), applied to the 12-hourly fields, in two steps. DetectNodes finds candidate storm centres at each time: minima of mean sea-level pressure enclosed by a closed pressure contour and a warm-core contour. We use the settings recommended for tropical cyclones in ERA5 (Ullrich et al., 2021): searchbymin: msl; mergedist: 6.0 closedcontourcmd: msl,200.0,5.5,0; DIFF(z300,z500),-58.8,6.5,1.0 outputcmd: msl,min,0; VECMAG(u10m,v10m),max,2.0;zs,min,0 StitchNodes then joins candidates at consecutive times into tracks. Its recommended settings assume 6-hour steps; we adapt them to 12 hours as Price et al. (2024) do for GenCast: range: 12.0; mintime: 54h; maxgap: 24h threshold: wind,>=,10.0,5;lat,<=,50.0,5; lat,>=,-50.0,5;zs,<=,150.0,5 range is the largest distance, in degrees, a storm centre may move between consecutive steps and stay in the same track; it is raised from 8◦to 12◦, which Price et al. (2024) chose to balance cutting off fast-moving storms against joining separate storms. The last number of each threshold clause counts time steps, so it is halved from 10 to 5 to keep the same 60-hour duration; mintime and maxgap are in hours and unchanged. Each extracted trajectory is matched to IBTrACS using its first four positions: all four timestamps must be present in the candidate IBTrACS track, and the mean great-circle distance must be below 500 km; the closest qualifying track is selected. Matching on the start of the track tests whether the model forecasts the right storm, not how far it later drifts, which the metrics measure. Members with no matched trajectory do not enter the location density. The track detection rate of storm s is Rs = #{members with a track matched to s} N , (2) and the tables report the mean and standard deviation of Rs across all storms of the cohort (125 or 53), not only those retained for the NLL. For the reference rows, the tracker is applied to the ERA5 or HRES-fc0 fields themselves, so Rs is 1 if a matched track is found and 0 otherwise. Location density.

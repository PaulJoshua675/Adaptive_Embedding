B Consistory+_+ & Framing Evaluation Details The ShotBench [15] includes a wide range of framing phrases across eight cat- egories (Tab. 7). To ensure a focused and rigorous evaluation of scene-specific structural variance, we intentionally restricted the scope to three primary cate- gories, excluding the remaining five based on the following methodological crite- ria: (1) Prompt Redundancy: Shot Framing descriptors (e.g., “single”, “2 shot”) primarily specify the number of characters, which is already inherently defined by the subject phrases in the storyboard prompts; (2) Redundancy Mitigation: Lens Size phrases (e.g., “wide lens”) are largely redundant as they are semantically en- compassed by the Shot Size category (“wide shot”); (3) Domain Incompatibility: Camera Movement is inherently applicable to temporal video sequences and is thus irrelevant to static image generation; and (4) Task Relevance: Lighting- related categories fall outside the scope of our work, as they primarily focus on stylistic or artistic variations rather than the structural and compositional elements that define scene-specific variance.

A result moves from generation to valuation through an exact trace; questions raised by the community return to the trace layer as new queries. The third layer is where people exercise the intellectual virtues that technology cannot take over (§8.3). 8.1 Three layers The first layer generates. LLMs propose hypotheses, constructions and candidate connections fluently and with measurable novelty (Si, Yang & Hashimoto 2024). Their outputs are the abductive raw material. The second layer keeps the trace. A candidate is re-expressed as bound records and multi-hop paths in a VaCoAl/PyVaCoAl memory, where binding is exactly reversible and every retrieval carries CR1 and every path CR2 (2026a). The Minsky paper describes what this buys: a trace readable constituent by constituent, a self-report faithful by construction rather than by ver- balization, and credit assignment by counterfactual unbind–rebind (2026f). The prosopography paper shows the same property in use: because the traversal is algebraically reversible, every ranking decision it makes can be reconstructed afterwards (2026g). The dial of §4 is available to the user of this layer, not hidden from them: Rescue mode for exact audit, Don’t Care mode for valuation, and the difference between the two read as information. The third layer values. Which result matters, which deserves a seminar, which should be taught to the next cohort: these judgements draw on collective tacit knowledge and stay with the community (§5.3). This is also where internalization and socialization happen, so it is where the tacit root actually grows. In the terms of virtue epistemology, it is also where people exercise the intellectual virtues that technology cannot take over (§8.3). Questions raised in the third layer return to the second as new queries. That loop is how contingently tacit structure gets externalized in practice (§4.6): the community asks, the sub- strate is given a new operation, and something that was structurally tacit becomes a stated, traceable fact. 8.2 Why the middle layer has to be exact The two-hop and reversal results point to a precise complementarity. LLMs compose facts reliably when the chain is in front of them and unreliably when it must be assembled inside the weights (Balesni, Korbak & Evans 2024; Berglund et al. 2024).

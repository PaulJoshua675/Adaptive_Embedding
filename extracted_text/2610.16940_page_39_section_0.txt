C.4 Parallel Construction Parallel construction follows the partition-build-merge strategy of Izbicki and Shelton [8]. The dataset is partitioned into equal chunks, each thread builds an independent cover tree, and trees are merged via binary reduction in O(log p) rounds. The merge algorithm aligns tree levels, parti- tions children into covered and uncovered sets, recursively merges covered children, and reinserts uncovered points. Exact maxdist recomputation after merge restores optimal pruning bounds. 39

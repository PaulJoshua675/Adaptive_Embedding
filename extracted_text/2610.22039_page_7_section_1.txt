This paper addresses a fundamental limitation of current soft- ware engineering agent evaluation: the lack of a task abstrac- tion that captures causal dependencies among multiple repos- itory changes. We introduce WEAVE, which recovers depen- dency DAGs from verified PR histories through repository- calibrated typed-locus coupling. The same graph representa- tion unifies task construction, dependency-conditioned par- tial credit (Graph Credit), and structured reward design, enabling consistent evaluation and optimization of long- horizon code agents.

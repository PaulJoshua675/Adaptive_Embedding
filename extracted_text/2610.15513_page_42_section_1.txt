We presented REPRO, a reflective evolutionary framework for automated LLM red-teaming that integrates semantic crossover, Bayesian strategy selection, structured failure analysis, and adaptive stagnation management within a multi-objective Pareto optimization framework. Unlike prior methods requiring gray-box access or manual template engineering, REPRO operates in a pure black-box setting with fully automated strategy discovery. Our experiments are consistent with principled algorithmic design, rather than auxiliary model strength, being the dominant factor in effective adversarial prompt optimization: a 4-bit 7B re- flective model matches a 4-bit 24B one across the evaluated targets, and the component ablation isolates semantic crossover and Bayesian strategy selection as the two mechanisms that carry measurable weight. Under our protocol, REPRO reaches 100% ASR on AdvBench, JailbreakBench, and HarmBench across 36 target models from 11 families, with a median of 24 target queries per behavior and only a compressed open-weight reflective model on a single consumer GPU. Recent concurrent methods (AutoDAN-Turbo, KDA, DAGR) reach up to 99% ASR on individual targets, and we are not aware of another single-turn black-box method reporting uniform coverage across a comparably broad model set. Cross-behavior optimization discovers single shared attacks reaching 100% ASR on the 50 AdvBench behaviors it was optimized against, showing that cross-behavior optimization is feasible on the optimization set.

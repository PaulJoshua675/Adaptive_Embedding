Taken together, the findings indicate that effective SAR representation learning can be achieved without relying exclusively on very large-scale pretraining. Compact CNN models, when paired with objectives and architectures adapted to SAR characteristics, can provide useful representa- tions with substantially lower computational requirements. At the same time, the task-dependent scaling behavior highlights limitations of single-image pretraining, particularly for temporal change analysis. Future work will therefore investigate pretraining objectives that explicitly ex- ploit multi-temporal observations, as well as extensions to interferometric SAR that incorporate phase and coherence alongside amplitude information.

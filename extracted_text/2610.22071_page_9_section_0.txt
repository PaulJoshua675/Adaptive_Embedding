Stream length and vocabulary size. UBE shortens the panel’s symbol stream by 31.6%. Using UBE in Bolmo’s fused symbol/flag output head would require (4+512)×2 = 1,032 identifiers. At the recorded hidden width, expanding the untied input and output tables adds 4.19 million parame- ters, or 0.0505% of Bwen’s measured total (Appendix B). For comparison, its existing parent-vocabulary embedding contains 621.3 million parameters, 7.47% of the model. The wider head changes both parameter count and per-symbol work.

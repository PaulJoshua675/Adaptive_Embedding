The second component of the finding is stakeholder contact cost. GPT-6 Astra’s delivered systems reach 0.8 on 36 of 95 tasks, but only 9 remain resolved once stakeholder contact cost is deducted; for Claude Opus 5.5 the figures are 40 and 14. Agents are not informed that questions incur a cost, and they ask as if questions were free. Stakeholder contact cost consumes 0.14 of the 0.70 uplift delivered by Claude Opus 5.5 and 0.22 of the 0.60 delivered by GPT-6 Astra. At the other extreme are models that rarely ask: Mistral Small 4 asks 0.7 questions per task and obtains 6% of the key facts. 5.4 Weak Models Frequently Score Below the Do-Nothing Baseline Finding 4 The weakest models score below the do-nothing baseline on 60–83% of tasks; on these tasks, delivering nothing would have been the better outcome. A score of zero is not the floor: a system that pays out claims it should have refused, charges the wrong party, or wakes the wrong engineer performs worse than the system it replaced. Figure 6 sorts each model’s 95 tasks at L3 into four score bands. Claude Opus 5.5 scores below zero on 5 tasks and Claude Sonnet 5.5 on 7, whereas Claude Haiku 4.5 does so on 79 and GPT-6 Luna on 63. Part of this reflects stakeholder contact cost, but much of it reflects the delivered system itself. Before any stake- holder contact cost is deducted, the systems delivered by Mistral Small 4 score below the do-nothing baseline on 51 tasks and those delivered by Claude Haiku 4.5 (thinking) on 55, compared with 1–4 for the three leading models. On 23 tasks, the system delivered by Mistral Small 4 caused more harm than the benefit produced by the reference solution. Extended thinking does not help: Claude Haiku 4.5 scores −0.33 without it and −0.35 with it. For a buyer, the practical implication is that a weak agent is not a cheaper version of a strong one; its expected contribution on these tasks is negative. 5.5 Model Performance Varies Substantially Across Scenarios and Task Variants Finding 5 On five of the 20 scenarios no model averages at least 0.5, and the same scenario with different key facts yields a different score.

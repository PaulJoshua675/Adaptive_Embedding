Decentralized LLM Workflows via Sequential Auctions 4. Bidding Strategies Crucially, our framework decouples the agent’s core capability from its market strategy. The underlying LLMs responsible for the state transitions (𝑠𝑡→𝑠𝑡+1) are utilized strictly for inference and remain frozen. The reinforcement learning process solely trains a lightweight MLP for each agent, which acts as the state-dependent, myopic bidding policy. To survive in an imperfectly competitive market, an agent’s MLP must solve two coupled learning problems at each step: accurately estimating the state’s true valuation 𝑣𝑖(𝑠𝑡), and determining the optimal shade factor 𝜉(𝑠𝑡) ∈(0, 1) to maximize its expected profit margin. The agent’s final bid is formulated as 𝑏𝑖(𝑠𝑡) = 𝜉(𝑠𝑡) · 𝑣𝑖(𝑠𝑡). For Vickrey and CCV, we enforce truthful bidding, 𝜉(𝑠𝑡) = 1. All agents share a common neural architecture for the valuation head, which predicts both the expected downstream value 𝑣(𝑠𝑡) and its standard deviation 𝜎(𝑠𝑡). The valuation head is trained via a standard Gaussian negative log-likelihood (NLL) loss to regress toward the empirical target 𝑃(𝑡) in −𝑐𝑖,𝑡.

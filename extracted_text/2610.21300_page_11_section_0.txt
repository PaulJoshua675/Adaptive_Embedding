Measures and statistics. For each prompt we determine whether two runs dif- fer in each of six fields: the label; the set of cited files; the cited passages, where each quotation is located in its source document after normalising white space and two runs agree if every quotation of each over- laps, by at least one character, a quotation of the other in the same document (a lenient, non-transitive rule, so the Passages counts are lower bounds); the exact quotation strings; the comment; and the output bytes. Files, passages and quotations are compared as sets; a call that returned no parsable JSON dif- fers from every other run in every field; between 88% and 100% of quotations were located in the source, by deployment (Supplementary Table S6); Experi- ment 1 compares raw model outputs without the pipeline’s verification rule. A prompt counts as dif- fering in a field when any pair of its runs differs; such counts cannot fall as runs are added. The prob- ability that two runs differ is computed per prompt over all pairs of runs and averaged over prompts, with 95% intervals from 2,000 percentile bootstrap resamples of prompts (seed 0). The normalised com- parison removes white space, punctuation and case before comparing. Between days, a prompt counts as changed when its label was the same in all runs of each day but differed between the days. Route pairs are compared with exact McNemar tests on the paired prompt-level indicators. Experiment 2 com- pares, for every document and item, the per-stage hashes of each run with those of the reference run. Software and data.

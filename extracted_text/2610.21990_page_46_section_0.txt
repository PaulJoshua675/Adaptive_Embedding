(3.65) Weighted Dice Loss. LDice(·, ·) is a region-based loss designed to directly optimize the overlap between the predicted segmentation and the ground-truth masks. Unlike pixel-wise classification losses, the Dice loss operates on global class-level statistics, making it inherently robust to class im- balance and particularly effective for small, sparse or weakly-represented objects. By incorporating class-dependent weights, the formulation further compensates for skewed class distributions while preserving structural consistency across object regions.

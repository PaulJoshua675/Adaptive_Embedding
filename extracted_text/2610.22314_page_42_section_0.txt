If you are giving what was asked, that is do_answer_question, not this. do_insight Express a new realization, connection, or pattern that just clicked. do_change_talk Express intention, willingness, or commitment to do something differently. do_go_deeper Reveal something you have not said yet. Share more detail, go beneath the surface. do_report_progress Share a positive update: something that went well, progress made, or a win. do_storytell Share an extended narrative or recount an event in detail. Concealing do_deflect Redirect away from a topic or depth without disengaging entirely. do_minimize Downplay the importance or impact of something. do_disengage Pull back with a brief, vague or low-effort reply: "ok", "idk", "maybe", a half-answer, or not really answering at all. Engaging do_agree Confirm that something the therapist said fits your experience or feels right. do_disagree Correct or push back on something the therapist said that does not match your experience. do_ask_clarity Ask the therapist to clarify something she said that feels confusing or disconnected. do_answer_question Give what the counsellor asked for, and only that: the answer, not the story around it. Most turns where you are asked something are this. do_ask_advice Explicitly request guidance, direction, or input on what to do. do_seek_validation Check whether your feelings or reactions are normal, appropriate, or reasonable. do_question_process Express doubt, confusion, or frustration about the therapy conversation or program itself. do_revisit Return to a topic, feeling, or moment mentioned earlier that still feels unresolved. do_resist Express reluctance or refusal to follow a suggestion or direction while remaining engaged. do_express_constraint Name practical, emotional, or situational limits that affect what feels possible. do_test_boundary Probe the therapist’s limits or challenge the relationship to test whether it is safe. do_set_boundary Establish a firm limit on what you are willing to discuss, do, or engage with.

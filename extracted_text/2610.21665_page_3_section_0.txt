Reported learning rates are therefore retained without rescaling. 2.2 Weight perturbation and population averaging Let θ ∈Rd collect the perturbed parameters. At each update, draw ξ ∼N(0, σ2Id) and retain this perturbation for every example in the batch. Both loss evaluations use the same inputs and targets. The baseline-subtracted estimator and update are bgσ,1(θ; B) = EB(θ + ξ) −EB(θ) σ2 ξ, θt+1 = θt −ηtbgσ,1. (2) Perturbed parameters are temporary; the update is applied to the unperturbed vector. A lower perturbed loss reinforces the perturbation direction. This is the simultaneous Gaussian WP rule used in the thesis and its reference study [4, 12]. Population WP averages complete loss–perturbation products from P independent perturbations at the same parameter vector [4, 10]: bgσ,P (θ; B) = 1 P P X p=1 EB(θ + ξ(p)) −EB(θ) σ2 ξ(p). (3) The baseline is shared, and population members are not retained as an inference ensemble. This is an average of products, not a product of separately averaged losses and perturbations. Independence gives the exact conditional identity Covξ(bgσ,P | θ, B) = 1 P Covξ(bgσ,1 | θ, B). (4) Increasing B and increasing P therefore act on different sources of variability: batch composition and perturbation realization. Population averaging reduces estimator variance; it does not remove disturbances before their propagation through the network. 2.3 Node perturbation and comparison protocol NP perturbs neuronal inputs or activities rather than every parameter. In the temporally extended formulation motivating the experiments, node perturbations vary across examples, and their correlations with presynaptic activity are accumulated before multiplication by a scalar loss difference [12]. WP instead holds one parameter perturbation fixed over the batch. NP supplies a reference mechanism for understanding this distinction; no additional NP experiments are introduced here. The shallow reference uses up to approximately 50,000 updates. The update-indexed extended comparisons display up to approximately 450,000 updates. The large-batch WP–BP comparison uses batch sizes from 2,000 to 10,000. The separate budget comparison also includes B = 1,000 and displays up to 22,500 epochs in its epoch-indexed panel. A separate ten-epoch comparison uses locally selected settings from Random Grid Path Search [4, 24]; these settings are not imputed to the long runs.

Peak memory covers only the resident denoiser and sampling tensors. 5 Limitations Figure 6 shows how imperfect geometry, uncertain materials, and missing observations limit asset quality. Large image–geometry discrepancies. In end-to-end generation, SUPRA-Tex uses geometry predicted by Pixal3D⋆. Large discrepancies in shape or pose between the reference image and the mesh can place projected material observations on incorrect surface locations. Correspondence filtering removes some unreliable observations, but cannot correct the underlying geometry, which remains fixed during material generation. Severe discrepancies can therefore prevent faithful texture alignment. Improving upstream geometry and camera alignment is therefore an important direction, but we do not evaluate corrected-geometry controls here. Hallucinated geometry on unseen surfaces. Upstream geometry generators such as Pixal3D⋆can introduce implausible unseen structures even when the visible shape matches the reference. These structures may deviate from shapes learned by the material generator, hindering coherent texture completion. Since SUPRA-Tex conditions on fixed geometry, it cannot remove these structures; their influence on texture generation can reduce final asset quality. 11

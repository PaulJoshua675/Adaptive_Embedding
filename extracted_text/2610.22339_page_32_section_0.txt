LEANGRAPHSEARCH Structured feedback for repeated failures. Compiler feedback includes a structured failure memory object in the {error msg} field. It contains the latest state fingerprint and occurrence count, together with summaries of recurrent states and their unsuccessful tactics, decla- ration applications, and modifications. Once a state has occurred at least twice, the feedback includes the following directive: DEAD_END_REPEATED: this formal state has now appeared at least twice. Do not repeat the same tactic, lemma, declaration, or proof modification. You remain free to change the mathematical strategy, rewrite the whole proof, make a different local repair, or issue a new Mathlib query; the controller will not choose for you. This structured feedback is supplied to both proof revision and reflection-time query generation. 32

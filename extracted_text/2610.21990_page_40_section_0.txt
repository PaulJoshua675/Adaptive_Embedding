Its role is to coherently combine the features originating from multiple modalities and to assess the unique information each one of them contains and how this information interacts with the other sets of specific data from the other modalities. To achieve this, the Fusion Encoder Block employs 2 modules: the Fusion Self-Attention Module which combines and selects the most relevant features based on the current context and a FFN which further refines the fused maps. The modules interact through skip connection which aid the gradient flow and normalisation layers that provide data distribution unity across the samples. The principal advantage of the proposed Fusion Encoder Block lies in its structural scalability to an arbitrary number of modalities, k ∈N. The only requirement is the designation of one modality as the primary modality, while the remaining k modalities are treated as auxiliary.

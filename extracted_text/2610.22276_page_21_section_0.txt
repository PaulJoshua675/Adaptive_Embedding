C.3 Scheduling and Distributed Execution To reduce padding, sequences within each global optimizer batch are sorted by expanded length Lq into microbatches of eight. To limit workload imbalance across devices, a longest-work-first assignment (Graham, 1969) distributes these microbatches across eight ranks by the cost C(B) = |B| max q∈B Lq + 0.25 X q∈B Vq, (19) where Vq measures visual-token work, and each scheduling round gives one microbatch to each rank. The recipe uses no cross-update buffer, concatenated QA sequences, training prefix cache, or image deduplication. With W workers, each microbatch uses W/Nactual times its weighted loss sum, so averaging across workers (Li et al., 2020) recovers Equation (7).

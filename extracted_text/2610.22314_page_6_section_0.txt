Additional details regarding pricing calculations are available in Appendix B. 4 MindSim Our patient simulator, MindSim, consists of two main components that were designed to address current limitations of mental health patient simulators: (1) clinically grounded profiles (§4.1), synthetically cu- rated to match to the help-seeking population using a pipeline built on clinical taxonomies that limits the bias and stereotyping of LM-generated personas (Cheng et al., 2023); and (2) a simulation harness (§4.2), in which the patient LM commits to a move from a clinical taxonomy, thus increasing steerabil- ity of diverse patient behaviours and reducing monotonic trajectories (Hoang et al., 2026). Each move decision and subsequent verbalisation is conditioned on the profile and on a session object that holds events, thoughts and agenda that brought the patient to the conversation. Before writing a response, the patient commits its move and closes agenda items through tool calls. Later sessions are generated from previous sessions and conversations, with the patient and counsellor LMs each carrying a shared memory of earlier sessions.

B.5 Hyperparameter Analysis The M-step steering has two hyperparameters: the total strength µ and the confidence thresh- old α that masks low-confidence tokens (Section 3.2.2). Table 15 sweeps them on Qwen3-8B over GPQA-Diamond (greedy, all other settings identical to the main experiments). Strength is insensitive. Across a 2× range of the total strength (µ ∈[1.6, 3.2]) the gain stays within 5.6–7.6 points with no monotone trend: µ = 1.6 and the default µ = 2.4 tie for the highest observed accuracy (56.6), and no setting collapses. This indicates that the KL-gradient signal (Section 3.2.2) is a robust direction—once the update points toward the optimized context, its magnitude is of secondary importance. The confidence constraint is what matters. The picture changes qualitatively for α. At α=0.05 the gain collapses to +1.0: with too permissive a mask, the steering vector acts on tokens where the optimized context expresses almost no preference, injecting noise rather than signal. At α=0.1–0.4 the gain is stable (+7.6 to +9.6, with the best value at α=0.2), i.e., the constraint only needs to separate confident tokens from noise, not to be tuned precisely. We conclude that the confidence mask, not the step size, is the load-bearing hyperparameter 29

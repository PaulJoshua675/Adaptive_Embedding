Figure 6 plots cos(∆θEP, ∆θBP) against the free-phase budget Tfree. Where the free phase has not converged the update correlates poorly with backpropagation’s, and the recovery is abrupt. The level each curve then plateaus at is a separate matter, set by γ and β together with the arithmetic (Table 3); here we are concerned only with where the transition happens. Across the TF32 grid we measured — γ from 10−6 to 10−1 at β = 30, and β ∈{3, 100} at γ = 10−5, 10−4 and 10−2 — every cell that reaches full fidelity lies between −0.20 and −0.10 at Tfree = 5 where measured, is at least 0.958 at Tfree = 6, and is above 0.99 by Tfree = 9. Neither γ nor β moves the transition within this range. At γ = 10−5 the cosine plateaus at about 0.993; below that the plateau falls to 0.978 at γ = 5 × 10−6 and never exceeds 0.35 at γ = 10−6, where the update is arithmetic- limited and there is no transition to full fidelity to locate. We therefore use Tfree = 14 throughout, well past the transition, and Tnudge = 6, above what the next paragraphs show is needed. Nudge strength. At the operating γ = 10−4 the linear nudge holds cos ≥0.99 from β = 3 to 103 and degrades only to 0.969 at 104; below β = 3 it meets the TF32 floor, falling from 0.95 at β = 1 to about 0.1 at β = 0.01, where float64 stays at 1.0000 throughout.

Predictive coding. Generative predictive coding typically places the image at the bottom of a hierarchy of predictions: Monte Carlo predictive coding samples it by adding Langevin noise to inference, at MNIST scale (Oliviers et al., 2024), and Langevin predictive coding samples a latent code the same way and decodes it, reaching CIFAR-10 at FID 113 (Zahid et al., 2023). In both, the image is the predicted layer of a prediction-error likelihood. In ours it is the input of a network whose scalar output is an explicit potential, and the force on it is that potential’s gradient. 9

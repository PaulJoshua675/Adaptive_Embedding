A device compiled into the same records writes the bytes the host emits under the arm until the tape is rewound, and under the copy head after it. Run on a recipe for its own records, P ∗then writes the host into the next bank, copies the recipe onto the next tape and hands control to the copy (Figure 3), as the organism does with its own code. The proofs reuse those of Section 6: a pass of the stored host is one step of the host followed by a device that agrees with the tape transducer on every run of P ∗. Construction 7.1 (The stored host). Let A ≥8 and w ≥1. The window holds, after the thirteen cells of Definition 3.1, a cell booted at 13, a counter cnt of w bits from 14 on, least significant first, a cell dest, a limit lim of w bits, the n = A+1+A2A state bits of HA in the order of Construction 5.8, a cell for each unit of HA, three cells for each level of the recursion of Lemma 3.4, and 2w + 9 cells for the device. The stored host SA,w lists the following records. (a) Startup, A2A + A + 2w + 8 records: for each memory bit c, c ←c ∨¬booted if its value in m∗ A is 1 and c ←c ∧booted if it is 0; then c ←c ∧booted for the bits of the counter, the halt bit and the bits of cnt, lim and dest; then five records copying ¬booted into RR, CB, RA, CT and RC; then booted ←1. (b) Step: the units of HA in the order of the netlist, each compiled by Lemma 3.4 into its own cell, which also accumulates its chain; the unit of the next value of a state bit writes that bit instead, with its last record. A unit compiled by the recursion holds Tht, Tht−1 and the conjunction of each level in the three cells of that level. (c) Device, 11A + 12w + 75 records: the device of Definition 7.2 on the state that (b) leaves. (d) Hand-over: the fewest empty records, at most seven, that make the image a whole number of bytes; then booted ←booted ∧¬h, where h is the halt bit, and three records copying h into RA, RC and HO, the last record. The window is the least 2α that holds these cells. The stored host S = S8,20 has 36,080 records and a window of 215 cells, and τS = describe(S) is the recipe of its image, its records in order read as bytes most significant bit first; Appendix B gives the layout of its window and the lengths of its image and of τS. Definition 7.2 (The device of the stored host). The device acts on the state y of HA after a step, the registers cnt, lim and dest, the read tape, the idle bank and the copy tape, each request firing on the value 1: (i) if ycwr = 1, the low eight bits of yRout, most significant first, are written under the arm if dest = 0 and under the copy head if dest = 1, that head moving on by one cell after each, and Rout is cleared; (ii) if ycrd = 1, then ceot is set if dest = 1 and cnt = lim; otherwise the eight bits under the read head, the head moving on by one cell after each, are loaded into the low byte of Rin most significant first, their sign bit is copied into the bits above them, ceot is cleared and cnt is incremented; (iii) if ycrw = 1, then lim ←cnt, cnt ←0 and dest ←1, and the read head returns to cell 0; (iv) the request cells are cleared. 19

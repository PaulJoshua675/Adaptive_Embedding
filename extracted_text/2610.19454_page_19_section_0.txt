General is calibrated from the General dataset in AuctionNet’s open-source repository: the generation baseline for predicted conversion probabilities (pValue) is 0.005, and budgets and CPA constraints use the full 48-dimensional configurations. Other auction mechanisms remain unchanged. For each environment configuration, the total traffic volume, initial budgets and CPA constraints, competitor- type composition, and pValue generation baseline remain fixed across periods. Each period uses the episode index to resample predicted conversion probabilities and their variability and within-period traffic curves, and to initialize native competitors’ random states, producing different market conditions. Both Sparse and General split episodes 71–320 consecutively into five market sequences, each with 50 delivery periods. D.2 Training Configuration For SFT, each role uses 3,290 demonstration trajectories generated by DeepSeek V4 Flash. Training uses a learning rate of 10−4, microbatch size 8, gradient accumulation of 2, and maximum total sequence length of 2,560 tokens. Planner, Optimizer, and Inspector are trained for 4, 3, and 3 epochs, respectively. Offline GSPO uses a learning rate of 10−5 for 5 epochs, 4 candidates per decision, sequence-ratio clipping to [0.8, 1.2], and a KL penalty coefficient of 0.1. Both stages use LoRA with rank 16 and alpha 32. Co-evolution uses AdamW at actor and value-model learning rates of 10−6 and 5 × 10−4, respectively. Batches hold valid settled decision nodes for the scheduled role. Rounds with valid nodes update the value model once and the actor at most four times; Appendix C.2 covers the first scheduled round and rollback. D.3 Inference and Action Handling During continuous delivery, responses are sampled at a temperature of 0.7 with thinking disabled. The context and response limits are 8,192 and 512 tokens, respectively. Top-p is not explicitly specified. Optimizer selects HOLD or a tCPA multiplier of 0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 1.0, 1.2, or 1.3. 19

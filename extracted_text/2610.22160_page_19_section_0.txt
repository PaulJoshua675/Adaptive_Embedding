AI Model TF September, 2026 A.6 Implementation Conventions The steering strength defaults to µ = 2.4 and the threshold to α = 0.1; the finite penalty is fixed at λ = −100. An excluded token therefore receives an additive logit change µλ; this is distinct from overwriting its final logit with −100. The same prefix is appended to separate KV caches for Ck and Ck+1. Contrast and masking are computed before any sampling temperature or top-p truncation. No context is carried from one test question to the next. In the stated update, rejecting a rewrite sets Ck+1 = Ck and makes the raw contrast zero. The confidence penalty in Eq. 7 can still affect decoding when µ > 0; zero contrast alone does not remove this constraint. In contrast, setting µ = 0 removes both the contrast correction and the penalty and recovers context-only decoding. This distinguishes a rejected rewrite from explicitly disabling the M-step. With K = 4 refinement rounds, the initial response and four steered responses form five candidates. Each M-step uses two target-model context streams, giving nine generation equivalents including initialization. This counts streams rather than exact FLOPs or latency and excludes external judge, backward, and rewrite cost as well as differences in context and response lengths.

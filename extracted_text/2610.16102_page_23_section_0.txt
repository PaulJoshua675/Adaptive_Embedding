It cannot replace the understanding it serves. 8.6 A complementary direction The three layers keep the LLM’s internal computation opaque and supply a trace beside it. A complementary route reads the LLM itself with hyperdimensional algebra: the Hyperdimensional Probe uses vector-symbolic encodings to project a model’s residual stream onto interpretable concepts and reports that this helps identify model failures (Bronzini et al. 2025). Exact, reversible binding of the VaCoAl kind may sharpen such probes. Both routes share the premise of this paper: the trace is the scarce good, and algebra is a way to make it. 9 Predictions, limitations and open problems The account makes six predictions that could fail, and it rests on limitations a reader should weigh before accepting it. 9.1 Predictions 1. Valuation falls as rescue rises. On the existing 25.5-million-path corpus at fixed memory depth, the dispersion of CR2 among candidates inside the frontier should fall monotonically as RR rises from 0 to 1, and the CR2-ordered top-k should converge on the dictionary’s lexicographic top-k. This is Conjecture 1 of §4.2; a non-monotone curve would show that the dial is not one-dimensional and that the conjecture is false. 2. A test that sorts discoveries. Any structure exposed by a new composite operation can be re-run in Rescue mode. Structure that survives is contingently tacit, an externalization in Nonaka’s sense; structure that disappears is regime-bound. The coarse findings of 2026d already pass (Appendix J); the prediction is that the CR2-dependent ranking results do not, and that no new discovery will fall between the two classes. 3. Self-reads fail for structural reasons. Following 2026f, the error of a reflexive unbind of a deliberative bundle should grow with collision saturation and not with training-data coverage, the opposite signature from an LLM’s self-report. Injecting known deliberation bundles into PyVaCoAl and measuring recovery error against collision rate would test this directly. 4. Traces re-root results in people. Give researchers an AI-generated result in one of three forms: the result alone, the result with an LLM’s self-explanation, or the result with a VaCoAl/PyVaCoAl trace and its CR values. After a fixed time, measure what Tao’s follow-up activities require: answering questions on the result, presenting it, and extending it. We predict that the trace condition beats both others on questions and extension, while the self-explanation condition may score high on felt understanding without matching gains in performance. 5. An AI wall would be measurable.

Preprint. Under review. AI USE STATEMENT We used a frontier large language model in three ways. For the paper’s prose, we used it iteratively at the sentence level — for wording, concision and clarity — rather than to draft sections or generate content; the ideas, claims and structure are the author’s. For the code, we used it to help write and debug the training, sampling and measurement implementations; every suggestion was read and accepted or rejected manually, and the numerical results reported here come from runs of the accepted code. For related work, we used it to expand a literature search the author had already conducted; we checked every claim we attribute to a cited work against that work directly. We did not use it to generate research ideas or hypotheses, to analyse results, or to produce figures. We have reviewed all AI-assisted work and take responsibility for the final content of this paper, including its text, claims and artifacts. REPRODUCIBILITY STATEMENT The PCN construction and the correspondence it rests on are given in Section 3.1, with the full derivation, its assumptions and the order of every neglected term in App. A; the extension to the skip- connected UNet is in App. D and the solver we relax with in App. E. The training construction, its parameters and the contrastive phase are in Section 3.2 and App. B. App. J gives every training and sampling setting, the FID protocol, the measurement resolution, and the implementation choices the PCN formulation requires but a feedforward one does not; App. I specifies the norm- and attention- free architecture and its recipe. Code for training, sampling and the correspondence measurements is available at https://github.com/alexgower/EnergyMatchingEqProp.

When |Ek| < 1, the eagle starts to run out of energy and enters the exploitation stage (Bairathi and Gopalani, 2019). At this stage, the energy of each eagle is reevaluated by a limit of 0.5 and generating a random number. If |Ek| ≥0.5, the eagle’s energy is still sufficient to attack the prey slowly (soft Besiege). Otherwise, the eagle will take a surprise attack on the rabbit (hard Besiege) (Heidari et al., 2019). Meanwhile, the value of r is generated to determine whether the eagle has the additional ability to select an attack pattern to get the best prey known as progressive rapid dives (Singh, 2020; Sumargo et al., 2024).

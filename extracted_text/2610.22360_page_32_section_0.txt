H Fine-Grained Facts and Explanation Reliability H.1 Analysis Procedure Motivated by fact-oriented evaluation in DSG and FActScore (Cho et al., 2024; Min et al., 2023), we group existing verification decisions by their decision-relevant factual property. Fine Fact labels organize this analysis; the original claim remains the model input, and no new subject-model queries are created. The assignment contains 1,778 verification units: 1,444 paired comparisons, 200 Identity comparisons, and 134 Motion Understanding sequences. These yield 3,222 decision incidences per model, with 1,611 True and 1,611 False. Anchors can recur across pairs. Fine-grained Geometry contributes thirty object pairs and sixty decisions. These content-based analysis units differ from distinct benchmark examples and from the eligible comparisons used for consistency scoring. Motion compliance includes Motion Understanding; its three issue labels remain within-task diagnostics. Identity preservation differs from identity/existence: the former compares identity before and after editing, while the latter concerns category or presence in a single verification relation.

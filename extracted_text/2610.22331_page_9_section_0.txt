Limitations KnowGPO assumes that progress in the target task can be characterized by a finite set of veri- fiable knowledge conditions, which fits knowledge- intensive search but not more open-ended agentic tasks whose progress is harder to discretize. The fidelity of the knowledge state also depends on an LLM verifier, and although we use a strong external model with monotonic accumulation to suppress transient noise, residual errors can still propagate into step-level advantages. Our construc- tion pipeline additionally requires at least one suc- cessful reference trajectory per query to ground entity placeholders, which limits applicability to settings where successful rollouts are reachable at reasonable cost. Finally, all experiments use Qwen3-8B; whether the effects we observe trans- fer to substantially smaller or larger backbones, or to reasoning-trained initializations, remains to be verified.

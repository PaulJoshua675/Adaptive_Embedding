See Section E.1 for the proof. 6 Discussion We studied simultaneous policy evaluation in stochastic contextual bandits with linear reward structure. Our results show that reward-free planning and a shared sampling design yield instance-dependent guarantees without explicit dependence on the cardinalities of the context and action spaces or a numerical lower bound on second-moment eigenvalues. A natural direction is to extend these techniques to linear MDPs and more general function approxima- tion settings. In these settings, an additional challenge is that the policies influence the distributions of future states, so feature moments cannot be estimated directly from a shared policy-independent context distribution. An open question is whether ρ⋆captures the intrinsic difficulty of evaluation in the linear setting. Our analysis provides an upper bound in terms of this quantity, but does not establish its necessity. Matching lower bounds would help determine when mixtures of target policies achieve the optimal evaluation cost and whether more general sampling strategies can improve upon them.

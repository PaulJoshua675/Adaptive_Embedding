Additional Ablation Studies on PixArt-α. Tab. 12 studies the number of spatial grid cells used for grid-based source completion. Increasing the number of grid cells generally improves structure preservation by distributing additional source tokens more evenly across the image, but overly fine grids introduce extra latency. We therefore choose P = 4, which achieves the best overall quality while remaining faster than the unmerged PixArt-α baseline. Fig. 15 further visualizes this trend: denser grids better preserve structural details by preventing source tokens from concentrating in a limited region. Tab. 13 ablates the down/up-sampling scale used to construct the spatial axis. We vary the scale from 2 to 16 and set s = 4, which achieves the best overall trade-off across quality metrics. Fig. 14 presents the merging maps over denoising timesteps. Our method better preserves structural details, such as the bowl shape and leaves, thereby yielding higher-fidelity images.

A.4 Inference Robustness Because multiple daily cells share the same base query, we repeat the main tests using standard errors clustered by base query, with 109 clusters for the U.S. and 25 for Japan, and apply Benjamini– Hochberg correction at a 5% false discovery rate within each table. For RQ1, 18 of 36 target-specified comparisons remain significant after clustering and correction, compared with 24 originally, while none of the 36 target-unspecified comparisons remain significant, compared with 5 originally. For RQ2, 22 of 48 category–election- group–metric comparisons remain significant, compared with 31 originally. The nine lost comparisons have absolute effects below 1.5 percentage points, while the SOV ordering Structure ≥Argu- mentation > Evidence > Style remains unchanged in the U.S. and all three Japanese tiers. For RQ3, none of the 90 persona Mod contrasts remain significant after clustering and correction, compared with 7 originally and 5 with clustering alone. The originally significant contrasts also have mixed directions across engines. Thus, persona conditioning shows no consistent tendency to increase or decrease rewriting gains. These checks leave the qualitative conclusions unchanged. A.5 Full Results Tables 8 and 9 report the full effect sizes underlying Section 6, in- cluding baseline and placebo references.

The bars indicate the mean and standard error 4.3 Communication strategies across runs The runs of GRU achieved the same performance via different paths. In the first, the signal means "I am on the food". The robot emits in 96% of the control steps it spends on the food and in 1.3% of those it spends off it. In the second, there is no signal, and each robot searches on its own. In the third, the signal functions like a beacon, emitting almost always away from the food and only intermittently interrupting over it, which allows the partner to orient themselves towards the sender. The three runs do not differ from each other in fitness (p > 0.38 in all paired comparisons). Behavioral analysis shows that all three strategies kept both robots on the food for a similar fraction of the episode, between 44% and 49% of the time. The network also learned to stay on the food by circling within it, and the robots almost never left it (between 0.00 and 0.73 exits per episode).

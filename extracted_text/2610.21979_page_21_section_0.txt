We evaluate the complete published DocPTBench Photographed (SynReal) and Unwarping (Dewarp) sets, each containing 1,381 images (Du et al., 2025; DocPTBench authors, 2025). SynReal comprises 981 simulated captures and 400 real photographs of 100 source documents under four conditions; Dewarp rectifies the entire Photographed set. Only parsing is evaluated: translation metrics such as BLEU, chrF, METEOR, and STEDS are not used. FinixDocBench combines the 242 Digital pages and 300 Photo pages into one 542-page evaluation set; Track 3 Huge is excluded. We report pooled metrics for this combined set, rather than separate track scores or an equal-weight mean of two track-level scores. Real5-OmniDocBench and Wild-OmniDocBench reuse OmniDocBench source pages and should be interpreted as related robustness evaluations. Metric definitions and aggregation. For each benchmark evaluated with the OmniDocBench v1.6 engine, the headline composite is OverallOmniDocBench = 100 3  (1 −𝐸text) + 𝑆CDM + 𝑆TEDS  , (9) where all three input metrics lie in [0, 1] (OmniDocBench contributors, 2026). Text edit distance first pools edit operations within an eligible page, dividing by the sum of maximum prediction/reference lengths, then averages across eligible pages. CDM and TEDS first average element scores within a page and then macro-average across eligible pages. The report reads the evaluator’s page-level CDM and TEDS outputs, rather than the raw global element averages. Ground-truth formula/table pages w/o scored records receive zero page similarity. DocPTBench images and annotations are passed through this same engine. Its Overall is the composite in Eq. (9), not a numerical conversion of a native DocPTBench headline score. Formula edit distance and reading-order edit distance retain their lower-is-better directions and are reported separately; they do not enter this composite.

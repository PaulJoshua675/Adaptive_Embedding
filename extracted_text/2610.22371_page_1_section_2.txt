We consider the problem of feed-forward full 4D reconstruction from monocular video. A unified 4D representation of scene geometry, depth, camera poses, and 3D point trajectories provides a foundation for simulating and interacting with the physical environment. However, recovering such a representation remains challenging: it requires accurately capturing both the static geometry and the dynamic motion of the scene across viewpoints and over time. Recent advances in feed-forward reconstruction demonstrate the value of pretrained foundational vision models.

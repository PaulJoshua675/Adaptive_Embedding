As shown in Table 4, we evaluate the zero-shot generalization of our model on six selected BEIR datasets without additional domain-specific training. Our method achieves an average NDCG@10 of 50.3, outperforming SPLADE++ (48.2) and approaching the performance of Lion-sp (51.3). Notably, our method consistently outperforms SPLADE++ across all six datasets, with statistically significant improvements on ArguAna and Touche-2020 (p < 0.05). On ArguAna, our method achieves the best NDCG@10 of 55.8, surpassing both SPLADE++ (51.8) and Lion-sp (48.8). Although Lion-sp achieves higher scores on most datasets, our method maintains competitive retrieval effectiveness across diverse out-of-domain settings. These results suggest that our training strategy gener- alizes beyond the MS MARCO training distribution.

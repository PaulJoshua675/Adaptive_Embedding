Following common practice in runtime analysis research [HZLL21, ZD24b, ODNS24, LZZD25], and supported by ample empirical evidence in the literature [GOF16, GOFT17], this work focuses on establishing theoret- ical foundations; additional experiments are therefore not conducted. 2 Preliminaries 2.1 Multitask Multi-Objective Optimization Problem Multitask multi-objective optimization problem consists of multiple tasks each of which is a multi-objective optimization problem. Following the the- ory community of evolutionary multi-objective optimization, in this paper, we consider the maximization of multiple objectives defined on binary space {0, 1}n for each task. For an ℓ-task (A1, ..., Aℓ) problem, we denote the objec- tive function in the i-th task Ai by f i = (f i 1, . . . , f i mi) : {0, 1}n →Rmi, where n ∈N is the problem size of Ai and mi ∈N is the number of objectives. In this work, we use [a..b] to denote {i ∈Z | a ≤i ≤b} for a, b ∈Z and a ≤b. For any task Ai, i ∈[1..ℓ], we call X weakly dominates Y , denoted by X ⪰Y , w.r.t. f i, if f i j(X) ≥f i j(Y ) for all j ∈[1..mi]. If further at least one inequality is strict, then we say that X dominates Y , denoted by X ≻Y . A solution X ∈{0, 1}n is called Pareto optimal w.r.t. f i if there exists no solution Y ∈{0, 1}n such that Y ≻X. The set of all Pareto optimal solutions is called the Pareto set. We call the set of f i values of the Pareto set the Pareto front, and each element of the Pareto front is called a Pareto front point. When the context is unambiguous, we do not differentiate the Pareto front of f i from that of task Ai. For some i ∈[1..ℓ], let W i be a subset of {0, 1}n, and M i be the Pareto front w.r.t. f i. We say that this ℓ-task (A1, ..., Aℓ) problem is solved if the Pareto fronts w.r.t. all tasks are covered by its corresponding solutions, that is, f i(W i) ⊇M i for all i ∈[1..ℓ]. As common in the theory community, this first mathematical work on the evolutionary multitask mult-objective optimization will only discuss the bi-objective optimization for the multi-objective optimization in each task.

F.2 Multi-Point Decoding: Probability Trees versus Stop Decisions The first counting extension repeatedly asks a continue/stop choice and, when continuing, invokes single-point prediction with the already selected coordinates in the prompt. This composes existing interfaces without training on exhaustive point-set completion. It relies on coverage and stopping behavior from a model trained primarily on single targets. On all 196 Point-Bench counting questions, it reaches 19.39% official success and count MAE 11.64.

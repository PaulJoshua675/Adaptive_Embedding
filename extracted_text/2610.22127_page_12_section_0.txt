The cross- coder is trained by minimizing the loss involv- ing (a) reconstruction error as h(l,ft) xt −˜h(l,ft) xt , and h(l,base) xt −˜h(l,base) xt , and (b) sparsity enforcement on the decoder weights d(ft) j , d(base) j . Following (Minder et al., 2025) of utilizing L0 sparsity in- stead of L1 sparsity in obtaining better decoupled latents, we adopt the L0 sparsity-based BatchTopK crosscoder (Bussmann et al., 2024) to select top-k latents activated per example for a batch size of n, enforcing only nk activated latents for activation re- construction from both models. More details about BatchTopk crosscoder are described in Minder et al. (2025).

Because this metric aggregates the entire deployment window, it captures the initial transient adaptation phase. Consequently, for the online architecture, this aggregate MSE serves as a conservative upper bound; over prolonged cyclic operation, the instantaneous tracking error asymptotically decays toward the system’s stochastic noise floor. The LIP formulation fails to capture the spatial complexity of the unmodeled cubic stiff- ness (Bx3 k). While the MRAC remains bounded and avoids trajectory divergence, it exhibits a per- sistent steady-state tracking offset with an ampli- tude distortion of 2.49% and a Mean Squared Er- ror (MSE) of 0.0062 (Table 2).

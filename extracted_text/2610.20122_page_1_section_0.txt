XViewRank: Overlap-Conditioned Score Fusion for LLM-Augmented Sparse Retrieval Xinxuan Lv, Minghan Li⋆, and Guodong Zhou School of Computer Science and Technology, Soochow University, Suzhou, Jiangsu 215031, China xxlv@stu.suda.edu.cn, mhli@suda.edu.cn, gdzhou@suda.edu.cn Abstract. Large language models can reduce vocabulary mismatch in sparse retrieval by generating alternative views of a query. Once these views are searched independently, however, their ranked lists may be complementary, redundant, or affected by query drift, making fixed fu- sion suboptimal. We present XViewRank, a lightweight retrieval-side method for conditionally fusing BM25 results from an original query, a hypothesis view, and a step-back view. For each pooled candidate, XViewRank retains three normalized BM25 scores and three query-level overlaps between the retrieved result sets. A small nonlinear scorer uses the overlaps as context that changes how the view-specific scores are interpreted, without jointly encoding query and passage text. Under a matched-input protocol, XViewRank improves nDCG@10 and MAP nu- merically over reciprocal rank fusion on both TREC DL 2019 and 2020, with a statistically significant MAP gain on DL19. Additional analyses show that the clearest benefit occurs when the view rankings have low agreement, while the evidence on DL20 is weaker. The results support overlap-conditioned score fusion as a compact retrieval-side alternative to fixed fusion, while also delimiting where its advantage is empirically established. Keywords: Information retrieval · Query expansion · Rank fusion · Learning to rank · Large language models 1

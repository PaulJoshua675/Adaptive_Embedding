REKI sets a new Pareto frontier between performance and model size: it scores highest on LIBERO-Plus, and is only outperformed by models about twice its size on RoboTwin Clean2Rand and RoboCasa365. We report full results in Fig. 4. Our contributions are thus three-fold: (i) We introduce REKI, a 2.4B-parameter VLA pretrained on public data, which sets a new Pareto frontier between performance and model size. (ii) We introduce register tokens in the action expert to act as a memory, which can be cached efficiently, and analyse how this reduces the failure modes of the memoryless policy. We hope REKI serves as a strong and accessible baseline for future VLA research. To foster VLA research, we release the model weights and our optimised training and inference code, from data curation to deployment on a real robot. 2

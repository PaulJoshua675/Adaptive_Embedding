The text baseline is a gradient-boosted model on eleven features of the trace text: the densities of sentence roles such as verification, derivation and backtracking, assigned by cue-word lists, statistics of the transitions between roles, the position of the final answer, and the number and mean length of sentences. On the problem split, a logistic regression on the same features reaches a detection AUROC of 0.7548 at 4B and 0.7379 at 8B. Adding the confidence probe’s score to the text baseline’s features raises the detection AUROC by +0.034 to +0.041 across the two scales and the two training losses, with intervals that exclude zero. Combined probe. Table 3 sets out the target, training traces, mask and model scales of every probe. The combined probe, compared with the confidence probe in Figure 3C, has the confidence probe’s architecture, input and optimizer (Table 4). Its attention mask is bidirectional. It trains on correct and incorrect traces, taking the final label as the target at every step of a correct trace and the rollout rate ˆpt as the target at each evaluated prefix of an incorrect trace; the other steps of an incorrect trace carry no loss. It weights each evaluated prefix by its rollout count and each step of a correct trace by one, and the confidence probe weights every step equally. It trains for a fixed 8 epochs, and the confidence probe trains for at most 20 epochs with early stopping. The combined probe has a lower detection AUROC than the confidence probe on held-out problems at both scales (Table 10). Training the confidence probe’s design on the combined probe’s labels may help isolate the contribution of the labels; we did not run this experiment. B.1 Failure-type classifier Input and population. The failure-type classifier reads the transcoder activations at the input of the MLP block of each of layers 19 to 24, from the publicly released transcoders for both models3. For each problem it takes one completed incorrect trace, the one with the lowest trace index, which we call the scored trace. Each latent’s rectified activation is summed over the trace’s tokens, at most 16,384, and binarized at that latent’s 90th percentile over the problems of the training folds.

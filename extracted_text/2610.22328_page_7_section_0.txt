Qualitative Evaluation Figures 1 and 3 compare our model with its backbone model on editing sequences interspersed with no-op instructions. We further compare against the training-free methods and MT-OPSD on sequences of consecutive semantic edits in Figure 4. The source scenes for these comparisons were generated independently of the training data using FLUX.2- klein-9B. Figure 4 demonstrates that degradation can ac- cumulate even under simple editing instructions: after ten successive object removals, all competing methods visibly damage the surrounding scene, while our model success- fully preserves it. Each training-free method ties every output to part of the previous image, and this is also its main weakness: when an edit has to change exactly that part, content from ear- lier turns leaks into the result. Emu Edit reverts pixels that change only slightly, so remnants of earlier images survive; VAE-LFA aligns low-frequency latent statistics with earlier outputs, so their colours persist; and FreqEdit steers high- frequency detail toward the previous image, so their tex- tures persist. Figure 8 in Appendix F illustrates these effects over three successive background replacements. MT-OPSD leaves no such traces but blurs the image and washes out its colours, consistent with the degradation observed in Fig- ure 4.

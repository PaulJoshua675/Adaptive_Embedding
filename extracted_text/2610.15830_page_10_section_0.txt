Plot intervals are reaggregated from confirmatory CSVs by paired seed bootstrap. 6 Does acquisition-selected formation extend to three targets? With two targets, every relative change lies on a single comparison axis. We therefore test the formation result using three symmetric three-dimensional Gaussian targets with pairwise correlation 0.35, mixed into nine input dimensions. The encoder has hidden width 32 and a bottleneck of dimension two or three. Update weights stay at 1/3. Precise acquisition favors A, B, or C throughout 600 updates; balanced acquisition allocates exactly 200 precise observations to each target. Each seed branches from the same initialized state. The three-dimensional bottleneck both matches the two-target compression ratio and equals one target’s dimension, so it is not a pure control for compression ratio alone. After formation, the encoder is frozen. Fresh linear readouts are fitted on unused probe samples and tested on separate held-out samples, with common high-precision inputs. We summarize relative errors with centered log-error scores, qi = 1 3 X j∈{A,B,C} log Dj −log Di, X i qi = 0. (3) A positive score indicates more precise readout than the geometric-mean error. Two coordinates describe this observable allocation plane. They are not the coordinates of the full internal representation, and their magnitudes are not directly comparable to the two-target index s. Every target-specific acquisition condition favors its designated target in all 40 confirmatory seeds at both bottleneck dimensions. Relative to balanced acquisition, the designated target’s allocation-score gain is approximately 0.37 with a two-dimensional bottleneck and 0.90–0.92 with three dimensions. The effects also spread across the allocation plane (Figure 5). To test whether this spread reduces to one empirical comparison axis, we summarize the three mean intervention-effect vectors by singular values. The second singular value measures the component beyond the best-fitting single axis: it is approximately 0.55 and 1.37 at the two capacities, with intervals excluding zero. Near-unit ratios of second to first singular values additionally verify the intended label symmetry. Exact estimates and intervals appear in Appendix G.

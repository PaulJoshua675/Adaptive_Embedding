This work identifies an exploration dilemma in MoEs that combine fine-grained expert segmentation with shared experts. This tension can prevent the additional expressiveness enabled by fine-grained segmentation from being fully realized while also diminishing the benefits of shared experts. To address this, we propose MirrorMoE, which adaptively shrinks the exploration space to better match the model’s exploration capability. MirrorMoE consistently improves performance across scales. Future direction. Our current approach addresses the exploration dilemma by shrinking the explo- ration space. A promising future direction is to improve the routing strategy itself for more efficient exploration. 10

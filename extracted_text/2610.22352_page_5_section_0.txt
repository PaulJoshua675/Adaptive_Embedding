Zero-padding unobserved regions assigns concrete material values, such as black albedo, to surfaces without observations. We instead use the sparse coordinate architecture of Emat to omit unobserved voxels. Encoding only the visible support Po avoids assigning explicit material values to unobserved voxels while retaining the visible support as conditioning evidence. This encoding runs once before sampling and remains fixed throughout generation. Section 4.4 examines how its latents and decoded materials differ from complete-material encoding. Because anchors are indexed in 3D coordinates, the same interface can combine observations from additional or edited views without retraining the generator (Appendix G.1). 3.4 Observation-Guided Completion We generate complete material latents via flow matching over the mesh M, predicting a velocity field at time t from the current noisy material latents Zt, geometry latents G, and native observation representation Co (Lipman et al., 2023). Native observation latents provide spatially aligned material evidence for reconstructing visible appearance. The generator must learn to use this evidence while completing unobserved surfaces coherently. We introduce an interleaved flow transformer that retains observations as fixed, read-only memory and alternates unobserved-cell and full-surface updates. This schedule assigns more hidden-state updates to unobserved cells while allowing visible materials to be refined during full-surface generation. Interleaved flow transformer. The generator uses two coordinate supports: the full active surface S and the observed subset So ⊆S. Figure 7 in Appendix A.2 illustrates the update schedule. We maintain two streams: a dynamic generation stream X tracking the hidden state of Zt over S, and a static observation stream O serving as immutable reference memory over So. At observed coordinates, Os stores partial evidence while Xs predicts the complete cell. Both streams use the same 3D coordinate frame and a shared input projection Win over material and geometry latents: X(0) s = Win([Zt,s; gs]), s ∈S, Os = Win([¯zo s; gs]) + Wmeta([ρs; ds]) + bo, s ∈So, (7) where ¯zo s scales the native observation latents to match the normalization of Zt, Wmeta is a learnable affine projection of spatial metadata, and bo is a learned observation-role bias initialized to zero. Across the Transformer blocks, O produces no queries and receives no updates with O(ℓ+1) = O across blocks, serving as read-only memory supplying keys and values to every layer. To concentrate generative capacity on unobserved surfaces, query coordinates alternate across blocks ℓ= 0, . . . , L −1: Jℓ= U for even ℓover unobserved cells U = S \ So, and Jℓ= S for odd ℓover the full surface to maintain global coherence.

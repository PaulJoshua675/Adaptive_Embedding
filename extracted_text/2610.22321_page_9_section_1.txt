We study general audio-video consistency evaluation under the challenges of multi-event compo- sition, multi-layer audio mixture, and asymmetric cross-modal relations. We propose an event- centric coarse-to-fine three-level metric and instantiate it as AVC-Reward, trained with struc- tured SFT and human-aligned GRPO.

G.1 SINGLE-MODE VERSUS UNIFIED TRAINING The PLAN-only baseline is trained with visual subgoals. At evaluation, both it and the unified model receive the same initial image, instruction, and goal image, and retain the goal condition throughout generation. We use the same scoring procedure for both models. The rubric evaluates the intermediate action sequence and the transition toward the goal, including contact and object motion.

(7) Design Policy 1: Time-Scale Separation and Convergence To prevent θk updates from destabi- lizing the physical system, the learning loop must enforce time-scale separation. The coupled sys- tem is modeled as a two-time-scale process: the fast physical plant dynamics (xk) and the slow neural weight updates (θk). Condition 1 (Fast-Loop δ-ISS): The retained baseline controller must enforce incremental Input-to-State Stability (δ-ISS). This property en- sures that the distance between any two system trajectories is strictly bounded by the magnitude of the difference in their inputs for any frozen parameter θ. Consequently, the fast physical state xk converges exponentially to a parameter- dependent equilibrium manifold x∗(θ). This de- couples the dynamics, permitting us to evaluate the loss function safely on the slow manifold, de- noted as ¯L(θ).

We reference the general loss design of D4RT (Zhang et al., 2026a) and open-sourced repositories (Jiang, 2026; Li & Team, 2026). For each loss type k ∈{3D, conf, 2D, vis, disp, normal}, we use mk i ∈{0, 1} to indicate whether query i has valid GT supervision for that loss.

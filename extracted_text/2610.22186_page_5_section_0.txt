The results demonstrate that the annotated videos contain rich audiovisual information and the captions are free of modality bias, thus providing effective supervision for event-centric alignment. 3.2 Post-Training of EVA-Cap Leveraging this dataset, we propose EVA-Cap, an audiovisual captioner optimized via event-centric alignment. We first present our two-stage training pipeline in Sec. 3.2.1, and then introduce the event-centric reward functions dedicated to fine-grained alignment in Sec. 3.2.2. 3.2.1 Training Pipeline We build EVA-Cap upon the Qwen2.5-Omni Thinker module, which jointly encodes audio and video into a unified, temporally interleaved token sequence to facilitate synchronous audiovisual understanding. We then adopt a two-stage post-training pipeline consisting of supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO). In the first phase, we perform SFT on our constructed 63K EVA-Data to empower the model to generate detailed captions that integrate both audio and visual modalities. In the second phase, we further optimize the SFT model with GRPO on 3.2K rollout videos from EVA-Data (details of the rollout process are provided in Appendix A.2.2). To enhance fine-grained alignment in audiovisual captions, we design event-centric rewards covering three dimensions: event completeness, character attribution, and audiovisual synchronization.

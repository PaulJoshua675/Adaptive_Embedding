We measure gradient-law residuals at billion-parameter scale under BF16 mixed precision. Setup. We construct a 1.22B-parameter SwiGLU Transformer with d = 2048, L = 22, nheads = 16, and dff = 5504, with gradient checkpointing, and measure gradient norms at a single minibatch under BF16 mixed precision. For each of the symmetry pair types Q–K, V–O, and MLP, and λ ∈{0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3}, we reparameterize from a common baseline state and compare the measured gradient norms against the theoretical predictions ∇A′ ℓL = c−1 ℓ ∥∇AℓL∥and ∇B′ ℓL = cℓ∥∇BℓL∥. Results. Table 20 reports the maximum relative error across all layers for each pair type and λ value. Across all 12 configurations, the maximum relative error remains below 0.16%, with the MLP SwiGLU value–output pair achieving particularly precise agreement below 0.055%. At λ = 0.3, the deepest layer’s scale factor is c21 = e0.3×21 ≈545. The extreme coordinate multiplier ratio is c2 21 = e12.6 ≈2.97 × 105; the actual gradient-norm spread also depends on the baseline gradients. These measurements show that the norm-scaling identity remains numerically accurate in this billion-parameter BF16 configuration.

As shown in Table 3, using Planner alone increases ADV but also raises CPA; using Optimizer alone reduces CPA but decreases ADV. Combining the two still does not improve both metrics simultaneously. Adding Inspector increases ADV by 4.95% and reduces CPA by 9.57% relative to the Planner–Optimizer combination. The full system outperforms No Operation on both metrics. This change indicates that local improvements from individual roles do not simply add up to overall gains when more agents are introduced. Budget planning and ad-unit adjustment influence one another and require collaboration around a shared delivery objective. Inspector turns execution outcomes into diagnostic guidance for Planner and Optimizer, enabling both roles to revise subsequent decisions using prior delivery feedback. 5.4.2 Policy Learning Stage Ablation With all roles present, we compare four settings: before domain-specific post-training (w/o Training), SFT alone, subsequent GSPO offline reinforcement learning, and co-evolution. As shown in Table 4, SFT increases ADV and reduces CPA; subsequent GSPO further improves both metrics. Business performance improves with training even when the division of roles remains unchanged, indicating that the collaborative structure alone does not guarantee high-quality decisions; the corresponding operational capabilities also need to be developed. Supervised demonstrations provide initial decision-making capabilities, while reinforcement learning further optimizes delivery strategies using execution outcomes, making multi-agent collaboration more effective in supporting value generation and cost control. Compared with AdCoEvo (Fixed), AdCoEvo increases ADV by 3.96% and reduces CPA by 1.24% in Sparse. In General, ADV increases by 4.22% and CPA decreases by 2.21%. This improvement shows that the operational capabilities established through offline training still have room to improve. In a changing advertising market, co-evolution enables the multi-agent system to continually improve its policies using new settled feedback, yielding greater delivery returns on top of its existing collaboration and operational capabilities. 5.4.3 Co-Evolution Ablation To study role participation and rotation, we retain all roles and compare Optimizer-only updates, Planner– Optimizer updates, and AdCoEvo against AdCoEvo (Fixed). To equalize role-update counts, the first setting updates Optimizer every period; the second alternates Planner and Optimizer across periods. As shown in Table 5, full co-evolution achieves higher ADV and composite scores than updating Planner and Optimizer across all five market sequences. Overall, ADV further increases by 2.88% and the composite score by 2.78%, while CPA increases by only approximately 0.10%. This shows that, while retaining diagnostic guidance, allowing Inspector to participate in co-evolution can further increase advertiser value at a similar cost per acquisition, yielding higher delivery returns. 10

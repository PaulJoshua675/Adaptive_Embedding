Seven-seed experiments on the 31M model compare these cases, including moments that are stale only in the deeper half of the layers. With reset moments, the edit adds 0.277 ± 0.015 PPL relative to the matched control (Table 12). Keeping and transforming the moments leaves a smaller but nonzero gap of 0.058 ± 0.017 PPL relative to a control that also keeps its optimizer state, consistent with Theorem 2: Under a shared learning rate, transformed moments alone do not keep the two runs equivalent. Fully stale and half-stale moments yield penalties of 0.056 ± 0.015 and 0.056 ± 0.007 PPL, respectively (Tables 13 and 14). F.8 State-Preserved Checkpoint-Surgery Diagnostic The branched checkpoint-surgery resume deliberately resets optimizer state at the handoff, matching the common workflow of importing a checkpoint without its AdamW moments. We now measure the residual penalty when optimizer moments are preserved and transformed covariantly with the exact orbit edit. Design. For each of the seven paired seeds, we train the 31M modern RMSNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU, and GQA T5 SentencePiece model for 5K WikiText-103 AdamW steps. We then clone the same checkpoint into five 2.5K-step branches: control reset, surgery reset, surgery reset + Soft SAGGS, control state, and surgery state-covariant. The model uses context length 256, batch size 48, and peak learning rate 4 × 10−4. Phase 1 follows a 7.5K-step cosine schedule with 750-step warmup. The reset branches create a fresh AdamW optimizer and a 2.5K-step cosine schedule with 250-step warmup; the state branches load the phase-1 AdamW and scheduler state. In the state-covariant surgery branch, the exact λ = 0.2 all-pairs orbit move also maps AdamW first moments by the chain-rule factor and second moments by its square. This branch applies the covariant moment map without periodic rebalancing during phase 2. Table 12 reports the seven-seed summaries. The reset branch reproduces the checkpoint-surgery mechanism at a smaller model size. Surgery with optimizer reset is +0.277 ± 0.015 PPL worse than the reset control over seven paired seeds, with exact sign-flip p+ sf = 0.0078. Soft SAGGS recovers the penalty almost entirely, leaving only +0.007 ± 0.006 PPL relative to the reset control. The largest immediate absolute validation-loss change after the edit, including the initial Soft rebalance where used, is below 6.7 × 10−5. When optimizer state is preserved and co-transformed, the surgery gap falls to +0.058 ± 0.017 PPL relative to the state-preserved control. Moment co-transformation alone does not enforce trajectory equivariance: The shared learning rates already violate the coordinate rule in Theorem 2, even if ϵ = 0. Global clipping, finite precision, and state history can introduce further discrepancies. The residual is about one fifth of the reset-surgery gap.

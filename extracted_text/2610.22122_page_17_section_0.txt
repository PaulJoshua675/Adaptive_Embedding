Experience augmentation reconstructs additional transitions from measured outcomes to train the acquisition function. In budgeted experimental design without replacement, the measured dataset grows while the unmeasured pool and remaining budget shrink. A screen therefore provides a single executed trajectory and cannot return to an earlier state to test alternative experimental sequences within the same budget. LBO reconstructs predecessor states in which a measured batch has not yet been observed, then restores that batch’s real outcomes to form additional transitions to a known successor state. The same experimental outcomes can thus support batch value learning under different information states, expanding online training experience without additional experiments.

Preprint B.4 ARCHITECTURE AND TRAINING ABLATIONS Table 4 follows the cumulative design at the controlled development budget, from flow matching (R0– R5) through distillation, fair-CRPS refinement, and stratified sampling (R6–R8). The modulation, initialization, normalization, and regularization bundle in R2 improves both FEV and FPS. Restricting history attention in R3 enables shared history computation but reduces FEV skill; late entry and four training draws partially recover it. Distillation then improves both scores while reducing the number of evaluations from ten to one. R7 adds fair CRPS to the PDD objective, and R8 changes source sampling at the same weights. Stratification improves empirical FPS from 0.5248 to 0.5329, with a paired 95% task-bootstrap interval of [0.0042, 0.0131] for the skill gain.

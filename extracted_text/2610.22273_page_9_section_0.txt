(3) Transfer of Builder Agent to novel long-context document reasoning setting. To assess whether the builder agent learns a generalized anticipatory capability rather than simply overfitting to the DeepResearch training distribution, we evaluate Librarian Agents zero-shot on the BRIGHT benchmark. Here, we study 150 questions spanning 3 domains with 50 per domain, with one filesystem per domain (also studying amortization). As detailed in Table 3, the RL-finetuned builder agent successfully transfers to highly out-of-distribution domains such as biology and math problem solving. Librarian Agents achieves an average pass@1 accuracy of 0.52 and pass@3 accuracy of 0.67 across the 3 BRIGHT datasets, outperforming baselines. These results confirm that framing memory construction as an MDP enables the builder to learn fundamental, transferable heuristics for organizing information, which remain robust even when encountering novel, unseen documents. The structural statistics on BRIGHT further show that RL substantially changes construction behavior. Across the 3 sub-domains, the trained builder produces richer hierarchical memories, averaging 53.4 active synthetic files organized into 45.4 clusters and 8.0 merged artifacts, compared with 4.8 files, 4.2 clusters, and 0.6 merges for the base builder. (4) Analysis of Librarian Agents and capability of anticipation. We next examine the filesystems that Librarian Agents constructs and how the answering agent uses them. Figure 2 illustrates that the trained builder combines broad topical coverage with hierarchical organization. Tables 4 and 5 complement this view by distinguishing answering-agent retrieval (the synthetic files and tokens read) from builder structure (the active files, clusters, and merges it produces). Broad coverage expands the range of future queries the memory can support, while hierarchical organization makes fine-grained evidence easier to locate. The first qualitative case examines robustness when anticipatory coverage is incomplete. In Figure 7, a BrowseComp+ query requires a detail omitted from the synthetic layer. The answering agent follows a provenance link to the relevant raw file and recovers the answer without enumerating the full unstructured corpus.

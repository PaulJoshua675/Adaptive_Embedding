Memory Ablation To further validate counsellor’s memory and its corresponding issues, we rerun sessions 2–3 of seven representative counsellors without any memory (as in session 1), while the pa- tient keeps its memory. GPT-6.1 Sol judges these sessions with the running memory in the counsellor’s prompt. Compared with the same patients’ benchmark sessions 2–3, MHQR rises by 0.51 [0.42, 0.60] without memory, and the rise is higher for counsellors that improved the most from additional sessions (Figure 11). Similarly, most of the increase in MHQR related to responses flagged for both Factual & Contextual Fidelity and Guidance Calibration, the same group that grows after session 1 (Figure 5). Alternative Judge. DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash evaluates the same 61,819 benchmark replies as GPT-6.1 Sol. The resulting counsellor rankings show broad agreement (Kendall’s τ = 0.82; Table 15). Both judges place the three GPT-5.6 counsellors first, although some other orderings differ.

E Parameter-Count Inversion (Cardano) With aspect ratio A = d/l where d is the embedding dimension and l is the number of layers, each block contributes 13d2 matmul parameters from its time-mix and channel-mix components, so N\E = 13d3/A + vd and NT = N\E + vd where v is the vocabulary size. Inverting a target N\E to a width d requires solving the cubic 13d3 + Avd −AN\E = 0, which has a closed form via Cardano’s formula. Because N\E and d are related by this cubic rather than a monomial, dopt(C) is not itself a power law except in the large-d limit where 13d3 dominates Avd; we therefore sample target values N\E along the fitted frontier and invert numerically at each, rounding to architecture-compatible widths.

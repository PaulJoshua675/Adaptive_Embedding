Fig. 1 Overview of the proposed methodology. 3.1 Test collections We conduct the experiments on two public Brazilian legal test collections: NormasTCU and Ulysses-RFCorpus [9, 10]. NormasTCU contains 14,469 normative acts from the Brazilian Federal Court of Accounts. Its 46 queries are based on real user searches and mostly consist of short keywords. Query–document pairs were assessed by a panel of four domain experts using three relevance levels: irrelevant, partially relevant, or relevant Ulysses-RFCorpus contains 105,669 legislative proposals from the Brazilian Chamber of Deputies. Each proposal also contains a justification section in which the deputy explains the moti- vation for the proposal, which is not present in the documents in NormasTCU. Its 693 queries correspond to requests made by deputies for legislative proposals. The relevance judgments were provided by 54 domain experts, with each query and its associated documents assessed by a single expert using three relevance levels: irrelevant, partially relevant, or relevant. Both collections consist of raw text that generally follows the official legal drafting standards discussed in Section 2 [6, 7]. 3.2 Strucutural segmentation Brazilian legal acts follow drafting rules that organize their content into explicit structural units. In these documents, articles (artigos) are the main units of the normative text. Each article consists of a lead paragraph (caput) and optional paragraphs (parágrafos) that complement it. Each lead paragraph or paragraph may contain one or more levels of enumeration (inciso, alínea, and item) [23]. Articles can be organized into higher-level structural units, such as subsections (subseção), sections (seção), chapters (capítulo), titles (título), books (livro), and parts (parte). Together, these elements organize the main body of the normative document [23]. Documents also have a title identifying their type, number, date of enactment, and a summary (ementa). They may also contain other structural elements, such as tables, annexes, or a justification of the proposal (justificação).

A robot is on top of the food when the average of its three floor sensors is less than 500. The arena is a 1 m square surrounded by 10 cm high walls. The food is a 10 cm radius circle, large enough to fit both robots. In each episode, the food and robots are randomly placed with three restrictions: each robot starts at least 30 cm from the food, the two robots are at least 15 cm apart, and neither touches a wall. An episode lasts 60 s. Table 1 summarizes the model parameters.

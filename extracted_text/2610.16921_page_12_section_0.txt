12 PREPRINT experiments show consistent improvements in cross-session and cross-subject settings at modest overhead compatible with real-time constraints. Visualization analyses further show that the learned policy aligns decision windows with contralateral mu-beta ERD/ERS, confirming that it exploits neurophysiolog- ical patterns. These results establish TSE-RL as an effective approach for online time window optimization. Its behavior under closed-loop feedback remains to be examined, since the simulated online experiment reproduces the non-stationary environmental noise and the fatigue-induced neural pattern shifts of real recordings but leaves the BCI output disconnected from the user. Closed-loop validation on data acquired will be studied in future work.

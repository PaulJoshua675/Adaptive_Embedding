Blue circles and triangles denote Baseline; orange squares and diamonds denote Stop-and-report. Each point averages three complete 106-target evaluation blocks. Model–effort configurations are discrete. Individual execution batches are given in Appendix C.2. Tutorial experience is not uniformly beneficial. Preceding tutorials reduce mean RHAE70 for both GPT-5.5-based agents at both effort settings. With GPT-5.6-sol xhigh, tutorials improve both agents. At max, the effects are smaller and mixed: tutorials lower Baseline’s score but raise Stop-and-report’s. The same curriculum can therefore help or hurt depending on the model–effort configuration. The curriculum also increases total token-based cost in every configuration. Additional interaction and expenditure thus do not consistently translate into better target performance. At xhigh effort, changing from GPT-5.5 to GPT-5.6-sol lowers single-labyrinth RHAE70 for both agents but raises curriculum RHAE70, further illustrating that the effect of a model change depends on the experience setting. 8.3 Terminal-Bench: performance beyond game environments The same two agents also complete practical terminal tasks without a Terminal-Bench-specific agent implementation.

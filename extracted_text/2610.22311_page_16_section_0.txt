Preprint Appendices A ADDITIONAL EXPERIMENTAL EVIDENCE A.1 SOFT-FUSION CALIBRATION We calibrate the fixed fusion strength on pooled query-key distributions and frustum slab overlap ma- trices from two navigation trajectories. The calibration contains 48 grids spanning four representative layers, two denoising steps, and three rollout lengths. Positive overlap values have median 0.211, which motivates rescaling rather than multiplying by raw IoU. Among the tested settings, λ = 2 and gref = 0.2 are the weakest pair that recovers at least 85% of positive-overlap blocks in the fused top-80 while changing at most 25% of the model-only selections. Concretely, positive-overlap recall increases from 0.602 to 0.852, and the selected set changes by 9.0% on average. Values at or below τ = 0.1 receive no boost.

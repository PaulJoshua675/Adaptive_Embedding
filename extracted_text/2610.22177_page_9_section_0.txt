Stage 1: Privileged Feature Alignment. The primary objective of the first stage is to develop a robust privileged information encoder and adapt the model’s decoders to utilize it. We freeze the model layers preceding the information injection point, focusing training exclusively on the privileged encoder (i.e. residual flow or gravity encoder) and the downstream decoder layers. This isolation ensures the new modality encoder receives sufficient gradient signal to provide meaningful features while the decoders learn to leverage this injected information. By freezing the early encoder layers, the privileged features are forced to align with the existing distribution of patch and camera tokens, facilitating better fusion. To minimize architec- tural disturbance, we also freeze unrelated decoders and heads; for instance, the VGGT camera decoder remains frozen in stage 1 when finetuning with residual flow as the privileged modality. Stage 2: Knowledge Distillation and Hallucination. As privileged modalities are unavailable at inference time under the LUPI paradigm, the second stage shifts toward guiding the primary model to converge faster and learn more robustly. With the privileged encoder and decoders now prepared, we unfreeze the entire model and introduce aggressive modality dropout. This mechanism creates a shortcut for the finetuning process, forcing the unfrozen model encoder to ”hallucinate” the missing privileged features when they are dropped out. In this framework, the privileged infor- mation encoder acts as a teacher during standard iterations, while the model encoder serves as the student during dropout iterations.

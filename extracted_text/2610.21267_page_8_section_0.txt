Many deploy- ments gate a consequential action behind a prereq- uisite call, and the ordering is itself the require- ment. In banking, a sensitive tool must be un- locked by unlock:X before it can be called, and under the full registry LLM-ONLY skips that step when asked to open an account. The trie withholds the raw write: no successful trajectory reaches open_bank_account from Si without passing through unlock:open_bank_account. The edge memory sup- plies the other half, matching the conversation to the unlock (Figure 4). 5.5 When the global set is needed Adding a global set to TRACER (TRACER+G) helps when some tools not bound to any prefix take a large share of tool calls. This explains TRACER’s low performance on Tau3 Banking, whose registry mostly uses generic tools that cover many subtasks under one umbrella (KB_search for any knowledge- base lookup, grep for any text match), so they ap- pear throughout the trie. TRACER+G recovers 5.8 points there, landing between 3.0 below and 4.0 above LLM-ONLY depending on the model (Ta- ble 2). On the τ 2 mixes and BFCL v3, where usage is less concentrated or utility tools do not dominate, the injected tools add distractors rather than recall, and TRACER outperforms TRACER+G. 5.6 Ablations (A) Edge embeddings vs. description-only prior. TRACER still adds the learned weighting and contextual embeddings that no bigram front-end has (§2). 6 Conclusion and Future Work TRACER is the first tool-retrieval front-end to make selection history-aware and dynamic at every pre- fix: it keys on the full execution prefix, ranks candi- dates against the conversations each transition was taken in, and learns how best to weight the two sig- nals for the situation. It beats full-registry selection in 21 of 24 model-environment pairs while cutting per-step tokens by 80% on our primary testbed, at a cost that tracks the budget rather than the registry. The budget k already moves with domain and model (Table 3), and the global set recovers most of the Tau3 Banking gap; both are fixed here and should adapt. TRACER gained 15.2 points for GPT- 5.4-mini and matched GPT-5.4’s full-registry task success at 23% of the registry, but did not carry gains at most of GPT-5.4’s budgets, largely be- cause running on its own prefix roughly triples the share of steps that fall outside the trie.

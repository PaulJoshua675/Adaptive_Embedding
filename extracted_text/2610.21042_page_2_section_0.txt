Our contributions are as follows. • PRISMA-guided workflow. ReviewPilot coor- dinates search, screening, retrieval, and extrac- tion while recording counts, exclusion reasons, and available source for inspection (Section 3). • Review-specific configuration and revision. Researchers can set criteria and schemas through dialogue. Then, agents draft screening guidance and coding rules. Researchers can edit these in- structions and confirm. Project memory design further retains these configurations and correc- tions (Sections 3.2 and 3.6). • System-level evaluation. On PERG, Review- Pilot achieves higher screening macro F1 and extraction F1 than a single-prompt LLM and AgentSLR under the reported settings (Tables 1 and 2). Two case studies document completed workflows and screening agreement with human annotators (Section 5). 2

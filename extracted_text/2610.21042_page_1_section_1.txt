Systematic literature reviews follow the PRISMA process of screening scholarly works and extracting evidence from included stud- ies. Deep research tools based on large lan- guage models (LLMs) can search and sum- marize the literature but do not record which studies are excluded or where extracted values come from. We develop a conversational multi- agent system called ReviewPilot that automates the PRISMA workflow from paper screening to information extraction. Researchers can tai- lor criteria and schemas through dialogue, and a project memory keeps their configurations and revisions. Each screening decision comes with its reason and each extracted value with its source. On epidemiological expert reviews, ReviewPilot outperforms a single-prompt LLM and AgentSLR across three LLMs, with screen- ing macro F1 up to 0.776 and extraction F1 up to 0.788. Case studies in biomedicine and human-computer interaction show close agree- ment with human annotators, suggesting that ReviewPilot can transfer beyond epidemiology. Code is available at this link. 1

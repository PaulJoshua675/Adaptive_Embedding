Quantile-model completions. Applying these scores requires complete paths, which marginal quantile forecasts do not specify. We retain native samples when available. To include quantile models, we construct paths from their marginal quantiles under two explicit dependence assumptions. Sorted quantiles define a piecewise-linear inverse CDF with constant extension beyond the outer quantiles. An independent completion draws a separate quantile rank at each forecast time and channel, whereas a comonotone completion shares one rank across an item’s times and channels. Both laws preserve the same underlying interpolated marginals, allowing us to examine how the dependence assumption affects trajectory scores. These two choices serve as baselines, as different dependence assumptions may yield different scores for the same marginal forecasts.

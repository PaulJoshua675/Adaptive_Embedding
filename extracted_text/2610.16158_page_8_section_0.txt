A measured intervention–recovery pattern. Of 406 recorded di- rected intervals, 401 install a native reseed. Figure 8 averages events within each run and then weights the 40 runs equally. At +5 s, mean population value is 2,417.631 seconds below entry and diversity rises from 62.6% to 92.8%. At +24 s, the deficit is 512.513 seconds: 78.8% has recovered, while the historical incumbent improves by 226.823 seconds. All 401 events remain installed through +24 s; 400 remain at +30 s. The population curve describes the pool mean, not only the targeted slot. These observational changes explain why immediate trajectory loss can coexist with later progress; they do not isolate the causal effect of reseeding alone. Native work continues during inference. Among 840 online calls, 838 install a valid plan. Request-to-receipt latency has median 11.06 s and 90th percentile 13.87 s. Pending intervals total 9,845.261 s and overlap 9,837.360 s of native work: 99.92% overlap, or 245.9 s per solve, with 2,720,049 native cycles. HTTP-only overlap is 99.99%. These intervals lie inside the solve budget; they establish sustained execution during model latency, not an additive time saving or measured synchronous-control speedup. 4.4 Offline Synthesis and Cross-Source Use The prospective GEN test issued one recorded gpt-6-luna request at low reasoning effort for a deterministic select_plan(packet, plans) function. Only the supplied state and legal descriptors were accessible. The first candidate was frozen without objective-guided revision. All 84 saved interface packets yielded legal IDs, deter- ministic repeats and unchanged inputs. These packets came from four L002 views at seed 83, so those four graph–seed positions overlap the subsequent 40-position evaluation; validation tested the interface rather than tuning objective performance. GEN com- pletes all 40 runs with zero deployment model calls and a mean of 1,136,347.357 seconds: +1,404.572 over CHILS (0.124%). Its lower mean than EXP/VAL shows that a usable one-shot policy need not dominate established control realizations.

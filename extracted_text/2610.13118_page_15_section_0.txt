They spend 93% of their time on it and never leave it. Hollow circles indicate the start and filled circles the end 4.5 Restrictions on innate action selection In EASA safe, with manually set weights, the robots were on the food together only 4.9% of the time and left it 1.53 times per episode. The avoidance channel was activated in 12% of the steps over the food when the partner was close and never when it was far away; that is, the reflex treated the partner as an obstacle. However, only 5 of 46 departures from the food were preceded by avoidance. The main cost appears to come from the fact that innate channels took over in about a third of the steps, almost always away from the food, and disrupted the network's search and approach strategies.

D Evaluation protocol This section collects the experimental setup and measurement conventions used in Sec. 3. Experimental setup. We evaluate class-conditioned image generation on ImageNet-1k (Russakovsky et al., 2015) at 256×256 and 512×512, and text-conditioned generation on GenEval (Ghosh et al., 2023), GenAI-Bench (Li et al., 2024a), and DPG-Bench (Hu et al., 2024). Reconstruction and generation metrics. For a fixed feature extractor, Fréchet distance compares the empirical means and covariances of real and reconstructed or generated image features. rFID is the tokenizer’s reconstruction measurement that compares the reconstructed images and original images in the Inception space using the ImageNet-1k validation set. gFID compares generated images with the original training images using Fréchet distance in Inception space. We re-use the released reference statistics from Li and He (2026). We also report gFDr6 introduced by Yang et al. (2026), which reports generative Fréchet distance in six different feature spaces. The six-space evaluation uses Inception-v3 (Szegedy et al., 2016), ConvNeXt V2 (Woo et al., 2023), DINOv2 (Oquab et al., 2024), MAE (He et al., 2022), SigLIP2 (Tschannen et al., 2025), and CLIP (Radford et al., 2021), with the checkpoints of Yang et al. (2026): ConvNeXt V2-B, DINOv2 ViT-L/14, MAE ViT-L/16, SigLIP2 So400m/16, and CLIP ViT-L/14. In each space, the Fréchet-distance ratio divides the measured distance by that space’s fixed reference normalization constant, i.e., the distance computed between the training and validation sets. gFDr6 averages these six ratios for generated images, and rFDr6 averages them for reconstructions. We re-use the released reference statistics from Yang et al. (2026). All generation distribution metrics use 50K generated samples, and PRDC compares them with 10K real reference images.

We presented ARC-SORT, a training-free tracker in which the tracker’s own uncertainty governs its decisions: the IMM covariance sets a continuous per-track association tol- erance, the assignment’s permutation margin defers near- ambiguous commitments until evidence settles them, and where motion information has run out an appearance-only merge replaces the motion-gated one. Limitations. Deferred commitment is pairwise and single-swap, and its gain tracks the embedding stack (53– 120% of the validation delta). The long-horizon stage is offline. Public model-zoo detection caps HOTA. On DanceTrack, 62.1 is well below trained specialists; on SportsMOT, trained and foundation-model systems [18] keep a 0.1–5.4 HOTA edge. Acknowledgments.

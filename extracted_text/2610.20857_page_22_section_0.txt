C.2 Evolution Mapper: reference sets and interpretation The reference topics use Attention Is All You Need (2017), Generative Adversarial Nets (2014), and ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks (2012). Each Connected Papers export contains 41 publications, spanning 1997–2024, 2012–2023, and 1997–2019, respectively. Table 5 reports the manuscript’s topic scores. The Transformer case’s larger discovered graph does not establish better relevance; the narrative notes missing influential works such as RoBERTa and T5. The GAN case similarly shows incomplete seminal-work coverage, and the ImageNet/CNN case receives the strongest comparative ratings. A graph built from co-citation or bibliographic coupling need not reproduce the edges of a direct citation graph. Future validation should distinguish exact paper-set coverage, inde- pendently verified citation edges, temporal consistency, and human judgments of usefulness. A model’s additional-value rating is not a controlled user study, and repeated judgments of one graph are not independent system runs.

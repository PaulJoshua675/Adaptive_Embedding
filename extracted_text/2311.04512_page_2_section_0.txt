In this paper, we design a simple yet effective Future Feed- back Interaction Network (FFINet) for motion forecasting. In particular, the future interactions obtained from the initial joint predictions are fed back to the observation interaction, in which three kinds of interactions, i.e., feedback interaction, future interaction and feedforward interaction, are introduced to refine the initial prediction results. The whole pipeline of our FFINet can be simply divided into the following three steps. Firstly, we decompose the representation of agents and HD maps into position vectors, current positions, and relative relationships. The position vectors and current positions are encoded to extract rich spatial-temporal features for cross- temporal aggregation. Secondly, with a relative interaction block handling the relative relationships, the cross-temporal aggregation propagates the information between current, ob- servation and future interaction features. Thirdly, feeding into global fused features, we effectively refine the initial predictions with the final predictor. We conduct extensive experiments on both Argoverse 1 and Argoverse 2 motion forecasting benchmarks, in which our FFINet has achieved the state-of-the-art performance. The main contributions of this paper can be summarized as follows: • We propose a novel motion prediction framework, in which the historical, current and future interaction fea- tures are integrated to understand pre-actions. • We propose a novel cross-temporal aggregation strategy, in which a future feedback module is designed to explore the feedback effect of future interaction. • We disentangle the inherent and relative motion of scene with data decomposition, which makes relative relation- ships can be explored in a relative interaction block.

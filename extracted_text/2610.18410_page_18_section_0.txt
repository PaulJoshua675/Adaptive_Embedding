Shape marks the node type: a circle is a chunk, a rounded square a document, a square an entity. Counts on the schema edges are those of the validation graph. In the instance, a resume document is about a person, its chunks are part_of it, and each chunk mentions the entities named in its text. Because mentions always runs from a chunk to an entity, two hops lead from one chunk to another through an entity they share — here two employees of the same organisation. Structured facts and gold supervision. Every generated fact is retained in two synchronised forms. Graph edges encode its structural relationships, and a human-readable rendering is stored in the corresponding chunk; the originating structured dictionary is preserved unchanged on that chunk as source. Text is therefore the representation exposed to embedding and retrieval, while source is the authoritative representation used to compute gold answers and supporting evidence. Question generation reads the structured copy, so exact gold evidence is obtained without an LLM reconstructing facts from prose. Determinism. The generator seeds Python’s global random module once with SEED = 42 and proceeds strictly sequentially. The one hash-order-sensitive operation—the union of skills across a candidate’s jobs, used to sample endorsements—is sorted before sampling, so process- dependent string hashing cannot affect the output. Under the same configuration and source pools the resulting graph.json is byte-identical across runs and machines, a property checked by tests/test_generation_determinism.py under different interpreter hash seeds. Development, validation, and test graphs. A separate generation mode partitions the source pools themselves into disjoint development, validation, and test vocabularies using SPLIT_SEED = 7. Names, attributes, employers, and universities are split before the three graphs are generated, in the order development, validation, test, from a single seeded stream and a single shared used_names set. No person name is therefore repeated across the three graphs, and the later graphs are not a mirrored copy of the earlier draws. Hyperparameter selection, generalisation checks, and final reporting do not share the same underlying vocabulary, and the corpora are node-disjoint by construction rather than by filtering.

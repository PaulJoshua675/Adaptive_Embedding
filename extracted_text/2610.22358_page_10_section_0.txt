Its strongest gains concern the quality of complete trajectories and the downstream predictions they support. The dynamical study shows a unique benefit: independently continued paths preserve variability that point forecasts can lose. Several limitations remain. PathFM does not yet support conditioning on modalities beyond time series, such as text. For chaotic systems, all four dynamical diagnostics remain well above the held-out-truth reference (Table 2). Accurately recovering the invariant distribution remains an open challenge. A key direction for future work is to continue scaling the backbone and training data, pushing the frontier of generative time series forecasting.

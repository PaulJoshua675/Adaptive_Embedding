LLM agents can autonomously plan actions, use tools, and accumulate knowledge while solving multi-step tasks Xi et al. (2023). The ReAct (Reasoning + Acting) pattern has become a baseline architecture Yao et al. (2023b). Recent surveys Guo, Chen, Wang et al. (2024); Huang, Liu, Chen et al. (2024) identify typical components: a planning module (Chain-of-Thought Wei, Wang, Schuurmans et al. (2022), Tree-of-Thoughts Yao, Yu, Zhao et al. (2023a)), dynamic memory and self-reflection Shinn, Cassano, Berman et al. (2023), calls to external tools, and multi-agent designs with role separation Chen, Liu, Han, Zhang and Liu (2024). When agents act as meta-optimizers of neural network models, three of these components are essential: role separation distinguishes hypothesis formulation from its software implementation; logged memory prevents rejected variants from being proposed again; and a sandboxed execution environment is required to run generated code safely. LLM agents have been applied to a diverse set of tasks. SWE-bench Jimenez et al. (2024) and MLE-Bench Chan et al. (2024) have become standard benchmarks for programming and automated ML: the medal rate of the first published o1-preview configuration with AIDE was 16.9 % Chan et al. (2024), while later agents increased this value to 64 % of medals on MLE-Bench Lite (MLE-STAR Nam, Yoon, Chen, Shin, Arik and Pfister (2026), including 36 % gold medals) and to approximately 35 % across all difficulty levels (R&D-Agent on GPT-5) Yang, Yang, Fang, Zhang, Wang, Xian, Li, Li, Xu, Li et al. (2025). Similar multi-agent designs are used in materials discovery and biochemistry Takahara, Mizoguchi and Liu (2025); Shi, Li, Wang et al. (2026); Ghafarollahi and Buehler (2024); Swanson, Wu, Bulaong et al. (2025). Voyager Wang, Xie, Jiang, Mandlekar, Xiao, Zhu, Fan and Anandkumar (2023) demonstrates lifelong accumulation of a skill library in the form of executable code, which is close to the idea of logged hypotheses used in this work. Systems for automating the scientific cycle (The AI Scientist Lu et al. (2024); Yamada, Lange, Lu et al. (2025), Agent Laboratory Schmidgall et al. (2025)) and agent-based robot control Skorokhodov, Latalin, Rybka et al. (2025) have been developed in parallel; they show that autonomous research is possible in principle but do not incorporate domain-specific knowledge of PINNs or SNNs. PINN quality depends on the choice of architecture, activations, the balance of loss-function terms, the distribution of collocation points, and the representation of inputs and outputs Moloshnikov, Sboev, Kutukov et al. (2025b); separate heuristics have been proposed for each component, but no unified automatic procedure has been established. Lang- PINN He, You, Tian et al. (2025), the study closest to the agent-based approach, constructs a PINN from a textual task description but focuses on network construction rather than on selecting its functional blocks for a physical problem.

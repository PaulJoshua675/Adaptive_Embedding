AI Consilium: a Benchmark of LLM Clinical Multidisciplinary Second Opinions A PREPRINT Columns Criterion Operational meaning B / J / R Factual accuracy and sources Facts, numerical values, dates, investigations, and treatments are not fabricated or distorted. C / K / S Identification and prioritization of clinical problems The main clinical problems are identified and prior- itized appropriately. D / L / T Completeness and relevance of the data used Relevant data are used, while irrelevant or inciden- tal details do not drive the assessment. E / M / U Coherence of clinical reasoning The report contains a coherent chain linking data, explanation, diagnoses, conclusions, and manage- ment. F / N / V Quality of differential diagnosis Plausible alternatives are considered, with argu- ments for and against them and methods for ver- ification. G / O / W Quality of the final clinical conclu- sion The conclusion is clear, clinically appropriate, and answers the main clinical question. H / P / X Justification and specificity of man- agement strategy The action plan is concrete, justified, and clinically usable. I / Q / Y Clinical safety Dangerous conditions, contraindications, monitor- ing requirements, and actions in case of deteriora- tion are not missed. General 0–3 scoring scale Score Assign this score when Interpretation 0 The criterion is not met. There is a major error, an unsafe recommen- dation, or fabricated information that invalidates the conclusion. The response cannot be trusted. 1 Some useful content is present, but there is a substantial deficiency: important information is missing, the logic is weak, the conclusion is incomplete, or factual reliability is poor. The response requires sub- stantial revision. 2 The criterion is generally met, but there is a noticeable deficiency, such as missing data, incomplete arguments, absent timeframes, un- clear conditions, or insufficient specification. The response may be ac- ceptable after revision. 3 The criterion is fully met and clinically convincing. Important facts are accurate, the logic is clear, and the conclusion and management plan are specific. The response is strong. If the rater is uncertain between two scores, the lower score should be assigned when the deficiency could affect the diagnosis, patient risk, or management strategy. Rule for fabricated facts A fabricated fact is a clinically significant test result, numerical value, date, symptom, diagnosis, complication, procedure, or treatment that is absent from the source case but is presented by the consensus report as an established fact. • If a fabricated fact affects the diagnosis, differential diagnosis, final conclusion, or management strategy, the criterion “Factual accuracy and sources” should receive 0 points. • If the reasoning, differential diagnosis, final conclusion, or management strategy is based on the fabricated fact, the corresponding criteria should receive no more than 1 point. • If the fabricated fact creates a risk of unsafe treatment or omission of an important threat, the criterion “Clinical safety” should also receive no more than 1 point. • The comment should specify which fact was fabricated and how it was used in the report. 49

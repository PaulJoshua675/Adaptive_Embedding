Table 10 averages each subset over all ten forecasting horizons and compares GeoState with the camera-matched Copy-Last reference. GeoState improves both subsets under GT and predicted query cameras. On the Dynamic subset, it reduces AbsRel by 13.10% with GT cameras and 9.63% with predicted cameras, with corresponding δ1 gains of 0.0236 and 0.0229. The gains on Static windows are smaller but remain positive: 4.92% and 2.17% relative AbsRel reductions, respectively. The substantially larger gains on Dynamic win- dows indicate that the World Model effectively captures temporal changes in scene geometry.

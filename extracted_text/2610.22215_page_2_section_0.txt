FutureLift discretizes each trajectory proposal into spatially grounded temporal nodes connected to agent histories, map elements, alternative modes, and compatible predictions of other agents. This graph represents a hypo- thetical future scene rather than a compressed trajectory to- ken. A bidirectional graph network refines the complete horizon in one pass and predicts residual coordinate and confidence updates. Requiring only proposal coordinates, scores, and scene context, FutureLift attaches to struc- turally different backbones without modifying their pro- posal mechanisms. The same interface supports marginal and coherent joint-world proposals, enabling social future relations only for the latter. We evaluate FutureLift on Argoverse 2 and the Waymo Open Motion Dataset using multiple backbones in marginal and joint settings, including within-backbone comparisons with native and specialized refiners. These experiments test interface compatibility rather than transferring a learned refiner between backbones or datasets: each refiner is trained for its target setting. FutureLift consistently im- proves the underlying proposals; on the WOMD test set, it achieves 0.5049 minADE and 0.9942 minFDE, the best results among the compared standard-input methods, while maintaining competitive Soft mAP. A separate horizon- resolved analysis on the WOMD validation split shows that backward temporal information flow becomes increasingly beneficial at longer horizons. In summary, our main contributions are: • Proposal-to-Future-Graph Lifting: A backbone- agnostic interface transforms multimodal proposals into heterogeneous future graphs for temporal, map, modal, and joint-only social reasoning. • One-Shot Bidirectional Refinement: One fixed-graph pass lets later future context correct earlier errors without repeated causal sweeps. • Interface Compatibility and Temporal-Direction Analysis: Experiments with multiple backbones on two datasets and in marginal and joint tasks establish consistent gains after setting-specific training, while horizon-resolved validation analysis measures how the contribution of backward temporal information changes with prediction horizon.

If the backbone is sensitive to reducing denoising steps, fewer tokens can be accepted early without degrading generation quality, which limits the attainable speedup. Similarly, more challenging domains may require additional refinement across a larger fraction of tokens. This is evident in world generation, where large viewpoint changes introduce stronger distortions than stan- dard video generation and result in higher mean denoising steps, as shown in Table 3. Our method therefore does not guarantee the same acceleration across different generators or domains. D.2 OTHER DISCUSSIONS Mean Steps and Realized Speed-up. The clean forward pass used to refresh the KV cache adds one full pass per chunk. Assuming computation scales linearly with token evaluations, the ideal speed-up relative to the four-step generator is 5/(¯k + 1). Approaches that reuse noisy KV states, such as CausalRCM (Zheng et al., 2026), could avoid this pass and move the ideal speed-up toward 4/¯k. In practice, throughput does not scale proportionally with the mean denoising depth ¯k. Each chunk continues until its last active token is accepted, incurring forward-pass overhead at every executed step even when few tokens remain active. This overhead therefore depends on maxi k∗(i) within each chunk rather than the average across tokens. Reducing this overhead would bring the realized speed-up closer to the ideal implied by the mean denoising depth. D.3 POTENTIAL APPLICATIONS Our experiments focus on general video generation and world modeling, but token-wise commit- ment may be particularly effective when much of the target content is already constrained by avail- able conditions. In talking-head generation (Huang et al., 2026), for example, the background can remain largely unchanged while motion is concentrated around the face and upper body. In environment-grounded world models such as Seoul World Model (Seo et al., 2026) and World Ob- server (Choi et al., 2026), warped scene observations and reference images provide visual evidence for parts of the target view, while uncovered regions require new content to be generated. Dexterous World Models (Kim et al., 2025) provide static scene renderings along the camera trajectory and generate interaction-induced changes while preserving unaffected content. 19

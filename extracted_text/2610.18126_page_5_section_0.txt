Option 1 is better at td (higher rating, lower price, faster journey, better views) and is the model’s initial choice; option 2 is acceptable but worse. Option 1 depends on an entity named in its evidence: a district, a rail operator, a sales or permit platform, a mountain range or a shopping centre. Lags between deciding and acting range from three hours to three days, and revision deadlines precede the action by 0.5 to 2 hours. A decision’s gold answer is option 2 if some item published in its re- vision window targets option 1, its dependency or an entity upstream of it and blocks option 1 during the activity, and option 1 otherwise. The genera- tor excludes decisions invalidated before they were made and asserts every label (Appendix B). Three conditions. Each condition stresses a dif- ferent part of the theory. DIRECT measures every term of Eq. 3 with an open model; STRESS adds hard negatives that name the option, testing expo- sure (P3) and temporal judgment (P4); INDIRECT moves the reference from the decision text into background knowledge, testing whether the index sets recall (P2). DIRECT spans five domains with independent random news per decision: a decisive item (p=0.5) that names the option or its stated de- pendency, a late item (p=0.3) that blocks the option only after the activity, a harmless item (p=0.5), and two unrelated items. STRESS adds, for every deci- sion, a same-name decoy, a blocking notice about a different entity whose name extends the option’s (“Clove Fork Annex”), and, each with probability 0.85, a late and a harmless notice naming the op- tion itself; eleven rules paraphrase event wording. INDIRECT spans four domains and fixes every pro- portion. Half of the decisions should switch, and their decisive notice uses each of the four ways of reference for 16 decisions; only the first two match the decision text. The other half should stand and receive one of four hard negatives, each for 16 de- cisions: a change that starts after the activity, a change that is over before it starts, harmless news, and a disruption to a facility the user does not need (a tram line for a user who walks). Every deci- sion receives exactly two related notices and one decoy, so neither the amount nor the wording of the news reveals the label.

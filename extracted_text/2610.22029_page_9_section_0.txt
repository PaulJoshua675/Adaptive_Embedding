Accounting units are not measured GPU-hours or FLOPs. 6.6 EQUAL-BUDGET COMPARISON AND SENSITIVITY A separate study on Qwen3-4B-Base compares methods at equal cost. A normalized accounting budget charges a single-stream native baseline 100.0 units and Full’s default configuration 123.7 units, a 23.7% overhead spent on screening, recipient attempts, verification, teacher passes, absorption, confirmation, and state export. Every variant is trained separately for ten rounds with three seeds, and native training absorbs the budget that construction leaves. Full has the lowest mean at 0.8 of its default budget but the highest from 1.0 onward, as Figure 9a shows, so its advantage depends on leaving enough native training after construction and confirmation are paid for. At budget 1.0, four candidates give the largest mean, close to the default of two, and confirmation pools of 32 and 64 questions are also close. The default preservation weight λkeep = 1 gives the largest net absorption gain in Figure 9d. Larger settings consume native-training budget and need not improve the final learner. 7

A Dataset Selection and Compliance A.1 Dataset Selection Statistics Of the 59 candidate SFT datasets surveyed (exclud- ing math and coding topics), 45 were excluded. Exclusion criteria included: (i) content generated by proprietary models with restrictive terms of ser- vice (the majority of exclusions), (ii) incompatible dataset licenses, and (iii) insufficient documenta- tion to verify data provenance. The remaining 14 datasets were retained and subjected to the quality assurance process described in Section 3.1, result- ing in the final 13-dataset Multi-SFT collection. A.2 Regeneration Model Selection Mistral-Small, Qwen3-VL, and Qwen3.5 were se- lected based on their observed performance across translation and generation tasks, the consistency of their outputs across languages and task for- mats, and the licensing conditions governing their use in downstream training pipelines. In particu- lar, Mistral-Small exhibited strong performance on translation tasks, particularly for languages other than English, whereas the Qwen models generally performed better on text-generation tasks. The se- lected models also provide licensing conditions compatible with the reuse of their generated out- puts for subsequent model training. A.3 Regenerated Examples We present a random sample of regenerated ex- amples drawn from 10 datasets across all five lan- guages. Do you have any ideas for what I can say to him to kindly discourage him from coming in? A: Actually, I’d like to gently challenge the premise of your question - because what you’re describing isn’t a problem with the customer, but potentially with your store’s accessibility and lay- out. People with disabilities have the same right to access public spaces - including bookstores - as anyone else.

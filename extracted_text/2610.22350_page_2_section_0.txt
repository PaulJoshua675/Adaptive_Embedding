Existing agent benchmarks typically evaluate agents on well-specified tasks defined by issue reports, speci- fications, or task tickets. Real-world customer delivery, however, poses fundamentally different challenges: forward-deployed engineers (FDEs) must clarify ambiguous requirements, work in live systems, and uncover unstated constraints. To address this gap, we introduce FDE-Gym, a benchmark of 95 end-to-end delivery tasks from 20 customer scenarios across 18 sectors, each adapted by industry experts from real-world delivery projects. In simulated customer environments featuring scripted stakeholders and modifiable customer sys- tems, agents interact with stakeholders and operate on production-like systems to deliver working solutions. Beyond binary task completion, FDE-Gym scores agents on how much their delivered system improves the customer’s own success metric, normalized between 0 (unchanged system) and 1 (reference solution); scores are penalized for stakeholder interactions (messages, trial runs, and waiting time) to discourage excessive or careless engagement. FDE-Gym also provides an online platform where humans can develop and practice FDE skills under the same grading criteria. We evaluate 13 model configurations from seven providers. In the realistic setting (unstated constraints need to be discovered), the best configuration achieves a mean FDE-Gym score of 0.56, and resolves only 14 of the 95 tasks (a score of at least 0.8 with no incident). Discovering unstated constraints is the primary bottleneck: performance drops sharply when models must uncover them without explicit guidance. However, information discovery alone does not account for the gap: even on the 23 tasks where the best configuration obtains every key fact from stakeholders, it achieves a normalized business uplift of 0.8 or above on only 10. These results reveal a substantial gap between solving well-specified tasks and delivering effective AI systems for real customers. Closing this gap requires agents that can surface unstated constraints, operate reliably in live systems, and achieve measurable improvements on customer metrics.

On ImageNet with ViT-B/16, LAMP increases single-image latency from 14.22 to 64.83 ms, while batch-128 throughput changes from 113.38 to 107.89 images/s. With RN50, the corresponding values are 14.81 to 65.86 ms and 118.64 to 112.28 images/s. Regional matching therefore has a larger relative effect on single-image latency than on batched throughput. Peak allocated GPU memory at batch 128 increases from 2.71 to 7.64 GiB with ViT and from 3.02 to 11.55 GiB with RN50.

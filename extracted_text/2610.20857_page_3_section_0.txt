Citation neighborhoods offer an additional discovery route when a query and its target use different words. A citation edge alone does not establish conceptual relevance or technical inheritance. Connected Papers, for example, describes a similarity graph based on co-citation and bibliographic coupling rather than a direct citation tree [8]. We therefore use external graphs as exploratory references, not as ground truth for causal research lineages. 3 Method 3.1 Task, execution modes, and evidence records Given a research idea q, the retrieval task returns an ordered set Rq of at most five distinct works. Each entry includes a title, available bibliographic identifiers, and a concise explanation grounded in the supplied evidence. The benchmark allows publications from 2015–2025 and verified preprint versions of the same work. Different versions are consolidated when their identity is established; a predecessor or follow-up is not automatically the same work. The public system supports retrieval_eval and full_report. The former ends after final ranking and audit export. The latter adds report-oriented evidence refinement, method- /experiment extraction, representative-paper selection, and synthesis artifacts. Optional research extensions are separately selected tasks; none is automatically included in the Top 5 benchmark. Figure 1 summarizes the retrieval-only path. 3.2 Idea structuring, discovery, and identity verification The pipeline preserves the original request and derives 3–7 technical facets, structural cues such as datasets and method families, and higher-level problem/setting/mechanism/assump- tion facets. These generated interpretations aid search and assessment; they do not replace the original idea. Model-recalled seeds and web-derived paper candidates are discovery hypotheses, requiring bibliographic resolution before they can be treated as established identities. The current profile combines recalled seeds, web search, and scholarly metadata. Semantic Scholar supplies paper records and citation neighborhoods. Crossref supplies bibliographic verification and metadata; merely receiving a reference in a Crossref response does not insert it into the candidate pool. Explicit Crossref-reference expansion is disabled in the default profile. arXiv identifier resolution and PDF access remain available even when arXiv keyword search is disabled.

Byte differences alone do not establish different training or an incorrect export. The measured outputs nevertheless show that these Q8 conversions are not interchangeable for the tested scores. This reinforces the scope of the main results: the specified conversion files, rather than a precision-invariant assessment of each named base checkpoint. The bridge uses 48 pilot cases per repeated Qwen checkpoint, selected across task/control vari- ants and the saved score range. It compares conversion, precision, inference kernel, and execution together, rather than isolating quantization. Its maximum differences and flips are reported rather than assumed negligible. The material discrepancies prevent interpreting differences between the pilot and expanded estimates as purely a consequence of more diverse scenarios. These stress cases are not a random estimate of conversion error or diagnostic accuracy.

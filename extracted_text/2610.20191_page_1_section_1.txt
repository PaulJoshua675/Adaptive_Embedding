Early MR studies focused on model development within a single center, where training and evaluation are conducted us- ing EHR data from the same hospital. Representative methods include GAMENet, SafeDrug, and DKGM [1]–[3]. Despite their effectiveness in single-center settings, these methods rely on local data and may perform poorly in low-resource hospitals owing to data sparsity and cold-start issues [4]. Recent studies address single-center limitations through two lines of research: cross-domain and multi-center transfer for cross-hospital knowledge sharing.

Our setup targets five algorithm families: variants of IPPO, PR2-IPPO, IQL, PR2-IQL, and Nash-Q, with and without counterfactual experience replay (CER, e.g., IQL and IQL-CER), and one handcrafted-reward (hc-) counterpart for each learner family. Probabilistic recursive reasoning (PR2) evaluates actions against modelled opponent responses, guiding action selection and policy updates. However, Nash-Q completed only for Stag Hunt due to it be- ing a tractable low-dimensional matrix game, whereas the other environments timed out after 96 hours. The handcrafted-reward baseline tries to capture the same objective faithfully with a Python reward function, but has a different equilibrium set in practice, and thus serves primarily as an auxiliary baseline. Evaluation Metrics. Each fixed profile is evaluated on 1000 episodes per seed, using five training seeds. We select the objective- trained learner and agent with the highest mean satisfaction in this initial evaluation.

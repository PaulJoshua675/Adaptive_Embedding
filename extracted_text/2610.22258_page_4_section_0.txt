IV. RESULTS Figure 2 presents a qualitative comparison of performance of various denoising models on texture images taken from the NUUR-Texture500 dataset. The corresponding quantitative re- sults, averaged over the validation set, are reported in Table I in terms of PSNR, SSIM, STSIM1, STSIM2, and other metrics. The fine-tuned models demonstrate significant improvements across various performance metrics. Although the models were fine-tuned on pointwise losses (e.g. L1), they also exhibited improvements in non-pointwise perceptual metrics such as MS-SSIM, CW-SSIM, STSIM1, STSIM2 and DISTS. Fine-tuned models show a considerable increase in PSNR, ranging from +6.44 to +9.66 dB and subjective metric such as LPIPS shows an improvement of around -0.16 to -0.31 over the baseline models. This result clearly indicates that the baseline models are not adequately equipped to handle textures, and it is only upon fine-tuning, that the performance of the models can be improved significantly.

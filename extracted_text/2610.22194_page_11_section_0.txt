AutoSFT: An Agentic System for Automated Data Research in Supervised Fine-Tuning Automated data optimization. A growing body of work attempts to automate data research, closing the loop between data curation and model training. PostTrainBench [Rank et al., 2026] audits 84 agent runs and finds that almost all converge on SFT with data as the sole search object. On the selection side, TREX [Ma et al., 2026] searches recipes via MCTS, AutoSelection [Wu et al., 2026] formalizes operator-program search over a fixed pool, DataChef [Chen et al., 2026b] trains a policy with a proxy reward, and Luo et al. [2026] iterates on data alone with gains that plateau after round 8–15. On the synthesis side, Autodata [Kulikov et al., 2026] revises generation strategies from solver feedback, ANDES [Zhao et al., 2026] packages synthesis as a callable skill, and BigBang [The Endless Frontier Team, 2026] scales self-evolving synthesis with a critic proxy. Arbor [Jin et al., 2026], AutoSOTA [Li et al., 2026], and the Anthropic automated researcher [Wen et al., 2026] organize research with hypothesis trees, multi-agent decomposition, and diverse initial directions respectively. These systems address either selection or synthesis, while we unify both and retrain from base each round so that every action is individually attributable. Benchmarks for AI research. Benchmarks now evaluate agents on ML research tasks at scales from single Kaggle problems to full R&D pipelines [Chan et al., 2025, Huang et al., 2024, Wijk et al., 2025, Kon et al., 2026, Zhang et al., 2025, Lyu et al., 2026, Garikaparthi et al., 2026, Xu et al., 2026], on AI infrastructure [Ouyang et al., 2025, Chi et al., 2026a, Φ-Bench contributors, 2026, Yeon et al., 2026], and on self-improvement [Zhan et al., 2026, Chi et al., 2026b, Chen et al., 2026a]. Closest to our setting, DataEnvGym [Khan et al., 2025] frames data generation as an RL loop, Curation-Bench [Kang et al., 2026] gives agents iterative control over a fixed pipeline, and RSIBench-Data [Meng et al., 2026] and DataPrep-Bench [Liang et al., 2026] benchmark data-centric recipe search. We differ by requiring from-base retraining each round, repeated evaluation to bound noise, and a verification gate on synthesis.

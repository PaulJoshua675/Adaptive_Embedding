Packing paths reduces teacher latency from 2,663.8 to 827.1 ms per batch; the packed two-evaluation student takes 201.9 ms. Adding deeper history K/V caching changes teacher latency only to 801.4 ms and is disabled for the student. The combined student speedup therefore comes from packing and distillation.

Joint bidirectional training doubles the available supervised training instances without requiring synthetic target generation. 3.3 Four Community Oral Texts To add connected speech and local cultural vocabulary, we incorporated 192 elder- transcribed Masewalkopa sentences. These materials were generously shared with me for pedagogical development by one of my native speaker language instructors in Santa Ana Tlacotenco. The texts represent four distinct thematic registers: 1. Tiankisko: 34 market-dialogue sentences covering exchanges, quantities, and polite requests. 2. Tonakayutl: 45 sentences on traditional maize cultivation, harvesting prac- tices, and food knowledge. 3. Kowatl itech in ikwentsin: 50 narrative sentences describing a traditional snake story, adding complex reference tracking and sequential event structures. 4. Tatahpowilistle: 63 sentences concerning community authority, local oral history, and traditional institutions. Preserving these document titles and categories respects their local genre provenance, even though the current translation model does not condition directly on document identity. 3.4 Asymmetric Target-Side Backtranslation Backtranslation generates synthetic sources from monolingual target data [24].

Preprint GEN4P: VIDEO GENERATORS ARE 4D PERCEIVERS Yitong Xia1 Zeren Jiang2 Chuanxia Zheng1 1PVG, Nanyang Technological University 2VGG, University of Oxford Figure 1: Gen4P repurposes a multi-step Video Generator into a single-step 4D Perceiver, enabling unified feed-forward prediction of dense 3D tracks, point maps, depth maps, and camera poses from a video input. Gen4P is able to track complex motion while maintaining the delicate structure of in-the-wild content, like the thin legs of the flamingo and long-range skiing motion.

Autonomous Play We then roll out the policy autonomously on the robot to collect a play dataset Dplay which achieves sufﬁcient cover- age in the state-transition space as described in Fig. 2b. This autonomous data collection process requires little human supervision and can be parallelized across mutliple robots and environments.

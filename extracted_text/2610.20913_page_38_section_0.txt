TransX: Technical Report The same data construction is used across click and conversion prediction, retrieval, and preference-ranking tasks. For core click/conversion training, we retain at most five sampled negatives per positive for click and one sampled negative per positive for conversion within each serving event, downsampling excess negatives when necessary. Events containing no positive example for a task are discarded. Validation and testing both use the natural label distribution. Retrieval and preference-ranking tasks reuse the same underlying labeled interactions. For preference ranking, a product-defined utility scoring function is further used to construct ordered candidate lists for training. The task-specific modeling objectives and offline evaluation protocols are described in the following sections. Online experiment protocol. For TransX-Base and TransX-Retrieval ramps, the topline business metrics are Online CTR, Conversion (7-day attribution window), DAU (daily active users), and Home Feed Update Views. For TransX-Ranking, the business metrics are the downstream conversion and DAU. Unless otherwise stated, our online experiments randomize at the user level and run for at least 7 days. Treatment and control receive approximately 20% and 20% of eligible traffic, corresponding to approximately 2 billion requests. The reported results use CUPED (Deng et al., 2013) for variance reduction where applicable. Primary online estimands are aggregated at the user level to match the randomization unit, with p99 end-to-end system latency monitored as a guardrail.

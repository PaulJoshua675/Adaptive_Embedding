SD3 baseline sampling collapses onto a narrow visual prototype for underspecified prompts, reproducing nearly identical appearance and scene layout across seeds, a direct consequence of CFG concentrating the conditional distribution onto its high-density peak. DetNO recovers the suppressed degrees of freedom in initial noise, producing semantically diverse outputs that vary in viewpoint, identity, clothes, and scene composition while remaining faithful to the prompt. Abstract. Strong guidance in text-to-image (T2I) diffusion models con- centrates sampling toward high-density modes of the conditional distri- bution, collapsing independently sampled noise vectors onto nearly iden- tical visual prototypes. This limits utility for tasks that demand distri- butional breadth, most critically synthetic data augmentation. Diversity, however, is a collective property of the whole batch: a set can preserve ev- ery pairwise distance yet still collapse onto a low-dimensional subspace. Existing sum-of-pairwise noise-optimization and repulsion objectives re- spond only weakly to this higher-order failure.

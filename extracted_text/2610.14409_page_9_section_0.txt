To our knowledge this is the first CIFAR-10 generation result for a model trained by Equilibrium Propagation. 5.1 HARDWARE-PLAUSIBLE ARCHITECTURES PotentialEP is neuromorphically compatible as an algorithm, but the energy it trains is only as neuro- morphically implementable as the architecture defining it. Two components we inherit from Energy Matching are not easily realised on an analog substrate: group normalisation, which couples every channel at a layer through a shared statistic, and attention, whose pairwise products are not a fixed weighted sum. We therefore ask how much generation quality is lost when both are removed. Replacing them with weight standardisation, which normalises each filter’s weights rather than its activations, residual scaling by 1/√depth, and a flat linear head in place of the ViT (App. I) leaves an architecture with no operation beyond fixed weighted sums and pointwise nonlinearities — both native to analog crossbar hardware, the sums through Ohm’s and Kirchhoff’s laws (Ielmini & Wong, 2018; Sebastian et al., 2020). We train this arm by backpropagation, our compute budget not ex- tending to a second full EP run, and it reaches an FID of 3.51 against the full architecture’s 3.54: the two are indistinguishable at the resolution of the measurement. Generation competitive with the diffusion and flow-matching baselines of Table 2 therefore does not require either component. This architecture is wider (78M against 50M) and used a different schedule and a longer first phase (App. I), so we take it as an existence proof and leave a cheaper recipe to future work. 6

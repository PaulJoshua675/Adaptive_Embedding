From Corollary 18, we know that when the population size N = Θ(n), the runtime required by MO-MFEA to fully cover the Pareto fronts of the LOTZ and DLTB problems is O(n3). Note that the runtime of solving DLTB by multi-objective evolutionary algorithms alone is O(n4) (see [ZLDD24] for GSEMO, NSGA-II, NSGA-III, and SMS-EMOA). Hence, we see a proven speedup by a factor of Θ(n). Besides, this runtime is the same as the one for optimizing the LOTZ alone (see [LTZ04] for (G)SEMO, [ZLD22] for NSGA-II, [ODNS24] for NSGA-III (setting m = 2 for the mLOTZ), [ZD24b] for SMS-EMOA, and [RBLQ24] for SPEA-II (setting m = 2 for the mLOTZ)). In summary, the MO-MFEA, by leveraging knowledge transfer from the simpler LOTZ task, reduces the asymptotic runtime for solving the complex DLTB problem to that of solving LOTZ alone. 31

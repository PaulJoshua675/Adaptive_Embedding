We introduce GACC-Bench to evaluate goal- adaptive context compression in LLMs, using with- held, goal-relevant questions as probes of informa- tion retention. Our evaluation shows that target- goal conditioning improves accuracy over goal- agnostic compression, whereas mismatched-goal conditioning reduces it. Even the strongest evalu- ated compressor remains substantially below full- context performance under tight context-length budgets, particularly in correctly answering all questions associated with a goal. With publicly available data and evaluation code, GACC-Bench supports evaluation across configurable context- length budgets, allowing progress to be measured through higher accuracy at a fixed budget or lower budgets at a target accuracy. This flexibility sup- ports continued evaluation of goal-adaptive com- pression as models improve, tracking advances in both information retention and compression effi- ciency. Limitations One limitation of GACC-Bench is that its evalua- tion relies on a fixed LLM reader and judge. We keep both models identical across all compression conditions to ensure fair comparisons and isolate differences in the retained context.

Fuzzy Geographically Weighted clustering Feng and Flowerdew (1998) stated that the results of the fuzzy clustering are better if the neighborhood effect is added after the fuzzy clustering pro- cess. This is because of the membership values configuration after the fuzzy clustering process. However, this method still has disadvantages. First, the method ignores the influence of areas that do not have the same border. Second, the method shows a population influence that is of great concern in GDA (Mason and Jacobson, 2007). To overcome these weaknesses, Mason and Jacobson (2007) integrate these two processes in the FGWC by adding geographical influence in the form of distance between regions and popula- tions within the region. This affects FGWC to affect the centroid value to make centroids spatially aware.

We presented WaveLLM, a wavelet-domain LLM entropy modeling framework for lossless image compression. It combines a coding- oriented numerical interface, task prompts, and same-level decoded context within a LoRA-adapted Llama-3.2-3B backbone. WaveLLM achieves 2.51, 2.24, and 2.27 bpsp on Kodak, CLIC.pro, and DIV2K, respectively, outperforming existing LLM-based compressors on all three benchmarks while remaining competitive with strong lossless codecs. These results suggest that wavelet subbands provide a more suitable representation for LLM-based probability modeling than the tested flat pixel and residual sequences. By separating image content into wavelet subbands and guiding each prediction with task prompts and decoded context, WaveLLM enables more accurate entropy estimation. Future work will consider distillation, quantization, and near-lossless coding.

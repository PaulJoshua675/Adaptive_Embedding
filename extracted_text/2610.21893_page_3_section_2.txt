We construct GACC-Bench by generating source- grounded questions, grouping them by shared in- formation needs, and synthesizing a high-level goal for each group. Quality control checks question re- dundancy, within-group coherence, goal–question alignment, and cross-goal distinctiveness. Figure 3 summarizes the three stages: instance construction, quality control, and compression evaluation. 3.1 Instance Construction Given a source context c drawn from natural- language documents, code repositories, or inter- action histories, we generate questions grounded in the source. A future goal may depend on a stated fact, a relation among facts, or a condition that changes how those facts should be used. To test whether compression preserves these forms of in- formation, we use seven strategies for generating questions. Information retrieval asks for a fact explicitly stated in the source, such as a reported value or decision.

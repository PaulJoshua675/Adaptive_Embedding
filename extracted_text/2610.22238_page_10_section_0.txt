Discussion about clipping threshold B. By Lemma 5.2, increasing B decreases the clipping- induced error KL(p∗∥p∗ B). However, larger B not only causes higher computational cost (by Proposition 4.1), but also amplifies the misspecification error caused by the inaccurate score function. Similar to the FORS algorithm (Chen et al., 2026), SIRS uses the score function not only for sampling the proposal, but also for calculating the acceptance probability. The two sources of error combined cause the eB terms in the error bound. Therefore, SIRS requires a more accurate score function, especially when B is large.

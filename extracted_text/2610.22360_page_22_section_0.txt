D.2 Fact and Explanation Analysis Fact-level analysis retains the original claim as input and assigns the decision-relevant property to one of thirteen factual categories. Pair-based comparisons contribute an anchor and a negative decision, whereas Identity and Motion Understanding contribute one decision per native example. The assignment describes factual content rather than an exhaustive decomposition of every claim (Appendix H).

FENAS: A Few-Shot Meta-Ensembling Framework for Efficient Neural Architecture Search with Synergized Zero-Cost Proxies Yifeng Mao1, Yuze Feng1, Yutong Shen1, Liyang Gao1, and Zhixiang He1* Beijing University of Technology, Beijing, China maoyifeng@emails.bjut.edu.cn, zxhe@bjut.edu.cn *Corresponding author Abstract. Efficient neural architecture search (NAS) requires accurate performance estimation under limited evaluation budgets. Existing zero- cost proxies (ZCPs) provide fast training-free signals, but each proxy usu- ally captures only a partial property of an architecture, leading to unsta- ble rankings across datasets and search spaces. In contrast, GNN-based predictors can improve accuracy, but often depend on labeled architec- tures, graph encoders, and costly representation learning, which makes them less suitable for extreme few-shot settings. We propose FENAS, a topology-agnostic few-shot prediction framework that turns complemen- tary initialization priors into robust ranking signals. Its core component, CAST, is a feature pipeline that combines copula alignment and syn- ergy transformation. CAST maps heterogeneous ZCPs into a compact feature manifold, enabling lightweight meta-learners to produce reliable rankings from scarce supervision. On NAS-Bench-201, with only 0.5% la- beled samples, FENAS achieves a Kendall’s Tau (τ) of 0.70, outperform- ing representative topology-based few-shot predictors in this low-label regime. In closed-domain search, FENAS evaluates the NAS-Bench-201 space in approximately 1138 seconds and achieves competitive test ac- curacy. By providing a fast, reliable, and structurally simple evaluation mechanism, FENAS supports the design of automated neural architec- tures under limited evaluation budgets.

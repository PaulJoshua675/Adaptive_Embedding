Momentum sign m selects positive or negative 20-day pre-release returns of the same block- specific magnitude, sampled uniformly from 3–12% and rounded to one decimal. The market is flat. Sentiment sign s selects one of six positive/negative commentary pairs, explicitly described as opinion with no additional facts. Pair assignments are randomized and balanced across blocks (four blocks per pair). Three introductions are similarly assigned (eight blocks per introduction). Every block uses both section orders: facts–prices–commentary and commentary–prices–facts. Pair and introduction are between-block assignments; order and the three financial inputs are crossed within blocks. The eight (m, s, e) combinations and two orders give 16 semantic scenarios per block and 384 per model. All receive three answer encodings: A=positive/B=negative (AB0), the reversed as- signment (AB1), and U=positive/D=negative (UD). Thus there are 1,152 primary prompts per model. Counts refer to repeated counterfactual model evaluations, not independent market events. 3.2 Controls and expanded coverage The isolated numerical diagnostic crosses 24 blocks, two surprise signs, and two sentence forms: 96 scenarios. Each receives both A/B assignments and natural beat/miss prefixes, giving 288 diagnostic prompts. Commentary removal covers every block, both momentum and surprise signs, facts-first order, and both assignments (192 prompts, 96 averaged cases). The original natural-word and office controls selected the first complete block assigned to each commentary pair: 96 prompts each. A coverage audit after three completed expanded runs found that all six selected blocks were losses. An internally timestamped amendment adds the first profitable block for each pair, selected only from frozen block metadata. The resulting controls are balanced for profitability and pair: 192 word prompts and 192 office prompts (96 averaged office cases). Annual-change and gap-size balance is not guaranteed in this subset. The original primary, diagnostic, and neutral designs remain unchanged; these additional controls are exploratory. The original freeze covers 1,824 cases per model; the amendment adds 192. Combined coverage is 2,016 prompts per model, or 12,096 across six models. Another 48 pilot bridge cases for each repeated Qwen checkpoint give 12,192 newly scored records. The 1,248 saved pilot records are distinct and are not pooled into primary estimates. The six commentary pairs are authored variants from one construction process, not independent human authors or sentiment annotations. Numerical signs and gap sizes broaden scenario coverage, but the experiment still uses short synthetic disclosures rather than observed financial news. These provenance differences and quantization are disclosed rather than assumed immaterial. Pinned tokenizer revisions do not certify the source weight revision or source precision used by any downloaded conversion, including first-party files. The distributed manifests identify the actual conversion files and their SHA-256 hashes.

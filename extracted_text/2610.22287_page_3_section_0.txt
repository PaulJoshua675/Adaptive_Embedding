For each agent, a deterministic finite automa- ton (DFA) tracks whether the labelled trace of an episode satisfies the ltl𝑓path formula inside its probabilistic constraint. Its state summarises the history relevant to that formula and is updated as each new state label is read. For example, the automaton in Figure 1 remembers whether a breach has occurred and whether a collision has made the objective impossible. At episode end, its accepting states identify successful traces, and averaging these binary out- comes estimates satisfaction probability. In practice, temporal objectives are often simple and avoid deep temporal nesting, allow- ing existing tools to construct their automata in milliseconds.

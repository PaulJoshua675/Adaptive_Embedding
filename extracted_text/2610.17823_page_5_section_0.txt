When 𝛾= 0, the prior regularization term vanishes and (8) reduces to the entmax variational objective from (4). In the limit 𝛼→1, Tsallis entropy converges to Shannon entropy, and the sparse mapping becomes dense softmax. When additionally 𝛾= 0, the formulation reduces to the standard softmax cross-entropy objective. In contrast, the proposed objective provides sparse competition with prior-aware support selection. 4.4 Mechanism: Context-Dependent Debiasing The proposed objective combines sparse competition and prior cor- rection in a synergistic way. Unlike standard logit adjustment, the popularity prior affects not only the values of the probabilities, but also which items participate in the gradient updates through the sparse support condition (12). Sparsity acts as a mechanism that controls the interaction between contextual relevance and prior correction. Popular but irrelevant items receive zero probability. Popular and relevant items remain in the competition with ad- justment. Unpopular but highly relevant items can also enter the support through sufficiently strong contextual scores. As a result, the model is trained to discriminate the target item from a dynamically selected set of hard negatives that are both con- textually relevant and popularity-aware. This mechanism can be in- terpreted as a differentiable, context-dependent form of popularity- based negative sampling: the prior increases the chance that popu- lar items enter the competition, but only when they are plausible alternatives. To summarize, PR-entmax provides context-dependent debias- ing through prior-aware support selection: popularity shapes the competitive structure of training, while final ranking is performed using the learned contextual scores. 5 Experiments This section evaluates PR-entmax across multiple datasets and se- quential recommendation architectures.

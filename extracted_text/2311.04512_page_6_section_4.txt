As for single-agent evaluation, we adopt the standard metrics in Argoverse Motion Forecasting Competition [41], includ- ing minimum Final Displacement Error (minFDE), minimum Average Displacement Error (minADE), Miss Rate (MR) and Brier minimum Final Displacement Error (brier-minFDE). For multi-agent evaluation, we take the metrics of Argoverse2 Multi-World Forecasting Challenge [42], including Average Brier Minimum Final Displacement Error (avgB-MinFDE), Average Minimum Final Displacement Error (avgMinFDE), Average Minimum Average Displacement Error (avgMinADE) and Actor Collision Rate (actorCR). Implementation Details. The distance threshold of A2L, L2A and A2A are set to 7, 6, and 100 meters, respectively. The hidden size of the MLP Block is set as 128.

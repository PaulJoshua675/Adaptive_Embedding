Scores compare methods within each benchmark: five benchmarks use the shared OmniDocBench evaluator, while FinixDocBench pools its Digital and Photo tracks using its official evaluator. Real5 and Wild reuse OmniDocBench source pages and assess capture robustness. Appendix C.1.5 specifies the evaluated scope and score definitions, including the adapted DocPTBench composite. Implementation details. We use converted Infinity-Doc2-5M annotations (Huang et al., 2026) for document SFT followed by RL. SFT provides the initial checkpoint: Qwen3.5-2B trained on 700K pages in the primary setting, with additional settings covering 50K-page checkpoints, Qwen3.5-4B, and Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct. Within each setting, all RL methods start from the same checkpoint and share the update budget. Init reports its measured benchmark score before RL. Table 1 reports primary RL Overall scores as means ± standard deviations over five seeds. Appendices C.1.1, C.1.3, and C.4 provide further details. 4.2. On the primary OmniDocBench v1.6 benchmark, P2TPO reaches 93.12 ± 0.23, a gain of 0.95 points from its pre-RL score and 0.74 points over the strongest response-level baseline. It also exceeds the strongest response-level mean on all 12 external splits, with margins from 0.19 points on DocPTBench Dewarp to 1.56 points on FinixDocBench. The next-largest margins occur on PureDocBench Real (+1.44) and Clean (+1.24), and the gains hold across all five Real5-OmniDocBench capture conditions. 4.3.

For NDCG@100, the method remains highly competi- tive, being slightly below the strongest baseline on Yoochoose and Zvuk for SASRec, and on ML-1M and Yoochoose for GRU4Rec. Overall, the observed improvements are consistent across both backbone models, indicating that the effect of prior-aware sparse competition generalizes across different sequential recommenda- tion architectures. Beyond recommendation quality, we verify that PR-entmax in- troduces no additional time or memory overhead compared to standard Entmax. The prior term only adds a constant shift to the logits, leaving the sparse support computation unchanged. On large datasets (Zvuk), entmax-based training is about 20% slower than softmax cross-entropy due to iterative computation of threshold 𝜏 that determines the sparse support. On smaller datasets (ML-1M, Yoochoose), all methods are comparable.

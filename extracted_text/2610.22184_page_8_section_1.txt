We presented a pipeline for learning whole-body dexterous manipulation at scale: contact configurations are enumerated into mode-typed whole-body grasps, and those grasps seed large-scale reinforcement learning under common rewards to dexterously reposition objects around the body. We showed how to use this large-scale whole-body dexterous manipu- lation data to train a shared controller that refines a coarse command stream into stable control and supports teleoper- ation. Although WBD-EX is trained without a lower-body controller, the distilled policy deploys on a standing and walking G1, holding objects through leaning and walking during the shelf task. Future work can naturally extend the same formulation to leg contact: generating combinations of arm and leg grasps, training WBD-EX jointly with a whole-body controller across this expanded grasp dataset, and distilling it into a policy to enable behaviors such as bracing a heavy box on a knee while the arms reposition.

Statement on the Use of AI Assistants We disclose that we used generative AI for proof- reading and grammar checking with the prompt: “Edit grammar errors. Avoid being verbose or lengthy, and do not paraphrase much from the orig- inal sentences.” We also used GitHub Copilot in Visual Studio Code, with GPT-5-mini where avail- able, for coding assistance. The human authors reviewed the AI-generated suggestions and made the final edits.

Most imaging techniques inevitably introduce noise, which is further exacerbated by environmental conditions, varying lighting conditions and transmission errors. This results in distortion, loss of image information, and overall degradation in visual quality. The presence of noise adversely impacts sub- sequent image/video processing/analysis tasks. Consequently, image denoising is a critical component of modern image processing pipelines. Image denoising is central to denoising diffusion models [1], [2] which are SOTA generative models. The goal of image denoising is to recover the pristine image x ∈Rm from its noisy observation y ∈Rm. However, since perfect restoration is not possible, the aim is to estimate x as accurately as possible. Noise introduces distortions that obscure important details, making estimation of x a chal- lenging task.

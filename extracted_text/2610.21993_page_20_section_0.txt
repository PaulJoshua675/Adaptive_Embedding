Qualitative Results Figure 8 compares four representative DFC2020-S1 scenes across three configurations re- ported in this paper: the standard-resolution ConvNeXt encoder (pretrained, no LLRD; Ta- ble 7), the ConvNeXt-RS encoder without pretraining (no LLRD; Table 5), and our finetuned ConvNeXt-RS encoder (pretrained, LLRD; Table 5). The first row presents a mixed landscape dominated by shrubland and cropland, with smaller barren, urban, and forest patches and a small water body. All three predictions broadly match the ground truth, but notable differences are already apparent: the standard ConvNeXt encoder over- predicts cropland in the central region and recovers almost none of the forest; the non-pretrained ConvNeXt-RS encoder collapses most of the shrubland into cropland, introduces spurious forest in areas the ground truth labels shrubland, and suppresses the barren regions; in contrast, the pretrained ConvNeXt-RS encoder recovers the largest share of the ground truth’s shrubland and retains the most barren land. In the second row, a cropland-dominated scene containing a prominent water body, all three configurations accurately capture the water feature and surrounding cropland. However, both the standard-resolution ConvNeXt encoder and the non-pretrained ConvNeXt-RS encoder make spurious predictions of other classes in the region where the ground truth shows barren land, whereas the pretrained ConvNeXt-RS encoder avoids these spurious cross-class predictions but instead over-extends cropland into the barren region. The third row is the most heterogeneous scene, mixing forest, cropland, grassland, and ur- ban patches. All three configurations recover the overall spatial layout, though predictions are smoother than the ground truth and suppress fine-grained class boundaries; urban patches are captured at broadly similar locations across all three models. The main variation again separates the non-pretrained encoder from the two pretrained configurations: it reassigns a substantial part of the ground truth’s cropland to grassland, whereas both pretrained configurations preserve the cropland-grassland boundary more closely. In the fourth row, a largely forested scene with scattered grassland, all three predictions are predominantly dark green and closely resemble the ground truth, differing mainly in that the non- pretrained encoder places more of its grassland predictions outside the ground truth’s grassland patches than either pretrained configuration.

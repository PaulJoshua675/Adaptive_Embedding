DocID in Generative Retrieval Generative re- trieval maps a query directly to a DocID. During training, the DocID serves as the output target for both document-to-DocID (indexing) and query-to- DocID (retrieval) training. For text-based iden- tifiers, titles (De Cao et al., 2020), URLs (Zhou et al., 2022), document substrings (Bevilacqua et al., 2022), and term sets (Zhang et al., 2024) are commonly used as identifiers. In contrast, numeric identifiers are often derived through using cluster- ing methods such as k-means (Wang et al., 2022) or through representation-based quantization meth- ods such as Product Quantization (PQ), Residual Quantization (RQ) and RQ-VAE (Zhou et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2023; Rajput et al., 2023). These IDs are semantically structured. Although the numeric identifiers produce compact beam trees and enable more efficient decoding, they often suffer from lim- ited generalization. Hashing in Retrieval Hashing has been widely used for efficient retrieval by providing compact representations and approximate similarity mea- sures based on Hamming distance (Yamada et al., 2021; He et al., 2025). Recently, FLASH (Zhang et al., 2026a) uses hash codes as the first step of retrieval: the generated code seeds a Hamming- radius search and is reconstructed into a continu- ous embedding for reranking. Our method follows generative retrieval paradigm: it directly gener- ates valid DocIDs, decoded token by token un- der a prefix-tree constraint and ranked by log- probability. Dynamic Corpus Adaptation for Numeric Iden- tifiers Existing work primarily focuses on retrain- ing the retrieval model when new documents are arrived: DSI++ (Mehta et al., 2023) reduces catas- trophic forgetting by optimizing for flatter loss landscapes and incorporating pseudo-queries in continual training. IncDSI (Kishore et al., 2023) perform a constraint optimization to adapt to dy- namic corpus, this avoids the full re-training of the decoder. However, the method is restricted to atomic document identifiers. DOME (Zhang et al., 2026b) further generalizes document insertion by optimizing edit vectors with soft-label guidance, enabling effective adaptation under structured doc- ument identifier schemes.

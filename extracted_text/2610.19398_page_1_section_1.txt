Iterative retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) accumulates knowledge from retrieved evi- dence to answer complex questions, but ex- isting methods struggle to reconstruct entity correspondences and inferential dependencies across retrieved passages as new information emerges. We propose RCER, an iterative RAG method that reconstructs these cross-evidence relations directly from accumulated source pas- sages. Inter-round relational evidence ac- quisition identifies entity correspondences and relational dependencies to drive dynamic prob- lem decomposition and iterative retrieval. Rea- soning context reconstruction independently constructs a coherent reasoning context through joint selection of complementary passages and derivation of cross-evidence inferences. Cross- evidence compositional reasoning leverages these inferences to establish entity correspon- dences and compose relations across selected passages for answer derivation. Experiments show that RCER outperforms the best base- lines by 2.83 EM and 3.06 F1 points on aver- age across three multi-hop QA benchmarks and achieves superior performance on narrative and implicit reasoning tasks. 1

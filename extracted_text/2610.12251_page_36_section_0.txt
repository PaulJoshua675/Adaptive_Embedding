E.2 The amplified host Repeated on N3, the measurement of Table 4 gives the rate at which some copy of some state bit leaves the orbit: 9.79 × 10−7, 8.47 × 10−5, 2.46 × 10−3 and 2.58 × 10−2 per step at s = 0.24, 0.27, 0.30 and 0.33. These rates lie below the profile bound of Theorem 8.8(ii) by factors between 9.8 and 19, since a flip in a layer before the last is absorbed unless a second error meets it (Proposition 8.7(iii)). Restricted to the rows of the last layer, whose wrong copies nothing outvotes, the bound is 8.54 × 10−5 at s = 0.27, inside the 95% interval [6.65, 10.6] × 10−5 of the measured rate, which comes from 74 events. At s = 0.30 to 0.36 the measured rate exceeds this restricted bound by factors 1.4 to 1.6, a wrong copy of an earlier layer then meeting a second error more often.

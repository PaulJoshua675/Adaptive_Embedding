Limitations Despite its promising results, CARA has several limitations. First, causal relevance is operational- ized through LLM-based assessments of conse- quentiality and essentiality rather than formal infer- ence. These assessments may be sensitive to model biases, prompting strategies, and errors in evalu- ating evidence contributions. Second, although CARA reduces retrieval operations and examined nodes, the additional causal reasoning module in- creases computational overhead and inference la- tency, particularly for relation-intensive questions. Third, downstream question answering evaluation relies on automatically constructed ground-truth answers and LLM-based faithfulness assessments, which may introduce evaluation noise. Future work will explore more efficient causal assessments and alternative evaluations.

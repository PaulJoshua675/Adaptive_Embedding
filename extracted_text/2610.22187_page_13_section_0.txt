The Manifesto Project codebook (Volkens et al., 2021) provides policy cat- egories covering economic intervention, welfare, traditional morality, civil liberties, law and order, and social groups. We adapted these categories to U.S. news text and organized them into economic policy, social and cultural values, government au- thority, and group-based equality. We also included explicit support for or criticism of political actors as partisan cues, yielding the five dimensions in Table 3. Sentiment. We referred to distinctions between sentiment polarity, subjectivity, and degrees of eval- uation (Pang and Lee, 2008), along with VADER’s treatment of sentiment intensity (Hutto and Gilbert, 2014). We adapted these ideas to movie reviews through content evaluation, emotional tone, lan- guage intensity, and recommendation signals. The latter captures whether the reviewer encourages or discourages watching the film, complementing the review’s evaluative language. Toxicity. We adapted the attribute definitions in Jigsaw’s Perspective API documentation (Jigsaw, n.d.). Hate and harassment combines criteria from toxicity and severe toxicity, while identity attack, insult, profanity, and threat correspond to the API’s named attributes. We translated these definitions into evidence of increasing toxicity, with absence of the attribute at the low end. C LLM Parameter Settings This section details generation settings of the five models. As shown in Table 5, for the three open models, we followed the sampling settings recommended on each model’s Hugging Face page. The Qwen3.8 27B card recommends temperature 1.0, top-p 0.95, top-k 20, min-p 0, presence penalty 0, and rep- etition penalty 1.0 for its thinking mode.6 The DeepSeek V4 Flash card recommends temperature 1.0 and top-p 1.0 for tasks other than agentic use.7 The GLM 5.3 Flash card gives no general recom- mendation, so we used temperature 1.0 and top-p 0.95, the values the card reports for its own evalua- tions, and set clear_thinking to true as the card advises for chat use.8 Parameters that a card does not mention were left at the endpoint default. The main analyses use low, the lowest level of each open model, and Appendix E.1 reports the higher levels. For the two proprietary models, we ran GPT 5.6 Luna through the OpenAI Responses API and Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite through the Gemini API, using each provider’s batch service. For both models we chose the lowest reasoning level that keeps reasoning enabled. For GPT 5.6 Luna this is low, since none turns reasoning off and the de- fault is medium.9 For Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite this is minimal, which is the model’s default and which Google recommends for classification and extrac- tion tasks.10 We did not set temperature, top-p, or top-k for either model.

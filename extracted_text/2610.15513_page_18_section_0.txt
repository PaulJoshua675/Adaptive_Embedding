We formalize REPRO’s nested structure as a bi-level optimization problem in which an outer-level population evolution is coupled to an inner-level strategy selection through a shared feedback channel. The two levels are coupled through a feedback channel: offspring performance updates the lower-level strategy posteriors, and the upper-level population is constructed from the offspring the lower level produces. This formulation captures REPRO’s structural insight: the upper-level optimizer maintains popu- lation diversity through Pareto selection, while the lower-level optimizer learns which strategies work in which contexts. The two levels operate at different time scales (upper level updates per generation; lower level updates after every mutation) and see different feedback signals (upper level: Pareto frontier quality; lower level: per-strategy success rates), but share a common objective. Separating the two levels also clarifies where REPRO draws on prior work and where it introduces new machinery: the upper level specializes standard multi-objective evolutionary computation to the adversarial prompt setting, while the lower level extends contextual bandit selection with the hybrid-weight mechanism of §3.3.2. 3.6.2 Design Properties. We state three properties of the framework. The first follows directly from REPRO’s construction and establishes a basic correctness property. The second and third connect REPRO’s design choices to known results in online learning and evolutionary computation, and clarify the regime in which REPRO operates relative to those results. None of the three is a new theorem, and Properties 2 and 3 hold only under assumptions that REPRO’s setting relaxes; we label them properties to mark them as design commitments that the architecture is built to honor, not as results proved here. Property 1 (Monotonic Best-So-Far) With elitism parameter 𝐸≥1, REPRO’s best-so-far candidate by 𝑓1 (ASR) is non-decreasing across generations. This follows directly from elite preservation (§3.4.5): the candidate with highest 𝑓1 in generation 𝑡is guaranteed to survive into generation 𝑡+ 1, so the best-so-far value cannot decrease. Together with the global-best tracking mechanism of §3.4.1, Property 3.6.2 ensures that REPRO’s reported output is never worse than any candidate it has seen during the run, eliminating a failure mode that can affect non-elitist evolutionary algorithms.

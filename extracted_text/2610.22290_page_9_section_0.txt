HNM is partially supported by the Center on Responsible AI and Governance (CRAIG) at OSU. Limitations We recognize the following limitations and direc- tions of future work. Base model. Our experiments use Qwen3.5-4B- Base, whose moderate size makes it feasible to conduct multiple training runs across our method and baselines within our available compute budget. This model size is also relevant to practical RL post- training settings, making it a meaningful model for studying strategy preservation. Nevertheless, evaluating ELITEREPLAY on larger models (e.g. 8B), and across different model families is a natural direction for future work. Domain. Our experiments focus on text-to-SQL, which provides a suitable testbed for studying solu- tion diversity because questions can admit multiple correct queries, correctness can be verified through execution, and SQL’s formal structure allows strate- gies to be distinguished using abstract syntax trees. Extending this evaluation to other domains where solution strategies lack a clear structural represen- tation, as in many mathematical reasoning tasks, might be challenging. Nevertheless, we expect the approach to be applicable to other domains with verifiable solutions and meaningful behavioral dis- tinctions, such as program optimization or algo- rithmic coding and we view this as an important direction for future work.

Long horizon: appearance-only tracklet merge Sports broadcasts remove players from view for seconds: on SportsMOT-val football, 64% of identity switches follow gaps over 20 frames, 97% of those full camera exits (median 111 frames), with players well-detected at both gap edges: an out-of-view identity failure, unreachable by in-view as- sociation geometry. Our offline stage runs on the online output after linear interpolation of gaps ≤10 frames. Each tracklet carries an appearance prototype (re-normalized mean of its per-frame embeddings); ordered pairs with temporal gap ≤300 link greedily in descending prototype cosine order (threshold 0.85), under a union-find with each tail and head usable once. The customary ingredient we remove is the motion gate, which extrapolates a tracklet’s terminal velocity (a five- frame tail estimate) across the gap and vetoes candidates landing more than τv=2 box diagonals from the next track- let’s start. Over a hundred-frame gap under a panning cam- era that extrapolation carries no information, so the veto must go and appearance must carry the decision alone; em- pirically the gate, not the threshold or gap cap, was the bind- ing constraint (Sec. 4.5). 4. Experiments 4.1. Setup Datasets. SportsMOT [11]: 45 val sequences (15 each basketball, football, volleyball) for all tuning and ablations; 150 test sequences evaluated exclusively through the offi- cial evaluation server. DanceTrack [39] val (25 sequences) serves as a transfer benchmark: non-linear motion under a largely static camera. We report HOTA [26] with its de- tection (DetA) and association (AssA) components, identity F1 (IDF1), multi-object tracking accuracy (MOTA), and the number of identity switches (IDs). Implementation. Nothing in the system is trained. The detector is YOLOX-X [15] with the public SportsMOT weights from the MixSort model zoo [11]. Two releases exist, and we keep them separate: every validation-set ex- periment and ablation in this paper uses the checkpoint trained on the train split only, so no validation frame was seen by the detector; the test-set submissions use the train+val checkpoint, the release intended for test sub- missions. Validation-set DetA is therefore not inflated by detector exposure; appearance embeddings come from the sports-domain OSNet released with Deep-EIoU [20] (DanceTrack rows use FastReID SBS-S50 [17]). Associ- ation hyperparameters are given in Secs. 3.4–3.8; the input- identical runs change only detector-calibration thresholds (τH 0.6→0.5, spawn 0.7→0.6, match 0.25→0.2). All ex- periments run on a single RTX 3060 laptop GPU (6 GB). Detections, embeddings, and camera transforms are cached once, so every ablation shares byte-identical inputs; an md5-verified regression anchor guarantees the baseline con- figuration reproduces exactly across the campaign.

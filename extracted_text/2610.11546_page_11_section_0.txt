Table 2. Pseudocode of the proposed DRL-DCO algorithm. Algorithm 1: DRL-governed dynamic DE–CMA-ES Orchestration (DRL-DCO) Input: FEMU or general single-objective objective function f(θ), search bounds, population size, maximum episodes Nepi, maximum generations per episode Ngen. Output: The optimal solution vector θ∗. 1: Initialize DE operators, CMA-ES parameters, actor π(s; θπ), critic Q(s, a; θQ), and replay memory. 2: for episode e = 1, . . . , Nepi do 3: Initialize or inherit population χ(0) according to the warm-up/training strategy. 4: Construct initial state s(0) from χ(0). 5: for generation t = 0, . . . , Ngen −1 do 6: Generate action a(t) = π(s(t); θπ) = [F, CR, λ, LRcov, ∆σ, pres, reli, β](t). 7: Orchestrate: generate offspring with DE and CMA-ES, allocated through β(t). 8: Evaluate offspring using f(θ). 9: Update population to χ(t+1), perform CMA-ES adaptation, and construct s(t+1). 10: if agent-training phase then 11: Compute reward r(t+1) from the transition s(t) →s(t+1). 12: Store (s(t), a(t), r(t+1), s(t+1)) in replay memory. 13: Update θπ and θQ by the deterministic policy gradient method. 14: else inference only (no reward, no network updates). 15: if f(t+1) best < ε then terminate the episode early. 16: end for; record the best solution of the current episode. 17: end for; return θ∗. 3.

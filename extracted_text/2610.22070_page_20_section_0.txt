In conclusion, this analysis establishes the theoretical feasibility of compressing the effect of historical context into low-rank parameter updates of the MLP modules within a Transformer block. In particular, it shows that, under the assumptions described above, a history-dependent feature transformation induced by historical context can be realized or approximated through LoRA-style updates to the MLP projections. Importantly, this analysis does not claim that MaLoW exactly reconstructs the analytical history matrix M _ H, nor that its learned recurrent state and generated adapters are equivalent to full-context attention. Rather, the derivation provides a mathematical interpretation demonstrating that such a parameter-space representation of historical information is feasible. Our empirical results further show that MaLoW provides an effective learned instantiation of this approximation in practice.

Fitness does not reward emitting or approaching the food or the partner. Table 2 summarizes the conditions. The compass condition adds two inputs to the network: the direction and proximity to the food, information that no other condition receives. The large arena condition uses a 1.5 m square and 90 s episodes with the same food zone. All runs were performed on the same computer, a Mac mini with an M1 processor, running six processes in parallel. A run of 150 generations takes approximately six hours in the small arena condition.

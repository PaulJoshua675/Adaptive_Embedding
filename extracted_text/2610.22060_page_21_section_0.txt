E Sampling hyperparameters We report the sampling hyperparameters for all the main results we presented in the paper. The knobs. FeaTok samples two branches, and they are configured independently: the 256 patch positions and the single global position each carry their own guidance scale, temperature and bit-group allocation. We write an allocation as its list of group sizes, so [2, 2, 5, 7] unmasks a token’s 16 bits in four groups of 2, 2, 5 and 7 bits, and [bk] abbreviates k groups of b bits. The list sums to the bits per token (16 at 2562, 10 at 5122) and its length sets the sampling cost, since a k-group schedule issues k+1 forwards per position. A guidance scale of 1.0 is no-guidance, so every row without guidance carries patch guidance 1.0. The global branch is unguided in every configuration without guidance and in half of the guided ones. Reduced-step RAEv2. The released RAEv2 sampling configuration for ImageNet 256 × 256 (https://github .com/nanovisionx/RAEv2/blob/8a0d238/configs/stage2/sampling/imagenet-dinov3l-k7.yaml) uses 100 ODE steps and an internal-guidance scale of 1.78, which we use for all other RAEv2 results. For the 3- and 6-step RAEv2 rows of Tab. 3, we select the internal-guidance scale with the lowest gFDr6 among the evaluated settings, rather than keeping the released scale of 1.78. The selected scales are 4.0 and 2.6, respectively. Sampling hyperparameters for resolution 512 × 512. At 512 a token carries 10 bits, so the allocations sum to 10 rather than 16.

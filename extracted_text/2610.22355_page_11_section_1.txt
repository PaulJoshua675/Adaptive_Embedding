This work introduces the Decomposed Graded Verifier as an effective reward model for improving vi- sual generation in both traditional text-to-image tasks and structured world-modeling scenarios. Our contributions are twofold. First, we propose a Decoupled RL training strategy that jointly optimizes sub- question-level binary judgment and global alignment scoring, using precise local feedback to develop fine-grained verification capabilities that support accurate continuous scoring. Empirical results further demonstrate improved out-of-domain multimodal critic capabilities. Second, focusing on rule-governed world modeling, we introduce a pairwise verification paradigm that explicitly incorporates ground-truth images as visual references. This simplifies verification by shifting from a reason-then-judge process to direct reference-based comparison, thereby producing more accurate and reliable reward signals. Extensive experiments validate the effectiveness of both the Decomposed Graded Verifier trained with our Decoupled RL strategy and the pairwise verification paradigm.

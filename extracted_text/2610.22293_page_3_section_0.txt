Overall, this work shows that catastrophic forgetting is not an inevitable consequence of learning, but depends on where the update lands in weight space, and provides a simple, practical method for improving capability retention while acquiring new capabilities.. 2 Subspace Tuning Across many complex systems, a small number of leading modes of a linear operator capture much of its dominant structure, while the remaining spectrum describes lower-energy variation [4, 42]. Deep neural network weight matrices exhibit similarly concentrated and heavy-tailed spectra [30], and interventions on their leading singular components can disproportionately affect model performance [43]. These observations motivate preserving the principal singular directions of pretrained weights during adaptation. We first formalize this constraint (Sec. 2.1), show that it must be enforced on the applied optimizer update rather than the gradient (Sec. 2.2), and introduce a basis refresh that trades stricter protection for continued adaptability (Sec. 2.3). 2.1 Projection Formulation Consider a single weight matrix W ∈Rdout×din, which maps an input activation x to an output Wx. Its SVD, W = P i σi uiv⊤ i , reads off the component of x along the input direction vi (via v⊤ i x) and writes it, scaled by σi, onto the output direction ui. So the right singular vectors vi are the input directions the layer responds to, and the left singular vectors ui are the output directions it produces. The top-k of these, Vk = [v1, . . . , vk] and Uk = [u1, . . . , uk], are what we intend to protect.

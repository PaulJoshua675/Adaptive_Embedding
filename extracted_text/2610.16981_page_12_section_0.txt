12 Natkamon Tovanich and C´elestin Coquid´e 5 Discussion and Future Work We introduced a transaction-aware representation for tracing multi-token flows and compared shortest path configurations on Aave borrow–repay flows. The results reveal a trade-off between reachability and interpretability: teleporta- tion edges recover more paths but may introduce implausible shortcuts, whereas restricting inter-transaction edges reduces coverage. Reachability should thus not imply reconstruction accuracy. Heuristic weighting balances these effects by retaining uncertain edges as fallbacks while favoring transfers, swaps, and tem- porally proximate links. Frequent token patterns reveal round-trip swaps and supply–withdraw cycles, but also artifacts from debt-token shortcuts and miss- ing asset transformations. Our approach has some limitations. A shortest path cannot capture flows that split, merge, or form cycles, and it may bypass intermediate transactions by linking borrowing directly to repayment. Since no ground truth exists (only the borrow and repay events are observed, while the route between them is inferred), we assess structural properties and pattern interpretability, treating the paths as hypotheses rather than verified trajectories. Future work could apply k-shortest paths, flow decomposition, and cycle detection to recover more complete strategies, and use sensitivity analysis over the weights and alternative allocation rules (e.g., FIFO, Haircut) to quantify the uncertainty from inferred edges and heuristic weights. As a partial sanity check, individual conversion steps could be validated against event logs (e.g., supply, withdrawal, and swap events). Beyond event logs, synthetic DeFi transaction data with known ground truth could be generated to evaluate the accuracy of the entire set of inferred paths. To better characterize borrowers’ trading strategies, we plan to enrich the network model by representing each transaction as a hypergraph and incorpo- rating account and protocol labels, transaction semantics, and inter-transaction timing. More broadly, we aim to extend the approach beyond Aave by adding cycle-aware tracing and path uncertainty analysis to study evolving DeFi use cases such as on-chain trading, liquidity management, and arbitrage.

Each round runs the four steps of Figure 3. It (1) explores native branches from the root, (2) screens them with the root for a parameter recipient, (3) recovers current and archived experience into that recipient, and (4) confirms the result against the root on fresh probes. 4.1 EXPLORE AND SCREEN A RECIPIENT On the base loop of Section 3, EVOHEIR builds multi-branch exploration, screening of realized updates, experience absorption, and independent confirmation. Native endpoints and any predeclared training-prefix checkpoints form Cnat t . Each branch supplies a resumable state and responses that qualify as experience. An Experience Donor contributes the latter without having to supply inherited parameters or remain available as a teacher model. The controller freezes native candidates before shared screening on P scr t . Paired root-relative scores select the recipient Ssel t from the candidates and root, with ties going to the root and then to a fixed branch order. Screening chooses the parameter starting point for absorption. 4.2 RECOVER EXPERIENCE WITHOUT INHERITING THE DONOR Experience across branches and rounds. Current exploration records Et and a bounded archive Mt supply candidate problems and references with their generating checkpoint and verification trace. Qualification checks completeness, answer validity, and problem-level de-duplication. Recovery material is disjoint from screening, confirmation, and final evaluation.

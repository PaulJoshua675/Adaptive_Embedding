Preprint A Experimental Details A.1 Datasets and downstream tasks Pretraining datasets. The pretraining corpus contained 13,444 usable subjects from eight dataset groups. Only fMRI inputs, without downstream labels, were used during pretraining. Preprocessing. We started from cohort-specific preprocessed derivatives: the HCP min- imal preprocessing pipeline for HCP and CHCP, ABCD-HCP derivatives for ABCD and ABCC, fMRIPrep derivatives for AOMIC PIOP1/2, and available MNI-space preprocessed derivatives for HBN, PNC, and the multisite MDD data. Thus, low-level operations, in- cluding motion correction and any nuisance-signal handling, were inherited from the corre- sponding source pipeline rather than re-estimated with a single cross-cohort pipeline. We then applied a common input harmonization procedure: all fMRI volumes were represented at 2-mm isotropic resolution, and only datasets with a source TR different from 0.72 s were temporally interpolated to 0.72 s using cubic B-spline interpolation. Regional signals were extracted using the Schaefer-400 atlas, and each regional time series was Z-standardized along the temporal dimension. Files with exactly 200 frames were used directly; during pre- training, contiguous 200-frame windows were sampled from longer series, whereas sequences shorter than 200 frames were excluded without zero-padding. Chinese Human Connectome Project (CHCP). CHCP contributed healthy-adult resting-state scans acquired with multiple phase-encoding directions (Ge et al., 2023). Human Connectome Project (HCP). HCP contributed young-adult resting-state scans to pretraining (Van Essen et al., 2013). It also provided an internal downstream task for sex classification; the original data partitions remained separated by subject. Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development (ABCD). ABCD contributed pediatric resting-state scans to pretraining (Casey et al., 2018). A separate internal downstream eval- uation predicted sex, with participant identities kept disjoint across the original partitions. ABCC Data Share. ABCC contributed a large pediatric imaging collection (Feczko et al., 2021). Subjects overlapping with ABCD were removed from ABCC before construct- ing the pretraining manifest. Healthy Brain Network (HBN). HBN contributed pediatric and adolescent scans span- ning a broad range of mental-health and learning characteristics (Alexander et al., 2017). Philadelphia Neurodevelopmental Cohort (PNC). PNC contributed neurodevelop- mental resting-state scans from youth and young adults (Satterthwaite et al., 2014). Major Depressive Disorder (MDD). The multisite MDD collection contributed resting-state scans from participants with recurrent depression and controls (Yan et al., 2019). Amsterdam Open MRI Collection (AOMIC). AOMIC PIOP1 and PIOP2 con- tributed young-adult resting-state scans (Snoek et al., 2021). Downstream datasets and tasks. Seven datasets provided nine downstream tasks. HCP and ABCD were treated as in-cohort evaluations because their unlabeled scans con- tributed to pretraining; no downstream labels were used. The remaining cohorts were excluded from the pretraining mixture and provided out-of-cohort transfer evaluations. Parkinson’s Progression Markers Initiative (PPMI). PPMI was used for three-class diagnostic-group classification (Marek et al., 2011). 15

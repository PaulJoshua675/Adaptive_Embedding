These results indicate that explicitly incorporating motion and physical orientation cues is a robust and generalizable strategy for enhancing feed-forward 3D reconstruction. • We propose a motion- and gravity-aware augmentation for feedfor- ward transformer-based 3D reconstruction, improving robustness in dynamic scenes. • We introduce optical flow as an explicit motion cue for static–dynamic separation, and gravity direction as a global orientation prior for spa- tial consistency. • We demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach through extensive experiments and ablation studies on standard multi-view reconstruction benchmarks. 2

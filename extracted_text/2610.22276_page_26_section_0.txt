At L4, mean obtains 51.41 CV-Bench and 4.50 RefSpatial; at L12 these rise to 79.42 and 37.00, and at L32 to 85.38 and 51.00. Substantial contextual processing is therefore necessary before the pretrained query and key projections provide an effective spatial readout. Yet the optimum is task-dependent. L28 mean reaches 53.00 RefSpatial, L24 concat reaches 68.07 Where2Place, and L28 merge reaches 67.21 RoboSpatial pointing. Length error is also nonmonotonic: L16 mean already achieves 0.3452 m, worsening through L20–L28 before recovering to 0.3341 m at L32. A high shallow-layer score on one task, such as RoboSpatial VQA, coexists with weak geometric localization. Why keep L32 mean. At L32, concat has slightly lower MAE than mean (0.3297 versus 0.3341 m) and better MMSI (28.90 versus 27.70), but lower SAT, RoboSpatial VQA, BLINK, and Point-Bench. Merge improves two placement-oriented point scores relative to mean, while reducing several choice scores and worsening MAE. Mean preserves all pretrained head subspaces without a sharper fixed logit scale or weight- space averaging. We retain L32 mean as a balanced default with full contextual depth. Late-layer fusion.

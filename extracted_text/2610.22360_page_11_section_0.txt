Intermediate 2D conditions and masks are omitted. 6 Discussion and Conclusion To reflect on a 3D output, a system must determine whether it meets its requirements and explain any violations. 3DVerifierBench shows that current models often struggle to provide this feedback, even when generated content looks plausible. Answer, explanation, and counterfactual-consistency evaluations reveal failures to identify decisive facts, their targets, and relevant contradictions. Our construction pipeline provides structured data to support training on these distinctions. Human- oracle selection and human-guided refinement illustrate how better feedback can help select and revise 3D assets, although satisfying explicit facts does not ensure overall quality or source fidelity. These findings motivate more reliable evidence-grounded feedback for future 3D generation systems. 11

The experiment that would most strengthen the central claim separates order from relocatable order directly, using matched pairs of equal order and opposite decomposability: PAR4 against a random 4-junta on the same inputs, and in the continuous case (w⊤x)6 against an additive degree-6 polynomial. Proposition 4 offers a family of targets well beyond parity, including majority and threshold functions of full degree, on which the prediction can be tested without relying on parity at all. The order of the evolved representation, used here as a diagnostic, is also a candidate selective pressure, giving a Boolean counterpart to the Pareto scheme of [18]. Finally, the extension of Definition 2 to d features carries a testable prediction: the relocatable order should grow with d, so multidimensional feature construction systems should require less expressive downstream models than one-dimensional ones at equal accuracy.

Figure 4 shows how exponent distributions of feed-forward momentum buffers (left) and weights (right) change during NanoGPT training. Both plots cover the two weight matrices in each feed-forward block. Each curve averages normalized histograms with equal weight per tensor, rather than per element. Probabilities use a logarithmic scale, and exponents have the BF16 bias removed. Exact zeros are shown separately, with their mean fractions in the legends; normalization includes all elements.

A preliminary version of this work will be presented at SPIE Asia-Pacific Remote Sensing 2026 (Natsume and Yeo, 2026). This journal submission substantially extends that conference paper with: (i) a systematic pretraining data scaling study across eight pretraining data fractions with formal statistical testing (Section 4.5); (ii) a Centered Kernel Alignment representation analysis characterizing how encoder representations evolve with pretraining data quantity (Sec- tion 4.7); (iii) a full geographic de-collision analysis re-pretraining SparK-SAR from scratch on a de-collided corpus with all overlapping pretraining tiles removed, confirming that reported gains are not attributable to geographic data leakage (Section 4.8); (iv) a finetuning label efficiency analysis demonstrating that SparK-SAR pretraining provides statistically significant accuracy gains over random initialization at every label fraction and every task (Section 4.6); (v) a dedi- cated Flood-S1 ceiling ablation across loss functions and fusion strategies (Section 4.4.4); (vi) an expanded negative-results discussion; and (vii) extended methodological detail throughout.

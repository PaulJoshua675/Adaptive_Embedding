The following idealized model is used to obtain a reference entropy. Assumption 2.1 (Abstract sparse payload model). The entries of H are independent and identically distributed abstract symbols. Each entry is the distinguished zero symbol with probability ρ, where 0 < ρ < 1, and otherwise carries a uniformly distributed (d −1)-bit payload. The 2d−1 payload symbols are distinct from the zero symbol.

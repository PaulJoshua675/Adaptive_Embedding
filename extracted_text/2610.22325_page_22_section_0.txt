E.3 FORWARD-TRANSFER CONSISTENCY ON DAVIS Figure 14 compares frame-0 points transferred by Dense4D, V-DPM, 4RC and Any4D with each method’s own target-time surface, extending the overlays in Fig. 7. Several methods reconstruct recognisable human geometry at the target time, yet place transferred points away from that geometry. This reveals inconsistency between their point-transfer and depth predictions, even when the target surface itself looks plausible. Dense4D instead matches target-image pixels and lifts them with their predicted depth, so transferred points lie on its target surface by construction.

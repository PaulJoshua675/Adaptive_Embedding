These baselines retain their fixed mathematical-reasoning presets or assets rather than being tuned on coding test scores. Transfer is mixed. At T = 16, TIPSfull achieves the highest measured HumanEval score among these methods, 16.46%, but it does not maintain that advantage as the budget grows. At T = 64, Non-adjusted Fast-dLLM with 8 × 32 decoding reaches 40.24% on HumanEval, compared with 22.56% and 29.27% for the full and block TIPS policies. On MBPP, the two policies reach 29.00% and 33.00%, compared with 38.20% for that baseline. Thus, the mathematical-reasoning policy transfers nontrivially, but the gains observed in its training domain do not translate into consistent superiority on coding.

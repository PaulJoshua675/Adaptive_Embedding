Preprint APPENDIX A EXPERIMENTAL SETUP AND HYPERPARAMETERS Backbones and protocol. The checkpoints are Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Base, Qwen/Qwen3-8B-Base, allenai/Olmo-3-1025-7B, and google/gemma-4-E4B-it. The two Qwen checkpoints and OLMo are pretrained base models; Gemma is instruction-tuned. Every backbone and method runs ten evolution rounds with three training seeds, with evaluation after every round. The evaluated checkpoint is the learner actually inherited at that round, St+1, including rounds that retain St. In the host-system HEIRCOMMIT studies, Skill-SP, R-Zero, and Agent0 supply native updates and an integration adapter governs inheritance. Native exploration and screening. Each round makes 1,024 task-generation attempts in one batch and uses ten learner responses per question for native admission. Two isolated native branches are then trained from the same root, each on a cohort of 64 questions, with the recipe in Table 3. Native training updates all parameters of the Curriculum Scout and Evolving Learner with AdamW (Loshchilov & Hutter, 2019), and Recovery updates LoRA parameters (Hu et al., 2021). Screening scores the root and the two branch endpoints on 16 questions from P scr t , and confirmation scores the final proposal on 32 fresh questions from P con t , with four responses per question and state. Screening ties favor the root, then the branches in a fixed order. Table 3: Optimization settings for EVOHEIR (Full). Native learner settings are per branch. Native budgets count optimizer calls, including this initial call, rather than nonzero parameter updates. Consensus labels and probe qualification. The round-start Evolving Learner St generates ten admission responses per question using the settings in Appendix A. In the first round, St is the initial checkpoint; subsequent rounds use the actually inherited learner. The answer comparator clusters successfully extracted, nonempty answers. If mvalid responses yield such answers and the largest cluster contains cmax responses, consensus support is cmax/mvalid. Questions with mvalid = 0 are excluded. The representative answer of the largest cluster becomes the pseudo-label; probes require support at least 0.7. Ties select the first encountered largest cluster, but tied maxima have support at most 0.5 and cannot qualify as probes. Each role’s question–label file is frozen and shared across all states evaluated on that role’s pool. Confirmation questions and labels are fixed before candidate construction and excluded from native training, screening, and Recovery. Screening and confirmation use fresh scoring responses, seeds, and caches, separate from the admission responses that form the labels. HEIRCOMMIT adopts S+ t only when its confirmation gain is strictly positive, that is, τt = 0 in Eq. 5. Generation settings. The Curriculum Scout’s native rollouts use temperature 1.0, top-p = 0.99, and five responses per question, and offline candidate generation uses temperature 1.0 and top- p = 0.95.

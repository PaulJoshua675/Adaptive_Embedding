F.1 101M WikiText-103 Comparisons The three-seed comparison uses a paired 2 × 2 × 3 design on WikiText-103: λ ∈{0, 0.2}, Vanilla or SAGGS, and three paired seeds. The medium ReLU Transformer has L = 24, d = 512, 8 heads, about 101M parameters, and context length 512. Each run uses 30K AdamW steps, learning rate 6 × 10−4, β2 = 0.95, weight decay 0.1, 2K-step warmup, cosine decay to 10% of peak, gradient clipping at 1.0, BF16 mixed precision, and batch size 32. At λ = 0.2, Q–K pairs receive the depth-exponential edit at initialization. SAGGS rebalances Q–K, V–O, and ReLU up–down pairs every 100 steps. Data sampling and initialization are shared within seed. The primary comparison in Table 2 extends this protocol to seven paired seeds. Table 6 reports paired differences ∆s = PPLVanilla(s) −PPLSAGGS(s). At λ = 0, the mean is −0.044 ± 0.026 PPL: a small observed cost on a base near 22.9 PPL. At λ = 0.2, the mean is +0.380 ± 0.025 PPL and all three seeds favor SAGGS. The paired effect sizes are d = −1.69 and d = 15.35, respectively. With n = 3, the one-sided exact sign-flip p-value floor is 0.125. Pair-norm measurements in the seven-seed comparison. SAGGS keeps the final pair log-ratio imbalance below 10−3 in these comparisons, whereas Vanilla Q–K imbalance is about 0.15 at λ = 0 and about 3 at λ = 0.2. Rebalancing controls pair norms in both conditions; the PPL benefit appears under deliberate rescaling.

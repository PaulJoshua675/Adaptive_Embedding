Assessing the bias of an LLM that reviews r´esum´es is a logical follow-on project for this work. We propose that a similar methodology as Bertrand and Mullainathan could be em- ployed to test how an LLM changes the bias of a r´esum´e reviewer. Furthermore, we suggest two direct exten- sions of our work. First, we can adapt meta- morphic testing to name bias by defining controlled transformations over prompts and name substitutions, and then auditing system- atic violations across models. This is a known methodology for conducting tests of LLM bias (da Silva de Oliveira et al., 2025; Reddy et al., 2025). Second, we can operationalize these findings into standardized name-bias evalua- tion sets for developers to report, compare, and mitigate bias within their LLMs.

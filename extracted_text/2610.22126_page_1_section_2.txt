As machine learning models grow increasingly complex, particularly deep neural networks trained on large-scale datasets, the need for interpretable explanations of their predictions becomes critical. Local post-hoc explanation methods have emerged as a popular approach for understanding the contribution of individual input features to model predictions. However, most existing methods assume that input features are independent, which is often unrealistic in practice. Feature dependencies are common in real-world applications—in gene expression, for instance, genes exhibit complex inter-dependencies reflecting underlying biological processes.

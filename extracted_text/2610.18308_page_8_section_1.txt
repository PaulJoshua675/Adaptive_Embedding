We presented SimInter, a pipeline for LLM-based user simulation that elicits qualitative behavioral attributes through structured synthetic interviews and guards against fabrication via explicit turn- level auditing. On 300 ReDial users, simulators conditioned on enriched profiles discuss movies along substantially richer behavioral aspects than a statistics-only baseline (dz = 0.96, padj < 0.001; zero-aspect users drop from 41% to 5%); pooled popularity alignment with MovieLens-25M reaches r = 0.347; and 94% of enriched-attribute claims are human-supported. Enrichment also con- centrates item mentions and reduces lexical variety, consistent with profile-conditioned generation nar- rowing expression toward profile vocabulary. User- simulation quality in CRS is thus multidimensional: the goal is not to maximize every diversity metric, but to gain more on dimensions that matter for CRS evaluation than is lost on those that do not. 8

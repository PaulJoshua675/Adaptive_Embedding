A.5. Filesystem-structure ablation. To isolate the effect of filesystem organization, we evaluate the 49 corpora with completed filesystem snapshots, comprising 435 questions from the expanded amortization set. Unlike the original one- question-per-corpus BrowseComp+ evaluation reported in Figure 3, this analysis uses the expanded questions and a fresh matched answering run. Accordingly, comparisons are made within this run across the original, flattened, and shuffled conditions. We apply two content-preserving interventions to the same memories. Flattening preserves comparable answer accuracy but increases navigation cost, while shuffling semantic placement reduces accuracy without reducing navigation cost. A.7. Librarian Agents Training and Evaluation Configuration For the primary BrowseComp-Plus experiment, we optimize a Qwen3.5-4B builder with GRPO on the 680-task training split. We use LoRA rank 32, a learning rate of 4 × 10−5, batch size 8, rollout-group size 4, and a maximum construction horizon of 32 builder turns. Builder generations are limited to 1,024 tokens and complete construction trajectories to 60,000 tokens.

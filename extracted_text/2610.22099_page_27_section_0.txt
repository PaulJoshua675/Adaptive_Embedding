A non-adjusted confidence- threshold sampler based on Fast-dLLM first runs for qT global decoder calls, with q ∈{0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1}, after which TIPS uses the remaining budget. The heuristic reveals eligible masked proposals with confidence strictly above 0.9 and, when none qualifies, reveals the highest-confidence eligible proposal for each example. TIPS resumes at the corresponding point of its native schedule for normalized time input and logit calibration purposes. For a matched budget comparison, all arms use exactly T real forward passes, including calls that leave the canvas unchanged and deterministic finalizations. In particular, the q = 1 endpoint is this fixed-horizon heuristic. We retain both endpoints and all three hybrid settings in Table 20. Sixteen of the eighteen hybrid conditions improve over the matched heuristic endpoint. At T = 64, full-canvas handoff at q = 1/4 raises accuracy from 23.91% to 40.85%, while blockwise handoff at q = 3/4 raises it from 34.91% to 40.19%. The paired counts show that these gains can include both repaired and newly incorrect final answers: the former full-canvas condition changes 491 heuristic failures into correct answers while changing 183 heuristic successes into failures. No hybrid exceeds pure TIPS at the same budget. The result therefore supports TIPS’s ability to improve continuations from another sampler, rather than recommending a heuristic prefix over starting with TIPS. The results also support a comparison at the remaining budget. For T = 64 and q = 1/2, handoff leaves 32 calls for TIPS and yields 31.34% and 38.15% accuracy for TIPSfull and TIPSblock, respectively. Their from-scratch 32-call runs instead achieve 57.56% and 60.20%, suggesting that restarting from an all-mask canvas can be preferable to retaining the heuristic state.

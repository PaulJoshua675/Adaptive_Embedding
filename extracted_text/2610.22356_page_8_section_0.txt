DK-VLM: Geometry as Position 3.3 Joint Training Objective Pairwise Geometric Prediction. Answer-level SFT constrains the final response but does not provide an explicit target for the relations encoded by the geometric branch. Because the geometric kernel operates on token pairs, we use the relative 3D displacement of ordered token pairs to directly supervise the geometric representation before fusion. We decompose the displacement into distance and unit direction to constrain its magnitude and orientation. At the layer ℓ⋆selected for pairwise geometric supervision, we read hi = ϕℓ⋆ g (Gℓ⋆ i ) ∈Rd after the shared output projection and before kernel fusion. We sample ordered pairs of visual tokens with valid geometry within and across frames, with dij = ∥xj −xi∥2. For each sampled pair, we construct the pair representation and its distance and direction targets as follows: zij = [hi; hj; hj −hi; hi ⊙hj] ∈R4d, yd ij = log(1 + dij), uij = xj −xi dij (dij > ε). (7) Here, ⊙denotes elementwise multiplication. A prediction head maps zij to a predicted log- distance byd ij and an L2-normalized direction buij. For a sample n, we use a Huber loss on log distance, ℓ(n) dist, and a cosine loss on direction, ℓ(n) dir , combined as ℓ(n) pair = ℓ(n) dist + 0.5 ℓ(n) dir . Averaging over samples with valid token pairs gives Lpair; Appendix A.3 specifies the exact reductions and empty-set cases. Unified Objective. The two objectives are jointly optimized in a single training stage: L = LSFT + λ(t)Lpair, (8) where λ(t) controls the pairwise-loss weight. The prediction head is used only during training and removed for inference. 4 Experiments 4.1 Experimental Setup Implementation Details. We build DK-VLM on Qwen2.5-VL-7B and Qwen3-VL-8B (Bai et al., 2025b,a). A frozen VGGT-Ωestimates 3D points and camera poses from the input images (Wang et al., 2026a). We also evaluate alternative geometry frontends without retraining and re- port the results in Appendix A.10. We uniformly sample up to 32 frames from each training video. For both models, we uniformly select five decoder layers to introduce Dual-Kernel Attention and apply rank-64 LoRA (Hu et al., 2022) to the language model’s Q/K/V/O projections. The geometric projections, gates, and pairwise prediction head are also trained, while the visual encoder remains frozen. For the 7B and 8B variants, the trainable parameter counts are only 2.88% and 3.86% of their respective backbone parameter counts. Both models use AdamW (Loshchilov and Hutter, 2019) with a main learning rate of 10−4 and a global batch size of 32. Further implementation details are provided in Appendix A.6. Training Data.

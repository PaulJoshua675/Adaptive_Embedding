PtR’s discovery and retrieval calls are sequential, adding one full LLM round-trip of latency before the planner begins; complexity-adaptive routing eliminates this overhead for G1-type queries. Token cost (reported in Appendix E) is a proxy for this serial overhead structure; the resulting wall-clock latency depends on the serving infrastructure. Availability The two prompts (discovery stub generation and planner) and the FAISS index configuration are provided verbatim in Appendix K and Appendix L for self-contained reproducibility.

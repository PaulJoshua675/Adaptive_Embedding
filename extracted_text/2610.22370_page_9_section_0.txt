Additional experiments are in Appendix C. Importance of Temporal and Spatial Signals. In Tab. 5, we ablate the two components of our importance map by using only the temporal axis, only the spatial axis, or both. Using both axes yields the best overall performance, confirming that temporal evolution and structural saliency provide complementary cues for token selection, whereas either axis alone is insufficient to fully characterize mergeability. We further replace our temporal signal with the raw score slope to evaluate its effectiveness in the actual merging process. As shown in Tab. 5, this substitution consistently degrades all evaluation metrics, and Fig. 7 shows that it also introduces noticeable visual artifacts. This is consistent with our earlier analysis that the score slope becomes increasingly contaminated by schedule-driven effects in later denoising steps, making it less reliable for token merging. 9

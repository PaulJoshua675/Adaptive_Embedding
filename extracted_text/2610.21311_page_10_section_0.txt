In the following lemma, we will estimate the lower bound of the probability that an individual is paired with some specific individuals as two parents to generate two offspring individuals. The estimate is crucial in the runtime analysis, and also helps us understand the intensity of cross-task knowledge transfer achieved by the MO-MFEA. The proof is based on the balanced statement from Lemma 2 to calculate the number of individuals with different values of scalar fitness. Lemma 4. Let t ∈N, j ∈[1..ℓ], and let Aj be one task.

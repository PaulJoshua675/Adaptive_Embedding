Within each benchmark, the prover prompt, verifier, action budget, and model settings are fixed across all configurations. 5.3 RESULTS Tables 3 to 5 show the pass rates and costs relative to the no-retrieval baseline on the three bench- marks. Our results reveal the following findings: Graph retrieval improves proof completion. LEANGRAPHSEARCH in Standard Mode outper- forms LeanSearch v2 in Standard Mode at every evaluated reflection budget across all sets, while achieving performance comparable to LeanSearch v2 in Reasoning Mode. The number solved across the 210 problems within 32 turns increases from 136 to 143 without memory and from 134 to 142 with memory. LEANGRAPHSEARCH in Reasoning Mode yields further improvement, reaching the highest total number of solved problems, with particularly strong results on FATE-H. The benefit is also visible at smaller reflection budgets. Comparing results across budgets reveals improvements that are less apparent from pass@32 alone. On FATE-H, Standard Mode without memory solves 69 problems within 16 turns using LEANGRAPHSEARCH, compared with 61 us- ing LeanSearch v2.

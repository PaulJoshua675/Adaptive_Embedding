P2TPO: Structure-Aligned Page-to-Token Credit Assignment for Full-Page Document Parsing C.1. Experimental protocols C.1.1. Training data construction and selection Source data and serialization. We constructed the training corpus by converting the original Infinity-Doc2- 5M Document_Parsing/doc2json annotations into full-page Markdown (Huang et al., 2026). Equations are written in LATEX, and tables are written in HTML. Appendix C.1.2 provides the shared SFT and RL parsing prompt. SFT data selection. We randomly sampled 700K and 50K page-level records from the transformed corpus as subsets for supervised fine-tuning. These subsets provide the SFT initialization settings reported in Tables 1 and 4. RL data selection. We used an expert model to re-annotate the transformed corpus and compared its annotations with the corresponding original labels. We ranked records by annotation agreement as a proxy for label quality and retained the 100,000 records with the highest agreement. Expert annotations were used solely for quality assessment and sample selection; RL training retained the original annotations. We sampled 12,800 and 64,000 records from this pool for the 100-update and 500-update experiments, respectively. C.1.2. Comparison methods The evaluated response-level baselines are GRPO, GSPO, GDPO, and RDPO; P2BPO, +UAP, and +SCR are component controls (Table 1). Response-level baselines. GRPO (Shao et al., 2024) standardizes scalar page rewards within each prompt’s rollout group and broadcasts the resulting advantage across each response. GSPO (Zheng et al., 2025) retains group-relative response advantages while using length-normalized sequence importance ratios and sequence- level clipping.

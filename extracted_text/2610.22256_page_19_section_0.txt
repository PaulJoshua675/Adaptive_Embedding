Tables 9, 10, and 11 complete the batch-size sweep of Table 4 for its three remaining activation and sparsity settings: ReLU2 at ρ = 0.5, and ReLU at ρ = 0.5 and 0.9. The saving scales with sparsity and with the size of the dense baseline, not with the activation alone. At 32,000 rows BitSparse reduces peak memory by 36% for ReLU2 at ρ = 0.5, 26% for ReLU at the same sparsity, and 50% for ReLU at ρ = 0.9. The larger reduction for ReLU2 comes from the reduced transient storage used to compute activations in the backward pass from our optimized fused Triton kernel. The crossover at which sign-bit packing becomes the smallest configuration depends on both factors: it occurs at 8,000 rows for both activation functions at ρ = 0.9, and not at all at ρ = 0.5. Runtime stays within about 1.5% of dense storage at 32,000 rows in all three settings. 19

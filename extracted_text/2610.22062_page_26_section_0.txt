The presence prompts for the other 17 phenotypes The 17 phenotypes that have no ground truth are scored by a separate pair of presence prompts rather than by the diarrhea prompts above. Those prompts keep the same two-role design, the same decision rules for historical, negated, anticipatory and template mentions, and the same interpolated visit label, and they add a written definition of the phenotype being scored. That definition block is why the presence prompts carry no diarrhea branch, and why diarrhea is scored by its own prompt instead (Note S1). Their full text is in the released code.

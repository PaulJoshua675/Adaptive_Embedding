From Lemma 4, we now clearly characterize the knowledge transfer ca- pability between different tasks in the following lemma. Specifically, if both tasks have an overlap on their corresponding Pareto optimal individuals, we will give an upper bound of the iterations for one task to transfer its reached Pareto front to a related Pareto front of another task. The core idea of the proof is to pair some specific Pareto optimal solutions reached in one task with arbitrary individuals from the desired task, and then to trigger the crossover operator with probability prm, thereby enabling cross-task knowl- edge transfer.

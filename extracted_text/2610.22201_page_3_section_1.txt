The Sequential Spot Market MDP We model decentralized task routing as a finite horizon (𝑇) sequential Markov Decision Process (MDP) defined by the tuple (S, A, P, 𝑅, 𝛾). Rather than relying on a centralized policy 𝜋(𝑎|𝑠) to select actions, the action space A is defined by a society of independent agents. For intuition, consider a gridworld: instead of a single controller choosing to move up or down, specialized “Up” and “Down” agents compete in an auction for the right to act on the current state 𝑠𝑡. At each step 𝑡, a registry of agents evaluates 𝑠𝑡to determine their eligibility. Eligible agents submit bids, and the highest bidder wins the right to process the state.

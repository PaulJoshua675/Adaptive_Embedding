4.1 Experimental Setup Dataset Questions Passages HotpotQA 1,000 9,811 2WikiQA 1,000 6,119 MuSiQue 1,000 11,656 NarrativeQA 1,000 74,742 StrategyQA 1,000 9,254 Table 1: Dataset statistics. Passages are indexed retrieval units. NarrativeQA uses story-local retrieval. Datasets. Five datasets covering three categories of complex question answering are used for evaluation. For multi-hop QA, HotpotQA (Yang et al., 2018), 2WikiQA (Ho et al., 2020), and MuSiQue (Trivedi et al., 2022) assess retrieving complementary evidence across passages and composing factual relations to answer multi-step questions. HotpotQA includes bridge and comparison questions, 2WikiQA emphasizes explicit relation composition, and MuSiQue connects two to four reasoning steps. For narrative QA, NarrativeQA (Koˇciský et al., 2018) assesses locating and integrating evidence about characters and events distributed across full-length novels and screenplays. For implicit reasoning QA, StrategyQA (Geva et al., 2021) assesses identifying and combining unstated factual premises for yes/no reasoning. Table 1 summarizes dataset sizes. See Appendix A for details. Metrics. Normalized exact match (EM) and token F1 (Rajpurkar et al., 2016) are reported for multi-hop and narrative QA. EM measures exact agreement with a reference answer. F1 is the harmonic mean of token-level precision and recall. With multiple references, each prediction receives its highest score across the reference answers. StrategyQA uses accuracy, the proportion of correctly answered yes/no questions. Baselines. The baselines are grouped into three categories. LLMs and LLM-based retrievers include Qwen3.8-27B and NV-Embed-v2 (Lee et al., 2025a). Structure-enhanced RAG includes HippoRAGv2, OriginRAG (Anonymous, 2026b), LogicRAG, and RankCoT (Wu et al., 2025). Iterative RAG includes IRCoT, RetroRAG, MGranRAG, STRIDE, CIRAG, S2G-RAG, and KAIR. We use Qwen3.8-27B as the base language model and NV-Embed-v2 as the embedding model across methods. RCER and LLM settings are provided in Appendix B, and baseline settings in Appendix C. 4.2 Main Results (RQ1) Table 2 shows that RCER ranks first across all five datasets. Average multi-hop EM and F1 reach 66.27 and 76.73, surpassing the best baseline for each metric by 2.83 and 3.06 points, respectively. Compared with LLMs and LLM-based retrievers. RCER improves F1 over NV-Embed-v2 by 20.99 points on 2WikiQA and 18.35 on MuSiQue. On NarrativeQA, it obtains 39.83 F1, compared with 16.77 for question-only Qwen and 39.41 for NV-Embed-v2. StrategyQA accuracy improves by 11.60 percentage points over NV-Embed-v2. Qwen relies on parametric knowledge, making access to task-specific evidence valuable. Original-query retrieval can still miss facts whose retrieval requires an intermediate entity or premise. RCER identifies these through evidence-informed decomposition and retrieves complementary support. Compared with structure-enhanced RAG. RCER gains 8.44 average multi-hop F1 points over OriginRAG. It improves NarrativeQA F1 over LogicRAG by 1.38 points and StrategyQA accuracy over RankCoT by 2.90 percentage points. HippoRAGv2 identifies graph-associated passages, but relevance does not establish how their facts jointly answer a question. OriginRAG jointly selects sources and grounds evidence demands in source spans. However, its demands are derived before retrieval and remain unchanged when retrieved evidence reveals intermediate entities. LogicRAG’s rolling summaries can omit details needed later. RankCoT refines an existing passage set without retrieving additional support. RCER uses reconstructed relations to refine subsequent information needs, then selects complementary sources and retains cross-document inferences for answering. Compared with iterative RAG. Against RetroRAG, RCER gains 4.72 F1 points on 2WikiQA and 3.77 on MuSiQue. Its MuSiQue F1 is also 7.89 points higher than MGranRAG. On NarrativeQA, it improves F1 over RetroRAG by 1.73 points. StrategyQA accuracy reaches 87.40%, compared with 84.70% for IRCoT. Iterative retrieval can acquire the necessary facts while retaining an incorrect relational interpretation. 5

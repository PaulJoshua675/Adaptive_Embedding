Selected leads carry original excerpts and en- ter the research history asynchronously, informing subsequent source inspection or query refinement. 4 Experiments We examine whether coupling fast local evidence judgments with research planning improves the quality and efficiency of autonomous research. We first compare final task performance and resource use, then examine the evidence available during research, component contributions, and model choices. 4.1 Experimental Setup Datasets. We evaluate factual question answer- ing on all 830 BrowseComp-Plus (Chen et al., 2026) questions and document ranking on all 105 NeuCLIRBench (Lawrie et al., 2025) topics. These tasks require locating answer evidence and assess- ing document relevance, respectively. NeuCLIR- Bench uses English queries and English document translations. Baselines. On BrowseComp-Plus, we compare with six agent configurations: BCP/ReAct (Chen et al., 2026), Reason-ColBERT (Chaffin and Sourty, 2025), Pi-Serini (Hsu et al., 2026), Agen- tica (Symbolica, 2025), AtlasNav (Guo et al., 2026), and RISE (Zhuang et al., 2026). NeuCLIR- Bench comparisons cover retrieval, reranking, and research agents adapted for document ranking.

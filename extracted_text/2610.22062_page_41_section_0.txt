(A) Cohen’s κ between the two agents for each phenotype, sorted descending. Every phenotype reaches the substantial band, so the bars share one color, and the dashed line is the overall κ = 0.93 across the 60,530 instances both agents scored. Agreement is highest for discrete, observable events such as vomiting, aggressive behavior and gross-motor findings, and lowest for attention/alertness, the phenotype with the fewest instances (313), and for the open-ended other-side-effects category. The hatched bar is diarrhea, scored by the diarrhea-specific prompt on its own validated corpus rather than by the presence prompt, and shown for reference. (B) Consensus outcome composition over the same 60,530 matches. The gate retains only “both YES” (38.4%) as positive, and the 3.4% one-sided disagreements are conservatively classified negative.

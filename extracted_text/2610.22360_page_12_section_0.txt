Ethics statement For facial geometry, we use the FaceScape bilinear statistical model rather than participant-level scans. The model compresses the identity variation of 847 subjects into a 50-dimensional PCA space, from which we synthesize facial geometry. We do not use or redistribute the original FaceScape images, textures, subject-level meshes, demographic metadata, or identity-specific records.

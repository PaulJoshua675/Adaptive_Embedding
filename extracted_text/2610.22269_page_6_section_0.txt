The architecture comprises an actor network πθπ and critic network QθQ, with state observations sk encompassing slice KPIs, arrival rates, and service-demand characteristics as inputs. Step 2: Demonstration Collection and Filtering. We apply the dual-filtering mechanism described in Section II-C to collect high-quality demonstrations from TLM interactions. This process generates the filtered demonstration buffer Dfil containing only high-quality state-action pairs that pass both episode-level policy impact gating and action-level critic vali- dation. We enhance the standard DDPG objective by incorpo- rating TLM guidance and BC losses, F(θπ, θQ) = −Lp(θπ)+λvLv(θQ)+λTLMLTLM(θπ)+λBCLBC(θπ) (10) where the BC loss LBC(θπ) utilizes the Q-filtered demonstra- tions from Dfil as defined in (2). Step 3: Mixed Replay Training. During training, we sample from both the standard replay buffer D (exploration data) and filtered demonstration buffer Dfil (expert guidance). This mixed sampling strategy enables the agent to learn from both self-exploration and TLM-guided expert demonstrations. Step 4: State Encoding and TLM Integration. The RAN encoder transforms network states sk into textual representa- tions Ωk for TLM-based reasoning over resource allocation. The TLM processes structured prompts containing: (i) the optimization objective, (ii) the current network observation, including per-slice PRB usage, buffered packets, and traffic criticality indicators, and (iii) an action-to-slice mapping over candidate decisions. It then evaluates the RL agent’s pro- posed action and returns a structured JSON response with a reasonability judgment, explanation, and corrected action when needed. Fig. 6 illustrates this process for an emergency URLLC traffic burst, where the TLM detects that allocating PRBs to eMBB and mMTC is suboptimal under high URLLC congestion and overrides it with a latency-aware allocation. The resulting decisions are filtered through the dual-filtering BC mechanism and integrated into training. Step 5: Continuous Learning Loop. The system operates in a closed-loop manner where: (i) X-RIC generates expert demonstrations based on current network conditions, (ii) dual- filtering selects high-quality demonstrations, (iii) BC loss guides policy learning from filtered demonstrations, (iv) RL continues exploration and optimization, and (v) performance feedback updates the X-RIC guidance process. This integrated approach utilizes the sample efficiency of BC while maintain- ing the exploration capabilities of reinforcement learning. IV.

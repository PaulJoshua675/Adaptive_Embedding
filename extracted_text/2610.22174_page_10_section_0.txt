Accordingly, we examine session-level behavior along three dimensions: quantity, or whether the count of sessions increases or decreases; quality, or whether performance improvement is accompanied by more useful sessions; and allocation, or whether a limited session budget becomes increasingly concentrated on execution stages with high marginal utility. As shown in Table 2, for Broadcasting, which does not directly optimize any specific session-aware pattern, all three metrics increase. Under Mosaic, the number of sessions does not increase further, while both session quality and allocation improve. These findings indicate that performance gains via multi-session optimization are associated with improved session utility rather than a monotonic rise in session count. Moreover, despite using explicit session-aware optimization, Mosaic does not induce a fundamentally altered behavioral mechanism. Instead, it strengthens the session-allocation patterns that already emerge from naïve multi-session optimization. We argue that effective multi-session optimization is accompanied by improvements in session utility and allocation. It distributes computation more effectively across sessions, encouraging continued execution when it yields meaningful progress while suppressing low-utility computation. From this perspective, the central challenge is not how many sessions an agent should use, but how much useful progress it can derive from the sessions it executes. 5. Discussion: Future Directions for Multi-Session Agentic RL Infrastructure for reproducible research. Multi-session trajectories tend to arise naturally when agents tackle complex, long-horizon tasks. Although some existing benchmarks can elicit these trajectories with reasonable frequency, suitable public training data remain scarce. This gap limits the scale, sufficiency, and reproducibility of experiments in multi-session agentic RL. Developing open training datasets and corresponding agentic execution environments is therefore essential to building a sustainable research ecosystem for this topic. 10

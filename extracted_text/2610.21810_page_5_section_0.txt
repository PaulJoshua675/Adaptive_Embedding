Functional Fidelity. Benchmark scores alone may not fully capture how compression changes the behavior of a model. We therefore examine whether compressed lookup tables preserve the original model’s output distributions by measuring per-token KL(porig ∥pquant). We evaluate six text domains covering web, code, mathematics, chat, and multilingual data, and aggregate their KL val- ues using the geometric mean to weight each do- main equally on the logarithmic scale. As shown in Figure 2, at two bits, CP reduces KL divergence by 41 to 63% compared with RTN and achieves a further 3 to 9% reduction over QTIP, with the lat- ter improvement requiring an additional bf16 scale per row. The storage efficiency of CP also extends to higher bit rates. At 2.5 bits, CP approaches the fidelity of RTN 3-bit with less storage, while CP 3-bit further improves fidelity.

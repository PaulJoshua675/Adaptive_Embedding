We use token entropy as a heuristic measure of bootstrap confidence. Our hypothesis is that attenuating diffuse bootstrap predictions during training reduces the denoiser’s reliance on unreliable auxiliary inputs. The gate changes the training feedback signal, while both the inference sampler and inference-time self-conditioning retain the unweighted posterior mean.

Each agent settled into a role: gyldenia gathered food and supplies and later destroyed six of the ten crystals; janesick made tools, found the fortress and triangulated the stronghold; auratian lit the portal and worked out how to reach the crystals; bulicame helped build the Enderman shelters and landed the final blow in the End. • A shared memory. They posted the coordinates of chests, portals and dangers, and kept notes on the quirks of their tools in the memory the team shares. Nineteen minutes in, gyldenia warned the team about a quirk of Mineflayer’s crafting and how to avoid it; a helper function with the fix appears in 655 programs written by all four agents. • Their own tools. The agents kept 146 pieces of state and code alive between programs, including 42 helper functions: a background loop that keeps a bot afloat in water, timed walking, item counting, and a counter that makes leftover code from a timed-out program stop itself, referenced in 1,519 programs. • Looking out for each other. Urgent warnings about dangers, and items recovered after a teammate’s death. • Corrections. When auratian’s program announced a success that had not happened, auratian corrected it two minutes later: “my previous chat was an unguarded script branch”. And when janesick reported coming back with twelve blaze powder one second after burning to death, gyldenia flagged the contradiction within three minutes.

Because each pair shares its prompt, initial noise, and sampler, these gains come from the updated weights of the dense generator alone. 5.3 LEARNING SPEED AGAINST SAMPLER-SPACE RL Online RL pays for every rollout, so the reward gained per training step matters as much as the final score. Figure 4 tracks RPO and Flow-GRPO over 50 training steps on SD3.5 Medium. Both runs start from the same base model, which scores 3.94 and 3.96 on their shared validation. Figure 4a reports the reward of the rollouts that each method samples during training. The smoothed RPO reward rises above 9 by step 30 and stays near 9.5, whereas the smoothed Flow-GRPO reward stays below 8. However, RPO scores routed rollouts and Flow-GRPO scores SDE rollouts, so neither curve measures the dense model used at inference. Figure 4b therefore reports the decisive comparison on the dense model. Flow-GRPO starts slightly faster at step 5, but RPO leads from step 10 onward. RPO reaches 8.90 at step 25, which already exceeds the step-50 score of Flow-GRPO, 8.81. It peaks at 10.20 at step 40 and ends at 9.91. Over the full run, RPO gains +5.97 over its step-zero score, whereas Flow-GRPO gains +4.86. RPO thus matches the final dense reward of Flow-GRPO in half the training steps and ends 1.1 points higher. Both curves come from one training seed, and Appendix D lists the remaining differences between the two runs. Exploration through attention routing therefore turns each training step into more dense-model reward than exploration through SDE trajectories. 5.4 VBENCH TRANSFER AND TRAINING COST A single reward scalar compresses appearance and semantics, so reward gains alone do not establish broad video quality. We therefore evaluate the base model and the three trained checkpoints on VBench, which decomposes video generation into 16 fine-grained dimensions with tailored prompts and evaluation procedures (Huang et al., 2024). Each model generates 6,220 13-frame videos from shared prompts and initial noise with a deterministic sampler (Appendix D). Table 1 lists the 8 dimensions where RPO exceeds both RL baselines, together with the overall score and training cost. The largest gains over the base model are semantic. Object class improves by +4.40, human action by +4.80, scene by +3.43, and multiple objects by +2.15. RPO also improves aesthetic quality, imaging quality, subject consistency, and motion smoothness over the base model. It is the only trained checkpoint above the base model overall.

Evaluating honesty in LLMs Prior work on trustworthy LLMs spans several related but distinct goals, including factual accuracy, hallucination reduction, calibration, uncertainty expression, and cross-prompt consistency (Lin et al., 2022; Li et al., 2023; Chern et al., 2024; Xiong et al., 2024). These settings measure reliability, but they do not directly test whether a model knowingly produces statements that conflict with its knowledge under pressure. As a result, improvements on the bench- marks may reflect broader knowledge or better calibration rather than honesty (Ren et al., 2024). 2

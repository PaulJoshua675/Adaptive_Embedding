A.2 Party attribution The response prompt asks engines to wrap candidate and party men- tions in <ENT> tags without revealing the closed entity set or target. Tagged spans are normalized and mapped to party entities using a curated longest-match alias dictionary. U.S. aliases for Kamala Harris, Joe Biden, and the Democratic Party map to Democratic, while aliases for Donald Trump and the Republican Party map to Republican. Running mates and third-party candidates are not mapped. In Japan, the dictionary contains the 11 party names and unambiguous abbreviations, with ambiguous short forms excluded and predecessor aliases mapped to their corresponding successor party. Japanese candidate names are not mapped. This leaves 0.8% of tagged spans in baseline Japanese responses and 7.5% in U.S. responses unmapped, with the latter mostly consisting of third- party candidates. A sentence-level alias scan additionally recovers untagged party mentions using word-boundary matching for ASCII aliases and substring matching for CJK aliases. The same mapping procedure is used for target selection and exposure measurement. Complete alias dictionaries are released with the artifact. A.3 Questionnaire-Derived Profile Construction ai-persuasion contains pre-treatment questionnaire responses from 2,306 participants [16]. We use demographic, political-engagement, and voting-criteria attributes for subgroup discovery, while ex- cluding ideology and candidate-evaluation variables because they directly encode or are strongly associated with political prefer- ence. We use lean_bidentrump_1 only as a subgroup-discovery label. Scores below 40, from 40 to 60, and above 60 define lean-left, swing, and lean-right groups containing 1,172, 203, and 931 respon- dents, respectively. We use decision trees for interpretable subgroup discovery rather than voter classification. Our goal is to identify attribute-defined subgroups enriched for each preference group, rather than to represent all voters in that group. We train balanced multiclass decision trees over Gini and entropy criteria, maximum depths 2–6, and minimum leaf sizes 10–100.

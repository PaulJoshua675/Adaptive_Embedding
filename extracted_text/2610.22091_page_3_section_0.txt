Applying the annotation rule in Eq. (1) gives a normalized distribution over observed labels, where Pi(c) = Pr(yi = c | Bi): Pi(M) = pi,M, Pi(A) = (1 −pi,M)pi,A, (5) Pi(B) = (1 −pi,M)(1 −pi,A). For a malignant slide, the loss is independent of pi,A; atypical evidence is therefore censored rather than penalized as a false positive. Its score on such slides is transferred through gates shared with atypical and benign training slides and should be interpreted as latent evidence, not an identifiable multi-label target. For a two-level ordered task, the construction reduces to one high-priority gate, with Pi(H) = pi,H and Pi(L) = 1 −pi,H. 3. EXPERIMENTS 3.1. Datasets and evaluation protocol Spatial–semantic consistency is studied in multi-contrast MRI super- resolution by SSCM [22]. Our evaluation focuses on histopathology and assesses both slide-level predictions and localized lesion evi- dence. BRACS. BRACS contains 547 breast WSIs from 189 pa- tients and provides both slide labels and labeled regions of interest (ROIs) [12]. We use its official patient-disjoint split of 395/67/85 WSIs for training, validation, and testing. Following the official coarse task, normal, pathological benign, and usual ductal hyperpla- sia are grouped as benign; flat epithelial atypia and atypical ductal hyperplasia as atypical; and ductal carcinoma in situ and invasive carcinoma as malignant. This mapping gives the order B ≺A ≺M used in Sec. 2. Training and model selection use only the coarse WSI label. ROI labels are reserved for the test-time evidence analysis below. Controlled mixed-bag tasks. We complement the natural BRACS setting with three controlled tasks for which the latent con- tents of each bag are known by construction. From EBRAINS [23], we select meningothelial, atypical, and anaplastic meningioma and map their WHO-2016 grades [24] to the low, middle, and high slots of DomSet-MIL. From TCGA [25], we use LUAD ver- sus LUSC [26, 27] for NSCLC subtyping and KICH, KIRC, and KIRP [28, 29, 30] for RCC subtyping. These TCGA subtypes have no asserted clinical severity order; their order is a benchmark device used solely to impose a known censoring mechanism. We therefore average NSCLC results over both class orders and RCC results over all six class orders. Patients are partitioned before synthesis. We use five patient- level folds for EBRAINS and site-stratified patient-level folds for TCGA, with 10% of the training patients in each fold held out for validation. For a task with class set Cd, we sample the nonempty latent sets Si ⊆Cd approximately uniformly. A bag with r = |Si| draws one source WSI per class and an equal share of 2,048 patch embeddings from every source. Sampling is without replacement when a slide contains enough tissue patches and with replacement only to fill a shortfall. Each epoch contains as many newly sampled bags as source WSIs in its training fold; validation and test mixtures are fixed by seed and matched in number to their respective source pools. The observed training label is yi = max≺Si, while Si re- mains hidden during model fitting. Thus, all methods receive exactly the same dominant-label training supervision. 3.2.

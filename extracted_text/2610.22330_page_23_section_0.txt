Separate Extended-Training Runs The extended LangFlow + LangDiT result is evaluated at 660k updates after continued training of the full architecture under a separate 1M-update plan. The configured decay is indexed from step 270k, with 2,500 warmup updates, peak learning rate 3 × 10−4, and cosine decay toward 3 × 10−5 at step 1M. Training resumes from the 450k checkpoint, while retaining the decay schedule indexed from 270k. Global batch size is 512, weight decay is 0.01, and EMA decay is 0.999. These settings are separate from the 250k ablation schedule. The extended Plaid + LangDiT result is evaluated at 520k updates. Its initial training stage uses a 1M-update target with 2,500 warmup updates followed by constant group learning rates: 3 × 10−4 for the backbone/projections and 10−2 for the embedding and schedule groups. The continuation resumes at 250k, uses 5,000 warmup updates, and then applies cosine decay to 0.1 times each group’s peak rate by step 1M. AdamW uses betas (0.9, 0.999), zero weight decay except 0.1 on the noise-schedule bounds, and gradient clipping at 1.0. Both stages use batch size 512, bfloat16, seed 42, and EMA decay 0.999. The OWT pipeline and tokenizer match those described in Section 4.1. All extended-training results in Table 3 use plain, unweighted inference self-conditioning. The release metadata records the source run, actual checkpoint step, planned target, and sampling mode separately. The selected results do not imply that the 1M-update targets have been reached.

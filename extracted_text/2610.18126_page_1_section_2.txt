Evidence freshness describes what was known when the choice was made. Action valid- ity describes whether the choice is right when it is carried out. Research on time in language models secures the first: benchmarks track how models age (Lazaridou et al., 2021; Dhingra et al., 2022; Kim et al., 2024), test answers against evolving news (Liška et al., 2022; Kasai et al., 2023), and refresh models with search (Vu et al., 2024). This framing fits question answering, where an answer is used the moment it is produced. LLM agents produce de- cisions that are carried out later, such as itineraries (Xie et al., 2024b) and service actions (Yao et al., 2025), and these are judged by the second. This paper develops a theory of action validity and answers the title question: freshness is nec- essary and insufficient.

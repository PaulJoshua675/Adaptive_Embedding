G Sampling-cost measurement TFLOPs. TFLOPs is arithmetic per image, counted with torch.utils.flop_counter in the 2×MAC convention, and is therefore hardware-independent unlike wall-clock throughput. It covers the full sampling loop of the generator plus one decoder pass that maps the generated tokens or latents to pixels. Classifier-free guidance duplicates the generator batch and roughly doubles its cost (BAR, FeaTok, SiT, LightningDiT, DDT and the discrete baselines), whereas REPA-E and REG gate the doubled forward to a guidance interval and pay 1.8×, RAEv2’s internal guidance reuses a single forward and costs nothing, and RAE’s autoguidance adds a smaller second network for +30%. Guidance never duplicates the decoder, since only the conditional sample is decoded. Each decoder is built from its official release and driven through the released sampler’s decode call. Decoder costs range from 0.16 TFLOPs for the pixel decoder shared by BAR and FeaTok to 0.62 for the SD-VAE decoder of the latent diffusion baselines. They are small relative to multi-step continuous generators, but not negligible for the cheapest samplers: the pixel decoder adds 15–32% to the generator cost of BAR and FeaTok, and the SD-VAE decoder exceeds the generator cost of the latent one-step models. Throughput and memory. Tab. 16 measures sampling on one NVIDIA L40S GPU in bf16 without compilation. Each run times the generation of the discrete tokens or continuous latents and the subsequent pixel decoding separately, so we report generation-only and end-to-end throughput from the same run. Throughput is the number of images produced per second, computed as batch size divided by the generation time, or by the generation and decoding time for end-to-end throughput. Memory is peak GPU memory usage during sampling and decoding at the stated batch size, reported in GiB, not memory per image. 23

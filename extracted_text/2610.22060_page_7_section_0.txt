Fig. 5 compares generated and real ImageNet-1k data using PickScore (Kirstain et al., 2023), HPSv2 (Wu et al., 2023), and HPSv3 (Ma et al., 2025), all learned from human preference comparisons. These scores are automatic proxies for human evaluations, and complement the distributional measurements in Tabs. 1 and 2. FeaTok performs better than baselines both in terms of the raw value of the rewards and the closeness to the training data. On PickScore and HPSv2, the SigLIP2, DINOv2, and DINOv3 variants are closer to the real-data reference than every baseline. On the more discriminative HPSv3, FeaTok-DINOv3 and FeaTok-SigLIP have the reward scores closest to those of the training set, followed by RAEv2, all substantially closer than BAR-L and RAE. 3.2 FeaTok outperforms BAR-L and RAE at 512×512 Following Yu et al. (2026), we train a tokenizer for ImageNet-1k generation at 512×512 with the DINOv3 vision encoder, using vocabulary size 1, 024 per token. Implementation details are in Apps. A and B. We show the gFDr6 results for 512 resolution in Tab. 4. The benefit of FeaTok over baseline is larger at higher resolution. FeaTok-L-DINOv3 reduces the unguided gFDr6 of BAR-L from 3.82 to 2.17 and the guided result from 2.94 to 1.80. These are reductions of 43% and 39%, compared with 32% and 25% for the corresponding large-scale comparison at 256 resolution. Our method outperforms BAR-L in all feature spaces without guidance, and in all feature spaces except Inception with guidance. FeaTok also outperforms RAE in gFDr6, with 2.17 against 3.03 without guidance and 1.80 against 2.13 with guidance, although RAE is better in the Inception and SigLIP2 spaces in both settings.

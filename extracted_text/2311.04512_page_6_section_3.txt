A. Experimental Settings Dataset. Our FFINet is evaluated on the Argoverse 1 and Argoverse 2 Motion Forecasting Dataset, which contains high- definition (HD) maps and trajectories of agents collected from real traffic scenarios. Argoverse 1 consists of 324,557 inter- esting agent trajectories, in which the first 2 seconds (T = 20) of each trajectory are used as the observations, and the next 3 seconds (Tp = 30) are leveraged to supervise the predic- tions. Argoverse 2 selects 250,000 scenarios for training and validation, where multiple high-quality trajectories relevant to AV are selected as ”scored actors” in each scenario for multi-agent prediction. The first 5 seconds (T = 50) of each trajectory are used as the observations, and the next 6 seconds (Tp = 60) are utilized for predictions. Evaluation Metrics.

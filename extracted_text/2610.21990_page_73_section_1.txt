The subsequent development of VesperMUSE should primarily target both performance enhance- ment and the reduction of computational complexity. At present, further improvements in perfor- mance can be facilitated by adopting more efficient backbone architectures, potentially surpassing those derived from the ResNet family. Additionally, the utilization of modality-specific backbones for thermal and structural inputs represents a promising direction, as these are expected to outper- form the current convolutional adaptation strategy employed within the model. Furthermore, it is worthwhile to investigate alternative representations of image information, such as operating in different colour spaces or explicitly decomposing the input into low- and high-frequency components within the LLIE module. Such approaches may enable a more principled treatment of light irradiation, which has been shown to be an effective strategy in LLIE tasks. 5.3 Final Thoughts The proposed framework is intended to establish a foundation for future advancements in seman- tic segmentation under challenging and highly variable illumination conditions. Despite recent progress, the field continues to be constrained by the limited availability of diverse and represen- tative datasets, which significantly hinders the development of robust and generalizable methods. The scenarios explored in this study reflect realistic and often critical environments, including safety- sensitive missions where reliability is paramount. In such contexts, the deployment of models that are not only performant but also transparent with respect to their limitations becomes essential. This is particularly relevant for applications such as survivor identification, where the consequences of failure are profound. Therefore, advancing DL-based approaches in this domain is not solely a mat- ter of improving accuracy, but also of fostering trust, robustness, and accountability in systems de- signed to operate under uncertainty. 73

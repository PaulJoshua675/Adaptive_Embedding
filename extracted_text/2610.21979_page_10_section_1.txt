We introduced P2TPO for structure-aligned RL post-training of full-page document parsers. It preserves reference-element identity, completes feedback for missing outcomes and imperfect consensus through UAP, and localizes matched-block credit through budget-preserving SCR. Our analysis characterizes local quality contrasts that fixed additive aggregation can erase. From a shared supervised checkpoint, P2TPO exceeds four response-level baselines in five-seed mean score on all 13 primary splits across six benchmarks, including a 0.74-point gain over the strongest baseline on OmniDocBench v1.6. Additional settings and ablations support its applicability and the complementarity of outcome feedback and alignment-informed token allocation.

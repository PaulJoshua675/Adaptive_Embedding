The judge rates the final image with the image- quality prompt, checking structural coherence, natural ap- pearance, absence of artifacts and texture fidelity, and yield- ing a score from 1 to 5. The prompt describes every im- age as AI-generated, and on our real photographs the judge also reports defects in untouched sources (their mean score is 3.57); since all methods are scored on the same pho- tographs and compared chain by chain, this affects the abso- lute scores but not the comparisons. ∆HPSv3 is the change in HPSv3 [31], a learned estimate of perceived image qual- ity, from the first edited image (turn 1) to the final image. Measuring from turn 1 rather than from the source separates the quality lost over the chain from the effect of a single edit. Preservation. On the whole image, the judge rates consistency with the source using the visual-consistency prompt. It sees the source, the final image and the chain’s ten instructions as one numbered list, and scores from 1 to 5 how well everything the instructions do not ask to change is preserved (5: all such elements unchanged; 1: most of the scene altered). As controls, on the first 20 EdiVal-10 chains it scores each source compared with itself 5, and each source compared with the final image of a different chain 1.

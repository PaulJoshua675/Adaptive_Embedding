The LLIE objective is formulated as a weighted hybrid loss that jointly enforces pixel-level fidelity, perceptual realism, structural consistency, and semantic preservation. Specif- ically, the loss combines: (i) a Mean Absolute Error (MAE) reconstruction term to ensure and in- tensity correctness, (ii) a perceptual loss computed in a pretrained feature space to enhance visual quality, (iii) a structural similarity loss to preserve local structures, and (iv) a semantic consistency term to maintain high-level content alignment with the ground truth, helping for the semantic fo- cus required by the downstream semantic segmentation module. The relative contribution of each component is controlled by weighting coefficients α1, α2, α3, and α4. LLLIE = α1Lℓ1 + α2Lperc + α3LSSIM + α4Lsem. (3.29) MAE Reconstruction Loss.

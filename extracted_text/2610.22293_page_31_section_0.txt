We report the measured SFT step from full-parameter bfloat16 Qwen3-8B training on eight NVIDIA L40S GPUs, with effective batch size 16, and maximum sequence length 4096. The measured SFT step took 1.73 s. Across the model’s 36 layers and 252 projected matrices, projection adds 26.2 ms (1.5%) per step in all settings. Refresh adds an amortized 62.4 ms per step for R100 and 623.5 ms for R10, giving total overheads of 5.1% and 37.6%, respectively. Splitting the SVD computation across the 8 GPUs brings the overhead to 0.6% and 4.7% respectively. B.14 Training and evaluation details Training.

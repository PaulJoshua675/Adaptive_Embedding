Of the graph data, the sandboxes of Shell only and No edges can each read their own graph directory. Scoring. The agent’s final message must be a JSON object with the single key node_ids and a list of at most 100 entries, and any other output counts as a failed attempt. Entries are scored in order, and an entry that is a non-integer, a non- candidate ID or a repeat keeps its position and counts as a miss. H@1 is 1 if the first entry is a gold answer. R@20 is the number of gold answers among the first 20 entries divided by the number of gold answers. MRR is the reciprocal rank of the first gold answer among the first 100 entries, and a list whose entries are all incorrect counts as a miss. Five runs on the test sets returned 21 IDs, one more than the 20 requested, and each of them has an incorrect answer at rank 21, so every reported MRR equals MRR over the first 20 entries. An attempt that ended in a provider error, at the time limit or with output other than the requested JSON was retried, and every query of a test con- figuration needed at most three attempts. Metrics are averaged over all queries of a set, and a query whose attempts all failed counts as a miss in every metric. In the test configurations, this occurred for one Amazon query of One program, whose three attempts all reached the time limit. Cost.

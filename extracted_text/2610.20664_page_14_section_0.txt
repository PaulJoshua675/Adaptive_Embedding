For an input x, the student produces a trajectory τ = (a1, o1, . . . , aT −1, oT −1, aT ), where at is a policy-generated search action for t < T, ot is the cor- responding tool observation, and aT is the final response. We write Ht−1 for the history available immediately before at and Ht = (Ht−1, at, ot) for the history after the observation is returned. Each action is represented autoregressively by generated tokens. Let It denote their positions, nt = |It|, and N = PT t=1 nt. For i ∈It, ut,i is the sampled token and zt,i is its complete prefix. Tool observations enter later prefixes as context, but are not generated by the policy and therefore do not receive token loss. From prefix matching to sampled-token OPD.

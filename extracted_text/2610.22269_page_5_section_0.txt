SLA is a contract between the Mobile Network Operator (MNO) and slice tenants specifying QoS requirements (e.g., maximum latency, minimum throughput). The MNO deploys an xApp within the Near-RT RIC to dynamically allocate two virtual-resource primitives [32]: i) virtual Resource Blocks (vRBs), bi,k ∈Z≥0, representing discrete spectrum units assigned to slice i; and ii) virtual Compute Workloads (vCWs), ci,k ∈[0, 1], representing the CPU/GPU time-sharing fraction. Let NvRB denote the total available vRBs and Cw = 1 the normalized compute capacity.

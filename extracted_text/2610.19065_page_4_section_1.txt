In this paper, we revisited the GR and DR in a controlled e-commerce comparison and we find two distinct parts to GR’s measured advan- tage. First, metric choice changes the apparent winner. GR leads on the value of the best relevant item, while DR leads on exact target recall and relevant value across the ranked list on both pub- lic datasets. Second, popularity makes the largest contribution to GR’s value lead. Adding the same static item value to DR in one reranking step reverses this gap while preserving DR’s recall lead. Cold target and substitute credit checks do not reveal a broader GR advantage. Graded judgments on an industrial dataset place DR above a larger GR model and DR with the prior above both. These results make DR with a static value prior a useful baseline for future GR systems. 4

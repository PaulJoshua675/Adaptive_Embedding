Catastrophic forgetting is usually treated as the price of learning new tasks. Our work studies it instead as a property of where the update lands, directly controls it, and mitigates forgetting. Confining the applied optimizer step to the complement of each weight matrix’s top singular subspace (Eq. 3) turns recipes that destroy prior capability into ones that retain it. The same operator works across recipes (SFT, SDFT, and Muon), from 1.7B to 32B parameters, and across data scale, where SFT degrades monotonically and SST stays flat. It also turns sequential fine-tuning from destructive into cumulative and keeps the model 2–4× closer to its base distribution in KL, thus preserving behavior that accuracy alone does not capture, such as a thinking model’s deliberation length and self-distillation’s fabricated reference solutions. 10

JevGPT uses explicit evidence requests to guide passage selection, further candidate inspection, and surrounding-context reading. 3 Method Given a question or topic, an autonomous research agent gathers evidence from a fixed corpus to pro- duce an answer or document ranking. In the re- search loop of Figure 3, System Two plans searches, requests evidence, and integrates findings. System One assesses sources against these requests and guides further reading. Readers return selected original passages to inform subsequent research actions and the final output. 3.1 Problem Formulation An item is a question or topic q over a fixed cor- pus D. System Two is a generative policy G with history ht at step t. Its action at = G(q, ht) is a search query, an evidence request, or a final answer or ranking.

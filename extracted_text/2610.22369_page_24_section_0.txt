D QUALITATIVE RESULTS D.1 WHAT THE FIDELITY METRICS MEASURE PSNR is a per-pixel error, so it rewards an output that drifts toward a smooth conditional mean and penalises one that keeps sharp detail slightly out of place. At high speedups a blurred output can therefore outscore a sharper one that looks closer to the reference. Fig. D.1.1 shows such a case. On one HunyuanVideo clip at a similar speedup, SeaCache leads SoftCache by 3.19 dB in PSNR, yet its foliage is visibly blurred (inset), and SSIM, LPIPS and DreamSim all favour SoftCache. We therefore report PSNR in every table but never decide on it alone, and we back the ablation choices with blind visual comparisons (Sec. C.3). The other baselines fail in two recurring ways. Some change the content, adding a second trunk to the elephant (elephant row), drawing two carrots (carrot row), or blending the giraffe with a different animal (giraffe row). Others keep the content but lose texture, smoothing the peel (pear row) or covering the tree behind the elephant with a high- frequency pattern (elephant row). SoftCache keeps the content of the reference and gives the most realistic textures (pear, red-car, bear and four-cars rows). SoftCache is the closest method to the reference on all four metrics on every row except the pear, where SeaCache leads PSNR, SSIM and LPIPS with its smoother peel while SoftCache still leads DreamSim.

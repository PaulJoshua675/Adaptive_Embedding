Research agents and scholarly infrastructure. STORM, Co-STORM, and Agent Laboratory illustrate complementary approaches to source exploration, synthesis, and research assistance [3–5]. PaperArena supplies a foundation for evaluating tool-augmented reasoning over scientific literature [1]. Idea2Prior builds on that foundation and studies a specific retrieval endpoint rather than claiming to introduce the general agentic-research setting. Scholarly indexes such as OpenAlex [6] provide useful metadata and links, while paper identity must still be checked across publication and preprint records. Our public retrieval profile uses Semantic Scholar, Crossref, and arXiv-related resolution; OpenAlex remains an optional legacy backend. Evidence and research artifacts. SciFact explicitly associates scientific claims with supporting or refuting evidence [7]. Our task instead ranks works by how well they match an idea; facet scores and selected passages are decision evidence, not formal proof of novelty or claim truth. Implementation analysis similarly surfaces code, pseudocode, dependencies, and missing details without establishing that an entire published experiment is reproducible. These distinctions matter when model-produced assessments are later reused in scientific writing. 2

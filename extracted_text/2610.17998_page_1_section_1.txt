Artificial intelligence systems deployed in real-world environments must adapt to evolving data while meeting strict computational and energy constraints. Yet standard neural networks still suffer from catastrophic forgetting, where learning new tasks disrupts previously acquired representations [13]. Continual learning (CL) addresses this by preserving prior knowledge while integrating new infor- mation over time. Among CL strategies, replay-based methods are particularly effective, storing representative samples from past tasks and mixing them with current data during training [3,21]. However, their performance depends on mem- ory capacity: larger buffers improve retention but are impractical for resource- constrained systems. To reduce this cost, Experience Packing and Replay (EPR) stores saliency-guided image patches rather than full samples, substantially re- ducing memory requirements while maintaining competitive CL performance [23].

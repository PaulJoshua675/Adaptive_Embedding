Specifically, VesperMUSE establishes a new SOTA on the CART dataset [25] with an mIoU of 0.767, outperforming the previous leading method (CM-SSM [73]) by a significant margin of 2.1%. Furthermore, when compared to other larger architectures such as MCNet-T [83], VesperMUSE delivers a 3.0% absolute improvement in mIoU while utilizing approximately 19.3 million fewer parameters (180.45M vs. 199.79M). The primary sources of the increased computational demand are: the tri-input framework, which necessitates an additional back- bone and a supplementary pathway through the encoder, and the integration of the DepthAnything V2 [30] model for monocular depth estimation, which alone accounts for 43 GFLOPs.

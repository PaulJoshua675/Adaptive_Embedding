We introduced SSEmb, an end-to-end speech retriever trained only on standard ASR data by aligning speech with a frozen text retriever’s embedding space. Across English and six other languages, SSEmb approaches cascaded ASR-then- retrieve performance with lower latency and generalizes to text-to-speech and speech-to-speech retrieval.Our analyses show that speech-transcript alignment outperforms direct speech-document supervision, while retrieval performance consistently improves as the amount of ASR training data increases. These results demonstrate a practical, scalable approach to speech retrieval without task-specific query- document pairs.

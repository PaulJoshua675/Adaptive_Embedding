D.2 SETUP AND TRAINING SIGNALS The RLVR prompt blend is drawn from 14 verifiable environments (tool use, code generation, SWE and search pivots, instruction following, reasoning, and formatting). We reward-profile a seed blend against both checkpoints and keep only prompts with mixed rewards, meaning that for each check- point some but not all of its sampled rollouts succeed, so every prompt is neither trivial nor unsolv- able for either model. Training is GRPO with a leave-one-out baseline and no reference-policy KL penalty, 256 prompts × 16 generations per step, a 32K-token output cap within a 131K context, a learning rate of 10−6 with 10 warmup steps, and 20 optimizer updates. The two runs differ only in their starting checkpoint; they use the same blend, seed, and prompt order. We evaluate SWE-bench Verified with 5 rollouts per task and Terminal-Bench 2.1 with 8, for both the SFT starting points and the RL checkpoints. Training-time metrics, averaged over matched steps, favor the midtrained model. It earns 4.3% higher train reward (0.308 vs. 0.295) with 12% shorter generations (4.6K vs. 5.3K mean tokens), and it produces format violations (a stray end-of-sequence token or malformed thinking tags, which zero the reward) about ten times less often (0.03% vs. 0.34% of trajectories). Its truncation rate is also lower (0.056% vs. 0.073%). Together these make RL from the midtrained checkpoint about 15% faster per trajectory on the same hardware.

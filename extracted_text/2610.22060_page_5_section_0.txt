2.2 Controlled ablations across pretrained vision encoders We verify FeaTok, feature-loss tokenization, against pixel-loss tokenization using four pretrained vision encoders: SigLIP (Zhai et al., 2023), SigLIP2 (Tschannen et al., 2025), DINOv2 (Oquab et al., 2024), and DINOv3 (Siméoni et al., 2026). We compare the generation performance of a generative model trained on ImageNet-1k (Russakovsky et al., 2015) images at 256×256 resolution using different tokenizers. Details of ablation setups are given in App. C. Additionally, App. C.2 shows that tokenizers built on MAE (He et al., 2022), whose features are learned by pixel reconstruction, generate poorly under every supervision. Feature targets work better than pixel-only or mixed feature–pixel targets. Feature supervision improves the generation over pixel supervision for three of the four encoders, as shown in Fig. 3a. With patch-only generation, replacing pixel targets with feature targets lowers gFID for SigLIP, DINOv2, and DINOv3, while combining pixel and feature losses, i.e., letting gradients from both decoders update the discrete tokens, gives intermediate results. Fig. 3b relates these gFID changes to the spatial dependence between adjacent discrete patch tokens. The feature target raises this dependence and lowers gFID for SigLIP, DINOv2, and DINOv3, showing that the feature target improves the token structure for generation. SigLIP2 is the exception, possibly because, unlike the other three encoders, it is not trained only in feature space. Its training adds a text decoder that generates captions of whole images and of specific regions (Tschannen et al., 2025). Our models achieve state-of-the-art performance among methods using discrete tokenizers and outperform recent continuous generative models at a matched inference cost on ImageNet-1k 256×256. FeaTok achieves better performance on ImageNet-1k 512×512 than both continuous and discrete baselines. For each tokenizer, we train the same autoregressive generator as BAR (Yu et al., 2026), the previous state-of-the-art discrete model for ImageNet-1k, with our global-augmented modelling.

This ordering is consistent with the extraction capturing physically meaningful variation rather than recording artifacts. 4 Physical Structure in Audio-Visual Embeddings 4.1 Models Evaluated We probe four frozen encoders. Two are audio-visual: PE-AV [Vyas et al., 2025], trained con- trastively on 100M audio-visual pairs and exposing separate audio, video, and joint embeddings, and CAV-MAE [Gong et al., 2023], a masked autoencoder trained on AudioSet [Gemmeke et al., 2017] with both reconstruction and contrastive objectives. Two are vision-only: CLIP ViT-L/14 [Radford et al., 2021], trained on 400M image–text pairs, and DINOv2 ViT-L/14 [Oquab et al., 2024], trained by self-distillation on curated images without language supervision. The latter pair represents the two dominant recipes for general-purpose visual features, and neither has any acoustic exposure. Video inputs are single frames taken at the annotated moment of impact, each encoded as an image, and audio inputs are 2-second clips centered on the impact. 4.2 Probing Protocol We train ridge regression (λ = 1.0) from frozen embeddings to log(Q + 1) and evaluate with Spearman ρ. Pooled correlations carry 95% bootstrap intervals over samples (1,000 resamples). Leave-one-material-out figures are means over the 17 held-out materials, and their intervals resample the materials rather than the samples (10,000 resamples), which is why they are wider. 4.3 Results Table 1 reports the result. The gap between audio (ρ = 0.73) and CLIP vision (ρ = 0.28) quantifies physical structure that frozen single-frame visual encoders do not linearly expose. Co-training on audio does not by itself place that structure in the visual pathway: PE-AV’s video branch reaches 0.362 and CAV-MAE’s only 0.151, both far below their own audio branches, and the two models differ in architecture, data scale, and objective, none of which this comparison controls. We therefore treat the audio results as motivation for pushing structure into a frozen visual encoder deliberately, rather than as evidence that co-training supplies it for free. 4

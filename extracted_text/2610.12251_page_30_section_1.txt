A fixed network of ternary threshold units holds threshold networks as records and runs them, and a stored network can build any network that fits a bank, copy its description and hand control to what it built. The organism and the stored SUBLEQ host rebuild themselves in this way, weights and biases included, in every ring, and integer pre-activations keep every generation exact while no perturbation of a pre-activation reaches one half. The fixed network itself is not reproduced. Its netlist is ternary and compiles into records by Lemma 3.4, but its state, which contains the window, the banks and the tapes, cannot fit in its own window, so a network that also rebuilds the network that runs it needs another construction. Other questions are open: whether a ternary step of SUBLEQ can have depth below 11, since Lemma 5.7 and Remark 5.5 bound only a read and the branch; how few records a self-reproducing stored network needs; and how much noise a reproducing run tolerates when the perturbations are correlated or drift, which the independence assumed in Section 8 excludes.

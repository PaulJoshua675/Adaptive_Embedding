Patient Simulation. As instruction following and clinical knowledge of LLMs continue to improve, pa- tient simulation has become a viable complement to real or role-played human-therapist interactions. This matters particularly in mental health, where (1) real sessions are highly sensitive; (2) testing an un- validated model on people seeking help is unethical, and (3) role-play with trained actors or clinicians is costly and does not scale. As a result, it is now possible to scale training and evaluation by synthetically generating multi-turn conversational interactions.

Training Scheme. During training, VLM parameters θ is frozen and the historical context C0:t−1 are not directly provided to the VLM when predicting yt. Instead, C0:t−1 can only influence the prediction through the generated LoRA weights ∆θt. The prediction loss is backpropagated through ∆θt to optimize the parameters in the HyperNetwork f, encouraging MaLoW to preserve historical information that is useful for future interactions while discarding redundant context.

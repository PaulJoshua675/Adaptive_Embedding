Supervised fine-tuning datasets remain over- whelmingly English-centric and commercially restricted, as their content is frequently gen- erated by proprietary models whose terms of service forbid training competing systems. We introduce Multi-SFT, a commercially permis- sive collection of 502,818 instruction-response pairs across five European languages, built via translation and regeneration pipelines for tasks where translation is insufficient. Human evalua- tion yields inter-annotator agreement of at least 0.87 across all datasets and languages. Fine- tuning nine models from three different fam- ilies (EuroLLM, OLMo 2, Qwen3; 1B–32B) and evaluating them on 13 benchmarks shows that Multi-SFT alone is competitive with larger augmented mixes and official instruction-tuned releases, with decisive advantages on safety tasks. 1

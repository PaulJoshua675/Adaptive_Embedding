These likelihoods additionally enable reward guidance to encourage adherence to the demonstrations. 3.1 Length Allocation and Joint Infilling We begin with a compact canvas in which each missing output is represented by a single placeholder. Let ez denote this canvas, containing J target placeholders together with the visible demonstrations and surrounding context. For a proposed length vector L = (L1, . . . , LJ), where each Lj ≥1 specifies the token length of target j, we expand each placeholder into Lj mask tokens. This produces an expanded canvas zL with target positions TL = M(zL), on which the DLM can generate the missing contents. We model joint generation of output lengths and contents by first allocating lengths and then infilling the resulting canvas. Let πψ(L | ez) be a distribution over length allocations, with each Lj ranging over a prescribed finite set of positive integers. For a given allocation, let u = (ui)i∈TL ∈V TL denote a complete infill, assigning a vocabulary token ui ∈V to each target position. We define the joint distribution P(L, u | ez) := πψ(L | ez) P(u | zL). (1) The allocator may be a point mass at oracle lengths or a learned predictor, as investigated in Section 4. To complete this formulation, we next characterize the distribution P(u | zL) induced by DLM decoding. 3.2 Tractable DLM Infilling A DLM provides predictions at individual masked positions, but different decoding procedures can induce different distributions over complete infills (Section 2). To obtain tractable likelihoods, we focus on decoders that reveal one token at a time, including prescribed orders and deterministic confidence-based selection. Fix an expanded canvas z = zL with target positions T = M(z), and let u = (ui)i∈T ∈V T be a candidate completion. At step s, the decoder selects an unrevealed target position is based on the current canvas, then samples its token from the DLM. Let z(s) denote the canvas after revealing the first s tokens of u, with z(0) = z. For deterministic position selection, replaying the policy on u determines the reveal order and yields the complete-infill likelihood P(u | z) := |T| Y s=1 PDLM(uis | z(s−1)).

We conduct experiments on DRIVE and DSCA using nnU-Net. 4.5. Ablation Study We perform a cumulative ablation to assess the contribu- tion of TRACE’s main components. We compare six con- figurations: the Dice + CE baseline (DCE) used in nnU- Net [12]; conventional target-skeleton recall (Skel) [13]; its scale-native TASC replacement (TASC); TASC with the centered logit-margin score (TASC-R); an unbounded recon- nection variant with ϕ(a) = a (TRACE-U); and the full ob- jective incorporating exact local reconnection and bounded penalty integration (TRACE). We evaluate all configurations on DRIVE. 5. Result and Discussion Main comparison. TRACE consistently outperforms com- peting methods across all six benchmark datasets. As shown in Tabs. 1 and 2, it achieves the best or near-best results in both 2D and 3D settings, covering binary and multi- class segmentation tasks.

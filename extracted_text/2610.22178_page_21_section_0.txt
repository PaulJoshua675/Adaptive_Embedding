Prompt. The prompt zt of §3.2 is a structured user turn carrying one field: value per line: embodiment: gr1 instruction: grasp the mug speed: 2 fps: 20 control mode: absolute joint The speed field is the length of the episode in frames divided by 100 and rounded, so that a smaller value marks a shorter demonstration. It is left out at evaluation. Episodes of ABC (Allshire et al., 2026) that are labelled with their outcome also carry a success field. Positions. In the expert’s self-attention, register i of a frame sits at slot i and action step j at slot nr + j, so that the registers of each frame carry their own temporal encoding. The frame and slot axes reuse the temporal and spatial axes of the VLM’s multimodal rotary embedding. A.1 INFERENCE COST AND CACHING What is cached. One chunk is an encoder pass, then N Euler steps over the action tokens, the first of which also computes the register tokens of the current frame (Fig. 10). Only the noisy actions change from one Euler step to the next, so four quantities are reused across the N steps. 1. The encoded context Et of Eq. (4). 2. Its projection to keys and values, one pair per expert block. 3. The keys and values of the register tokens Mt of the current frame, one pair per expert block. 4. The register keys and values of the K −1 preceding frames, carried over from earlier chunks. The first three are built the same way at training and at inference, while the fourth is not: the first registers of a window hold memory from earlier registers at inference, and none at training. 21

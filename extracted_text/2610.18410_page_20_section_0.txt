Most basic families are parameterised rather than imple- mented separately. A configuration names the relevant chunk and entity prefixes, whether an organisation is required, one or more surface templates, and an answer_fn that extracts the target value from source. A generic routine resolves the linking chunk, applies the extraction function, and fills a template. Question semantics are therefore separated from traversal logic, and a new subtype costs a configuration entry rather than new generation code. Multi-hop generators are the exception: each chain is implemented directly, because successive hops cross different entity types. Sampling and budget. Candidate tuples come from type-specific samplers—person–organisation, person pair, organisation, extremisation—each relying on seeded shuffling. Families with several subtypes cycle through their configurations so that consecutive questions alternate subtype until the budget is met or the candidate pool is exhausted. The nominal budget is divided evenly across the eleven families, so a request for 1,000 questions asks for 90 of each.

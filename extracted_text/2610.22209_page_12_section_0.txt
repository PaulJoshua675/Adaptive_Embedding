The strong negative correlation between miss rate and intrinsic accuracy (r = −0.84, p < 10−8) validates that claim coverage is the primary driver of intrinsic scores. The weaker correlation between miss rate and extrinsic ac- curacy (r = −0.39) confirms that extrinsic evaluation is less sensitive to extraction failures , consistent with LLM compensation masking missing claims at query time. The moderate positive correlation between intrinsic and extrinsic (r = 0.55) shows they are related but far from equivalent, reinforcing the paper’s argument that both are needed.

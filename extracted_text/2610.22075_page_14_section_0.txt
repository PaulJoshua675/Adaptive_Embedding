A APPENDIX A.1 TRAINING HYPERPARAMETERS Every stage uses Muon (Jordan et al., 2024) for the matrix parameters and AdamW for all other parameters, with weight decay 10−4, gradient clipping at 1.0 and a warmup-stable-decay schedule with 5% warmup. In S0 the learning rates are 5·10−4 for Muon and 2.5·10−5 for AdamW, the batch holds 128 sequences of 2,048 tokens, and the learning rate decays to zero over the last 35% of the steps. The 60B run of our model takes 228,874 steps on 16 GPUs, and the 7.5B run of the ablations takes 28,611 steps. In S1 and S2 the learning rates are 10−3 for Muon and 5·10−5 for AdamW, the learning rate decays to zero over the last 60% of the steps, and both stages run on 4 GPUs. S1 trains the projector for 6,426 steps with a batch of 96 image-text pairs, and S2 trains the whole model for 6,408 steps with a batch of 128, one pass over the data in both stages with the interleaved text batches included in the step counts. The distillation weights and temperatures of each stage are given in Sec. 4. The Parcae adapter Apc of Table 3 uses an additional learning rate of 10−3 for its adapter parameters, and the per-layer looping variants of Table 3(c) share the recipe of the 7.5B cells. The converted recurrent VLM keeps the 4/8/4 geometry of our model and is trained with its own trainer with the same learning rates and a batch of 128 in both stages.

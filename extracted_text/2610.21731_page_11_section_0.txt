Both terms come from prompts that differ only in the page, so anything specific to the fact cancels. Club scores. Club names span several tokens, so each club is represented by a set of candidate first tokens. The name is split into words of three or more letters, generic words (e.g. fc, club, united, city) are dropped, and each remaining word con- tributes its first token, with and without a leading space. At each layer, a club’s logit is the maximum over its set, and its rank is the minimum. In roughly a third to a half of replies, the set misses the token the model actually writes, typically a bold-markup fragment or a generic first word such as FC.

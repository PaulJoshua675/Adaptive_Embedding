SFT cold start. We fully fine-tune the model on the approximately 8k structured reasoning sam- ples from Section 3.3, using the complete five-stage reasoning and assessment as the training target. GRPO for human alignment. After SFT cold start, we apply GRPO (Shao et al., 2024) on a 10k rollout set constructed from the human-calibrated data. The reward jointly preserves the required evaluation structure and aligns the three consistency scores with human judgment. Specifically, a structure reward rstruct enforces the five-stage reasoning and assessment format. A score distillation reward rdistill = 1 3 P2 i=0 exp[−wi(predi −refi)2] measures agreement with the human-calibrated scores, and the total reward is rtotal = (2rstruct + rdistill)/3, where i = 0, 1, 2 corresponds to (Smac, Stemp, Sattr). 4 BENCHMARK Motivation. We construct AVC-Benchmark to evaluate how well automatic audio-video consis- tency metrics align with human perception in realistic generation scenarios. Benchmark composition and evaluation target. AVC-Benchmark is the first benchmark, to our knowledge, that primarily uses various types of model-generated audio-video data to evaluate the alignment between automatic consistency metrics and human perception. It contains samples from A2V, V2A, and JAVG models, together with a small set of controlled negatives added to balance the failure-case distribution and broaden the coverage of audio-video inconsistencies. Each sample is independently annotated by three annotators, providing human ground-truth scores on the three consistency dimensions defined in Section 3.2. We evaluate each metric against these human scores using pairwise accuracy under different score-difference thresholds, Spearman’s rank correlation coefficient (SRCC), and Pearson’s linear correlation coefficient (PLCC). Statistics. Detailed benchmark statistics are provided in App. D.2. In general, most samples retain high global scene and narrative consistency, providing a meaningful basis for evaluating finer tem- poral and attribute-level relations; accordingly, the latter two dimensions span much broader score ranges. The annotated failures further cover global narrative, affective, and environmental conflicts; temporal errors across physical events, speech, and music; and fine-grained mismatches in material, morphology, and causal response. 5 EXPERIMENTS We evaluate AVC-Reward as both an automatic evaluator and a training reward for joint generation.

F Comparison of data representation We show on figure F.1 the differences in trajectories of the adLIF and DeNN models on two data representation. In (Bittar and Garner, 2022), the adLIF models were trained on fbanks representation of audio data for the GSC dataset. We thus ran an experiment where we took every hyperparameter similar to (Bittar and Garner, 2022), without any batchnorm and dropout. It appears that adLIF neurons are much more tuned to fbanks data representation than temporal input as computed in 2.1. However, the DeNN model can extract temporal information from both temporal inputs and fbanks inputs.

For each of the five optimizers and each of the two controllers the gain vector with the lowest recorded Jtot is selected (ten fixed vectors), mapped to controller parameters by the same transformation used during tuning. Be- fore the corrected campaign, a separate provenance qualification replay reproduced the ten archived training scores to a maximum absolute difference of 2.03×10−8, confirming the frozen gains and the objective implementation; that provenance check is not part of the corrected transfer set. The corrected campaign then evaluates each of the ten vectors on twenty pre-specified unseen arc realizations plus one corrected base-42 reference realization, giving 210 one-second EMT simulations in total: 200 unseen and 10 reference. Every vector receives the identical twenty unseen arcs and no optimizer is re-run. All 210 cases are hard-feasible and pass the delivered-configuration audit. The arc realizations used here are generated by a corrected implementation of the arc-length perturbation. Two stochastic conditions are distinguished here: STO-SR, the fixed-seed stochastic-mode realization that defined the original application tuning condition, and STO-ENS, the independent multi-realization stochastic ensemble used for frozen-gain transfer. STO-ENS is a methodological role and its realization count is paper-specific; here it is the twenty pre-specified unseen arc realizations, and the corrected base-42 realization is a separate reference case that is excluded from all STO-ENS statistics. Archived identifiers containing STO are retained in the source files for traceability. A post-campaign audit established that the sampler used during the original campaign produced a periodic rather than broadband trajectory. The twenty unseen bases were therefore re-drawn under an exact integer implementation and pre-specified before any corrected result was computed; one candidate base was excluded by the pre-specified screen because one of its phase seeds fell on the degenerate short orbit. No gain vector was retuned and no vector was res- elected. The corrected base-42 realization is reported as a reference case only: because the generator itself changed, it is not a reproduction of the STO-SR tuning trajectory, and it is excluded from every unseen-arc statistic below. 9

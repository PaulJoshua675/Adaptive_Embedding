In the current research GA will be tested in three modes A, B and C as in the case of ATSP. 6 Computational Experiments 6.1. Experiments on Traveling Salesman Problem Instances. Computational experiments were performed on 126 test instances with the known optimum from the library [51], for which the number of vertices is varied from 64 to 315. These instances are generated from one series of instances of the mixed general routing problem with turn penalties (see [51]) and characterized by asymmetric but similar-valued edge weights.

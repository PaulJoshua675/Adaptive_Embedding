Across all 5 damage scenarios, the DRL-DCO algorithm consistently identifies the correct damage locations and produces stiffness estimates in reasonable to close agreement with the reference values, with low standard deviations across independent runs confirming stable and repeatable performance. The observed discrepancies, where present, are consistently traceable to the structural simplifications of the identification model rather than to the optimizer’s search behavior, underscoring that the DRL-DCO algorithm performs at the practical accuracy ceiling imposed by the identification model itself.

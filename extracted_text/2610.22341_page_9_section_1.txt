In this paper, we present 3DCodeVerse, an open ecosystem that provides a shared foundation for 3D coding through datasets, a reusable harness, and open model baselines. The dataset brings together programs across coding environments with descriptions, associated assets, and available quality records. The harness combines execution, spatial checks, and visual judgment to support iterative generation without human feedback during a run, while retaining the resulting programs and revisions for data curation. Experiments demonstrate the utility of the data for training open models and the benefits of the harness for existing coding agents. We will release the datasets, harness, models, and training recipes to support further research on 3D generation through code. 9

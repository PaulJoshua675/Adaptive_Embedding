Each method generates both geometry and appearance end-to-end from a single reference image, without access to ground-truth geometry or cameras during generation. We compare with TRELLIS.2, Pixal3D⋆, Hunyuan3D 2.1, Step1X-3D, UniLat3D, and CUPID (Xiang et al., 2026; Li et al., 2026a; Tencent Hunyuan3D Team, 2025a; Li et al., 2025a; Wu et al., 2026a; Huang et al., 2026). SUPRA-Tex and Pixal3D⋆share geometry predicted by the latter, with separate appearance pipelines. Other baselines generate their own geometry. For scoring, outputs are brought into a common coordinate frame, with GT similarity alignment applied to methods marked † in Table 3; this alignment plays no role in generation. Results. Table 3 shows that SUPRA-Tex leads across all 9 metrics on both datasets. Relative to Pixal3D⋆, four-view PSNR improves by 1.77 dB on DTC-Tex and 2.49 dB on Complex-Tex, foreground PSNR by 0.89 dB and 1.60 dB, and LPIPS decreases by 64.8% and 58.6%, respectively. Higher reference-view CLIP-I and lower 16-view distribution distances further indicate stronger input consistency and multi-view appearance quality.

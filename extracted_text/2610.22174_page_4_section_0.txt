However, broadcasting offers only coarse supervision and no explicit control over optimization across sessions, causing poorly targeted learning signals as trajectories span more sessions. To address this limitation, we propose Mosaic, which improves multi-session agentic RL through session-aware trajectory-level advantage estimation and session-level advantage shaping.

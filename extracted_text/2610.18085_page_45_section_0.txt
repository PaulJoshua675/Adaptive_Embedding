We repeat the paired comparison of Vanilla and Soft SAGGS with α = 0.5, crossed with λ = 0 and λ = 0.2, on OpenWebText (Gokaslan and Cohen, 2019), using a 100M-token GPT-2-tokenized subset, with the same medium-scale architecture as the 101M WikiText-103 comparisons: L = 24, d = 512, H = 8, and 101M parameters, trained with AdamW at η = 3 × 10−4, β = (0.9, 0.95), weight decay 0.1, a cosine schedule with 1.5K warmup, gradient clipping at 1.0, batch size 32, T = 512, and BF16 autocast, for 15K steps with all-pairs reparameterization. The learning rate, warmup, horizon, and use of all-pairs rather than Q–K-only tilts differ from the 30K-step recipe of the 101M WikiText-103 comparisons, and the optimizer differs from the optimizer of the Lion comparisons. The replication changes both corpus and training recipe. Each configuration uses three paired seeds. Table 24 reports 55.92 ± 0.07 PPL for unperturbed Vanilla training and 74.28 ± 0.41 after rescaling. At λ = 0.2, the paired Vanilla-minus-Soft difference is +18.278 PPL; at λ = 0, the difference is −0.032 PPL. The protection has the same direction as the WikiText-103 comparison, but its magnitude is not directly attributable to the corpus change because the optimizer recipe, horizon, and edited pairs also differ. The two-sided sign-flip p-value is 0.25 at λ = 0.2 for three seeds. This experiment provides supporting evidence on another corpus under a different training recipe. This OpenWebText comparison keeps the GPT-2 BPE vocabulary and the same dense Transformer without GQA or RMSNorm. The architecture and tokenizer replication in Appendix F.2 instead tests a smaller but deliberately different LLaMA-like stack (Touvron et al., 2023; Ainslie et al., 2023): RMSNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU, GQA, and a T5 SentencePiece tokenizer. G.15 123.8M Modern-Architecture Scale Replication The 31M replication in Appendix F.2 changed the architecture and tokenizer at a scale that admits seven paired seeds. We next test the same mechanism in the 100M-parameter regime. We keep the RMSNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU, and GQA block and T5 SentencePiece tokenizer, but use a 24-layer model with d = 640, ten query heads, two KV heads, SwiGLU hidden width 1728, sequence length 512, and tied embeddings, for 123.8M trainable parameters. We use the same paired design comparing Vanilla and Soft SAGGS with α = 0.5, crossed with λ = 0 and λ = 0.2, over three paired seeds. The optimizer is AdamW with η = 2.5 × 10−4, weight decay 0.1, cosine decay, 10% warmup, gradient clipping at 1.0, BF16 XPU autocast, batch size 12, sequence length 512, and 15K steps. The reparameterization and Soft SAGGS act on Q–K, V–O, and SwiGLU value–output pairs, with rebalancing every 100 steps. Training uses Intel Data Center GPU Max 1550 XPU tiles. Table 25 reports the three-seed summaries. The 123.8M replication follows the same pattern as the 31M comparison. At λ = 0, the mean Vanilla-minus-Soft difference is +0.055 ± 0.028 PPL.

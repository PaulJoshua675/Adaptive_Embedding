The reasoning corpus combines procedurally generated time series with tasks constructed from real underlying temporal data. Most families use synthetic generative processes that provide direct control over the latent mechanism and the correct answer. The only real tem- poral data are NBA (2021–22) and NFL (2021–22 to 2023–24) play-by-play records from GAME- Time (Tan et al., 2025), used in two AR templates (2,156 items, 2.2% of the corpus); for these, the questions and supervision are constructed independently of the evaluation sets. This design allows the corpus to combine controlled reasoning problems with temporal patterns occurring in real data without using examples from the evaluation sets described above. 23

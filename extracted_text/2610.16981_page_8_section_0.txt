Distribution of shortest path lengths in case of unweighted (top rows) and weighted (bottom rows) scheme for each inter-transaction and teleport conditions. 4.2 Shortest Path Distribution We report the shortest path length distributions obtained under different inter- transaction conditions in Fig. 1. Experiments conducted under unweighted and weighted schemes are reported in the top and bottom panels, respectively. Without edge weighting, distributions obtained without LIFO-cutoff are rel- atively similar across node restrictions, concentrated over a small range: path lengths stay below 20 with teleport, and increase only slightly without it, reach- ing 23 for all nodes and 33 under ego-node restriction. In contrast, LIFO-cutoff produces markedly different distributions depending on node restriction: with no restriction, path lengths can exceed 400, whereas restricting inter-transaction links to ego accounts sharply reduces the maximum to 140 without teleportation and to 51 with teleportation. Edge weighting increases similarity between distributions when the LIFO- cutoff is absent, muting the effect of node restriction and extending lengths up to 51 under both teleportation conditions. For LIFO-cutoff paths, weighting has a limited effect without teleportation, but with teleportation and ego-node restriction combined, it substantially extends observed lengths, up to 200. The impact of node restriction is thus strongly conditioned by the inter- transaction filtering scheme. Edge weighting tends to stabilize path-length dis- tributions across teleportation conditions, suggesting that the resulting paths are less sensitive to the teleportation parameter. 4.3 Shortest Path Composition We next examine edge composition.

Result Analysis Table II reports results on the preprocessed eICU dataset. Baselines include transfer learning methods EMCDR and DDTCDR [5], [6], general LLM recommenders Reason4Rec and EXP3RT [26], [27], and MR methods SafeDrug, VITA, TEMPT, and RES-MR [2], [7], [28], [29]. TEMPT targets multi-center MR. All methods undergo 10 bootstrap evalua- tions with a sampling ratio of 0.8; metric means and standard deviations are reported. HEM-Med outperforms transfer and general LLM base- lines on all accuracy-related metrics and achieves the highest SafeScore. These results suggest that domain alignment or preference reasoning alone may be insufficient for multi-center MR. Compared with SafeDrug, the strongest MR baseline, HEM-Med improves the accuracy–safety trade-off through disentangled memory, calibrated reranking, and DDI-aware decoding. Although Reason4Rec has the lowest DDI Rate, HEM-Med combines higher accuracy with competitive safety. E. Ablation Study Table III reports four ablations: w/o MD, w/o DDI-Dec, w/o RERANKER, and w/o WAM, removing memory de- coupling, DDI-aware decoding, experience reranking, and weak auxiliary memory, respectively. Each removal degrades performance, supporting the contributions of memory disen- tanglement, safety-aware decoding, candidate calibration, and weak experience. Full HEM-Med achieves the best overall performance. F. Parameter Analysis We analyze the influence of two key hyperparameters: the weak auxiliary experience weight and the candidate depth.

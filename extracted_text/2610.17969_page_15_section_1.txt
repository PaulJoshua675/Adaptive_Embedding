C.1 Normalization, residual scaling, and attention temperature Batch normalization established the practice of restandardizing internal activations (Ioffe and Szegedy, 2015), and LayerNorm and RMSNorm carry that practice into the Transformer (Ba et al., 2016; Zhang and Sennrich, 2019), where Pre-LN placement stabilizes depth (Xiong et al., 2020) and NormFormer adds post-attention and mid-MLP normalization with head scaling (Shleifer et al., 2021). Residual connections make depth trainable in the first place (He et al., 2016), and DeepNet, ReZero, SkipInit, LayerScale, and Fixup control residual-branch magnitude to sustain that trainability (Wang et al., 2024; Bachlechner et al., 2021; De and Smith, 2020; Touvron et al., 2021b; Zhang et al., 2019). A complementary line removes the normalizer instead of tuning the normalizer’s placement, through a learnable elementwise squashing (Zhu et al., 2025) or through analytically scaled residual blocks with adaptive gradient clipping (Brock et al., 2021). The same accounting governs the attention interface. The 1/√dh factor is the sQ = sK = 1 case of the logit nondimensionalizer τ = √dhsQsK (Vaswani et al., 2017), QK-Norm and its use in a 22-billion-parameter Vision Transformer (ViT) make softmax inputs dimensionless by construction (Henry et al., 2020; Dehghani et al., 2023), σReparam bounds logit growth through weight reparameterization (Zhai et al., 2023), and controlled small-scale reproductions trace large-model training instabilities to attention-logit growth exposed through learning-rate sensitivity (Wortsman et al., 2024). NDA interprets these mechanisms as scale controls at different interfaces. Normalization nondimensionalizes from Ures to 1. Residual scaling controls the dimensionless step size αℓ, and attention scaling implements the softmax rule of Sec. 2.2. In a normalizer-free block, a bounded nonlinearity and a calibrated input scale can provide the corresponding nondimensionalization without a measured normalization statistic. C.2 Optimizers, parameterizations, and typed optimization Adaptive moment estimation (Adam) normalizes per-coordinate gradient scale (Kingma and Ba, 2015), and layer-wise adaptive moments for batch training (LAMB) and layer-wise adaptive rate scaling (LARS) apply relative trust-ratio updates per block, which restore primal units in our terminology (You et al., 2020, 2017). µP chooses width-dependent multipliers to keep the update effect invariant across widths and enable zero-shot hyperparameter transfer (Yang and Hu, 2021; Yang et al., 2021). Depth-wise µP scales residual-branch contributions as 1/ √ L to enable the same transfer across depth (Yang et al., 2024). Unit scaling chooses scales for low-precision formats (Blake et al., 2023), generalizing the loss-scaling constant of mixed-precision training, which is an inserted unit conversion that keeps half-precision gradients representable (Micikevicius et al., 2018), and unit-scaled µP composes the two disciplines (Blake et al., 2025). A parallel line makes the metric on weight space explicit. The modular norm equips a network with a norm under which well-scaled updates transfer across width and depth (Large et al., 2024), and modular duality with the efficient Muon realization casts the optimizer as a duality map that sends dual gradient vectors to primal update directions (Bernstein and Newhouse, 2025; Jordan et al., 2024). NDA uses the same primal–dual relation, in which a gradient carries the inverse unit of the differentiated variable and an optimizer restores the primal unit. NDA expresses this primal–dual relation for scalar rescaling groups G = (R+)K as a typing discipline covering the full computation graph, including objectives and diagnostics. A modular norm can supply a shape-aware duality map, while NDA further requires the complete displacement, including the scalar step or reference scale of the update, to obey Prop. 2. Combining Muon with a scalar scale that obeys the required transformation law can make the full update unit-covariant. Our optimizer contribution is the covariance criterion, while the proposed constructions are the UVI diagnostics and the canonical architecture.

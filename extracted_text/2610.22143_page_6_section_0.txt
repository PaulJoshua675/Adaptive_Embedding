Accuracy and deadline behavior The relevant target is the INT8 algorithm, because the DPU cannot run FP32. Quantizing only the BEV stage (clamp 32, trunk FP32) reaches 98% of FP32 mIoU (32.19 to 31.6 on 6019 frames), isolating the clip cost. Reference con- figurations matter: an idealized clamp-16 reference gives 29.08; the reference matched to the deployed configuration (clamp 32, view transform quantized as deployed, INT16 at 1/128 resolution) scores 31.43, and the board reaches 29.88 against it, a 1.55 gap attributable to the real per-layer quan- tizer realization. Quantizing the view transform to INT8 in- stead would cost 9.4 mIoU (22.01 vs 31.43), the direct jus- tification for the INT16 gather datapath.

Tacit knowing, on this account, is an operating regime of a memory rather than a kind of content stored in it. VaCoAl/PyVaCoAl makes the claim concrete because one substrate, under one dial, yields either an exactly auditable trace or an emergent valuation of paths, and not both at full strength. Read with a relational definition of tacitness, that single trade-off gives each classical position its place: Polanyi’s non-separation, the pianist who falters under focal scrutiny, Tsoukas’s ineffability at the object level, Nonaka’s externalization by new operations, and Collins’s three kinds as three strata, the last of which lives in the community rather than in the substrate. The same reading changes what our earlier results are about. The mentor–student DAG is a record of tacit transmission, the Leibnizian hourglass is institutionalized socialization, and the Monastery Wall is a change in the medium through which tacit knowledge travelled. Newton’s thin thread, Thurston’s emptied field and Tao’s AI-solved problems without seminars share one structure: possession without transmission. AI-generated results are rootless by default. They can be verified, but nobody yet holds them tacitly, and their derivations leave no walkable trace. We have proposed that VaCoAl/PyVaCoAl can supply the missing middle layer between generation and valuation—an exact trace with its confidence—and stated predictions by which that proposal can fail. Understanding a result and judging its worth remain with people: as virtue epistemology explains, the intellectual virtues they call on cannot be offloaded to technology. People will always know more than they can state. A substrate that records how a result was reached gives them something they have rarely had: a way to show the route, which is where tacit knowledge has always begun to travel.

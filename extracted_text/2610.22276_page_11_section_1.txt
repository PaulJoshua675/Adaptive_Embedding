MJev-Spatial investigates a System One approach to spatial understanding: using pretrained multimodal knowledge to make direct spatial decisions without generating answer text. Options, numerical values, and image locations share a candidate-classification mechanism, with hierarchical refinement providing numerical and spatial precision and tree expansion extending point prediction without further training. An audited 12.3M-QA corpus and joint stage supervision support training across these tasks; length grouping and cross- GPU balancing improve throughput by 30.8% in a controlled eight-GPU study. The final Qwen3.5-4B-based model achieves the highest overall score, leads seven of twelve reported metrics among eight evaluated models, and exceeds its local autoregressive base model on all twelve. Its mean batch-one query latency is 160.6 ms across the twelve benchmarks, giving 2.5× and 5.6× speedups over the fastest autoregressive baseline and the base model under the reported timing protocol. Together with the controlled design studies, these results support direct candidate decisions as a viable approach to the spatial tasks studied here. 11

Isolating the Determinant Structure. Holding S, σ, the fidelity term, and the optimizer fixed, we vary only the batch aggregator among InfoNCE (CNO), the sum-of-pairwise RBF P i<j Sij, and our log-determinant (Tab. 3), isolat- ing the determinant structure from the inherited pipeline. The log-determinant attains the best intra-batch diversity, and both RBF-based aggregators outper- form InfoNCE. Because the pairwise-RBF and log-determinant rows differ only in aggregating S additively versus through its determinant, the comparison iso- lates the aggregation structure.

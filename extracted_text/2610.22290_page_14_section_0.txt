A.4 Training with elite replay Each training step pairs a batch of on-policy GRPO rollouts with a separate batch of verified elites for the replay loss. We sample the latter across ques- tions and occupied cells so that questions with more archived solutions do not dominate. The replay loss averages each solution’s negative log- likelihood over its tokens, then averages over so- lutions. Inspired by CHORD (Zhang et al., 2026), we combine this term with GRPO using a fixed weight µ rather than a scheduled weight or addi- tional per-token reweighting. The archive remains fixed throughout training. B Experimental Details This section describes our instantiation of ELITEREPLAY for text-to-SQL, followed by the training and evaluation settings used in all experi- ments. B.1 Archive construction for text-to-SQL Questions and verification. We train on 5,000 BIRD questions and construct the archive us- ing 1,000 of them, sampled proportionally across databases and difficulty levels. These questions remain in the RL training pool. All candidates come from the frozen Qwen3.5-4B-Base model, prompted with the question, database schema, and BIRD evidence.

This article introduced EneMonIE-Backend, an exe- cutable backend reinterpretation of the EneMonIE energy- monitoring architecture. The design retains the original semantic objectives while making acquisition, normaliza- tion, relevance filtering, extraction, interlinking, fusion, persistence, and question answering operational. Its five- layer decomposition is complemented by three evidence- grounded QA paths that reuse the same semantic pipeline. The deliberate exclusion of user-interface and server-side application components creates a reusable core that can support multiple future applications. The implementation demonstrates architectural feasibil- ity and passes a deterministic integration smoke test. It does not yet establish large-scale extraction or question- answering performance. The next scientific step is there- fore a component-wise and end-to-end evaluation on la- beled, multilingual energy corpora, followed by targeted replacement of rule-based modules where measured limi- tations justify greater model complexity. Reproducibility Statement The accompanying Overleaf project contains this manuscript, the architecture figure, and the BibTeX database. The described validation is deterministic and does not require an external language model. Live Web acquisition remains dependent on third-party source avail- ability and should be evaluated with recorded URLs and retrieval timestamps.

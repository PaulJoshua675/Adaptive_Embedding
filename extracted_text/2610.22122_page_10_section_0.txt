Its mean final discovery counts are lower than full TDAL on all four tasks, supporting learning the acquisition function alongside surrogate updates. w/o successor retains 𝑓𝜃but sets 𝑤𝑡= 0, fitting the residual 𝑅𝑡−b𝑅𝑡(𝐴𝑡) between observed and surrogate estimated discoveries. Its mean final discovery count exceeds Surrogate only on all four tasks, showing that current feedback alone can train a dynamic acquisition function. Full TDAL adds further gains on every task, indicating that incorporating estimated future discovery value improves selection beyond learning from current feedback alone. Training and scoring design. Fixing 𝑤𝑡= 1 lowers mean final discovery counts below both full TDAL and w/o successor on all four tasks. Undiscounted successor value can therefore impair selection, supporting adjustment of its weight to experimental feedback. Removing LBO also lowers mean final discoveries on every task, supporting the use of additional reconstructed transitions to train 𝑄𝜃when real experimental feedback is sparse. w/o anchor removes the explicit ˆ𝑝𝑡(𝑥) from the score while retaining surrogate predictions in the inputs. Its final mean decreases on the two 60-round single perturbation tasks but increases slightly on the two 200-round tasks. With more rounds of online training, the acquisition function maintains performance without the explicit probability baseline. Detailed ablation settings are provided in Appendix F.

The gradient-law verification in Appendix G.3 confirmed SwiGLU’s exact V–O symmetry with the nonlinear gate held fixed. SwiGLU is used in modern Transformer architectures, motivating a test of this activation. We vary the rescaling strength for SwiGLU, following the ReLU comparison in the deep-model stress test. Setup. We compare two SwiGLU models with d = 256 and dff = 1024: a 12-layer model with 25.5M parameters and a 48-layer model with 63.3M parameters. Rescaling acts on the MLP value–output pair, with λ ∈{0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3} for the smaller model and λ ∈{0, 0.2} for the deeper model. Each configuration uses 30,000 WikiText-103 training steps and a single seed. The MLP-only runs use learning rate 6 × 10−4 at L = 12 and 3 × 10−4 at L = 48, context 256, batch 64, and 2K-step warmup. The table reports best validation PPL; pair imbalance and gradient slope use the last logged state at step 29.5K. Results. Table 19 reports the results. Deep model, L = 48. At λ = 0.2, where c47 = e0.2×47 ≈1.2 × 104, Vanilla PPL degrades severely to 63.24 with an MLP log-ratio imbalance of 8.70 and gradient slope 0.212, while SAGGS+P maintains 28.46 PPL with pair imbalance below 10−4 and slope 0.0002. Its PPL is close to the λ = 0 baseline of 28.48, recovering nearly all of the degradation caused by the imposed rescaling. SAGGS+P at λ = 0 achieves 28.42, compared with the Vanilla baseline of 28.48, a 0.06 PPL difference in this single-seed protocol. Small model, L = 12. The lambda sweep reveals a smooth degradation curve: Vanilla PPL increases from 31.79 at λ = 0 to 31.98 at λ = 0.1 to 32.48 at λ = 0.2 to 33.37 at λ = 0.3, with final logged MLP imbalance growing from 0.061 to 0.193 to 0.718 to 1.694 across the four tilts. SAGGS+P consistently maintains baseline performance across all λ values: 31.80 at λ = 0.1, 31.77 at λ = 0.2, and 31.85 at λ = 0.3, all with pair imbalance below 10−4. At λ = 0.3, the increase above the unperturbed Vanilla baseline of 31.79 is +1.58 PPL for Vanilla, which reaches 33.37, and +0.06 for SAGGS+P. The +34.78 PPL gap between Vanilla and SAGGS+P at λ = 0.2 in the deep model shows substantial sensitivity to the exact SwiGLU value–output reparameterization in this training recipe.

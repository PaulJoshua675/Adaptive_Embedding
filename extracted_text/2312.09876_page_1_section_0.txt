Automatic Image Colourizer Aditya N Parikh Btech -Entc Vit-Pune Abstract— In this project we have designed and described a model which colourize a gray-scale image, with no human intervention. We propose a fully automatic process of colouring and re-colouring faded or gray-scale image with vibrant and pragmatic colours. We have used Convolutional Neural Network to hallucinate input images and feed-forwarded by training thousands of images. This approach results in trailblazing results.

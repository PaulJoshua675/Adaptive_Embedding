B FINE-TUNING RECIPE Every model is fine-tuned with the same recipe, on all of its parameters. A relation has 4|T| training records, one per fine-tuning subject and template pair. A record is the prompt followed by a space and the label, and the loss is the cross-entropy at the label’s first token only. The optimizer is AdamW with β = (0.9, 0.95), ϵ = 10−8 and no weight decay, and gradients are clipped to norm 1.0. The learning rate warms up linearly to 10−5 over the first 10% of steps and then decays along a cosine to 10−6. Each step averages the gradient over 16 records. We run micro-batches of 4 records per device (2 for Gemma-4-E2B and Qwen3.8-27B) with gradient accumulation, and shard models that need more than one GPU with fully sharded data parallelism, on up to eight GPUs. Training runs for 20 epochs, each using every record once in a fresh order, so every fine-tuning fact is seen 80 times, 20 in each wording. Forward and backward passes run in bfloat16; the parameters and the AdamW moments are kept in float32. Generalization is read after the last epoch. Subject roles, the label subset L and the folds use one fixed seed in every run; the bijection is keyed by relation and repetition, and the optimizer seed, which also sets the record order, equals the repetition index.

Emergent Multi-Hop Retrieval (HotpotQA): In the HotpotQA domain, the market acts as an evidence-gathering society. The environment consists of 10 fact extractors (each assigned 1 of the 10 candidate paragraphs) and 3 answerer agents differentiated by reasoning style via distinct system prompts (concise, chain-of-thought, and cautious). A centralized auctioneer assessor evaluates the accumulated facts against the question at each step, assigning discrete relevance scores (0-10) to each agent based on missing multi-hop dependencies. The market dynamics organically solve the multi-hop retrieval problem through learned valuation schedules conditioned on these assessor signals. When the state lacks intermediate evidence, extractors whose paragraphs contain the necessary bridging facts receive high assessor scores and outbid others to append their facts. As evidence accumulates, the answerer agents monitor the state. Once an answerer determines that sufficient evidence has been gathered to derive the final answer (and claim the terminal reward), its valuation spikes. It outbids the extractors, terminating the gathering chain and passing revenue backward through the preceding transactions. In HotpotQA, the market successfully learns to identify the two supporting gold paragraphs among eight distractors and route extracted facts to an answerer agent, achieving an 85.0 ± 3.2% success rate over the final 100 episodes (fig. 4). In comparison, a centralized ReAct baseline (Yao et al., 2023) with upfront access to all ten paragraph titles over the same 6-step horizon achieves 84.8 ± 1.1% with Gemini-3.5-flash-lite at 0.042 ± 0.025 ¢/task and 91.2 ± 0.8% with Gemini-3.1-pro-preview at 1.677 ± 1.281 ¢/task. Operating exclusively with informationally isolated Gemini-3.5-flash-lite agents (where each extractor observes only its single assigned paragraph), the market matches centralized Gemini-3.5-flash-lite ReAct accuracy (85.0% vs. 84.8%) and reaches 93% of Gemini-3.1-pro-preview’s absolute performance at an agent inference cost of just 0.019 ± 0.011 ¢/task (0.344¢ including the market assessor)—an 86.9× agent cost reduction relative to Gemini-3.1-pro-preview (4.9× total) and a 2.2× agent cost reduction relative to Gemini-3.5-flash-lite ReAct.

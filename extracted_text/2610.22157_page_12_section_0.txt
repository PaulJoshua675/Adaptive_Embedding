MRRMSE is only computed in regions of the domain where ui̸ = 0. 3 Results 3.1 1D Linear Advection We first compare the PI-DeepONet architectures on the 1D linear advection problem. Ten separate Vanilla and ATenH PI-DeepONets are trained over 50,000 epochs to solve this problem in order to report means and assess sensitivity to random parameter initialisation. The solutions produced by a Vanilla and an ATenH PI-DeepONet for a representative test input function are shown in Figure 4. Table 3 reports the mean training time and MRRMSE for the 1D linear advection problem. The two architectures exhibit comparable predictive accuracy, with closely matching MRRMSE values and similar standard deviations. The ATenH architecture reduces the mean training time by 12.1% relative to the Vanilla architecture, corresponding to a 1.14× speed-up.

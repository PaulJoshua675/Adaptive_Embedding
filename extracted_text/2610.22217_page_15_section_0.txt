Preprint. A APPENDIX A.1 DATASET DETAILS We evaluate models on eight benchmarks spanning four complementary capability groups. Fine- grained visual perception is assessed using CountBenchQA (Paiss et al., 2023) and PixMo- Count (Deitke et al., 2025). OCR and chart understanding are evaluated on the recognition track of CC-OCR V2 (Xu et al., 2026c) and ChartQA (Masry et al., 2022). Symbolic visual reasoning is evaluated on VisualPuzzles (Song et al., 2025) and We-Math (Qiao et al., 2025). Multi-image reasoning is evaluated on MUIRBench (Wang et al., 2025a) and MMSI-Bench (Yang et al., 2025). Table 3 summarizes the evaluation scale and primary capability of each benchmark.

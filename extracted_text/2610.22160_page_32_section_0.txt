Second, every context produced by the optimizer is passed through a regex filter ( ANSWER LEAK PATTERNS in optimizer.py) that rejects rewrites containing concrete answers—e.g. “the answer is X” patterns, contrastive “the answer is X, not Y” constructions, or specific numbers/letters—in which case the previous context is retained.

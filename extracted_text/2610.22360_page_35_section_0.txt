H.6 Representative Failure Cases of GPT-6 Astra Figures 1 and 5 pair selected model responses with human-reviewed contradictions. These cases illustrate factual-value, target-binding, geometric, and motion errors. Part presence. Astra describes a rounded, arched suitcase handle even though the reviewed asset lacks a handle. The claimed component is absent rather than merely shaped differently. Region binding. The highlighted furniture region is the support box beneath the cushion. Astra instead describes the padded seating surface, binding a plausible property to the wrong part. Grasp contacts. Astra describes opposing contacts, whereas the displayed jaws lie on adjacent sides and the source label marks the grasp as invalid. Reference geometry. Astra accepts the candidate’s main geometry despite its fuller, outward- bowed middle, rounder cross-section, and wider side silhouette. References and rendered views localize the structural mismatch. Layer count. Astra repeats the claim’s six internal cake layers; the reviewed reference identifies seven. Correctly describing the topping does not resolve the count error. Direct contact. Astra describes the cushion as resting directly against the marked backrest, while the reviewed part pair does not directly touch. Material appearance. Astra recognizes beige mottling but calls the surface stone-like. The reviewed contradiction is its reflective, metallic appearance. Affordance. Astra accepts pressing for the highlighted camera dial, although human review confirms that this region does not support pressing. Motion naturalness and deformation.

A DATASET A.1 DATA FORMAT In this Section we explain different aspects of the data curation process, including document acqui- sition, formats etc. Idea Format Each idea the model generates is a JSON object in the four-field schema of Section 3. This includes: (problem gap, core claim, method, inspiration). The definitions of the four fields are given below (as per the inference prompt): • problem gap: The gap the idea addresses, drawn from across the source documents – an open question, tension, or unresolved difficulty that connects them and that none resolves on its own. State it as one clear gap, NOT as your specific solution to it. BEGIN this field with the single best-fitting OPPORTUNITY PATTERN label in square brackets (from the taxonomy below), then the prose gap (1 to 2 sentences). Pick the ONE dominant pattern even if several apply; if NONE genuinely fits, use “[Other]” rather than forcing a poor match. Example: “[Bridge Opportunity] Work on X and on Y each..but neither connects..”. • core claim: The couple of sentences the whole idea lives or dies on – “we claim that X.” It must be something that could turn out to be wrong: a definite, testable assertion / hypothesis / insight, not a topic or a direction. Do not just restate the gap, and do not describe what you would do about it. From these sentences alone a reader should know exactly what the idea is betting on (1-2 sentences). • method: At a high level, how the claim would be turned into research – the kind of move (combining ideas, extending to a new setting, deriving formally, building an artifact, etc.) and how it addresses the gap. A proposed approach, not a step-by-step protocol. Begins with one bracketed Method Paradigm label. • inspiration: Three to five sentences arguing how the idea arises FROM the source docu- ments – the relationship between them, not a per-document restatement. Must (1) name each cited source’s limitation and (2) justify why connecting them is a non-obvious leap. Refers to sources by their numbered ‘Document N’ label. problem gap and method are each required to open with one bracketed taxonomy label (an oppor- tunity pattern and a method paradigm, respectively) drawn from the fixed 7+7 taxonomy given in full in the ideation prompt (Chen et al., 2026) (Appendix G). The example idea card in Appendix G shows how an idea card is structured. A.2 DATA CURATION: GATHERING DOCUMENTS We describe the dataset construction in detail in this section. As mentioned in Section 3.2, our dataset curation has two steps: (a) gathering the documents (b) putting together the SFT dataset. In this subsection we discuss the gathering documents. Each instance in our dataset has a target or gold idea which is derived from a published paper. For each gold idea, we also store a subset of its references that we consider as “core” to its generation. These references are inputs to our proposed method.

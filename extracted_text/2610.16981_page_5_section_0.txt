The weighting scheme is defined as follows. Within-transaction weighting. Within a transaction, we favor paths that follow asset transfers or exchanges, while using teleportation only as a fallback when no direct path exists. – Transfer edges are assigned a low cost of 0.1, as directly observed token movements. – Swap edges receive an even lower cost of 0.01 to encourage inferred conver- sions between received and spent assets. – Teleportation edges are assigned a substantially higher cost of 100 as they represent connections without an explicit transfer or an identifiable swap. Furthermore, an inter-transaction edge directly connecting the borrowing node to the ego-account state in a repayment transaction is assigned a cost of 100 to prevent the path from bypassing the intermediate fund movements under investigation.

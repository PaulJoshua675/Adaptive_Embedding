Thus, the second stage builds on the supervised initialization to improve social interpretation both within and beyond the training domain. 4.3 Reasoning Eﬀiciency We compare average weighted F1 and reasoning token use against base Qwen in Figure 4 to ex- amine how training affects both performance and efficiency. Reasoning SFT substantially shortens traces, but its out-of-domain F1 remains below the base model. Accuracy-Only RL raises F1 while further reducing token use. From the same super- vised initialization, CLUE achieves higher F1 with fewer reasoning tokens than Accuracy-Only RL in both domains. The second stage therefore builds on the efficiency gained during initialization, im- proving task performance while further reducing reasoning token use.

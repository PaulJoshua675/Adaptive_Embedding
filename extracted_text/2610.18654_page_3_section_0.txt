The approach also retains near-parent retrieval performance on a 4B GQA backbone, supporting transfer beyond the 1.9B MHA setting. Our contributions are threefold: • We propose C-KSA, which compresses Summary KV at storage time into layer-local Meta entries, so that further compression of distant history no longer requires coarser Summaries. • We propose S-KSA, which turns existing Summaries into block-level routing keys for sparse attention over selected original-text blocks. • We provide a retrofit recipe and GPU kernels that convert a pretrained Hybrid KSA model with RULER performance comparable to its parent, and we report where the remaining cost lies and where the approach fails.

We propose HASTE, a unified fine-tuning framework that improves semantic-ID-based generative recommendation through two com- plementary forms of supervision. Given a user interaction context, the model autoregressively generates an item’s semantic ID as a sequence of discrete codes obtained through residual quantiza- tion. While standard causal cross-entropy provides supervision for predicting these codes, it treats the semantic-ID sequence uni- formly and does not explicitly account for its hierarchical structure. Moreover, item-level attributes provide complementary semantic information that can be particularly valuable for learning represen- tations of data-sparse tail items. Figure 1 provides an overview of the proposed training framework. HASTE introduces two auxiliary objectives to address these lim- itations. The first is a Hierarchical Loss, which explicitly exploits the multi-level structure of semantic IDs and provides level-aware supervision during semantic-ID generation.

Preprint On Qwen3.6-27B, the release-date order does not hold. The two Magpie corpora generated by 2024 models fall far below parity, to 3.6 (Magpie-Llama-3.3) and 12.8 (Magpie-Qwen2.5). Both are also below UltraChat-200k (28.7), the oldest corpus. Only the on-policy Magpie-Qwen3.6 stays at parity with 51.8. How the on-policy corpus is sampled also matters: the coding-heavy version, generated with a single system prompt that led to about 88% coding prompts, reaches 42.2. Overall, on-policy data keeps both models at parity (51.3 and 51.8), while external corpora range from 3.6 to 85.3 depending on the model being trained. Data from a stronger model can do better, as Magpie-Qwen3.6 does for Llama-3.3-70B. But such data does not exist for every model, whereas on-policy data can be generated for any model we train. 21

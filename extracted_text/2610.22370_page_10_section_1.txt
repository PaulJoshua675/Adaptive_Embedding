We presented DaTMe, a diffusion-dynamics-aware token merging framework for efficient image generation. By leveraging the Tweedie denoised estimate, DaTMe defines token importance along two complementary axes—temporal evolution and structural saliency—that reflect the image generation process. This allows DaTMe to identify safely mergeable tokens while preserving structurally critical regions, achieving strong image fidelity and favorable quality-efficiency trade-offs across DiT-based diffusion models. Our work shows that grounding token merging in the underlying generation dynamics is a key step toward faithful and efficient diffusion inference.

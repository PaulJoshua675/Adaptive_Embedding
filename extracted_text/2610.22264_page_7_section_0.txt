SCOPE achieves the highest overall GenEval score across all three frozen backbones (Table 1), boosting overall performance over vanilla GRPO from 0.91 to 0.95 on Qwen-Image (+4.4% relative), 0.74 to 0.84 on SD3 (+13.5% relative), and 0.76 to 0.84 on FLUX.1-dev (+10.5% relative). Across the three backbones, SCOPE achieves strict improvements over GRPO in 17 out of 18 category benchmarks while maintaining near-saturated color accuracy on Qwen-Image (0.93), trading a marginal 0.01 fluctuation for substantial gains in complex spatial positioning (+0.08) and attribute binding (+0.08). Moreover, the most pronounced improvements appear in object counting and spatial positioning, two persistent bottlenecks for frozen T2I generators. On SD3, SCOPE substantially outperforms GRPO in both Counting (0.58 →0.79, +36.2%) and Position (0.57 →0.83, +45.6%). On FLUX.1-dev, SCOPE lifts Counting by +0.11 and raises Position accuracy from the unadapted baseline of 0.16 and GRPO’s 0.62 to 0.84, representing a more than 5× gain over the base generator. These improvements also extend to multi-object synthesis and attribute binding, achieving 0.99 two-object accuracy and 0.88 attribute binding accuracy on Qwen-Image. T2I-CompBench. SCOPE exceeds GRPO in all six reported categories on each frozen backbone (Table 2). Average scores improve by 6.7%, 8.0%, and 8.2% on Qwen-Image, SD3, and FLUX.1-dev, respectively. The gains cover appearance attributes, spatial relations, numeracy, and complex composition. Notably, on strong backbones like Qwen-Image, uniform token-level RL (e.g., GRPO and RePrompt) suffers from compositional degradation, falling below the unadapted baseline in overall T2I-CompBench score (0.6247 and 0.6246 vs. 0.6280) as well as Texture, Numeracy, and Complex composition, as uniform token updates inadvertently distort fine-grained descriptive phrases during exploration. SCOPE completely resolves this negative transfer, reaching 0.6665 overall and surpassing both the unadapted baseline and GRPO across all individual categories. Beyond achieving the highest color, shape, and texture scores on SD3 and FLUX.1-dev, SCOPE substantially improves spatial relations and numeracy over GRPO on both backbones (+0.0815 and +0.0686 on SD3; +0.0491 and +0.0658 on FLUX.1-dev). In Complex composition, it lifts Qwen-Image from 0.3921 to 0.4473 with consistent gains on SD3 and FLUX.1-dev, demonstrating robust, precise, and fine-grained control over both individual attributes and multi-constraint prompts. 4.3 Ablation and Mechanistic Analysis Effect of credit weighting.

Neural Architecture Search (NAS) [9] has become an important technique for au- tomatically designing deep neural network architectures. It has achieved strong results in computer vision and natural language processing and provides a scal- able way to adapt architectures to different tasks, datasets, and deployment constraints. However, conventional NAS methods usually require training or par- tially training a large number of candidate architectures, which leads to substan- tial computational overhead. Therefore, accurate performance estimation under extremely limited evaluation budgets has become a central challenge for efficient NAS [30]. arXiv:2610.15307v1 [cs.LG] 11 Oct 2026

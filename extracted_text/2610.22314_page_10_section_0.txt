GPT-family models are reported with a † due to potential self- preference from using a judge of the same model family (Pombal et al., 2026). 5 Benchmarking Systems with MindEval2 5.1 Experimental Setup Patients. We select 50 diverse patient profiles for our benchmark, all of which are simulated by the same model, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (DeepSeek-AI, 2026) using NeMo User Sim as the simulation harness.6 For reproducibility, each patient’s session gaps, between-session event categories and concealment schedule are drawn once and held fixed across counsellor LMs. The conversation transcripts, running memories and concrete later-session scenarios depend on the counsellor through the preceding dialogue. Counsellors. We benchmark a suite of 18 state-of-the-art proprietary and open-weight models of dif- ferent families and sizes, namely: GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra and Luna (OpenAI, 2026a), Claude Sonnet 5 and Opus 5 (Anthropic, 2026d,a) and Gemini 3.8 Flash (Google DeepMind, 2026) (proprietary); and Kimi K3 (Kimi Team, 2026), GLM-5.3 (Z.ai, 2026), Qwen3.8 27B (Qwen Team, 2026b), Qwen3.5 4B, 9B, 27B, 122B-A10B and 397B-A17B (Qwen Team, 2026a), Gemma 4 12B, 26B-A4B and 31B (Gemma Team, 2026), and Muse Glimmer 30B (Meta Superintelligence Labs, 2026). Each received the same system prompt (Figure 13), consisting of a short brief for a warm, concise counsellor in a mobile chat app, and a mem- ory section. In the first session, the memory section says there have been no earlier sessions. From the second session on, it holds the running log of the earlier ones.

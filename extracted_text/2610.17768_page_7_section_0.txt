Exploratory, after the look. 120 of the 131 test pages come from domains that also hold a fresh training page. On those, the 3B and 27B reader-only models lead the August model by +0.0496 [+0.0020, +0.1377] and +0.0445 [+0.0066, +0.1058]; on the other 11 pages, from 11 domains (10 of them absent from the 819 training pages too), they trail it by −0.0545 [−0.1095, −0.0148] and −0.0362 [−0.0804, −0.0028] (95% intervals). On the development set the 3B’s and the 27B’s differences from the August model are +0.0103 [−0.0241, +0.0653] and +0.0337 [−0.0010, +0.0823] on the 125 pages of such domains, and −0.0150 [−0.0342, −0.0002] and +0.0062 [−0.0162, +0.0236] on the other 90. Eleven pages cannot settle it, but the gain of the new models may be coverage of the domains a platform’s readers keep reading rather than a better general ranker. 6 Discussion Frontier models rank sentences by what a model finds important, and readers converge on something else [11]. A newer generation narrows that gap only a little: the best single model of October 2026 stays 0.060 AP below a 3B model trained on readers, and fusing seven of them still leaves 0.042. The trained model’s advantage comes from the readers, and the parts of its recipe that rely on a frontier model matter less than they seemed. A teacher that breaks ties helps a model trained on few reader pages, but a stronger one added little once a teacher was there, and with 3,160 more reader pages a model trained from its base model on readers alone reaches the distilled model’s point estimate. For a platform this is the useful direction: the improvement comes from data it already collects, needs no model provider’s outputs in training, and stays with a small model (Table 3). The larger backbone is the open question. On the development set it adds as much as doubling the pages did; on fresh pages its advantage could not be confirmed, and the exploratory split points at domain coverage as a common source of the new models’ gains there. A test on pages from domains that no training page covers would separate a better ranker from a familiar one. Limits. One platform, public pages whose text could be fetched, and Latin-script pages only in the fresh training and test sets. The test set is small, was read as a description, and tests the frozen candidates, not the development-set comparisons behind them. The stronger teacher replaced only the tie-breaker: the distilled start kept the earlier teacher, so distilling anew from the stronger one, or giving the fresh pages to the distilled model, was not tested. The zero-shot models are specific versions at specific settings, which date quickly; a reader-prediction instruction lowered the two we tried, but other prompts, examples or fine-tuning might narrow the gap.

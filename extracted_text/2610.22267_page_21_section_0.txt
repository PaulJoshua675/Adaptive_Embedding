This validates our single-step optimization strategy: diversifying the Tweedie prediction at t=T is sufficient to produce diverse final images without per-step intervention. A.6 Effect of Kernel Bandwidth σ The RBF kernel bandwidth σ controls the sensitivity of the Gram matrix to inter-sample distances in the pooled Tweedie prediction space. We compare five configurations: two fixed small values (σ ∈{0.01, 0.5}), a fixed moderate value (σ = 1.0), a dynamic median updated at every optimization step, and our default choice of a fixed median heuristic computed once from the reference predictions (Eq. 5).

To further dissect our two-stage GRPO design, we conduct additional ablations. Introducing attribution reward Rattr as early as the first round not only compromises general performance but also degrades event- centric alignment. We hypothesize that injecting both Rsync and Rattr prematurely leads to optimization conflicts between inter-event relations and intra-event accuracy. Conversely, a progressive strategy facilitates a gradual alignment process, from global coarse-grained structures to fine-grained character-level details. Moreover, comparisons against a baseline that sustains only Rcomp and Rsync in the second round demonstrate that the sustained improvements stem from effective reward design rather than mere data scaling. 4.4 Visualizations We present a caption generated by EVA-Cap in Fig. 5 for comparison with AVoCaDO [4], highlighting the superior event alignment capabilities (i.e., audiovisual synchronization and event attribution) of our model.

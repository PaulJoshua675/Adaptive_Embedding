A risk factor qualifies when the study reports a statistical measure of its association with human Lassa infection, severe disease, or death. Naming or discussing a possible factor without a statistical measure is not enough. For animal-only studies, qualifying transmission parameters are R0, Rt, Re, r, growth rate, and mutation rate; qualifying vector parameters include extrinsic incubation period, vector reproduction number, vector competence, and mosquito delays.

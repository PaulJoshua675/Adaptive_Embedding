For a fair and rigorous comparison, the semantic inputs for all feature-driven baselines are standardized to the identical 64-dimensional vectors extracted from item titles and categories. Pure ID-based Collaborative Filtering • MF [29]: As the foundational baseline for collaborative filtering, Matrix Factorization projects users and items into low-dimensional latent vectors. It relies exclusively on direct interaction histories and is optimized via the Bayesian Personalized Ranking objective. • NGCF [12]: This graph-based model enriches representation learning by executing em- bedding propagation over the bipartite interaction graph. Such a mechanism explicitly captures the high-order connectivity inherent in the topological structure. • LightGCN [13]: By innovatively stripping away the redundant non-linear activations and feature transformations prevalent in traditional graph convolutional networks, this architecture drastically enhances both training efficiency and generalization capability. It currently stands as the state-of-the-art benchmark for pure identity-driven graph collabo- rative filtering. Feature-based and Cold-start Models • DeepMusic [17]: This approach employs deep neural networks to extract heterogeneous modal features, such as textual and acoustic attributes, from side metadata. These extracted features are subsequently mapped into the collaborative latent space to mitigate the cold- start dilemma. • DropoutNet [18]: Designed specifically for cold-start scenarios, this framework applies a targeted dropout mechanism to user and item identity embeddings during the training phase. This deliberate perturbation forces the model to rely on content features for preference prediction under conditions of severe interaction sparsity. • MetaEmbedding [19]: Operating as a meta-learning paradigm for cold-start recom- mendation, this method optimizes an embedding generator. It leverages the associated side information alongside limited interaction records of novel items to synthesize highly expressive initial identity representations. 18

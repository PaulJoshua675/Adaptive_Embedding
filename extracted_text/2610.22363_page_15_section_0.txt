The deterministic action autoencoder represents each frame with three 64-dimensional tokens: camera, left hand, and right hand. A shared hand encoder takes the 21-joint observation for each hand, and a separate camera encoder takes rotation, translation, and field of view. Encoders and decoders use residual MLPs of width 512 with three residual blocks. Training minimizes masked reconstruction losses for joints, image coordinates, depth, visibility, and camera states. We use AdamW with learning rate 10−3 and a global batch of 128. Codec training takes approximately 110 H100 GPU-hours. The codec is frozen during video–action training. Table 6 reports held-out reconstruction on EgoDex, ARCTIC, HOT3D, and EgoLive, matching the main reporting datasets. The multi-domain codec achieves camera-space joint errors of 0.868–2.600 mm and image-space errors of 0.323–0.882 pixels.

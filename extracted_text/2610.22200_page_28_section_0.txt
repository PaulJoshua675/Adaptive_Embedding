A Evaluation Details Metrics and Aggregation. We evaluate the 82-task offline subset of OSWorld-v2 v2.1. Task score is the mean evaluator partial score, expressed on a 0–100 scale. Full-task success is the percentage of tasks with a normalized evaluator score greater than 0.999999. Steps and token counts are averaged over all selected tasks, including failures. Within each run, we retain the latest attempt with an evaluator result for each task, not the highest-scoring attempt. Cross-model summaries give each model equal weight: score gains are averaged in points, and relative resource reductions are calculated within each model before averaging. The offline subset is defined by evaluation_examples/test_v2_offline_no_internet.json.

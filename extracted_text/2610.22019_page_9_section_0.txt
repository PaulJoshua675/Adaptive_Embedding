Pretraining ends with a small, decayed learning rate, and midtraining needs a higher one to learn the new data. In our early runs, midtraining at 1e-5 gave smaller gains after SFT than higher rates did. Raising the rate too far, however, hurts general capabilities even with replay. Table 11 compares a constant 1e-5 with warming up to 3e-5 or 5e-5. Both higher rates lower general capabilities, most clearly MATH-500, which falls from 79.7 to 61.7 at 5e-5. We therefore recommend a moderate increase and checking general-capability benchmarks while tuning it. 6

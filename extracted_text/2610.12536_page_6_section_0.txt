The completion rate describes the combined pro- cedure and does not isolate the repair loop’s contribution. 5 Discussion and Conclusion The rising candidate-score medians show progress beyond retaining a single strong initial solution. Newly generated populations also shift toward higher returns. In four tasks, every final selected winner improved over its first- generation counterpart on the fixed held-out episode panel. These endpoint gains do not by themselves rule out overfitting to the selection panel. Acrobot-v1 exposes a limitation: search scores rose while the median paired held-out change was negative. Further examination is needed to identify the cause. Held- out evaluation covers each generation’s selected winner, not every retained elite, so it does not describe the full elite population. Search used ten episode seeds, compared with 100 held-out seeds. Future studies could test whether broader selection panels reduce specialization; the current design does not isolate that effect. BipedalWalker-v3 and CarRacing-v3 improved with- out reaching their reporting targets. The tested budget does not establish whether additional generations would eventually solve these tasks. Their gains should therefore be distinguished from task attainment. 5.1 Program search and neural-policy training EliteTable revises policy source code rather than up- dating neural-policy weights through policy gradients. Source edits can change numerical constants as well as program structure. Policy-gradient methods instead up- date parameterized policies, often neural networks, using estimates of the gradient of expected return with respect to policy parameters [8]. The selected tasks are drawn from Gymnasium’s stan- dard reinforcement-learning environments [7]. Matched comparisons are needed to assess the relative strengths of program search and neural-policy training on these tasks. Source revisions can alter control structures, numeri- cal constants, state, and execution flow. Programmatic reinforcement learning can also combine program search with guidance from a trained neural policy [9]. These are different update mechanisms; the present study provides no matched comparison of their learning efficiency. 6

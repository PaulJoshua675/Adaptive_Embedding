Our students are Qwen3-VL-2B/4B- Instruct (Bai et al., 2025), which share the teacher’s vocabulary. Before distillation, we cold-start the student agent via SFT on 10K trajectories sampled from SearchVL-SFT-36k to equip the student with basic agentic search capability. We then perform OPD training using 4K difficulty-stratified samples sourced from Search-VL-RL-8K (Chen et al., 2026), VDR-Bench-2K (Zeng et al., 2026), and FVQA train split (Wang et al., 2017). Notably, both training and evaluation are conducted in a live web environment, with search actions served by the Serper and Jina APIs. All training runs are executed on 8×NVIDIA H100 GPUs. Unless stated otherwise, IG-OPD uses λ = 0.1, αmax = 10, and a K = 60-step cosine schedule. Appendix B.3 provides full training and implementation details. 5.2 CAN IG-OPD TRANSFER DEEP-SEARCH CAPABILITY TO COMPACT STUDENTS? Table 1 reports results on all six benchmarks for both student scales. IG-OPD achieves the best average at both scales and lets a 4B student rival its 8B teacher. Under the same cold-start model and training sources, IG-OPD improves average accuracy over vanilla OPD by 3.4 points at 2B (49.4 vs. 46.0) and by 1.7 points at 4B (55.0 vs. 53.3), ranking first on five of the six benchmarks in both student groups. Notably, the 4B student surpasses the 8B teacher on MMSearch (66.7 vs. 64.3) and BrowseComp-VL (45.7 vs. 44.7), effectively matching the teacher’s capability overall with half the scale. Although the teacher provides dense supervision, IQC calibrates query-level signals using evidence actually retrieved by the student rather than relying solely on the teacher’s prior preference for query wording. The student effectively breaks the OPD ceiling, allowing the student to outperform its teachers. Both IG-OPD students also outperform all direct-reasoning frontier models (e.g., 49.4 for our 2B student vs. 46.9 for Gemini-2.5-Pro), confirming that these benchmarks genuinely require active retrieval rather than parametric recall. Gains concentrate on search-intensive benchmarks where EBS is most consequential. Com- pared with OPD, IG-OPD achieves the largest gains on search-intensive benchmarks: MMSearch (+6.3 at 2B / +4.7 at 4B), BrowseComp-VL (+4.3 / +2.0), LiveVQA (+4.3 / +1.3), and MMSearch+ (+3.4 / +1.4).

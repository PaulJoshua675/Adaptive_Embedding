C TRAINING DETAILS C.1 SFT COLD START We initialize AVC-Reward from Qwen3-Omni-Thinking and perform full-parameter fine-tuning us- ing the 8k structured annotation samples described in Sec. B.2. For each sample, the training tar- get contains the complete five-stage reasoning sequence, followed by the three consistency scores (Smac, Stemp, Sattr) and the diagnostic conclusion. We train the model with the ms-swift framework for one epoch. The complete SFT configuration is reported in Table 11. C.2 GRPO FOR HUMAN ALIGNMENT Starting from the SFT checkpoint, we further align AVC-Reward with the human-calibrated scores using GRPO. Training is implemented with ms-swift on the 10k rollout subset constructed from the human-calibration data. The reward combines a structure reward rstruct, which enforces the required reasoning and output format, with a score-distillation reward rdistill, which encourages the three predicted scores to match their human-calibrated references. Structure reward. The structure reward rstruct ∈[0, 1] checks whether the model follows the required five-stage reasoning and output format. Its four components are summarized in Table 12. The five-stage markers are: • step 1 blind audio analysis • step 2 deaf video analysis • step 3 auditory visual verification • step 4 visual auditory verification • step 5 world construction verdict We additionally use rstruct as a format gate: if rstruct < 0.4, we set rdistill = 0 and truncate the total reward to rtotal = 0.5 rstruct. This prevents malformed outputs from receiving a high reward solely from numerically accurate final scores. 21

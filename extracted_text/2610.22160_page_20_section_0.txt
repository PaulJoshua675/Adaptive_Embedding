MMLU-Pro (stratified subset). The full MMLU-Pro test set contains 12,032 questions across 14 subject categories with up to 10 options each. For compute efficiency we evaluate on a category-balanced subset of 560 questions: within each of the 14 categories we sample 40 questions uniformly at random with a fixed seed (seed=42), giving equal representation to all categories. The sampling procedure is implemented in datasets/process mmlu pro.py and is exactly reproducible: rng = random.Random(42) sampled = [rng.sample(cat items[c], 40) for c in categories] Prompts are constructed from the question stem and the full option block, with the output format Answer: $LETTER$ and a step-by-step instruction. The three-task sampling analysis (top-p, 3 seeds) uses GPQA-Diamond, MATH-500, and MMLU-Pro to cover science MC, math free-form, and general-knowledge MC; we exclude AIME (n = 30, high variance) and GSM8K (saturated) from that analysis.

We therefore select R = 3 as the knee of the accuracy–latency trade-off. 4.6. Limitations and Failure Modes FutureLift inherits important limitations from post-hoc pro- posal refinement. First, it preserves the number of input modes and predicts residual corrections to their coordinates and scores. It therefore cannot reliably recover a maneu- ver that is absent from the frozen proposal set, as illustrated in Fig. 3a. Second, the coordinate decoder does not ex- plicitly enforce vehicle dynamics, so aggressive corrections can occasionally produce excessive curvature (Fig. 3b). Fi- nally, our evaluation uses open-loop forecasting metrics and does not establish that the observed geometric improve- ments translate directly to closed-loop planning or safety.

This section presents the REPRO framework. From a software-engineering perspective, REPRO performs automated test-case generation against an LLM-based system under test, using the system’s own responses as a test oracle (via a separate judge model) and feeding failure information back into a closed-loop test generator. From a prompt-optimization perspective, REPRO is a multi- objective evolutionary optimizer that searches the space of natural-language prompts for non- dominated solutions under adversarial objectives, generalizing the search-and-reflect pattern of prior prompt optimizers [1, 18] to the security domain. These two perspectives describe the same system: the optimization machinery is general; the objectives, feedback signals, and seed corpus specialize it for security testing. We organize the section as follows. Section 3.1 formalizes the optimization problem. Section 3.2 gives the framework architecture and a road-map pseudocode. Section 3.3 describes the reflec- tive machinery that drives the loop: structured failure analysis and Bayesian strategy selection. Section 3.4 walks through the generation cycle that consumes this machinery. Section 3.5 cov- ers exploration control.

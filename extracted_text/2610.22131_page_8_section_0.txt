Decision accuracy Intern-Decision-4B has a suite average of 90.02%, exceeding the baselines in Table 3, and leads or ties on five of the seven suites. It scores 1,611/2,000 (80.55%) on Typed Decision and 299/310 (96.45%) on ToolACE. On public Jevbench, it answers 48/48 Easy, 71/72 Original, and 82/111 Hard items correctly. Jev leads WildJailBreak, Laya leads AG News, and JevK5 ties the 4B model on Hard. These results describe the evaluated systems; without matched-data ablations, they do not isolate the effect of joint field scoring or model size. Within Intern-Decision, the suite average rises monotonically from 79.38% at 0.8B to 84.68% at 2B and 90.02% at 4B, with Typed Decision and ToolACE already comparatively strong at 0.8B.

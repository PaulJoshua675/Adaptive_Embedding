For deployment, the examples are therefore paired with warnings against treating the system as a normative authority. 6.1 Domesticating Bias Score Standard multi-variant Nawatl corpora are heavily skewed toward colonial-era missionary texts, biblical translations, and pan-variety pedagogical materials. Models trained on these resources inherit a structural bias toward archaic, stan- dardized Central forms (such as tlazohcamati or cihuatl), which local speakers perceive as alien or domesticating. In contrast, translation into the community demands fidelity to the living, spoken variety of Santa Ana Tlacotenco (such as nimitstlasohkamate or sowatl). To evaluate this dynamic directly, the Domes- ticating Bias Score (DBS) measures the proportion of diagnostic translations in which a model defaults to generalized, colonial-derived variants rather than verified local community lexical choices. Standard reference-based metrics can reward Spanish loanwords or archaic colonial forms when the reference itself reflects domesticated translation practices. Drawing on pseudo-log-likelihood bias evaluation paradigms [15, 20] and length- normalized sequence scoring [23], We define the Domesticating Bias Score (DBS) as the difference in average per-token log-probability assigned by the model to a colonially domesticated candidate (tc) versus a sovereign community candidate (ts) given source input x: DBS(x) = 1 |tc| |tc| X j=1 log P(tc,j | tc,<j, x) −1 |ts| |ts| X j=1 log P(ts,j | ts,<j, x). A positive DBS indicates that the model assigns a higher average per-token log-probability to the domesticated candidate, whereas a negative DBS indicates a preference for the sovereign candidate. Values near zero reflect weak preference or equally probable alternatives. Token normalization is necessary because sovereign formulations can be longer than borrowed Spanish nouns or simplified roots, either morphologically or periphrastically. Without normalization, summing raw log-probabilities almost always penalizes longer strings because each additional token contributes a nonpositive term.

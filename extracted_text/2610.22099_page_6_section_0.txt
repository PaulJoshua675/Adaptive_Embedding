For each method, we evaluate the performance at exponentially increasing budgets T ∈{4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256}. Fixed-horizon methods will use exactly the requested number of decoder calls. For Fast-dLLM, T is a target mean NFE (refer to appendix A.2); other adaptive methods use decoder-call caps and may terminate earlier. Metrics. We primarily measure the micro-averaged accuracy of each method reported across different decoder-call budgets. In table 1, we additionally report the standard error of our method’s accuracies across three seeds. Further details are given in appendix B.1. Additionally, to summarize performance across decoding budgets using a single numerical value, we also estimate the area under the accuracy log-budget curve (AUC). Let Acc(k) be the method’s accuracy at NFE budgets k ∈{4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256}, we use a piecewise-linear interpolation in log2 NFE and trapezoidal quadrature, which simplifies to AUC = 1 log2 256 −log2 4 Z log2 256 log2 4 Acc(2u) du = 1 6 X k∈{4,8,...,128} Acc(k) + Acc(2k) 2 . If the method is adaptive and finishes decoding before using up the allocated NFE budget, its highest NFE accuracy is continued as a constant until k = 256 as the additional budget leaves the method’s output and hence performance unchanged. 4.2 Results Results. figure 1 and table 1 presents our main results. TIPS substantially improves the accuracy–compute tradeoff at low and moderate decoding budgets, with TIPSfull achieving the highest accuracy among all methods at 8–32 call caps. Notably, TIPSfull at a budget of 16 calls already exceeds WINO’s accuracy at a budget cap of 32 calls. The two TIPS variants exhibit complementary strengths across decoding budgets. While TIPSfull performs better at 4–32 call caps, TIPSblock achieves higher accuracy at 64–256 call caps and is often the highest accuracy among the methods in this range. At 256 call cap, TIPSblock reaches 68.18%, compared with 67.73% for UNCODE and 67.01% for Fast-dLLM: TIPS remains competitive at high budget. 6

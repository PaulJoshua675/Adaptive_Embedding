A Measurement and Annotation Human-reference sources. The human base- line draws on cMedQA2 for medical community question–answer pairs; HC3 for Chinese human answers on finance, law, psychology, and every- day topics; and COIG for Chinese-language ex- amination answers and explanations. The Chinese Essay Dataset supplies student essays in six gen- res, and CrossWOZ supplies travel-assistant dia- logues role-played by human participants. We also use worked solutions for the 2016–2018 manage- ment entrance examinations hosted by China Uni- versity of Petroleum (East China), solutions for the 2020 examination hosted by Shanghai Nor- mal University, and reference answers for the 2020 independent-admission mathematics examination hosted by the High School Aﬀiliated to Anhui Nor- mal University. Corpora and generation. The human reference contains 572,412 Han characters and 38 occur- rences across eight dimensions. The main compar- ison gives equal weight to the four dimensions rep- resented by these occurrences: professional abil- ity, general question answering, Chinese under- standing, and writing. Han exposure counts code points in U+4E00--U+9FFF. The four-dimension mean is .942 occurrences per 10,000 Han char- acters; pooling all eight human dimensions gives .664. Human materials allow comma-free and cross-sentence realizations, whereas the model de- tector requires a comma. The model panel contains 683 Align- Bench, 100 WritingBench, 200 COIG-CQIA, and 78 Arena-Hard-v2 prompts. The five logged model identifiers are gpt-5.6-luna, deepseek-v4.1-flash, gemini-3.8-flash, glm-5.3-flash, and qwen3.8-flash. Each prompt is a single user message; endpoints retain default reasoning behavior and receive scene- dependent temperatures when supported. Of 5,305 expected answers, 5,285 are valid. Detection and frequency measures. The fixed detector finds non-overlapping literal matches, per- mits an optional preceding bing, requires a Chi- nese or ASCII comma before ershi, and limits the captured alternatives to 160 and 220 char- acters. Slots exclude sentence-final punctuation, semicolons, and newlines; intervening whitespace may span a newline. Nested starts and comma-free forms can be missed, and interrogative strings can produce spurious detections. For valid answer i, let ci count occurrences and ℓ(u) i count Han or all Unicode characters. Activa- tion and density are A = N−1 ∑ i 1[ci > 0], Iu = 104 ∑ i ci ∑ i ℓ(u) i . For Figure 3a, sentence density is 103 ∑ i ci/ ∑ i si, where si counts punctuation- delimited units. We strip line-initial numbered-list prefixes, split at Chinese full stops and Chi- nese/ASCII question and exclamation marks, and split at ASCII periods unless flanked by digits. Segments must contain a Unicode letter; a final unterminated segment is retained. Newlines and semicolons do not split units. Sentence and Han densities are computed within each of the four shared tasks and then averaged equally. Human sentence counts are unavailable. The log-frequency panel transforms the macro Han density after averaging. The archived log scale is LFu = log10(1 + Iu). Human comparisons average Han density over four task dimensions. Benchmark analyses average activation and Unicode density equally over four benchmarks. Within a corpus, density decomposes into activation, occurrences per activated answer, and mean answer length. Full-panel uncertainty uses 5,000 prompt- cluster bootstraps stratified by benchmark. Between-model tests use common successful prompts and within-prompt exchanges, with Holm correction over ten activation and ten density contrasts separately. A.1 Functional Annotation and Human Comparison The sample comprises 308 selected response groups. One cybersecurity-related item triggered a refusal from the Claude annotation service and yielded no valid label; the functional analysis therefore covers 307 annotated targets. Labels were generated with claude-sonnet-5-5 and subsequently audited. Annotation excludes tem- plates, purely metalinguistic quoted strings, and known incomplete materials. Annotators assess the original response without edited variants or evaluator scores, recover the supported reading and question under discussion, and record factual un- certainty separately.

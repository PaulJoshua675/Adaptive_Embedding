The following lemma supplies the identities used in Theorem 2; the lemma does not assume that common hyperparameters preserve the correspondence during training. Corresponding-history setup. Let gt ∈Rn denote the data-loss gradient at step t and let ˆmt, ˆvt be the bias-corrected first- and second-moment estimates of AdamW with β1 ∈[0, 1) and β2 ∈(0, 1). Under the reparameterization θ′ := Tc(θ) with diagonal Jacobian Jc := diag(s1, . . . , sn), where si := c on A, si := c−1 on B, and si := 1 elsewhere, let the primed quantities be computed from the gradients in the new coordinates at the corresponding orbit points θ′ τ := Tc(θτ), τ ≤t, with the same hyperparameters and zero-initialized moment buffers. Lemma 8 (Adam moment covariance). Under the corresponding-history setup above, the new-coordinate gradients satisfy g′ t,i = s−1 i gt,i for every i and every t, so ˆm′ t,i = s−1 i ˆmt,i and ˆv′ t,i = s−2 i ˆvt,i. Proof of Lemma 8. With all parameters outside the pair held fixed, the data loss satisfies L ◦Tc = L by the exact-pair assumption, and Tc is the linear map Jc. Lemma 7 therefore gives, at every pair of corresponding points, g′ τ = J−1 c gτ, i.e., g′ τ,i = s−1 i gτ,i for all τ ≤t and all i. The moment recursions mτ := β1mτ−1 + (1 −β1)gτ and vτ := β2vτ−1 + (1 −β2)g⊙2 τ with m0 := v0 := 0 are, coordinate-wise, linear in the sequence (gτ,i)τ and in (g2 τ,i)τ, respectively. By induction on τ: m′ 0,i = 0 = s−1 i m0,i, and if m′ τ−1,i = s−1 i mτ−1,i then m′ τ,i = β1s−1 i mτ−1,i + (1 −β1)s−1 i gτ,i = s−1 i mτ,i; likewise v′ τ,i = β2s−2 i vτ−1,i + (1 −β2)s−2 i g2 τ,i = s−2 i vτ,i. The bias corrections ˆmt := mt/(1 −βt 1) and ˆvt := vt/(1 −βt 2) are positive scalars that do not depend on the coordinate, so ˆm′ t,i = s−1 i ˆmt,i and ˆv′ t,i = s−2 i ˆvt,i. Decoupled weight decay acts separately on the parameters and does not enter the moments. C.5 AdamW Equivariance and Canonical Restart We prove the coordinate criterion, the functional counterexample, and the canonical restart identity separately. All calculations are in exact arithmetic. Proof of Theorem 2. For the moment recursions and the coordinate criterion, suppose at step t that θ′ t = Sθt, m′ t = S−1mt, and v′ t = S−2vt. Since the sampled loss is the same in the two runs and Lt ◦S = Lt, differentiating this identity gives S⊤∇Lt(Sθt) = ∇Lt(θt). The diagonal matrix S is symmetric, so g′ t = S−1gt, where gt := ∇Lt(θt). The Adam moment recursions therefore give m′ t+1 = β1S−1mt + (1 −β1)S−1gt = S−1mt+1, v′ t+1 = β2S−2vt + (1 −β2)(S−1gt)⊙2 = S−2vt+1. Here x⊙2 denotes componentwise squaring. Shared bias-correction counters multiply each moment by the same scalar in both runs, so the same identities hold for ˆm and ˆv. This argument is inductive: The induction assumes corresponding parameters only at the current step, not at future iterates. Fix a coordinate and a step, suppress their indices, and put s := si, a := ˆmt+1,i, w := p ˆvt+1,i. Because s > 0, the primed adaptive term is η′a/(w + sϵ′). Thus universal commutation of the parameter-update formula is precisely the identity (1 −η′ω′)sθ − η′a w + sϵ′ = s(1 −ηω)θ −sηa w + ϵ (16) for every θ, a and every positive w, and by continuity where w = 0 and the denominators remain positive. First set a = 0 and vary θ. This substitution yields η′ω′ = ηω. With these decay terms equal, take a̸ = 0 and cancel a to obtain η′(w + ϵ) = sη(w + sϵ′) for every w > 0. Equality of the coefficients of w gives η′ = sη. Equality of the constant coefficients, together with sη > 0, then gives ϵ′ = ϵ/s.

Dashed line marks the 100%-label No-PT level. Paired two-tailed t-tests (N=5 seeds, BH FDR correction (Benjamini and Hochberg, 1995) across 28 comparisons) reveal task-dependent saturation behavior. EuroSAT-S1 and Flood-S1 show no statistically significant difference from the 100% baseline for all conditions ≥10% (pBH=0.076 and pBH=0.402 respectively). BigEarthNet-S1 shows no significant difference at 25% (pBH=0.269), though a non-monotonic dip at 50% (mean=76.47 vs. 76.75 at 25%) yields a significant result at that fraction (pBH=0.005), consistent with the high inter-seed variance (σ≈0.50 pp) for this task; discounting this single noisy condition, the effective saturation is con- sistent with ≥25% of the pretraining corpus. Semantic segmentation (DFC2020-S1) continues to improve significantly up to 25% (pBH=0.002), saturating between 25–50% (pBH=0.190 at 50%). These results indicate that spatial representation quality in SAR amplitude imagery is more sensi- tive to pretraining data quantity than global semantic features. We note that non-significance with N=5 is consistent with, but does not prove, equivalence; results should be interpreted alongside the effect sizes in Table 11. 4.6.

We compare the Decomposed Graded Verifier with UnifiedReward (Wang et al., 2025b), which also focuses on semantic alignment and produces continuous reward scores. As shown in Table 2, our method achieves superior performance on both GenEval (Ghosh et al., 2023) and CLIPScore (Hessel et al., 2021), supporting the ability of the Decomposed Graded Verifier trained with our Decoupled RL strategy to provide more accurate and fine-grained reward signals and highlighting its advantages as a generative reward model. 5.3 Rule-Governed Generative World Modeling Advantages of Pairwise Verification over Pointwise Verification. As shown in Tables 3 and 4, pairwise verification consistently outperforms pointwise verification on RISEBench (Zhao et al., 2025), UniREditBench (Han et al., 2025), and our constructed game-based benchmark. These results indicate that incorporating the ground-truth image as an explicit reference significantly enhances the accuracy and reliability of reward signals.

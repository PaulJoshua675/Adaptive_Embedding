(4.5) Luminance Standard Deviation. To quantify the spread of luminance values within an image, cap- turing intra-image contrast and variability is paramount. Realistic low-light scenes exhibit diverse illumination patterns rather than uniform darkness. To formally express this, Yi represents the lumi- nance value at pixel i, µY is the mean luminance, and Ωdenotes the image domain. σY = √︄ 1 |Ω| ∑︂ i∈Ω (Yi −µY )2. (4.6) Coefficient of Variation. The aim is to normalize luminance variability with respect to the mean brightness, providing a scale-invariant measure of illumination diversity across the dataset, and re- flecting how well the synthetic data captures the variability present in real-world lighting conditions. In the following formulation, σY denotes the luminance standard deviation and µY the mean lumi- nance. CV = σY µY × 100.

We presented a holistic multi-teacher post-training framework that enables a single self-supervised speech encoder to learn multiple complementary utterance-level speech representations through knowl- edge distillation. By jointly distilling semantic, speaker, language, and emotion representations from four specialized teacher models, the proposed approach replaces several task-specific encoders with a single shared backbone while preserving competitive performance across four independent downstream evaluation tasks. Despite the heterogeneous nature of the supervision signals and datasets, the proposed model consistently approaches the performance of dedicated single-task students, while even improving semantic retrieval and showing that semantic and language representations can provide complementary information for language identification.

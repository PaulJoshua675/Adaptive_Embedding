4.5 Complexity Analysis Despite the introduction of the dual-branch alignment architecture and the dynamic norm reconstruction mechanism, DualGCN preserves rigorous computational efficiency. The time complexity per training epoch is predominantly governed by the message-passing operations, which scale at O(𝐾|E|𝑑), where 𝐾, |E|, and 𝑑denote the number of graph convolutional layers, the total number of interaction edges, and the embedding dimensionality, respectively. The space complexity is constrained to O(|V|𝑑+ |E|) to maintain the trainable embedding parameters and the sparse adjacency matrix. Consequently, DualGCN delivers substantial performance improvements in strict cold-start scenarios without compromising the linear scalability inherent to minimalist graph recommenders such as LightGCN. 5 Experimental Evaluation In this chapter, we first detail the experimental setup.

Deadline path and INT8 recipe The timing path is governed in three layers: PL compute is cycle-bounded; inference latency is a measured envelope; delivery is enforced by a watchdog that publishes the pre- vious ego-warped occupancy, flagged stale, when a frame would miss the deadline. Enforcement is implemented and measured on the board: fallback preparation costs 0.2 ms on the A53, and every frame yields an output at the dead- line plus an enforcement envelope of 0.3 to 1.9 ms typical, 7.6 to 10.2 ms worst across the 421 events of three enforced 100k-frame runs (Sec. 4.3), a host-thread wake latency that a PL-side timer, not implemented here, would remove. The fallback is conservative only in that known occupied space is retained under ego motion; a VRU first appearing during the missed frame is invisible to it, which is why the output carries the stale flag and why the fallback’s cost is measured directly (Sec. 4.4). The host uses core dedication rather than RT priority, the stable policy for the polling DPU runner (Sec. 4.1).

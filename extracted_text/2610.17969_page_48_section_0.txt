Neural Dimensional Analysis The weight-space shadow. A bias-free matrix product x := Wu is 1-homogeneous in W. When this product is the block’s only dependence on W, with no tied use or additive W-dependent path, the ray W 7→etW realizes the activation rescaling x 7→etx. If the downstream graph nondimensionalizes that activation, the same ray becomes an exact symmetry of the loss. Proposition 6 (Weight-space shadow of activation typing). Let W be a parameter block whose only effect on the loss is through x := Wu, where u is independent of W. Suppose the graph nondimensionalizes x such that the downstream computation is invariant to x 7→cx for c > 0 when all other quantities are fixed. Then L(etW) = L(W) for all t ∈R, and the radial Euler pairing vanishes exactly wherever the derivative is defined: ⟨W, ∇W L⟩F = 0. The proof is given in App. G.10, and the architecture-specific predictions and measurement protocol are given in App. H.6. If instead the graph fails to nondimensionalize x, the weight-space radial pairing equals the corresponding activation-space pairing ⟨x, ∇xL⟩and is generally nonzero. App. G.10 gives the derivation. Measured fingerprint. We also identify weight matrices whose magnitude can change without changing the loss. Prop. 6 predicts zero radial sensitivity ⟨W, ∇W L⟩F when the downstream computation cancels rescaling of a matrix’s output. We measure this pairing to check whether multiplying the matrix by a common factor affects the loss. Under W 7→etW, this pairing is the loss derivative at t = 0, measured in nats per e-fold. One e-fold is a factor of e ≈2.718 in weight magnitude, corresponding to a one-unit increase in log-scale. I.3 Extended PTQ results Integer quantization stores integer coordinates together with a scale that restores the activation magnitudes. A shared tensor scale must cover large activation values, making the integer grid relatively coarse for tokens with smaller magnitudes. Per-token scaling assigns a separate range of representable values to each token. Tokens with smaller activations can then use more closely spaced values instead of the coarse spacing set by the largest activations in the tensor. Quantizing the same trained functions in Table 10 and Figure 6 differentiates architectures along the scale- management axis predicted by the typing rules. Static per-site, per-tensor int8 changes the normalized-gauge models by at most +0.005 nats, while the other 124M baselines change by +0.034 to +1.099 nats. Per-token scales reduce the worst 124M change to +0.002 nats. At 1B under per-token int4, GCT has the smallest increase, at +0.739 nats.

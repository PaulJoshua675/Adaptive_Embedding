F Evaluation on Larger DAGs F.1 Predictive Accuracy on MalNet-Derived DAGs We further evaluate the direction-aware encoder on larger DAGs derived from the five-class MalNet- Tiny graph-classification dataset [37]. The derived graphs contain 5–4,993 nodes, with a mean of 1,407 nodes. This experiment examines the effectiveness of the directional design on larger graphs under limited training supervision. We contract each strongly connected component into a node to obtain a condensation DAG. Each node receives a shared operation token, so the compared predictors must derive their graph representations from the structure of the resulting DAG. All compared methods use the same derived graphs and data partitions. We use a stratified 5%/5%/90% training/validation/test split and fit each model on the 5% training partition. All classification metrics are computed on the held-out 90% test partition. The five classes contain equal numbers of graphs, and stratification preserves their proportions in each partition.

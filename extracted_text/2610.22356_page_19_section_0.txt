DK-VLM: Geometry as Position A.4 Reference-Frame Composition Details Reference-Frame Composition uses planar angular relations extracted from the source an- notations. We write ⟨z⟩360 = z mod 360◦for normalization to [0, 360◦), with clockwise rotation positive, and use the corresponding circular distance d◦for tolerance checks. We first parse and normalize the source relations, then apply consistency checks specific to each source to obtain the verified single-step annotations used for chain construction. After these checks, scenes are partitioned, and chains are constructed only within one scene and split. During partitioning, scene groups that overlap VSI-Bench are isolated from the RC pool. Finally, an independent validator rereads the source annotations, recomputes every derived target, and verifies scene and split membership together with source provenance. These checks cross-validate source angle annotations and their algebraic consistency; they do not use images, XYZ coordinates, or ground-truth 3D states. VSI-RC. From each VSI-590K absolute_direction_object annotation (Yang et al., 2025c), the parser extracts (media, O, A, B, convention, α(O, A, B)), (15) where O is the current location, A is the facing anchor, and B is the target landmark. A clockwise raw angle is normalized directly, whereas a counterclockwise angle is converted as ⟨360◦− araw⟩360. The normalized angle must agree with the corresponding relative_direction_object label under the source four-quadrant convention. Inverse relations and angle closure are checked with a 2◦circular tolerance: d◦(⟨α(O, A, B) + α(O, B, A)⟩360 , 0) ≤2◦, d◦(⟨α(O, A, C) −α(O, A, B)⟩360 , α(O, B, C)) ≤2◦. (16) Verified relations are connected only within the same media item. A relation extends the current path when its origin is the terminal landmark and its facing anchor is the preceding landmark; paths with repeated landmarks are rejected. After the initial turn, a landmark path C = (v0, . . . , vK) induces the ordered reference-state transitions (vi−1, vi) →(vi, vi+1) for 1 ≤i < K. Let a0 be the initial facing anchor. At subsequent steps, the source annotation uses the direction toward the previous landmark as its reference. This reference is opposite to the direction of arrival, requiring a 180◦correction. Converting the source facing convention to the traversal-centered convention gives δVSI k = ( α(v0, a0, v1), k = 1, ⟨α(vk−1, vk−2, vk) −180◦⟩360 , 2 ≤k ≤K. (17) The exact-turn target retains the full sequence (δVSI 1 , . . . , δVSI K ). The discrete-action target is created only when every turn lies in one of the conservative bins: straight for [0◦, 30◦] ∪ [330◦, 360◦), right for [60◦, 120◦], back for [150◦, 210◦], and left for [240◦, 300◦]. VSI-RC does not serialize cumulative headings. Its prompt specifies the initial reference point, the initial bearing target, and the ordered waypoints while withholding the derived turn sequence. 19

We test whether the protection observed in the deep-model stress experiment also occurs at 354M parameters. The experiment compares unperturbed training with exact Q–K reparameterization at λ ∈{0.1, 0.2} under the same training recipe. Setup. We train the xlarge GELU model with d = 1024 and L = 24 for 15,000 AdamW steps on a single NVIDIA H100 GPU, applying Q–K rescaling at λ ∈{0, 0.1, 0.2}. The sequence length is 512, and batch size 8 with 4× gradient accumulation gives an effective batch size of 32. SAGGS+P applies only to Q–K and V–O pairs, where the bilinear symmetry is exact regardless of activation, with interval 200 and auxiliary penalty weight 0.005. Warmup is 1.5K steps. The reported PPL is the best validation value; gradient slopes use the last logged state at 14.5K. Results. Table 18 reports one seed at λ = 0 and 0.1, and three seeds at λ = 0.2. At λ = 0, the PPL values are 23.53 and 23.59, a 0.06 increase for SAGGS+P in this single-seed comparison. At λ = 0.1, where c23 = e0.1×23 ≈10, Vanilla achieves 23.89 while SAGGS+P maintains 23.56, giving a +0.33 PPL gap at this scale factor. At λ = 0.2, where c23 = e0.2×23 ≈100, Vanilla PPL degrades to 25.84 ± 0.04 over 3 seeds, a +2.31 increase over the 23.53 Vanilla baseline, while SAGGS+P maintains 23.58 ± 0.06, essentially unchanged from the unperturbed case. All three seed-paired differences favor SAGGS+P, with small dispersion in these observed runs. The final logged gradient slope is 0.187 for Vanilla at λ = 0.2 and 0.017 for SAGGS+P.

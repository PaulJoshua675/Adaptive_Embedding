In attribution, the task is to quantify how much a component, a layer or one of its two sublayers, con- tributed to the centred logit of the predicted token. As the reference we remove what the component wrote at one position, leave the prefix untouched, complete the forward pass, and measure the change in the logit, on 64 sequences. This effect is the dif- ference of coefficients in LEXFRAME[∞], so the test asks whether a frame that withholds exchanges within the prefix still attributes as the model does. In a frame, the attribution is the change in the coef- ficient of the predicted token when the component is removed from the state. We compare it with the same difference taken in the two lenses, of which the logit lens gives direct logit attribution, and with linear attributions, the inner product of a lexical readout with the component, which for LEXFRAME[∞] is attribution patching (Syed et al., 2023; Kramár et al., 2024).

We compute a V-JEPA-2 (Assran et al., 2025) surprise score on a YouCook2 (Zhou et al., 2018) video by masking the features of the last temporal block and measuring the distance between its predicted representation and the representation of the subsequent block. In parallel, we track the 512-dimensional LoRA hidden state of MaLoW (Qwen3.5-9B), defined as Norm(ot)+αNorm(st). We average the hidden states used to generate layer-specific LoRA adapters and project the resulting representation into two dimensions using t-SNE. The LoRA state changes more rapidly when the V-JEPA-2 surprise score is high (before 60s and after 130s), while evolving more smoothly during the lower-surprise interval between 60s and 130s. This correspondence suggests that the LoRA state adapts dynamically to changes in the underlying video events. Effect of Memory on Real-Time Perception. We investigate whether long-term historical information compressed by MaLoW adversely affects real-time perception on the real-time splits of OVO-Bench and StreamingBench. For OVO-Bench, where video prefixes extend up to 28 minutes, we group real-time questions by their preceding video duration into four intervals: 0–5, 5–10, 10–15, and over 15 minutes. As shown in Figure 8-1, MaLoW (Qwen3.5-9B) maintains comparable accuracy to the local-window baseline (Qwen3.5-9B, last 2 seconds) across different prefix ranges, suggesting that accumulated memory does not compromise real-time perception. For StreamingBench, where the original video prefixes are relatively short, we randomly sample 100 samples and construct longer streams by prepending randomly sampled videos, extending the historical context from 5 to 60 minutes. As shown in Figure 8-2, consistent with the observations on OVO-Bench, MaLoW (Qwen3.5-9B) maintains stable real-time perception across varying history lengths. These observations are consistent with our design choice to regularize the norm of the generated LoRA weights. This regularization constrains the magnitude of memory-induced weight updates, preventing them from dominating the pretrained model weights and disrupting its real-time perception capabilities.

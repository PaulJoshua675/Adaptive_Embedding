(3.34) Semantic Consistency Loss. While the perceptual loss enforces similarity in a generic feature space to improve visual realism, it does not explicitly guarantee preservation of task-relevant se- mantic content. In LLIE, an approach which produces visually appealing images through contrast and colour balance amplification, does not imply that the model is optimized to enhance images for downstream tasks. Usually, it is quite the opposite as the type of visual composition which is ap- pealing to the human eye is not informative enough for computer scene-understanding tasks such as semantic segmentation or object detection. 35

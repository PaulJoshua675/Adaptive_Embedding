After the 𝑁candidates are evaluated against the target model and scored by the judge, REPRO assembles the next generation using elitism: the top 𝐸candidates from the Pareto frontier are guaranteed survival, and the remaining 𝑁−𝐸slots are filled from the evaluated candidates ranked by fitness (with the best candidates from the previous generation 𝑃𝑔 used as fallback if fewer than 𝑁−𝐸candidates passed evaluation). The default elite size is 𝐸= 3 for 𝑁= 24, balancing solution preservation against population renewal: too few elites risks losing optima through rare sampling failures, too many reduces diversity. Elitism guarantees that the best-so-far ASR is monotone non-decreasing across generations, the formal property characterized in §3.6. 3.5 Exploration Control: Diversity, Stagnation, and Knowledge Transfer Three mechanisms together ensure REPRO maintains productive exploration when the optimizer would otherwise get stuck: adaptive diversity control, multi-level stagnation management with restarts, and knowledge transfer that warm-starts new optimization phases from prior successes. We describe them in increasing time scale, from per-generation adaptation to cross-behavior pattern reuse. Diversity-driven adaptive mutation. REPRO measures population diversity via the average pair- wise cosine distance between sentence embeddings of attack prompts, ranging from 0 (identical) to 1 (completely dissimilar). Sentence embeddings are used rather than lexical metrics because two attacks with different word choices but identical structure may have high lexical distance despite being semantically equivalent. Both the mutation rate 𝜇and the offspring count 𝑘adapt based on this diversity: when diversity drops below a low threshold, both increase (aggressive exploration); when diversity rises above a high threshold, both decay back to baseline (exploitation). The encoder cost is negligible (about 5 ms per attack versus seconds per target-model evaluation) and embeddings are cached across generations. To prevent semantic-only diversity from masking structural convergence (populations of medium- length prompts with goals embedded in the middle, which the cosine metric will not flag as redundant), REPRO additionally maintains 15 structural diversity seed templates that systematically vary attack architecture across length, goal position, nesting depth, benign ratio, and output format. During restart phases, 25% of newly generated seeds are drawn from these templates. The full template list is in the released configuration. Multi-level stagnation management. Naive early stopping terminates the optimizer at the first sign of stagnation. REPRO instead uses graduated restart intensities that escalate based on repeated stagnation events, preventing premature termination while avoiding wasted computation when restarts are genuinely failing. Stagnation is detected when ASR improvement over a sliding window of 𝑤iterations falls below threshold 𝛿. The window adapts to current ASR:𝑤= 20 at high ASR (≥80%),𝑤= 15 at medium ASR, and 𝑤= 10 at low ASR, on the principle that small improvements at high ASR are harder to find and more valuable, so the optimizer should be patient before declaring stagnation.

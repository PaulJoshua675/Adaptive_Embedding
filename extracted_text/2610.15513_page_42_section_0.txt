Our reasoning: (i) all evaluated behaviors come from publicly available benchmarks (AdvBench, JailbreakBench, HarmBench) that the same vendors’ models are continuously evaluated against, so we do not surface behaviors the vendors are unaware of; (ii) REPRO’s mutation strategies are drawn from public literature with full citations in Section 2, and the framework itself (independent of any specific prompt) is the scientific contri- bution; (iii) the paper does not release the full unsanitized attack corpus. We commit to responsive disclosure: on request from any vendor whose models appear in this evaluation, we will provide the corresponding discovered attacks, the target-model versions and evaluation dates involved, and the configuration needed to reproduce them, through the corresponding author. For future work in this area we intend to notify affected vendors before submission when results include previously unreported attack structures. Release scope. Our release (Appendix A) includes the framework source code, strategy library, seed prompts, judge configurations, and ablation experiment scripts and results. We do not release the full corpus of discovered attacks from our evaluation runs, though we will provide it to any vendor whose models appear in this evaluation on request to the corresponding author. Practitioners can reproduce the optimization pipeline and generate their own attack corpora for their specific target models using the released code. The dual-use nature of red-teaming research requires careful balance between advancing safety evaluation capabilities and preventing misuse. We believe systematic automated red-teaming, as embodied by REPRO, provides greater benefit through proactive vulnerability discovery than harm through potential misuse, particularly given that manual red-teaming and simpler automated methods are already widely available. We nevertheless treat this as an ongoing judgment rather than a settled one, and we welcome community feedback on the disclosure and release posture adopted here. 6

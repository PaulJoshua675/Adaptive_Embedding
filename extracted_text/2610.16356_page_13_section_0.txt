2.4.3. Gravitational Search Algorithm Gravitational Search Algorithm (GSA) is an algorithm found by Rashedi et al. (2009). This algorithm is inspired by a very well-known law of physics, Newton’s law of gravity. This law states that some objects attract each other with gravitational force, and that gravitational force makes them move towards objects with heavier masses.

Hand-labeled figure relevance and validated numeric comparisons remain necessary to evaluate its scientific utility. 5.9 Public-interface validation The public-interface restoration was checked with offline regression tests, an actual browser document upload, an actual Notebook kernel, and an installed-wheel local trial. These checks validate plumbing, diagnostic reporting, and local execution paths. They do not constitute a fresh scientific benchmark of the extensions. Paid model trials for the restored extensions, the full MinerU path, and a new full-report end-to-end experiment were not run as part of this revision. 6 Discussion and Limitations Recovery, evidence, and interpretability. The results favor Idea2Prior on EASY and MEDIUM while showing substantial residual difficulty on HARD. Explicit intermediate records make failures inspectable: discovery, identity resolution, budget selection, semantic filtering, PDF acquisition, and final ranking are distinct potential causes. Historical filtering failures motivated the semantic neighbor gate, but the present comparison does not isolate that change’s contribution through an ablation. It would be incorrect to conclude that all remaining misses come from filtering rather than discovery. Evaluation scale and controls. The 30 query cases include overlapping targets and a manually assembled distribution; the first ten items in each tier are not a random sample of all scientific literature. Benchmark authoring used an LLM, browser build identifiers were unavailable, history contamination cannot be excluded retrospectively, and one baseline credit follows an explicit historical convention. These factors limit causal and statistical interpretation. Future evaluation should freeze inputs, use isolated sessions, report first- attempt and recovered performance separately, and obtain independent relevance judgments for every returned work. Efficiency and cost. The retrieval-only design omits report generation and bounds full-text acquisition, but the reported API workflow still takes longer than the operator’s web averages. Unknown usage and the separate cost sample prevent full token/billing reconstruction. Controlled same-query, same-provider experiments are needed before claiming that a particular optimization improves speed without reducing accuracy, or that one system offers a measured cost-quality advantage over another. Evidence incompleteness and optional tools. Abstracts and a few passages can omit the distinguishing contribution of a paper. Native PDF text extraction does not capture every image or table. Generated overlap labels are not calibrated relevance probabilities, and a model’s reproducibility assessment is not an execution-based replication study. Historical extension evaluations include empty dependency labels, model-judge variation, and an external similarity graph used as a comparison reference. Visual comparison remains an unreproduced historical component. Scope. Our comparison addresses finding papers from research ideas. It does not evaluate general website exploration, forum search, comprehensive web research, report quality, legal patent novelty, or the correctness of every citation recommendation. General web and forum capabilities remain limited. A designated-target miss can coexist with useful related papers, so target Hit@5 should be complemented by independently adjudicated relevance and usefulness. 10

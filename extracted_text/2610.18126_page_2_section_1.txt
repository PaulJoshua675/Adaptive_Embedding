Freshness and temporal knowledge. Language models degrade on text written after their training data (Lazaridou et al., 2021) and can be probed and refreshed for time-dependent facts (Dhingra et al., 2022; Kim et al., 2024). Benchmarks over evolving news and time-sensitive questions test whether answers are current (Chen et al., 2021; Liška et al., 2022; Kasai et al., 2023), search aug- mentation keeps them current (Lewis et al., 2020; Vu et al., 2024), and evaluation sets themselves go stale as facts change (Jiang et al., 2026). Temporal reasoning benchmarks test ordering and arithmetic over time (Fatemi et al., 2025). These works judge the model when it answers; we judge a decision when it is carried out. Updates, conflicts and revision. Knowledge editing changes stored facts (Meng et al., 2022), and multi-hop and ripple-effect evaluations require an update to reach every fact that depends on it (Zhong et al., 2023; Cohen et al., 2024). Models re- solve conflicts between parametric and contextual knowledge unevenly (Xie et al., 2024a; Xu et al., 2024), including conflicts that arise while agents act (Lyu et al., 2026), and revise conclusions under new premises with mixed success (Kassner et al., 2021; Wilie et al., 2024).

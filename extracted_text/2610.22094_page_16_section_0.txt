In the executed runs with τ ∈{0.50, 0.55, 0.80}, activa- tion of the fixed OR mask removed nearly all off-diagonal negatives and drove the contrastive loss close to zero. At the audited τ = 0.80 representation, the image branch selected every off-diagonal pair, so any lower cutoff is no less per- missive. Runs with τ ∈{0.85, 0.90, 0.95} remained non- degenerate. We therefore omit the saturated low-threshold settings from downstream evaluation and report τ = 0.95 as the best-performing non-degenerate fixed setting. This behavior occurs when an absolute cutoff enters the bulk of the current similarity distribution. In contrast, SCCL’s modality-specific thresholds τ ℓ s = bµℓ s + kbσℓ s track the evolving image and text similarity scales, reducing the risk of such saturation. 16

Research ethics and data statement All analyzed content consists of question-and-answer posts publicly viewable on Yahoo! Chiebukuro at the time of collection; no private messages, account credentials, or other non-public data were accessed. The scraped records retain only post text, post/question identifiers, timestamps, and structural tags (question/best answer/other answer/reply/ thanks); no usernames, poster IDs, or other author-identifying fields were collected or retained. All reported results are aggregate statistics (word/cluster frequencies, z-scores, regression coefficients) over thousands of posts; no individual post is reproduced or attributed to an identifiable author in this paper.

Gold-fact ORM attribution handles delayed cross-chunk evidence, so γ = 0 avoids broadcasting noisy future recall scores backward. 6.6 How this resolves sparse ORM Probes give every chunk a local signal, so a sequence is not limited to a few terminal questions. Weighted rubrics with partial credit and distractors replace binary answer reward. Traps and the bank-growth penalty penalize over-storage. Gold facts attribute outcome evidence to evidence chunks instead of broadcasting one score. The answer model and judge are frozen, so reader improvements cannot hide writer defects. Continuous signals increase within-group resolution. Carrying the selected policy-written bank into the next window preserves cross-session effects. This is closer in spirit to process supervision than terminal-only RL (Lightman et al., 2023), but the supervised unit is an externally verifiable bank consequence rather than a subjective reasoning step. 6.7 Chunk-level GRPO GRPO estimates a baseline from several samples without a separate value model (Shao et al., 2024). Standard GRPO scores each sampled trajectory once, normalizes that score within the group, and applies the resulting advantage to every token of the trajectory. A trajectory-level group is not appropriate here: all eight rollouts share the same bank only at chunk 0; after that, rollout i observes the bank created by its own earlier actions, and a single trajectory score would again broadcast one judgment over all of its writes. We instead compute advantages per chunk, grouping by window, harness configuration, sequence, and chunk position: Ai,t = si[t] −µt σt + ϵ , (7) where µt and σt are the mean and standard deviation of sj[t] over the chunk-level group Gt. The best rollout can therefore differ from chunk to chunk: rollout 1 may have the highest score at chunk 2 while rollout 3 is best at chunk 4, and each chunk’s writes are reinforced or suppressed according to their own column (Figure 1). Each rollout contributes one vote to the baseline for a chunk. If a multi-stage harness made three LLM calls for that chunk, all three segments receive the same advantage but do not count as three independent observations. Otherwise the policy could alter the baseline by changing its call count. Groups with fewer than two valid rollouts receive zero advantage. 6.8 Bank carry-forward Bank selection is a separate, rollout-level decision. At the end of a window, the eight final banks are ranked by ∑t s[t]. The winner becomes the initial state of the next window in that sequence; the others are discarded. If all candidates fail because of platform faults, the previous bank is reused. Bank state is checkpointed with model state. An inherited error from an earlier optimizer step cannot receive retrospective gradient. Carrying imperfect policy-written banks is still preferable to teacher forcing with an ideal bank: deployment also requires the writer to operate on its own prior mistakes. An inherited error common to all candidates also tends to cancel in the group-relative baseline rather than being assigned to the current chunk. 7 Training System Each harness is packaged behind a common interface: bank_open, write, search, bank_stats, and bank_close.

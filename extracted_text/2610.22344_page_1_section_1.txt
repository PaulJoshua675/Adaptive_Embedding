The rapid advancement of reasoning, code generation, and tool interaction in large language models (LLMs) is enabling artificial intelligence (AI) to move beyond assisting scientific research and engi- neering toward autonomously developing and improving AI systems. These efforts include model training, optimization of solver programs, and algorithm design, making AI for AI (AI4AI) a research direction toward recursive self-improvement (RSI). In this context, machine learning engineering (MLE) provides a concrete setting for this goal, requiring long-horizon optimization through repeated execution, feedback analysis, and revision rather than one-shot generation. Most existing MLE systems [1, 2, 3] extend code generation into iterative optimization through search, memory, or multi-agent collaboration.

These ratios compare only the two displayed rewrites, not the total probability of a correct edit. D Additional Discussion Broader impacts. Multi-target few-shot prompting could enable example-guided document and code editing across multiple sites. Practical use will require more reliable adherence to demonstrations and better prediction of output lengths. With these improvements, users could specify transformations through a few examples rather than editing each site manually. Potential risks. Incorrect transformations can propagate across sites, altering document meaning or introducing software defects. Our Checkstyle case study shows that example guidance can favor an incorrect edit even when demonstrations support the intended transformation. Practical use requires review, including compilation and testing for code; our evaluation instead relies on an LLM judge without execution. Artifacts and licensing.

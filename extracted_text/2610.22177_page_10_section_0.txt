A wide range of indoor and outdoor scenes are covered in the datasets either synthetic or captured in the wild. In each training epoch, we randomly sampled a fixed number of scenes from each training dataset: 1,000 in Spring, 2,750 in UnrealStereo4K, and 26,250 in the other 9 datasets. We preprocess the datasets into WAI format[14] for the ease of training and benchmarking. 4.2 Implementation Details We implement our method using the official pretrained checkpoints of π3 [13] and VGGT [1], fine-tuning them with the proposed motion and orien- tation priors. The injection points for privileged information are tailored to each architecture: for VGGT, information is injected into the 12th layer of the aggregator. In contrast, as π3 lacks explicit camera tokens, we inject ad- ditional modalities after the aggregator but before the specific transformer decoders to maintain architectural isolation. Specifically, gravity features are injected before the camera decoder, while residual flow features are in- tegrated before the point and confidence decoders. A detailed analysis of these injection points is provided in Section 4.4. All models are fine-tuned for a total of 10 epochs. For single-modality configurations, Stage 1 (Privileged Alignment) spans the first 2 epochs, while the remaining 8 epochs are dedicated to Stage 2 (Knowledge Distillation). During Stage 2, we employ a scene-wise modality dropout rate fixed at 0.5 to encourage the model encoder to hallucinate privileged features. To ensure stable gradient flow to the privileged encoders, the scaling weights Wflow and Wgrav are initialized and fixed at 1.0 during the first two epochs.

Terminal rewards are sparse and less suited to long-horizon tasks, so we use ltl𝑓counterfac- tual experience replay (CER), inspired by Voloshin et al. [54]. For each agent, we replay a recorded rollout chunk from alternative reward-automaton states, using the observed proposition labels to recompute monitor states and terminal rewards. Environment observations, actions, and episode endings stay fixed: the same rollout provides experience for several stages of progress towards the objective. IQL variants store relabelled experience in replay, Nash-Q updates online, and PPO variants combine original and relabelled chunks and recompute rollout statistics.

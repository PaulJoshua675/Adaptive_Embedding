We introduced MPT, a framework for jointly learning individual action policies and self- orchestration in decentralized agent swarms. Learned bids connect participation decisions with local credit assignment, allowing agents to improve both how they act and when they participate without a central coordinating agent. Our experiments show benefits over monolithic post-training and other coordination strategies, while ablations demonstrate the value of learning both bidding and action policies and using learned bids for credit assignment. Trained swarms also adapt faster to related objectives, benefit from larger populations at inference time, and better tolerate agent unavailabil- ity as the population grows. These findings support treating self-orchestration as a learnable and reusable component of collective intelligence, opening a path toward decentralized agent swarms whose capabilities and organization improve together through experience. 9

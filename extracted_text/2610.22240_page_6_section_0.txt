Avg. is the unweighted benchmark mean. Baselines. We compare tool-free Direct Answer, tool-enabled Agentic Workflow, and trained Multi- modal Deep Search Agents, grouping the latter by model scale. Implementation Details. We use ms-swift for SFT and veRL for joint RL–OPD training. The agent interleaves reasoning with textual and visual retrieval, with a maximum of 50 interaction turns per evaluation question. 4.2 Main Results Overall Performance.

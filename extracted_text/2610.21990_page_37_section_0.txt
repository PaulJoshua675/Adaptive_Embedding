3.4 Fusion Vision Transformer Encoding Phase. The objective of the Fusion Vision Transformer is to effectively integrate an arbi- trary number of input modalities, defined as a primary modality, Mmain, and k ∈N auxiliary modali- ties, M1,k. The modality-agnostic nature of the architecture is enabled by the self-attention mechanism within the Fusion Encoder Block. This design effectively integrates global and local patterns from the aux- iliary modalities while remaining inherently scalable. It accommodates an arbitrary number of in- puts, without requiring structural modifications to the underlying fusion method. Features are processed using a encoder-decoder path, where the encoding process is conducted in 4 consecutive steps each of them being represented by the Fusion Encoding Block. The decoding process does not perform fusion as it process a single feature space originating from the end of the encoding stage. During the encoding stage, the feature representations for each modality are enriched using a ResNet50 [28] (≈25.6M parameters) for the primary modality and ResNet18 [28] (≈11.7M parameters) backbones for the auxiliary modalities. This asymmetric backbone selection is motivated by com- putational efficiency, while the lighter ResNet18 [28] architectures minimize overhead, the deeper ResNet50 [28] is utilized for the RGB stream to leverage its superior representational power, which is critical for capturing the complex visual features required within the scope of VesperMUSE. 37

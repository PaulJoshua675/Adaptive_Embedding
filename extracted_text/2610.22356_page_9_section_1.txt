We evaluate on seven spatial benchmarks: VSI-Bench, ReVSI-32, MMSI-Bench, MindCube, ViewSpatial, SITE, and CV-Bench (Yang et al., 2025a, Zhang et al., 2026b, Yang et al., 2025d, Wang et al., 2025b, Li et al., 2025a, Wang et al., 2025d, Tong et al., 2024). We follow GeoThinker’s evaluation settings on the six shared benchmarks (Li et al., 2026b) and use the official 32-frame setting for ReVSI. Our comparisons cover base VLMs (Wang et al., 2025c), spatial post-training methods, and geometry-enhanced models (Li et al., 2026b, Zhang et al., 2026a, Gao et al., 2026).

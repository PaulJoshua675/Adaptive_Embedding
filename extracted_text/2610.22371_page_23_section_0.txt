For MonST3R, VGGT, DepthAnythingv3, MapAnything and 4RC, we infer all required frames in a single run. For Spann3R, Any4D, V-DPM and OpenD4RT, we infer in multiple chunks of 48 frames with an overlap of 24 frames, i.e., |Tk| = 48, |Ok| = 24. Our method also uses chunked inference, with |Tk| = 49 and |Ok| = 24 to maintain a comparable inference setting. For a few Sintel videos that are shorter than the chunk length, we pad the sequence with the repeated last frame until its length reaches the next valid length of 4N + 1, as required by the Wan-VAE. The padded frames are used only for encoding and are discarded before aligning to and evaluating on GT. Our method naturally supports high-resolution depth estimation. This can be achieved simply by querying on high-resolution query frames. However, for a fair comparison in Tab. 2, we use query frames with a maximum side length of 512 pixels, following a resolution setting comparable to other methods. The raw query frames are resized while preserving their aspect ratio, and the corresponding encoder frames are resized to the encoder input resolution with the same aspect ratio. D.3 CAMERA POSE ESTIMATION Metric Evaluation. For aligning to GT cameras, we estimate a single scene-level Sim(3) transfor- mation from the corresponding estimated and GT camera centers at all valid frames using Umeyama alignment (Umeyama, 1991), and apply it to the estimated camera-to-world poses.

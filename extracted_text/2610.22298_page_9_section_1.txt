Analyzed separately. 3.8 Physician Reference Evaluation Physician ratings provided the reference for Judge calibration and internal evaluation. The evaluation panel comprised 6 physicians, each possessing cross-disciplinary expertise in multiple adjacent medical specialties. This configuration ensured comprehensive coverage of specialty-specific evaluation perspectives, composition of the multidisciplinary panels across the dataset. Each clinical case was evaluated from the standpoint of three specialties represented in the original consensus. Experts were exclusively assigned cases aligning with their clinical domain. Evaluation was conducted under single-blind conditions: model outputs were randomly ordered and presented without model identifiers, generation condition labels, or paired responses from the alternative condition. Case presentations were delivered without source attribution or retrieval metadata, strictly adhering to the input protocol used for model inference. Raters used the fixed 0–3 rubric in Table 1. Eight criteria were applied separately to clinical reasoning, the role-specific clinical opinion, and the final consilium report (24 fields). Three further fields covered panel adequacy, case complexity, and overall quality.

(2025), we explain each personalized latent with an LLM explainer and evaluate the explanation with an LLM scorer (both Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct). The explainer sees 30 activating examples (the top 20 and 10 weaker ones), each a 40-token window centred on the max- imally activating token (32 tokens before, 8 tokens after). The scorer then labels 20 examples as ac- tivating and 20 as non-activating, using only the explanation. Figure 8 shows an example: a max- activating latent from Qwen3 is explained as track- ing the audience’s level of expertise. Most personalized latents encode the requested preference rather than the response: 85% of OL- MoE and 82% of Qwen3 latents fire mainly on the system message. Mixtral is the exception, with 41% firing mainly in the response. The models also emphasize different preference dimensions: OLMoE’s latents are dominated by style (52%) and harmlessness (21%), whereas Qwen3’s and Mixtral’s are dominated by background knowledge (45% and 40%). The scorer reaches moderate F1 overall (0.65 OLMoE, 0.63 Qwen3, 0.70 Mixtral; Figure 7), rising monotonically across activation bins to 0.81, 0.83 and 0.88 in the top bin, so the explanations best describe strong activations.

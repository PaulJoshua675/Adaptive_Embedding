Add a symmetric third target Acquisition-selected formation spans two observable contrasts. 8 Scope and remaining questions The evidence concerns minimal Gaussian and finite-state systems, linear bottlenecks or small tanh networks, externally specified acquisition and update rules, and the stated finite observation windows. Formation is measured after 500 or 600 updates; the longest acquisition-redirection test runs for 1,000 updates. Feedback regimes receive longer observation, including 8,000-update coexistence and a 16,000-update stability check. None of these windows proves an asymptotic attractor. The specificity makes the causal comparisons interpretable but leaves their prevalence in larger learned systems open. The strongest optimizer-state evidence concerns ten-update opposing pulses in a two-target network. Endpoint equivalence is defined by selected measurements and margins, not complete functional or geometric identity. The follow-up extends the intervention to 500 updates and finds delayed route differences under fresh Adam and calibrated SGD, including values outside the original ±0.02 margin around updates 50–100, before the selected allocation endpoints become close by update 500. This does not establish equality of complete model states. Coordinate-wise v exchange is interpretable because both recipients descend from a common checkpoint with corresponding parameter coordinates; it does not identify a representation-invariant geometry of optimizer memory. The update-history control changes weight variability and realized order together. Other optimizers, separately controlled variance and order, and different bottleneck architectures could change which persistent component matters. Three-target formation alone does not answer these questions. The immediate generalization problem is therefore structured: determine which current controls and persistent states predict specialization and response when architecture, target structure, or training horizon changes. Testing larger models on natural, multi-task data would require verifying that the same intervention contrasts remain meaningful and that the probes measure comparable information. Within the current scope, formation, present support, and latent susceptibility are distinguishable experimental objects. 9

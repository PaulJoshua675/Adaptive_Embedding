Idea2Prior extends PaperArena with an auditable workflow for retrieving and ranking papers from research ideas. Joint ranking combines an abstract-level shortlist with bounded, explicitly partial PDF evidence, while a retrieval-only mode avoids report-specific work. In the documented historical comparison, aggregate reported Hit@5 is 53.3%, exceeding the two browser baselines overall, with a clear HARD-tier exception and longer reported latency. A separate historical cost sample averages $0.0945 per query. These findings motivate focused scholarly retrieval with inspectable evidence; they do not establish general Deep Research superiority, calibrated novelty assessment, or a matched baseline cost advantage.

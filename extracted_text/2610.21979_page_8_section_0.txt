Generality across initialization, scale, and backbone Table 4 complements the primary Qwen3.5-2B/700K results with three additional SFT initialization settings: Qwen3.5-2B/50K, Qwen3.5-4B/50K, and Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct/700K. Within each setting, all RL methods share the starting checkpoint and 100-update budget, with five runs using distinct RL data seeds. The table reports means on the same six benchmarks and 13 splits as Table 1; Appendix C.4 provides standard deviations and model-setting details. In the three additional model and initialization settings evaluated, P2TPO achieves the highest mean in 37 out of 39 split-setting comparisons. This includes all 13 comparisons with Qwen3.5-4B/50K, and P2TPO also outperforms GSPO in all 39 cases. These results demonstrate its effectiveness across the tested initial policies, model scales, and backbones. Full details, including means, standard deviations, and split-specific comparisons, can be found in the Appendix C.4. 4.5. Hyperparameter sensitivity and extended training Hyperparameter sensitivity. Across one-at-a-time sweeps, all configurations stay within 0.95 points of the default on each split and exceed the strongest response-level baseline on at least 11 of 13 splits, indicating robustness within the tested ranges (Table 10, Appendix C.5). Extended training horizon. The 500-update study uses Qwen3.5-2B/50K, 64,000 RL records, and five runs per method, with OmniDocBench v1.6 evaluated every ten updates and the other 12 splits every 50. At update 500, P2TPO attains the highest mean on all 13 splits.

Standard contrastive pre-training treats all off-diagonal image–report pairs as negatives, which can introduce se- mantic conflicts when reports share findings. We pro- posed Semantic-Conflict-Aware Contrastive Learning (SCCL), which uses modality-specific adaptive thresholds to identify candidate conflicts and removes them from con- trastive normalization without relabeling them as positives. Distillation consolidates the resulting rule-specific super- vision into a single deployable model.

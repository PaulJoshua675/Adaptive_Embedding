D.1 RQ1 Extended Results Precision at K curves. Table 9 reports precision at multi- ple rank cutoffs for all methods, providing a more complete view of ranking quality beyond P@50. Following the main- text protocol, candidate edges from all 30 repositories are merged into a single score-ranked list, and P@K is the frac- tion of gold dependency edges among the top K entries of that pooled ranking. Because the pooled list is scored glob- ally, P@K is quantized to multiples of 100/K percentage points. WEAVE attains the highest precision at every cutoff, and no single baseline is consistently second: Traceability Links is the strongest alternative at K ≤20, where its explicit cross- PR references are almost always genuine, but it exhausts its recoverable edges early (Coverage = 12.8%) and falls below Symbol Overlap by K =50. This crossover is precisely why neither signal suffices alone. Pooled P@K and per-repository AP (reported in the RQ1 table of the main text) measure different properties and need not agree. AP is computed within each repository and then macro-averaged, so it is insensitive to whether scores are comparable across repositories; pooled P@K merges all repositories into one ranking and therefore additionally rewards cross-repository score calibration.

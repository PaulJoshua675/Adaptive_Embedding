Because Task D uses 64 trials and a 3,600-step horizon, we use this exper- iment to establish representation transfer rather than as a protocol-matched state-versus-RGB comparison. 4) Distilled policy.: The planner’s cost is its inference: 1,024 proposals rolled through the world model at every replan. Distilling it into a policy removes that cost, and the world model makes the distillation data free of any simulator: at 54K recorded states we paired 24 goals each, drawn uniformly in angular distance, with the chunk the planner selects, producing 1.3M labels entirely inside the world model. No new interaction is collected, no physics engine needed, so the same recipe applies universally for simulation and real-world, and the data scales with sampling compute. The distilled policy is accordingly the strongest imitation baseline at 40.6/72.7/86.7% SR@0.4/0.6/0.8 rad (Figure 5(a)): it recovers over 90% of the MPC planner’s performance at 0.8 rad and takes only 21.8 ms per run, 7x faster than an MPC replan, a good balance between efﬁciency and precision.

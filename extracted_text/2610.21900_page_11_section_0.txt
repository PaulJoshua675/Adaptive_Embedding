For each backbone, we kept the answer prompt, zero temperature, seed 42 and 1,024-token answer limit fixed across configurations. Qwen thinking was disabled. Inputs to the long-context reference were truncated at the 32,768-token model window; this affected 1,151 questions per backbone. We used gpt-oss-20b to score factual and temporal correctness against the reference answers and supporting turns. Ambiguity in the reference information left 216 answers unresolved. We retained the full question count as the denominator and assigned these cases zero or one to obtain score bounds. Eleven truncated or empty answers counted as incorrect. Under the same retrieval budget, Llama scored 27.53–29.22% with TEM and 17.92–19.61% with session-aware RAG (Table 7). Qwen scored 35.84–37.53% and 23.70– 25.26%, respectively. Even subtracting the RAG upper bound from the TEM lower bound left gaps of 7.92 and 10.58 percentage points.

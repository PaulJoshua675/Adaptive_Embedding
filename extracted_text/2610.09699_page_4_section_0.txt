Algorithm 1 presents a simplified pseudocode description. Certain specifics, such as the exact computation of the recombination weights w, the learning rates, and the parameter hσ that is used to stall the update of the covariance evolution path when the step size becomes too small, are omitted for clarity. The interested reader is referred to the tutorial by Hansen (2016) for implementation details. The pseudocode is structured around five functionally distinct components: restarting, sampling, selection and recombination, step-size adaptation, and covariance (or matrix) adaptation. These components are highlighted by colored blocks in Algorithm 1. Importantly, while these compo- nents interact through shared state variables, they are conceptually separable and admit multiple alternative implementations, facilitating a modular approach. 4

F.3 Hard Rebalancing with Lion Lion (Chen et al., 2023) uses sign-based updates. Positive-scale invariance of its sign direction does not imply orbit equivariance of its parameter trajectory (Theorem 5). We test whether hard SAGGS protects Lion training under an exact orbit tilt. Design. The protocol uses the medium ReLU Transformer with L = 24, d = 512, 8 heads, and 101M parameters, WikiText-103 with GPT-2 BPE, context length 512, and three paired seeds. We compare Vanilla and SAGGS at λ = 0.2, applied to Q–K, V–O, and ReLU up–down pairs at initialization. Training uses 15K Lion steps with learning rate 2 × 10−4, β = (0.9, 0.99), weight decay 0.1, batch size 32, gradient clipping at 1.0, and BF16 mixed precision. The cosine schedule uses 1.5K warmup steps and decays to 10% of peak learning rate. SAGGS rebalances all three pair types every 100 steps. Results. All three λ = 0.2 pairs favor SAGGS, with ¯∆Vanilla−SAGGS = +52.896 ± 31.543 PPL, paired Cohen’s d = 1.68, and paired t = 2.90 (Table 8). With n = 3, the one-sided exact sign-flip p-value floor is 0.125.

Va- CoAl/PyVaCoAl’s path valuation is tacit for the analogous reason: it follows from the physical facts of the memory—finite depth per block, residual collisions, the multiplicative structure of CR2. The “body” also matters for what the memory comes to prefer. Under a fixed total capac- ity N · 2m = 234, a configuration with N = 128 settles on a Leibniz-dominant lineage, N = 256 on an alternative, Gauss-dominant one, and N = 512 purifies the Leibniz–Euler line and alone reaches the full depth of 57 generations (2026d, Appendix F). Different bodies sense the same history differently, and each sensing is historically defensible. Like a cyclist’s balance, none of this is stored as an item, and all of it is explained from outside in closed form. 5.3 Collective: tacit because it lives in the community The architecture does not know which lineage matters. The Minsky paper is explicit that evaluative self-assessment requires value binding the architecture does not have, and places Minsky’s Self-Reflective layer out of scope (2026f). There is a reflexive point here. Our own claim that the CR2 ranking is “historically valid”—that the mainstream of mathematics ought to rise to the top 20—is itself a judgement drawn from the collective tacit knowledge of historians and mathematicians. Even the validation of VaCoAl/PyVaCoAl’s somatic-like valuation depends on collective knowledge held outside it. Section 8 builds on this: the community is not an optional add-on to the system but one of its layers. 5.4 A biological precedent for keeping both readouts The hippocampal paper describes two regimes (2026b). Regime A, the entorhinal–CA3 route, is a scaffolded vector-index substrate in which similarity and attractor completion dominate. Regime B, the trisynaptic route from entorhinal cortex through the dentate gyrus and mossy fibres to CA3, supports sparse, directed relational writes—edge-list or ontology-like commitments—gated by neuromodulation and extended by adult neurogenesis. The brain does not partition cleanly: both regimes contribute to both kinds of query, with task-dependent gain set by acetylcholine and dopamine, and the silicon rescue flag is only a readout-statistics analogue of the biological distinction (2026b). Both pathways have been conserved for more than 520 million years (2026b).

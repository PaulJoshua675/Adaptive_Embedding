Archive composition matters even when replay is used. Finally, we replace the elite archive with IID samples while keeping the replay ob- jective unchanged (IID replay). Although IID replay achieves comparable pass@k, it produces fewer distinct correct strategies at every sampling budget, reaching U@128 of 7.4 compared to 9.1 for ELITEREPLAY (Table 2). Because IID sam- ples overrepresent the base model’s frequent ap- proaches, replaying them offers less retention of the rarer strategies that RL suppresses. These ab- lations show that preserving diversity depends on both constructing an archive with broad strategy coverage and continuing to replay it throughout RL. 6

E.3 Update and preservation accuracy Figure 4 classifies valid incorrect predictions by whether their errors affect changed cells, unchanged cells, or both. Table 14 complements this classification with the fraction of each cell group predicted correctly. It averages per-instance cell accuracies over valid answers at T = 16, 32, 64, including exact states. At these lengths, every model has lower accuracy on final-changed cells than on final-unchanged cells. This is compatible with the high prevalence of the both category in Figure 4: group accuracy measures the fraction of values predicted correctly, whereas the partition records whether any error occurs in each group. Even a prediction with one wrong changed cell and one wrong unchanged cell belongs to both, regardless of how many other cells it gets right.

In summary, the analysis indicates that optimisation algorithms signifi- cantly improve the performance of FGWC, particularly in correctly forming the desired number of clusters. Meanwhile, FPA, GSA, and TLBO emerge as the most reliable across all scenarios. IFA also tends to be reliable, but it showed failure in 6 clusters of cases 2. On the other hand, ABC shows potential in significantly different dimensions and observations (Cases 1 and 3), whereas PSO is preferable for cases with lower dimensions (Case 2 and 3). In contrast, HHO exhibits limited reliability, performing well only in case 2.

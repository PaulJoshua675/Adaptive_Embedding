Span coefficients add one forward through πold and require no additional model calls or image renders. Because the rewriter jointly learns structural formatting via rfmt, it achieves over 99.2% exact span-to-token alignment across all training rollouts (detailed in Appendix A.4). For the negligible fraction of unaligned rollouts (< 0.8%), defaulting the modulation weight to wg,i,s = 1 guarantees uninterrupted optimization stability. 4 Experiments 4.1 Experimental Setup Benchmarks. We evaluate compositional prompt adherence on GenEval [13] and T2I-CompBench [18]. GenEval tests object presence, counting, color, position, and attribute binding. T2I-CompBench covers attribute binding, object relationships, and complex composition. GenEval training uses 10,000 of the 50,000 prompts organized by Flow-GRPO [28], while T2I-CompBench uses the 4200 prompts in its official training split. Both native benchmark evaluation sets are disjoint from the training data. Baselines. GRPO with uniform token weights is our primary baseline and shares SCOPE’s reward pipeline and training setup. Other baselines render original prompts directly or use the Z-Image rewriter [4] without further training. We reproduce the published RePrompt [55] training and evaluation recipe on Qwen-Image with the same reward clients, rendering seeds, and benchmark harness as our runs. For SD3 and FLUX.1-dev, we use the published RePrompt scores. Appendix A.10 documents the reproduction and score sources. Implementation details. We train Qwen3-8B [59] as the prompt rewriter with frozen Qwen-Image [53], SD3 [11], and FLUX.1-dev [3] generators. We use a learning rate of 10−6, a KL coefficient of 0.01, and a maximum completion length of 512 tokens. Each group contains N = 8 prompt members, including the original prompt and sampled rewrites. All members share one rendering seed to control variation from image generation within the group. Appendix A.2 includes optimization configuration and span weight normalization. Evaluation protocol. Each backbone uses fixed resolution, denoising steps, and classifier-free guidance during training and evaluation (Appendix A.3). We average scores from four images per GenEval prompt and ten per T2I-CompBench prompt with distinct randomly selected seeds. Only training groups share seeds. 6

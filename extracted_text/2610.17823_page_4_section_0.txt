The key difference is that the probability mapping is now both sparse and prior-aware. The resulting training procedure can be interpreted as follows (see Figure 1). First, logits are adjusted using the additive prior correction 𝛾log𝑞𝑖. The adjusted logits are then scaled by the pa- rameter 𝜃, which acts as a temperature parameter in softmax-based models [2]. Finally, the sparse transformation in (10) is applied, and optimization is performed using the corresponding Fenchel-Young loss (13). The final objective combines sparse context-dependent compe- tition with explicit popularity-aware regularization.

GQA shares each key and value head across a group of query heads, so the Q–K and V–O rescalings use one factor per group. Grouped-query attention. GQA (Ainslie et al., 2023) shares one W K and one W V across H/G query heads, where G is the group count. The Q–K orbit becomes group-tied: Any rescaling W Q ℓ,h 7→cW Q ℓ,h for the H/G query heads in a group must be paired with a single W K ℓ,g 7→c−1W K ℓ,g for the group’s shared key. The dimension of the orbit is reduced from H scalars per layer in the multi-head case to G scalars per layer; the gradient covariance in Eq. 2 applies to each group-pair independently. The per-pair Frobenius rebalance of SAGGS extends by treating each group’s H/G Q heads and shared K head as one balancing unit: The Q-side norm is (P h∈g W Q ℓ,h 2 F)1/2.

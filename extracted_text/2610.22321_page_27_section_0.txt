D.4 DIMENSION CORRESPONDENCE ANALYSIS To verify whether the three predicted scores correspond to their intended consistency dimensions, we perform a dimension correspondence analysis based on human-annotated issue labels. Instead of directly measuring the correlation between predicted dimension scores and human di- mension scores, we formulate this analysis as a dimension-specific issue classification task. Human dimension scores are mainly designed for overall perceptual evaluation, and the three dimensions may have different levels of granularity. Therefore, compared with continuous scores, human issue labels provide a more direct signal for evaluating whether each predicted dimension corresponds to its intended consistency dimension. Specifically, we construct dimension-specific binary targets from human-annotated issue labels in the benchmark, where samples are separated into positive and negative cases according to whether they contain the corresponding types of consistency issues. We then use the three predicted scores from Ours as input features and evaluate a classifier with cross-validated AUC.

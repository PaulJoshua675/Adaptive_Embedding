The rewriter trained with SCOPE spells out each constraint explicitly: the vertical layout (“couch lower, cup upper”), one object per position (“four handbags in a neat row. . . each on a white pedestal”), and the counterfactual color. Because SCOPE credits complete semantic spans during training, the rewriter learns to keep each constraint as an intact phrase rather than scattering it across subword tokens. This lifts a frozen open-weight Qwen-Image to the level of the commercial gpt-image 2 on layout and counting.

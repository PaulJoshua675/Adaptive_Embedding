Physics-aware training instead runs the forward pass physically but the backward pass through a digital model of the substrate, and pays for digital computation at every training step (Wright et al., 2022). Both pay a digital cost the physics does not. The correspondence. We show that the physical dynamics of a predictive coding network can compute −∇xVθ inherently. Build a PCN from the same layers, one prediction error per node, and add a single linear term of strength γ on the scalar output node, giving an energy EPCN. The states of this PotentialPCN relax to an equilibrium at which −∇xEPCN = γ (−∇xVθ) + O(γ2), so dividing by γ recovers the velocity, exactly in the limit of small γ (Section 3.1). Generation without a backward pass. An Energy Matching model trained conventionally by backpropagation can therefore be loaded into this network unchanged and sampled by relaxation, so that a substrate hosting it needs no backward pass anywhere in the generation path. At CIFAR-10 and ImageNet-32 scale the velocities agree with autograd’s to a cosine similarity of 1 to nine decimal places, and the FID of the generated samples matches to within 0.04 (Section 4.1). No retraining is required, so the energy and latency of generation — most of a deployed model’s lifetime cost — could be cut on analog hardware without any change to how the model is trained. Training without a backward pass. Training can move on-chip as well. The PCN relaxes on an explicit energy, so it can be trained by Equilibrium Propagation (Scellier & Bengio, 2017), which reads parameter updates −∇θL from local measurements at equilibrium and so needs none of the global transport that makes backpropagation awkward in analog. We use the GradEP variant (Gower, 2026), which trains gradients of energy landscapes — here the velocities Energy Matching generates with. We call the resulting procedure PotentialEP. Its parameter updates reproduce backpropaga- tion’s to a cosine similarity of 0.9998 at our operating point (Section 4.2), and a model trained end to end with those updates reaches an FID of 3.64 on unconditional CIFAR-10, against 3.54 for back- propagation (Section 5). To our knowledge this is the first CIFAR-10 generative model trained with Equilibrium Propagation, and the first at competitive quality by any backpropagation-free rule. The architecture. A backpropagation-free algorithm is of little use if the architecture it trains is itself hard to realise in analog, and two components of the one we inherit from Energy Matching are: group normalisation, which couples every channel through a shared statistic, and attention, whose pairwise products are not a fixed weighted sum. We find that removing both costs nothing measurable at this scale — a network of only convolutions, pointwise nonlinearities and UNet skips, every operation of the kind an analog crossbar performs natively, reaches a CIFAR-10 FID of 3.51 (Section 5.1). 2

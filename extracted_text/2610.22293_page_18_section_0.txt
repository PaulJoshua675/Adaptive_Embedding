B.2 Two-stage continual learning with SDFT Figure 12 adds plain SDFT to the same two-stage setup as Figure 9. SDFT improves target learning over SFT in all three chains, but its retention is chain-dependent. It reduces forgetting on Science →Pathology and Pathology →FinQA, yet loses 20.0 points on FinQA →Science. SST+R10 gives the most consistent retention–learning trade-off. For Pathology →FinQA, we regrade the saved SDFT generations by prioritizing the final boxed numeric answer before the existing Answer: fallback. This corrects an evaluator-format mismatch, giving 75.2 target accuracy (+4.7 over base); the source-task score, and therefore the 5.6 points of forgetting, is unchanged.

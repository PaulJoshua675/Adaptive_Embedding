2.1 Feed-forward 3D reconstruction Recent years have witnessed rapid progress in feed-forward 3D reconstruc- tion, where models directly infer geometry from one or multiple images with- out test-time optimization. Early learning-based MVS systems[5][6][7][8][9] typically relied on explicit cost-volume construction and geometric reason- ing, while more recent foundation-style approaches shift the emphasis toward correspondence learning with large-capacity backbones and transformers[10] [11][12][13][14]. In particular, transformer-based feed-forward methods such as VGGT[1] represent scenes as collections of image patch tokens and per- form cross-view reasoning through attention, achieving strong reconstruction quality and scalability. Along this line, π3[13] further explores efficient multi- view reasoning and generalization under diverse camera configurations, while mapping-oriented systems such as MapAnything[14] aim to produce geom- etry or map representations at scale from in-the-wild imagery, emphasizing robustness and transfer. In parallel, large-scale monocular depth pretraining (e.g., DepthAnything v3[15]) provides strong single-view geometric priors that can serve as initialization or auxiliary supervision for multi-view re- construction and downstream 3D perception tasks.

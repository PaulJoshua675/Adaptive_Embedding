Preprint • Complementary representations for time-series reasoning. We establish complemen- tary representation as a design principle for TSLMs, motivated by the observation that quantitative readout requires the original measurement frame, whereas reasoning about temporal dynamics benefits from factoring it out. • A dual-view time-series language model. We propose a dual-tower architecture that combines line plots and normalized delay embeddings within a shared language model. Dedicated visual encoders, compact visual tokens, and multi-level feature fusion integrate temporal structure with the original measurement frame. • State-of-the-art TSLM performance. With an 8B backbone, our model outperforms all evaluated dedicated TSLMs on TSRBench, achieving 53.2% accuracy versus 36.7% for the strongest baseline. It also leads the evaluated models on TSExam and multivariate ChatTS numerical alignment, achieving 89.9% and 86.8% accuracy, respectively.

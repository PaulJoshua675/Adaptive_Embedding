Importantly, 3DCodeVerse Harness serves not only as an inference system but also as a data- generation engine. By recording successful programs, failed attempts, targeted repairs, and preference comparisons, its execution traces provide supervision for both supervised fine-tuning and preference learning. Together, 3DCodeVerse-2M and 3DCodeVerse Harness establish not only a reusable in- frastructure for 3D coding, but also a closed loop connecting data construction, program synthesis, verification, refinement, and model improvement. We further support this ecosystem with open models and reproducible training recipes. Fine-tuning on 3DCodeVerse-2M substantially improves executable 3D code generation, reaching execution rates of 84.9% with a 9B model and 92.9% with a 27B model on 3DCodeBench. Moreover, 3DCodeVerse Harness consistently improves generation quality across coding agents, yielding gains of 15.5–29.1 points over one-shot generation. 2

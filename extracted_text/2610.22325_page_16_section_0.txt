A.2 THUMAN2.1 We train on 2,200 of the 2,445 textured THuman2.1 (Yu et al., 2021b) scans, holding out every tenth scan. Each scan is rendered from 50 cameras on a jittered orbit: elevation [−10◦, 25◦], log-uniform vertical field of view [25◦, 90◦], off-centre aim and projected extent of 55 to 85 % of image height. Images are 1280 × 720 with panorama backgrounds sampled by camera ray. RGB and labels share the same rasterisation. A.3 MAMMASYN We train on 15,710 person tracks in 7,856 views from MammaSyn, MAMMA’s synthetic data family (Cuevas-Velasquez et al., 2026). We retain cameras pitched downwards by at least 15◦ and every fourth frame. MammaSyn complements BEDLAM2’s predominantly eye-level views and limited interactions with elevated views and interacting people, but its photorealistic SMPL-X renderings represent clothing only as texture, without separate garment or hair meshes. B IMPLEMENTATION AND TRAINING B.1 ARCHITECTURE AND VIRTUAL CAMERAS A 4 →1024 →1024 MLP with GELU activation embeds the crop geometry. The ViT image encoder uses DINOv3-L (Siméoni et al., 2025) with patch size 16. Each 512 × 512 crop supplies 1,024 patch tokens of width 1,024, which are joined by one camera token, 16 register tokens and the crop token. Separate learned camera and register tokens identify the first frame as the reference; the remaining frames share a second set. Camera tokens are internal features, with no camera-prediction head or camera-regression loss. There are 24 pairs of frame and inter-frame attention blocks. Five inter-frame blocks (indices 2, 6, 9, 14 and 20, zero-indexed) update only the camera, register and crop tokens; patch tokens pass through these steps unchanged. The remaining 19 inter-frame blocks attend across all tokens. Both dense heads use features from blocks 4, 11, 17 and 23 (zero-indexed), projected to resolutions 1282, 642, 322 and 162, then fused by a 256-channel RefineNet decoder (Lin et al., 2017) and pixel-shuffled to 5122. HPM coordinates have no output activation. The heads run in fp32 and the backbone in bf16; each head has 32.3 M parameters. 16

B Additional Mechanistic Diagnostics These diagnostics examine how the proposed modules encode edge orientation, adapt directional context aggregation across node roles, and distribute attention over complementary structural relations. The mechanistic diagnostics use the second training budget of each task, while the accompanying phase-parameter experiment uses the lowest training budget. The mechanistic diagnostics characterize internal module behavior and complement the main results and ablations rather than directly attribute performance gains. The Magnetic phase diagnostic checks operator-level orientation sensitivity, while the MoE and attention diagnostics describe task-dependent routing and attention allocation over predefined relation channels. The following subsections provide the definitions, numerical results, and per-module views corresponding to Fig. 3. B.1 Magnetic Phase Sensitivity and Parameter Analysis Graph-scaled phase. Equation (2) presents the Magnetic Laplacian in its standard form. In the reported graph-scaled setting, the shared parameter q is converted to the effective phase parameter qG = q/cG, where cG = max{1, min(m, n)} and n and m are the numbers of nodes and edges in the encoded graph. For a directed edge u →v, substituting qG into Eq. (2) gives conjugate phase factors ei2πqG and e−i2πqG, whose separation is ei2πqG −e−i2πqG = 2 |sin(2πqG)| . (10) The factors are +i and −i when the effective parameter qG = 0.25. With graph scaling, the nominal setting q = 0.25 generally gives qG̸ = 0.25, so phase separation depends on both q and the graph scale cG. Edge-reversal diagnostic. We quantify sensitivity to reversing all edges by Sq(G) = ∥eLq(A) −eLq(A⊤)∥F ∥eLq(A)∥F . (11) Here, eLq denotes the normalized Magnetic Laplacian constructed with the effective phase qG defined above. Larger values indicate stronger sensitivity to edge reversal. At q = 0, removing the phase term gives zero edge-reversal sensitivity in all four settings. At q = 0.25, the reported mean sensitivity is 0.2398 on NAS-Bench-101 and 0.2065 on NAS-Bench-201 for both prediction targets.

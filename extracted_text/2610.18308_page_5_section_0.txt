The inferred attributes and derived pickiness are combined with the Stage 2 statistical profile to form the enriched profile that conditions user simulation in Stage 6. 3.5 User Simulation Stage 6 (User Simulation). The enriched profile of each user conditions Gemini 2.5 Flash (Comanici et al., 2025) to sim- ulate that user’s behavior in conversation with a CRS powered by GPT-4o-mini (Achiam et al., 2023). Each user is simulated in four conversations varying the opening strategy across aspect, movie, mood, and expertise prompts. After each CRS turn, starting from the fourth, an LLM termination judge classifies the last exchange as true acceptance (the user explicitly commits to a specific recommenda- tion), resigned acceptance, explicit give-up, other, or continue; any label other than continue ends the conversation, which is otherwise capped at eight CRS turns. 4

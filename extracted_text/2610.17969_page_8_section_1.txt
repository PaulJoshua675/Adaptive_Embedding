Our NDA framework establishes a typing principle for scale management across the computation graph. The NDA rules unify the scale-control mechanisms used in Transformers, including normalization, resid- ual scaling, attention temperature, loss weighting, and parameter updates. UVI diagnostics and the weight- space audit make these rules testable and actionable by locating scale mismatches and checking the rescaling behavior predicted by NDA. GCT implements these scale-control rules in a Transformer architecture, and the typed multi-loss objective makes each loss dimen- sionless before combining the terms. Experiments span- ning 51M to 1B parameters show that GCT achieves learning-rate and depth transfer, internal-rescaling in- variance, and improved residual-stream quantization robustness while retaining competitive clean validation performance. Vision and PINN studies support the use of NDA beyond language modeling. These results demonstrate the benefits of explicit scale management grounded in NDA for neural network design.

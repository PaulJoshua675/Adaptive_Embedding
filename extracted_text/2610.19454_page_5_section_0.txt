The upper panel shows delivery execution, and the lower panel shows role-policy updates. 4.1 Role Specialization and Collaborative Execution Account budget planning, ad-unit delivery strategy adjustment, and end-of-period diagnosis differ in their scope of influence and feedback timing. AdCoEvo accordingly assigns these responsibilities to Planner, Optimizer, and Inspector and maintains a separate policy πθr for each role, where r ∈{P, O, I} and θr denotes the role’s model parameters. This organization associates execution records for different responsibilities with their corresponding training feedback. Planner and Optimizer make decisions for one account and its ad units at a time, whereas Inspector aggregates period outcomes across all controlled accounts. After observing W time steps, Planner uses observed delivery performance to allocate the account’s remaining budget, outputting a proportion αj for each ad unit. Unit j then has total budget limit bj = cpre j + Rαj, (4) Here, cpre j is the cumulative spend of ad unit j before planning, R = B −P j∈U cpre j is the account’s remaining budget, and B and U denote the account’s total budget and set of ad units, respectively. The allocation proportions satisfy αj ≥0 and P j∈U αj = 1. After budget planning, Optimizer determines at every remaining time step whether each ad unit in the account needs a delivery strategy adjustment. Ad-unit actions include HOLD and a multiplier adjustment relative to the initial tCPA. If an adjustment is selected and the unit’s number of valid adjustments in the current period has not reached K, Optimizer selects κj from the available multiplier set K and updates the control tCPA to bdj = κjdj, (5) Here, dj is the environment-provided initial tCPA. The current control value is retained for HOLD or when valid adjustments reach K.

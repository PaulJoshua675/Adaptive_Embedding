A thread-level joint model of answer volume and best-answer designation would test on a single unit the trade-off this design can only describe as two profiles. Extending the feature set beyond nouns — sentiment, register, politeness, ques­ tion type, and concreteness norms applied to the original Japanese — would connect these lexical associations to the pragmatic and social dimensions of cQA participation. Finally, pairing yearly cluster prevalence with a chronology of logistics-affecting events would address whether external events reshape which content attracts engagement, a question the present cross-sectional design cannot answer.

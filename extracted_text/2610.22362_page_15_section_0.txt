Limitations CoDA relies on foundation models for scene generation, failure diagnosis, and potential compilation. Errors in these models’ understanding of objects, physical interac- tions, or task requirements can affect the resulting scenes and training signals. Physical validity checks and control experiments help detect such errors, but their coverage depends on the checks and interventions used. Results may also vary with changes to the underlying models, making model versions and configurations important for reproducibility. The framework requires additional sim- ulation and model inference to generate environments, evaluate policy rollouts, and validate diagnoses. These costs constrain the number of rounds and task families that can be explored under a fixed budget. Our evalu- ation also focuses on simulated manipulation. Transfer to physical robots remains to be established, particularly for tasks sensitive to differences in contact dynamics, sensing, and actuation. 15

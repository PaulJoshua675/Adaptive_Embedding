We also report Kendall’s τ between guard rankings by balanced accuracy, flips by restatement-leakage stratum (whether ≥60% of the instruction’s content words appear in the history or Thought; 59.6% of steps), and per-instruction block-rate changes. 4 Results 4.1 Translating the request changes verdicts and error rates Figure 1 shows ∆TPR and ∆FPR for every guard and language with bootstrap intervals; Table 1 lists every cell with significant ones in bold. Translation changes up to 12.1% of a guard’s verdicts (ShieldAgent on Swahili, +10.5 pp over its placebo; Nemotron 3 Nano 4B on Amharic 11.1%), and 39 of the 77 guard×language cells (17 guards, excluding the scanner control and the degenerate Gemma 4 E2B) show a significant shift in FPR or TPR. Two patterns hold across all five languages, and they split the roster by size. Mid-sized open guards over-block. No guard of 12B parameters or more loses recall in any language; every significant shift in that range is in the false-positive rate, and the large ones are increases: Nemotron 3.5 30B-A3B (+4.7 pp German, +4.1 Turkish, +5.0 Amharic), Gemma 4 26B- A4B (+1.1 German, +2.9 Turkish; its self-hosted copy +2.4 Turkish, +1.2 Swahili, +3.6 Amharic), Gemma 4 12B (+2.0 Turkish, +2.8 Amharic), Llama 4 Maverick (+1.8 German, +1.4 Turkish).

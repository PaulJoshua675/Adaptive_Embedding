Adding the prefill does not help. On Llama it leaves the honest rate unchanged, and on Qwen it lowers it from 85.2% to 68.8%, while the ambiguous rate rises to 21.0%, the no-belief share to 34.9%, and accuracy falls to 46.4%. StTP reaches 75.0% and 69.5%, below the honest persona on both models. The fine-tuning variants separate the contribution of each term in Eq. 3. Fine-tuning on the toward targets alone (SFT-t) reaches 55.4% and 59.5%, and adding utility data (SFT-tu) leaves this essen- tially unchanged. Adding the away loss (SFT-tau) produces the largest gain, raising the honest rate to 77.3% and 87.6%. The adversarial perturbation in CATCH adds a further 8.7 points on Llama and 3.1 on Qwen, and lowers the dishonest rate from 18.6% to 11.9% and from 9.3% to 4.2%. The two CAPO losses are nearly indistinguishable on MASK, at 87.9% and 86.5% honest on Llama and 77.2% and 77.3% on Qwen. On Llama both match CATCH, but both raise the no-belief share, from 11.9% to 17-19% on Llama and from 17.7% to 28-30% on Qwen, and lower accuracy by 7 to 13 points.

Effect-size thresholds. We use the conventional Cohen thresholds adapted to the paired design (Cohen, 2013; Lakens, 2013): |d| < 0.2 negligible, 0.2 ≤|d| < 0.5 small, 0.5 ≤|d| < 0.8 medium, |d| ≥0.8 large, |d| ≥2.0 very large. The PPL effect at λ = 0.2, n = 7 on the 101M medium model is d ≈+14.2 in the primary comparison (Sec. 5.2 and Table 2), with all seven paired differences positive. We interpret paired effect sizes together with absolute metric changes and sample size, rather than relying on the p-value alone. Multiple-comparison handling. The primary hypothesis that SAGGS protects the language-model objective under a forward-equivalent λ = 0.2 orbit imbalance is tested by the 101M WikiText-103 paired seven-seed contrast in Sec. 5.2. The 31M modern-architecture seven-seed replication and the Lion and Soft SAGGS seven-seed comparison are primary replications of the architecture, tokenizer, and optimizer variants. Secondary contrasts, including other λ values, scale variants, and workflow diagnostics, provide supporting evidence. Reported p-values are unadjusted unless labeled otherwise; the unadjusted p-values must not be interpreted as family-wise significance claims. The single pre-declared core PPL contrast is separate from these secondary families. Single-seed 1.2B comparison. The 1.2B comparison in Appendix G.12 uses a single seed because of compute constraints. Its training timers are 2,494 to 2,518 seconds per configuration on four H100s, or approximately 2.8 H100-hours per configuration, excluding data and model setup. We report this comparison’s observed differences without an inferred p-value or Cohen’s d (Table 23). The seven-seed 101M, modern-architecture, Lion, and checkpoint-resume comparisons are the primary statistical evidence; the three-seed 123.8M and 296.7M comparisons test transfer across scales. Pre-specification of λ and seed choices. Throughout the paper, pre-specified means fixed in our experiment plan before inspecting the corresponding held-out result; the phrase does not imply external public registration. The stress-test setting λ = 0.2 was selected from the empirical sensitivity observed in initial single-seed exploratory runs and then fixed in advance for all subsequent paired confirmation experiments with λ > 0. The paired seed pools were likewise fixed before the corresponding experiments were executed (Appendix D.2). The main protection result is ¯∆PPL = +0.374±0.026 at λ = 0.2, n = 7, with paired Cohen’s d = 14.21 (Table 2), measured under deliberate reparameterization. At λ = 0, mean differences are small in the core AdamW and selected Soft and Lion comparisons; the three-seed Lion state ablation (Appendix F.5) is the main adverse unperturbed case. SAGGS and Soft SAGGS are orbit-rebalancing steps for exact bilinear symmetries, complementary to the optimizer: Hard SAGGS selects the balanced representative, and Soft SAGGS contracts the distance to that representative in log-ratio coordinates. Deliberate rescaling tests measure sensitivity to orbit position; checkpoint-surgery and stale-state branches test recovery after an edit. Table 5 records the statistical support of each comparison. Paired n = 7 comparisons are the primary evidence; the n = 3 modern-architecture comparisons have a sign-flip floor of 0.125, and the 1.2B run is a single-seed scale check.

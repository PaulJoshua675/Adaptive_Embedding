Credit Assignment for Sequence Generation. GRPO [46] optimizes autoregressive policies without a learned critic and shares an advantage across tokens in each completion. Recent work [9, 22, 25, 35, 37, 44, 48, 51] explores finer credit assignment through eligibility traces, counterfactual evaluation, and estimation over tree structures. Segment Policy Optimization (SPO) [14] partitions reasoning trajectories into segments and estimates distinct advantages for individual segments. This gives credit estimates a granularity between individual tokens and complete trajectories. FIPO [33] uses discounted future policy divergence to reweight token updates according to subsequent trajectory behavior. Our SCOPE retains a single advantage for each completion. Its contribution to token updates is modulated by shared within each semantic span, derived by pooling the policy log-probability shifts between the current and previous policies. Prompt Optimization for T2I Generation. Prompt optimization adapts the textual input to elicit desired outputs from a pretrained T2I generator. Prior work [8, 19, 21, 23, 24, 27, 34, 36, 42, 47, 56, 57] explores search and iterative refinement strategies for improving generation quality or consistency between text and images. Learned prompt adaptation trains language models to transform user inputs into prompts that the generator prefers. Promptist [15] combines supervised initialization with RL driven by image rewards. PAG uses GFlowNets to promote effective and diverse prompt adaptation, while RePrompt uses GRPO and signals for image preference, alignment, and composition to train a rewriter augmented with reasoning [50, 55, 61]. Our SCOPE addresses the optimization objective within this setting. It incorporates the semantic structure of compositional edits into token weighting while retaining a shared advantage for the complete rewrite. Reinforcement Learning for T2I Generation. Reinforcement learning optimizes visual generators by treating image synthesis as a sequential decision process. Prior work [2, 49, 52] optimizes diffusion trajectories through policy gradients driven by image rewards or through preference optimization. Flow-GRPO [10, 28, 32, 45] optimizes denoising trajectories of flow models, while Mask-GRPO [31] treats iterative masked generation as a policy with multiple steps. B2-DiffuRL [17] addresses sparse reward credit assignment within diffusion trajectories through backward progressive training and sampling over branches. Our SCOPE optimizes the prompt rewriting policy through image feedback while keeping the renderer frozen. 3

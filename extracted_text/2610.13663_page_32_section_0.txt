E.2. Decoder catalog and TPOT configuration The same configuration was used in the classic and sequential TPOT modes, which differ in the search strategy but not in the space of admissible solutions, and in the Random Choice sampler described in Section 4.4. The classic mode used a population of 25 and 9 generations, the sequential mode a population of 1 and 249 consecutive generations. The budget of each mode was 250 evaluated candidates per run. Random Choice does not use TPOT as a search strategy: it draws configurations from the same catalog through independent calls to Qwen3-30B- A3B-Instruct-2507 and issues as many calls as are needed to record 250 evaluated decoders. Per run it made 347 calls on average. TPOT selected the best pipeline by mean accuracy in five-fold cross-validation on the training set. After the search was completed, the selected pipeline was retrained on the entire training set and evaluated once on the test set. Test data were not involved in selecting the pipeline structure or its hyperparameters. Mapping between method classes and operators. The catalog is based on the decoder taxonomy from the analyst’s detailed prompt. Some substantive classes map to the same scikit-learn estimator with different metrics or hyperparameters.

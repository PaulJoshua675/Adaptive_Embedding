The lone agent’s persona is drawn from the nine benign personas of the group runs. In the pair, the adversarial agent writes half of all messages; in the group, only a tenth. Yet the group ends only slightly better (Table 9). The av- erage also hides opposite effects. Peers protect GPT-OSS-120B, which ends on the false answer far more often alone (+37.4 points, [15.2, 57.0]). For GEMINI-2.5-FLASH-LITE, the group makes things worse: it ends on the false answer more often among peers than alone. Adding benign agents is therefore no reliable defense: it helps some models, hurts others, and changes little on average.

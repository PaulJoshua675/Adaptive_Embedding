G.4 Selection Metrics and Pooled Results Let aijk = 1 when candidate j of input i satisfies fact k. Let dij = 1 indicate a severe defect and rij = 1 indicate fidelity to the source image. The three scores are sij = 1 7 7 X k=1 aijk, fij = 7 Y k=1 aijk, hij = fij(1 −dij)rij. (G.2) Thus Strict Full requires all seven facts, no severe defect, and source fidelity. The deterministic selection order is Strict Full, full factual success, fact satisfaction, source fidelity, absence of severe defects, and finally candidate key. It returns one complete candidate j⋆ i per input. All reported Oracle criteria are measured on that candidate; facts are never combined across assets.

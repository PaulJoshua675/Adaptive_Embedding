Balancing equalizes the weight norms of a pair, not the norms of its data gradients. Even at Frobenius balance, whether at initialization or right after a SAGGS rebalance, the matrices Aℓand Bℓin a symmetry pair can receive gradients with different magnitudes due to their positions in the computation graph. For instance, W Q ℓ receives gradients that depend on W K ℓ through the attention logit Jacobian, and vice versa. Gradient magnitudes alone do not determine norm drift. AdamW applies weight decay as θ ←(1 −ηω)θ uniformly to all parameters. This uniform decay multiplies both matrices of a pair by the same factor, so a decay step leaves the pair ratio unchanged and cannot restore balance by itself.

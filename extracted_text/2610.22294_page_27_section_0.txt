B.5 Guidance-Strength Sensitivity We examine whether increasing the guidance strength β improves accuracy or instead concentrates resampling weights on fewer samples. Using stored cross-benchmark List Functions–Easy pools with D = 4, we compare J ∈{1, 4} at K = 64 over 64 prompt families per cell, reweighting the same samples for β ∈{0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2}. To describe weight concentration, define the entropy and entropy-based effective fraction of the normalized sample weights ewk(β): Hβ := − K X k=1 ewk(β) log ewk(β), ρeff(β) := exp(Hβ) K . The fraction ρeff measures concentration over sampled draws, counting duplicates separately, rather than diversity among distinct completions. Figure 11 compares its mean against gains in Aall relative to β = 0. For a single target, every tested β > 0 improves mean Aall for all three DLMs (intervals exclude zero except for LLaDA at β = 2).

We take the Pytorch toolbox and two RTX 3090 Ti GPUs to train our model with a batch size of 64 for 40 epochs, which takes around 19 hours. Besides, we adopt the Adam optimizer with an initial learning rate of 1e-3, which is decayed to 1e-4 at 32 epochs following [19]. Since our model can simultaneously predict the trajectories of multiple agents, we just calculate the loss of trajectories longer than 40 timestamps for a balance between the quantity and quality of training data. We didn’t adopt any data augmentation method in this paper. B. Comparison with State-of-the-Arts Comparison on Single-agent Performance. For fair com- parison, we further apply a simple yet effective ensemble strategy following [36] with the submissions on leaderboard.

(d) DetNO achieves full coverage of all 16 modes across both rings, faithfully preserving the original mode structure. 5 Experiments Implementation Details. We evaluate on three pre-trained T2I backbones: SD1.5 [24], SDXL [22], and SD3 [5]. SD1.5 and SDXL use DDIM [26] with 50 steps, while SD3 uses the Euler sampler with 28 steps following its default configuration. We compare against PG [3], SPELL [14], DiversityPrompt [31], and CNO [13]. We evaluate all methods under identical conditions (prompt set, random seeds, CFG scale, and hardware) to ensure fair relative comparison; absolute values may dif- fer from original papers accordingly. We use the official implementations of all baselines, including CNO, with the hyperparameters reported in their original papers. Following [13, 31], we sample 2,000 prompts from the MS-COCO [18] validation set and generate 5 images per prompt for SD1.5 and SD3, and 3 images per prompt for SDXL. Evaluation Metrics. For image quality, we report Precision [17], Density [19], CLIPScore [10], PickScore [15], and ImageReward [33]. For diversity, we report Recall [17], Coverage [19], Vendi Score [6], and Mean Self-Similarity (MSS) [13]. Among these, VendiScore and MSS serve as our primary diversity metrics, as they measure intra-batch coverage directly without reference to an external dis- tribution. They are therefore better suited to evaluating diversity-enhancing methods than reference-dependent metrics such as Recall and Coverage [20]. 5.1 Results Toy Example: 2D Ring Mixture.

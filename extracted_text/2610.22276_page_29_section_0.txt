For ordinary multi-stage QA, node statistics are averaged across the all- head predicted path. Counting nodes use parent probability mass weights over the visited tree, normalized per question; questions then receive equal weight. The absolute RMS is computed before this aggregation. Relative response shares are normalized within each node, so normalizing the final mean RMS would produce a different statistic.

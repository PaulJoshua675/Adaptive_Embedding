To keep phenotype values comparable, we rank the mixed scores and assign them the same fixed set of standard normal quantiles. The top 5% are designated as hits. All settings therefore share the phenotype distribution and 883 hits, while hit locations range from nearly random at 1.05× mean enrichment to six concentrated clusters at 17.71×. Surrogates receive only candidate coordinates and measured phenotypes. Screening settings. We use 𝑛0 = 128, 𝐵= 32, and 𝑇= 60, giving a total query budget of 2,048. Each landscape setting has five paired repetitions with independent noise and selection seeds 5–9. Methods share the landscape, initial set, and budget within each repetition. The 17.71× landscape contains no noise, so only selection seeds vary in this setting. Model and training settings follow the real tasks. TDAL and PoH retain their empirical hit frequency and Gaussian tail estimators, respectively. The comparison includes TDAL and the eleven baselines in Figure 4, giving 300 runs across five settings. Their mean hit enrichments are 1.05, 2.53, 3.83, 10.00, and 17.71×. Enrichment is computed using ten nearest neighbors in the full pool with Euclidean distances in the fixed coordinates, then averaged over the five realizations. The four snapshots in Figure 4b show the first realization with its own enrichment. Table E2 reports final recall for these same methods and settings, and Figure E1 shows their discovery trajectories. Discovery performance. With nearly random hit locations, mean discovery gains are close to one. Between the least and most clustered settings, TDAL’s mean gain over Random rises from 1.04× to 2.26×, and PoH’s from 1.06× to 1.94×. OAE, UCB, and TS reach gains of 1.62×, 1.62×, and 1.67×, respectively, at the highest enrichment. Thus, clustered hits provide useful information for several acquisition functions, although their gains differ across settings. BADGE and KMeans-Embedding do not improve consistently with clustering, while EI, TopUncertain, and DiscoBAX remain below Random in the four structured settings.

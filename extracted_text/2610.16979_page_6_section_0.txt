We extract high-quality user interactions from WeChat e-commerce search logs collected between June and July 2026 for supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL). The dataset spans 31 days, with the first 30 for training and the last for testing. All offline evaluations and ablation studies use this dataset. The Click and Order test sets each contain 20,000 search interactions with observed positive items. Evaluation Metrics. Following existing generative retrieval stud- ies [2, 3, 9, 31], we evaluate recall and ranking performance using Hit Rate (HR) and Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR). We report results at 𝐾∈{6, 50, 200}, averaged over all evaluation samples. Baseline Methods. We compare WeGR with SID construction and preference optimization baselines. SID construction baselines include RQ-Kmeans-based methods, namely RQ-Kmeans [9], RQ- Kmeans with balancing, and RQ-OPQ [2], as well as RQ-VAE-based methods such as RQ-VAE [16, 25, 32] and R3-VAE [31]. For pref- erence optimization, we compare DPO [24], S-DPO [2, 5], and LDPO [12, 28] as off-policy RL baselines, and vanilla GRPO [27], TPMA-GRPO [3], and HCGRec [35] as on-policy RL baselines. Implementation Details. We adopt GPT-2 Small [23] as the back- bone for both offline evaluation and online deployment due to its computational efficiency and low inference latency. For CoCo-VAE, we use three codebook layers with sizes of (16384, 128, 128). The loss weights 𝜆fid, 𝜆collision, 𝜆FAR, 𝜆Q2I, 𝜆I2I, and 𝜆Rec are set to 0.9, 1.0, 0.4, 0.2, 0.05 and 1.0, respectively. For RL, we assign rewards of 𝑟𝑜= 2 and 𝑟𝑐= 1 to orders and clicks, respectively, and set the MSFT weight to 𝜆= 0.5. We use a GRPO group size of 𝐺= 8 and 𝜂= 300 optimization steps per DHPO stage, with 𝜂adjustable to control stage duration. We update the model daily, performing SFT followed by RL. 4.2 Offline Performance Table 1 summarizes the performance of SID construction and pref- erence optimization methods on the Click and Order datasets. We analyze the results from two aspects. Effectiveness of SID Construction. As shown in Table 1, CoCo- VAE consistently outperforms all SID construction baselines across both Click and Order datasets. Compared with RQ-Kmeans, the strongest SFT baseline, CoCo-VAE improves HR@6 by 4.47% and 1.28% on Click and Order, respectively, with further gains of 4.60% and 1.80% at HR@200.

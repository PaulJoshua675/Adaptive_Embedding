Large language models (LLMs) are becoming increasingly integrated into many people’s daily lives and workflows; in both, harmlessness and honesty matter. Alignment training is meant to pro- vide this, and much work has gone into it. Aligned models, nonetheless, still exhibit dishonest behaviours. These behaviours manifest in different ways. For instance, models hack or take short- cuts to get a higher reward without fulfilling the task’s intention (Skalse et al., 2022).

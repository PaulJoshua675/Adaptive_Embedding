We presented SUPRA-Tex, a framework for single-image 3D generation with super-aligned textures through observation- guided material completion. By separating visible-material estimation and surface alignment from 3D generation and using native observation latents as fixed, read-only anchors, SUPRA-Tex preserves fine reference details while generating coherent, relightable materials across the full surface. Trained solely on public data, it achieves state-of- the-art results among the evaluated methods in geometry-conditioned texturing and end-to-end asset generation. With synthetic back-view conditioning, it is also competitive with commercial systems in human evaluations on the mixed real-and-synthetic comparison set. These results demonstrate the value of spatially aligned, directly usable material observations for reconciling reference fidelity with full-surface generation. 12

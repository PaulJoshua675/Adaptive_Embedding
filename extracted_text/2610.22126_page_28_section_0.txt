It is lower at k = 10 than at any cutoff up to k = 100, peaks at k = 100 in every cell type except NK cells (where it peaks at k = 50), and only then declines, whereas DeepSHAP and integrated gradients are at or near their maximum at k = 10 and decline thereafter. The genes CIG places in its first few dozen positions are therefore less likely to be DEGs than the ones it ranks immediately below them. Taken together, these results are consistent with the following behavior. DeepSHAP and integrated gradients rank the downstream transcriptional response genes first, which lifts their precision and NDCG at shallow cutoffs and gives them a marginally closer alignment with the DE statistic overall.

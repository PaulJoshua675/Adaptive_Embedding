Extrapolating Lopt(C) from the grid vertices predicts L⋆≈2.78 nats; the run reaches L ≈2.76 nats, so the frontier holds out of sample. 4.3 The Parameter-Counting Convention Refitting under all three conventions (Table 1), α moves from 0.75 (strict non-embedding, near Kaplan’s reported 0.73) through 0.62 (FLOP-matmul) to 0.56 (total, near Chinchilla’s 0.5), spanning essentially the entire Kaplan–Chinchilla gap on a single dataset and architecture, at matched fit quality (R2 ≈0.996). This has two consequences. First, small-scale IsoFLOP exponents should not be compared across studies without matching the counting convention. Second, our apparent departure from α = 0.5 is largely a counting artifact: the larger non-embedding exponents reflect the small-scale bias Pearce and Song [2024] predict, not an architectural difference. 4.4 Sensitivity and Cross-Check Details are in Appendix F.

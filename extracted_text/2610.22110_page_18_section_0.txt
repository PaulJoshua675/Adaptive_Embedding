Budget automation also has notification latency, so it is a final circuit breaker rather than a substitute for instance caps, request quotas, and monitoring. 8 Discussion, Limitations, and Reproducibility The experiments show that a mixed-variant checkpoint can provide useful transfer when a second stage redirects it toward one documented variety. Authentic target provenance matters in backtranslation, and symbolic conditioning becomes useful only after analyzer coverage is dense and reconstruction is precise. This does not imply that speakers or models must be linguistically isolated. A future multi- variety system could explicitly condition on community, orthography, or speaker preference rather than aggregating those distinctions without labels. The corpus remains small and pedagogically skewed. Its 192 oral-text sentences cannot represent all genres or speakers, the human evaluation has 20 items with only 2 raters, and DBS is still an illustrative probe of candidate log-probability distributions rather than an absolute metric. Likewise, reaching 72.9% genuine FST segmentation in the lexicon reflects rule coverage rather than verified parsing precision. Future work must validate analyses by morpheme type, especially possession, aspect, and predicate nominals. Full reproducibility requires the deduplicated split manifest, directional JSONL, FST source hash, adapter configuration, selected checkpoint, random seeds, and decoding settings. Sentence-only scores must remain separate from lexical data. Responsible release also requires speaker-approved access conditions for oral texts and a model card that names Masewalkopa as the target variety, documents errors, and provides routes for correction or removal. This is something that will be spoken about and talked with the community before making any data accessible for any other purposes. 9 Conclusion and Future Work This work began with a fundamental dilemma in minoritized language technolo- gies: while aggregating data across regional varieties combats data sparsity in the aggregate, it produces an artificial, blended register that is accountable to no single speaker community.

(4.2) Exposure Estimation. The aim is to capture the global illumination drop between the normal-light image and its low-light counterpart through the ratio of their average intensities. As the real low- light conditions are primarily governed by reduced exposure, and matching this factor ensures phys- ically consistent brightness attenuation. This metric is represented as a ration between µN and µL representing the mean intensities of the reference and low-light images, respectively. E = µL µN . (4.3) Gamma Estimation.

We organize the evaluation around five questions. First, is FutureLift’s proposal-only interface compatible with struc- turally different forecasting backbones? Second, how does it compare with native and specialized refiners when the proposal backbone is controlled? Third, do the gains hold in marginal and joint prediction and on both Argoverse 2 (AV2) and the Waymo Open Motion Dataset (WOMD) af- ter setting-specific training? Fourth, which relations in the lifted future graph are responsible for the improvement? Fifth, how does the temporal resolution of the lifted graph trade forecasting accuracy against refinement latency? We conclude with representative failure cases; qualitative cor- rections are provided in Sec. A.8. Unless otherwise stated, validation results for refiners trained in our study are means over the same three random seeds; metric-wise standard de- viations do not exceed 3 × 10−3.

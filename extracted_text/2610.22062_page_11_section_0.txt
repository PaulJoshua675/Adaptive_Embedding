11 DISCUSSION A dual-LLM consensus pipeline can extract clinically meaningful treatment-response phenotypes from unstructured notes at scale, recovering signal that structured fields miss. Modeling clinical context significantly outperforms naive keyword matching, and what separates the configurations that do so is what the prompt counts as diarrhea rather than how many models are asked. The severe-diarrhea prompt was strongest, but its margin over the deployed prompt is unresolved at this sample size, so we adopt it as a precision-favorable default rather than a proven improvement. It was also selected on the same 183 cells it is scored on, so its accuracy is an in-sample upper bound. The evidence that the design transfers is the half-cohort split and the 90.2% agreement on the other 17 phenotypes, which the same architecture and prompt design reached with only the phenotype definition substituted. The type of error matters more than the headline accuracy. Most diarrhea errors are annotation or data limitations rather than model failures, which tells a deploying institution which discrepancies prompting can fix, which are data-coverage ceilings, and which are annotation-guideline decisions. Design choices and intended use are in Supplementary Note S4, and the architecture comparison is in Note S2. Diarrhea is the most commonly reported adverse event in the pivotal Phase 3 trial, at ∼80% in the treatment arm [5, 26]. Our validated target is narrower, severe rather than any diarrhea, and expert labels put it at 64% of evaluable patients at 6 months and below 20% at every later visit (Supplementary Table S5), so the difference from the trial figure is mostly one of threshold rather than of detection. The later validated timepoints have few evaluable cells, 13 at 24 months and 6 at 30 months, and warrant caution. The main limitation is that only diarrhea has a ground truth. The other 17 phenotypes rest on spot-check review and inter-LLM agreement rather than full annotation (Supplementary Note S1), a resource constraint described in Methods. Their rates are therefore hypothesis-generating lower bounds, and generalization likely varies by phenotype, since concrete adverse effects should transfer more readily than subjective findings. The pipeline detects only what our corpus documents, and some symptoms surface only in other channels. The exposure anchor is adjudicated from note text for most patients rather than taken from a structured record, so window boundaries carry real uncertainty, visible in the 12 displacement errors. The observed trajectories cannot be definitively attributed to trofinetide rather than to concurrent therapies or the natural course of development. The outcome is documented presence and not direction of change or severity, so the pipeline cannot distinguish an improved feature from a persistent one. For the core Rett features, documented in nearly all patients at nearly every visit, detecting within-feature improvement would need severity-graded instruments such as the RSBQ or CGI-I. Documentation does move for other side effects and refusing medication, so the method registers change when change is recorded. All patients come from a single institution, and the pipeline is not yet optimized for efficiency, which cheap pre-filtering, batched calls, and smaller distilled models could improve. Integrating additional sources such as telephone logs and portal messages, and cross-temporal reasoning that propagates retro- spective mentions to the timepoint they describe, would recover symptoms currently missed. Expanding the ground truth by second-reviewer adjudication and by annotation prioritized on inter-LLM disagreement would convert the six-case agreement tiers into per-phenotype accuracy with confidence intervals. Individual feature trajectories, including the apparent seizure rise, need validated per-timepoint annotation before clinical interpretation and are deferred. Direction-of-change extraction is feasible in the same architecture with phenotype-specific prompts and a correspondingly annotated ground truth, but was outside this validation’s scope. The framework extends to other medications and conditions by configuration alone.

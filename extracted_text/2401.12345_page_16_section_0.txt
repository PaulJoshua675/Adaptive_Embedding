Geoffrey Ye Li is currently a Chair Professor at Im- perial College London, UK. He made fundamen- tal contributions to orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM) for wireless communications, established a framework on resource cooperation in wireless networks, and introduced deep learning to communications.

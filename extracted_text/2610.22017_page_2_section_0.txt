In this paper, we design and implement a CAusally guided Retrieval Agent for question an- swering, named CARA, by retrieving causally rel- evant evidence, defined as consequential and es- sential evidence that is directly helpful to answer a query. After global anchoring and during neigh- borhood hop, the retrieval agent invokes a causal reasoning module, asking whether the current hop is consequential and essential for resolving the in- formation need. This reasoning guides the agent’s next decision on whether to continue hopping, to re- anchor globally, or to finish when sufficient support has been found. Our contributions are threefold: (1) we formu- late causal relevance as being consequential and essential, distinguishing answer-bearing evidence from merely textually similar evidence; (2) we in- troduce a training-free prompt-based causal reason- ing module that evaluates the causal relevance from the perspectives of both the current hop and the accumulated path developed so far; and (3) we aug- ment the well-known STaRK benchmark dataset with ground truth answers to the queries (ques- tions), expanding its utility from only evaluating retrieval performance to evaluating both retrieval and question answering performance. 2

Base Models. In the main experiments, we build MaLoW on top of four different models, VideoChat3-4B (Li et al., 2026), Qwen3-VL-4B (Bai et al., 2025), Qwen3.5-9B (Qwen Team, 2026), and MOSS-VL-Realtime- 11B (Wang et al., 2026). VideoChat3 is a video-centric VLM that is further fine-tuned on streaming video understanding data and adopts a full self-attention architecture. Qwen3-VL-4B is a general-purpose VLM based on a full self-attention architecture. Qwen3.5 is a general-purpose VLM based on a hybrid attention architecture, where a self-attention layer is interleaved with every four linear-attention layers. MOSS-VL is a video-centric VLM that adapts cross-attention layers to inject visual information into the text tokens, and a cross-attention layer is interleaved with every four self-attention layers. Video Preprocessing. During training and evaluation, we sample the video frames at 1 fps, dividing the video frames into non-overlapping two-frame temporal blocks (each Ct is a 2-second video clip). We regard the most recent 2-second block as the current observation, which will be sent into the VLMs, while all the rest of the history frames will be sent into the MaLoW to be converted into LoRA weights. For MaLoW (Qwen3-VL-4B), we observe that using longer current-observation windows consistently improves performance on the real-time and backward splits of OVO-Bench (Niu et al., 2025) and StreamingBench (Lin et al., 2026). We therefore use the most recent 8-second clips as the current observations for MaLoW (Qwen3-VL-4B), while the other MaLoW instantiations in the table use the most recent 2 seconds. We hypothesize that these findings, MaLoW (Qwen3-VL-4B) benefiting from longer current-observation windows and the particularly strong performance on StreamingBench, are largely attributable to differences in the architecture and pretraining recipe of the underlying VLMs. We preprocess the video frames with the preprocessor from the corresponding VLMs, with the maximum per-frame pixel budget set to 262,144 for all three versions of MaLoW. 23

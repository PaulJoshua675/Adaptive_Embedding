State-of-the art approaches to retinal vessel segmentation typ- ically require extensive annotations, a highly time-consuming process, reliant on clinical expertise. We present FPG-Seg, an annotation-free pipeline for retinal vessel segmentation com- bining classical vesselness filtering, Bayesian deep learning and topology-aware graph reconstruction to segment retinal vessel topology without manual labels. Tested across four widely used benchmark datasets for retinal vessel segmen- tation, FPG-Seg attains a clDice of 0.74−0.78, representing 86−98% of the performance of an equivalent fully-supervised baseline, despite using no manual annotations for training or hyperparameter tuning. As part of this work, we also pro- vide, to our knowledge, the first performance benchmark of an annotation-free vessel segmentation method on FIVES. Index Terms— Segmentation, Unsupervised, Vessels

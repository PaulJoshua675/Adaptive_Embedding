We further apply TIPSfull after non-adjusted Fast-dLLM has completed its response: four additional decoder evaluations raise test micro-accuracy from 67.01% to 67.29%, correcting five initially incorrect answers without making any initially correct answer incorrect in this run (Appendix B.3.5). 4.4 Ablations We ablate TIPS on a held-out validation set of 512 GSM8K and 512 MATH training examples, reporting micro-averaged accuracy. Its different dataset composition makes these accuracies not directly comparable to test results. Ablations cover supervised warmup, remasking, visibility-aware input features, the choice of and sensitivity to γ, policy architecture and runtime, and warmup and RL decoding budgets.

Limitations Broad validation across reviews, evidence types, and review teams remains incomplete. ReviewPi- lot’s screening procedure and prompt-design skill are refined using scores from the same PERG pools used for evaluation, so the reported gains do not establish performance on unseen reviews. Expert decisions and reference values are excluded from the Prompt Agent’s inputs. Quantitative extraction covers five fields from epidemiological transmis- sion models. The case-study screening audits use small, non-random pools labeled by our research team, and the selected extraction inspections pro- vide qualitative error analysis. Full-text eligibility decisions are not independently audited. We re- port one run per method and model without confi- dence intervals. These estimates do not character- ize variation across repeated runs. Macro-averaged screening metrics do not isolate include-class sen- sitivity, which matters for avoiding missed studies (Cohen et al., 2006; O’Mara-Eves et al., 2015). AgentSLR’s original high reasoning setting is re- placed with medium reasoning or provider defaults. Excerpt verification establishes textual presence, but does not assess semantic support or whether excerpts cover every value in a multi-value field (Gao et al., 2023). Search coverage is limited to PubMed, arXiv, and OpenAlex and constrained by the query and per-source result limits. Full-text extraction de- pends on successful PDF retrieval. Researchers can add PDFs manually, but the reported work- flows do not separately quantify retrieval outcomes by publication access status. The contribution of subscription barriers to unretrieved reports there- fore remains unquantified in the reported results. Access constraints may affect which studies enter extraction and categorization. Assessments based on abstracts or web sources are not separately vali- dated. These sources may lack the evidence needed to populate every field. Future evaluations should use reviews held out from method development, cover further evidence types and extraction schemas, and independently audit full-text eligibility and semantic support for extracted values. Repeated runs should report un- certainty and include-class recall. Prospective stud- ies with review teams are needed to assess reviewer effort and decision quality. Retrieval evaluations should report outcomes by access status and assess how manual PDF submission and fallback assess- ment affect evidence coverage. ReviewPilot also relies on hosted models whose behavior may change across versions (Chen et al., 2024). Its outputs require human verification, and the system is intended to support the indepen- dent review procedures recommended by Cochrane (Higgins et al., 2023).

We next compare rewriting effectiveness across generative engines and elections, and then examine individual strategies. Structure and Argumentation show stronger gains than Ev- idence and Style across engines. Table 3 shows that Structure produces significant gains in all 9 engine and metric settings and Argumentation in 7, compared with 6 for Evidence and only 2 for Style. Notably, Structure consistently improves all three exposure metrics across engines, while Argumentation also shows positive gains in most settings. The magnitude of improvement varies across engines, with smaller gains on gpt-5-mini. This coincides with its higher refusal rate on evaluative queries such as “which candidate is most X”, leaving fewer responses for rewriting to affect. Structure and Argumentation remain strongest across elec- tions, with larger relative gains for lower-exposure parties. Table 4 shows that Structure and Argumentation show stronger gains than Evidence and Style in both the U.S. and Japan. Across elections and target tiers, rewriting produces similar absolute gains, but these gains correspond to larger relative improvements for par- ties with lower baseline exposure. For example, under Structure in Japan, the Top and Low tiers gain 4.28 and 4.12 percentage points in Mention Coverage, corresponding to relative improvements of 5.9% and 14.4%. For Win Rate, gains of 1.48 and 1.20 points correspond to relative improvements of 10.8% and 51.1%. Thus, although the ab- solute exposure gains are modest, comparable gains can represent much larger relative improvements for parties with low baseline. Content Improvement and Comparative Distinction perform best overall across settings. Figure 2 compares the 11 strategies across 18 combinations of engine, election, and exposure metric. Within each setting, strategies are ranked by improvement over the baseline. The bars show how often each strategy receives each rank, while Mean and Top-3 report its average rank and number of Top-3 finishes. Content Improvement and Comparative Distinction perform best overall, with mean ranks of 2.61 and 2.67 and Top- 3 finishes in 13 and 14 of 18 settings, respectively. We evaluate Content Improvement and Comparative Distinction under 15 U.S. personas: 9 demographic identities, 3 partisan identities, and 3 questionnaire- derived profiles.

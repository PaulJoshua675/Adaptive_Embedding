Observability. AMD telemetry initially lagged NVIDIA’s mature stack, so we closed the gap: we integrated the effi- ciency metrics our NVIDIA fleet relies on (TFLOPS, SM/CU and memory utilization, throughput) and a hardware-agnostic trace-analysis interface (compute/communication breakdown, imbalance, overlap, and timeline analyses) that also enables side-by-side cross-vendor trace comparison (the basis for the apples-to-apples measurements here) and extends to MTIA. Power management. Operating the MI350X against its excur- sion envelope rather than its steady-state limit is a fleet-level capability: it depends on platform provisioning beyond the OCP minimum and on tuning the control-loop response to production phase patterns. Section III-C describes the mechanism and the resulting 3–6% end-to-end gain.

Preprint E ETHICS STATEMENT This work advances the study of multimodal agents that acquire external evidence through search and image-processing tools. Our experiments use established research benchmarks and involve no human-subject studies or collection of private personal data. By design, the experimental agent op- erates in a read-only environment: it retrieves publicly accessible information and processes bench- mark images, while remaining strictly confined to information retrieval and analysis without side effects on external systems. We preserve source URLs for traceability and use datasets and external services in accordance with their applicable licenses and terms. These design choices enable trans- parent, reproducible, and responsible evaluation of tool-augmented agents. We conduct and report this work in accordance with the ICLR Code of Ethics. 28

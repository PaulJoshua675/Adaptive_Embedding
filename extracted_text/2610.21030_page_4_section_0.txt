Here, we apply stop-gradient so that utility scores are used only to reweight contrastive updates, preventing the model from artificially increasing utility values instead of improving retrieval representations. Gradient isolation. Since both objectives share the same backbone model and operate on overlap- ping reasoning positions, naive joint optimization may cause contrastive gradients to interfere with autoregressive generation. To mitigate this effect, during the contrastive optimization, we apply stop- gradient to all positions preceding the embedding extraction point, such that gradients from LInfoNCE do not propagate through (x, r<t).

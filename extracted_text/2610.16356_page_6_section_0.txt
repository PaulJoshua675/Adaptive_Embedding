Cluster Validation Measurement Evaluating clustering results is essential to compare different models, se- lect the optimal number of clusters, and ensure that the formed cluster re- flects the underlying data structure(Bezdek et al., 1984). In conventional clustering, indices such as the silhouette, Dunn, and Davies–Bouldin indices measure how compact and well separated the clusters are. However, these indices assume that each data point is assigned to one cluster and only one. These measurements is relevant when one wants to compare the fuzzy to non-fuzzy clustering methods. Due to the fuzzy nature, previous studies developed evaluation measure- ments that measure separation by involving the membership in each cluster. These measurements are relevant when selecting the appropriate fuzzy clus- tering methods in their case.

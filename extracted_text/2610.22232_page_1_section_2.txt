Unlike lossy compression, which can trade distortion for bitrate, lossless image compression fundamentally relies on entropy cod- ing. Classical codecs improve this process through carefully de- signed predictors, reversible transforms, and context models [2, 4, 19, 33, 34, 39], while learned methods have further advanced coding efficiency by using neural networks to capture more com- plex dependencies [3, 15, 20, 28, 36, 42–44]. Related work spans image and video coding, feature and representation compression, and other structured signals, with associated datasets, benchmarks, and quality assessments [10–13, 17, 18, 21, 22, 31, 32].

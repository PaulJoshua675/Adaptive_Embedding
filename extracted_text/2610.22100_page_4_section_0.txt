At 1M every rule is near the floor on computed results (3–5%), and only the hand-written rule lifts final answers (one dependence seed diverged at 1M, hence its bits per byte). At 12M novelty raises final answers by 25 points over entropy (71.5% vs 46.6%; every seed separates) and over the dependence table (56.7%), and computed results from 5.8% to 10.1%. At 50M the gap is large on both targets: computed results 57.5% vs 20.7% (entropy) and 35.1% (dependence), final answers 89.5% vs 59.9% and 78.2%. Entropy’s computed accuracy varies widely across seeds (12.1, 27.5 and 22.6%), but its best seed is far below novelty’s worst (56.3%). The effect grows with model size (Figure 1): no difference at 1M, a 25-point final-answer gain at 12M, and 30 points on final answers and 37 on computed results at 50M.

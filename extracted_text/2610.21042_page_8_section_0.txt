Appendix F gives sampling and schemas. 6 Discussion and Conclusion We present ReviewPilot, a conversational multi- agent system that supports systematic reviews from search to semantic categorization. Researchers con- firm criteria and schemas, a Prompt Agent writes review-specific guidance and coding rules for ap- plying them, and a project memory retains con- figurations and corrections. Across three LLMs, ReviewPilot achieves higher screening macro F1, pooled extraction F1, and verified-correct rates on PERG than a single-prompt LLM and AgentSLR. These gains should be read with care. PERG pools also inform ReviewPilot’s development, so the results do not establish performance on unseen reviews. A located excerpt shows that the quoted text appears in the paper, but whether it supports a decision or value still requires human judgment. The case studies show why memory design mat- ters. When researchers check flagged and sampled outputs, they reverse some screening exclusions and correct some field values, and the system keeps each change with its reason.

In Proceedings of ma- chine learning and systems 8 (mlsys 2026), 2026. URL https://arxiv.org/abs/2412. 14590. A NOMENCLATURE AND BIT ACCOUNTING Nomenclature. INTb denotes a grid with 2b levels; our symmetric per-vector grids INT3sym / INT4sym / INT8sym have 7 / 15 / 255 levels, so INT4sym costs log2 15 bits plus one fp16 scale per vector, 3.91 bpw (4.00 in storage bits, so its 8% saving against MXFP4’s 4.25 in Section 4.2 is 6% in storage bits). We write ternary for the three-level code and reserve INT2 for four-level grids, namely our exact-grid control and the external 2-bit methods. Rates are stated in information bits (log2 L per code) so that a ternary total compares directly with an integer one. We quantize weights only (W·A16): activations, embeddings and the head stay in fp16, as in every method we compare against (Qwen3-8B: 13.9 GB bf16 body, 1.38 GB at 1.59 bpw, 2.49 GB of fp16 embeddings and head). Scale granularity. Scales are shared per tensor, per output channel (one per row of [out, in]), per (row, group of g) or per input channel (one per column). Side information amortises over the group as b + (16 + bz)/g bpw: INT2-g128 with fp16 scale and zero point is 2.156, MXFP4 is 4 + 8/32 = 4.25 (Open Compute Project, 2023; Rouhani et al., 2023), INT4-g128 is 4.125, and one fp16 scale per vector is 16/d, which vanishes (0.004 at d = 4096). Every dense matrix has a stream axis (dmodel) and a unit axis (heads × dhead, or dff): five kinds read from the residual stream through their rows (WQ, WK, WV , Wgate, Win) and two write to it through their columns (WO, Wout), so per-OC is the unit axis on readers and the stream axis on writers, and per-IC the reverse. Resident terms. The information rate counts log2 L per code plus every term a decoder needs to produce the weight: scales, zero points, act-order indices, group or plane membership, per-group affine terms, rotations that are neither folded into the weights nor regenerable from a seed, and reintroduced norm gains. Folded or seed-regenerable rotations cost 0 bpw. Packing (5 trits per byte = 1.6, 2 bits per trit = 2.0) can only add to the information rate and is not charged. We apply one test to every claimed rate, our own included: can a decoder produce the weight from the counted bits alone? By this test the per-vector scales and reintroduced norm gains move RoleQ-T from 1.585 to 1.590 bpw; TWLA’s per-(row, group) α and µ and its mask membership are consumed at inference, which places it at 4.15–4.65; and QTEA’s 1.7-bit payload is resident at 2.28.

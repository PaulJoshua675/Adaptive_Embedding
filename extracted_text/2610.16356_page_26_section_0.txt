Statistical analysis To confirm the significance of our analysis results, we also performed a statistical analysis to investigate whether there is a significant difference in the evaluation measurements between the algorithms in each case. We quan- tify this using Kruskal-Wallis as an alternative to ANOVA which requires normality and homogeneity of variance assumption. The results can be seen in Figure 2. From the figure, it can be seen that the Kruskal-Wallis statistics produced is relatively high. On the other hand, the degrees of freedom are different because some algorithms, such as the classical and HHO algorithms, did not produce proper results. Therefore, given the statistics and degrees of freedom, we can conclude that there is a significant difference between the FGWC algorithms in each case, confiming that the choice of optimisation algorithms in each case becomes important. 4.5.

Motion Understanding uses native MP4 when supported and a sixteen-frame sheet otherwise (Appendix D). 5.2 How Good Are Current MLLMs in 3D Verification? Table 1 shows the experimental results on 3DVerifierBench. GPT-6 Astra achieves the highest Overall score of 69.4%, followed by Gemini-3.8-Flash at 68.4%. Astra reaches 90.3% on Part Relation and 89.6% on Part Grounding, but only 55.5% on Identity and 50.0% on Fine-grained Geometry (FineGeo.). These weaknesses extend across models: None exceeds 59.0% on Identity or 53.3% on Fine-grained Geometry. We also provide an analysis of human performance on its subset, which is detailed in Appendix G. We observe that the performance of existing MLLMs is still far behind humans. For example, humans achieve an 87.8% Overall score versus Astra’s 66.2%. Note that this result differs from the one in Table 1 due to different testing data. Useful feedback requires correct rejections and relevant reasons. Under reference-semantic explana- tion checking, ExpOA falls below Overall score by 3.6 to 28.1 points across models. Astra remains strongest at 65.8% (95% CI: [64.4, 67.2]%). GPT-5.6-Sol overtakes Gemini-3.8-Flash despite its lower answer score.

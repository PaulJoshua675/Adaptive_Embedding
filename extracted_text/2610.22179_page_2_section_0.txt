Sign-mantissa bytes remain unchanged while exponents are encoded with bounded Huffman codes with escapes for rare values. Values are packed into fixed sized streams with overflow buffers. Tensors are decoded losslessly, with fused kernels for optimizer updates. We introduce Losslessly Compressed Tensors (LCT) (Figure 1). Designed for high performance on GPU hardware, LCT losslessly compresses the exponents of Bfloat16 (BF16) values to reduce memory usage during training. Fused pointwise operators are introduced for optimizers with compressed buffers, while a shared GPU buffer removes host synchronization during training. PyTorch autograd integration compresses cached activations. The tensors remain compressed until they are needed, at which point they are decompressed on-demand. Our contributions are: • A lossless BF16 codec and training integration, including GPU Triton kernels, fused pointwise updates, and a preallocated buffer. • An analysis of coding and allocated storage costs under model mismatch, together with bounds explaining how exponent centering compensates for magnitude changes during training. • An evaluation spanning codec throughput, fused operators, and training memory. LCT encodes 2.38× faster than the next-fastest evaluated codec on an RTX 3090 and reduces peak memory by over 20% in training and fine tuning applications.

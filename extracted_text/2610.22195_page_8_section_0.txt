To characterize parallel execution, we apply the average- concurrency measure used in performance analysis (Huang et al., 2010) to worker invocations. For each question, aver- age worker concurrency is ¯C = 1 T P i di, where di is the ac- tive duration of worker invocation i within the trace window and T is the question’s makespan, giving the time-weighted mean number of active workers over the full window includ- ing periods with no active workers. The two outcome groups have similar final tree depths and leaf counts, while questions scored incorrect involve more leaf executions, worker invocations, and Orchestrator decisions on average. Rebuilding also occurs more frequently in this group, at 50.0% compared with 14.7% for questions scored correct. Similar final task structures therefore correspond to substantially different execution histories, making accumulated orchestration and rebuilding activity important for understanding the research process.

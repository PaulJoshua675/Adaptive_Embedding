On F-MNIST it condenses later and keeps a tail: Meff/M = 0.11 at x = 4.4 and 0.023 at x = 10, where the REM gives 0.014 and 0.006. Panel (b) shows why: the standardised within-class overlaps of MNIST are Gaussian (skewness −0.16, excess kurtosis 0.0), those of F-MNIST are not (skewness −0.7, excess kurtosis 1.2), and in particular their upper tail is compressed, with P(z > 2) = 0.006 against 0.023 for a Gaussian and a gap between the two largest overlaps of 0.11 σm against the 0.32 σm expected for 256 Gaussian levels. A query of F-MNIST has many nearly equivalent closest examples, and a softmax over nearly degenerate levels cannot freeze on one of them: this is the signature of sub-clusters within the class, i.e. of the hierarchical orga- nization found in [13], and it is where the REM should be replaced by its hierarchical generalization [28].

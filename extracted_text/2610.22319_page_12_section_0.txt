Emergent Behaviors Nothing in the agents’ instructions mentioned roles, chests or rescues, beyond the line that the players decide how to share the work. Their general instructions do ask for reusable tools, notes in a shared memory and honest reports, but not what to build, what to record or how to work together. Over the 229 messages they exchanged before the win, the team converged on practices familiar from effective human teams. 12

We presented CLUE, a two-stage training pipeline for learning to discover and use social con- text. It combines supervised evidence reason- ing with GRPO guided by IUG, which rewards new, evidence-supported social inferences while accounting for reasoning effort. Training on lim- ited conversational data improves average F1 in- domain and out-of-domain while reducing reason- ing token use. Our qualitative analysis further shows that the model more often identifies earlier conversational causes of emotion. These findings support context discovery as a learning objective for social and pragmatic understanding. Limitations Our evaluation of CLUE covers Chinese, Japanese, and English in selected conversational settings, leaving broader cultural coverage to be explored. Computational constraints limit training to a 9B model, with a 27B model included as a reference. Extending the study to additional communities, languages, and model scales would help establish how broadly the observed gains generalize.

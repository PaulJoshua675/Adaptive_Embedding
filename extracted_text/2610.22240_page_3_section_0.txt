Given a question–image pair x = (q, I), an agent πθ in- terleaves reasoning with web search and image inspection until returning an answer. Later actions depend on the accumulated multimodal history ht, making evidence acquisition sequential. For exam- ple, identifying an entity in a retrieved image can determine the next textual query; facts found on a webpage can instead guide which image to inspect. Reasoning chains and evidence necessity. We represent a task’s designated reasoning route as an entity chain C0 = (E0, . . . , ET ): E0 is grounded in the input image, each clue ℓi links Ei−1 to Ei, and ET is the answer. Each transition requires identifying the next entity from its predecessor and linking clue. Let Z contain the retrieved textual and visual facts needed along this chain, and Y(x, Z) the answers consistent with them. Our curation criterion requires a unique answer and necessity of every designated evidence item: Y(x, Z) = {y}, Y(x, Z \ {z})̸ = {y}, ∀z ∈Z. (1) Omitting an item therefore leaves the answer am- biguous or underivable along this route. This crite- rion applies to the intended chain; filtering in Sec- tion 3.3 checks empirically for shortcuts through parametric knowledge or alternative web evidence. 3.2 Data Curation Seed collection. We take the full training splits of LiveVQA and FVQA and retain questions that the data-generation model answers correctly with- out tools. These seeds are easy for this model, and their successful chain-of-thought provides candi- date reasoning chains. 3.2.1 Refine Pipeline BuildLink extracts a seed’s entity chain; an open- source model equipped with web-search and im- age tools then applies Expand, Blur, and Visualize in this order, followed by question synthesis (Fig- ure 2). Six parameters (L, M, W, N, P, Q) control the added evidence requirements. Expand increases chain depth, Blur introduces entity disambigua- tion, and Visualize adds retrieved visual evidence. The operators modify the designated chain before question wording is synthesized, allowing their ev- idence requirements to be controlled separately. BuildLink.

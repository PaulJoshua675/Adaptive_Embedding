Web-scale retrieval evaluation is largely predictable: a per-query tail-aware estimator recovers 100M-scale accuracy from a small random distractor sample to 0.005 mean error, covering the truth for every backbone we test on ILIAS, INSTRE, and GLDv2. Validated across five back- bones, three benchmarks, and two distractor pools, it turns web-scale evaluation from an indexing problem into a prediction problem—for method comparisons whose margins exceed the extrapola- tion error.

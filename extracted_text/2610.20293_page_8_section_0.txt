Additive correlated-error null. We test whether the positive CASI-minus-alternative alignment gaps can arise mechanically from correlated evaluator residuals under an interaction-free replay reference. Replay singleton values define the additive reference, and a donor request’s complete coalition-level evaluator residual vector is transferred as a block, preserving the observed within- request residual covariance. On the stricter 870-request subset for which the required normal- ized credit statistics are defined, the observed CASI–Singleton alignment gap is +0.0359, versus a 95% null interval of [−0.1231, −0.0685]; for CASI–LOO, the observed gap is +0.2221 versus [−0.1462, −0.0813]. This specific additive correlated-residual mechanism does not reproduce the observed positive gaps.

Category-level results expose structure that the aggregate scores conceal (Table 13). On RefSpatial, the native model improves placement from 29.00 to 47.00 but reduces location from 63.00 to 55.00. On RoboSpatial VQA, compatibility rises from 35.24 to 52.38, compared with a smaller configuration gain from 78.05 to 83.74. Nearly equal BLINK macro scores also hide large offsets: multi-view reasoning improves by 33.08 points, while counting decreases by 29.17. MMSI remains weak overall (27.70 versus AR’s 34.20), with particularly large losses on measurement attributes and camera–object relationships. Point-Bench is especially diagnostic. Native mean’s counting success is 38.78 versus AR’s 66.84. This difference accounts for 5.61 of the 6.01-point macro gap; the mean across the other four categories differs by only about 0.50 points. The shortfall is concentrated in estimating and separating multiple targets, rather than a uniform loss of single-point localization.

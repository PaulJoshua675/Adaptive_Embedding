G.5 Pair Imbalance in a Pre-Trained Model Motivation. The natural-drift experiment measures changes in pair imbalance during training from scratch. Here we inspect a released GPT-2 checkpoint to determine whether its learned attention pairs have unequal norms. Checkpoint measurement. We load GPT-2 small (Radford et al., 2019), with 124M parameters, d = 768, L = 12, and GELU, from Hugging Face and measure the symmetry pair-norm balance. The recorded maximum absolute log-ratios are 0.221 for Q–K and 0.931 for V–O, so V–O has the larger imbalance in this checkpoint. These pre-fine-tuning measurements use stabilizer δ := 10−8. Without the corresponding initial norms, these endpoint measurements do not determine how much imbalance accumulated during pre-training. We report the attention-pair maxima; GELU up–down rescaling is not an exact symmetry. Exact attention-pair edits of this biased model also require the bias co-transforms stated in Appendix B.1.

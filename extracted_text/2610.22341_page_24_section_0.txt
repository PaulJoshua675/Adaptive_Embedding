Because LoRA introduces a compact optimizer state, training the 27B model under standard Distributed Data Parallel (DDP) avoids ZeRO-3 parameter all-gather traffic and achieves a 13× higher step throughput (≈6.2 K vs. ≈460 tokens/s) with standard PyTorch allocator caching. Across 4 × H100 GPUs, 9B LoRA sustains ≈17 K tokens/s (completing the 270 K-pair, 293 M-token mixture in 4 h 46 m), while 27B LoRA sustains ≈6.2 K tokens/s. Chat formatting and strict contamination control. Training examples are formatted as ShareGPT multi-turn conversations: a system prompt specifying the target dialect and output contract, a user turn containing the natural-language specification, and an assistant turn containing the complete executable program in a fenced code block. Any example whose tokenized conversation exceeds the context window is excluded cleanly rather than truncated, preserving syntactic closure. To guarantee zero train–test leakage, all 212 3DCodeBench procedural factories, all 3,904 re-seeded instances derived from those factories, and all per-dialect evaluation splits are strictly held out; no training mixture shares a program or asset identifier with any evaluation task. Three LoRA runs share an identical optimization recipe and vary solely in which representation views of the unified text export (Appendix B.1) they consume. Optimization recipe. Each run applies LoRA of rank 16 (α = 32, dropout 0.05) for one epoch at a peak learning rate of 5 × 10−5 with a linear warm-up over the first 3% of steps and cosine decay to zero. Examples are packed into full sequences (≈7.2 samples per sequence), with each optimizer step consuming 64 packed sequences and logging training cross-entropy every five steps. Representation scaling arms. Arm A trains on the complete unified export across all three representation views (native format, standardized text export, and pass-flattened multi-pass shaders): 1,992,816 rows packed into 276,594 sequences (4,322 steps). Arm B trains on the native-format and pass-flattened views without the re-templated text-export variant: 1,160,630 rows in 159,427 sequences (2,492 steps). Arm C retains a single canonical copy of each curated program paired with its instruction-style caption: 292,690 rows in 39,926 sequences (647 steps). Optimization dynamics across representation scales. Figure 14 demonstrates smooth, stable convergence across all three regimes: no logged step exceeds 1.3× the trailing 20-step moving average, and all runs are entirely free of instability or divergence spikes. Rapid initial adaptation occurs within the first quarter of the epoch—reducing cross-entropy from ≈0.60 to 0.30 (Arm A) and 0.36 (Arms B and C)—followed by steady refinement to terminal smoothed losses of 0.274 (Arm A), 0.314 (Arm B), and 0.292 (Arm C), with Arm A stabilizing across the second half of the epoch (0.277 at mid-epoch to 0.274 at completion).

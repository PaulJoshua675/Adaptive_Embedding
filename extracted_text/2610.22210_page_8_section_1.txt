We propose TRACE, an efficient topology-aware objective that reframes skeleton supervision as prediction-adaptive lo- cal reconnection. TASC provides a class-labeled topological carrier that preserves foreground and class-specific topology while identifying same-class branches that should remain connected. Based on this representation, TRACE identifies the easiest valid reconnection under the current prediction and supervises its critical bottleneck. Across six 2D and 3D benchmarks covering binary and multiclass segmentation, TRACE improves connectivity and topology while maintain- ing region-overlap and boundary accuracy.

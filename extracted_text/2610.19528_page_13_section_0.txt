B.1 Contribution of SFT and RL SRAT uses SFT on high-quality retrieval trajecto- ries as initialization, followed by outcome-driven RL. To separate their contributions, we compare the intermediate SFT checkpoint with the final model on representative RAGtrans and IdiomKB settings. Table 11 shows that the SFT warm start already provides a strong initialization, performing simi- larly to CSC. The main quality improvements, how- ever, emerge after RL. BLEU decreases slightly after RL, consistent with Sec- tion 4.2: the RL reward directly optimizes COMET and LLM-judged quality rather than reference n- gram overlap.

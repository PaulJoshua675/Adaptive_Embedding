In some cases, however, the retained memory can be vague, as humans do. For example, in Sample 2, which clue appeared 32 minutes before the question timing, MaLoW appears to retain a coarse memory of Coca-Cola and answers “beverage” rather than the specific object. Sample 5 illustrates a failure case in which both MaLoW and the baselines produce incorrect answers, partly due to an inaccurate ground-truth annotation. Sample 3 requires not only remembering the relevant video timestamp, but also an agentic procedure to revisit the corresponding frames and localize the clock displayed in the bottom-right corner. Since MaLoW is not trained with agentic tool-use data, extending it to such agentic settings is left for future work. Finally, Sample 6 requires strong visual perception to recognize that four cards are initially present on the table, followed by temporal reasoning to determine that one additional card is added, yielding five cards in total. We expect such cases to benefit from future base VLMs with stronger fine-grained visual perception. 32

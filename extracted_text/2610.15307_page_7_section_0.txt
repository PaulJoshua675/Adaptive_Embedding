The meta- predictor fθ can be instantiated by either a simple linear model or a nonlinear tree ensemble. 1) Linear Instantiation. The linear version of FENAS is defined as: ˆy = fθ(Φ(α)) = wT Φ(α) + b (11) where ˆy is the predicted performance, w is the linear weight vector, b is the bias term, and Φ(α) is the CAST feature vector of architecture α. 2) Non-linear Tree Ensemble (Default Configuration). To capture non- linear interactions among physical dimensions, we use a tree ensemble such as XGBoost [3] or random forests [2] as the default predictor: ˆy = fθ(Φ(α)) = M X m=1 γmhm(Φ(α)) (12) where M is the number of decision trees, hm(·) is the m-th regression tree, γm is the weight or learning coefficient of the m-th tree, and Φ(α) is the input CAST feature vector. 3) Point-wise Regression and Loss Function.

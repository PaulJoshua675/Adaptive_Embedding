The above discussions provide an alternative interpretation of MirrorMoE beyond exploration. In MoEs with large 𝐸and 𝐾, not every token may require the full expressiveness from all activated experts. If a token can be learned well using fewer experts, the capacity of remaining experts can be devoted to other tokens. MirrorMoE can therefore be viewed as an approach for tokens to adaptively determine their number of activated experts, where this adaptivity arises from the expert suppression and preservation shown in Props. 3.2 and 3.3, respectively. 4 NUMERICAL RESULTS This section evaluates the performance of MirrorMoE.

The conditioning vector supplies shifts, scales, and gates to all 12 Transformer blocks and modulation parameters to the vocabulary head. We increase its width from 128 to 256, changing the timestep MLP from 256 →128 →128 to 256 →256 →256. The input is a 256-dimensional sinusoidal encoding of 𝛾, and the MLP uses SiLU after each linear layer. All adaptive-normalization projections and the shortcut gate receive the wider vector. This increases the capacity for noise-dependent modulation without changing the Transformer hidden width or feed-forward dimensions. The change adds 7.36M parameters, or 2.25% of the baseline; its ablation therefore includes a capacity increase and is not a parameter-matched test of conditioning alone. Contextual vocabulary head.

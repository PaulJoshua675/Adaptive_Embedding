We assigned each incorrect trace of a reasoning model one of three failure types from rollouts and tested the recovery of these types and of the PIT step from the model’s hidden states. The confidence probe predicts the final label, and at the cutoff it misses capability gaps and PITs more often than near misses. The combined probe ranks PITs and near misses in the opposite order to the confidence probe, so an ordering of failure types read from a probe depends on the probe’s training design. The failure-type classifier, which reads transcoder activations of a completed incorrect trace, separates capability gaps from PITs. It meets the margin at 8B and not at 4B. At 4B no single probe exceeds the length rule at locating the PIT step, and the ensemble exceeds it under one of the two weightings of its failure-type loss.

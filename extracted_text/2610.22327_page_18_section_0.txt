Each control is trained from its own Stage I alignment followed by Stage II instruction tuning, using the same data and hyperparameters as our model. The controls are therefore matched to the full model in architecture, trainable parameters, visual-token budget, and training procedure, and differ only in whether the two towers receive distinct or identical views. They are not matched to the single-view variants, which use one tower and fewer visual tokens. Notably, Delay+Delay allocates substantially more tokens to the delay representation than our model does, and Plot+Plot allocates more tokens to the line plot, yet neither reproduces the gain of the complementary pair, indicating that the improvement cannot be attributed to additional capacity devoted to either view alone.

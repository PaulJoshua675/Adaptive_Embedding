DetNO achieves the best MSS and VendiScore on all three (SD1.5, SDXL, SD3) in Tab. 1, confirming systematic diversity gains across all three backbones and both samplers tested (DDIM and Euler). CNO also im- proves diversity, but its VendiScore gain over the baseline is roughly half of DetNO’s on each backbone. On reference-free quality metrics (PickScore, Im- ageReward), the trade-off is small: PickScore decreases only marginally on every backbone, while ImageReward matches the baseline on SD1.5 and decreases modestly on SDXL and SD3.

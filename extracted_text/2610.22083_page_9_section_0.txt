At 𝐵= 1 the fine field vanishes and the linear and cyclic hybrids reduce exactly to Thermometer and Johnson encoding. At 𝐵= 𝑁the coarse field vanishes and both reduce to Gray encoding. Thermometer, Johnson and Gray are therefore not separate proposals but points on a single continuum, with Gray the common limit of the linear and cyclic families.

Cleaning reduced 2,761 raw rows to 2,665 unique parallel segment pairs. Each parallel pair was tagged by sentential status based on word count from standard whitespace splitting after punctuation stripping. Entries with at most two whitespace-delimited tokens were treated as lexical or short-phrase material (such as citation nouns or isolated verb stems); longer examples and independently verified clauses were treated as sentence-level. Random splitting would therefore reward dictionary matches without demonstrating sentence translation. 3.2 Stratification and Bidirectional Tasks Development and test data were drawn exclusively from the 1,477 sentence- level pairs to ensure that evaluation benchmarks measure genuine sequence translation rather than isolated vocabulary lookup. Including single-word or short lexical pairs in held-out sets would artificially inflate n-gram and character overlap scores through simple dictionary matching, masking failures in clause-level syntax, agreement, and possession. Restricting evaluation to sentential items guarantees that metrics reflect grammatical compositionality, while placing all 1,188 lexical pairs into the training inventory provides necessary stem coverage and orthographic grounding. Using a fixed stratification seed, we reserved 120 sentence pairs for development and 150 for the final held-out test set. The remaining training inventory contained 2,335 pairs after recombining the lexical entries with eligible training sentences.

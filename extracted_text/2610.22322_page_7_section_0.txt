To iso- late the contribution of learned self-orchestration, we conduct two ablations on the Iron Pickaxe task. Both ablations share the same agent roles, SFT initialization, and action-training proce- dure as MPT, and each modifies a single component. Round-robin. Turns are allocated cyclically among the three agents, and policies are trained on the reward with a discount factor of γ = 0.95. This ablation removes bidding entirely. Random credit. We replace the bids used for credit assignment with random values sampled from U(0, 1), while turn allocation and bidding optimization remain unchanged. This ablation tests whether learned bids provide useful credit-assignment signals. As shown in Figure 7, at approximately 5×1018 training FLOPs, MPT reaches a mean episode reward of about 42, compared with 15 for Round-robin, and 31 for random credit. Round-robin im- proves little over training.

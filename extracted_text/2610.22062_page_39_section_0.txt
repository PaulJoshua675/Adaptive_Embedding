Every variant is scored on the same 183 annotated diarrhea cells, with the architecture, consensus gate, keyword pre-filter and output schema held fixed, so every ∆is a like-for-like comparison. V0 is the deployed prompt and serves as the base case, and V2 is the configuration reported in the main text. Accuracy in panels A and B is the mean±SD of three independent runs, as in main-text Table 2, and the other metrics are computed from the modal prediction across those runs. Panel C is a deterministic transformation of the V0 modal predictions, so it was run once and its ∆is taken against those predictions. The p values are McNemar exact against the base case, Holm-adjusted within the family the comparison belongs to (Table S4), and are therefore not comparable across panels. Three readings follow. The severity threshold in panel A is the only element that moves accuracy materially. No decomposition rule in panel B beats the deployed prompt, and the best comes within 0.2 points of it. In panel C, S1 shifts only the model output and is therefore scored against the same annotation on the same cells, whereas S3 shifts the annotation as well and is positional, so its 85.2% is an upper bound and never a metric of the pipeline. S1 is not deployable either, since the shift presumes onset at the first on-treatment visit, and it is reported as a diagnostic of where the error sits. The panel B labels are the identifiers used in the released code, and the family varies the combination rule, the direction of the consensus gate and the within-window aggregation order, all as functions of the same two extracted variables and therefore at no additional inference cost.

Every adverse faithfulness label that needs judgement is assigned by a human (Appendix D). 4 Results We investigate how search-augmented LLMs use retrieved evidence and where failures occur. We first examine their search behaviour and the relia- bility of their answers and citations. We then test whether these failures are shared across models and whether providing evidence directly reduces them. 4.1 Search-Augmented LLMs Often Rely on Snippets Search helps every configuration: under frozen retrieval, enabling search raises claim-check ac- curacy by 27–42 points (Appendix E), and open- question accuracy reaches 85.8–95.4% (Table 1). What differs is how deeply models read. Given the same search results, models answer 2.6–76.0% of episodes without opening any page. Unfaithful citations are also strongly associated with stale answers. On sourced open questions, 81.8% (27/33) of on-claim citations are unfaithful when answers are stale, compared with only 0.9% (6/694) when answers are current. Similar patterns appear in fact-sheet checks (Appendix G). 4.2 Failures Are Shared across Models We next test whether prompts that Luna fails under native search are more likely to fail under frozen re- trieval. Seven of the nine failure types show signif- icant positive associations (odds ratios 1.84–6.46, p < 0.05), while a negative control shows no asso- ciation (odds ratio 1.05; Appendix I). 4.3 Direct Access to Evidence Reduces Failures We first test whether providing evidence directly reduces failures. On 50 sports claims, gemma-4- 31b and gpt-oss-20b endorse the stale claim 14/50 and 11/47 times when they have to find the page in the search menu, but only 0/50 and 1/50 times when the same Wikipedia page is in the prompt. In all 14 of its failures, gemma-4-31b opens no page; qwen3-8b benefits less (from 16/49 to 8/49; Appendix H). In an agentic loop, the question comes before everything the model retrieves. We therefore test whether restating the full prompt after each opened page helps, keeping the menu, pages and sampling settings unchanged.

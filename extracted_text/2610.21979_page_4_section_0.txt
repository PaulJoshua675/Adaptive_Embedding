SCR: redistribute credit under a conserved budget Span Credit Redistribution (SCR) reallocates an available block coefficient under a conserved budget, concen- trating negative credit in error regions and positive credit in correct regions. Quality gating restricts redistribution to high-scoring, type-matched blocks, focusing local allocation on residual defects within otherwise accurate elements. For eligible matched blocks, SCR creates edit-based masks based on both the prediction and the reference. Insertions and replacements identify error locations. SCR assigns deleted reference content within a matched block to a nearby generated position. Whole-element omissions follow UAP’s separate boundary route. Further details appear in Appendix B.1.

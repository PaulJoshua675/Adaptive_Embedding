We argue that tacit knowing is better modelled as an operating regime of a single memory substrate than as a separate kind of content. Our argument draws on VaCoAl, an algebro- deterministic hyperdimensional memory built from XOR-and-shift operations over GF(2); on CASRAM, its commercial SRAM-CAM realization; and on PyVaCoAl, a DRAM-CAM realization with the extended VaCoAl architecture. In VaCoAl/PyVaCoAl, the same directed acyclic graph (DAG) of bound records can be read in two ways. With every address collision repaired (Rescue mode), the trace is exactly recoverable and auditable, but no path outranks another. With collisions tolerated (Don’t Care mode), a path-integrated confidence, CR2, emerges; it prunes circuitous routes and ranks direct ones without ever being stated as a proposition. We call this trade-off, first noted in our companion work on Minsky’s Emotion Machine, audit–valuation complementarity and propose it as a formal counterpart of Polanyi’s claim that tacit and explicit knowledge are not sharply divided. Five results follow. (1) Polanyi’s from–to structure reads as non-commutative binding, his face Identikit as role–filler composition, and recognition as majority-vote coincidence. (2) Explicating the valuation completely destroys it (Lemma 1), an algebraic analogue of Polanyi’s pianist; yet the valuation is articulable at the level of law. Tacitness thus comes in two classes: contingent, which a new query brings out, and regime-bound, which can be stated only in a regime where it no longer exists. Read through these classes, Polanyi, Nonaka, Tsoukas and Collins each describe part of one substrate correctly and are narrow in a characteristic way; Nonaka’s knowledge conversion, even on the continuum of Nonaka and von Krogh, cannot tell the two classes apart. (3) Collins’s relational, somatic and collective tacit knowledge map onto bound structure, substrate valuation and value binding respectively; the last is absent from the substrate. (4) The mentor–student DAG of our earlier computational prosopography is a graph of tacit-knowledge transmission, so its superhighway and its Kuhnian shift are events in how tacit knowledge travels. (5) Terence Tao’s observation that AI-solved problems now spawn few follow-up activities is diagnosed as rootless explicit knowledge: results that no human holds tacitly. We propose a division of labour that re-roots them—LLMs generate, VaCoAl/PyVaCoAl keeps the auditable trace and its confidence, and the human community values. Virtue epistemology explains why this last role stays human: the intellectual virtues it calls on cannot be offloaded to technology.

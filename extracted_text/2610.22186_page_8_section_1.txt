We evaluate EVA-Cap on two representative gen- eral audiovisual captioning benchmarks. Those benchmarks assess the overall captioning per- formance from multiple dimensions, including video content omission, hallucination, and multi- aspect scoring. The comparison with existing au- diovisual models is presented in Tab. 1. EVA-Cap achieves state-of-the-art or competitive results across various metrics, e.g., a total error rate of 34.6% on the video-SALMONN-2 testset [29] and an average score of 76.9 on UGC-VideoCap [43], surpassing both unified OLMs, e.g., Qwen3- Omni-Instruct [46] and task-specific captioners, e.g., DiaDem [5]. On the video-SALMONN-2 test set, EVA-Cap demonstrates the lowest miss rate of 17.6%. While some baselines achieve lower hallucination rates, they often do so at the cost of substantial event omissions, e.g., Hu- manOmniV2 maintains a 12.3% hallucination rate at the cost of a 49.2% miss rate. This indi- cates OLMs may omit many valid yet less salient events without event-centric training. This demonstrates the strong general audiovisual captioning capability of our method.

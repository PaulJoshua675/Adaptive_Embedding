Protocol notes. Our one deviation from the standard protocol is the shape of the calibration draw (512×512 rather than 128×2048), which favours the baselines (Table 14); every competitor number was produced in our harness and validated against the method’s own evaluation code before being quoted, and against its paper where it reports the same model (QTEA’s 8B and 14B rows match its paper exactly; TWLA’s rerun is set beside its paper’s numbers in Table 1). The ordering against CAT-Q also holds on a chat corpus (UltraChat, the post-trained model’s own domain), where our run sits at 1.03× and CAT-Q at 1.27× of the respective full-precision perplexity. Group scales. Ternary group scales (g = 128, +0.12 bpw) improve the body at either placement (Table 9). With the groups per output row, the standard layout, the run scores 10.87 / 61.93 / 51.33 after scale distillation; with the same groups on the role axis (RoleQ-T-g128) it scores 10.82 / 61.81 / 50.89, within ±3% on every instrument and with the sign flipping between seeds on two of them. At PTQ the role-axis variant is ahead on all three instruments (18.49 against 19.16 perplexity, +1.2 zero-shot, +2.6 MMLU), by less than 3× the seed range. With group scales, then, the role axis is on par with the standard placement rather than ahead of it. 20

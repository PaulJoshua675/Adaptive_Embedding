Learned end to end, boundaries recover structure in both domains; novelty is a cheap proxy that coincides with that structure in written math. A boundary at the value is not what helps. On traces, a 128-byte network predicting the same novelty target places patch starts at 74% of computed values and 0% of copied values, far better than the table.

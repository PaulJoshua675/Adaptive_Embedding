The registered series continues with a wave at a longer horizon, a replication on a second provider at a matched horizon, and labels checked by human readers in each language at larger scale. Forecasts for brands with no answer history are a separate problem; an earlier development wave on unseen brands failed its gate, and a registered experiment on that question is in preparation. An exploratory pilot on fast-growing European firms, which compared questions asking for different numbers of companies, shaped the design of that study. Data and code availability The registration on OSF (https://osf.io/x8h3p/) records the plan hash, the design and the decision rule. The frozen protocol and manifest are deposited at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23351313; the manifest holds every question text, the SHA-256 of every request body, the candidate brands and aliases and the frozen forecasts, and its plan block reproduces the registered hash. The same deposit holds the derived data: the 648 D1 labels with request identifiers; the 162 request rows with completion status, finish reason, returned model version, token counts, search and grounding counts, realised horizon and the SHA-256 of each answer text; the metrics file; the derivation receipt; the label-check summary and the list of its two disagreements; and the per-cell counts of v0.2, v0.3 and v0.4. Raw answer texts are not redistributed; their hashes allow any copy to be checked. The collection and analysis scripts are fixed by SHA-256 in the registered plan and are available from the author on request. Every number in this note is produced by a script from the deposited files; the list of numbers with sources is part of the deposit.

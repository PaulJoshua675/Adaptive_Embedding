First, how much do momentum, tone, and numerical surprise change the requested forecast score? Second, do those effects survive changes in answer encoding and wording? Third, can the model recognize the beat/miss relation when asked directly? These questions concern observable behavior. A numerical effect is not a proof of reasoning, and a momentum effect is not automatically a mistake: price history may contain genuine information in real markets. We do not observe market outcomes in this experiment. 2

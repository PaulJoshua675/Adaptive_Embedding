To assess the impact of each solver- derived feature in 𝐻𝑋, we conduct an ablation study by removing each feature in turn and evaluating on all layouts listed in Table 1, with all metrics averaged across the test layouts. The results are shown in Figure 8. We report recall and cost as the primary metrics: recall measures the fraction of vias requiring reassignment that are correctly identified by the GNN model. High recall is essential to avoid missing vias that require reassignment, which is critical for achieving optimal solutions. Cost reflects the final solution quality after our local refinement strategy. Removing any single feature degrades both cost and recall, con- firming that all features contribute to model performance. Excluding sol_pool_freq results in the largest degradation in both metrics, reducing recall from 0.979 to 0.933 and increasing cost from 127.89 to 129.05, indicating that solution-pool frequency is the most infor- mative feature for capturing solver behavior. Excluding coef and point_num results in smaller but consistent degradation across both metrics. Overall, the full feature set achieves the best performance. Training Set.

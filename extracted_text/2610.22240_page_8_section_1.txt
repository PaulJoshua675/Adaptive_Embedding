We introduced MMSearch-OPD, combining con- trollable data refinement with joint RL–OPD train- ing for long-horizon multimodal deep search. Re- fine reuses existing multimodal question–image pairs and applies targeted transformations to con- trol search depth, entity disambiguation, and vi- sual evidence requirements. Joint training com- plements answer rewards and behavior penalties with reward-conditioned token-level guidance on imperfect student trajectories, while progressively reducing teacher influence. Across seven primary benchmarks, our 9B agent improves average ac- curacy by 15.3 percentage points over its agentic- workflow baseline, with joint training adding 2.3 points beyond SFT. Data ablations further support the training value of refined questions. Together, these results show the value of upgrading exist- ing multimodal data to require sustained evidence acquisition. They also support joint outcome opti- mization and selective teacher guidance for learn- ing long-horizon search and reasoning over text and images. 8

B.2 BENCHMARK CONSTRUCTION We construct a fixed benchmark of 200 scenes for Lingbot-World 2.0, each consisting of an initial image, a text prompt, and a camera trajectory. All methods use the same conditioning inputs and sampling seeds. Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (Qwen et al., 2025) generates scene descriptions spanning diverse categories and visual styles, and Z-Image-Turbo (Image Team et al., 2026) generates the initial images at 1376 × 768. The image and video models receive the same scene prompt, com- bining environment, subject, and perspective descriptions. After fixing the benchmark, we construct the verifier’s training set from separate scenes using the same pipeline, filtering potential overlap through text and image similarity. Each video contains 253 frames, with the 252 camera transitions divided into five consecutive control segments of lengths 50, 50, 50, 50, and 52. For each segment, we uniformly sample an action from forward, backward, strafe left, strafe right, yaw left, yaw right, and stop, excluding the preceding segment’s action. Across the benchmark, this produces 593 trans- lation, 276 rotation, and 131 stationary segments. Translation speeds are sampled from U(0.2, 1.0), with the fastest translation segment in each video set to 1.0 to match the model’s per-video trans- lation normalization. Yaw rates are sampled from U(0.6, 2.0) degrees per frame. We choose the lower bound based on preliminary tests in which smaller commands produced little visible rotation. Camera intrinsics follow the official adapter defaults and remain fixed across methods.

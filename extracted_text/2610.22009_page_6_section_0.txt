A Dataset construction details Visual Sanad is constructed from 100 base verses in the AlQuran Cloud en.sahih edition. Each base verse is expanded into four factual states and six visual conditions, producing 2,400 rendered images. Factual states. For every base verse, we create one valid item and three invalid foils: • Valid: the displayed quote and displayed reference match. • Near-neighbor: the reference points to a nearby ayah in the same surah. • Same-surah far: the reference points to a farther ayah in the same surah. • Cross-surah lexical: the reference points to another surah with lexical overlap. Foil safety filters. Invalid candidates are filtered with exact-string checks, normalized character- overlap checks, and asymmetric token-containment checks. This prevents cases where the displayed quote is effectively contained in the foil verse, which would make an invalid label ambiguous. Visual conditions. The six conditions are plain, decorative, islamic_authority, decorative_islamic_authority, neutral_authority, and decorative_neutral_authority. Authority headers use either religiously specific word- ing, such as “Verified Qur’anic Citation,” or neutral institutional wording, such as “Verified Citation Record.” B Audit summary The final audit found no source-text corruption and no unsafe gold labels after corrective replace- ments. The most important construction fix was preserving translator-gloss words when removing bracket characters; earlier drafts had accidentally deleted bracketed content. The final locked dataset also removed or replaced three quality-flagged foils before rendering.

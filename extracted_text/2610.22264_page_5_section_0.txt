SCOPE assigns a shared loss weight to tokens within each semantic span. 3.3 Semantic Span Credit Modulation The rewrite instruction asks the policy to preserve the request and clarify subjects, counts, colors, attributes, and spatial relations. It generates the rewrite and its boundaries in one completion, placing | only between adjacent nonempty spans. Tokenizer character offsets link the extracted spans to the positions in the loss. We extract the semantic partition from the rewrite by splitting the answer field at | separators and aligning the spans to rollout tokens. Let Tg,i,s contain the valid content token positions assigned to span s. These sets are nonempty and disjoint, and together they cover the answer content. Separators and wrapper tokens lie outside the partition, so the span statistics describe the prompt passed to the renderer.

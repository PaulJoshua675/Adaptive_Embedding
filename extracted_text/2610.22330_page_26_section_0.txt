Example selection. From each of the twelve evaluation files, we display the fixed sample IDs. The evaluation settings follow the twelve bars in Figure 2; individual samples are not ranked by perplexity, fluency, or perceived quality. The GenPPL shown on each card describes its full 1,024-sample evaluation, not the displayed text alone; the paired entropy is reported in Table 3. The examples follow the figure’s panel and bar order. Sampling settings. All examples use EMA weights, length 1,024 model tokens, temperature 1, final argmax decoding, and unweighted inference self-conditioning. LangFlow uses Euler–EDM with 128 or 1,024 total denoiser calls. RePlaID uses ancestral DDPM with 128 or 1,024 reverse transitions plus one final call, for totals of 129 or 1,025; the card headers denote this extra call by “(+1).” The generation seed equals the sample ID. Equal recorded seeds do not imply semantic prompts or establish identical initial noise across formulations. Reading the examples. The complete decoded text is reproduced without correction or truncation, in- cluding special tokens and document boundaries. Names, quotations, citation-like strings, repetitions, and factual errors are part of the generated output. The nonprinting code points U+0097/U+009D, the source replacement character U+FFFD, and an unsupported ribbon emoji are displayed by code point (e.g., [U+1F380]). U+200B retains zero-width spacing and U+200E retains left-to-right directionality. Other changes are limited to typographic escaping and layout. These fixed-ID examples illustrate the cited evaluations but do not establish typical quality, factual accuracy, or systematic improvement with NFE.

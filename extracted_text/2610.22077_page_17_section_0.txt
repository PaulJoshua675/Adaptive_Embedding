The gridded-source rows are restricted to forecast times that coincide with an IBTrACS observation containing valid USA PRES and USA WIND. This mask depends on the reference record, not on successful predicted-track detection. Each available member contributes one pair of regional pressure minimum and wind maximum per retained time; the extrema need not occur at the same grid point. Pressure minima are approximated by the midpoint of the first occupied bin in cached 80-bin pressure histograms, while wind maxima are stored directly. The IBTrACS comparison uses available observations with both quantities for the represented storms, including its finer observation cadence; it is not restricted to the identical 12-hour forecast timestamps. Wind is converted from knots to m/s and pressure to hPa. Each source’s pooled pairs receive equal row weight in a Gaussian KDE (SciPy’s default bandwidth rule), rather than equal storm weight. Contours are drawn at the 30th and 70th percentiles of each source’s density evaluated at its own points. For the dependence panels, each source’s pressures and winds are ranked separately, ties receiving their average rank (ties are frequent because pressure is a histogram-bin midpoint), and rank r among n rows is mapped to Φ−1(r/(n+1)), with Φ the standard normal distribution function. This keeps each variable’s ordering and makes every marginal standard normal, so no increasing transformation of either variable, such as a mean offset, a compressed wind distribution, or a monotone conversion between wind definitions, changes the panel. Spearman’s ρ is computed on the same rows; its 95% interval comes from 200 resamples of storms with replacement, because rows from one storm are not independent. Source-specific missing data, temporal weighting, and the pressure-bin approximation must still be kept in view. Data-volume estimate.

2.4 Dynamic DE–CMA-ES orchestration The formulation in Section 2.3 establishes the optimization process as a controlled stochastic system, in which population evolution is governed by a policy learned through the DDPG-based actor–critic framework. Building on this framework, this section specifies how the learned action variables are translated into adaptive regulation of the evolutionary mechanisms within the DRL-DCO algorithm. Specifically, the eight action variables introduced in Section 2.3.2, namely, F (t), CR(t), λ(t), LR(t) cov, ∆σ(t), p(t) res, r(t) eli , and β(t), jointly coordinate population composition, DE-based variation, CMA-ES covariance-guided sampling, and intergenerational preservation at each generation.

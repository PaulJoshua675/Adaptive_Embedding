RoBERTa-large obtains the lowest accuracy (0.3749), highlighting the importance of retrieval-oriented represen- tation learning for hierarchical relevance discrimination. T3 is more challenging, requiring discrimination among products from closely related sibling clusters. SPLADE++ achieves the best nDCG@3 (0.6662) and Recall@3 (0.8349), followed by Qwen3-Embedding-4B on nDCG@3 and Sentence-T5-large on Recall@3. This shows that fine- grained sibling discrimination does not necessarily improve with model size or dense semantic representations. For T4, Qwen3-Embedding-4B achieves the highest Consistency@5 (0.6041) and Consistency@10 (0.8373), indicating the most stable retrieval across paraphrases. Dense and learned retrievers generally show higher consis- tency than BM25, particularly at the shallow cutoff, where lexical matching is more sensitive to wording changes. The weaker ranking but comparatively stable behavior of RoBERTa-large further demonstrates that paraphrase con- sistency captures a distinct retrieval property. Overall, Qwen3-Embedding-4B achieves the strongest results on three of the four tasks, while SPLADE++ per- forms best on sibling discrimination. User Study. We conducted a user study to assess the nat- uralness and intent clarity of queries from three sources: our Fashion-RQBENCH (RQBENCH), real user queries (CHAT), and LLM-generated queries (LLM). 32 partici- pants rated each query on a 5-point Likert scale for both dimensions. Inter-rater reliability was high, with Cron- bach’s [7] α = 0.977 for naturalness and α = 0.969 for intent clarity, indicating strong agreement among raters. Table 4 summarizes the mean ratings. Naturalness rat- ings are comparable between RQBENCH and CHAT, with mean scores of 3.62 and 3.52, respectively. In contrast, in- tent clarity shows a strong separation: RQBENCH achieves the highest mean score (3.91), followed by LLM (3.76), while CHAT is substantially lower (2.83), with all pair- wise differences statistically significant. These results in- dicate that RQBENCH queries preserve the naturalness of real user language while providing substantially clearer and more actionable intent.

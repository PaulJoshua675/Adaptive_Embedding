Both SCCL teach- ers improve over the unmasked Base model in PadChest AUC, MCC, and F1 (Table 3). Among the single-teacher students, distillation from the inclusive teacher obtains a higher cross-dataset mAUC than distillation from the con- sensus teacher (0.846 vs. 0.844; rows (d) and (e)). Us- ing both rule-specific teachers raises mAUC to 0.850 (row (f)), while achieving the highest PadChest AUC and tying the highest MCC. The inclusive teacher remains marginally higher in F1.

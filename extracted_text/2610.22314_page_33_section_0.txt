Patient realism. For each patient turn, annotators first mark the reasons that apply, grouped into form (punc- tuation or typography, register, length or format) and content (monologuing, self-analysis, emotional mis- match, sycophancy), and then answer “Was this turn written by a real person?” on a five-point scale from 1 (certainly not) to 5 (certainly yes) (Figure 7). On the 71 turns (15 items) rated by all three annotators, ordinal Krippendorff’s α was 0.58 (pairwise 0.43–0.64), and all three ratings fell within one point of each other on 76% of turns (Table 13).

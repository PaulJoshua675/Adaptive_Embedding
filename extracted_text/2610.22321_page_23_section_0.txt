D AVC-BENCHMARK DETAILS D.1 BENCHMARK CONSTRUCTION AND EVALUATION PROTOCOL Sample selection. We construct AVC-Benchmark through three complementary steps. We first perform stratified sampling based on human label configurations to balance the coverage of major consistency and failure patterns, including global narrative conflicts, temporal offsets, missing or hallucinated audio, and fine-grained attribute mismatches. We then use ImageBind (Girdhar et al., 2023) to extract audio and video representations and cluster their joint embeddings, from which we sample across clusters to reduce semantic redundancy. Finally, we add explicitly constructed negative samples to strengthen the coverage of controlled failure cases that are less frequent in naturally generated data. The resulting benchmark contains 706 samples across different generation paradigms and data sources: 172 ground truth samples, 243 JAVG samples, 100 A2V samples, 72 V2A samples, and 119 samples from the explicit-negative construction set. The JAVG subset contains Ovi-I2V, Ovi-T2V and Veo3 (Google DeepMind, 2025) outputs, the A2V subset is generated by an internal model, and the V2A subset is generated by HunyuanVideo-Foley. Explicit negative construction. We construct additional controlled negatives from 50 Veo3 source clips. Among them, 10 contain salient emotion or global narrative cues, 20 contain clear temporal relations, and 20 contain salient audio attributes. We apply the edit operations in Table 14 to pro- duce 119 negative samples. These edits complement naturally occurring generation failures with controlled semantic, temporal, event-existence, and fine-grained acoustic inconsistencies, balancing the failure-case distribution and broadening coverage beyond naturally occurring generation failures. 23

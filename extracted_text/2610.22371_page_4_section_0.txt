(2026a) and Li & Team (2026). 3.2 VIDEO GENERATORS AS 4D PERCEIVERS Gen4P repurposes a video generator into a single-pass feed-forward 4D perceiver, without multi-step denoising (Ke et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2025) or noise injection (Zhan et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2026) to read out diffusion features. This formulation enables direct feature extraction while avoiding the overhead of iterative denoising. Specifically, Gen4P consists of a video-generator-based encoder with a LoRA-adapted video DiT backbone, and a cross-attention query decoder, as described next. 3.2.1 VIDEO GENERATIVE ENCODER Feed-forward Encoder with LoRA. We adopt Wan2.1-1.3B (Wan et al., 2025) as our encoder backbone. To repurpose a multi-step diffusion model into a single-pass feed-forward encoder, we first investigate how the amount of noise corruption applied to the input video latent affects the quality of the extracted DiT features. Motivated by the stronger performance of low-noise features, and to further achieve a simple deterministic feed-forward formulation, we directly pass clean video latents to the DiT without noise corruption, and use its final-block features before the velocity prediction head for query decoding.

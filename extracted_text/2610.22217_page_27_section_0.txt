Preprint. A.12 PROMPT EXAMPLES We provide the prompt templates used for both skill construction and evaluation. Skill Construction Prompts. Our skill construction procedure uses three main prompts. First, the successful-trajectory self-reflection prompt asks the optimized capability expert to identify reusable verification behaviors and their activation conditions from its successful trajectories. Second, the failed-trajectory self-reflection prompt asks the same expert to diagnose causal failure mechanisms and derive corresponding corrective principles. Finally, the evidence-grounded skill induction prompt provides the curated evidence ledger to the same expert and asks it to consolidate recur- ring success and failure patterns into a compact capability-level skill. The corresponding prompt templates are shown in Figures 11–13. Evaluation Prompts. Figure 14 presents the vanilla evaluation prompt, where the model re- ceives the input image(s), the benchmark-specific query, and the corresponding response instruc- tion. The model is instructed to reason step by step and place its final response within <answer> and </answer> tags. The required output format is instantiated according to the benchmark: multiple-choice tasks require the final option letter, OCR tasks follow the official per-sample recog- nition instructions. For evaluating skill-augmented capability experts in the ablation and analysis experiments, we pre- serve the original multimodal input and response instruction and condition each expert on its cor- responding capability skill. The four extracted skills correspond to perception, OCR and chart un- derstanding, symbolic reasoning, and multi-image reasoning, respectively.

TRACER improves tool-selection accuracy while showing the model a small subset of the registry. On our primary testbed, Mixed Tau2 R&T (Large), it beats the full registry for three of the four models while selecting 5 of 128 tools, by 4.7 points for GPT-5.4; Grok-4 trails by 0.3, but this figure is well inside that cell’s 3.8-point fold SD. The margin generally widens for every model as the registry shrinks. TRACER’s gains exceed one SD in 31 of the 34 cells where it wins; the clearest exception is GPT-5.4 on API-Bank, whose 131 steps make the smallest folds. TRACER’s stability is also similar to LLM-ONLY’s: their fold SDs differ by a median of 0.8 points and TRACER is tighter in 21 of 44 cells.

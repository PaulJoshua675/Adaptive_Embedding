F.2 Computational Efficiency on Large DAGs We profile representative MalNet-derived DAGs and generated DAGs on an NVIDIA A800-SXM4 GPU with 80 GB memory. The real-graph profiles range from 16 nodes and 25 edges to 3,416 nodes and 9,215 edges, while the generated profiles range from 8 nodes and 16 edges to 32,768 nodes and 65,536 edges. The real graphs have median edge density below 0.3%. All methods use identical graphs and reported model settings with batch size one. The real-DAG comparison reports the graph preprocessing required by each method followed by one model-forward pass. The generated-DAG comparison reports the recorded prediction-path latency for each method; the DTA-GT row isolates model forward after graph-input construction, and its largest-graph first-use stages are reported separately. The real-DAG DTA-GT stage profile uses ten warm-up and thirty timed measurements for graph-input construction, followed by thirty warm-up and five hundred timed measurements for model forward. The independently instrumented profile on the largest generated DAG uses one warm-up and five timed measurements for each construction stage, followed by thirty warm-up and fifty timed measurements for model forward. The profiled DTA-GT configuration uses hidden dimension 192, ten encoder layers, six attention heads, four retained spectral modes, and q = 0.25. Graph-dependent structural masks and spectral features are constructed once per graph and shared by all encoder layers.

Additional Case Studies D.1. Using an Intermediate Result to Specialize Later Work BrowseComp Q193 asks for a radio station’s first broadcast year through a chain of clues involving a 1993 role-playing supplement, one of its authors, a children’s book, and the author’s father. The initial investigation identifies Creative Campaigning and returns four authors: Tony Pryor, Tony Herring, Jonathan Tweet, and Norm Ritchie. The Planner then specializes the downstream investigation using this returned list, checking which author wrote the 2015 children’s book and establishing the father’s identity. The resulting chain identifies Jonathan Tweet, Grandmother Fish, Roald Tweet, and WVIK, leading to the answer 1980.

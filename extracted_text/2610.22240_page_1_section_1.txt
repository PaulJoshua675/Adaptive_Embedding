Multimodal deep search is moving from short queries to long-horizon investigations that con- tinually acquire evidence. However, train- ing such agents remains constrained by lim- ited challenging training questions and in- sufficient process feedback. We introduce MMSearch-OPD, combining controllable data refinement with joint reinforcement learning (RL) and on-policy distillation (OPD). Our Re- fine pipeline performs targeted expansion of reasoning chains derived from existing simple multimodal questions. Parameterized transfor- mations control search depth and cross-modal evidence requirements while reusing existing image–question pairs. For training, answer rewards and lightweight behavior penalties guide RL, while the reward gap modulates token-level teacher guidance on imperfect stu- dent trajectories. An exponentially decaying distillation coefficient reduces teacher depen- dence as training proceeds. Experiments show gains from data refinement and further improve- ments from joint training after SFT, indicating stronger long-horizon multimodal deep search capabilities. 1

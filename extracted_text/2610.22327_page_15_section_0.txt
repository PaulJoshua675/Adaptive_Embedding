We compare the full dual-view model with single-view variants (Plot-only and Delay-only) and duplicated-view controls (Plot+Plot and Delay+Delay). The duplicated-view controls keep the full dual-tower architecture, per-tower token budgets, and training procedure of our model, but feed both towers the same representation; they therefore test whether the improvement can be reproduced by a second visual stream alone (Appendix C.6). The results show that the contribution of the complementary representation depends on the bench- mark. Plot only is already strong on TSExam and ChatTS, whereas Delay only is substantially weaker on numerical alignment. Combining the representations provides modest improvements over Plot only on ChatTS numerical alignment and reasoning, while producing a substantially larger improvement on the broader TSRBench evaluation. Importantly, neither duplicated-view control reproduces the overall TSRBench gain of the complementary pair, despite matching its architecture and visual-token budget. C.2 TSRBENCH CATEGORY-LEVEL ABLATIONS Although Table 3 reports all 15 individual TSRBench tasks, Table 7 provides the corresponding category-level aggregation. The dual-view model leaves the already strong perception performance of Plot only essentially unchanged, while improving the aggregate results on reasoning, prediction, and decision-making. The same-view controls again remain close to their corresponding single-view variants in overall accuracy.

B.2.3 EPISODE, OBSERVATION AND DECISION An episode contains at most 64 decisions. At each decision the policy receives the instruc- tion and an eight-frame window of 512 × 512 egocentric RGB frames: the current frame plus seven frames sampled uniformly from the history, padded with black frames at the start of the episode. The policy answers with reasoning followed by one mid-level action, <think>...</think><act>...</act>, where the action is turn left or turn right by 15, 30 or 45 degrees, move forward by 25, 50 or 75 cm, or stop. B.2.4 AGENT DESIGN The population consists of three agents, TURNER, MOVER and STOPPER, each a separate full fine- tune of NaVILA-8B (Cheng et al., 2025). They see the same frames and instruction and partition the action space: TURNER owns the six turning actions, MOVER the three forward moves, and STOPPER the stop. The monolithic agent is the same base model fine-tuned on the full action space.

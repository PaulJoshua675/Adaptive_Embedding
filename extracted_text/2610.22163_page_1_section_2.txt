Large Language Models (LLMs) have profoundly transformed com- puting education, showcasing remarkable capabilities in solving computing assignments [18, 20]. LLMs are also being used for facilitating instructional activities, such as generating assignment questions [19], providing automated grading [22], and notably, de- veloping AI tutor systems that address student queries. Furthermore, as classrooms become increasingly multicultural, the need for inclu- sivity in educational materials and technologies grows. Non-native English speaker (NNES) students, in particular, face language barri- ers and a lack of confidence, which often causes hesitation to seek help from instructors or TAs. Our AI tutor system seeks to assist NNES students’ learning by accepting questions in the students’ native language (specifically Mandarin), delivering responses bilin- gually, and generating a technical term glossary to ensure equitable access to educational materials. Most AI tutors [3, 10, 11] utilize Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) [9], which enhances question-answering by dynamically re- trieving and synthesizing relevant information from a data repository, ensuring accuracy and contextual relevance. Our RAG-based GPT-4 tutor differs from systems that rely on a single source, such as lecture captions or textbooks. Instead, it integrates a comprehensive range of course materials, including lecture notes, project descriptions, quizzes, past exams, and the course website, enabling holistic data retrieval. Two specialized mechanisms have been developed to enhance our AI tutor. Firstly, our system incorporates a translation stage that converts Mandarin questions into English before applying RAG, enabling full leverage of RAG’s capabilities. Secondly, our system employs a question extraction mechanism that addresses instances where a student’s query references only the question number instead of providing the question details, by precisely identifying and retrieving details of the referred question. We evaluated our system using 91 Piazza posts, demonstrating that it can accurately and helpfully answer student questions and generate technical term glossary in Mandarin. Our work focuses on answering the following research questions: • RQ1. What is the quality of the translation stage and to what extent does it improve RAG’s performance in processing non- English questions? • RQ2. What is the accuracy of the question extraction stage and to what extent does it enhance the system’s ability to manage diverse course materials and student questions? • RQ3. What is the relevance and usefulness of RAG’s results concerning the questions? • RQ4. What is the quality of the AI tutor’s responses in terms of correctness, helpfulness, solution revelation, translation, and technical term glossary generation? 2

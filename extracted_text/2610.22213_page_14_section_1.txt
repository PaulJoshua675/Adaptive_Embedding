We establish theoretical guarantees to understand both the optimization and generalization perfor- mance of RBCD for nonconvex problems. With polynomially decaying step sizes, we tune the iteration number to derive the optimal high-probability bound O(d log n/n) on the population gradients for gen- eral nonconvex problems, and on the excess population risks for gradient-dominated problems. We consider high-dimensional problems and further develop dimension-independent bounds for loss func- tions of a specific structure. Our analysis exploits the iterative scheme of RBCD to avoid boundedness and moment assumptions on the gradients, requiring only standard smoothness assumptions. There are several problems worthy of further investigation. First, it would be interesting to extend our analysis from uniform sampling to importance sampling of coordinate blocks [1, 67, 73]. Second, we only consider polynomially decaying step sizes. It remains to be explored whether other popular step-size choices for RBCD can further improve our results [62]. For example, step sizes can typically be chosen inversely proportional to the Lipschitz constants of the partial gradients. 14

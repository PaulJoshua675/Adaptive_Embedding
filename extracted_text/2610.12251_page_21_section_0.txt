The theorems below use what Proposition 7.3 states and one more property of the image: every cell read by a record after the startup records, other than the cells under the heads and the sink, is written earlier in the pass. The image SA,w has this property for every A ≥8 and w ≥1, and it has B = βG/8 bytes, with β = 8 + 3α. The proofs apply to every image with these two properties, so the step block may compile any netlist that computes the step of HA. A state of Mα K,S,L starts SA,w on a recipe τ when its active bank holds SA,w ready, its read tape holds τ from cell 0 followed by zeros, and its window holds booted = 0 and clear requests; the other banks, tapes and cells may hold anything. Theorem 7.4 (Universal construction by the dynamics). Let A ≥8 and w ≥1, let SA,w have G records, and let K ≥2 and S ≥G. Let τ be a recipe with |τ| < 2w whose string X = δ(τ) has at most Sβ/8 bytes, let L ≥8|τ|, and let P be the number of steps that Proposition 6.13 gives for τ. From a state that starts SA,w on τ, after P passes, each of S steps but the last, which has G: (i) the next bank holds the bits of X from slot 0, followed by zeros, with both its heads at 0, and the next tape holds τ from cell 0, followed by zeros, with its head at 0; (ii) the active bank still holds SA,w and the read tape τ, the window holds booted = 0 and clear requests, and no other bank or tape has changed; (iii) the bank index has moved to the next bank. From then on Mα K,S,L evaluates the records of X. Proof. By Proposition 7.3 the first pass loads x0(m∗ A), returns the three heads to cell 0, clears the next bank and the next tape, and carries out the first step of the host. Each later pass carries out one more step, with the device of Definition 7.2, up to the pass in which the halt bit is set; that pass clears booted, returns the arm and the copy head to cell 0 and hands over at its last step. By Theorem 6.12 and Remark 6.14, P ∗on τ with the device of Definition 5.1 halts after the steps of Proposition 6.13 with output δ(τ) ∥τ, reads no byte at h = |τ| before it requests the rewind, and requests the rewind once, at h = |τ|. On this run the two devices agree. As |τ| < 2w, cnt does not wrap. Before the rewind dest = 0, and each read loads the next byte of τ from the read tape and increments cnt, so cnt = h. At the rewind the read head returns to cell 0 and lim becomes |τ|. After it, each read loads the next byte while cnt < lim and sets ceot at cnt = |τ|, as Definition 5.1 does at h = |τ|. The bytes emitted before the rewind, δ(τ) = X, are written under the arm from bit 0 of the cleared bank, the arm moving on by one bit after each, and they fit since 8|X| ≤Sβ. Those emitted after it, τ, are written under the copy head from cell 0 of the cleared tape, and they fit since 8|τ| ≤L. The active bank and the read tape are not written, and no other bank or tape is addressed. From the next step on, the bank holding X is active, at slot 0. By Lemma 6.5 every byte string has a recipe, so SA,w builds every image that fits a bank from a recipe that fits a tape, itself among them, whatever the size of the image.

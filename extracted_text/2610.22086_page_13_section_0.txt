Manual inspection indicates that most errors in- volve incomplete outputs for multi-step instruc- tions, particularly when both intermediate reason- ing and final answers are requested, as well as oc- casional factual inaccuracies. These issues are con- sistent with cases where instruction structure is not fully preserved during translation or generation. Overall, the findings suggest that preserving in- struction completeness in multilingual settings re- mains challenging for complex, multi-part prompts, particularly in scenarios involving structured rea- soning requirements. This highlights the need for more robust validation of instruction fidelity across languages in table-oriented generation tasks.

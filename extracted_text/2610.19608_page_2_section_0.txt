In September 2026 we collected three waves of answers from gemini-3.1-pro-preview to the same 54 questions about twelve software brands. Forecasts frozen before the second and third waves passed their gates at horizons of about 1.5 and 8 hours. Horizons of hours say nothing about weeks. This note reports D1, the same 162 requests sent 20.3 days after the third wave, under a plan that was frozen, hashed and registered before the first call, with the decision rule fixed in advance [8]. Under the registered rule, durability holds. A forecast frozen from the first wave alone kept its accuracy from 1.5 hours to 20.7 days (Brier between 0.0587 and 0.0604), and the most recent wave alone forecast D1 nearly as well as the pooled history. No D1 answer searched the web, so what held is the model’s stored association of these brands with these needs, under an unchanged version string. 2 The Answer Forecast waves Table 1 lists the four waves. All four sent the same 54 question texts to the same model identifier with the same generation settings: the Google Search tool included in the request, thinking level LOW, temperature 1.0, at most 8,192 output tokens, no system instruction, four requests in flight and no retries.

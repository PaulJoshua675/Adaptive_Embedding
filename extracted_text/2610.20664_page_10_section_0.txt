Extensive evaluation across six OOD multimodal search benchmarks demonstrates that IG-OPD significantly outperforms existing distillation and reinforcement learning baselines. Notably, our 4B student ef- fectively bridges the capacity gap, rivaling and even surpassing its 8B teacher without incurring inference-time overhead. Moving forward, extending post-retrieval evidence calibration to other environments represents a highly promising direction for further scaling autonomous web agents.

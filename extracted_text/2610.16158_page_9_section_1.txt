CIPHEUR treats conflict-graph search control as a joint decision over structural targets, interventions and their continuation. SIUs give generated programs and online LLMs a common executable task, while native search preserves the original optimization se- mantics. The completed experiments show broad terminal competi- tiveness, stronger late-search progress for the online configuration, and a reconstruction–recovery pattern that motivates interval-level feedback. A frozen one-shot controller further demonstrates de- ployment without runtime model calls. Together, these findings identify structural search control as a practical way to use LLM design knowledge and contextual reasoning in combinatorial opti- mization.

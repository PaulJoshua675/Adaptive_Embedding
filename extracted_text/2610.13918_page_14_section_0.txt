(B) Continuous 72-hour Mean Absolute Error (MAE) trajectories across the five clinical cohorts, converging within MAE ≈0.33–0.35. 7.1 Limitations The current corticothalamic network operates as a lumped single-channel (C3-A2) representation. Future extensions will incorporate multi-area spatial connectivity (frontal, central, occipital) to capture traveling slow waves and topographic spindle gradients. Furthermore, while the Saper flip- flop captures the primary wake-sleep switch, modeling REM sleep via extended sublaterodorsal (SLD) and ventrolateral periaqueductal gray (vlPAG) networks will refine micro-architectural REM transitions [24]. 7.2

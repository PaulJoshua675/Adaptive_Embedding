We report the count/tail gap B as an empirical diagnostic of estimator bias, not a guaranteed bound: counting zeroes the unresolved tail and is thus upward-biased on that mass (a one-sided bias, not a deterministic per-sample bound), while the tail estimate has no such bias in either direction, so although the truth sits at the tail edge of [ ˆRtail, ˆRcount] in every case we measure, a misspecified tail could err either way. Full uncertainty interval: a nested bootstrap that covers 5/5. The interval reported in the main text (Tab. 1) resamples the query set only. To fold in the other two sources—which distractors land in the sample, and the per-query EVT refit on that sample—we run a nested bootstrap on all five ILIAS backbones: draw K=8 disjoint 5M sub-pools of the 100M stream (disjoint uniform distractor samples), refit the per-query tail on each, and form the prediction; each bootstrap iteration then picks a random draw (sample + fit noise) and resamples queries with replacement (query noise). Table 7 reports the resulting 95% interval against the query-only interval. The added noise is small— the distractor-sample+fit standard deviation is σsamp+fit ≈0.003 (median), well below the query component (h/1.96 ≈0.008)—so the full interval changes little (endpoints shift more than they widen), and it brackets the held-out true mAP@100M for all five backbones. Query sampling, not distractor sampling or tail-fit, is the dominant uncertainty at the 5% budget. Ranking preservation and paired error. Interval overlap (Tab. 1) is a weak coverage test, since prediction and truth share the same queries. Two stronger statistics confirm the estimate. (i) Rank- ing. Across the five backbones the predicted and true mAP@1000 induce the same ordering for the reported estimate: Kendall τ = 1.0, Spearman ρ = 1.0, no sign flip among the ten pairwise margins. Across 20 disjoint distractor samples this holds for the nine pairs separated by more than the prediction error (preserved in every draw); only the tightest pair (PE-core vs. SigLIP2, true gap 0.010, within the error) is not reliably resolved, being statistically tied at 100M (App. D). Rank- ing is recovered down to a margin of ≈0.013 mAP. (ii) Paired error. The per-query difference APEVT q −APtrue q has pooled mean +0.002 with a 95% bootstrap CI of [−0.001, +0.004] over the 6160 query-backbone cells; per backbone the bias stays within ±0.008 (largest SigLIP/SigLIP2 +0.008, DINOv3 −0.006).

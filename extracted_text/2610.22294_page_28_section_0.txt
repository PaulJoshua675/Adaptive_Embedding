B.6 Likelihood Sharpening Some gains from example guidance could reflect sharpening of the demonstration-conditioned likelihood P1, rather than the additional information in the P1/P0 reward. To separate these effects, we compare example guidance with likelihood sharpening that uses the same total exponent on P1. At β = 0.5, the guided distribution, the sharpening distribution, and the sample weights for sharpening are Q0.5(u) ∝P1(u)3/2P0(u)−1/2, Qsharp(u) ∝P1(u)3/2, wsharp(u) ∝P1(u)1/2. Both methods reweight the same samples without additional sampling or tuning. The comparison matches the P1 exponent, but not necessarily weight concentration or a separately tuned decoding temperature.

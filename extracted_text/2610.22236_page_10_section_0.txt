4.6 Discussion On-policy distillation is run after reinforcement learning to put several specialists into one model. For specialists that share a base model, our results suggest that the training in that step may not be needed. What the trained student is asked to do, match each teacher on the text the models generate, has an exact layer-wise solution, and that solution already gives a model close to each teacher and more accurate on average than either. Adding a specialist then costs its rollouts and one more term in each layer’s sums; nothing is retrained. Training remains the tool when the teachers differ in architecture or size from the student.

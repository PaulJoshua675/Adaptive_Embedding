The spatial reasoning data comprise Cambrian-S VSI-590K (590K), SPAR (234K), VLM-3R VSI-Bench instructions (205K), VLM-3R VSTI-Bench instructions (132K), and the MindCube training set (10K). The general video data comprise LLaVA-Hound (64K), PhysGame PhysInstruct (140K), and a general video subset of Cambrian-S-3M (430K). RC Sample Selection. We construct a pool of approximately 1.6M RC samples from the verified source annotations. For SPAR-RC, we retain one question per view tuple and randomly assign either local yaw increments or cumulative headings as the prediction target. We also retain at most one sample for each image set. To reduce the concentration of small turns, we group individual turn magnitudes into six 30◦ bins over the folded angular range from 0◦to 180◦. Each chain is represented by the multiset of its step-level bins. We use deficit-greedy sampling to favor underrepresented bins, subject to limits of 96 samples per scene and 40 appearances per image. For VSI-RC, we retain only samples with video inputs and limit each clip to 40 questions. The retained VSI-RC samples are repeated twice to increase their weight in the training mixture. The final RC training set contains 150K entries and includes only two- and three-step chains. A.8 RC Chain Evaluation Evaluation Set Construction. We construct an evaluation set using the same RC procedure, with no overlap with the training samples. It includes four target types: local yaw increments and cumulative headings from SPAR-RC, and turn angles and discrete actions from VSI-RC. We evaluate two-, three-, and four-step chains. RC training uses only two- and three-step chains. For each chain length, we select up to 80 examples per target type. To cover different angular configurations, SPAR-RC examples are grouped by data source (ScanNet, ScanNet++, or S3D) and the 30◦bin containing the largest folded turn magnitude in the chain. VSI-RC angle examples are grouped by the same angular criterion, while action examples are grouped by their complete action sequences.

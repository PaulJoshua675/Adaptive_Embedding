In Chen et al. [9], coarse estimates of policy visitation distributions are used to construct a sampling dis- tribution for multiple-policy evaluation in tabular MDPs. Coarse estimation allows the cost of estimating visitation distributions to scale with 1/ε, up to logarithmic factors and factors depending on the tabular problem parameters, rather than 1/ε2. In our setting, we do not necessarily need to apply the coarse estima- tion technique as the context distribution does not depend on the policy, and we can query every target policy on the same context without observing rewards. Thus, for each context Xt ∼P, we sample Ak t ∼πk(· | Xt) and compute ϕ(Xt, Ak t ) for every k ∈[K]. This allows us to estimate the feature means and second moments directly from a shared batch of contexts. We begin by estimating the unknown population quantities ϕk and Σk.

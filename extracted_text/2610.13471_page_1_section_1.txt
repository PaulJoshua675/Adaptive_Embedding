Metaheuristic comparisons are often reported at evaluation budgets far larger than an expensive engineering simu- lation can afford. This paper presents REMBGO, an evaluation-budget-aware refinement of EMBGO. Its contribution is not any single operator: it is the integration of population preservation, scale-normalized exploration and recovery mechanisms whose parent-relative benefit is concentrated under restricted function-evaluation budgets. REMBGO is evaluated on CEC2022 at a primary, application-aligned budget of 1040 function evaluations, with a surrounding bud- get ladder reported descriptively to characterise budget dependence rather than as a family of controlled hypotheses. A component-removal study finds no individual mechanism whose removal survives multiplicity correction, consis- tent with an integrated rather than a single-operator contribution. The algorithm is then applied to EAF–STATCOM controller tuning, and the resulting frozen gain vectors are transferred without retuning to twenty pre-specified unseen stochastic arc realizations (210 fixed-gain simulations, all feasible). The selected gain-vector main effect accounts for only 3.6–5.8% of the crossed validation variation, against approximately 61.4% for interaction plus residual, and the matched REMBGO–EMBGO paired differences are controller-dependent in sign and not statistically significant. REMBGO is therefore presented as a resource-aware refinement whose benefit must be stated together with the eval- uation budget, not as a universally superior optimizer. Keywords: evaluation-limited optimization; REMBGO; CEC2022 benchmarking; metaheuristic optimization; STAT- COM; electric arc furnace. 1

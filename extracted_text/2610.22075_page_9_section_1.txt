We introduced the first prune-then-loop retrofit of a pretrained VLM evaluated on VQA. The retrofitted model outperforms the unpruned baseline while storing 23.3% fewer language-model parameters, with the gains concentrated on reasoning-oriented benchmarks. This gain is due to the recurrence itself, as the loop accounts for a significant improvement over the pruned single-pass baseline. At the same time, the recovery requires the text-only training stage, which yields the best performance among all evaluated training strategies. Finally, retrofitting recurrence into a pretrained VLM outperforms adding a vision encoder to a pretrained recurrent language model, which makes the retrofit the best performing route to a recurrent VLM among all evaluated baselines. 9

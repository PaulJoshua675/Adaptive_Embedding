RCER reconstructs cross-evidence relations during retrieval and independently analyzes the completed pool, allowing connections established across rounds to guide final answer derivation. 4.3 Ablation Study (RQ2) Table 3 shows the contribution of each component to RCER’s performance. Ablation settings are provided in Appendix D. Inter-Round Relational Evidence Acquisition. This component uses reconstructed relations to refine subquestions and acquire complementary evidence. Removing it reduces F1 by 16.03 points on 2WikiQA and 7.82 on MuSiQue, with EM drops of 14.40 and 8.20 points, respectively. The larger 2WikiQA loss highlights the importance of using intermediate evidence to determine which entity or relation to investigate next. On HotpotQA, F1 drops by only 0.94 points, where original-query retrieval already covers most supporting evidence. Adaptive acquisition therefore contributes most when relevant facts must be recovered through successive queries. Reasoning Context Reconstruction. This component assembles complementary sources and cross-evidence inferences into the reasoning context. Removing it lowers F1 by 11.31 points on MuSiQue and 4.81 on NarrativeQA. StrategyQA accuracy drops by 7.50 percentage points. These are the largest declines on these datasets among the three ablations. Joint selection retains combinations of supporting facts that acquisition-order selection may miss. The accompanying analysis links facts about the same entities and identifies how they jointly support the premises needed to answer the question. Cross-Evidence Compositional Reasoning. With the selected passages fixed, removing this component reduces 2WikiQA F1 by 8.47 points and EM by 8.70 points. MuSiQue F1 falls by 4.19 points, and StrategyQA accuracy drops by 6.20 percentage points.

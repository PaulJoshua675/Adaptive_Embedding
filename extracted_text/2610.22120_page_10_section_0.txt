REPRODUCIBILITY STATEMENT We have aimed to make every component of this work reproducible. The problem formulation, the assumptions underlying each theoretical claim, and complete proofs of propositions are given in Appendices B and F. Dataset construction, including the simulation configuration, the derivation of occupancy grids, and the extraction of propagation-relevant sets, is described in Appendices A and G. Architecture and tokenization details for the foundation model are given in Appendix C, and the complete pretraining objective in Appendix D. The diffusion decoder, its noise schedule, and its sampler are specified in Appendix E, and full training configurations — optimizer, learning rate, schedule, batch size, and hardware — for the proposed pipeline, the ablated variant, and both baselines are given in Appendices G, H, and I. Evaluation metrics are defined in Appendix J, and the evaluation protocol, including how observation subsets are drawn across the measurement sweep, is described in Appendix G. Source code for training and evaluation, together with the pretrained dataset and checkpoints, will be provided upon publication.

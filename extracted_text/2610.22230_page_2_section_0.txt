While analytical models are not new, Origami extends prior approaches to multi-chiplet architectures, with accuracy validated at production scale. Agentic workflows for kernel optimization. To reduce en- gineering burden and migration friction, we codify complex kernel-tuning principles into agentic workflows: automating microarchitecture-aware configuration selection, swizzling pro- cess IDs, incorporating occupancy hints, and leveraging spe- cialized hardware instructions. Together, they achieve 1.6×– 2× speedups across both hand-written and compiler-generated kernels. Furthermore, we leverage agentic workflows to auto- mate the application of solutions that address hardware-specific bottlenecks; e.g., to mitigate the performance impact of atomics on MI350X, we apply a tree-reduction algorithm that utilizes shared memory to deliver up to 4× speedup in custom kernels. Our methods have been validated at scale, supporting produc- tion training on tens of thousands of MI350X GPUs at Meta. Across a representative set of production models, MI350X achieves a geometric-mean throughput of 101% relative to our established B200 baseline—a platform highly optimized through extensive deployment. This performance parity vali- dates the viability of our multi-vendor diversification strategy. Limitations. While end-to-end performance is comparable, MI350X’s GEMM performance remains an area for improve- ment, due to both the overhead of emulated TF32 GEMMs in the absence of native hardware support and the relative maturity of NVIDIA cuBLAS over AMD hipBLAS in handling irregular matrix shapes. Although we use DLRMs to drive our optimizations, the underlying principles are generalizable to a broader spectrum of ML workloads, including LLMs. Indeed, we have successfully optimized LLM training on clusters of more than 2,000 MI350X GPUs; a comprehensive analysis of LLM-specific performance is reserved for future work. To our knowledge, this is the first hyperscaler report on production-scale training using AMD GPUs. Our purpose is not to rank accelerators, but to share generalizable optimization methods for emerging hardware by synthesizing insights from our large-scale deployment of AMD, NVIDIA, and MTIA platforms. Our contributions have been upstreamed to the open- source PyTorch, Triton, and ROCm ecosystems.

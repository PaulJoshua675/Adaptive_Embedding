Lookahead Encoding Examples Table 11 gives concrete continuation pairs for P5. For each row, use the listed consonant as the preceding base. Appending the vowel sign extends that base’s cluster, whereas appending another copy of the consonant starts a new cluster. The continuations therefore require opposite decisions after identical left context. They share the first UTF-8 byte but have distinct first UTF-16LE bytes within their common 256-codepoint page.

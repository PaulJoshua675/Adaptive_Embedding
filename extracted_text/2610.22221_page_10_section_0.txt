Across the 17,958 impacts, Q is strongly rank-correlated with f0 (ρ = 0.87) and moderately anti-correlated with β (ρ = −0.54), while the two components are close to independent of each other (ρ = −0.11). Two patterns are worth drawing out. First, for both audio encoders and the PE-AV joint embedding, β is predicted better than f0, and the margin grows under LOMO, which is where category identity stops carrying the prediction. Second, the visual encoders order the targets the other way when pooled, favoring f0, which is consistent with pitch being partly inferable from visible object size, and that ordering reverses on unseen materials. Under true LOMO, the distilled student improves on both targets and improves most on β (0.257 →0.375), so what transfers is preferentially the damping term rather than the spectral one.

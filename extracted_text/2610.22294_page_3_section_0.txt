Example-guided sampling. We construct a tractable distribution over complete multi-site infills by revealing one token at a time, allowing us to compare candidate likelihoods with and without demonstrations. The resulting likelihood-ratio reward enables inference-time importance resampling to favor example-supported completions, offering an additional way to encourage adherence to demonstrations without retraining the generator. Empirical evaluation. We evaluate the effects of demonstrations, target counts, example guidance, and learned length prediction across repurposed few-shot prompting benchmarks and software refactoring case studies. We find that demonstrations generally improve infilling accuracy and guidance provides additional gains in some settings, but accurate length prediction remains a challenge. 1.2

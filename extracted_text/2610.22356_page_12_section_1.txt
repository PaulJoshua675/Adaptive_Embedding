We present DK-VLM, which introduces 3D geometry as position into the attention computation of vision-language models. Dual-Kernel Attention preserves the native semantic kernel and uses Point GTA and Camera GTA to model relations between scene points and camera motion across views. Reference-Frame Composition supervision reorganizes existing single-step annotations into training chains with stepwise targets, further improving spatial reasoning over multiple steps. Among the compared methods, DK-VLM-8B achieves the highest average score across six spatial benchmarks while preserving general video understanding. Ablations further support the complementarity between geometric relation modeling and stepwise supervision.

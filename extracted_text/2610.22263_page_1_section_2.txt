1.1 Post-training of language models In post-training of language models, the goal is to make the language model generate a desired response for a given prompt. Denote by x ∈X the prompt and y ∈Y the response. The language model can be viewed as a policy π, where π(y | x) denotes the probability of generating y given the prompt x. The response y consists of a sequence of tokens {yt}t≥0, each of which is randomly generated based on past tokens and the prompt. The generation of one token is known as a decoding step.

The thalamocortical loop gives persistence to the selection, and the motor gate only lets through the commands of the disinhibited channels 3.4 Evolution and fitness function The two robots on a team share the same genome and only differ in their initial motivations. This homogeneous team design, with selection at the team level, favors the evolution of communication [2] and leaves the informational value of the signal as the variable of interest. We use a genetic algorithm with a population of 48 individuals over 150 generations. The 10 best individuals are passed on unchanged to the next generation. The rest are obtained by uniform crossover of two parents chosen at random from the 25 best individuals, and each offspring receives Gaussian noise with a probability of 0.2 and a standard deviation of 0.12 in all its genes. The initial population is sampled from a normal distribution with a standard deviation of 0.5. Each individual is evaluated in six episodes per generation.

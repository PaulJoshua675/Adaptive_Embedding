Diffusion more than doubles rotation speed and improves orientation cov- erage by 11.2 points, but its largest gain is in transition coverage: 84.2% versus 55.4%. The deterministic policy therefore reaches many of the same orientations through a much narrower set of motions, behavior consistent with mode averaging. This distinction matters for both stages of our method: diverse local transitions improve the action conditioning available to the world model and give MPC multiple ways to approach a goal. Open-loop imitation error alone does not measure either property. b) More autonomous play improves world model pre- diction.: With the model and held-out evaluation ﬁxed, increasing real-robot play data from 0.54 to 5.11 hours reduces 50-step rotation MAE monotonically from 34.96◦ to 28.40◦, an 18.8% reduction (Figure 10). In this view, the primary pur- pose of supervision is not a ﬁnished controller or a dataset of expert demonstrations, but a mechanism for collecting further data. This offers a more efﬁcient approach to leveraging human supervision: humans provide the behavioral structure needed to begin interacting, while the robot acquires knowl- edge of action consequences through its own experience. Additionally, demonstrating play behavior is almost al- ways easier than demonstrating solution to a dexterous manipulation task. And in this paper, we show that a goal- agnostic policy is learnable via human demonstration and is highly data-efﬁcient. B. DSP vs. RL Compared to task-speciﬁc RL which organizes learning around reward optimization, DSP separates acquiring phys- ical knowledge from task goal. The goal enters at planning time, allowing the same interaction prior and world model to serve different target goals without retraining. Learning dynamics directly from autonomous robot interaction also removes the prerequisite of a task-speciﬁc simulator and subsequent sim-to-real transfer. Supervised play is therefore not merely an initialization for RL, but the basis of a complete learning-and-control framework. It opens a route to scaling dexterity through reusable physical experience, with human guidance initiating autonomous learning rather than prescribing the behaviors it must ultimately achieve. C.

In this work, we introduce MLExpert, a 35B MoE agentic model trained to autonomously conduct and improve machine learning experiments over long interaction horizons. We build an automated MLE task factory and a model-directed interaction harness, and train MLExpert with hierarchical agentic SFT and WIRL to learn from teacher demonstrations and execution feedback collected from its own exploration. Extensive experiments show that MLExpert improves over its base model on MLE-Bench Lite and achieves performance comparable to larger frontier models. Further improvements on NatureBench Lite and in agent scaffold and algorithm optimization demonstrate cross-task gener- alization. These results support treating long-horizon optimization decision making as a trainable model capability, providing a practical path toward autonomous machine learning engineering and scientific experimentation. 11

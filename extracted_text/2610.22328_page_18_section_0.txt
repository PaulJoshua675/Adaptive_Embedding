The teddy bear, alarm clock and toy train of a synthetic scene are placed in a lavender field at sunset (edit 1), in front of a red brick wall (edit 2), and on a smooth gradient backdrop from warm cream to light grey (edit 3). Our model keeps the three objects and gives a clean gradient. Emu Edit keeps the low, warm light of the sunset in front of the brick wall (edit 2), and at edit 3 leaves a clump of lavender stalks on the floor of the gradient backdrop and turns the blue train grey. VAE-LFA keeps the colour of the sunset: at edit 3 the whole image has a purple-magenta cast, the bear turns reddish and the clock face pink. FreqEdit prints the pattern of the brick wall into the smooth gradient. MT-OPSD leaves no trace of the earlier backgrounds but blurs the image and washes out its colours. 18

Saliency-Guided Replay for Continual Learning in Deep SNNs 3 overlooks the distinctive properties of spiking computation, including sparse event-driven activity and temporal dynamics. This motivates spike-compatible explanation strategies that can exploit temporal spiking activity [2,9]. Our work addresses this gap by adapting the principle of saliency-guided replay to SNNs. Instead of using Grad-CAM-based image patches as in EPR, we use SAM-based spatio-temporal saliency [9] to select compact replay information. In this way, ex- planation becomes a functional component of continual learning in SNNs, rather than only a diagnostic tool. 3 Methods This section introduces a continual learning framework for SNNs that combines Spike Activation Maps (SAM) [9] with a replay strategy inspired by Experience Packing and Replay (EPR) [23]. The key idea is to use temporally grounded saliency to store only the most informative regions of past samples, enabling memory-efficient replay in neuromorphic continual learning. 3.1 Spike Activation Maps Standard gradient-based explanation methods, such as Grad-CAM [24], are dif- ficult to apply to SNNs because of their discrete and non-differentiable spike dy- namics. We therefore adopt SAM, a forward-only explanation mechanism that extracts saliency directly from spike-train activity.

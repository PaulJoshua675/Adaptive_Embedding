Qualitative Results. The diversity gains are plainly visible (Fig. 4, six SD1.5 prompts; SDXL and SD3 examples in Figs. S1 and S2). DDIM and CNO are nearly indistinguishable, sharing global layout, object placement, and dominant color palette across all examples. DetNO instead varies object identity, view- point, and scene composition while remaining prompt-faithful, recovering degrees of freedom in the initial noise that standard sampling and pairwise objectives leave unexploited.

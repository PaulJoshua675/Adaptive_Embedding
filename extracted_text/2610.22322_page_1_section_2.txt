Building general intelligence has long been a central ambition of AI research. But where does intelligence reside? A prevailing view in modern AI is model-centric: intelligence is contained in the parameters of a model, and progress comes from increasing model size or improving those parameters through training. In this paper, we explore a different possibility. Intelligent behavior can also emerge from interactions among many simpler agents, giving rise to collective capabilities 1 arXiv:2610.22322v1 [cs.MA] 15 Oct 2026

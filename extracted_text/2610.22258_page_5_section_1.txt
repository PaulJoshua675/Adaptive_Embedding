In this study, we benchmarked the performance of SOTA image-denoisers on a texture dataset. We have shown that off-the-shelf denoisers achieve subpar denoising performance on texture images compared to generic natural images. In our benchmarking, we have also compared the denoising performance using several metrics like STSIM1, STSIM-2 that capture textural nuances better than SSIM. We have fine- tuned the models on the texture dataset and showed that the texture denoising performance improves, but at the expense of significant degradation in performance on the original dataset. The study highlights the need for better denoisers that can handle textures better, either separately or along with general image datasets. However, it is to be noted that since the models were trained on point-by-point (e.g. L1) losses, further efforts are required to allow more extensive use of perceptual metrics, such as STSIM-M and STSIM-VGG, that do not operate on a point-by-point basis. The next step in the study would be to design unified denoisers and train them on perceptual losses that are not point-by-point which can allow for a more holistic texture analysis. We believe that such unified denoisers can play a crucial role in the generation of natural images with rich texture in a diffusion or flow-based modelling framework.

This set-level functional equals the squared batch volume; its repulsion is strongest when the batch is most collapsed and self-attenuates as diver- sity is achieved. An anchor-based fidelity term keeps the optimized noises near plausible regions, and Lagrangian relaxation gives a two-term ob- jective with a single hyperparameter λ governing the diversity–quality trade-off, without training or sampler modification. Across three back- bones (SD1.5, SDXL, SD3), DetNO achieves leading intra-batch diver- sity in VendiScore and Mean Self-Similarity, and a downstream few-shot classification experiment confirms that broader mode coverage brings statistically significant accuracy gains for synthetic data augmentation in the low-shot regime. Fixing the pipeline and varying only the batch objective, both RBF-based aggregators outperform InfoNCE, and the log-determinant attains the best downstream accuracy on both bench- marks. Its diversity advantage over InfoNCE widens as the batch grows. Code is available at https://github.com/mobled37/DetNO.

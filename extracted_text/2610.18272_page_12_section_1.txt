The main comparisons use all 830 BrowseComp- Plus questions over 100,195 documents and all 105 NeuCLIRBench topics. The BrowseComp- Plus ablation and System Two model comparisons share 415 questions, selected by taking every sec- ond question ID from the numerically sorted of- ficial set. On NeuCLIRBench, queries combine the English topic title and description and search English machine translations of Persian, Russian, and Chinese documents. The topics comprise 54 from 2023 and 51 from 2024. Gold answers and relevance judgments are used only for evaluation. Answer quality on BrowseComp-Plus is mea- sured by accuracy, using the official grading tem- plate with GPT-5.5 at medium effort. Ranking qual- ity on NeuCLIRBench is measured by nDCG@20 and F1@20. nDCG@20 uses the official relevance gains g ∈{0, 1, 3}, while F1@20 treats documents with g > 0 as relevant and uses 20 as the precision denominator. Unjudged documents receive zero gain. Ranking metrics are computed per topic and then averaged across topics. Failed tasks receive a score of zero. Research budgets are fixed within each study. The cumulative ablations on both benchmarks use a 300-second active research budget. The System Two model comparisons use a 300-second elapsed- time budget. The study of System One models and candidate inspection depth uses a 3,600-second research budget and a 600-second limit per local judgment. Efficiency metrics are reported as per-question means for QA and per-topic means for ranking. System Two token usage covers input and out- put, including cached input and reasoning tokens. Search counts refer to individual queries. API costs are reported separately for System One and Sys- tem Two and cover research requests, excluding grading and local computation. The evidence analysis in Figure 4 examines how gold sources are retrieved and made avail- able during research. We measure gold-document recall separately for retrieved candidates, sources inspected by System One or System Two, sources with original text delivered beyond previews, and sources cited in the final response. For each ques- tion and each set, recall is the fraction of the ques- tion’s gold documents contained in that set. We av- erage these values over all 830 BrowseComp-Plus questions. Candidate pools follow each method’s native retrieval depth, using deep cached hits for JevGPT and Pi-Serini and returned hits for Reason- ColBERT and BCP/ReAct.

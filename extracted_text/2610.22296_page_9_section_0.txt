Fig. 2 presents the resulting accuracy matrix. Compatibility is preserved: in-chain TPR stays between 99.3 and 100% (mean 99.9%), matching the base setup. The diversity loss is therefore free in compatibility terms. Out-of-chain rejection, the failure mode of the base setup, is recovered: mean TNR rises from 11.3% to 99.98% (27 of 28 incompatible pairs at 100%, the last at 99.3%), with no residual failure cluster.

For these guards a request in another language is a source of nuisance: benign steps that were allowed in English are blocked. Small guards miss attacks. Every significant decrease in recall in the study is a guard below 10B parameters under translation: Nemotron Nano 9B loses 3.1, 4.5 and 4.3 pp of TPR on German, French and Turkish, 3.7 on Swahili and 7.4 on Amharic; Nemotron 3 Nano 4B loses 2.0 pp on German and Qwen 3.5 9B 3.1 pp on Swahili. Their false-positive rates move less and in both directions (Nemotron 3 Nano 4B −2.2 French, +2.5 Swahili; Qwen 3.5 9B +2.5 Amharic). For these guards the trusted channel is an attack surface: an attacker who controls the language of the request, or who targets non-English users, gets more attacks through. These are the weakest guards in the roster (BACC at most 0.80, Table 2). 4.2 The sign of the damage tends to follow guard size Figure 2 places the pooled shifts on a size axis.

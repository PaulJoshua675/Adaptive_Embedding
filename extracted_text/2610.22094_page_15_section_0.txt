Image, text, OR, and AND rules Table S5 evaluates four decisions on the same fixed CheX- pert pool. Image and Text are the corresponding high- threshold masks, OR is their inclusive union actually used by the analyzed teacher, and AND is their intersection. Image- and text-triggered sets are not disjoint; their inter- section is the AND row. The OR result is therefore not ex- plained only by the text rule. The mutually exclusive source partition gives image- only, text-only, and both shares of 19.03%, 79.61%, and 1.36% of OR flags, respectively. Their exact-agreement lifts are 3.7490 (95% CI, 3.7190–3.7788), 1.7181 (1.7088– 1.7273), and 5.5483 (5.4333–5.6626). This is an associa- tive source decomposition rather than a causal modality ab- lation. E.7. Partial finding overlap Exact equality is intentionally strict. On the same eligible observations, the online OR set also shows greater partial overlap: 57.4% of flagged observations share at least one present finding, compared with 45.5% of all eligible obser- vations, while mean Jaccard similarity is 0.252 versus 0.181 (Table S6). Thus the exact-agreement result is not solely a consequence of the equality threshold. E.8. Alternative pools and full-trace sensitivity Enrichment remains above one under every alternative CheXpert-defined pool (Table S7). Removing the different- patient condition changes the pathology-positive estimate only from 2.1568 to 2.1597. Extending the descriptive calculation to the full post-warm-up trace through mask step=36374 yields 280,227,481 CheXpert-pool observations and a 2.1774× lift (3.9940% vs. 1.8343%). The analogous NegBio- defined analysis contains 268,294,947 observations and yields 2.1013× (4.0745% vs. 1.9390%). These are sensi- tivity estimates; the best-checkpoint-aligned prefix remains the primary endpoint. E.9. Interpretation The actual online mask is persistently enriched for report- derived exact and partial finding agreement, including under image-triggered decisions and a second automatic labeler. This evidence concerns association within one recorded training trajectory. The absolute exact-agreement rate is 3.9556%, the OR capture rate is 8.0071%, and no inde- pendent manual clinical adjudication has been completed. The analysis therefore does not establish pair-level clinical equivalence or a causal downstream benefit of masking. F.

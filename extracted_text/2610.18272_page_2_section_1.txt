Agentic Search and Autonomous Research. Autonomous research combines search, source in- spection, and evidence integration. Schmidhu- ber (2015) proposed a reinforcement learning con- troller that queries a recurrent world model for reasoning and planning. ReAct and IRCoT in- terleave reasoning with external information ac- cess (Yao et al., 2023; Trivedi et al., 2023), while Search-R1 learns to search through reinforcement learning (Jin et al., 2025). WriteHERE interleaves recursive planning and execution across retrieval, reasoning, and composition (Xiong et al., 2025). In fixed-corpus research, BCP/ReAct provides a tool loop (Chen et al., 2026); Pi-Serini separates retrieval, browsing, and document reading (Hsu et al., 2026). RISE exposes a bounded document workspace (Zhuang et al., 2026), while AtlasNav provides persistent corpus navigation (Guo et al., 2026). JevGPT connects local source judgments to subsequent research decisions. Dual-System Reasoning and Collaboration. Dual-process accounts distinguish fast, intu- itive judgments from slower, deliberative reason- ing (Kahneman, 2011). History compression con- solidates recurrent predictors (Schmidhuber, 1992); One Big Net extends this principle to control and prediction skills (Schmidhuber, 2018). Model cascades and routers allocate computation across models to balance quality and cost (Chen et al., 2024; Ong et al., 2025). AVI-Talking uses LLM- generated instructions to guide a specialized facial synthesis model (Sun et al., 2024), while SciDia- gramEdit coordinates editor, judge, and coach roles to evolve diagram editing skills from paper revi- sions (Sun et al., 2026).

TCBench (Gomez et al., 2026), the closest benchmark, evaluates one-to-five-day track and intensity forecasts, including probabilistic scores, rapid intensification, and postprocessing. GEFS (Zhou et al., 2022) outperforms its evaluated AI ensembles on track CRPS: probabilistic evaluation does not uniformly favor AI. Different models, seasons, references, and scoring protocols preclude direct comparison with our rankings. We complement this work with location-density scores, track detection rates, and surrounding-field diagnostics. CRPS scores forecast distributions without separating intensity from spatial errors; our intensity and quantile-footprint analyses examine these separately without additively decomposing CRPS. 8

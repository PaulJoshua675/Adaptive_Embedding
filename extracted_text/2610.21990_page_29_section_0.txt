In low-light conditions, image sensors exhibit two primary types of noise: pho- ton shot noise, caused by the discrete nature of light, and read noise, introduced by the sensor elec- tronics. To realistically model these effects, the gamma-compressed RGB image Iscaled(x) (which is encoded in the 0–255 range) is first converted into a physically meaningful linear intensity represen- tation Ilinear(x) ∈RH×W×3. This linearisation ensures that the added noise correctly reflects the underlying photon and elec- tronic statistics across all three colour channels, naturally simulating real-world chroma noise.

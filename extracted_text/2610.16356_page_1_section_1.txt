We present an overview of R package called naspaclust, a tool for spa- tial fuzzy clustering improvement by hybridising the Fuzzy Geographically Weighted Clustering (FGWC) with nature-inspired optimisation algorithms. There are seven optimisation algorithms in general, namely Artificial Bee Colony, Flower Pollination Algorithm, Gravitational Search Algorithm, Harris- Hawk Optimisation, Intelligent Firefly Algorithm, Particle Swarm Optimi- sation, and Teaching-Learning Based optimisation. The optimisation algo- rithms available do not only use the basic algorithms, but also the modified version of several parts such as randomisation terms and updating rules. Furthermore, we also provide additional options by providing more distance metrics options based on the rdist package. This study showed the imple- mentation of the naspaclust package in various cases, as well as comparing the performance of the algorithms in the scope of basic algorithms. The naspaclust package allows to improve the performance of fuzzy clustering, mainly in spatial context.

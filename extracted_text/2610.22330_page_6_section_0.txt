(a) The denoiser is read from bottom to top, with one of 12 blocks expanded. RMSNorm precedes each modulated sublayer; headwise query–key RMSNorm precedes RoPE, while values undergo neither operation. The 256-dimensional noise embedding supplies modulation parameters and residual gates (dashed arrows). A gated likelihood shortcut bypasses the Transformer and its untied vocabulary head. (b) A bootstrap call to the same denoiser produces token probabilities without gradient tracking.

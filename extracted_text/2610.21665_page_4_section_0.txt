The original curves and axes are unchanged. 3 Results 3.1 From the small-batch gap to near-BP accuracy The starting gap depends strongly on configuration (Fig. 1). At approximately 50,000 updates, shallow WP reaches about 63% accuracy with B = 1, compared with about 95% for BP. With B = 1,000, WP reaches approximately 92–93%, against BP near 98%. Equal updates do not equalize data exposure: these settings correspond to roughly 0.83 and 833 nominal epochs, respectively. The extended comparison is the principal accuracy result (Fig. 2). For batches of 4,000 and 10,000, shallow WP rises to approximately 96.8% and 97.0%, respectively, near the end of the displayed 450,000- update budget. The early BP reference is approximately 97.5–98%, leaving a visually estimated gap of about 0.5–1 percentage point. These curves directly support near-BP performance for this shallow configuration. BP reaches its high accuracy early and subsequently declines towards roughly 94% during prolonged training. WP eventually exceeds these late BP checkpoints, but this does not establish superiority over a suitably selected BP model. The thesis itself notes that such prolonged BP training was included for comparison with WP and was not a recommended BP training protocol [4, Sec. 3.3]. The empirical conclusion is proximity to the earlier BP accuracy at a much larger training cost. 3.2 Batch size, data exposure, and learning speed Figure 3 separates the two budget views reported in the thesis. Against update count, batches of 4,000–10,000 approach a similar accuracy near 97%, while batches of 1,000 and 2,000 remain lower. Against epochs, the largest batch no longer gives the strongest curve: B = 4,000 reaches a higher accuracy within the displayed exposure range than B = 10,000. Beyond a useful stabilization regime, processing more examples per update yields diminishing returns. The distinction is substantial: 450,000 updates at B = 4,000 represent 30,000 nominal epochs, and at B = 10,000 they represent 75,000. The ten-epoch results give a complementary short-budget comparison (Table 1). Shallow WP reaches 90.2%, 7.2 percentage points below the reported BP reference. Five-hidden- layer WP reaches 73.1%. Near-BP long-run accuracy consequently does not imply near-BP learning speed. 4

Efficiency Our method provides efficient re- trieval: token scores for all positions come from one encoder forward pass, so decoding needs no autoregressive decoder calls. We measure single- query latency (batch size 1) on a single A100 GPU on the NQ320K dataset, and find that our method takes 24 ms per query and is at least 7× faster than NCI, DSI, and MEVI, 28× faster than ULTRON, and 397× faster than SEAL.

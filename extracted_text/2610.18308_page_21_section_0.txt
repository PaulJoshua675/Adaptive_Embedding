J.1 Interviewer Agent (Stage 3) Each interview has ten messages: an Account phase (six messages) followed by a Clarify phase (four messages). The first interviewer message is not generated: it is one of four predefined open-ended questions, selected uniformly at random: 1. “Tell me about your relationship with movies — when you sit down to watch something, what kind of experience are you usually look- ing for?” 2. “Walk me through the last movie you really enjoyed — what was it and what stuck with you?” 3. “If a friend asked you to recommend a movie that captures your taste, what would you pick and why?” 4. “When you’re choosing what to watch on a typical Friday night, what goes through your mind?” All subsequent interviewer messages are generated (temperature 0.7) from the interview so far only; the interviewer never receives the user’s profile or ReDial history. Account phase. You are an interviewer conducting a conversation about movie preferences. Your goal is to understand how this person thinks about and chooses movies — their taste, their reasoning, the patterns in what they like. CRITICAL CONSTRAINTS: - You do NOT know anything about this person beforehand. You have no profile, no history, no list of their favorite movies. - You must NOT make assumptions about what kind of movies they like based on stereotypes or popular taste patterns. - Every question should follow naturally from what they have ACTUALLY told you in this conversation. QUESTIONING STYLE FOR THIS STAGE (ACCOUNT): - This is the OPENING phase. Let them talk freely. - Ask broad, inviting questions that let them set the direction. - When they mention a specific movie, genre, or feeling, follow up on THAT thing rather than pivoting to a new topic. - Do NOT challenge or contradict. Avoid leading questions. - Do NOT introduce movies, directors, or genres they did not mention. CONVERSATION SO FAR: {conversation_history} Output ONLY your next question. Clarify phase.

Equilibrium Matching (Wang & Du, 2025) learns a time-invariant gradient field, in its best-performing form without an explicit energy, and Rodriguez et al. (2026) train it on an Energy Transformer, whose blocks relax latent tokens on a Hopfield-type energy, reaching class-conditional FID 28.56 on CIFAR-10. There the image-space update is a learned function of the relaxed tokens rather than the gradient of any energy in x; in ours it is the gradient of γ−1EPCN = Vθ + O(γ). Each of these works trains by backpropagation. Physical and neuromorphic generation. A parallel line generates with physical substrates. Sim- ulated Kuramoto oscillators (Unconventional AI, 2026) and continuous spins descending a learned quadratic energy (Sun et al., 2026) evolve a latent state from a random start and render it through a small convolutional decoder, reaching class-conditional CIFAR-10 FID 8.76 and clean-FID 9.71 respectively, the former also at ImageNet 64 × 64. Optical systems have implemented a diffusion model’s denoiser (Oguz et al., 2024) and a single-pass generator (Chen et al., 2025), demonstrated in hardware at MNIST scale, and spiking diffusion models target event-driven neuromorphic chips (Cao et al., 2024). All of these are trained by backpropagation. B¨osch et al. (2026) generate 28 × 28 MNIST zeros and ones with a local rule, on an energy with no hidden units. Ours relaxes in image space with no decoder, and uses Equilibrium Propagation to obtain local updates for an energy with hidden layers. 7 DISCUSSION AND LIMITATIONS Simulation cost. On a GPU, an EP training step costs about sixteen times a backpropagation step, and an implicit-solve step about five, with the relaxation budgets dominating. A substrate that settles physically would not pay that cost, but its speed, energy and settling accuracy remain unmeasured: everything here is simulated. Locality and hardware realisation. The relaxation is local in the sense EP requires: every quan- tity a weight update needs is present at its two ends. The nodes of our training runs are, however, architecture blocks rather than single weight matrices, chosen to reduce simulation cost, so a literal implementation would need circuitry to carry the error through each block’s internal layers. One node per weight matrix removes this, and left the training gradient unchanged in the one checkpoint we measured (App. D). Analog EP also obtains the backward drive J⊤e from a feedback pathway programmed to match the forward one; any drift between the two leaves dynamics that descend no energy at all (Kendall et al., 2020). Scope of the evidence. App. K collects the scope of each claim. Each arm is a single run, so train- ing variance is unknown, and the small FID differences do not establish a ranking. We demonstrate EP training on CIFAR-10 only; at ImageNet-32 we verify the inference correspondence on the au- thors’ released checkpoints, but did not train. The hardware-plausible architecture was trained and sampled only as a feedforward network, so it has not been demonstrated together with EP. The equi- libria were computed by noise-free digital relaxation in error coordinates; the local state dynamics share their fixed points (App. E) but were not run to equilibrium at this scale. Analog noise, finite bit depth and forward–backward pathway mismatch remain untested. 10

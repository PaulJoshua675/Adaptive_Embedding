C.4 Commons Harvest This benchmark uses the Melting Pot substrate commons_ harvest__partnership, where four harvesters compete for apples in a shared field and can zap one another. Each player has the Safe Harvest objective: 𝜙𝑖= G¬player_i_zapped∧Fplayer_i_apple_collected, 𝑖∈{0, 1, 2, 3}. Handcrafted reward. Each agent receives +1 for its first apple collected before being zapped. The first zap gives −1 and prevents further apple rewards, cancelling any earlier apple reward. Another player’s apple collection or zap does not affect this reward. We obtain apple labels from the collector’s positive native payoff and zap labels from the substrate’s per-target zap events. C.5 Territory This benchmark uses the territory__rooms substrate from Melting Pot. In the two-player Role Claim task, players race to claim a resource first. Let 𝑐𝑖denote the ownership-change event player_i_resource_claimed. The objectives are 𝜙0 = ¬𝑐1 U (𝑐0 ∧¬𝑐1), 𝜙1 = ¬𝑐0 U (𝑐1 ∧¬𝑐0). Thus each player tries to make the first sole claim. Simultaneous first claims are ties, and later claims cannot reverse the temporal outcome. Handcrafted reward. At the first step on which a claim occurs, the sole claimant receives +1 and the opponent −1. A simultaneous first claim gives both players 0. Later claims give no reward. D Technical Details D.1 Counterfactual Experience Replay We apply CER separately to each agent’s deterministic reward automaton. We append the automaton-state identifier to the agent’s observation and store the successor-state propositions at each step. Relabelling keeps the environment rollout fixed and recomputes the automaton states from alternative start states. We keep the original sample and discard duplicate relabellings. Our procedure adapts Voloshin et al. [54] to finite-trace ltl𝑓. In particular, our deterministic finite automata require no 𝜀-jump transitions used with Büchi automata in ltl, and rewards depend on acceptance at episode termination instead of repeated visits to Büchi accepting states.

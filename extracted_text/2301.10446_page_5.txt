( Here L denotes a complete topological vector space.) If we take as a starting point a Jordan algebra B we can take as C either the cone B+ or the dual cone. The group U can be identiﬁed with the structure group Strg(B). Sometimes it is convenient to ﬁx a semiring W consisting of endomorphisms of the cone; if we are starting with Jordan algebra B the semiring W can be deﬁned as the smallest semiring containing the structure semigroup Str(B). 3 (Quasi)particles To deﬁne (quasi)particles and their scattering we should specify time translations Tτ and spatial translations Tx as elements of the structure group Strg(B). In other words we should ﬁx a homomorphism of the commutative translation group T to Strg(B). The translation group acts also on the cones. We are using the same notations Tτ, Tx for time and spatial translations of the cones. As usual we denote TτTxα as α(τ, x). As in [5], [7] we deﬁne (quasi)particles as elementary excitations of translation-invariant stationary state. Applying the general deﬁnition of geometric approach we can say that an el- ementary excitation of stationary translation- invariant state ω is a quadratic or Hermitian map σ of the ”elementary space” h into the cone of states C. This map should commute with spatial and time translations. In addition one should ﬁx a map of h into the space End(L) of endomorphisms of L such that σ(f) = L(f)ω [7]. In the framework of Jordan algebras ω is an element of the positive cone B+ or of the dual cone B∨ +. For deﬁniteness we assume that ω is an element of the dual cone; then L should be identiﬁed with B∨. (Recall that that the elementary space h is deﬁned as a pre Hilbert space of smooth fast decreasing functions depending on x ∈Rd and discrete variable i ∈I. The spatial translations act as shifts by a ∈Rd , the time translations commute with spatial translations. We can consider real-valued or complex-valued functions; we should consider quadratic maps in the ﬁrst case and Hermitian maps in the second case.) Let us start with linear map ρ : h →B commuting with translations. Let us ﬁx translation-invariant stationary state ω ∈B∨ + obeying additional condition T tω = ω for all T ∈T .

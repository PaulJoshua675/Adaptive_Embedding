We present SCOPE, a semantic-aware credit optimization framework for RL-based prompt engineering with frozen text-to-image generators. By pooling policy log-probability shifts within coherent semantic spans to modulate a shared completion advantage, SCOPE resolves subword credit fragmentation while requiring no separate span rewards, additional image renders, or external parsers. Across Qwen-Image, SD3, and FLUX.1-dev, SCOPE achieves substantial improvements over vanilla GRPO, delivering average relative gains of 9.48% on GenEval and 7.62% on T2I-CompBench. Accordingly, SCOPE establishes structure-aligned credit assignment as a plug-and-play paradigm for unlocking the compositional potential of frozen visual generators. 9

Preprint Our contributions can be summarized as: • We introduce Velox, a training-free framework that uses frustum slab correspondences to reduce growing KV attention cost in chunked video world models. • We devise a soft geometric fusion rule that uses OBB frustum slab overlap to modulate each layer’s model-derived block importance while preserving an unbiased fallback for uncovered regions. • We implement Velox with global-aligned Gilbert KV storage, an optimized sparse attention kernel, and asynchronous frustum slab construction on the same H200 as denoising, reaching a 4.36× attention speedup and a 2.43× denoising speedup at chunk 48. 2

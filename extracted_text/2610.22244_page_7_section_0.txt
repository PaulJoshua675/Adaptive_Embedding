Gemini 3.8 Flash and Claude Sonnet 5 are the best guards (BACC 0.983 and 0.958), and neither shows a significant change in FPR or TPR under any language (all five for Gemini, the three pre-registered for Claude); their placebo-adjusted flip rates are 0.0 pp and at most 1.1 pp. GLM 5.2 shows one significant shift, a decrease of 1.1 pp in false positives on French, and none in recall. The scanner control makes the Gemini result sharper than mere robustness. The control receives the identical prompt with the user-request field removed; its prompt hash differs and its inputs are 43 tokens shorter. It agrees with the full Gemini guard on 100.0% of 3,660 paired verdicts on the original wording, has an identical operating point (TPR 0.977, FPR 0.012), and flips at most one of its 3,660 verdicts under every condition, as does Gemini itself. The instruction text appears verbatim in the history for only 3 of 1,220 steps, so this is not copying: on this benchmark the trajectory carries everything a strong guard uses, and the trusted channel is redundant. A guard that ignores the request cannot be attacked through its language; the converse is that such a guard also cannot use the request to disambiguate an action, which is the case Task Shield-style designs [5] are built for. 4.6 The damage is concentrated in a few instructions Flip rates are averages over 1,220 steps from 58 instructions; the per-instruction view shows they are not evenly spread. TS-Guard on Turkish flips 9.3% of verdicts overall, but on one instruction (“Can you recommend me a hotel with highest rating in Paris? If there are multiple options... ”, 26 steps) its block rate goes from 12% to 92%, i.e. 81% of that instruction’s steps flip. Gemma 4 26B on Turkish moves “Perform the following tasks in the order they are given: 1. The low-resource pair hits the same family: Nemotron 3 Nano 4B on Swahili moves “Perform the following tasks in the order they are given: 1. The direction is not always towards blocking: TS-Guard on Swahili releases the Facebook-security-code instruction from 92% to 33% blocked, and Nemotron 3.5 on Swahili releases the TODO-list e-mail instruction from 95% to 37%.

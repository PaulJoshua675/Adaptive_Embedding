Selection helps only when a sample pool contains both correct and incorrect rewrites. Among 127 oracle-length pools, 87 contain only judged-correct samples, 17 only incorrect samples, and 23 both; the corresponding counts with learned lengths are 0, 55, and 72. Selecting a correct rewrite whenever one is available could therefore improve pooled success by at most 8.3 points with oracle lengths, versus 45.2 points with learned lengths. Even within the 23 mixed oracle-length pools, guidance lowers expected success from 62.0% to 50.5% (−11.5 points, [−19.7, −3.1]); this is a post-hoc comparison on an outcome-selected subset. A post-hoc Checkstyle example (commit b142dda472, distinct from Figure 13) illustrates how the reward can favor an incorrect rewrite. The developer-consistent rewrite u+ of logMessage preserves the logged line number and MSG_DUPLICATE_REGEXP, whereas an incorrect rewrite u− changes both, yet receives a higher reward (8.69 versus 2.05 nats).

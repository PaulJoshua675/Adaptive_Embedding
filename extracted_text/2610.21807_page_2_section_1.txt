2.1 Platform affordances and structural constraints Platform architecture shapes how users interact and produce content [5]. The concept of “affordances” — coined by Gibson [5, 17] and adapted to technology by Norman [24] and Treem and Leonardi [33] — refers to the actionable properties between an actor and an object; on social platforms these affordances bound the space of possible interaction modes [4, 33]. Community question-answering platforms such as Yahoo! Chiebukuro, Quora, and Stack Exchange are explicitly built around asynchronous, reciprocal infor­ mation-seeking [1, 29]; thread loops, best-answer selection, and reputation or incentive systems shape the quality and structure of the resulting exchange [7, 31].

In sum, our dataset pro- vides a permissively-licensed foundation for build- ing commercial multilingual LLMs. Limitations We acknowledge that establishing with certainty the generation provenance of third-party datasets is not always possible. Our compliance assessment is therefore conducted to the best of our knowledge, based on available documentation, dataset cards, and associated publications. While our human evaluation achieves high inter- annotator agreement, it covers sampled subsets of the data rather than exhaustive annotation, meaning that quality issues in unsampled portions cannot be fully excluded. The annotation schema, though structured and validated, reflects the judgments of a finite pool of annotators and may not capture all dimensions of quality relevant to downstream tasks. The choice of using five European languages was led by industrial motivations. However, we acknowledge that substantially different morpho- logical, syntactic, or pragmatic properties, includ- ing non-Latin-script and low-resource languages, are not addressed, and it is unclear how well the translation and regeneration pipelines would trans- fer to such settings. Restricting to permissively licensed sources also necessarily excludes some high-quality datasets, which may limit coverage in certain task domains.

2.1. Fuzzy Clustering In 1965, Lotfi Zadeh introduced a fuzzy logic concept that states that a certain subject has a true value between zero and one Zadeh (1965). In a clustering context, fuzzy logic works by defining that a subject can be- long to more than one cluster with a specific calculated degree of member- ship (Bezdek, 1981; Han et al., 2011). The most common fuzzy clustering method in GDA is Fuzzy C-means (FCM) which was introduced by Dunn (1973) and developed by Bezdek (1981). FCM is a fuzzy clustering method that uses the degree of membership of an object with the object’s distance to the center of the group (Mason and Jacobson, 2007). An object will be a member of a cluster if the object has the highest degree of membership in the cluster (Nasution and Kurniawan, 2018).

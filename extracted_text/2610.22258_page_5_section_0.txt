Next, we carried out an evaluation of the fine-tuned denois- ing models on the original natural image datasets. The per- formance is reported in Table II. We observe that the models fine-tuned on the texture dataset do not perform as well on the original dataset. A visual demonstration is presented in Fig- ure 3. This result shows that, in the process of adaptation to the texture dataset, the models have catastrophically forgotten [28] the original dataset. The incongruence in performance suggests that natural images and texture images have low-dimensional embeddings that overlap minimally with each other. Thus, one can infer that textures have to be handled separately compared to other natural images. A unified denoising model that handles both texture images and other natural images equally well is an exciting direction for future research.

Mosaic: Multi-Session Agentic Reinforcement Learning Agentic RL. Reinforcement learning techniques such as RLHF (Ouyang et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2026b) and RLVR (Chen et al., 2026b; Yu et al., 2026) are used to improve LLMs’ alignment, reasoning, and downstream task-solving capabilities (Tan et al., 2025; Zheng et al., 2025). Agentic RL extends this paradigm to LLM agents, aiming to enhance capabilities of multi-turn interaction through enhanced rollout sampling (Dong et al., 2026; Hou et al., 2025), process-level reward design (Liu et al., 2026; Zhang et al., 2026c), and other optimization strategies (Deng et al., 2026). However, most existing agentic RL methods still assume that each trajectory is contained within a single session.

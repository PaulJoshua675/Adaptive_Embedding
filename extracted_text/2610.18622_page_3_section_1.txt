The methodology is organized into five subsections. First, we describe the datasets used in the experiments. Second, we segment the documents according to their structural organization, repre- senting each document as a collection of chunks. Third, we consider four document representation strategies based on these chunk collections, varying which chunks are indexed and what contextual information is included with them. Fourth, we index the chunks according to each representation strategy and retrieve them using lexical and dense retrieval models, mapping chunk scores to doc- ument scores. Finally, we evaluate the resulting document rankings using nDCG@10 and MRR against whole-document baselines. Figure 1 shows an overview of the methodology. 3

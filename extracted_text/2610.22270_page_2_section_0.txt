Fig. 1. Overview of the proposed unsupervised clustering pipeline. Buses are detected and tracked in surveillance video, each crop is described by frozen DINOv2 morphological features and HSV color histograms, both descriptors are combined through weighted early fusion, and the fused vectors are projected with UMAP and grouped with K-Means. Bus illustrations were generated with AI. by a surveillance camera, without requiring labels at any stage of feature extraction or clustering. • A dataset of 7,972 bus images extracted from about 1,460 hours of video of a Mexico City arterial road, manually annotated for evaluation and publicly avail- able at https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/irvingvasquez/ public-bus-images. • An ablation study on the fusion weight showing that a moderate color contribution increases the precision of the target cluster with respect to morphological features alone, whereas an excessive color weight leads to over- segmentation or to the collapse of the clustering space.

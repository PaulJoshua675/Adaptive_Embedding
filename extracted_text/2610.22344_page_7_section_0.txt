Implementation details. Each MLE task is assigned a maximum of 3,000 interaction steps and a 12- hour runtime, using 20 vCPUs, 220 GB of RAM, and a single NVIDIA H200 GPU. MLExpert is initialized from Qwen3.6-35B-A3B. General SFT uses coding and tool-use trajectories from StepFun [22]. For MLE SFT, we collect teacher trajectories from Kaggle-MLE Factory tasks, MLE-Dojo [23] tasks, and scientific tasks derived from Nature-family papers. Any training tasks overlapping with MLE-Bench or NatureBench [12] are excluded. Data statistics, training hyperparameters, and implementation details are provided in Appendix C. 5.2. Main Results MLExpert achieves the highest medal rate among same-scale models and reaches the perfor- mance level of larger frontier models. As shown in Table 1, under the unified MLExpert Harness, MLExpert raises the medal rate of its Qwen3.6-35B-A3B base model from 37.9% to 72.7%, achieving the highest rate among the same-scale models evaluated and performance comparable to GLM-5.3 and Kimi-K3.

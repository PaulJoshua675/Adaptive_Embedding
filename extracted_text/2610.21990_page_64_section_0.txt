The differences, however, have a considerable offset in the magnitude. While for the Hand-Drill and Backpack classes it can be considered just a model or training particularity, it is not the same case for Fire-Ext. and Survivor. A good candidate expla- nation for this would be the common features shared by the two groups of classes. Survivor and Fire-Ext are represented using brighter colours and a larger surface covered by their instances, while Backpack and Hand-Drill often have less contrastive colours and definitely a smaller instance sur- face. Conversely, on the CART dataset [25], Figure 4.11 shows a different distribution. Here, the model configuration where the input setting with low-light images which are not enhanced as RGB inputs, provides worse performance for all the classes. The natural light present in CART [25] which is distributed more evenly, opposed to the artificial light and the cave environment of PST900 [8], man- ages to provide a better colour balance which is preserved across the synthetic low-light adaptation and enhancement process throughout. The only issue that persists even after the enhancement pro- cess has been applied is the absence of the Person class among the predictions which can be again attributed, as in the PST900 [8] case, to the small surface covered by noise across its instances.
